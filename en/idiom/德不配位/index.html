德不配位

dé bù pèi wèi

In English

  • unworthy of one's position
  • unfit for the role

Meaning

Unworthy of one's position.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • not up to the job

Example

德不配位的領導者，會失去眾人的信任。

A leader who is unworthy of his position will lose people's trust.

Usage

From the Book of Changes; used to criticize those in power.

Similar

  • 🪑尸位素餐shī wèi sù cān

    draw a salary without doing the job; hold a sinecure — Drawing a salary without doing the job.

    Officials who draw a salary without doing the job should resign.

  • 🎫名不副實míng bù fù shí

    not live up to its name — Not living up to the name.

    This "five-star" restaurant doesn't live up to its name.

Opposite

  • 🎗實至名歸shí zhì míng guī

    be well deserved — Recognition that is fully deserved.

    Winning the annual award was well deserved.

  • 🧓德高望重dé gāo wàng zhòng

    venerable — Venerable and highly respected.

    The venerable teacher was respected by three generations of students.
