10 · Art & Performance

引人入勝

yǐn rén rù shèng

In English

  • captivating

Meaning

Captivating.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • gripping
  • a page-turner

Example

第一章引人入勝。

The opening chapter is captivating.

Usage

For writing, stories and scenery.

Similar

  • 💗扣人心弦kòu rén xīn xián

    a nail-biter; cliffhanger — A nail-biter.

    The final game was a nail-biter.

  • 😋回味無窮huí wèi wú qióng

    finger licking (good) — Finger-licking good.

    The soup was finger-licking good.

Opposite

  • 🥱平淡無奇píng dàn wú qí

    run-of-the-mill — Run-of-the-mill.

    It was just a run-of-the-mill day at the office.

  • 💤過目即忘guò mù jí wàng

    instantly forgettable; unmemorable — Instantly forgettable.

    The film was underwhelming and instantly forgettable.
