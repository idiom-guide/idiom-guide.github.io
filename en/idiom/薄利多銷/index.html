薄利多銷

bó lì duō xiāo

In English

  • low margins, high volume

Meaning

Low margins, high volume.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • sell cheap and sell a lot
  • pile it high, sell it cheap

Example

折扣零售商靠薄利多銷生存。

Discount retailers live on low margins and high volume.

Usage

For retail and business strategy.

Similar

  • 🏷️物美價廉wù měi jià lián

    good value for money — Good value for money.

    The brand built its name on good value for money.

Opposite

  • 💲漫天要價màn tiān yào jià

    ask a sky-high price — A sky-high price.

    The seller asked a sky-high price, expecting to be talked down.

  • 🎰一本萬利yī běn wàn lì

    a small outlay for a huge return — A small outlay for a huge return.

    There is no such thing as a small outlay for a guaranteed huge return.
