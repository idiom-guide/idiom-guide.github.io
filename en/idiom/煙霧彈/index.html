煙霧彈

yān wù dàn

In English

  • a smokescreen
  • a red herring

Meaning

A smokescreen.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • a decoy

Example

這項宣布只是掩護裁員的煙霧彈。

The announcement was just a smokescreen for the layoffs.

Usage

Often 放煙霧彈 (throw up a smokescreen).

Similar

  • ↔️聲東擊西shēng dōng jī xī

    make a diversion — Feinting east and striking west.

    The general made a diversion in the east and then attacked from the west.

  • 🕵️暗渡陳倉àn dù chén cāng

    take a covert route; feint to the east and strike to the west — Taking a covert route.

    The general feinted to the east and struck to the west.

Opposite

  • 🔓開誠布公kāi chéng bù gōng

    in full transparency — Open and frank.

    The two leaders spoke in full transparency about their differences.

  • 📢直言不諱zhí yán bù huì

    speak frankly; not mince words — Speaking bluntly.

    She spoke frankly and pointed out the flaws in the plan.
