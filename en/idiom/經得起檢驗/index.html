經得起檢驗

jīng de qǐ jiǎn yàn

In English

  • (not) hold up to scrutiny

Meaning

Able to stand up to scrutiny.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • hold up to scrutiny
  • stand the test
  • withstand scrutiny

Example

好的理論必須經得起檢驗。

A good theory has to hold up to scrutiny.

Usage

Often with 時間, 事實 or 公評, as in 經得起時間的檢驗 (stand the test of time).

Similar

  • 🕰️歷久彌新lì jiǔ mí xīn

    timeless — Gaining value over time; about time.

    Her designs are timeless.

  • 💯無懈可擊wú xiè kě jī

    be flawless; be unassailable — Flawless.

    Her argument was flawless.

Opposite

  • 🦶站不住腳zhàn bù zhù jiǎo

    untenable; doesn't hold water — Doesn't hold water.

    His argument simply doesn't hold water.

  • 🥚不堪一擊bù kān yī jī

    collapse at the first blow; be easily crushed — Collapses at the first blow.

    The team looks strong but crumbles at the first blow.
