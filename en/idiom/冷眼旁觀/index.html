冷眼旁觀

lěng yǎn páng guān

In English

  • on the sidelines

Meaning

Watching from the sidelines.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • look on coldly
  • sit on the fence

Example

別人在爭吵時，她在一旁冷眼旁觀。

While the others argued, she watched from the sidelines.

Usage

Can mean detached and objective, or cold and unhelpful.

Similar

  • 🍿作壁上觀zuò bì shàng guān

    watch from the sidelines; stand idly by — Taking a back seat.

    While the two firms fought a price war, the rest of the industry could only watch from the sidelines.

  • 😑漠不關心mò bù guān xīn

    uncaring — Uncaring.

    The landlord was uncaring about his tenants' complaints.

Opposite

  • ❄️雪中送炭xuě zhōng sòng tàn

    help when it is needed most — Help when it's needed most.

    A true friend helps when it is needed most.

  • 🦾鼎力相助dǐng lì xiāng zhù

    generous support — Generous support.

    This event would not be possible without your generous support.
