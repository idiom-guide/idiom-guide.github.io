緊追在後

jǐn zhuī zài hòu

In English

  • with sb on/at its heels

Meaning

Hot on someone's heels.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • hot on sb's heels
  • with sb on its heels
  • close behind

Example

快艇疾駛而去，海巡緊追在後。

The speedboat sped off with the coast guard on its heels.

Usage

For chases, or rankings and results closing in.

Similar

  • 👀緊迫盯人jǐn pò dīng rén

    keep sb on a tight leash — Marking someone closely.

    The defender kept the striker on a tight leash.

  • 🏇並駕齊驅bìng jià qí qū

    running neck and neck — Neck and neck.

    The two runners were running neck and neck down the home stretch.

Opposite

  • 💨望塵莫及wàng chén mò jí

    lead by a great margin; sth set sb apart from sb — Left far behind.

    Her sales record leaves everyone else far behind.

  • 🐘實力懸殊shí lì xuán shū

    A overmatch B in sth; be overmatched by — A mismatch.

    The local team was overmatched by the national champions.
