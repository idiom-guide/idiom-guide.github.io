無風不起浪

wú fēng bù qǐ làng

In English

  • no smoke without fire

Meaning

There's no smoke without fire.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • there is no smoke without fire
  • there must be something to it

Example

那個傳言一定有原因，無風不起浪。

There must be something behind that rumor. There's no smoke without fire.

Usage

Implies a rumor isn't entirely baseless; neutral to skeptical.

Similar

  • ℹ事出有因shì chū yǒu yīn

    there is a reason behind it — There's a reason for it; urges finding out the cause.

    There is a reason behind his temper today.

  • 🍠其來有自qí lái yǒu zì

    have its origins; there is a reason for it — It has its origins; neutral.

    This view has its origins; it didn't come out of nowhere.

Opposite

  • 🐟無稽之談wú jī zhī tán

    sheer nonsense; baseless talk — Groundless talk.

    That claim is sheer nonsense.

  • 🎐捕風捉影bǔ fēng zhuō yǐng

    chase shadows; act on groundless rumors — Chasing shadows.

    These reports are groundless speculation with no evidence behind them.
