居高思危

jū gāo sī wēi

In English

  • stay vigilant at the top
  • don't rest on one's laurels

Meaning

Staying vigilant at the top.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • be humble in success

Example

即使位居高位，好的領導者也會居高思危。

Even at the top, a good leader doesn't rest on her laurels.

Usage

Close to 居安思危 (be mindful of danger in times of peace); a reminder to leaders and market leaders.

Similar

  • 🎪戒慎恐懼jiè shèn kǒng jù

    walk a tightrope — Walking a tightrope; ever watchful.

    Working for a volatile boss feels like walking a tightrope.

  • ☂️未雨綢繆wèi yǔ chóu móu

    be fully prepared; provident — Preparing for a rainy day.

    Keep an emergency kit at home; it pays to be fully prepared.

Opposite

  • 😎得意忘形dé yì wàng xíng

    got carried away — Getting carried away.

    He got carried away after the first win and started bragging.

  • 🐑毫無戒心háo wú jiè xīn

    unsuspecting — Completely off guard.

    The unsuspecting tourists walked straight into the trap.
