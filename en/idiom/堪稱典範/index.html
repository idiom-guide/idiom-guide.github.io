堪稱典範

kān chēng diǎn fàn

In English

  • sth be exemplary

Meaning

Exemplary.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • a model of
  • set the standard

Example

她在危機中的領導堪稱典範。

Her leadership during the crisis was exemplary.

Usage

For leadership, conduct, work and design.

Similar

  • 👨‍🏫為人師表wéi rén shī biǎo

    teacher of exemplary virtue — A role model in conduct.

    A teacher should be a model of virtue for students.

  • 🙇備受推崇bèi shòu tuī chóng

    widely honored/acclaimed — Widely acclaimed.

    The professor's book is widely acclaimed.

Opposite

  • 🪑德不配位dé bù pèi wèi

    unworthy of one's position; unfit for the role — Unworthy of one's position.

    A leader who is unworthy of his position will lose people's trust.

  • 🐭一顆老鼠屎，壞了一鍋粥yī kē lǎo shǔ shǐ, huài le yī guō zhōu

    a bad apple spoils the bunch — One bad apple spoils the bunch.

    One dishonest employee ruined our reputation: a bad apple spoils the bunch.
