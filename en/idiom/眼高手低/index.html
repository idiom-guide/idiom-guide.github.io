眼高手低

yǎn gāo shǒu dī

In English

  • aim high but fall short
  • all talk and no skill

Meaning

All talk and no skill.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • can talk the talk but can't walk the walk

Example

他眼高手低。

He aims high but falls short.

Usage

For criticizing people whose ambition outstrips their ability.

Similar

  • 🌠好高騖遠hào gāo wù yuǎn

    aim too high; overreach — Aiming too high.

    He aims too high and ignores the basics; try to be more down-to-earth.

  • 📄紙上談兵zhǐ shàng tán bīng

    armchair general — An armchair general.

    He is an armchair general who has never visited a real battlefield.

Opposite

  • 👣腳踏實地jiǎo tà shí dì

    staying grounded and pushing ahead — Down-to-earth.

    He is a down-to-earth man who stays grounded and keeps pushing ahead.

  • 🖌️有兩把刷子yǒu liǎng bǎ shuā zi

    know one's stuff; have real skill — Knowing one's stuff.

    That mechanic has some secret sauce: he fixes any engine in minutes.
