核心原則

hé xīn yuán zé

In English

  • cardinal principle

Meaning

A core principle: the most important, unshakable rule.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • the core principle
  • the cardinal principle
  • a guiding principle

Example

誠實是新聞工作的核心原則。

Honesty is the cardinal principle of good journalism.

Usage

核心 goes before nouns, as in 核心價值 and 核心原則.

Similar

  • 💰金科玉律jīn kē yù lǜ

    golden rules — Golden rules; sounds authoritative, even sacred.

    "The customer comes first" is the golden rule in our shop.

  • 🥇首要規則shǒu yào guī zé

    cardinal rule — The number-one rule.

    The cardinal rule of interpreting is to stay faithful to the speaker's meaning.

Opposite

  • 🤏無關緊要wú guān jǐn yào

    immaterial — Of no importance.

    Whether we meet at six or seven is immaterial.

  • 🔹枝微末節zhī wēi mò jié

    minor details; trivial matters — Trivial details.

    Don't spend too much time on trivial details.
