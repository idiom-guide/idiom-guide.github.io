12 · Duration & Speed

迫在眉睫

pò zài méi jié

In English

  • time is a luxury we don't have
  • sth. is looming

Meaning

Looming.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • sth is looming
  • imminent

Example

截止日期迫在眉睫，我們沒有時間了。

The deadline is looming, and time is a luxury we don't have.

Usage

For deadlines, crises and problems.

Similar

  • 🚨刻不容緩kè bù róng huǎn

    urgent; brook no delay — Urgent.

    Climate action is urgent.

  • 🧯燃眉之急rán méi zhī jí

    in extremis — A pressing need.

    The hospital needed supplies in extremis, and the delivery arrived just in time.

Opposite

  • 🏜️遙遙無期yáo yáo wú qī

    nowhere in sight — Nowhere in sight.

    A settlement is nowhere in sight.

  • 🌅來日方長lái rì fāng cháng

    there will be many more opportunities ahead — Many more opportunities ahead.

    There will be many more opportunities ahead to work together.
