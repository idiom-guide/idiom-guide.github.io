心驚膽戰

xīn jīng dǎn zhàn

In English

  • frightened

Meaning

Frightened.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • scared stiff
  • terrified

Example

響亮的雷聲讓我們心驚膽戰。

We were frightened by the loud thunder.

Usage

Same as 膽戰心驚, with the characters reordered.

Similar

  • 😨膽戰心驚dǎn zhàn xīn jīng

    tremble with fear — Trembling with fear.

    He trembled with fear in the dark.

  • 😱非常驚駭fēi cháng jīng hài

    shit a brick — Jumping out of one's skin.

    He nearly jumped out of his skin when the alarm went off.

Opposite

  • 🦁天不怕地不怕tiān bù pà dì bù pà

    I eat fear for breakfast — Afraid of nothing.

    The toddler is fearless. She eats fear for breakfast.

  • 😎泰然自若tài rán zì ruò

    unfazed — Unfazed.

    She remained unfazed by the critics' attacks.
