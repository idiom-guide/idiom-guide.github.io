濃妝豔抹

nóng zhuāng yàn mǒ

In English

  • heavily made-up
  • overdone

Meaning

Thick makeup and gaudy dress: overdone in appearance.Negative · General

Example

她濃妝豔抹地出席，像要上台表演。

She showed up heavily made-up, as if for a stage show.

Usage

Leans toward overdoing it and carries criticism. The opposite of light makeup or none at all.

Similar

  • 👗花枝招展huā zhī zhāo zhǎn

    all dolled up; gorgeously dressed — Dressed up brightly and showily; about a festive look.

    The young women were all dolled up for the banquet.

  • 🤳搔首弄姿sāo shǒu nòng zī

    preen; strike poses — Deliberately posing coquettishly; about behavior.

    She struck coquettish poses one after another in front of the camera.

Opposite

  • 🧼素面朝天sù miàn cháo tiān

    bare-faced; no makeup — No makeup; about plainness.

    She went out bare-faced and looked completely at ease.

  • 🌸天生麗質tiān shēng lì zhì

    a natural beauty; naturally beautiful — Born beautiful; about natural looks.

    She is a natural beauty and hardly needs makeup.
