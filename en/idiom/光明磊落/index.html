光明磊落

guāng míng lěi luò

In English

  • scrupulous
  • upright
  • honorable

Meaning

Open and aboveboard.Positive · Formal

Example

他是光明磊落的商人，一向遵守規矩。

He is a scrupulous businessman who always follows the rules.

Usage

Describes character and conduct.

Similar

  • ⬜光明正大guāng míng zhèng dà

    fair and square — Fair and square.

    We won the contest fair and square.

  • 😌問心無愧wèn xīn wú kuì

    true to oneself; look oneself in the mirror — With a clear conscience.

    I did my best, so I can look myself in the mirror with a clear conscience.

Opposite

  • 🕳️居心叵測jū xīn pǒ cè

    have an ulterior motive — Having an ulterior motive.

    I suspect he has an ulterior motive.

  • 🎭心口不一xīn kǒu bù yī

    disingenuous (slightly dishonest); disingenuous — Disingenuous.

    He claims to support the plan but quietly works against it, which is disingenuous.
