不合時宜

bù hé shí yí

In English

  • anachronism anachronistic

Meaning

Out of step with the times.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • anachronism
  • anachronistic
  • out of step with the times

Example

在這條高速公路上，馬車顯得不合時宜。

A horse and carriage is an anachronism on this highway.

Usage

For ideas, systems, behavior and objects.

Similar

  • 🦕食古不化shí gǔ bù huà

    dinosaur — A dinosaur.

    The manager is a dinosaur who refuses to use email.

  • 🎏不識時務bù shí shí wù

    (not) know/see which way the wind blows — Not knowing which way the wind blows.

    He didn't know which way the wind blew and argued with the boss.

Opposite

  • ⏩與時俱進yǔ shí jù jìn

    keep evolving — Keeping up with the times.

    A good company keeps evolving with the times.

  • 🆕最新流行zuì xīn liú xíng

    the latest trend — The latest trend.

    Check out the latest trend in sneakers.
