時間小偷

shí jiān xiǎo tōu

In English

  • time vampire

Meaning

A time vampire.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • time sink
  • time waster

Example

我們那位愛聊天的同事是個時間小偷。

Our chatty colleague is a time vampire.

Usage

Colloquial; for work and everyday efficiency.

Similar

  • 🔁沒完沒了méi wán méi liǎo

    incessant; never-ending — Incessant.

    The incessant chatter on the train gave me a headache.

Opposite

  • ⏳分秒必爭fēn miǎo bì zhēng

    not a moment to be lost; time-sensitive — Not a moment to lose.

    In the emergency room, there is not a moment to be lost.
