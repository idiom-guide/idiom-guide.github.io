神智不清

shén zhì bù qīng

In English

  • not in sb's right mind

Meaning

Not in one's right mind.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • disoriented
  • delirious

Example

事故之後，他神智不清。

He was not in his right mind after the accident.

Usage

For the effects of injury, illness or drink.

Similar

  • 🤯神經錯亂shén jīng cuò luàn

    unhinged — Unhinged.

    His sudden shouting made some people think he had become unhinged.

  • 👻魂不守舍hún bù shǒu shè

    sb's not functioning — In a daze.

    After the breakup, he's not functioning and just stares at the wall.

Opposite

  • 💎思考清晰sī kǎo qīng xī

    clear insight — Clear thinking.

    Her answer showed clear insight into the problem.
