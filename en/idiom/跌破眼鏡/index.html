跌破眼鏡

diē pò yǎn jìng

In English

  • incredulous

Meaning

Nobody saw that coming.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • nobody saw that coming
  • defy all expectations
  • be astonished

Example

那支弱隊奪冠，讓我跌破眼鏡。

I was incredulous when the underdog won the championship.

Usage

Often 跌破大家的眼鏡; for surprising results in competitions or elections.

Similar

  • 🎉出乎意料chū hū yì liào

    take sb by surprise — Unexpected.

    The sudden announcement took everyone by surprise.

  • 🎁出人意表chū rén yì biǎo

    you will never know — Surprising and ingenious.

    With his surprising ideas, you never know what he will do next.

Opposite

  • 📓意料中事yì liào zhōng shì

    be in the playbook — Just as expected.

    Blaming the previous manager is straight out of the playbook, so nobody was surprised.

  • 🔮不出所料bù chū suǒ liào

    predictably — As predicted.

    Predictably, the train was late again on the first day of school.
