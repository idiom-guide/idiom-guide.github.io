金科玉律

jīn kē yù lǜ

In English

  • golden rules

Meaning

A golden rule that must not be broken.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • golden rule
  • a cardinal rule

Example

「顧客至上」是我們店裡的金科玉律。

"The customer comes first" is the golden rule in our shop.

Usage

Originally precious legal texts; now creeds in a trade or in life.

Similar

  • 📿至理名言zhì lǐ míng yán

    a wise saying; words of wisdom — A wise saying; about words, not rules.

    He keeps his father's words of wisdom firmly in mind.

  • 🥇首要規則shǒu yào guī zé

    cardinal rule — The number-one rule; 金科玉律 stresses that it's inviolable.

    The cardinal rule of interpreting is to stay faithful to the speaker's meaning.

Opposite

  • 📃形同虛設xíng tóng xū shè

    be a dead letter; exist in name only — A rule that exists only on paper.

    The rule is a dead letter.

  • 📦打破常規dǎ pò cháng guī

    think outside the box — Breaking with convention.

    To solve this problem, we need to think outside the box.
