任重道遠

rèn zhòng dào yuǎn

In English

  • the road ahead is long and the responsibility heavy

Meaning

A long road and a heavy load.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • a long road ahead
  • a heavy burden to bear

Example

前方的路還很長，我們任重道遠。

The road ahead is long and the responsibility heavy.

Usage

From the Analects.
