性情中人

xìng qíng zhōng rén

In English

  • passionate soul
  • wear one's heart on one's sleeve

Meaning

A person who is sincere and straightforward, strong in feeling, and who hides neither joy nor sorrow.Positive · General

Example

他是個性情中人，喜怒形於色，朋友有難絕不袖手旁觀。

He is a passionate soul who shows his feelings openly and never stands by when a friend is in trouble.

Usage

Used of a person, as in "he is a 性情中人". Mostly praise for someone sincere, spontaneous and loyal, with nothing put on. It can also hint at strong mood swings or a tendency to act on feeling, so context decides. Compared with 真情流露 (a moment of feeling) and 心直口快 (blunt speech), this idiom describes a person's whole character.

Similar

  • ⚡心直口快xīn zhí kǒu kuài

    straight shooter — Saying whatever is on one's mind without beating about the bush; about speech, whereas this idiom is about a person's whole temperament and feelings.

    Her quick tongue sometimes gets her into trouble, but at least she is a straight shooter.

  • 💗真情流露zhēn qíng liú lù

    the revelation of true inner feelings — True feelings showing naturally; about a moment of emotion, whereas this idiom describes a person's character.

    His speech was a revelation of his true inner feelings.

  • 🧡重情重義zhòng qíng zhòng yì

    loyal and warm-hearted; value loyalty and affection — Valuing affection and loyalty; about how one treats others, whereas this idiom is about being genuine and unguarded.

    Loyal and warm-hearted, he is always the first to help when a friend is down.

Opposite

  • 🎭虛情假意xū qíng jiǎ yì

    insincere; hollow sentiments — Putting on feelings one does not truly have; the opposite of open sincerity.

    Her feigned friendliness fooled no one.

  • 🎀矯揉造作jiǎo róu zào zuò

    mannerism; cutesy — Deliberately artificial in manner; the opposite of being natural and unaffected.

    Her fulsome compliments sounded insincere.
