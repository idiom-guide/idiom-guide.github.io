緣木求魚

yuán mù qiú yú

In English

  • climb a tree to catch a fish
  • fruitless approach

Meaning

A fruitless approach.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • barking up the wrong tree

Example

想從他那裡得到誠實的答案，就像緣木求魚。

Looking for honest answers from him is like climbing a tree to catch a fish.

Usage

From Mencius, King Hui of Liang.

Similar

  • 💨徒勞無功tú láo wú gōng

    a fool's errand; fruitless — A fool's errand.

    Searching for her keys felt like a fool's errand.

  • ⛔行不通的xíng bù tōng de

    impracticable — Unworkable.

    Your plan is impracticable.

Opposite

  • 💊對症下藥duì zhèng xià yào

    a targeted remedy; address the root cause — A targeted remedy.

    Without reliable data, the government cannot offer a targeted remedy.

  • ✨事半功倍shì bàn gōng bèi

    great result with less effort — Great results with less effort.

    A good tool can get great results with less effort.
