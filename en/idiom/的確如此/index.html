的確如此

dí què rú cǐ

In English

  • to all intents and purposes

Meaning

That's right; that's exactly how it is.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • indeed
  • that is indeed the case
  • exactly

Example

的確如此，這個專案實質上已經完成，只剩潤飾細節。

他說這家店很好吃，的確如此。

To all intents and purposes, the project is finished; we are just polishing the details.

Usage

Used to agree with what someone has just said.

Similar

  • ❕千真萬確qiān zhēn wàn què

    be absolutely true — Absolutely true; stronger.

    What he says is absolutely true; I saw it with my own eyes.

  • 💯毫無疑問háo wú yí wèn

    indisputably — Without a doubt; about certainty.

    She is indisputably the best player on the team.

Opposite

  • ❎並非如此bìng fēi rú cǐ

    that is not the case — That's not the case.

    Everyone thinks he is rich, but that is not the case.

  • 🙃恰恰相反qià qià xiāng fǎn

    quite the opposite — Quite the opposite; emphasizes contrast.

    He isn't lazy; quite the opposite, he works harder than anyone.
