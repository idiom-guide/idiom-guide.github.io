半信半疑

bàn xìn bàn yí

In English

  • dubious
  • take sth. with a grain of salt

Meaning

Half-convinced; not sure whether to believe it.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • be half-convinced
  • take sth with a grain of salt
  • have one's doubts

Example

他的承諾，我聽了半信半疑。

I took his promise with a grain of salt.

Usage

Often after 聽了 or 看了; means one can't tell if it's true.

Similar

  • 🧂謹慎看待jǐn shèn kàn dài

    take with a grain of salt — Taking it with a grain of salt.

    Take the rumor with a grain of salt until the company confirms it.

  • 🔅疑點重重yí diǎn chóng chóng

    be full of doubts; be riddled with questions — Full of suspicious points; about the matter, not one's attitude.

    The case is full of doubts and must be re-investigated.

Opposite

  • 🛡️深信不疑shēn xìn bù yí

    an unshakable faith — Absolutely convinced.

    She has an unshakable faith in her students.

  • ☑️信以為真xìn yǐ wéi zhēn

    take sth at face value; believe it to be true — Taking something false as true.

    She took the rumor at face value.
