10 · Art & Performance

渾然天成

hún rán tiān chéng

In English

  • seamless
  • naturally perfect

Meaning

Seamless.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • effortless

Example

這座庭園渾然天成，彷彿出自大自然之手。

The garden looks seamless, as if nature made it.

Usage

Formal; for artworks, writing and landscapes.

Similar

  • 🧵天衣無縫tiān yī wú fèng

    flawless; seamless — Flawless.

    The magician's trick was flawless.

  • ☁️行雲流水xíng yún liú shuǐ

    fluid and effortless — Fluid and effortless.

    Her calligraphy is fluid and effortless.

Opposite

  • 🎀矯揉造作jiǎo róu zào zuò

    mannerism; cutesy — Affected.

    Her fulsome compliments sounded insincere.
