針鋒相對

zhēn fēng xiāng duì

In English

  • be diametrically opposed
  • go head to head

Meaning

Diametrically opposed; going head to head.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • diametrically opposed
  • at loggerheads

Example

兩位候選人在稅制上針鋒相對。

The two candidates were diametrically opposed on taxes.

Usage

For debates, negotiations and politics.

Similar

  • 🥊正面交鋒zhèng miàn jiāo fēng

    go toe-to-toe — Going toe-to-toe.

    The two candidates went toe-to-toe in the debate.

  • ⚔️唇槍舌戰chún qiāng shé zhàn

    a war of words; verbal sparring — A war of words.

    The debate turned into a war of words.

Opposite

  • ☮️和平共處hé píng gòng chǔ

    peaceful coexistence — Peaceful coexistence.

    Both sides reaffirmed their commitment to peaceful coexistence.

  • 🤝化解歧見huà jiě qí jiàn

    settle disagreements — Settling disagreements.

    The mediator helped them settle their disagreements.
