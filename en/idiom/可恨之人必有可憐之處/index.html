可恨之人，必有可憐之處

kě hèn zhī rén, bì yǒu kě lián zhī chù

In English

  • cruelty springs from weakness

Meaning

Behind every detestable person lies something to pity.Neutral · Formal

Example

可恨之人，必有可憐之處：愛傷人的人，往往自己也在受傷。

Cruelty springs from weakness: people who lash out are often hurting inside.

Usage

A common saying urging empathy.

Similar

  • 🐡色厲內荏sè lì nèi rěn

    be fierce in appearance but weak within — Fierce outside, timid inside; bluster.

    He looks fierce but is really weak inside.

  • 🐶虛張聲勢xū zhāng shēng shì

    bluff; more bark than bite — Pretending to be strong to scare others.

    His threats were just a bluff; he is more bark than bite.

Opposite

  • 👼菩薩心腸pú sà xīn cháng

    have a heart of gold; be kind-hearted — Kind-hearted and compassionate.

    She has a heart of gold and can't bear to see others suffer.

  • 🔪心狠手辣xīn hěn shǒu là

    ruthless; cold-blooded — Cruel and ruthless.

    The villain is ruthless and cold-blooded.
