2 · Change & Fate

脫胎換骨

tuō tāi huàn gǔ

In English

  • totally changed
  • transformed significantly

Meaning

Transformed inside and out.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • transformed completely

Example

在國外三年，讓她脫胎換骨。

Three years abroad transformed her completely.

Usage

Originally a Daoist term for becoming an immortal; now of people or organizations changing completely.

Similar

  • 🛀洗心革面xǐ xīn gé miàn

    turn over a new leaf; reform oneself — Turning over a new leaf; about conduct.

    After his release he turned over a new leaf and started afresh.

  • 🌋翻天覆地fān tiān fù dì

    a sea change in sth; tectonic shift(s) — A sea change; usually of surroundings or eras.

    The internet brought a sea change in how we shop.

Opposite

  • 🐂牛牽到北京還是牛niú qiān dào běi jīng hái shì niú

    A leopard cannot change its spots — A leopard can't change its spots.

    He promised to change, but a leopard cannot change its spots.

  • 🗿一成不變yī chéng bù biàn

    unchanging; set in stone — Set in stone.

    The curriculum is not set in stone.
