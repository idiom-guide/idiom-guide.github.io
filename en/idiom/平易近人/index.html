平易近人

píng yì jìn rén

In English

  • approachable

Meaning

Approachable.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • down-to-earth
  • easygoing

Example

教授平易近人，學生有任何問題都會去找他。

The professor is so approachable that students visit him with every question.

Usage

Often of teachers, bosses and public figures.

Similar

  • 🤝通情達理tōng qíng dá lǐ

    reasonable; as a matter of course — Reasonable and understanding.

    He is a reasonable man who listens to both sides.

  • 🎩彬彬有禮bīn bīn yǒu lǐ

    courteous; well-mannered — Courteous, though not necessarily warm.

    The waiter was courteous throughout.

Opposite

  • 🚜盛氣凌人shèng qì líng rén

    steamroller — Overbearing.

    The new manager steamrolled over every objection.

  • 😐不苟言笑bù gǒu yán xiào

    dour — Dour.

    The dour accountant rarely smiled.
