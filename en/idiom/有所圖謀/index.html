有所圖謀

yǒu suǒ tú móu

In English

  • see/have an angle

Meaning

Having an angle.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • be up to something
  • have designs on

Example

他太殷勤了，我覺得他有所圖謀。

He's too nice. I think he has an angle.

Usage

For suspecting someone's kindness.

Similar

  • 🕵️別有用心bié yǒu yòng xīn

    ulterior motives — Hidden agendas.

    I suspect he has ulterior motives for being so nice.

  • 🕳️居心叵測jū xīn pǒ cè

    have an ulterior motive — An ulterior motive.

    I suspect he has an ulterior motive.

Opposite

  • 🎁不求回報bù qiú huí bào

    no strings attached — Expecting nothing in return.

    She helped the neighbors with no strings attached.

  • 💝好心好意hǎo xīn hǎo yì

    well-meaning; well-intentioned — Well-meaning.

    She was well-meaning but clumsy.
