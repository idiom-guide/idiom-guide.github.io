人無完人

rén wú wán rén

In English

  • perfect people don't exist

Meaning

Nobody's perfect.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • nobody is perfect
  • no one is without flaws
  • to err is human

Example

人無完人，原諒他的小錯吧。

Nobody is perfect, so forgive his small mistakes.

Usage

Used to urge tolerance of others or of oneself; often paired with 金無足赤.

Similar

  • 🟡金無足赤jīn wú zú chì

    there is no pure gold; nothing is perfect — No gold is pure; often paired with this saying.

    Nobody is perfect and nothing is flawless, so don't demand perfection.

  • 🔷瑕瑜互見xiá yú hù jiàn

    have both flaws and merits — Strengths and flaws together; an assessment, not advice.

    The novel has both strengths and weaknesses: some brilliant passages and some tedious ones.

Opposite

  • 🔟十全十美shí quán shí měi

    be perfect in every respect; be flawless — Perfect in every way; often in negatives.

    There is no perfect plan; we can only do our best.

  • 🌕盡善盡美jìn shàn jìn měi

    be perfect; leave nothing to be desired — Perfection; the ideal 人無完人 says is unreachable.

    The performance was flawless.
