避之惟恐不及

bì zhī wéi kǒng bù jí

In English

  • avoid...like the plague

Meaning

Avoiding like the plague.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • steer well clear of
  • give a wide berth

Example

吵架之後，他對她避之惟恐不及。

After the argument, he avoided her like the plague.

Usage

For people, places and topics one wants nothing to do with.

Similar

  • ☣️少碰為妙shǎo pèng wéi miào

    be worth avoiding — Best avoided.

    That street-food stall is worth avoiding.

  • 🥶不理不睬bù lǐ bù cǎi

    give sb the cold shoulder; ignore — The cold shoulder.

    She gave him the cold shoulder.

Opposite

  • 🦆趨之若鶩qū zhī ruò wù

    flock to; rush after — Flocking to it.

    People flocked to the new shop when it opened.

  • 🤩求之不得qiú zhī bù dé

    couldn't ask for more; more than welcome — Couldn't ask for more.

    A free ticket to the concert? More than welcome!
