受益良多

shòu yì liáng duō

In English

  • learned a lot

Meaning

Benefiting greatly.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • benefit greatly
  • get a lot out of

Example

這門課讓我受益良多。

I learned a lot from the course.

Usage

For courses, books, conversations and experiences.

Similar

  • 💥醍醐灌頂tí hú guàn dǐng

    have a sudden enlightenment; be struck by sudden insight — A mind-blowing revelation.

    His advice struck me like a sudden enlightenment.

  • 📌謹記在心jǐn jì zài xīn

    be taken to heart — Taken to heart.

    Her advice will be taken to heart.

Opposite

  • 💨徒勞無功tú láo wú gōng

    a fool's errand; fruitless — A fool's errand.

    Searching for her keys felt like a fool's errand.
