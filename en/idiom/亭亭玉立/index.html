亭亭玉立

tíng tíng yù lì

In English

  • slender and poised
  • graceful and tall

Meaning

Slim, tall and lovely, standing like a jade pillar; also said of lotus flowers standing tall.Positive · Formal

Example

女兒長成了亭亭玉立的少女。

Her daughter has grown into a slender, poised young woman.

Usage

For girls and young women, and also for tall, upright flowers such as lotus.

Similar

  • 💃婀娜多姿ē nuó duō zī

    graceful; willowy — Graceful; about soft, flowing movement.

    The dancer was graceful and willowy on stage.

  • 🥺楚楚動人chǔ chǔ dòng rén

    winsome; endearingly delicate — Delicate and endearing; about presence.

    In her pale blue dress, she looked delicate and winsome.

Opposite

  • 🍃弱不禁風ruò bù jīn fēng

    frail; delicate — So frail that a gust could topple one; about weakness.

    She looked frail in the cold.

  • 🐫彎腰駝背wān yāo tuó bèi

    hunched; stooped — Hunched over.

    After years at a desk, he gradually became hunched.
