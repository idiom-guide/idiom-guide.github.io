衣衫襤褸

yī shān lán lǚ

In English

  • in rags
  • ragged

Meaning

Clothes tattered and torn: a picture of poverty or hard times.Negative · General

Example

一個衣衫襤褸的男孩站在門口乞討。

A ragged boy stood at the door, begging for food.

Usage

For poverty or a fallen state; a common image in narration.

Similar

  • 🥀蓬頭垢面péng tóu gòu miàn

    unkempt and grimy — Messy hair and a dirty face; about untidiness.

    After days of working through the night, he looked unkempt and grimy.

  • 🧹灰頭土臉huī tóu tǔ liǎn

    covered in grime; bedraggled — Covered in dust; about looking bedraggled.

    The workers came off the site covered in grime.

Opposite

  • 👔衣冠楚楚yī guān chǔ chǔ

    dapper; impeccably dressed — Neatly and respectably dressed.

    He turned up at the interview looking dapper in a suit and tie.

  • 💅光鮮亮麗guāng xiān liàng lì

    glamorous; dressed to the nines — Dressed brightly and attractively.

    She is always glamorously dressed.
