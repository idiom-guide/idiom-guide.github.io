無縫接軌

wú fèng jiē guǐ

In English

  • a seamless transition

Meaning

A seamless transition.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • dovetail
  • seamlessly

Example

兩個團隊成功無縫接軌。

The two teams managed a seamless transition.

Usage

For handovers, system changes and linking up schemes.

Similar

  • 🧵天衣無縫tiān yī wú fèng

    flawless; seamless — Seamless, with no flaw.

    The magician's trick was flawless.

  • 🌉承先啟後chéng xiān qǐ hòu

    bridge the past and the future — Bridging past and future.

    The exhibition bridges the past and the future of the craft.

Opposite

  • 🐟格格不入gé gé bù rù

    be out of place; be like a fish out of water — Out of place.

    He felt like a fish out of water among his new colleagues.

  • 🚧遭遇阻力zāo yù zǔ lì

    hit a (major) speedbump — Meeting resistance.

    The project hit a major speedbump when the funding fell through.
