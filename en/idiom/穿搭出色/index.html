穿搭出色

chuān dā chū sè

In English

  • sth looks exquisite on sb

Meaning

Well dressed.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • well dressed
  • dressed to impress

Example

他穿那套深藍色西裝，穿搭出色。

That dark-blue suit looks exquisite on him.

Usage

Colloquial; for outfits and personal style.

Similar

  • 👗盛裝打扮shèng zhuāng dǎ bàn

    be dressed to the nines — Dressed up for an occasion.

    Everyone was dressed to the nines for the gala.

  • ✨光彩照人guāng cǎi zhào rén

    radiant; dazzling — Radiant.

    At the gala she looked radiant; her beauty had an incandescent quality.

Opposite

  • 🧔不修邊幅bù xiū biān fú

    scruffy, slovenly, disheveled, unkempt, slob — Scruffy.

    He turned up scruffy and unshaven for the interview.
