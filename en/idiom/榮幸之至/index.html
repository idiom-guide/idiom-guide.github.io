榮幸之至

róng xìng zhī zhì

In English

  • feel more than blessed

Meaning

Truly honored.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • I'm truly honored
  • it's a privilege

Example

今晚能在這裡，我榮幸之至。

I feel more than blessed to be here tonight.

Usage

A polite phrase in speeches or when invited.

Similar

  • 🎖️至高榮幸zhì gāo róng xìng

    it is my great honor; I am deeply honored — It is my great honor.

    It is my great honor to speak here today.

  • 🎖️備感榮幸bèi gǎn róng xìng

    deeply honored — Deeply honored.

    I am deeply honored to speak here today.

Opposite

  • 🥚丟盡顏面diū jìn yán miàn

    have egg on sb's face — Egg on one's face.

    After the failed demo, the CEO had egg on his face.
