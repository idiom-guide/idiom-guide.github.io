異口同聲

yì kǒu tóng shēng

In English

  • with one voice
  • in unison

Meaning

With one voice; in unison.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • with one accord

Example

同學們異口同聲地回答：「好！」

The students answered in unison.

Usage

Many people saying the same thing at once; can also mean complete agreement.

Similar

  • 🤝不謀而合bù móu ér hé

    happen to agree; see eye to eye without consulting — Arriving at the same idea without discussing it.

    We never compared notes, yet we came up with the same idea.

  • 🐧眾口一詞zhòng kǒu yī cí

    speak with one voice; tell the same story — All telling the same story; may suggest it was rehearsed.

    The witnesses all told the same story, saying they saw him run east.

Opposite

  • 💬眾說紛紜zhòng shuō fēn yún

    opinions vary widely — Opinions vary widely.

    Opinions vary widely on what caused the blackout.

  • 🤷‍♂️莫衷一是mò zhōng yī shì

    be unable to agree on any one view — No agreement on which is right.

    Experts disagree, and the public cannot tell which view to believe.
