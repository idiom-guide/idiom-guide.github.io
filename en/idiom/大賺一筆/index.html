大賺一筆

dà zhuàn yī bǐ

In English

  • made a killing on sth

Meaning

Making a killing.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • make a killing
  • rake it in
  • strike it rich

Example

他在股市大賺一筆。

He made a killing on the stock market.

Usage

Colloquial; for investments, business and flipping.

Similar

  • 💵日進斗金rì jìn dǒu jīn

    rake in money — Raking in money.

    The app was raking in money within months.

  • 🧧大發利市dà fā lì shì

    earn a lot; make a fortune — Doing a roaring trade.

    The vendor made a fortune during the festival.

Opposite

  • 🕳️血本無歸xuè běn wú guī

    lose one's entire investment — Losing one's entire investment.

    Many retail investors lost their entire investment.
