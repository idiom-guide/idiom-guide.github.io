4 · Pain & Unease

萎靡不振

wěi mǐ bù zhèn

In English

  • listless
  • in low spirits

Meaning

In low spirits.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • down in the dumps

Example

戰敗後球隊萎靡不振。

The team was in low spirits after the defeat.

Usage

For people, teams and markets.

Similar

  • 😞士氣低落shì qì dī luò

    demoralized -ing — Demoralized.

    The constant criticism was demoralizing the team.

  • 🥱無精打采wú jīng dǎ cǎi

    listless; lethargic — Listless.

    He looked listless after the long trip.

Opposite

  • 🌬️意氣風發yì qì fēng fā

    be in high spirits; full of vigor — In high spirits.

    The graduates were in high spirits.

  • 🔔精神抖擻jīng shén dǒu sǒu

    full of energy; bright-eyed and bushy-tailed — Raring to go.

    He turned up to work with bells on.
