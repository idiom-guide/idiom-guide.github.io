量入為出

liàng rù wéi chū

In English

  • live within one's means

Meaning

Living within one's means.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • cut one's coat according to one's cloth
  • balance the books

Example

新創公司必須量入為出。

A startup has to live within its means.

Usage

Formal; from the Book of Rites; for personal finance and budgets.

Similar

  • 🧮精打細算jīng dǎ xì suàn

    astute with money; budget carefully — Astute with money.

    She is astute with money and plans every purchase.

  • 🚰開源節流kāi yuán jié liú

    boost revenue and cut costs — Boosting revenue and cutting costs.

    The CFO's plan is simple: boost revenue and cut costs.

Opposite

  • 💸入不敷出rù bù fū chū

    run a deficit; spend more than one earns — Running a deficit.

    The hospital has been running a deficit for years.

  • 🗓️寅吃卯糧yín chī mǎo liáng

    borrow against the future — Borrowing against the future.

    Funding today's pensions with new debt is borrowing against the future.
