四季分明

sì jì fēn míng

In English

  • xxx has seasons

Meaning

Four distinct seasons.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • have four distinct seasons
  • seasonal

Example

這個地區四季分明。

The region has four distinct seasons.

Usage

For describing climate.
