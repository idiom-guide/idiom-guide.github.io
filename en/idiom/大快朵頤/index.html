大快朵頤

dà kuài duǒ yí

In English

  • feast on
  • dig in
  • tuck in

Meaning

Eating with great gusto, as if working one's cheeks with joy.Neutral · General

Example

爬完山，我們圍著一大鍋火鍋大快朵頤。

After the hike, we feasted on a steaming hot pot.

Usage

For the pleasure of a good meal. Light in tone and common in food reports.

Similar

  • 🐺狼吞虎嚥láng tūn hǔ yàn

    wolf down — Eating fast and fiercely; about manners, not necessarily enjoyment.

    The hungry kids wolfed down their dinner.

  • 😋津津有味jīn jīn yǒu wèi

    with great interest; with relish — Eating with obvious enjoyment; about the look of relish.

    The students listened to the story with great interest.

Opposite

  • 😣食不下嚥shí bù xià yàn

    cannot eat a bite; lose one's appetite — Unable to eat, usually because of mood or the food.

    After hearing the bad news, he could not eat a bite.

  • 🤢食慾不振shí yù bù zhèn

    off one's food; have no appetite — Having no appetite.

    He has been off his food lately and can hardly eat anything.
