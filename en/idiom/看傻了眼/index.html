看傻了眼

kàn shǎ le yǎn

In English

  • be petrified

Meaning

Stunned.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • stunned
  • gobsmacked

Example

突如其來的爆炸讓觀眾看傻了眼。

The audience was petrified by the sudden explosion.

Usage

Colloquial; from shock or from amazement.

Similar

  • 👀面面相覷miàn miàn xiāng qù

    look at each other in dismay — Looking at each other in dismay.

    The team looked at each other in dismay when the boss resigned.

  • 😱非常驚駭fēi cháng jīng hài

    shit a brick — Jumping out of one's skin.

    He nearly jumped out of his skin when the alarm went off.

Opposite

  • 😐面不改色miàn bù gǎi sè

    not (even) bat an eyelid — Not batting an eyelid.

    He didn't bat an eyelid when he heard the price.

  • 🥱司空見慣sī kōng jiàn guàn

    a common sight; commonplace — A common sight.

    Delays are commonplace on this line.
