2 · Change & Fate

銷聲匿跡

xiāo shēng nì jì

In English

  • off the grid
  • nowhere to be found

Meaning

Vanishing without a trace.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • vanish without a trace

Example

醜聞爆發後，這名演員銷聲匿跡。

After the scandal, the actor went off the grid.

Usage

For people, products and trends that disappear from public view.

Similar

  • 💨煙消雲散yān xiāo yún sàn

    vaporized, vanished, be gone — Vanished into thin air; usually of feelings or things.

    His worries vanished the moment he heard the news.

  • 🚩偃旗息鼓yǎn qí xī gǔ

    call a halt; lie low — Calling a halt; lying low.

    The band announced they would call a halt for now: a pause, they said, not a farewell.

Opposite

  • 💪強勢回歸qiáng shì huí guī

    make a strong comeback — A strong comeback.

    The veteran star made a strong comeback this season.

  • ☀️重見天日chóng jiàn tiān rì

    have sb's day in the sun — Back in the light.

    After ten years in a drawer, the manuscript finally had its day in the sun.
