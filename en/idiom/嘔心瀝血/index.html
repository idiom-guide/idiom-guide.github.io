嘔心瀝血

ǒu xīn lì xuè

In English

  • work one's heart out

Meaning

Pouring one's heart and soul into something.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • pour one's heart and soul into
  • blood, sweat and tears

Example

作者嘔心瀝血，完成了這部辭典。

The author worked his heart out to finish the dictionary.

Usage

For creative work, research and major projects.

Similar

  • 💻廢寢忘食fèi qǐn wàng shí

    work day and night; forget to eat and sleep — Forgetting to eat and sleep.

    He worked day and night on the proposal, barely stopping to eat or sleep.

  • 😓勞心勞力láo xīn láo lì

    exhaust oneself; brain and brawn — Brain and brawn.

    Running the shop alone exhausted him, in both brain and brawn.

Opposite

  • 😪漫不經心màn bù jīng xīn

    be offhand; do sth carelessly — Offhand.

    He answered offhandedly, as if he couldn't care less.
