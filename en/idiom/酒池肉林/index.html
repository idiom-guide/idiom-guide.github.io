酒池肉林

jiǔ chí ròu lín

In English

  • bacchanal
  • decadent revelry

Meaning

Wine enough for a pool and meat enough for a forest: shameless luxury and indulgence.Negative · Formal

Example

這場派對酒池肉林，極盡奢華。

The tycoon's party was a bacchanal of wine and roast meat.

Usage

Disapproving: criticizes extravagance and excess. The image goes back to the tyrant of the Shang dynasty.

Similar

  • 🍾紙醉金迷zhǐ zuì jīn mí

    a life of decadence; extravagant and dissolute living — Lost in a luxurious, hedonistic life; broader than eating and drinking.

    The film portrays a life of decadence.

  • 🏮燈紅酒綠dēng hóng jiǔ lǜ

    feasting and pleasure seeking — Glittering nightlife; leans toward the nighttime scene.

    The district is a place of feasting and pleasure seeking.

Opposite

  • 🥣粗茶淡飯cū chá dàn fàn

    plain fare; simple meals — Plain, simple meals; leans toward a frugal life.

    He lived on plain fare all his life and was perfectly content.

  • 🥖縮衣節食suō yī jié shí

    ppl living on the margins; austerity — Skimping on clothes and food to get by.

    Austerity has left many people living on the margins.
