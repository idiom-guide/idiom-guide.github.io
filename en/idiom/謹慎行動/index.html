謹慎行動

jǐn shèn xíng dòng

In English

  • tread carefully

Meaning

Treading carefully.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • act with caution
  • play it safe

Example

在陌生的市場，謹慎行動才划算。

In unfamiliar markets, it pays to tread carefully.

Usage

For investment, diplomacy and the workplace.

Similar

  • 🥚小心翼翼xiǎo xīn yì yì

    tread carefully; be extremely careful — Extremely careful.

    He chose his words very carefully to avoid trouble.

  • 🧊如履薄冰rú lǚ bó bīng

    walk on thin ice; tread carefully — Walking on thin ice.

    After the scandal, the manager had to walk on thin ice.

Opposite

  • 🧗鋌而走險tǐng ér zǒu xiǎn

    put one's neck on the line; take a desperate gamble — Taking a desperate gamble.

    Desperate for cash, he took a desperate gamble.

  • 💥意氣用事yì qì yòng shì

    emotional (player) — Acting on emotion.

    He's an emotional player who makes rash choices when angry.
