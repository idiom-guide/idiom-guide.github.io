感觸良多

gǎn chù liáng duō

In English

  • mixed feelings/reactions

Meaning

Having many thoughts and feelings stirred.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • have a lot of thoughts and feelings
  • be deeply moved
  • reflect with emotion
  • have mixed feelings

Example

重回母校，讓我感觸良多。

Visiting my old school left me with mixed feelings.

Usage

感觸 means feelings prompted by something; not necessarily sad, can be gratifying. The listed mixed feelings suggests ambivalence, which differs slightly.

Similar

  • 💞心有戚戚焉xīn yǒu qī qī yān

    have the same feeling — Striking a chord.

    When she described her childhood, I had the same feeling, as if she were telling my story.

  • 🤗感同身受gǎn tóng shēn shòu

    relatable — Feeling someone's pain as one's own.

    Her story was so relatable that I felt every word as if it were my own.

Opposite

  • 😑漠不關心mò bù guān xīn

    uncaring — Indifferent.

    The landlord was uncaring about his tenants' complaints.

  • 📴事不關己shì bù guān jǐ

    none of one's business; not one's concern — Not one's business.

    It was none of his business, so he stood by and did nothing.
