派系衝突

pài xì chōng tū

In English

  • factionalism

Meaning

Factional infighting.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • factional infighting
  • turf wars

Example

派系衝突讓這個政黨陷入癱瘓。

Factionalism has paralyzed the party.

Usage

For political parties, companies and clubs.

Similar

  • 🤼政治角力zhèng zhì jué lì

    political wrangling — Political wrangling.

    The bill died amid months of political wrangling.

  • 🤺針鋒相對zhēn fēng xiāng duì

    be diametrically opposed; go head to head — Diametrically opposed.

    The two candidates were diametrically opposed on taxes.

Opposite

  • ✊團結是力量tuán jié shì lì liàng

    strength in numbers — Strength in numbers.

    There is strength in numbers, so let's march together.

  • ⛵同舟共濟tóng zhōu gòng jì

    solidarity; help each other — In the same boat, helping each other through.

    In times of crisis, neighbors help each other.
