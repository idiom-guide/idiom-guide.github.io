甜而不膩

tián ér bù nì

In English

  • not cloying
  • just sweet enough

Meaning

Sweet but never heavy: the sweetness is just right.Positive · Colloquial

Example

這家的芋頭甜點甜而不膩，我一口氣吃了兩碗。

The taro dessert here is just sweet enough, never cloying, and I finished two bowls.

Usage

For judging desserts and drinks. Very common in dessert and bubble-tea shop copy in Taiwan.

Similar

  • 🥓肥而不膩féi ér bù nì

    unctuous; rich but not greasy — Rich in fat yet not greasy; about richness.

    The Dongpo pork is rich but not greasy and melts in the mouth.

  • 🥗清淡爽口qīng dàn shuǎng kǒu

    refreshing; light — Light on the palate; about freshness.

    After the greasy barbecue, a plate of refreshing cold cucumber salad hit the spot.

Opposite

  • 🍬甜得發膩tián dé fā nì

    sickly sweet; cloying — Sickly sweet.

    The drink was sickly sweet, and I put it down after one sip.
