11 · Getting Along & Making Up

息事寧人

xī shì níng rén

In English

  • smooth things over

Meaning

Smoothing things over.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • keep the peace
  • pour oil on troubled waters

Example

經理道歉只是為了息事寧人。

The manager apologized simply to smooth things over.

Usage

Sometimes implies giving way just to keep the peace.

Similar

  • 🕊️以和為貴yǐ hé wéi guì

    value harmony above all — Valuing harmony above all.

    In family disputes, we should value harmony above all.

  • 🕊️調解爭端tiáo jiě zhēng duān

    pour oil on troubled waters — Pouring oil on troubled waters.

    The mayor poured oil on troubled waters by meeting both sides.

Opposite

  • 🌬️興風作浪xīng fēng zuò làng

    make waves; fuel divisions and sow chaos — Stirring up trouble.

    A few troublemakers made waves in the village.

  • 🔥搧風點火shān fēng diǎn huǒ

    fan the flames of — Fanning the flames.

    The tabloid fanned the flames of the dispute.
