12 · Frequency & Trends

一成不變

yī chéng bù biàn

In English

  • unchanging
  • set in stone

Meaning

Set in stone.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • static

Example

課程並非一成不變。

The curriculum is not set in stone.

Usage

For rules, systems and practices; often negated.

Similar

  • 🍪千篇一律qiān piān yī lǜ

    be formulaic; follow the same pattern — All the same.

    These ads are all formulaic and utterly unoriginal.

  • 🚋墨守成規mò shǒu chéng guī

    stick to old rules; stay in a rut — Sticking to the old rules.

    The company sticks to old rules and finds it hard to innovate.

Opposite

  • ♻️推陳出新tuī chén chū xīn

    breathe new life into tradition — Breathing new life into tradition.

    Young designers are breathing new life into tradition.

  • 🧩因地制宜yīn dì zhì yí

    adapt to local conditions — Adapting to local conditions.

    Policies should be adapted to local conditions.
