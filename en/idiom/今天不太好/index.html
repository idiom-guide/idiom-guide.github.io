今天不太好

jīn tiān bù tài hǎo

In English

  • had better days

Meaning

I've had better days.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • I've had better days
  • it's not my day
  • rough day

Example

我今天不太好。

I've had better days.

Usage

Colloquial; a gentle way to say one is not doing well.

Similar

  • ☔時運不濟shí yùn bù jì

    be down on one's luck; have bad timing — Down on one's luck.

    He started his business in a recession; it was simply bad timing.

  • 🌇風光不再fēng guāng bù zài

    sb has lost one's shine; have seen better days — Seen better days.

    The old star hotel has seen better days.

Opposite

  • 😆興高采烈xìng gāo cǎi liè

    gleeful; a gleeful smile/shout — Gleeful.

    The children ran out of school in gleeful anticipation of summer.

  • 😊滿心愉悅mǎn xīn yú yuè

    delight — Delight.

    She smiled with delight at the surprise.
