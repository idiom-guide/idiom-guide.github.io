紅顏禍水

hóng yán huò shuǐ

In English

  • femme fatale

Meaning

A femme fatale.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • a beauty who brings ruin
  • a dangerous woman

Example

在那部老電影裡，紅顏禍水為遇到她的每個男人帶來毀滅。

In the old film, the femme fatale brings ruin to every man she meets.

Usage

An old, sexist notion that beautiful women bring disaster; now mostly used critically or jokingly.

    The generous offer was a poisoned chalice, loaded with endless obligations.
