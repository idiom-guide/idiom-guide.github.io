三思而行

sān sī ér xíng

In English

  • look before you leap
  • think it through

Meaning

Look before you leap.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • think twice

Example

簽約前要三思而行，把事情想清楚。

Look before you leap: think it through before you sign.

Usage

From the Analects (季文子三思而後行); common in advice.

Similar

  • 🗺️謀定而後動móu dìng ér hòu dòng

    think before you act — Plan first, then act.

    Think before you act, and make a plan first.

  • 🗿深思熟慮shēn sī shú lǜ

    think it through carefully; give careful thought — Well thought out.

    Major decisions require careful thought, not haste.

Opposite

  • 💥意氣用事yì qì yòng shì

    emotional (player) — Acting on emotion.

    He's an emotional player who makes rash choices when angry.

  • ⚡不假思索bù jiǎ sī suǒ

    on the spur of the moment; on a whim — Without a second thought.

    On the spur of the moment, he jumped into the river to help.
