無福消受

wú fú xiāo shòu

In English

  • don't have the luxury of

Meaning

Not having the luxury.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • can't afford to enjoy
  • not for the likes of me

Example

我無福消受長假。

I don't have the luxury of a long vacation.

Usage

Colloquial; often self-deprecating or a polite refusal.

Similar

  • 🤹分身乏術fēn shēn fá shù

    spread too thin; can't be in two places at once — Spread too thin.

    With two jobs, I'm spread too thin.

Opposite

  • 🤩求之不得qiú zhī bù dé

    couldn't ask for more; more than welcome — Couldn't ask for more.

    A free ticket to the concert? More than welcome!
