關鍵時刻

guān jiàn shí kè

In English

  • moment of truth
  • defining/watershed moment
  • critical juncture

Meaning

The moment of truth; a critical moment.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • the moment of truth
  • a critical juncture
  • a defining moment

Example

輪到罰踢十二碼球時，關鍵時刻到了。

When the penalty kick came, it was the moment of truth.

Usage

Often 在關鍵時刻 (at the crucial moment) or 關鍵時刻掉鏈子 (choke when it matters).

Similar

  • 🌊背水一戰bèi shuǐ yī zhàn

    a last stand; do-or-die — A do-or-die battle with no way back.

    With their backs to the wall, the team played a do-or-die match.

  • 💀攸關生死yōu guān shēng sǐ

    a matter of life and death — A matter of life and death.

    For patients on the waiting list, this decision is a matter of life and death.

Opposite

  • 🤏無關緊要wú guān jǐn yào

    immaterial — Of no importance.

    Whether we meet at six or seven is immaterial.

  • 🥱平淡無奇píng dàn wú qí

    run-of-the-mill — Plain and unremarkable.

    It was just a run-of-the-mill day at the office.
