非常簡單

fēi cháng jiǎn dān

In English

  • easy breezy

Meaning

Easy-breezy.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • a piece of cake
  • as easy as pie

Example

這道食譜非常簡單。

The recipe is easy breezy.

Usage

Colloquial; for steps, methods and tasks.

Similar

  • 🖐️易如反掌yì rú fǎn zhǎng

    has never been so easy — As easy as turning over one's hand.

    With the new software, filing taxes has never been so easy.

  • 🧁輕而易舉qīng ér yì jǔ

    be a piece of cake — Effortless.

    For him, this is a piece of cake.

Opposite

  • 🧗難如登天nán rú dēng tiān

    insanely difficult — Next to impossible.

    Finding a taxi at rush hour in the rain is insanely difficult.

  • 🤕傷透腦筋shāng tòu nǎo jīn

    head-scratcher — A head-scratcher.

    This riddle is a real head-scratcher.
