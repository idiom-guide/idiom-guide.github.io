養尊處優

yǎng zūn chǔ yōu

In English

  • over privileged

Meaning

Living a life of privilege.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • live in the lap of luxury
  • born with a silver spoon

Example

那位養尊處優的繼承人從沒自己提過包。

The over-privileged heir had never carried his own bag.

Usage

Usually critical, implying ignorance of hardship.

Similar

  • 🍼嬌生慣養jiāo shēng guàn yǎng

    pampered; spoiled — Pampered and spoiled.

    The pampered prince had never washed a dish.

  • 🛍️紈絝子弟wán kù zǐ dì

    spoiled rich kid; playboy — A spoiled rich kid.

    The spoiled rich kid crashed his father's car.

Opposite

  • 🐂吃苦耐勞chī kǔ nài láo

    workhorse — Hardworking and uncomplaining.

    He is a workhorse who never complains.

  • 🏗️白手起家bái shǒu qǐ jiā

    a self-made man/woman; start from scratch — Self-made.

    She is a self-made woman who started from scratch.
