明察秋毫

míng chá qiū háo

In English

  • discerning

Meaning

Sharp-eyed enough to see the smallest detail.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • eagle-eyed
  • miss nothing

Example

這位偵探明察秋毫，連最細微的線索都能發現。

The detective was discerning enough to spot the tiniest clue.

Usage

Formal; 秋毫 is the fine new down of birds and beasts in autumn; from Mencius.

Similar

  • 🔍觀察入微guān chá rù wēi

    observant — Observant.

    She is an observant teacher who notices when a student is struggling.

  • 👁️獨具慧眼dú jù huì yǎn

    have unique insight into sth — A unique eye.

    The editor had unique insight into what readers wanted.

Opposite

  • 🙈視而不見shì ér bù jiàn

    effectively blind — Effectively blind.

    The government seemed effectively blind to the problem.

  • 🃏忙中疏漏máng zhōng shū lòu

    lost in the shuffle — Lost in the shuffle.

    In the rush, her request got lost in the shuffle.
