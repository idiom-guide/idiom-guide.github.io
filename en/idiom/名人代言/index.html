名人代言

míng rén dài yán

In English

  • celebrity branding
  • brand ambassador

Meaning

A celebrity endorsement.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • celebrity endorsement

Example

這個球鞋品牌簽下一位流行歌手當代言人，打出名人代言。

The sneaker brand signed a pop star as its brand ambassador.

Usage

For advertising and marketing.

Similar

  • 🎓專家背書zhuān jiā bèi shū

    has an academic seal of approval; endorsed by — An expert endorsement.

    The new product is endorsed by leading doctors.

  • 📢大力鼓吹dà lì gǔ chuī

    champion; advocate — Vigorously promoting.

    My mother is all for learning a second language.

Opposite

  • 🐁默默無聞mò mò wú wén

    be unknown; toil in obscurity — Unknown and unnoticed.

    He toiled in obscurity for years until that film brought him attention.

  • 🔈自說自話zì shuō zì huà

    keep asserting without proof; talk to oneself — Talking to oneself, with no one to back it up.

    He just keeps asserting things without producing any evidence.
