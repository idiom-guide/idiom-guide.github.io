加以證明

jiā yǐ zhèng míng

In English

  • make a (convincing
  • strong) case for sth
  • endorse

Meaning

To prove something with facts, data or reasoning.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • make a strong case for
  • prove
  • validate

Example

延後上課的主張，已有數據加以證明。

The data make a strong case for starting classes later.

Usage

加以 is a formal auxiliary followed by a two-character verb.

Similar

  • 🧪得到驗證dé dào yàn zhèng

    be borne out; be validated — Proven after testing; from the side of what gets proven.

    His prediction was borne out by the final results.

  • 📇有憑有據yǒu píng yǒu jù

    be well grounded; be backed by evidence — Backed by evidence.

    His accusation is backed by evidence; it's not empty talk.

Opposite

  • 👅空口無憑kōng kǒu wú píng

    words alone prove nothing — Words without proof don't count.

    Words alone prove nothing; please give me written proof.

  • 🐟無稽之談wú jī zhī tán

    sheer nonsense; baseless talk — Groundless talk.

    That claim is sheer nonsense.
