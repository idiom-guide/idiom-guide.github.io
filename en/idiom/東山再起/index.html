東山再起

dōng shān zài qǐ

In English

  • make a comeback

Meaning

Making a comeback.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • bounce back
  • rise again

Example

沉寂多年後，這位歌手東山再起。

After a long absence, the singer made a comeback.

Usage

From the story of Xie An of the Eastern Jin, who returned to office after retiring to Mount Dong.

Similar

  • 🌪️卷土重來juǎn tǔ chóng lái

    stage a comeback; resurgent — Back again, sometimes with a threatening edge.

    The resurgent party is trying to stage a comeback in the next election.

  • 💪強勢回歸qiáng shì huí guī

    make a strong comeback — A strong comeback.

    The veteran star made a strong comeback this season.

Opposite

  • 🤕一蹶不振yī jué bù zhèn

    never recover; be unable to get back on one's feet — Never recovering.

    He never recovered after the scandal.

  • 👻銷聲匿跡xiāo shēng nì jì

    off the grid; nowhere to be found — Vanishing without a trace.

    After the scandal, the actor went off the grid.
