1 · Knowing & Thinking

好好想想

hǎo hǎo xiǎng xiǎng

In English

  • let that sink in

Meaning

Think it over.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • think it over
  • give it some thought
  • think twice

Example

回答之前，先好好想想。

Just let that sink in for a moment before you answer.

Usage

Advice or a reminder; gentle or stern depending on the situation.

Similar

  • 🗿深思熟慮shēn sī shú lǜ

    think it through carefully; give careful thought — Well thought out.

    Major decisions require careful thought, not haste.

  • 🧩煞費思量shà fèi sī liang

    mind-bending; puzzling — Racking one's brain.

    The riddle was mind-bending, and it took me a week to solve it.

Opposite

  • ⚡不假思索bù jiǎ sī suǒ

    on the spur of the moment; on a whim — Without a second thought.

    On the spur of the moment, he jumped into the river to help.

  • 🤧脫口而出tuō kǒu ér chū

    blurt out; say sth without thinking — Blurting something out without thinking.

    Asked her age, she blurted out, "Thirty!"
