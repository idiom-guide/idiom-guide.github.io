4 · Incompetence & Failure

不受青睞

bù shòu qīng lài

In English

  • out of favor
  • unpopular

Meaning

Out of favor.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • fall flat

Example

新車款在買家之間不受青睞。

The new model was out of favor with buyers.

Usage

For products, proposals and candidates.

Similar

  • 🕸️乏人問津fá rén wèn jīn

    attract no buyers; go unnoticed — Attracting no takers.

    The old house has attracted no buyers.

  • 🥶冷眼看待lěng yǎn kàn dài

    faces a frosty reception — A frosty reception.

    The proposal faced a frosty reception from the board.

Opposite

  • 🔥很受歡迎hěn shòu huān yíng

    sought after — Very popular.

    Handmade soap is sought after at the weekend market.

  • ♨️炙手可熱zhì shǒu kě rè

    the sexiest (job) — Red-hot.

    Data science has been called the sexiest job of the century.
