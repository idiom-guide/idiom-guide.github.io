緊密關連

jǐn mì guān lián

In English

  • no daylight between us

Meaning

Closely connected; bound up with each other.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • be closely linked
  • be closely connected
  • be inextricably linked

Example

在這個議題上，兩位部長的立場緊密關連，看不出任何分歧。

環境與健康緊密關連。

On this issue, there is no daylight between the two ministers.

Usage

Also written 緊密關聯; used for cause-and-effect or dependence.

Similar

  • 🧬息息相關xī xī xiāng guān

    be closely related to; closely bound up — Closely linked; affecting each other.

    Our health is closely bound up with the environment around us.

  • 🖇密不可分mì bù kě fēn

    be inseparable — Inseparable.

    Language and culture are inseparable.

Opposite

  • 🔌互不相干hù bù xiāng gān

    be unrelated; have nothing to do with each other — Unrelated to each other.

    These two matters are unrelated; don't lump them together.

  • 🔇無關痛癢wú guān tòng yǎng

    be of no consequence; be beside the point — Of no concern; usually about attitudes or remarks.

    These inconsequential comments needn't be taken to heart.
