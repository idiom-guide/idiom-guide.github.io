舉止跋扈

jǔ zhǐ bá hù

In English

  • overbearing
  • domineering

Meaning

Overbearing.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • bossy

Example

老闆舉止跋扈，嚇壞了員工。

The owner's overbearing manner scared the staff.

Usage

For bosses, the powerful and parents.

Similar

  • 🚜盛氣凌人shèng qì líng rén

    steamroller — Steamrolling.

    The new manager steamrolled over every objection.

  • 👉頤指氣使yí zhǐ qì shǐ

    order people about; lord it over — Ordering people about.

    She lords it over her colleagues.

Opposite

  • 🤗平易近人píng yì jìn rén

    approachable — Approachable.

    The professor is so approachable that students visit him with every question.

  • 🎩彬彬有禮bīn bīn yǒu lǐ

    courteous; well-mannered — Courteous.

    The waiter was courteous throughout.
