塵埃落定

chén āi luò dìng

In English

  • waiting for the dust to settle

Meaning

Once the dust settles.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • when the dust settles
  • it's all settled

Example

等塵埃落定再做決定吧。

Let's wait for the dust to settle before we make any decision.

Usage

For elections, deals, disputes and reshuffles.

Similar

  • 🔨板上釘釘bǎn shàng dìng dīng

    a done deal; locked in — A done deal.

    The contract has been signed, so the deal is locked in.

  • ⛵木已成舟mù yǐ chéng zhōu

    ship has sailed — The ship has sailed.

    It's too late to complain; the ship has sailed.

Opposite

  • 🎈懸而未決xuán ér wèi jué

    sth be still up in the air; sth be up in the air — Still up in the air.

    The decision is still up in the air.

  • 🥴撲朔迷離pū shuò mí lí

    be baffling; be shrouded in mystery — Murky and confusing.

    The case is baffling, and the police cannot yet make sense of it.
