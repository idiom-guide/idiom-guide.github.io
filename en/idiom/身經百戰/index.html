身經百戰

shēn jīng bǎi zhàn

In English

  • battle-hardened

Meaning

Battle-hardened.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • a seasoned veteran
  • been there, done that

Example

身經百戰的中士在炮火下仍保持冷靜。

The battle-hardened sergeant stayed calm under fire.

Usage

For soldiers and, figuratively, veterans of any field.

Similar

  • 🥋訓練有素xùn liàn yǒu sù

    well trained — Well trained.

    The rescue dogs are well trained.

  • 🌶️薑是老的辣jiāng shì lǎo de là

    experience counts; there's no substitute for experience — Experience counts.

    Experience counts: the veteran solved it in minutes.

Opposite

  • 🐣職場菜鳥zhí chǎng cài niǎo

    be new to the workforce — New to the workforce.

    I'm new to the workforce, so please be patient with me.
