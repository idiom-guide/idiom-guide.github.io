聚精會神

jù jīng huì shén

In English

  • concentrate
  • stay focused
  • be laser focused on sth

Meaning

Concentrating hard; with rapt attention.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • be laser-focused
  • be fully focused
  • with rapt attention

Example

學生們整場考試都聚精會神。

The students stayed laser-focused throughout the exam.

Usage

For listening, watching, writing or thinking; complimentary.

Similar

  • 🧲全神貫注quán shén guàn zhù

    be fully absorbed; with rapt attention — Totally focused; giving everything to it.

    The children were fully absorbed in the story.

  • 👀密切關注mì qiè guān zhù

    keep tabs on sth/sb — Keeping a close eye on something.

    Investors are keeping tabs on the central bank's next move.

Opposite

  • 💭心不在焉xīn bù zài yān

    distracted absent minded — Absent-minded.

    He was distracted and absent-minded during the meeting.

  • 😪漫不經心màn bù jīng xīn

    be offhand; do sth carelessly — Offhand and careless.

    He answered offhandedly, as if he couldn't care less.
