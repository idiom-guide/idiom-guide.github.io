點石成金

diǎn shí chéng jīn

In English

  • Midas touch

Meaning

The Midas touch.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • turn everything to gold
  • work wonders

Example

這位製作人有點石成金的本事，每張專輯都賣破白金。

The producer has the Midas touch; every album he makes goes platinum.

Usage

For producers, editors, investors and coaches who make anything succeed.

Similar

  • 💓起死回生qǐ sǐ huí shēng

    bring back from the dead; a miraculous recovery — Bringing back from the dead.

    The new owner's plan brought the company back from the dead.

  • 🔨匠心獨具jiàng xīn dú jù

    show true craftsmanship — True craftsmanship.

    Every detail of the garden shows true craftsmanship.

Opposite

  • ↩️適得其反shì dé qí fǎn

    sth risks backfiring; backfire — Backfiring.

    His efforts to calm the crowd only backfired.

  • 🤦‍♂️弄巧成拙nòng qiǎo chéng zhuō

    outsmart oneself; be too clever by half — Being too clever by half.

    He tried to hide it but outsmarted himself.
