歌功頌德

gē gōng sòng dé

In English

  • sing the praises of sb/sth

Meaning

Singing someone's praises; now usually sycophantic flattery.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • heap praise on
  • sing sb's praises

Example

演講全在對市長歌功頌德。

The speeches did nothing but sing the praises of the mayor.

Usage

Once neutral, now often sarcastic about relentless praise.

Similar

  • 🍯阿諛奉承ē yú fèng chéng

    flatter shamelessly; be sycophantic — Flattering to curry favor.

    He flatters his boss shamelessly.

  • 👏大加讚賞dà jiā zàn shǎng

    speak highly of — Heaping praise on; sincere, without sarcasm.

    Critics spoke highly of the young director's first film.

Opposite

  • ✒️口誅筆伐kǒu zhū bǐ fá

    pilloried in the press — Condemning in speech and writing.

    The company was pilloried in the press for the recall.

  • 👃嗤之以鼻chī zhī yǐ bí

    pour scorn on sb; scorn — Sniffing at it.

    Experts poured scorn on the miracle diet.
