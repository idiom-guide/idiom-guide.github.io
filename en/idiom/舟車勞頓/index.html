舟車勞頓

zhōu chē láo dùn

In English

  • fatigued by the long journey

Meaning

Travel-weary.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • travel-weary
  • jet-lagged

Example

我們舟車勞頓地抵達。

We arrived fatigued by the long journey.

Usage

Often a courtesy when welcoming guests from afar.

Similar

  • ⛽精疲力盡jīng pí lì jìn

    exhausted; running on fumes — Running on fumes.

    By Friday I was running on fumes.

  • 🔋身心俱疲shēn xīn jù pí

    physically and mentally exhausted — Physically and mentally exhausted.

    After the long shift, she was physically and mentally exhausted.

Opposite

  • 🌞神采奕奕shén cǎi yì yì

    look radiant — Looking radiant.

    After a good rest, she looked radiant.

  • 😁精神飽滿jīng shén bǎo mǎn

    (high) spirited — In high spirits.

    The children arrived in high spirits.
