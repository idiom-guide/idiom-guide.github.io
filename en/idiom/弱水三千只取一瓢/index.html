弱水三千，只取一瓢

ruò shuǐ sān qiān, zhǐ qǔ yī piáo

In English

  • out of all the beauties, I only have eyes for you

Meaning

I only have eyes for you.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • you're the only one for me
  • I only have eyes for you

Example

世上美人無數，弱水三千，只取一瓢，我眼中只有妳。

Out of all the beauties in the world, I only have eyes for you.

Usage

For declaring devotion to one person.

Similar

  • 💘情有獨鍾qíng yǒu dú zhōng

    have a soft spot for sb/sth — Having a soft spot for someone.

    She has a soft spot for old movies.

  • 😍情人眼裡出西施qíng rén yǎn lǐ chū xī shī

    Beauty is in the eye of the beholder — Beauty is in the eye of the beholder.

    He thinks she's gorgeous. Beauty is in the eye of the beholder.

Opposite

  • 🆕喜新厭舊xǐ xīn yàn jiù

    fickle in love; always chasing the new — Always chasing the new.

    She accused him of being fickle in love.

  • 💔移情別戀yí qíng bié liàn

    fall for someone else — Falling for someone else.

    He fell for someone else while working abroad.
