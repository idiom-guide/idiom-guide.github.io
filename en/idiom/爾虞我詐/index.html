2 · Competition & Power

爾虞我詐

ěr yú wǒ zhà

In English

  • deceive and outwit one another
  • cutthroat scheming

Meaning

Mutual deceit and scheming.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • double-dealing

Example

談判變成一場爾虞我詐的較量。

The negotiation became a game of deceiving and outwitting each other.

Usage

Describes both sides deceiving each other; for business, politics and negotiations.

Similar

  • 🃏勾心鬥角gōu xīn dòu jiǎo

    intrigue; scheming against each other — Office intrigue and infighting.

    All the office intrigue is wearing everyone out.

  • 🧮機關算盡jī guān suàn jìn

    Machiavellian — Machiavellian scheming by one side.

    The Machiavellian adviser schemed his way into power.

Opposite

  • 🖖坦誠相對tǎn chéng xiāng duì

    level with sb — Being straight with each other.

    I'll level with you: we are running out of money.

  • 🤲推心置腹tuī xīn zhì fù

    speak from the heart; confide in sb — Confiding sincerely.

    After years of friendship, she could confide in him about anything.
