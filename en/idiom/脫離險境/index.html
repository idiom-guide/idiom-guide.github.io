脫離險境

tuō lí xiǎn jìng

In English

  • be out of the woods

Meaning

Out of danger.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • out of danger
  • in the clear

Example

醫生說病人終於脫離險境了。

The doctors said the patient is finally out of the woods.

Usage

For illness, disasters and financial crises.

Similar

  • 😅僥倖脫險jiǎo xìng tuō xiǎn

    a close call — A close call; escaped by luck.

    The car skidded off the road, but it was a close call and nobody was hurt.

  • 🚶全身而退quán shēn ér tuì

    just walk away — Walking away unscathed.

    He managed to just walk away from the deal.

Opposite

  • 🕸️陷入困境xiàn rù kùn jìng

    beleaguered — Beleaguered.

    The beleaguered mayor faces a vote of no confidence.

  • 🧱走投無路zǒu tóu wú lù

    have nowhere to turn — Nowhere to turn.

    With no job and no savings, he had nowhere to turn.
