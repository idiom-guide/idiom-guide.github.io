爭氣一點

zhēng qì yī diǎn

In English

  • step up the game = improve

Meaning

Step up; don't let us down.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • step up the game
  • pull oneself together
  • make something of oneself

Example

爭氣一點，決賽就在下週！

Come on, step up your game. The final is next week.

Usage

Encouraging or scolding; from elders to juniors, coaches to players.

Similar

  • 🚵力爭上游lì zhēng shàng yóu

    strive to move upward; aim high — Striving to move up.

    He strove upward and finally got into the school he wanted.

  • 🚴奮發圖強fèn fā tú qiáng

    make a determined effort to improve — Making a determined effort to grow stronger.

    He made a determined effort and became an industry leader three years later.

Opposite

  • 🤕一蹶不振yī jué bù zhèn

    never recover; be unable to get back on one's feet — Never recovering after one setback.

    He never recovered after the scandal.

  • 🔻自暴自棄zì bào zì qì

    give up on oneself — Giving up on oneself.

    Don't give up on yourself because of a single failure.
