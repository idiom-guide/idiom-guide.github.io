絢麗多姿

xuàn lì duō zī

In English

  • gorgeous and colorful

Meaning

Gorgeous and colorful.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • vibrant
  • resplendent

Example

節慶服裝絢麗多姿。

The festival costumes were gorgeous and colorful.

Usage

Formal; for costumes, scenery and performances.

Similar

  • 🌷萬紫千紅wàn zǐ qiān hóng

    a riot of colors — A riot of colors.

    Spring brought a riot of colors to the hillside.

  • 🌺奼紫嫣紅chà zǐ yān hóng

    a blaze of bright colors — A blaze of bright colors.

    In April the garden turns into a blaze of bright colors.

Opposite

  • 🥱平淡無奇píng dàn wú qí

    run-of-the-mill — Run-of-the-mill.

    It was just a run-of-the-mill day at the office.
