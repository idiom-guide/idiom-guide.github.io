下好離手

xià hǎo lí shǒu

In English

  • all bets are in
  • no take-backs

Meaning

No take-backs.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • no backsies

Example

下好離手，不能反悔。

All bets are in, so no take-backs.

Usage

Colloquial; from the gaming table, a reminder that a decision is final.

Similar

  • 🥛覆水難收fù shuǐ nán shōu

    what's done cannot be undone — No use crying over spilled milk.

    What's done can't be undone, and spoken words can't be taken back.

  • 🔨板上釘釘bǎn shàng dìng dīng

    a done deal; locked in — A done deal.

    The contract has been signed, so the deal is locked in.

Opposite

  • ➰出爾反爾chū ěr fǎn ěr

    go back on one's word; flip-flop — Going back on one's word.

    He went back on his word as soon as he had given it.

  • 🤷躊躇不定chóu chú bù dìng

    vacillate between sth — Vacillating.

    She vacillated between the two job offers for weeks.
