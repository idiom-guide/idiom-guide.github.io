近在咫尺

jìn zài zhǐ chǐ

In English

  • a stone's throw away

Meaning

A stone's throw away.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • just around the corner
  • within reach

Example

車站近在咫尺。

The station is a stone's throw away.

Usage

Mostly for distance; also for goals that are almost reached.

Similar

  • 🌊一衣帶水yī yī dài shuǐ

    close neighbors separated only by a strip of water — Close neighbors across a strip of water.

    As close neighbors separated only by a strip of water, the two countries have traded for centuries.

  • 🔜指日可待zhǐ rì kě dài

    just around the corner — Just around the corner.

    With trials complete, approval is just around the corner.
