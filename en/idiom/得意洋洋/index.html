得意洋洋

dé yì yáng yáng

In English

  • triumphant
  • smug

Meaning

Smug; triumphant.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • cock-a-hoop

Example

他得意洋洋地笑著，對勝利沾沾自喜。

He grinned, smug about winning.

Usage

For the look on someone's face after a win or award.

Similar

  • 😎得意忘形dé yì wàng xíng

    got carried away — Getting carried away.

    He got carried away after the first win and started bragging.

  • 😏不可一世bù kě yī shì

    act high and mighty; cocky — High and mighty.

    The new champion acted high and mighty.

Opposite

  • 😟愁眉苦臉chóu méi kǔ liǎn

    look so woebegone; with a woebegone expression — Woebegone.

    He looked so woebegone that the waiter asked if he was ill.

  • 🥀萎靡不振wěi mǐ bù zhèn

    listless; in low spirits — In low spirits.

    The team was in low spirits after the defeat.
