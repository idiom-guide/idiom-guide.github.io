3 · Joy & Satisfaction

如沐春風

rú mù chūn fēng

In English

  • feel so good
  • pleasant

Meaning

Like a spring breeze.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • like a breath of fresh air

Example

和她談話讓我如沐春風。

Talking with her made me feel so good, like a spring breeze.

Usage

For time spent with kind or inspiring people.

Similar

  • 🌄心曠神怡xīn kuàng shén yí

    completely relaxed and happy — Refreshed and happy.

    A walk by the lake leaves me completely relaxed and happy.

  • 🌬️春風化雨chūn fēng huà yǔ

    nurture with love and care/patience — Nurtured by good teaching.

    The kindergarten teacher nurtures her pupils with love and patience.

Opposite

  • 🥶冷眼看待lěng yǎn kàn dài

    faces a frosty reception — A frosty reception.

    The proposal faced a frosty reception from the board.
