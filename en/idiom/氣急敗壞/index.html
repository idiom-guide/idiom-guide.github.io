4 · Pain & Unease

氣急敗壞

qì jí bài huài

In English

  • infuriating
  • desperation
  • angrily rush to
  • exploded in rage

Meaning

Exploding in rage.Negative · General

Example

他氣急敗壞，勃然大怒。

He was so infuriated that he exploded in rage.

Usage

For flustered fury.

Similar

  • ⚡大發雷霆dà fā léi tíng

    be incandescent with rage — Hitting the roof.

    The coach was incandescent with rage after the loss.

  • 🚪惱羞走人nǎo xiū zǒu rén

    storm off — Storming off.

    Embarrassed and angry, he stormed off in the middle of the meeting.

Opposite

  • 🧊保持冷靜bǎo chí lěng jìng

    be dispassionate — Staying calm.

    In an emergency, try to stay dispassionate and think clearly.

  • 🦢從容不迫cóng róng bù pò

    unruffled — Unruffled.

    The host remained unruffled when the lights went out.

Also

fume over sth
