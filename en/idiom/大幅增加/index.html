大幅增加

dà fú zēng jiā

In English

  • by leaps and bounds

Meaning

A sharp increase.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • increase sharply
  • surge

Example

今年的銷售量大幅增加。

Sales have grown by leaps and bounds this year.

Usage

Common in reports and news, often followed by figures or percentages.

Similar

  • 🐇突飛猛進tū fēi měng jìn

    advance by leaps and bounds — Advancing by leaps and bounds.

    Her English has advanced by leaps and bounds.

  • 📈與日俱增yǔ rì jù zēng

    grow by the day — Growing by the day.

    Pressure on the minister is growing by the day.

Opposite

  • 📉一落千丈yī luò qiān zhàng

    plummet; nosedive — Plummeting.

    Sales plummeted after the recall.

  • ↘️每況愈下měi kuàng yù xià

    go from bad to worse — Going downhill.

    The company's finances went from bad to worse.
