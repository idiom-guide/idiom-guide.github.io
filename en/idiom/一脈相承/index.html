10 · Culture & Heritage

一脈相承

yī mài xiāng chéng

In English

  • in an unbroken line

Meaning

In an unbroken line.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • descend from the same tradition
  • cut from the same cloth

Example

這兩個畫派一脈相承。

The two schools of painting descend in an unbroken line.

Usage

Formal; for schools of thought, ideas, crafts and families.

Similar

  • 🌉承先啟後chéng xiān qǐ hòu

    bridge the past and the future — Bridging past and future.

    The exhibition bridges the past and the future of the craft.

  • 🏞️源遠流長yuán yuǎn liú cháng

    a long course — A long history.

    Tea culture in Asia has a long course of history.
