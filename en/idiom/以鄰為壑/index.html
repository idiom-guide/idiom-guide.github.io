以鄰為壑

yǐ lín wéi hè

In English

  • beggar-thy-neighbor

Meaning

Beggar-thy-neighbor.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • shift the problem onto one's neighbor
  • export one's problems

Example

競相貶值是以鄰為壑的政策。

Competitive devaluation is a beggar-thy-neighbor policy.

Usage

Formal; from Mencius; for trade and environmental policy.

Opposite

  • 🏘️敦親睦鄰dūn qīn mù lín

    good-neighborliness — Good-neighborliness.

    The foreign minister stressed good-neighborliness in his first speech.

  • 🔦守望相助shǒu wàng xiāng zhù

    look out for one another — Looking out for one another.

    In this neighborhood, residents look out for one another.
