唇亡齒寒

chún wáng chǐ hán

In English

  • share a common fate

Meaning

Sharing a common fate.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • if one falls, the other is exposed
  • in the same boat

Example

兩個鄰國唇亡齒寒，一方倒下，另一方就會暴露在危險中。

The neighbors share a common fate: if one falls, the other is exposed.

Usage

From the Zuo Zhuan; for international relations and supply chains.

Similar

  • 🥨唇齒相依chún chǐ xiāng yī

    be as interdependent as lips and teeth — Mutually dependent.

    The two countries are as interdependent as lips and teeth; their security is tightly linked.

  • 👭休戚相關xiū qī xiāng guān

    be bound up together; share weal and woe — Bound up together.

    The company's survival is bound up with the fate of every employee.

Opposite

  • 📴事不關己shì bù guān jǐ

    none of one's business; not one's concern — None of one's business.

    It was none of his business, so he stood by and did nothing.
