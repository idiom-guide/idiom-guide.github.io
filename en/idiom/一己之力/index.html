一己之力

yī jǐ zhī lì

In English

  • single-handedly

Meaning

Single-handedly.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • on one's own
  • by oneself

Example

她以一己之力架設了整個網站。

She built the whole website single-handedly.

Usage

Often 以一己之力 (on one's own); can praise ability or lament being alone.

Similar

  • 🦸‍♀️孤軍奮戰gū jūn fèn zhàn

    fight a lone battle; go it alone — Fighting a lone battle.

    She fought a lone battle on the board with no one behind her.

  • 🎩身兼數職shēn jiān shù zhí

    wear many hats — Wearing many hats.

    As the only doctor in a small town, she wears many hats.

Opposite

  • 🧠集思廣益jí sī guǎng yì

    draw on collective wisdom — Pooling everyone's ideas.

    The company draws on collective wisdom by asking every employee for ideas.

  • 🧠群策群力qún cè qún lì

    pool ideas and efforts — Pooling ideas and efforts.

    We pooled our ideas and efforts to meet the deadline.
