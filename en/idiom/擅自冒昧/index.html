擅自、冒昧

shàn zì, mào mèi

In English

  • took the liberty of v-ing

Meaning

Taking the liberty.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • take the liberty of V-ing
  • presume to
  • without asking

Example

我冒昧先訂了位子。

I took the liberty of booking a table.

Usage

擅自 is negative (without permission); 冒昧 is usually a polite, modest preface.

Similar

  • 🙇冒昧擅自mào mèi shàn zì

    take the liberty of V-ing — Taking the liberty.

    I took the liberty of ordering for everyone.

  • 👨‍✈️自作主張zì zuò zhǔ zhāng

    take matters into one's own hands; act on one's own authority — Taking matters into one's own hands.

    He took it upon himself to change the contract, and it caused trouble.

Opposite

  • 📪事先請示shì xiān qǐng shì

    ask for approval beforehand — Asking first.

    Important decisions must be approved beforehand.
