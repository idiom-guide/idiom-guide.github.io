大放厥詞

dà fàng jué cí

In English

  • spout nonsense
  • fling wild accusations

Meaning

Spouting nonsense.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • mouth off

Example

他在直播節目上大放厥詞。

He spouted nonsense on live TV.

Usage

Originally Han Yu's praise for lavish prose; now critical.

Similar

  • 🗯️胡說八道hú shuō bā dào

    hogwash; nonsense — Hogwash.

    That's hogwash.

  • 😮口無遮攔kǒu wú zhē lán

    sb have no filter; shoot from the lip — Having no filter.

    My uncle has no filter; he says whatever pops into his head.

Opposite

  • 🔎字字斟酌zì zì zhēn zhuó

    carefully examine — Weighing every word.

    She carefully examined every word of the contract before signing.

  • 📝言簡意賅yán jiǎn yì gāi

    concise and to the point — Concise and to the point.

    Her report was concise and to the point.
