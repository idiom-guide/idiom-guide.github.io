12 · Duration & Speed

經年累月

jīng nián lěi yuè

In English

  • year after year

Meaning

Year after year.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • over the years
  • for ages

Example

石階經年累月被磨平了。

The stone steps have been worn down year after year.

Usage

Formal; for the results of long accumulation.

Similar

  • 🧱日積月累rì jī yuè lěi

    no quick fixes — Little by little.

    There are no quick fixes in language learning; progress comes from steady, accumulated effort.

  • 🐌曠日持久kuàng rì chí jiǔ

    protracted — Protracted.

    The lawsuit turned into a protracted battle.

Opposite

  • 🌗一朝一夕yī zhāo yī xī

    overnight — Overnight.

    Fluency cannot be achieved overnight.

  • ⚡彈指之間tán zhǐ zhī jiān

    in the blink of an eye — In the blink of an eye.

    Twenty years passed in the blink of an eye.
