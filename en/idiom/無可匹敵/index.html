無可匹敵

wú kě pǐ dí

In English

  • unmatched

Meaning

Unmatched.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • unrivaled
  • in a league of one's own

Example

她的小提琴技巧無可匹敵。

Her skill on the violin is unmatched.

Usage

For skills, strength and products.

Similar

  • 🥇無人能及wú rén néng jí

    second to none; unrivaled — Second to none.

    Her skill at chess is second to none.

  • ⚔️所向披靡suǒ xiàng pī mǐ

    unbeatable — Unbeatable.

    The team was unbeatable that season.

Opposite

  • 🥚不堪一擊bù kān yī jī

    collapse at the first blow; be easily crushed — Collapsing at the first blow.

    The team looks strong but crumbles at the first blow.

  • ⚖️勢均力敵shì jūn lì dí

    be equally matched — Evenly matched.

    The two teams are equally matched.
