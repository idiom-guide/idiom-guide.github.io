不苟言笑

bù gǒu yán xiào

In English

  • dour

Meaning

Stern; seldom smiling.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • stern
  • straight-faced

Example

那位不苟言笑的會計師很少笑。

The dour accountant rarely smiled.

Usage

For strict teachers, officials and serious personalities.

Similar

  • 🕊️莊嚴肅穆zhuāng yán sù mù

    solemn; solemn and serene — Solemn; usually of occasions.

    The ceremony was solemn and serene.

Opposite

  • 🍸談笑風生tán xiào fēng shēng

    chat and laugh with ease — Chatting and laughing freely.

    Everyone chatted and laughed easily around the table.

  • 🤗平易近人píng yì jìn rén

    approachable — Approachable.

    The professor is so approachable that students visit him with every question.
