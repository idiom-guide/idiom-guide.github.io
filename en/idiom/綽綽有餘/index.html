綽綽有餘

chuò chuò yǒu yú

In English

  • more than enough

Meaning

More than enough.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • plenty
  • ample

Example

一萬元用來旅行綽綽有餘。

Ten thousand dollars is more than enough for the trip.

Usage

For money, time, space and ability.

Similar

  • 🔋行有餘力xíng yǒu yú lì

    has the heft to do sth — Having resources to spare.

    If you have energy to spare after your own tasks, please help a teammate.

  • 🔪遊刃有餘yóu rèn yǒu yú

    looks effortless; be more than equal to — Looking effortless.

    She handles the busiest shifts with ease, making it look effortless.

Opposite

  • 👕捉襟見肘zhuō jīn jiàn zhǒu

    cash-strapped; stretched thin — Stretched thin.

    The cash-strapped agency froze hiring.

  • 🥤杯水車薪bēi shuǐ chē xīn

    far too little to help — Far too little to help.

    The subsidy is far too little to help.
