處境艱難

chǔ jìng jiān nán

In English

  • be in a tough spot/corner

Meaning

In a tough spot.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • in a bind
  • up against it

Example

她處境艱難：不能辭職，也待不下去。

She is in a tough spot: she can't quit and she can't stay.

Usage

For individuals, companies and countries in difficulty.

Similar

  • 😬尷尬處境gān gà chǔ jìng

    predicament — A predicament.

    He was in a real predicament: he had promised to attend two weddings on the same day.

  • ↔️進退維谷jìn tuì wéi gǔ

    be in a quandary; caught in a bind — In a quandary.

    She is in a quandary over the job offer.

Opposite

  • 🏄左右逢源zuǒ yòu féng yuán

    reap rewards; benefit from both sides — Benefiting from both sides.

    A savvy broker can reap rewards by benefiting from both sides.

  • ⛵一帆風順yī fān fēng shùn

    smooth sailing — Smooth sailing.

    I wish you smooth sailing in your new post.
