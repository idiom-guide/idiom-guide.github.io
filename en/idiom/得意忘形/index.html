得意忘形

dé yì wàng xíng

In English

  • got carried away

Meaning

Getting carried away.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • let success go to one's head
  • full of oneself

Example

首勝之後他得意忘形，開始吹噓。

He got carried away after the first win and started bragging.

Usage

For people who let success go to their heads.

Similar

  • 😏得意洋洋dé yì yáng yáng

    triumphant; smug — Smug.

    He grinned, smug about winning.

  • 🎭樂極生悲lè jí shēng bēi

    extreme joy turns sorrow — Joy turning to sorrow.

    At the party he danced so hard that he broke his leg; extreme joy turned to sorrow.

Opposite

  • 🙇‍♂️虛懷若谷xū huái ruò gǔ

    be humble and open-minded — Humble and open-minded.

    The more accomplished a person is, the more humble and open-minded they tend to be.

  • 🐭不出鋒頭bù chū fēng tóu

    unassuming — Unassuming.

    He is an unassuming man who never seeks the spotlight.
