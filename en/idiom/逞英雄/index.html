逞英雄

chěng yīng xióng

In English

  • play the hero
  • show off

Meaning

Playing the hero.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • be a hero

Example

別逞英雄，快找人幫忙。

Don't play the hero. Call for help.

Usage

Colloquial; often a warning not to push one's luck.

Similar

  • 📢愛出風頭ài chū fēng tou

    sb is loud — Loving the limelight.

    My cousin is loud and loves being the center of attention.

  • 🐗有勇無謀yǒu yǒng wú móu

    foolhardy — Foolhardy.

    The foolhardy knight charged alone.

Opposite

  • 🧮量力而為liàng lì ér wéi

    act within one's means — Doing what one can afford.

    When starting a business, act within your means and don't borrow too much.

  • 🐭不出鋒頭bù chū fēng tóu

    unassuming — Unassuming.

    He is an unassuming man who never seeks the spotlight.
