頗好此道

pō hào cǐ dào

In English

  • voracious consumer of sth

Meaning

A big fan.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • a big fan of
  • into

Example

他頗好此道，偵探小說一本接一本地讀。

He is a voracious consumer of detective novels.

Usage

For hobbies, collecting and reading.

Similar

  • 🔁樂此不疲lè cǐ bù pí

    never get tired of it; never get used to it — Never tiring of it.

    The kids never get tired of the playground.

  • 📖手不釋卷shǒu bù shì juàn

    an avid reader; always has a book in hand — Never putting a book down.

    He is an avid reader who always has a book in his hand.

Opposite

  • 😑興致缺缺xìng zhì quē quē

    lukewarm; showing little interest — Not keen.

    He showed a lack of appetite for the trip.

  • 🚪門外漢mén wài hàn

    layperson; outsider — A layperson.

    I'm a layperson when it comes to law.
