油嘴滑舌

yóu zuǐ huá shé

In English

  • flippant

Meaning

Flippant.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • glib
  • smart-mouthed

Example

他油嘴滑舌地敷衍我。

He gave me a flippant answer.

Usage

Colloquial; for glib, insincere talk.

Similar

  • 🍯花言巧語huā yán qiǎo yǔ

    sweet talk; smooth talk — Sweet talk.

    Don't be taken in by his sweet talk.

  • 👄耍嘴皮子shuǎ zuǐ pí zi

    talk is cheap — Talk is cheap.

    Talk is cheap. Show me the results.

Opposite

  • 🙏誠心一點chéng xīn yī diǎn

    say like you mean it — Saying it like you mean it.

    If you want to apologize, say it like you mean it.

  • 💌肺腑之言fèi fǔ zhī yán

    from the bottom of my heart — From the bottom of one's heart.

    These words come from the bottom of my heart.
