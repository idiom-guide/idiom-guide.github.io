一字不假

yī zì bù jiǎ

In English

  • unadulterated

Meaning

Every word of it is true.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • every word is true
  • be the gospel truth
  • be unadulterated truth

Example

他說的話一字不假，全是千真萬確的事實。

Every word he said was unadulterated truth.

Usage

Stresses that one isn't lying; strong, almost an oath.

Similar

  • ❕千真萬確qiān zhēn wàn què

    be absolutely true — Absolutely true; even stronger.

    What he says is absolutely true; I saw it with my own eyes.

  • 🈶句句屬實jù jù shǔ shí

    every sentence is true — Every sentence is factual; formal, for testimony and reports.

    After checking, police found every sentence in the witness's statement to be true.

Opposite

  • 🍜添油加醋tiān yóu jiā cù

    embellish; add colorful details — Embellishing the story.

    He embellished the story when retelling it and changed it completely.

  • 🔉言過其實yán guò qí shí

    exaggerate; overstate — Exaggerated.

    The restaurant's advertising overstates the reality.
