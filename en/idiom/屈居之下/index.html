2 · Competition & Power

屈居之下

qū jū zhī xià

In English

  • play second fiddle to sb

Meaning

Playing second fiddle.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • play second fiddle to
  • be overshadowed by
  • come second to

Example

多年來，她一直屈居於名氣更大的哥哥之下。

For years, she played second fiddle to her more famous brother.

Usage

Often 屈居第二 (come second) or 屈居人下 (be beneath someone).

Similar

  • 🥈相形見絀xiāng xíng jiàn chù

    pale in comparison with; be overshadowed by — Paling in comparison.

    My cooking pales in comparison with hers.

  • 🌘相形失色xiāng xíng shī sè

    A pales beside B — Paling beside someone.

    Last year's profit pales beside this year's.

Opposite

  • 🌟蓋過鋒芒gài guò fēng máng

    outshine — Outshining someone.

    The newcomer's talent outshone the veteran's.

  • 🔝位居上風wèi jū shàng fēng

    have the upper hand — Having the upper hand.

    Our team has the upper hand in the second half.
