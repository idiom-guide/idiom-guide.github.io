不受打擊

bù shòu dǎ jī

In English

  • undeterred

Meaning

Undeterred.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • unfazed
  • bounce back

Example

她不受打擊，第一次被拒後又申請了十所學校。

Undeterred by the first rejection, she applied to ten more schools.

Usage

For bouncing back from rejection, criticism or failure.

Similar

  • 🦾不屈不撓bù qū bù náo

    invincible, indomitable, dauntless — Indomitable; stronger.

    The dauntless climber kept going despite the storm.

  • 🌬️再次振作zài cì zhèn zuò

    get a second wind — Getting a second wind.

    After a short rest, the runner got a second wind.

Opposite

  • 🤕一蹶不振yī jué bù zhèn

    never recover; be unable to get back on one's feet — Never recovering.

    He never recovered after the scandal.

  • 🥚不堪一擊bù kān yī jī

    collapse at the first blow; be easily crushed — Collapsing at the first blow.

    The team looks strong but crumbles at the first blow.
