槁木死灰

gǎo mù sǐ huī

In English

  • utterly lifeless
  • apathetic

Meaning

Utterly lifeless.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • a shell of a person

Example

輸了之後，她看起來形如槁木死灰。

After the loss, she looked utterly lifeless.

Usage

From Zhuangzi's 'Discussion on Making All Things Equal'.

Similar

  • 🥀心如槁木xīn rú gǎo mù

    dead inside — Dead inside.

    After the loss, he felt dead inside.

  • 🥀萎靡不振wěi mǐ bù zhèn

    listless; in low spirits — In low spirits.

    The team was in low spirits after the defeat.

Opposite

  • 🐉生龍活虎shēng lóng huó hǔ

    be alive and well — Full of life.

    Despite his age, grandpa is alive and well and plays tennis every day.

  • 🔥生命熱情shēng mìng rè qíng

    zest for life — A zest for life.

    Her zest for life inspires everyone around her.
