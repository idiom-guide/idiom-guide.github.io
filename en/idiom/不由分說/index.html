不由分說

bù yóu fēn shuō

In English

  • indiscriminate + n

Meaning

Without letting anyone explain.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • without giving a chance to explain
  • summarily

Example

警方不由分說地大舉逮捕，不給任何人解釋的機會。

The police made indiscriminate arrests, giving nobody a chance to explain.

Usage

For high-handed or summary action.

Similar

  • 🐺一意孤行yī yì gū xíng

    go it alone; act arbitrarily — Going it alone.

    He went it alone, ignoring the advice of every adviser.

  • 🛁不分良莠bù fēn liáng yǒu

    Throw the baby out with the bathwater; baby with the bathwater — Throwing the baby out with the bathwater.

    Banning all drones would throw the baby out with the bathwater.

Opposite

  • 📂就事論事jiù shì lùn shì

    judge the matter on its own merits; stick to the issue — Sticking to the point.

    Let's stick to the issue and leave personal grudges out of it.

  • 👂洗耳恭聽xǐ ěr gōng tīng

    be all ears — All ears.

    Go ahead, I'm all ears.
