趁火打劫

chèn huǒ dǎ jié

In English

  • fish in troubled waters

Meaning

Profiteering from a crisis.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • profiteer
  • loot

Example

風災過後，有些房東趁火打劫，調漲房租。

Some landlords fished in troubled waters by raising rents after the storm.

Usage

For exploiting disasters and emergencies.

Similar

  • 🐟渾水摸魚hún shuǐ mō yú

    catch fish in muddy water — Fishing in muddy waters.

    Some traders catch fish in muddy water during a market panic.

  • 🦶落井下石luò jǐng xià shí

    kick sb when he's down; pile on — Kicking someone when they're down.

    Don't kick him when he's down.

Opposite

  • ❄️雪中送炭xuě zhōng sòng tàn

    help when it is needed most — Help when it's needed most.

    A true friend helps when it is needed most.

  • ⛈️患難與共huàn nàn yǔ gòng

    stick together through thick and thin — Sticking together through hard times.

    They stuck together through thick and thin.
