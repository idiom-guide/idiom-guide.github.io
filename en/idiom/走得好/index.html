走得好

zǒu de hǎo

In English

  • good riddance

Meaning

Good riddance.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • glad to see the back of sb
  • don't let the door hit you on the way out

Example

他辭職了？走得好。

He quit? Good riddance.

Usage

Colloquial and cutting; for when someone unwelcome leaves.

Similar

  • 🙌大快人心dà kuài rén xīn

    give a lift to the heart — Immensely satisfying.

    The news that the corrupt official had been jailed gave everyone a lift.

Opposite

  • 😢依依不捨yī yī bù shě

    reluctant to say goodbye — Reluctant to say goodbye.

    We are all reluctant to say goodbye.

  • 👋珍重再見zhēn zhòng zài jiàn

    take care and goodbye — Take care and goodbye.

    Take care, and goodbye for now.
