顧名思義

gù míng sī yì

In English

  • as (the title) implies

Meaning

As the name suggests.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • as the name suggests
  • as the name implies

Example

顧名思義，快閃店只會短暫出現。

As the name implies, a pop-up store appears only for a short time.

Usage

Usually opens a sentence as a parenthetical.

Similar

  • 📔望文生義wàng wén shēng yì

    guess the meaning from the words alone — Guessing the meaning from the words alone; often misleading.

    Guessing the meaning from the words alone, without knowing the allusion, easily leads to errors.

  • 🚥一目了然yī mù liǎo rán

    be clear at a glance — Clear at a glance; not limited to names.

    The chart is clear at a glance.

Opposite

  • 🎫名不副實míng bù fù shí

    not live up to its name — Not living up to the name.

    This "five-star" restaurant doesn't live up to its name.

  • 🧶穿鑿附會chuān záo fù huì

    farfetched — Far-fetched interpretation; the opposite of natural inference.

    His claim that the poem predicted the election is farfetched.
