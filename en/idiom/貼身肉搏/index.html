2 · Competition & Power

貼身肉搏

tiē shēn ròu bó

In English

  • dogfight

Meaning

Hand-to-hand combat; a dogfight.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • a dogfight
  • hand-to-hand combat
  • up close and personal

Example

兩位戰鬥機飛行員陷入一場貼身肉搏的纏鬥。

The two fighter pilots were locked in a dogfight.

Usage

Figurative for tight battles in elections, business or sport.

Similar

  • 🥊正面交鋒zhèng miàn jiāo fēng

    go toe-to-toe — Going toe-to-toe.

    The two candidates went toe-to-toe in the debate.

  • ⚔️你死我活nǐ sǐ wǒ huó

    cutthroat competition; a fight to the death — A fight to the death.

    The two companies are locked in cutthroat competition.

Opposite

  • 🍿作壁上觀zuò bì shàng guān

    watch from the sidelines; stand idly by — Watching from the sidelines.

    While the two firms fought a price war, the rest of the industry could only watch from the sidelines.

  • 🧍冷眼旁觀lěng yǎn páng guān

    on the sidelines — Watching coolly from the sidelines.

    While the others argued, she watched from the sidelines.
