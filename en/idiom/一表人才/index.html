一表人才

yī biǎo rén cái

In English

  • a fine figure of a man
  • good-looking and capable

Meaning

Outstanding in looks and also talented.Positive · General

Example

他一表人才，前途無量。

He is a fine figure of a man with a bright future.

Usage

Mostly for men. Praises both looks and ability, a stock phrase when introducing someone, often followed by a note on a bright future.

Similar

  • 🌲玉樹臨風yù shù lín fēng

    dashing; strikingly handsome — Handsome and upright; about appearance.

    He cut a dashing figure in his tailored suit.

  • 🕴️儀表堂堂yí biǎo táng táng

    distinguished-looking; of imposing bearing — Dignified in looks; about presence.

    The new ambassador is a distinguished-looking man in his fifties.

Opposite

  • 🤷其貌不揚qí mào bù yáng

    unprepossessing; plain-looking — Plain-looking; a gentler way of putting it.

    He was plain-looking, but everyone admired his talent.

  • 🥱平淡無奇píng dàn wú qí

    run-of-the-mill — Ordinary, with nothing special about it.

    It was just a run-of-the-mill day at the office.
