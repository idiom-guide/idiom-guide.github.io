7 · Public Opinion

順應民意

shùn yìng mín yì

In English

  • heed public opinion

Meaning

Heeding public opinion.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • listen to the people
  • bow to public pressure

Example

議會順應民意，撤銷了這項計畫。

The council heeded public opinion and scrapped the plan.

Usage

For decisions by governments and councils.

Similar

  • 🧲民心向背mín xīn xiàng bèi

    where public support lies — Where public support lies.

    Elections are decided by where public support lies.

  • 👂重視基層zhòng shì jī céng

    listen to the ground — Listening to the ground.

    A good manager listens to the ground before making policy.

Opposite

  • 🐺一意孤行yī yì gū xíng

    go it alone; act arbitrarily — Going it alone.

    He went it alone, ignoring the advice of every adviser.

  • 🛑剛愎自用gāng bì zì yòng

    opinionated; stubborn — Opinionated.

    The stubborn chairman ignored every warning.
