動盪不安

dòng dàng bù ān

In English

  • deeply unstable

Meaning

Deeply unstable.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • in turmoil
  • volatile

Example

該地區仍然動盪不安。

The region remains deeply unstable.

Usage

For regions, politics and society.

Similar

  • 🏇兵荒馬亂bīng huāng mǎ luàn

    chaos; turmoil — Turmoil.

    The city fell into chaos.

  • 🌊動盪不定dòng dàng bù dìng

    looming precarity — Precarious.

    The company faces looming precarity as orders dry up.

Opposite

  • 🕊️長治久安cháng zhì jiǔ ān

    lasting stability — Lasting stability.

    Lasting stability depends on institutions, not on any single leader.

  • 🎤歌舞昇平gē wǔ shēng píng

    peaceful and prosperous — Peaceful and prosperous.

    The capital looked peaceful and prosperous, with no sign of the war to come.
