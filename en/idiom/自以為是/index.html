自以為是

zì yǐ wéi shì

In English

  • in the belief that

Meaning

Thinking one knows best.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • think one knows best
  • self-righteous

Example

他自以為是，認為自己比誰都懂。

He did it in the belief that he knew better than everyone else.

Usage

For criticizing people who won't listen.

Similar

  • 🛑剛愎自用gāng bì zì yòng

    opinionated; stubborn — Stubbornly self-willed.

    The stubborn chairman ignored every warning.

  • 💡自作聰明zì zuò cōng míng

    smart aleck — A smart aleck.

    Nobody likes a smart aleck who corrects the teacher.

Opposite

  • 🙋不恥下問bù chǐ xià wèn

    not too proud to ask — Not too proud to ask.

    A real scholar is not too proud to ask.

  • 👂洗耳恭聽xǐ ěr gōng tīng

    be all ears — All ears.

    Go ahead, I'm all ears.
