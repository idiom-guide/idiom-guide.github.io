漫天要價

màn tiān yào jià

In English

  • ask a sky-high price

Meaning

A sky-high price.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • highway robbery
  • start high and haggle down

Example

賣家漫天要價，等著對方殺價。

The seller asked a sky-high price, expecting to be talked down.

Usage

Often paired with 就地還錢 'counter with a lowball offer'.

Similar

  • 🔺坐地起價zuò dì qǐ jià

    jack up the price at the last minute — Jacking up the price at the last minute.

    The contractor jacked up the price at the last minute.

  • 🤑討價還價tǎo jià huán jià

    haggle; bargain — Haggling.

    The two sides haggled over the delivery schedule.

Opposite

  • 🏷️物美價廉wù měi jià lián

    good value for money — Good value for money.

    The brand built its name on good value for money.

  • 🛒薄利多銷bó lì duō xiāo

    low margins, high volume — Low margins, high volume.

    Discount retailers live on low margins and high volume.
