達到巔峰

dá dào diān fēng

In English

  • reach a fever pitch

Meaning

Reaching a peak.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • reach a peak
  • hit its height

Example

辯論的激烈程度達到巔峰。

The debate reached a fever pitch.

Usage

For debates, emotions, careers and competitions.

Similar

  • 🏔️登峰造極dēng fēng zào jí

    reach the pinnacle — Reaching the pinnacle of skill.

    Her career reached its pinnacle with that role.

  • 🔥如火如荼rú huǒ rú tú

    in full swing — In full swing.

    The election campaign is in full swing.

Opposite

  • 🚩偃旗息鼓yǎn qí xī gǔ

    call a halt; lie low — Calling a halt.

    The band announced they would call a halt for now: a pause, they said, not a farewell.

  • 💨煙消雲散yān xiāo yún sàn

    vaporized, vanished, be gone — Vanishing into thin air.

    His worries vanished the moment he heard the news.
