3 · Talent & Character

惹人憐愛

rě rén lián ài

In English

  • adorable

Meaning

Adorable.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • endearing
  • lovable

Example

小狗穿著小外套，惹人憐愛。

The puppy looked adorable in its tiny coat.

Usage

For babies, pets and endearing people.

Similar

  • 🐥人畜無害rén chù wú hài

    innocuous, disarming — Harmless and disarming.

    With his disarming smile, he seemed completely harmless.

Opposite

  • 🤮令人反感lìng rén fǎn gǎn

    distasteful; off-putting — Distasteful.

    His constant bragging was distasteful.
