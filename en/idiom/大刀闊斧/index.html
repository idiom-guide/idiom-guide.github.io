大刀闊斧

dà dāo kuò fǔ

In English

  • go the whole hog

Meaning

Bold and sweeping action.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • bold and sweeping
  • wield the axe

Example

新任執行長大刀闊斧地推動重整計畫。

The new CEO is going the whole hog with her restructuring plan.

Usage

For reforms, layoffs and cuts; decisive in tone.

Similar

  • 🏗️大破大立dà pò dà lì

    tear down in order to rebuild; radical overhaul — Tearing down to rebuild.

    The firm needs a radical overhaul.

  • ⚡雷厲風行léi lì fēng xíng

    swift and decisive — Swift and decisive.

    The new director was swift and decisive.

Opposite

  • 🤔猶豫不決yóu yù bù jué

    dilly-dally — Unable to make up one's mind.

    Stop dilly-dallying and make a decision.

  • 🥚小心翼翼xiǎo xīn yì yì

    tread carefully; be extremely careful — Extremely careful.

    He chose his words very carefully to avoid trouble.
