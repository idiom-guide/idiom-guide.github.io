職場菜鳥

zhí chǎng cài niǎo

In English

  • be new to the workforce

Meaning

A rookie at work.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • new to the workforce
  • a rookie
  • a greenhorn

Example

我是職場菜鳥，請多多包涵。

I'm new to the workforce, so please be patient with me.

Usage

Colloquial, often modest or teasing.

Similar

  • 🧭尋找方向xún zhǎo fāng xiàng

    fight for traction — Finding one's footing.

    After college, I spent a year fighting for traction in my career.

  • 🏃躍躍欲試yuè yuè yù shì

    raring to go; itching to try — Itching to try.

    The kids were itching to try the new game.

Opposite

  • 🐎老馬識途lǎo mǎ shí tú

    elder wisdom — An old hand who knows the way.

    The old guide knew the mountain paths, and his elder wisdom saved the day.

  • 🚗駕輕就熟jià qīng jiù shú

    mastering; getting into one's stride — Skilled and practiced.

    After a month, she was getting into her stride at the new job.
