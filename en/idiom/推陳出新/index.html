10 · Culture & Heritage

推陳出新

tuī chén chū xīn

In English

  • breathe new life into tradition

Meaning

Breathing new life into tradition.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • out with the old, in with the new
  • reinvent

Example

年輕設計師為傳統推陳出新。

Young designers are breathing new life into tradition.

Usage

For art, products and culture.

Similar

  • 🏺古為今用gǔ wéi jīn yòng

    adapt the past for the present — Adapting the past for the present.

    The architect adapts the past for the present.

  • 💡別出心裁bié chū xīn cái

    original; ingenious — Ingenious.

    He came up with an ingenious plan for the party.

Opposite

  • 🚋墨守成規mò shǒu chéng guī

    stick to old rules; stay in a rut — Sticking to the old rules.

    The company sticks to old rules and finds it hard to innovate.

  • 🍪千篇一律qiān piān yī lǜ

    be formulaic; follow the same pattern — All the same.

    These ads are all formulaic and utterly unoriginal.
