誠心一點

chéng xīn yī diǎn

In English

  • say like you mean it

Meaning

Say it like you mean it.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • say it like you mean it
  • put some sincerity into it
  • be sincere

Example

要道歉就誠心一點，別敷衍我。

If you want to apologize, say it like you mean it.

Usage

A demand, often about apologies or requests, when someone seems insincere.

Similar

  • 💗發自內心fā zì nèi xīn

    from the bottom of one's heart — From the heart.

    She thanked him from the bottom of her heart.

  • 💌肺腑之言fèi fǔ zhī yán

    from the bottom of my heart — Heartfelt words.

    These words come from the bottom of my heart.

Opposite

  • 🎭虛情假意xū qíng jiǎ yì

    insincere; hollow sentiments — Feigned feelings.

    Her feigned friendliness fooled no one.

  • 📋例行公事lì xíng gōng shì

    formality — A mere formality.

    The weekly check-in is just a formality.
