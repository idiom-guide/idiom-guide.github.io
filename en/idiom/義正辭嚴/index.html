義正辭嚴

yì zhèng cí yán

In English

  • in a stern, righteous tone

Meaning

In a stern, righteous tone.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • take a firm moral stand
  • speak with righteous conviction

Example

他義正辭嚴地拒絕了賄賂。

He turned down the bribe in a stern, righteous tone.

Usage

Used when refusing, rebutting or condemning; stresses being in the right and speaking sternly.

Similar

  • 🔊擲地有聲zhì dì yǒu shēng

    carry real weight; be forceful and weighty — Words that carry weight.

    His speech carried real weight and silenced the room.

  • 🦒理直氣壯lǐ zhí qì zhuàng

    be fully justified and assertive; with righteous confidence — Assertive because one is in the right.

    He demanded an apology with righteous confidence.

Opposite

  • 🐶虛張聲勢xū zhāng shēng shì

    bluff; more bark than bite — Bluffing.

    His threats were just a bluff; he is more bark than bite.

  • 🙁理屈詞窮lǐ qū cí qióng

    have no reply; be at a loss for words — Speechless because one is in the wrong.

    Once his lie was exposed, he had no reply.
