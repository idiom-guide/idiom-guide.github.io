池魚之殃

chí yú zhī yāng

In English

  • collateral damage

Meaning

Caught in the crossfire.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • caught in the crossfire
  • innocent bystander

Example

大企業倒閉，小供應商遭受池魚之殃。

Small suppliers suffered collateral damage when the giant firm went bankrupt.

Usage

From 城門失火，殃及池魚, 'when the city gate burns, the fish in the moat suffer'.

Similar

  • 💥間接傷害jiàn jiē shāng hài

    collateral damage — Collateral damage.

    Civilians suffered collateral damage in the dispute.

  • 🥚覆巢之下無完卵fù cháo zhī xià wú wán luǎn

    in a great disaster no one can escape unscathed — No one escapes a great disaster unscathed.

    As the banks fell one after another, it became clear that in a great disaster no one escapes unscathed.

Opposite

  • 🛡️毫髮無傷háo fà wú shāng

    unscathed; scathing — Unscathed.

    Both drivers walked away unscathed.
