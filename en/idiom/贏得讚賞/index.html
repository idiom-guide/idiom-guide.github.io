贏得讚賞

yíng dé zàn shǎng

In English

  • earn plaudits from sb

Meaning

Earning praise.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • win praise
  • be applauded

Example

年輕主廚的料理贏得讚賞，評論家紛紛叫好。

The young chef earned plaudits from critics.

Usage

For performances, work and conduct.

Similar

  • ⭐佳評如潮jiā píng rú cháo

    rave reviews; glowing reviews — Rave reviews.

    The novel won rave reviews.

  • 🙇備受推崇bèi shòu tuī chóng

    widely honored/acclaimed — Widely acclaimed.

    The professor's book is widely acclaimed.

Opposite

  • 👃嗤之以鼻chī zhī yǐ bí

    pour scorn on sb; scorn — Pouring scorn on it.

    Experts poured scorn on the miracle diet.

  • 👉千夫所指qiān fū suǒ zhǐ

    universally condemned — Universally condemned.

    The attack was universally condemned.
