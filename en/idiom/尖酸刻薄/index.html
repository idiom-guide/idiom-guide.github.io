4 · Character & Conduct

尖酸刻薄

jiān suān kè bó

In English

  • vitriolic, uncharitable

Meaning

Vitriolic.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • vitriolic
  • uncharitable
  • acid-tongued

Example

評論家尖酸刻薄的評論很不厚道。

The critic's vitriolic review was uncharitable.

Usage

For remarks, reviews and personalities.

Similar

  • 🥀有失厚道yǒu shī hòu dào

    unkind; uncharitable — Uncharitable; milder.

    It was uncharitable to mock her mistake.

  • 😤咄咄逼人duō duō bī rén

    confrontational; pugnacious, aggressive — Pressing hard with words.

    The lawyer's confrontational style made the witness nervous.

Opposite

  • 🙌隱惡揚善yǐn è yáng shàn

    overlook others' faults and praise their virtues — Overlooking faults and praising virtues.

    A good eulogy overlooks faults and praises virtues.

  • 💝好心好意hǎo xīn hǎo yì

    well-meaning; well-intentioned — Well-meaning.

    She was well-meaning but clumsy.
