4 · Pain & Unease

心如槁木

xīn rú gǎo mù

In English

  • dead inside

Meaning

Dead inside.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • numb
  • hollow

Example

失去之後，他覺得心如槁木。

After the loss, he felt dead inside.

Usage

For the aftermath of devastating loss.

Similar

  • 🍂槁木死灰gǎo mù sǐ huī

    utterly lifeless; apathetic — Utterly lifeless.

    After the loss, she looked utterly lifeless.

  • 💨希望幻滅xī wàng huàn miè

    hopes were dashed — Hopes dashed.

    Their hopes were dashed.

Opposite

  • 🔥生命熱情shēng mìng rè qíng

    zest for life — A zest for life.

    Her zest for life inspires everyone around her.

  • 🌬️意氣風發yì qì fēng fā

    be in high spirits; full of vigor — In high spirits.

    The graduates were in high spirits.
