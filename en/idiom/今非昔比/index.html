2 · Change & Fate

今非昔比

jīn fēi xī bǐ

In English

  • things are different now

Meaning

Not what it used to be.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • not what it used to be
  • a far cry from the past

Example

今非昔比，這一帶以前是貧民窟。

Things are different now; this neighborhood used to be a slum.

Usage

Can mean better or worse; context decides.

Similar

  • 🌌物換星移wù huàn xīng yí

    things change — Things change over the years.

    Things change as the years go by; the old market is now a shopping mall.

  • 🎓吳下阿蒙wú xià ā méng

    no longer the same person; not what one used to be — No longer the same person; usually of self-improvement.

    After a year of hard study, he is no longer the same person as before.

Opposite

  • 🗿一成不變yī chéng bù biàn

    unchanging; set in stone — Set in stone.

    The curriculum is not set in stone.

  • 🐂牛牽到北京還是牛niú qiān dào běi jīng hái shì niú

    A leopard cannot change its spots — A leopard can't change its spots.

    He promised to change, but a leopard cannot change its spots.
