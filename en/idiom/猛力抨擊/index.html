猛力抨擊

měng lì pēng jī

In English

  • blistering remarks

Meaning

Blistering criticism.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • tear into
  • go after

Example

參議員對該計畫猛力抨擊。

The senator launched blistering remarks against the plan.

Usage

For politics, commentary and debate.

Similar

  • 💥猛烈抨擊měng liè pēng jī

    lambaste, harsh criticism — Lambasting.

    Critics lambasted the film as shallow.

  • ✒️口誅筆伐kǒu zhū bǐ fá

    pilloried in the press — Pilloried in the press.

    The company was pilloried in the press for the recall.

Opposite

  • 👏大加讚賞dà jiā zàn shǎng

    speak highly of — Speaking highly of.

    Critics spoke highly of the young director's first film.

  • 🎤歌功頌德gē gōng sòng dé

    sing the praises of sb/sth — Singing someone's praises.

    The speeches did nothing but sing the praises of the mayor.
