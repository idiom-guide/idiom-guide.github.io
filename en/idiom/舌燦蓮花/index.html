舌燦蓮花

shé càn lián huā

In English

  • articulate

Meaning

Silver-tongued; eloquent enough to make lotuses bloom.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • silver-tongued
  • have the gift of the gab

Example

那位業務員舌燦蓮花，什麼人都能說動。

The salesman was so articulate that he could talk anyone into buying.

Usage

For sales pitches, debates and persuasion; sometimes hints that it's too good to be true.

Similar

  • 💎妙語如珠miào yǔ rú zhū

    witty; scintillating repartee — Full of witty remarks.

    The speaker was witty, and her talk was full of scintillating repartee.

  • 🍯花言巧語huā yán qiǎo yǔ

    sweet talk; smooth talk — Sweet talk that can't be trusted; negative.

    Don't be taken in by his sweet talk.

Opposite

  • 😶不善言辭bù shàn yán cí

    be poor with words; be a poor speaker — Poor with words.

    He is poor with words, but he is very reliable.

  • 😵語無倫次yǔ wú lún cì

    ramble incoherently — Rambling incoherently.

    He was so nervous that he rambled incoherently.
