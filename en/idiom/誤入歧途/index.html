誤入歧途

wù rù qí tú

In English

  • stray from the path

Meaning

Going astray.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • go astray
  • go off the rails

Example

許多年輕人失業後誤入歧途。

Many young people strayed from the path after losing their jobs.

Usage

For young people, crime and moral decline.

Similar

  • 😵一時糊塗yī shí hú tú

    a lapse of judgment — A lapse of judgment.

    In a lapse of judgment, he signed the form.

Opposite

  • 🛀洗心革面xǐ xīn gé miàn

    turn over a new leaf; reform oneself — Turning over a new leaf.

    After his release he turned over a new leaf and started afresh.

  • 📏循規蹈矩xún guī dǎo jǔ

    by the book; play by the rules — By the book.

    He plays by the rules and never takes risks.
