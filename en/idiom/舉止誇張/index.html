舉止誇張

jǔ zhǐ kuā zhāng

In English

  • theatrical

Meaning

Theatrical.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • over the top
  • melodramatic

Example

他舉止誇張，逗得孩子們很開心。

His theatrical gestures amused the children.

Usage

Neutral for a performing style, or critical of affectation.

Similar

  • 🦩誇張造作kuā zhāng zào zuò

    flamboyant (gesture, lifestyle, clothes) — Flamboyant.

    Her flamboyant gestures drew every eye in the room.

  • 😏裝腔作勢zhuāng qiāng zuò shì

    posturing; putting on airs — Posturing.

    His whole speech was a pompous charade.

Opposite

  • 😐不苟言笑bù gǒu yán xiào

    dour — Dour.

    The dour accountant rarely smiled.

  • 👴一本正經yī běn zhèng jīng

    be dead serious; with a straight face — Dead serious.

    He said with a straight face that there was no work tomorrow.
