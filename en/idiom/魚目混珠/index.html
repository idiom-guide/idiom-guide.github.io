魚目混珠

yú mù hùn zhū

In English

  • pass off fakes as genuine

Meaning

Passing off fakes as genuine.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • sell a pig in a poke
  • knock-offs

Example

有些賣家魚目混珠，以假亂真。

Some sellers pass off fakes as genuine.

Usage

For counterfeits, shoddy goods and impostors.

Similar

  • 💼欺詐腐敗qī zhà fǔ bài

    chicanery and corruption — Fraud and corruption.

    The company's collapse revealed years of chicanery and corruption.

Opposite

  • 💎貨真價實huò zhēn jià shí

    the genuine article — The genuine article.

    Customers want to know the product is the genuine article.

  • 💎真材實料zhēn cái shí liào

    the real McCoy; the real deal — The real deal.

    This shop sells the real deal.
