紙醉金迷

zhǐ zuì jīn mí

In English

  • a life of decadence
  • extravagant and dissolute living

Meaning

A life of decadence.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • la dolce vita

Example

這部電影描繪紙醉金迷的生活。

The film portrays a life of decadence.

Usage

For depicting lavish, dissolute lifestyles or eras.

Similar

  • 🏮燈紅酒綠dēng hóng jiǔ lǜ

    feasting and pleasure seeking — Feasting and pleasure-seeking.

    The district is a place of feasting and pleasure seeking.

  • 🍾縱情享樂zòng qíng xiǎng lè

    indulge oneself; live it up — Living it up.

    They indulged themselves at the festival.

Opposite

  • 🌾樸實無華pǔ shí wú huá

    unsophisticated — Plain and unadorned.

    The village church is unsophisticated, yet beautiful.

  • 🐂吃苦耐勞chī kǔ nài láo

    workhorse — Hardworking and uncomplaining.

    He is a workhorse who never complains.
