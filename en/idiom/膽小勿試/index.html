膽小勿試

dǎn xiǎo wù shì

In English

  • not for the faint of heart

Meaning

Not for the faint of heart.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • not for the timid
  • only for the brave

Example

這座雲霄飛車膽小勿試。

This roller coaster is not for the faint of heart.

Usage

Common in ads for rides, spicy food and horror films; sounds like a dare.

Similar

  • ⚠️風險自負fēng xiǎn zì fù

    at one's (own) peril — At one's own risk.

    Swimming beyond the buoys is at your own peril.

  • 🙈心驚膽戰xīn jīng dǎn zhàn

    frightened — Terrified; 膽小勿試 is a warning in advance.

    We were frightened by the loud thunder.

Opposite

  • 🖐️易如反掌yì rú fǎn zhǎng

    has never been so easy — As easy as turning over one's hand.

    With the new software, filing taxes has never been so easy.

  • 🧁輕而易舉qīng ér yì jǔ

    be a piece of cake — As easy as pie.

    For him, this is a piece of cake.
