沸沸揚揚

fèi fèi yáng yáng

In English

  • acrimonious (dispute)

Meaning

The talk of the town; in an uproar.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • the talk of the town
  • in an uproar
  • acrimonious

Example

預算的爭議吵得沸沸揚揚，成了全城的話題。

The acrimonious dispute over the budget has been the talk of the town.

Usage

For rumors, scandals and disputes that have blown up; often after 傳得, 吵得 or 鬧得.

Similar

  • 💬眾說紛紜zhòng shuō fēn yún

    opinions vary widely — Opinions vary widely.

    Opinions vary widely on what caused the blackout.

  • 📰輿論譁然yú lùn huá rán

    a public uproar — Public outcry; about the public's reaction.

    The leaked memo caused a public uproar.

Opposite

  • 🦗鴉雀無聲yā què wú shēng

    be dead silent; you could hear a pin drop — Dead silence.

    After the result was announced, the hall fell dead silent.

  • 🐁默默無聞mò mò wú wén

    be unknown; toil in obscurity — Unknown and unnoticed.

    He toiled in obscurity for years until that film brought him attention.
