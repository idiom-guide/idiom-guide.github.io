口齒留香

kǒu chǐ liú xiāng

In English

  • lingering aftertaste
  • linger on the palate

Meaning

A fragrance that stays in the mouth after eating or drinking.Positive · Formal

Example

這杯烏龍茶入喉甘潤，口齒留香。

The oolong is smooth going down, and its fragrance lingers on the palate.

Usage

For tea, wine, coffee and spices. Leans toward the lingering scent, while the idiom for endless aftertaste is broader.

Similar

  • 😋回味無窮huí wèi wú qióng

    finger licking (good) — The aftertaste is worth savoring; broader in range.

    The soup was finger-licking good.

  • 👃香氣撲鼻xiāng qì pū bí

    aromatic; a heady aroma — A strong fragrance; about the first whiff.

    The moment she lifted the lid, a heady aroma of ginger duck filled the kitchen.

Opposite

  • 😑味同嚼蠟wèi tóng jiáo là

    bland; insipid — Bland and joyless, like chewing wax.

    Without any seasoning, the soup was bland and insipid.
