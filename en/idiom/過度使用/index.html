過度使用

guò dù shǐ yòng

In English

  • overutilized = overused

Meaning

Overused.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • overused
  • overutilized
  • worn out

Example

廣告裡「超讚」這個詞被過度使用了。

The word "awesome" is overutilized in advertising.

Usage

For words, resources, equipment or medicine.

Similar

  • 📻老調重彈lǎo diào chóng tán

    rehash — Trotting out the same old tune.

    The speech just rehashed old arguments.

  • 🍪千篇一律qiān piān yī lǜ

    be formulaic; follow the same pattern — All the same.

    These ads are all formulaic and utterly unoriginal.

Opposite

  • ✋適可而止shì kě ér zhǐ

    know when to stop — Knowing when to stop.

    Criticism is fine, but know when to stop.

  • 📦未盡其用wèi jìn qí yòng

    underused; underutilized — Underused.

    The new gym is underutilized on weekdays.
