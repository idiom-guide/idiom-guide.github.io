火力全開

huǒ lì quán kāi

In English

  • be firing on all cylinders
  • do sth at full throttle

Meaning

Firing on all cylinders.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • firing on all cylinders
  • at full throttle
  • all guns blazing

Example

下半場球隊火力全開。

The team was firing on all cylinders in the second half.

Usage

Originally military; now for sport, work or criticism.

Similar

  • 🏃全力以赴quán lì yǐ fù

    go all out — Going all out.

    The whole team went all out to finish before the deadline.

  • ⚾全力出擊quán lì chū jī

    swing for the fences, full throttle — Swinging for the fences.

    In the last round, he swung for the fences.

Opposite

  • 🥱無精打采wú jīng dǎ cǎi

    listless; lethargic — Listless.

    He looked listless after the long trip.

  • 🛑半途而廢bàn tú ér fèi

    give up halfway — Giving up halfway.

    Don't give up halfway.
