俗豔浮誇

sú yàn fú kuā

In English

  • meretricious

Meaning

Gaudy.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • gaudy
  • tacky

Example

飯店大廳俗豔浮誇，到處都漆成金色。

The hotel lobby was meretricious, with gold paint on everything.

Usage

For décor, clothing and design.

Similar

  • 🦩誇張造作kuā zhāng zào zuò

    flamboyant (gesture, lifestyle, clothes) — Flamboyant.

    Her flamboyant gestures drew every eye in the room.

  • 🎁華而不實huá ér bù shí

    overhyped; frivolous — All show, no substance.

    The ad was flashy, but the product turned out to be frivolous and overhyped.

Opposite

  • 🌾樸實無華pǔ shí wú huá

    unsophisticated — Plain and unadorned.

    The village church is unsophisticated, yet beautiful.

  • 🌿渾然天成hún rán tiān chéng

    seamless; naturally perfect — Naturally perfect.

    The garden looks seamless, as if nature made it.
