無憂無慮

wú yōu wú lǜ

In English

  • blithely
  • with a blithe disregard for

Meaning

Carefree.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • carefree
  • without a care in the world

Example

孩子們在陽光下無憂無慮地玩耍。

The kids played blithely in the sun.

Usage

For children or holidays; 'blithe disregard' in English is negative, meaning heedless.

Similar

  • 🕊️無拘無束wú jū wú shù

    unfettered by — Unfettered.

    On the road, he felt unfettered by rules.

  • 😄樂在其中lè zài qí zhōng

    find great pleasure in — Finding great pleasure in it.

    He finds great pleasure in gardening.

Opposite

  • 😔心事重重xīn shì chóng chóng

    preoccupied; weighed down by worries — Weighed down by worries.

    He seemed preoccupied all day.

  • 🦋惴惴不安zhuì zhuì bù ān

    feel unsettled; have butterflies in one's stomach — Butterflies in one's stomach.

    She felt butterflies in her stomach before the interview.
