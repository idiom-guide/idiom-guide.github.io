承先啟後

chéng xiān qǐ hòu

In English

  • bridge the past and the future

Meaning

Bridging past and future.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • carry on the legacy
  • link the old and the new

Example

這場展覽承先啟後，連結這項工藝的過去與未來。

The exhibition bridges the past and the future of the craft.

Usage

Formal; for cultural heritage and generational change.

Similar

  • 🧬一脈相承yī mài xiāng chéng

    in an unbroken line — In an unbroken line.

    The two schools of painting descend in an unbroken line.

  • 🏺古為今用gǔ wéi jīn yòng

    adapt the past for the present — Adapting the past for the present.

    The architect adapts the past for the present.

Opposite

  • 🌱數典忘祖shǔ diǎn wàng zǔ

    forget sb's root — Forgetting one's roots.

    To abandon your own language is to forget your roots.
