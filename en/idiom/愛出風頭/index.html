愛出風頭

ài chū fēng tou

In English

  • sb is loud

Meaning

Loving the limelight.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • love the limelight
  • attention-seeking

Example

我表弟很愛出風頭，喜歡當焦點。

My cousin is loud and loves being the center of attention.

Usage

Colloquial; mildly critical.

Similar

  • 🦸逞英雄chěng yīng xióng

    play the hero; show off — Playing the hero.

    Don't play the hero. Call for help.

  • 🎪嘩眾取寵huá zhòng qǔ chǒng

    play to the gallery; sensationalize — Playing to the gallery.

    The mayor's stunts were just playing to the gallery.

Opposite

  • 🐭不出鋒頭bù chū fēng tóu

    unassuming — Unassuming.

    He is an unassuming man who never seeks the spotlight.

  • 🐴深藏不露shēn cáng bù lù

    keep one's talents hidden; be a dark horse — Hidden depths.

    He kept his talents hidden, and nobody knew he was the champion.
