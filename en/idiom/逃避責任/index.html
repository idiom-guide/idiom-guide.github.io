逃避責任

táo bì zé rèn

In English

  • skive v

Meaning

Dodging responsibility.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • shirk responsibility
  • pass the buck

Example

他想逃避責任，不做他那份家事。

He tried to skive off his share of the housework.

Usage

For duties at work, at home or after an accident.

Similar

  • 🛋️怠忽職守dài hū zhí shǒu

    be negligent in one's duties — Negligent in one's duties.

    The guard was fired for being negligent in his duties.

Opposite

  • 🛠️責無旁貸zé wú páng dài

    it's my duty — One's bounden duty.

    Looking after my parents is my duty.

  • 🚪引咎辭職yǐn jiù cí zhí

    resign in disgrace — Resigning to take responsibility.

    The minister resigned in disgrace over the data leak.
