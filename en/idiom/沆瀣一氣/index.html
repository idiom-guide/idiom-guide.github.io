沆瀣一氣

hàng xiè yī qì

In English

  • in cahoots
  • hand in glove
  • collude with each other

Meaning

In cahoots; colluding with each other.Negative · Formal

Example

調查人員發現，檢查員和建商沆瀣一氣。

Investigators found that the inspectors were in cahoots with the developers.

Usage

From a Tang dynasty story about the examiner Cui Hang and his student Cui Xie, a joke on their linked names; now means wrongdoers colluding.

Similar

  • 🦝一丘之貉yī qiū zhī hé

    birds of a feather; cut from the same cloth — Equally bad; about being the same kind rather than colluding.

    Whichever party wins, they're all cut from the same cloth.

  • 🦹狼狽為奸láng bèi wéi jiān

    be in league with each other; gang up to do evil — Partners in crime.

    The two officials were in league with each other and embezzled public funds.

Opposite

  • 🧥兩袖清風liǎng xiù qīng fēng

    be incorruptible; have clean hands — An official with clean hands.

    The old county magistrate was incorruptible all his life.

  • ⚜公正不阿gōng zhèng bù ē

    rectitude; unbiased — Upright and incorruptible.

    The judge was known for his rectitude and never favored the rich.
