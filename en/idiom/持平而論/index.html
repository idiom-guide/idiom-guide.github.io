持平而論

chí píng ér lùn

In English

  • To be fair

Meaning

To be fair; speaking impartially.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • to be fair
  • in all fairness
  • speaking objectively

Example

持平而論，裁判只有一瞬間可以做出判斷。

To be fair, the referee had only a split second to decide.

Usage

Often an aside introducing a more balanced, or contrary, view.

Similar

  • 🧘‍♂️平心而論píng xīn ér lùn

    in all fairness; speaking dispassionately — In all fairness; stresses staying calm.

    Speaking dispassionately, the film's script isn't bad at all.

  • 📂就事論事jiù shì lùn shì

    judge the matter on its own merits; stick to the issue — Sticking to the matter at hand.

    Let's stick to the issue and leave personal grudges out of it.

Opposite

  • 🎧偏聽偏信piān tīng piān xìn

    believe only one side; be swayed by one-sided accounts — Believing only one side.

    The manager believed one side only, which led to a wrong decision.

  • 🥉厚此薄彼hòu cǐ bó bǐ

    play favorites; treat one side better than the other — Treating two sides unequally.

    A teacher shouldn't play favorites; everyone should be treated alike.
