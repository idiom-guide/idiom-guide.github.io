4 · Pain & Unease

非常驚駭

fēi cháng jīng hài

In English

  • shit a brick

Meaning

Jumping out of one's skin.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • jump out of one's skin
  • terrified

Example

警報響起時，他非常驚駭，差點跳起來。

He nearly jumped out of his skin when the alarm went off.

Usage

For sudden frights.

Similar

  • 😨膽戰心驚dǎn zhàn xīn jīng

    tremble with fear — Trembling with fear.

    He trembled with fear in the dark.

  • 😲觸目驚心chù mù jīng xīn

    shocking — Shocking.

    The photos of the flooded town were shocking.

Opposite

  • 😐面不改色miàn bù gǎi sè

    not (even) bat an eyelid — Not batting an eyelid.

    He didn't bat an eyelid when he heard the price.

  • 😎泰然自若tài rán zì ruò

    unfazed — Unfazed.

    She remained unfazed by the critics' attacks.
