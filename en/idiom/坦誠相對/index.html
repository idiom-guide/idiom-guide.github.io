坦誠相對

tǎn chéng xiāng duì

In English

  • level with sb

Meaning

Being completely honest with each other.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • be frank with each other
  • be completely open

Example

我就跟你坦誠相對吧：我們的錢快用完了。

I'll level with you: we are running out of money.

Usage

A state between two parties; common in relationships and negotiations.

Similar

  • 🤲推心置腹tuī xīn zhì fù

    speak from the heart; confide in sb — Opening one's heart to someone.

    After years of friendship, she could confide in him about anything.

  • 🔓開誠布公kāi chéng bù gōng

    in full transparency — Open and frank; stresses transparency.

    The two leaders spoke in full transparency about their differences.

Opposite

  • 🎭虛情假意xū qíng jiǎ yì

    insincere; hollow sentiments — Feigned feelings.

    Her feigned friendliness fooled no one.

  • 😘逢場作戲féng chǎng zuò xì

    dalliance with sb — Just going through the motions.

    It was only a brief dalliance with the actress.
