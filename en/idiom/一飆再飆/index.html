一飆再飆

yī biāo zài biāo

In English

  • surge upon a surge

Meaning

Soaring again and again.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • soar again and again
  • surge upon surge
  • keep skyrocketing

Example

房價漲了又漲，一飆再飆。

House prices soared, then soared again: a surge upon a surge.

Usage

For prices, figures or temperatures.

Similar

  • 🐸大幅增加dà fú zēng jiā

    by leaps and bounds — A sharp increase.

    Sales have grown by leaps and bounds this year.

  • 🚤水漲船高shuǐ zhǎng chuán gāo

    a rising tide lifts all boats — A rising tide lifts all boats.

    When the index rallies, a rising tide lifts all boats.

Opposite

  • 📉一落千丈yī luò qiān zhàng

    plummet; nosedive — Plummeting.

    Sales plummeted after the recall.

  • ↘️每況愈下měi kuàng yù xià

    go from bad to worse — Going downhill.

    The company's finances went from bad to worse.
