危機四伏

wēi jī sì fú

In English

  • be fraught with risks

Meaning

Fraught with risks.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • a minefield
  • perilous

Example

叢林小徑危機四伏。

The jungle path is fraught with risks.

Usage

For environments, markets and situations.

Similar

  • 🕳️防不勝防fáng bù shèng fáng

    be impossible to guard against — Impossible to guard against.

    Scams keep evolving, so it's impossible to guard against them all.

  • 🏰深陷危機shēn xiàn wēi jī

    under siege — Under siege.

    The island nation is under siege from rising seas.

Opposite

  • 🔐萬無一失wàn wú yī shī

    foolproof; failsafe — Foolproof.

    The plan is foolproof.

  • 🕊️長治久安cháng zhì jiǔ ān

    lasting stability — Lasting stability.

    Lasting stability depends on institutions, not on any single leader.
