格格不入

gé gé bù rù

In English

  • sth. be foreign to sb's nature
  • like a fish out of water

Meaning

Like a fish out of water.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • out of place
  • not fit in

Example

在古板的辦公室裡，那位年輕設計師顯得格格不入。

In the stuffy office, the creative young designer felt like a fish out of water.

Usage

For people and their surroundings, or clashing ideas.

Similar

  • 🔀道不同不相為謀dào bù tóng bù xiāng wéi móu

    have nothing in common; go separate ways — Parting ways over different values.

    We want different things, so we should go our separate ways.

  • 🙉話不投機半句多huà bù tóu jī bàn jù duō

    have nothing to say to each other; not see eye to eye — When you don't get along, even half a sentence is too much.

    We don't see eye to eye, so let's call it a day.

Opposite

  • 🍲融合共處róng hé gòng chǔ

    melting pot — A melting pot.

    Our city is a melting pot of cultures.

  • 🤝同道中人tóng dào zhōng rén

    sisters/brothers in arms; welcome to the club — Kindred spirits.

    You like old maps too? Welcome to the club!
