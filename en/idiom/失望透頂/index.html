4 · Pain & Unease

失望透頂

shī wàng tòu dǐng

In English

  • huge disappointment
  • underwhelmed

Meaning

A huge disappointment.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • utterly disappointed

Example

這個結局讓人失望透頂。

The ending was a huge disappointment.

Usage

Stronger than 大失所望.

Similar

  • 😞大失所望dà shī suǒ wàng

    sorely disappointed; let down — Sorely disappointed.

    We were sorely disappointed by the ending.

  • 😢令人沮喪lìng rén jǔ sàng

    disheartening — Disheartening.

    The long waiting list is disheartening.

Opposite

  • 🎯不負眾望bù fù zhòng wàng

    live up to sb's name/reputation/expectation/billing — Living up to expectations.

    The young goalkeeper lived up to expectations.

  • 🤩歎為觀止tàn wéi guān zhǐ

    breathtaking; awe-inspiring — Breathtaking.

    The view from the summit was breathtaking.
