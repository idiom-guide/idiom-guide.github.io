夢寐以求

mèng mèi yǐ qiú

In English

  • dream
  • long-sought

Meaning

Longed for even in one's sleep; a goal wanted for a very long time.Positive · General

Example

能進入這家公司工作，是他夢寐以求的事。

Working at this company is a dream come true for him.

Usage

Mostly an attributive before nouns such as job, opportunity, dream or prize, as in a long-cherished chance. It expresses intense longing and is positive in tone.

Similar

  • 🤩求之不得qiú zhī bù dé

    couldn't ask for more; more than welcome — Something one would gladly accept; often used to welcome an offer.

    A free ticket to the concert? More than welcome!

  • 🌆朝思暮想zhāo sī mù xiǎng

    think of day and night; yearn for — Thinking of it from morning to night; about longing, often for a person or hometown.

    After twenty years, he finally returned to the hometown he had yearned for day and night.

Opposite

  • 🏃避之惟恐不及bì zhī wéi kǒng bù jí

    avoid like the plague — Avoiding it like the plague; strongly averse.

    After the argument, he avoided her like the plague.

  • 🙄不屑一顧bù xiè yī gù

    be dismissive of sth — Too disdainful to give it a glance.

    The critic was dismissive of the new play.
