恕我直言

shù wǒ zhí yán

In English

  • with all due respect
  • frankly speaking

Meaning

With all due respect.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • no offense, but

Example

恕我直言，這個計畫有幾個問題。

With all due respect, I think the plan has a few problems.

Usage

A polite opener usually followed by criticism; in English, "with all due respect" also signals disagreement.

Similar

  • 📢直言不諱zhí yán bù huì

    speak frankly; not mince words — Speaking bluntly; an attitude, not an opener.

    She spoke frankly and pointed out the flaws in the plan.

  • 👨‍🎤坦率直言tǎn shuài zhí yán

    give sb straight talk about sth — Speaking frankly; more positive.

    My coach gave me straight talk about my chances of making the team.

Opposite

  • ↪️拐彎抹角guǎi wān mò jiǎo

    beat around the bush; obliquely — Beating around the bush.

    Stop beating around the bush and tell me what happened.

  • 😬支吾其詞zhī wú qí cí

    hem and haw; stammer evasively — Hemming and hawing.

    He hemmed and hawed when asked about the money.
