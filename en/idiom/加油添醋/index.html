加油添醋

jiā yóu tiān cù

In English

  • add(pour) fuel to(on) the fire

Meaning

Embellishing the story.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • embellish
  • embroider the story

Example

別再重複那個謠言加油添醋了。

Don't add fuel to the fire by repeating the rumor.

Usage

Colloquial; for retelling, gossip and news.

Similar

  • 🔥搧風點火shān fēng diǎn huǒ

    fan the flames of — Fanning the flames.

    The tabloid fanned the flames of the dispute.

  • 🐍誇大不實kuā dà bù shí

    exaggerate; snake oil — Snake oil.

    The ads for the miracle pill were exaggerated snake oil.

Opposite

  • 🕊️調解爭端tiáo jiě zhēng duān

    pour oil on troubled waters — Pouring oil on troubled waters.

    The mayor poured oil on troubled waters by meeting both sides.

  • ⚛實事求是shí shì qiú shì

    seek truth from facts; be down-to-earth — Seeking truth from facts.

    Research should seek truth from facts, not start from a conclusion and look for evidence.
