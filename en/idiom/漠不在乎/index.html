漠不在乎

mò bù zài hū

In English

  • nonchalant

Meaning

Nonchalant.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • indifferent
  • shrug it off

Example

他聳聳肩，一副漠不在乎的樣子。

He shrugged with a nonchalant air.

Usage

For attitudes and expressions.

Similar

  • 😑漠不關心mò bù guān xīn

    uncaring — Uncaring.

    The landlord was uncaring about his tenants' complaints.

  • 😪漫不經心màn bù jīng xīn

    be offhand; do sth carelessly — Offhand.

    He answered offhandedly, as if he couldn't care less.

Opposite

  • ⚖️患得患失huàn dé huàn shī

    be obsessed with sth; care too much about sth — Anxious about gains and losses.

    Don't be obsessed with winning or losing; just enjoy the game.

  • 👨‍💼認真看待rèn zhēn kàn dài

    take sth seriously — Taking it seriously.

    You should take safety rules seriously.
