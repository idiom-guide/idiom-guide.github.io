坦率直言

tǎn shuài zhí yán

In English

  • give sb straight talk about sth

Meaning

Speaking frankly and directly.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • give sb straight talk
  • speak frankly
  • be outspoken

Example

教練坦率直言，告訴我入選球隊的機會。

My coach gave me straight talk about my chances of making the team.

Usage

Positive; stresses sincerity rather than rudeness.

Similar

  • 📢直言不諱zhí yán bù huì

    speak frankly; not mince words — Speaking bluntly, not fearing offense.

    She spoke frankly and pointed out the flaws in the plan.

  • ⚡心直口快xīn zhí kǒu kuài

    straight shooter — Outspoken by nature.

    Her quick tongue sometimes gets her into trouble, but at least she is a straight shooter.

Opposite

  • ↪️拐彎抹角guǎi wān mò jiǎo

    beat around the bush; obliquely — Talking in circles.

    Stop beating around the bush and tell me what happened.

  • 🌁含糊其詞hán hú qí cí

    talk in generalities; be coy with sb — Being deliberately vague.

    Asked about the budget, the spokesman talked in generalities.
