名留青史

míng liú qīng shǐ

In English

  • make history

Meaning

Going down in history.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • go down in history
  • be remembered for ages

Example

這位工程師將因這座橋名留青史。

The engineer will make history with this bridge.

Usage

青史 means history books, from the bamboo slips once used for records.

Similar

  • 📚載入史冊zǎi rù shǐ cè

    go down in history — Going down in the history books.

    The 1969 moon landing will go down in the history books.

  • 🏛️遺風永存yí fēng yǒng cún

    leave a legacy that lives on — A legacy that lives on.

    The great teacher left a legacy that lives on.

Opposite

  • 👻銷聲匿跡xiāo shēng nì jì

    off the grid; nowhere to be found — Vanishing without a trace.

    After the scandal, the actor went off the grid.
