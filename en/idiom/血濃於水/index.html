11 · Family & Kinship

血濃於水

xuè nóng yú shuǐ

In English

  • blood is thicker than water

Meaning

Blood is thicker than water.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • family comes first
  • kinship

Example

他們常吵架，但血濃於水。

They argue a lot, but blood is thicker than water.

Usage

For ties between family members or compatriots.

Similar

  • 👦兄友弟恭xiōng yǒu dì gōng

    brotherly harmony — Brotherly harmony.

    The household was known for its brotherly harmony.

Opposite

  • 😡反目成仇fǎn mù chéng chóu

    fall out and become enemies — Falling out and becoming enemies.

    The business partners fell out and became enemies.

  • ✂️恩斷義絕ēn duàn yì jué

    cut all ties — Cutting all ties.

    After the betrayal, she cut all ties with him.
