不證自明

bù zhèng zì míng

In English

  • self-evident

Meaning

Self-evident; needs no proof.Neutral · Formal

Example

對大多數人來說，教育的價值不證自明。

To most of us, the value of education is self-evident.

Usage

Formal or academic; used for axioms and widely accepted truths.

Similar

  • 🤫不言而喻bù yán ér yù

    self-evident — Understood without being said.

    It is self-evident that a healthy diet leads to a healthier life.

  • 👀顯而易見xiǎn ér yì jiàn

    beyond obvious — Plain to see; about facts being obvious.

    It was beyond obvious that he wasn't listening.

Opposite

  • 🤨莫名其妙mò míng qí miào

    inexplicable; baffling — Baffling; makes no sense.

    His sudden anger was inexplicable to everyone in the room.

  • 🌌難以理解nán yǐ lǐ jiě

    unfathomable — Hard to understand without explanation.

    Why she quit such a good job is unfathomable to me.
