投石問路

tóu shí wèn lù

In English

  • put a toe in the water
  • a tentative step toward

Meaning

Testing the waters.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • test the waters
  • fly a kite

Example

公司先開一家小店投石問路。

The company put a toe in the water by opening a small shop first.

Usage

For tentative moves in business or politics; neutral.

Similar

  • 🥁旁敲側擊páng qiāo cè jī

    probe — Probing indirectly.

    He probed gently to find out whether she planned to quit.

  • 🚶謹慎前進jǐn shèn qián jìn

    proceed with caution — Proceeding with caution.

    On the icy road, we proceeded with caution.

Opposite

  • 🎲孤注一擲gū zhù yī zhì

    a desperate play — A desperate all-or-nothing bet.

    Selling his house to fund the film was a desperate play.

  • ⚡不假思索bù jiǎ sī suǒ

    on the spur of the moment; on a whim — Without a second thought.

    On the spur of the moment, he jumped into the river to help.
