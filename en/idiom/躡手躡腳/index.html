躡手躡腳

niè shǒu niè jiǎo

In English

  • tiptoe
  • move stealthily

Meaning

Treading very lightly with hands and feet: careful not to make a sound.Neutral · General

Example

她躡手躡腳地走出房間，怕吵醒寶寶。

She tiptoed out so as not to wake the baby.

Usage

Can be considerate (not wanting to disturb) or sneaky (not wanting to be caught), depending on context. Usually followed by a marker for manner.

Similar

  • 🐾輕手輕腳qīng shǒu qīng jiǎo

    tiptoe; move softly — Moving very lightly; about consideration.

    So as not to wake the baby, he tiptoed out of the room.

  • 🤫偷偷摸摸tōu tōu mō mō

    surreptitious — Sneaky and secretive; about stealth.

    She made a surreptitious call from the hallway.

Opposite

  • 🐧大搖大擺dà yáo dà bǎi

    swagger; strut — Walking as if no one else is there; about showiness.

    He swaggered into the meeting as if he owned the place.
