好人有好報

hǎo rén yǒu hǎo bào

In English

  • good/bad karma

Meaning

Good things happen to good people.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • good things come to those who do good
  • what goes around comes around
  • good karma

Example

她幫過鎮上每個人，如今大家都來幫她，這就是好人有好報。

She helped everyone in town, and now they are all helping her. That's good karma at work.

Usage

Full saying: 好人有好報，惡人有惡報.

Similar

  • 🏵善有善報shàn yǒu shàn bào

    good deeds are rewarded — The more formal version, often paired with 惡有惡報.

    Good deeds are rewarded; his efforts finally paid off.

  • 🧿吉人天相jí rén tiān xiàng

    good people are watched over by heaven — Good people are protected by heaven; often said to comfort.

    Good people are watched over by heaven; he will surely get through this safely.

Opposite

  • ♻惡有惡報è yǒu è bào

    the wicked get what they deserve; what goes around comes around — Evil is repaid with evil.

    Those who do evil will in the end get what they deserve.

  • 🦹‍♂️惡人當道è rén dāng dào

    the wicked are in power — Bad people in power while the good suffer.

    The world has gone downhill and the wicked are in power.
