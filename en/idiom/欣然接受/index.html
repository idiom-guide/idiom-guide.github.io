3 · Joy & Satisfaction

欣然接受

xīn rán jiē shòu

In English

  • with alacrity

Meaning

Gladly accepting.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • gladly accept
  • jump at

Example

她欣然接受了邀請。

She accepted the invitation with alacrity.

Usage

For invitations, offers and positions.

Similar

  • 😊樂意之至lè yì zhī zhì

    gladly; be more than happy to — More than happy to.

    Would you help me move? I'd be more than happy to.

  • 🍽️來者不拒lái zhě bù jù

    nothing's off menu — Nothing's off the menu.

    At our open-mic night, everyone is welcome and nothing's off the menu.

Opposite

  • 😒不情願bù qíng yuàn

    half-hearted (negotiation) — Half-hearted.

    After a half-hearted negotiation, the two sides signed nothing.

  • 👃嗤之以鼻chī zhī yǐ bí

    pour scorn on sb; scorn — Pouring scorn on it.

    Experts poured scorn on the miracle diet.
