靈光一閃

líng guāng yī shǎn

In English

  • have an epiphany

Meaning

A flash of inspiration.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • a flash of inspiration
  • a lightbulb moment
  • strike sb

Example

洗碗的時候，他靈光一閃，想通了設計上的難題。

While washing the dishes, he had an epiphany and solved the design problem.

Usage

Also 靈光乍現; stresses the instant.

Similar

  • 💫福至心靈fú zhì xīn líng

    serendipity — Inspiration that comes with good luck.

    Serendipity struck when I found the book I needed on the way home.

  • 🪔靈機一動líng jī yī dòng

    have a bright idea; it suddenly occurs to sb — A bright idea strikes.

    A bright idea struck him, and he turned the carton into a small bookshelf.

Opposite

  • 🌑毫無頭緒háo wú tóu xù

    grope in the dark; have no clue — Having no clue where to start.

    I have no clue where to begin.

  • 🤷百思不解bǎi sī bù jiě

    flummoxed — Unable to figure it out.

    I'm flummoxed by why the system keeps crashing.
