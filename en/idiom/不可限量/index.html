不可限量

bù kě xiàn liàng

In English

  • off the charts
  • unbridled = unlimited, uncontrolled

Meaning

Limitless.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • unbridled
  • limitless

Example

這項技術的潛力不可限量。

The potential of this technology is off the charts.

Usage

Often 前途不可限量 'a bright future' or 潛力不可限量 'limitless potential'.

Similar

  • 🦅鵬程萬里péng chéng wàn lǐ

    a bright future ahead — A bright future ahead.

    To the graduates: you have a bright future ahead.

  • 🌈前程似錦qián chéng sì jǐn

    a bright future — A bright future.

    We wish you a bright future.

Opposite

  • 🏞️江河日下jiāng hé rì xià

    in steady decline — In steady decline.

    The industry has been in steady decline.

  • 🔋欲振乏力yù zhèn fá lì

    lackluster — Lackluster.

    The economy is lackluster and can't recover its strength.
