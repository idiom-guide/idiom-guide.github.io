鋌而走險

tǐng ér zǒu xiǎn

In English

  • put one's neck on the line
  • take a desperate gamble

Meaning

Taking a desperate gamble.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • resort to desperate measures

Example

他急需現金，於是鋌而走險。

Desperate for cash, he took a desperate gamble.

Usage

For crime or speculation; often after 走投無路 (having nowhere to turn).

Similar

  • ☠️冒險玩命mào xiǎn wán mìng

    tempt fate — Tempting fate.

    Driving through this storm is tempting fate.

  • 🎲孤注一擲gū zhù yī zhì

    a desperate play — A desperate all-or-nothing bet.

    Selling his house to fund the film was a desperate play.

Opposite

  • 🐾謹慎行動jǐn shèn xíng dòng

    tread carefully — Treading carefully.

    In unfamiliar markets, it pays to tread carefully.

  • ⛑穩紮穩打wěn zhā wěn dǎ

    build steadily; play it safe and steady — Steady and sure.

    A start-up should build steadily and not rush to expand.
