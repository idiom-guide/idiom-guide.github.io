達成協議

dá chéng xié yì

In English

  • seal the deal

Meaning

Reaching an agreement.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • reach an agreement
  • strike a deal

Example

經過三天談判，雙方終於達成協議。

After three days of talks, the two sides finally sealed the deal.

Usage

For negotiations, partnerships and settlements.

Similar

  • 🤝一言為定yī yán wéi dìng

    deal — It's a deal.

    Ten o'clock tomorrow? Deal.

  • 🤝化解歧見huà jiě qí jiàn

    settle disagreements — Settling disagreements.

    The mediator helped them settle their disagreements.

Opposite

  • 🗨各執一詞gè zhí yī cí

    each tell a different story; stick to one's own version — Each side sticking to its own story.

    The two men each gave a different account of what happened, leaving the police unable to decide.

  • 🤺針鋒相對zhēn fēng xiāng duì

    be diametrically opposed; go head to head — Diametrically opposed.

    The two candidates were diametrically opposed on taxes.
