另立新主

lìng lì xīn zhǔ

In English

  • fly a new flag

Meaning

Switching allegiance to a new leader.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • switch allegiance
  • pledge loyalty to a new leader

Example

對舊領袖失望後，叛軍決定另立新主。

Disappointed with the old leaders, the rebels decided to fly a new flag.

Usage

For political or organizational splits.

Similar

  • 🔪一刀兩斷yī dāo liǎng duàn

    make a clean break — Making a clean break.

    He decided to make a clean break with his old life.

  • 🔀分道揚鑣fēn dào yáng biāo

    part ways — Parting ways.

    After the project, the partners decided to part ways.

Opposite

  • 🐕忠心耿耿zhōng xīn gěng gěng

    be loyal to — Loyal to the core.

    The old servant was loyal to the family until the end.

  • 💍臣服於…chén fú yú…

    kiss sb's ring — Kissing someone's ring.

    Smaller states had to kiss the empire's ring to stay safe.
