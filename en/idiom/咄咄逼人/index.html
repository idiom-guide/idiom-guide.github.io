咄咄逼人

duō duō bī rén

In English

  • confrontational
  • pugnacious, aggressive
  • harsh, aggressive

Meaning

Aggressive; confrontational.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • aggressive
  • pushy

Example

律師咄咄逼人的問話方式，讓證人很緊張。

The lawyer's confrontational style made the witness nervous.

Usage

For attitudes, tone or questioning.

Similar

  • 🚜盛氣凌人shèng qì líng rén

    steamroller — Steamrolling others.

    The new manager steamrolled over every objection.

  • 😈毫不留情háo bù liú qíng

    ruthless; without mercy — Merciless.

    The critic was ruthless in his review.

Opposite

  • 🎩彬彬有禮bīn bīn yǒu lǐ

    courteous; well-mannered — Polite and courteous.

    The waiter was courteous throughout.

  • 😚和顏悅色hé yán yuè sè

    be kind and friendly; with a pleasant look — Warm and friendly.

    He always treats customers with a kind and friendly look.
