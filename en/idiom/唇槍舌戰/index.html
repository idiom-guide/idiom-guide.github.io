4 · Words & Quarrels

唇槍舌戰

chún qiāng shé zhàn

In English

  • a war of words
  • verbal sparring

Meaning

A war of words.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • a heated debate

Example

這場辯論變成一場唇槍舌戰。

The debate turned into a war of words.

Usage

For debates, negotiations and arguments.

Similar

  • 🥊相互攻訐xiāng hù gōng jié

    be at each other's throats — At each other's throats.

    The two partners are at each other's throats.

  • 🤺互相挖苦hù xiāng wā kǔ

    trade barbs with sb. over sth — Trading barbs.

    The two comedians traded barbs over their ratings.

Opposite

  • 🤝握手言和wò shǒu yán hé

    shake hands and make peace — Shaking hands and making peace.

    The two captains shook hands and made peace.

  • 🧖心平氣和xīn píng qì hé

    be calm and composed; keep one's temper — Calm and even-tempered.

    Let's talk calmly and keep our tempers in check.
