舞文弄墨

wǔ wén nòng mò

In English

  • wordsmith
  • fiddle at writing
  • have a way with words

Meaning

A wordsmith.Neutral · General

Example

她擅長舞文弄墨，文字很有魅力。

She is a wordsmith with a way with words.

Usage

Originally of twisting legal texts; now of writing, often modest or teasing.

Similar

  • 🖋️妙筆生花miào bǐ shēng huā

    brilliant writing — Brilliant writing.

    The column is known for its brilliant writing.

  • ⛲文思泉湧wén sī quán yǒng

    a flood of ideas; be bursting with ideas — Bursting with ideas.

    He was bursting with ideas at midnight.

Opposite

  • 😶不善言辭bù shàn yán cí

    be poor with words; be a poor speaker — Not good with words.

    He is poor with words, but he is very reliable.
