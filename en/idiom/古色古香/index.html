古色古香

gǔ sè gǔ xiāng

In English

  • of antique beauty

Meaning

Of antique beauty.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • old-world charm
  • quaint

Example

這間茶館古色古香。

The tea house has an antique beauty.

Usage

For buildings, furnishings and streets.

Similar

  • 🖼️如詩如畫rú shī rú huà

    picturesque; idyllic — Picturesque.

    The village is picturesque.

Opposite

  • 📼陳舊過時chén jiù guò shí

    obsolescence — Obsolete.

    The software suffers from obsolescence.
