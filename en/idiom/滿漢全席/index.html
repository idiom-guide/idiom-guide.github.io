滿漢全席

mǎn hàn quán xí

In English

  • imperial banquet
  • a feast fit for an emperor

Meaning

A huge court banquet from the Qing era that combined Manchu and Han dishes. Now used for any very rich spread.Neutral · General

Example

媽媽煮了滿滿一桌菜，簡直像滿漢全席。

My mother cooked so much that dinner was an imperial banquet.

Usage

Today mostly a playful exaggeration for a table loaded with dishes. The original was a grand Qing-era banquet with a huge range of courses.

Similar

  • 🦞山珍海味shān zhēn hǎi wèi

    delicacies; a lavish spread — Prized ingredients and a rich spread; leans toward rarity.

    The wedding table groaned with delicacies from land and sea.

  • 🛍️琳琅滿目lín láng mǎn mù

    a superb collection of beautiful things; a dazzling line-up — So many things to see; not limited to food.

    The market offered a dazzling lineup of handmade crafts.

Opposite

  • 🥣粗茶淡飯cū chá dàn fàn

    plain fare; simple meals — Plain, simple meals; leans toward a frugal life.

    He lived on plain fare all his life and was perfectly content.

  • 🍚家常便飯jiā cháng biàn fàn

    daily occurrence — An ordinary home meal; plain and everyday.

    Power cuts are a daily occurrence here.
