令人激賞

lìng rén jī shǎng

In English

  • commendable
  • admirable

Meaning

Highly impressive; earns great admiration.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • be admirable
  • be commendable
  • be impressive

Example

她對學生的耐心令人激賞。

Her patience with the students is truly admirable.

Usage

For performance or character; 激賞 is stronger than 欣賞.

Similar

  • 🤩歎為觀止tàn wéi guān zhǐ

    breathtaking; awe-inspiring — Breathtaking; stresses amazement.

    The view from the summit was breathtaking.

  • 👏大加讚賞dà jiā zàn shǎng

    speak highly of — Heaping praise on.

    Critics spoke highly of the young director's first film.

Opposite

  • 🤏不值一提bù zhí yī tí

    not worth mentioning; hardly worth a mention — Not worth mentioning.

    Compared with his effort, these results are not worth mentioning.

  • 🥱平淡無奇píng dàn wú qí

    run-of-the-mill — Plain and unremarkable.

    It was just a run-of-the-mill day at the office.
