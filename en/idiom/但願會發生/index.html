但願會發生

dàn yuàn huì fā shēng

In English

  • from your lips/mouth to God's ear

Meaning

From your lips to God's ear.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • from your lips to God's ear
  • fingers crossed
  • let's hope so

Example

「真希望決賽能贏。」「但願會發生。」

"I hope we win the final." "From your lips to God's ear."

Usage

Colloquial; a reply to someone's hopeful words or good wishes.

Similar

  • 🌠心想事成xīn xiǎng shì chéng

    may all your wishes come true — May all your wishes come true.

    Happy birthday, and may all your wishes come true.

Opposite

  • 💨希望幻滅xī wàng huàn miè

    hopes were dashed — Hopes dashed.

    Their hopes were dashed.
