見識卓越

jiàn shí zhuó yuè

In English

  • savvy read
  • visionary

Meaning

Visionary insight.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • insightful

Example

這位年輕投資人對市場見識卓越。

The young investor has a visionary grasp of the market.

Usage

For investors, leaders and thinkers.

Similar

  • 🦉先見之明xiān jiàn zhī míng

    be prescient of; proved prescient — Proved prescient.

    Her decision to sell early proved prescient.

  • 👁️獨具慧眼dú jù huì yǎn

    have unique insight into sth — A unique eye.

    The editor had unique insight into what readers wanted.

Opposite

  • 🚇目光短淺mù guāng duǎn qiǎn

    short-sighted — Short-sighted.

    The decision was short-sighted.

  • 🌓一知半解yī zhī bàn jiě

    have only a smattering of knowledge; know just a little — A little knowledge.

    He has only a smattering of knowledge about the issue, yet loves to give his opinion.
