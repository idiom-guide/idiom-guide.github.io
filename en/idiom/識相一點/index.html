識相一點

shí xiàng yī diǎn

In English

  • read the room

Meaning

Read the room.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • take the hint
  • know one's place

Example

識相一點，別拿裁員開玩笑。

Read the room, and don't joke about the layoffs.

Usage

Colloquial, a warning or reminder; not very polite.

Similar

  • 🎏觀察風向guān chá fēng xiàng

    see where the wind is blowing — Seeing which way the wind blows.

    Before speaking out, the politician saw where the wind was blowing.

  • ✋適可而止shì kě ér zhǐ

    know when to stop — Knowing when to stop.

    Criticism is fine, but know when to stop.

Opposite

  • 🎏不識時務bù shí shí wù

    (not) know/see which way the wind blows — Out of touch with the times.

    He didn't know which way the wind blew and argued with the boss.

  • 🐽得寸進尺dé cùn jìn chǐ

    give an inch and take a mile — Give an inch and they take a mile.

    Don't push your luck; I have already given way a lot.
