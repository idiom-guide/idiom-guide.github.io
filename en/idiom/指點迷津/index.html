指點迷津

zhǐ diǎn mí jīn

In English

  • point sb in the right direction

Meaning

Showing someone the way when they are lost.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • show sb the way
  • give guidance

Example

感謝老師在我最迷惘時指點迷津。

My mentor pointed me in the right direction when I was lost.

Usage

Used of mentors, elders or sages; also in requests such as 請您指點迷津.

Similar

  • 💥醍醐灌頂tí hú guàn dǐng

    have a sudden enlightenment; be struck by sudden insight — A sudden enlightenment.

    His advice struck me like a sudden enlightenment.

  • 📞當頭棒喝dāng tóu bàng hè

    a wake-up call — A wake-up call.

    That failure was a wake-up call for him.

Opposite

  • 🛤️誤入歧途wù rù qí tú

    stray from the path — Going astray.

    Many young people strayed from the path after losing their jobs.

  • 🌫️一頭霧水yī tóu wù shuǐ

    baffled; at a loss — Completely confused; the state of being lost.

    I'm baffled by this form.
