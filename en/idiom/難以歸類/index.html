難以歸類

nán yǐ guī lèi

In English

  • beyond categorization
  • description

Meaning

Defying classification.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • defy classification
  • hard to pigeonhole
  • fall between categories

Example

她的音樂難以歸類，既像爵士，又像民謠。

Her music is beyond categorization; it is part jazz, part folk.

Usage

For art, people and products; sometimes a compliment (unique).

Similar

  • 🐟不倫不類bù lún bù lèi

    neither fish nor fowl; a hodgepodge — Neither one thing nor the other; usually negative.

    The building is a hodgepodge: neither fish nor fowl.

  • ◾灰色地帶huī sè dì dài

    a gray area — A gray area.

    The law leaves a gray area around this kind of conduct.

Opposite

  • 🐼黑白分明hēi bái fēn míng

    be clear-cut; be black and white — Clear-cut, black and white.

    He sees things as clear-cut and leaves no gray area.

  • 🔘非此即彼fēi cǐ jí bǐ

    either-or; one or the other — Either this or that, no third option.

    Don't see it as either-or; there is a third possibility.
