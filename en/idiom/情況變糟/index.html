情況變糟

qíng kuàng biàn zāo

In English

  • things go south

Meaning

Things going south.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • take a turn for the worse
  • go downhill

Example

主要投資人撤資後，情況變糟。

Things went south when the lead investor pulled out.

Usage

General; for plans, relationships and illnesses.

Similar

  • ↘️每況愈下měi kuàng yù xià

    go from bad to worse — Going from bad to worse.

    The company's finances went from bad to worse.

  • ❄️雪上加霜xuě shàng jiā shuāng

    to make matters worse; add to one's woes — To make matters worse.

    To make matters worse, the power went out.

Opposite

  • 🌅否極泰來pǐ jí tài lái

    the tide will turn; every cloud has a silver lining — The tide will turn.

    After a hard year, the tide will turn.

  • 🎡時來運轉shí lái yùn zhuǎn

    one's luck turns; one's time has come — One's luck turning.

    After years of bad luck, his time has come.
