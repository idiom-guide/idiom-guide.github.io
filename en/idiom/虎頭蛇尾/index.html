4 · Incompetence & Failure

虎頭蛇尾

hǔ tóu shé wěi

In English

  • start with a bang and end with a whimper
  • peter out

Meaning

Starting with a bang and ending with a whimper.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • fizzle out

Example

這場活動虎頭蛇尾。

The campaign started with a bang and ended with a whimper.

Usage

For plans, campaigns and works.

Similar

  • 🛑半途而廢bàn tú ér fèi

    give up halfway — Giving up halfway.

    Don't give up halfway.

  • 🌤️一曝十寒yī pù shí hán

    sporadic effort — Sporadic effort.

    His sporadic effort won't get him anywhere.

Opposite

  • 🖌️一氣呵成yī qì hē chéng

    at one fell swoop — In one go.

    She wrote the whole essay at one fell swoop.

  • 🏋️堅持不懈jiān chí bù xiè

    committed — Never letting up.

    Her committed practice paid off in the end.
