不情願

bù qíng yuàn

In English

  • half-hearted (negotiation)

Meaning

Reluctant; half-hearted.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • reluctant
  • grudging

Example

雙方不情願地談了一輪，最後什麼都沒簽。

After a half-hearted negotiation, the two sides signed nothing.

Usage

For grudging agreement or going through the motions.

Similar

  • 😑興致缺缺xìng zhì quē quē

    lukewarm; showing little interest — Not keen.

    He showed a lack of appetite for the trip.

  • 😪漫不經心màn bù jīng xīn

    be offhand; do sth carelessly — Offhand.

    He answered offhandedly, as if he couldn't care less.

Opposite

  • 🤗欣然接受xīn rán jiē shòu

    with alacrity — Gladly accepting.

    She accepted the invitation with alacrity.

  • 😊樂意之至lè yì zhī zhì

    gladly; be more than happy to — More than happy to.

    Would you help me move? I'd be more than happy to.
