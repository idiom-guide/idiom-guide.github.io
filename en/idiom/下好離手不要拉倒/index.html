下好離手，不要拉倒

xià hǎo lí shǒu, bù yào lā dǎo

In English

  • take it or leave it

Meaning

Take it or leave it.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • final offer
  • that's my final answer

Example

這是我最後的價格：下好離手，不要拉倒。

That's my last offer: take it or leave it.

Usage

Colloquial; for an ultimatum in negotiations.

Similar

  • 🙅‍♂️沒得商量méi de shāng liang

    non-negotiable; not up for discussion — Non-negotiable.

    The deadline is non-negotiable.

  • 🃏一翻兩瞪眼yī fān liǎng dèng yǎn

    a coin toss; all or nothing — A coin toss.

    It's a coin toss now: either we win the contract or we lose everything.

Opposite

  • 🤑討價還價tǎo jià huán jià

    haggle; bargain — Haggling.

    The two sides haggled over the delivery schedule.
