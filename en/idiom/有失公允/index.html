有失公允

yǒu shī gōng yǔn

In English

  • not fair

Meaning

Unfair; showing bias.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • be unfair
  • be not fair
  • be biased

Example

這份報告對小公司有失公允。

The report was not fair to the smaller companies.

Usage

A formal criticism, more polite than saying 不公平.

Similar

  • 🥉厚此薄彼hòu cǐ bó bǐ

    play favorites; treat one side better than the other — Unequal treatment; 有失公允 stresses the loss of fairness.

    A teacher shouldn't play favorites; everyone should be treated alike.

  • 🎧偏聽偏信piān tīng piān xìn

    believe only one side; be swayed by one-sided accounts — Believing only one side; one cause of unfairness.

    The manager believed one side only, which led to a wrong decision.

Opposite

  • ⚜公正不阿gōng zhèng bù ē

    rectitude; unbiased — Upright and incorruptible.

    The judge was known for his rectitude and never favored the rich.

  • ⚖️不偏不倚bù piān bù yǐ

    impartial — Impartial.

    A good referee stays impartial and favors neither side.
