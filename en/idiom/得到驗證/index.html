得到驗證

dé dào yàn zhèng

In English

  • be borne out
  • be validated

Meaning

Proven right after being tested.Neutral · Formal

Example

他的預測在最終結果出爐後得到驗證。

His prediction was borne out by the final results.

Usage

Follows predictions, hypotheses or theories; the opposite is 被推翻 (overturned).

Similar

  • 🔬經得起檢驗jīng de qǐ jiǎn yàn

    (not) hold up to scrutiny — Able to withstand scrutiny.

    A good theory has to hold up to scrutiny.

  • 📊事實勝於雄辯shì shí shèng yú xióng biàn

    facts speak louder than words; the facts speak for themselves — Facts speak louder than arguments.

    The sales figures say it all: facts speak louder than words.

Opposite

  • 🦶站不住腳zhàn bù zhù jiǎo

    untenable; doesn't hold water — Doesn't hold water.

    His argument simply doesn't hold water.

  • ⤵不攻自破bù gōng zì pò

    collapse on its own; fall apart without being refuted — Collapses on its own without needing to be refuted.

    The rumor fell apart on its own after a few days.
