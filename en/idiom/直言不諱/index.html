直言不諱

zhí yán bù huì

In English

  • be open about that
  • frankly
  • let's call a spade a spade
  • outspoken

Meaning

Speaking frankly, without fear of offending.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • speak frankly
  • be outspoken
  • call a spade a spade

Example

我們就直言不諱吧：這個計畫失敗了。

Let's call a spade a spade: the plan has failed.

Usage

不諱 means not avoiding taboos; for criticism and exposés.

Similar

  • 👨‍🎤坦率直言tǎn shuài zhí yán

    give sb straight talk about sth — Speaking frankly; more positive.

    My coach gave me straight talk about my chances of making the team.

  • 🚪開門見山kāi mén jiàn shān

    get straight to the point — Getting straight to the point.

    He got straight to the point and asked about the price.

Opposite

  • 🌁含糊其詞hán hú qí cí

    talk in generalities; be coy with sb — Being deliberately vague.

    Asked about the budget, the spokesman talked in generalities.

  • ↪️拐彎抹角guǎi wān mò jiǎo

    beat around the bush; obliquely — Beating around the bush.

    Stop beating around the bush and tell me what happened.
