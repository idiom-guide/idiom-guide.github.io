老調重彈

lǎo diào chóng tán

In English

  • rehash

Meaning

A rehash.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • the same old story
  • old hat

Example

這場演說只是老調重彈。

The speech just rehashed old arguments.

Usage

For speeches, arguments and campaign pledges.

Similar

  • 🦜陳腔濫調chén qiāng làn diào

    cliché; platitude — Clichés.

    The speech was full of clichés.

  • 🍪千篇一律qiān piān yī lǜ

    be formulaic; follow the same pattern — All the same.

    These ads are all formulaic and utterly unoriginal.

Opposite

  • ♻️推陳出新tuī chén chū xīn

    breathe new life into tradition — Breathing new life into tradition.

    Young designers are breathing new life into tradition.

  • 💡別出心裁bié chū xīn cái

    original; ingenious — Original.

    He came up with an ingenious plan for the party.
