愁雲慘霧

chóu yún cǎn wù

In English

  • gloom and doom

Meaning

Gloom and doom.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • a pall of gloom
  • doom and gloom

Example

裁員之後，辦公室籠罩在愁雲慘霧中。

A mood of gloom and doom hung over the office after the layoffs.

Usage

For describing an atmosphere.

Similar

  • 😨人心惶惶rén xīn huáng huáng

    widespread jitters; public panic — Public panic.

    Rumors of a bank run caused widespread jitters.

  • 😞士氣低落shì qì dī luò

    demoralized -ing — Demoralized.

    The constant criticism was demoralizing the team.

Opposite

  • 🎉歡樂融洽huān lè róng qià

    fun — Warm and convivial.

    The dinner was fun, and everyone got along.

  • 📣振奮人心zhèn fèn rén xīn

    uplifting; inspiring — Uplifting.

    The coach's speech was uplifting.
