冷眼看待

lěng yǎn kàn dài

In English

  • faces a frosty reception

Meaning

A frosty reception.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • give the cold shoulder
  • be met with indifference

Example

這項提案受到董事會冷眼看待。

The proposal faced a frosty reception from the board.

Usage

For proposals, newcomers and ideas that get a cold response.

Similar

  • 🥶不理不睬bù lǐ bù cǎi

    give sb the cold shoulder; ignore — The cold shoulder.

    She gave him the cold shoulder.

  • 👎不受青睞bù shòu qīng lài

    out of favor; unpopular — Out of favor.

    The new model was out of favor with buyers.

Opposite

  • 🎉熱烈歡迎rè liè huān yíng

    roll out the welcome wagon for — A warm welcome.

    The town rolled out the welcome wagon for the visiting team.

  • 🛎️備受禮遇bèi shòu lǐ yù

    feel very privileged — Treated with great courtesy.

    The visiting scholars felt very privileged.
