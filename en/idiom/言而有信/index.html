言而有信

yán ér yǒu xìn

In English

  • keep one's word

Meaning

Keeping one's word.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • as good as one's word
  • true to one's word

Example

供應商信任她，因為她言而有信。

Suppliers trust her because she keeps her word.

Usage

From the Analects.

Similar

  • 🤝一言為定yī yán wéi dìng

    deal — Deal.

    Ten o'clock tomorrow? Deal.

  • 🤝言行一致yán xíng yī zhì

    practice what one preaches; walk the talk — Walking the talk.

    A good leader practices what he preaches.

Opposite

  • 🤥言而無信yán ér wú xìn

    go back on one's word — Going back on one's word.

    No one wants a partner who goes back on his word.

  • ➰出爾反爾chū ěr fǎn ěr

    go back on one's word; flip-flop — Flip-flopping.

    He went back on his word as soon as he had given it.
