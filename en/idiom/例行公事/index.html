例行公事

lì xíng gōng shì

In English

  • formality

Meaning

A routine; a mere formality.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • a formality
  • routine
  • going through the motions

Example

每週的進度回報只是例行公事。

The weekly check-in is just a formality.

Usage

Neutral for everyday procedure, negative for empty, perfunctory routines.

Similar

  • 📋按部就班àn bù jiù bān

    step by step; by the book — Step by step.

    New hires are trained step by step.

  • 🍪千篇一律qiān piān yī lǜ

    be formulaic; follow the same pattern — All the same.

    These ads are all formulaic and utterly unoriginal.

Opposite

  • 📦打破常規dǎ pò cháng guī

    think outside the box — Thinking outside the box.

    To solve this problem, we need to think outside the box.

  • 💡別出心裁bié chū xīn cái

    original; ingenious — Original and ingenious.

    He came up with an ingenious plan for the party.
