出人意表

chū rén yì biǎo

In English

  • you will never know

Meaning

Surprising everyone; full of surprises.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • take everyone by surprise
  • be full of surprises
  • be unexpected and clever
  • you never know what sb will do next

Example

他的點子總是出人意表，你永遠猜不到他下一步。

With his surprising ideas, you never know what he will do next.

Usage

Usually praise for something novel and clever; more about ingenuity than 出乎意料.

Similar

  • 🎉出乎意料chū hū yì liào

    take sb by surprise — Unexpected.

    The sudden announcement took everyone by surprise.

  • 👓跌破眼鏡diē pò yǎn jìng

    incredulous — Nobody saw that coming.

    I was incredulous when the underdog won the championship.

Opposite

  • 📓意料中事yì liào zhōng shì

    be in the playbook — Just as expected.

    Blaming the previous manager is straight out of the playbook, so nobody was surprised.

  • 🥱司空見慣sī kōng jiàn guàn

    a common sight; commonplace — So common no one is surprised.

    Delays are commonplace on this line.
