對症下藥

duì zhèng xià yào

In English

  • a targeted remedy
  • address the root cause

Meaning

A targeted remedy.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • the right tool for the job

Example

沒有可靠的數據，政府就無法對症下藥。

Without reliable data, the government cannot offer a targeted remedy.

Usage

For policy, management, education and medicine.

Similar

  • 🩺標本兼治biāo běn jiān zhì

    tackle both symptoms and root causes — Tackling both symptoms and causes.

    The plan tackles both the symptoms and the root causes of youth unemployment.

  • 🧩因地制宜yīn dì zhì yí

    adapt to local conditions — Adapting to local conditions.

    Policies should be adapted to local conditions.

Opposite

  • 🌳緣木求魚yuán mù qiú yú

    climb a tree to catch a fish; fruitless approach — A fruitless approach.

    Looking for honest answers from him is like climbing a tree to catch a fish.

  • 🗜一概而論yī gài ér lùn

    lump everything together; generalize — Lumping everything together.

    Everyone's situation is different, so we can't lump them together.
