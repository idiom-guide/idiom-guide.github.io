昭然若揭

zhāo rán ruò jiē

In English

  • can't be more obvious

Meaning

Glaringly obvious, as if held up for all to see.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • be plain for all to see
  • be as clear as day
  • be blatantly obvious

Example

他想接管公司的盤算昭然若揭。

His plan to take over the company couldn't be more obvious.

Usage

Usually of ambitions, motives or the truth, often negative; not for ordinary things.

Similar

  • 🧣欲蓋彌彰yù gài mí zhāng

    draw attention by trying to cover up; backfire — The harder one hides it, the more it shows.

    His long denial only drew attention; trying to cover up the mistake backfired.

  • 🔆真相大白zhēn xiàng dà bái

    the truth comes out; come to light — The truth comes out.

    After the investigation, the truth finally came to light.

Opposite

  • 🐴深藏不露shēn cáng bù lù

    keep one's talents hidden; be a dark horse — Keeping one's abilities or intentions hidden.

    He kept his talents hidden, and nobody knew he was the champion.

  • 😐不動聲色bù dòng shēng sè

    keep a poker face; show no emotion — Keeping a poker face.

    He kept a poker face and quietly got the plan ready.
