哀兵政策

āi bīng zhèng cè

In English

  • play the underdog
  • play the sympathy card

Meaning

Playing the underdog; playing the sympathy card.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • cast oneself as the victim

Example

這個政黨打哀兵政策，爭取選民同情。

The party played the underdog to win sympathy.

Usage

For elections, competition and negotiation.

Similar

  • 🐷扮豬吃老虎bàn zhū chī lǎo hǔ

    sandbagging — Playing dumb to win.

    He played dumb at the table, sandbagging his rivals before winning the pot.

  • 🎣欲擒故縱yù qín gù zòng

    give sb enough rope; let sb go in order to catch them later — Letting go in order to catch later.

    He let the suspect go on purpose, giving him enough rope.

Opposite

  • 💪耀武揚威yào wǔ yáng wēi

    show muscles; shit on others — Flexing one's muscles.

    The winners strutted around, showing off their muscles.

  • 🐶虛張聲勢xū zhāng shēng shì

    bluff; more bark than bite — Bluffing to look strong.

    His threats were just a bluff; he is more bark than bite.
