威嚇逼迫

wēi hè bī pò

In English

  • terrorize sb. into doing sth

Meaning

Forcing someone through threats and fear.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • terrorize sb into doing sth
  • intimidate
  • strong-arm

Example

幫派試圖威嚇逼迫店家繳交保護費。

The gang tried to terrorize shopkeepers into paying protection money.

Usage

For gangs, bullying, the workplace or political pressure.

Similar

  • 🥕威脅利誘wēi xié lì yòu

    cajole and entice; be cajoled into Ving — Threats mixed with inducements.

    He was cajoled into signing with a mix of threats and promises.

  • 😤咄咄逼人duō duō bī rén

    confrontational; pugnacious, aggressive — Aggressive and pushy.

    The lawyer's confrontational style made the witness nervous.

Opposite

  • 🐾循循善誘xún xún shàn yòu

    guide patiently, step by step — Guiding patiently.

    She guides her students patiently, step by step.

  • 🤝通情達理tōng qíng dá lǐ

    reasonable; as a matter of course — Reasonable and understanding.

    He is a reasonable man who listens to both sides.
