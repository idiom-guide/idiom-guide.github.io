怨聲載道

yuàn shēng zài dào

In English

  • widespread discontent

Meaning

Widespread discontent.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • complaints everywhere
  • a chorus of complaints

Example

票價調漲引起怨聲載道。

The fare hike caused widespread discontent.

Usage

For policies or services that anger the public.

Similar

  • 🌋民怨沸騰mín yuàn fèi téng

    public anger boils over — Public anger boiling over.

    Public anger boiled over after the scandal broke.

  • 📰輿論譁然yú lùn huá rán

    a public uproar — A public uproar.

    The leaked memo caused a public uproar.

Opposite

  • 😻讚不絕口zàn bù jué kǒu

    be full of praise — Full of praise.

    Everyone was full of praise for the dish.

  • 🗳️眾望所歸zhòng wàng suǒ guī

    the people's choice; enjoy popular support — The people's choice.

    She was the people's choice for the post.
