花拳繡腿

huā quán xiù tuǐ

In English

  • all show and no substance
  • flashy but ineffective

Meaning

Showy moves with no real power.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • style over substance

Example

公司的新策略只是花拳繡腿，毫無實質內容。

The company's new strategy is all show and no substance.

Usage

Originally a martial-arts term; now used to criticize people, plans or works that look impressive but lack real ability.

Similar

  • 🦚虛有其表xū yǒu qí biǎo

    all show and no substance; more style than substance — Looks good outside, empty inside; broader, not limited to skill.

    The hotel is all show and no substance.

  • 🎁華而不實huá ér bù shí

    overhyped; frivolous — Showy but impractical; leans toward things that look good but are of no use.

    The ad was flashy, but the product turned out to be frivolous and overhyped.

Opposite

  • 💎貨真價實huò zhēn jià shí

    the genuine article — The genuine article, through and through.

    Customers want to know the product is the genuine article.

  • ⚔️身經百戰shēn jīng bǎi zhàn

    battle-hardened — Battle-hardened.

    The battle-hardened sergeant stayed calm under fire.
