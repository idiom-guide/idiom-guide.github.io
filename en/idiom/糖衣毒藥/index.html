糖衣毒藥

táng yī dú yào

In English

  • poisoned chalice

Meaning

A poisoned chalice.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • a Trojan horse
  • too good to be true

Example

這份優渥的提議是糖衣毒藥，背後有無盡的義務。

The generous offer was a poisoned chalice, loaded with endless obligations.

Usage

For offers, posts and deals.

Similar

  • 💰金錢誘惑jīn qián yòu huò

    financial entrapment — The lure of money.

    The official fell into a financial entrapment.

Opposite

  • ❄️雪中送炭xuě zhōng sòng tàn

    help when it is needed most — Help when it's needed most.

    A true friend helps when it is needed most.
