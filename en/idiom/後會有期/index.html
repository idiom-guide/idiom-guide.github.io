9 · Closing & Farewell

後會有期

hòu huì yǒu qī

In English

  • until we meet again

Meaning

Until we meet again.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • see you around
  • our paths will cross again

Example

謝謝各位，後會有期。

Thank you, and until we meet again.

Usage

A farewell that looks forward to meeting again.

Similar

  • 👋珍重再見zhēn zhòng zài jiàn

    take care and goodbye — Take care and goodbye.

    Take care, and goodbye for now.

  • 🌅來日方長lái rì fāng cháng

    there will be many more opportunities ahead — Many more opportunities ahead.

    There will be many more opportunities ahead to work together.

Opposite

  • 🔪一刀兩斷yī dāo liǎng duàn

    make a clean break — A clean break.

    He decided to make a clean break with his old life.
