來日不多

lái rì bù duō

In English

  • be terminal

Meaning

One's days are numbered.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • one's days are numbered
  • not long for this world

Example

醫生說病人來日不多。

The doctors said the patient is terminal.

Usage

For the dying, and for regimes or things nearing their end.

Similar

  • ⏳氣數將盡qì shù jiāng jìn

    sb's days are numbered — Days numbered.

    The old regime's days are numbered.

  • 🏥病入膏肓bìng rù gāo huāng

    beyond cure; terminally ill — Beyond cure.

    The economy seems beyond cure.

Opposite

  • 🌅來日方長lái rì fāng cháng

    there will be many more opportunities ahead — Many more chances ahead.

    There will be many more opportunities ahead to work together.
