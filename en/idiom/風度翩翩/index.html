風度翩翩

fēng dù piān piān

In English

  • suave
  • urbane

Meaning

Graceful and elegant conduct, as if fluttering lightly: a man of outstanding poise.Positive · Formal

Example

主人風度翩翩，讓每位賓客都如沐春風。

The host was a suave gentleman who put every guest at ease.

Usage

Mostly for a man's manner and speech. About poise rather than looks.

Similar

  • 🕴️風流倜儻fēng liú tì tǎng

    dashing; debonair — Carefree and debonair; leans toward temperament.

    The prince is dashing and debonair.

  • 🌲玉樹臨風yù shù lín fēng

    dashing; strikingly handsome — Handsome and upright; about appearance.

    He cut a dashing figure in his tailored suit.

Opposite

  • 🧔不修邊幅bù xiū biān fú

    scruffy, slovenly, disheveled, unkempt, slob — Careless about one's appearance.

    He turned up scruffy and unshaven for the interview.
