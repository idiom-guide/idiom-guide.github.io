小力一點

xiǎo lì yī diǎn

In English

  • dial back

Meaning

Dial it back.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • dial it back
  • go easy
  • tone it down

Example

小力一點，速度放慢些。

Dial back your speed a little.

Usage

Colloquial; for telling someone to ease off.

Similar

  • ✋適可而止shì kě ér zhǐ

    know when to stop — Knowing when to stop.

    Criticism is fine, but know when to stop.

  • 🌡️過猶不及guò yóu bù jí

    too much is as bad as too little — Too much is as bad as too little.

    With revision, too much is as bad as too little.

Opposite

  • 👊高壓手段gāo yā shǒu duàn

    heavy handed — Heavy-handed.

    The regime's heavy-handed methods only made people angrier.
