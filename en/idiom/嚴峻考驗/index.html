嚴峻考驗

yán jùn kǎo yàn

In English

  • fiery crucible
  • the acid test

Meaning

A severe test; the acid test.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • a severe test
  • trial by fire

Example

這場水災是城市緊急應變計畫的嚴峻考驗。

The flood was the acid test of the city's emergency plan.

Usage

Often 面臨嚴峻考驗 (face a severe test).

Similar

  • 🔥震撼教育zhèn hàn jiào yù

    baptism of fire — A baptism of fire.

    Her first week in the emergency room was a baptism of fire.

  • 🏔️艱鉅任務jiān jù rèn wù

    Herculean effort/task — A daunting task.

    Rebuilding the town after the earthquake was a Herculean task.

Opposite

  • 🧁輕而易舉qīng ér yì jǔ

    be a piece of cake — As easy as pie.

    For him, this is a piece of cake.

  • 🖐️易如反掌yì rú fǎn zhǎng

    has never been so easy — As easy as turning over one's hand.

    With the new software, filing taxes has never been so easy.
