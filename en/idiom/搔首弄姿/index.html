搔首弄姿

sāo shǒu nòng zī

In English

  • preen
  • strike poses

Meaning

Stroking one's hair and putting on a flirtatious look: deliberately showing off charm.Negative · Formal

Example

她在鏡頭前搔首弄姿，不停變換姿勢。

She struck coquettish poses one after another in front of the camera.

Usage

Disapproving: deliberately posing for attention. Keep the critical tone when interpreting.

Similar

  • 🎀矯揉造作jiǎo róu zào zuò

    mannerism; cutesy — Affected and unnatural; broader in range.

    Her fulsome compliments sounded insincere.

  • 💄濃妝豔抹nóng zhuāng yàn mǒ

    heavily made-up; overdone — Heavy makeup; about dress-up.

    She showed up heavily made-up, as if for a stage show.

Opposite

  • 🧼素面朝天sù miàn cháo tiān

    bare-faced; no makeup — No makeup; about plainness.

    She went out bare-faced and looked completely at ease.
