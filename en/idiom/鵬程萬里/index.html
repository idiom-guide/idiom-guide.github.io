9 · Congratulations & Good Wishes

鵬程萬里

péng chéng wàn lǐ

In English

  • a bright future ahead

Meaning

A bright future ahead.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • the sky's the limit
  • go far

Example

祝各位畢業生鵬程萬里。

To the graduates: you have a bright future ahead.

Usage

A blessing from Zhuangzi; for graduations, promotions and study abroad.

Similar

  • 🌈前程似錦qián chéng sì jǐn

    a bright future — A bright future.

    We wish you a bright future.

  • 🚀前途無量qián tú wú liàng

    got a future — Got a future.

    The young scientist has a bright future ahead of her.
