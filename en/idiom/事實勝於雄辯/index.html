事實勝於雄辯

shì shí shèng yú xióng biàn

In English

  • facts speak louder than words
  • the facts speak for themselves

Meaning

Facts speak louder than words.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • the proof of the pudding is in the eating

Example

銷售數字就在這裡，事實勝於雄辯。

The sales figures say it all: facts speak louder than words.

Usage

Used when producing evidence against empty talk.

Similar

  • 🗄鐵證如山tiě zhèng rú shān

    the evidence is irrefutable; the evidence is overwhelming — Evidence as solid as a mountain; about evidence itself.

    The evidence is irrefutable; he can no longer argue his way out.

  • 👁️‍🗨️有目共睹yǒu mù gòng dǔ

    be plain for all to see; be witnessed by everyone — Plain for all to see.

    His contribution to the company is plain for all to see.

Opposite

  • 📄紙上談兵zhǐ shàng tán bīng

    armchair general — Armchair theorizing.

    He is an armchair general who has never visited a real battlefield.

  • 🍯花言巧語huā yán qiǎo yǔ

    sweet talk; smooth talk — Sweet talk that can't be trusted.

    Don't be taken in by his sweet talk.
