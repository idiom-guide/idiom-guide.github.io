全盤托出

quán pán tuō chū

In English

  • lay/put sb's cards on the table
  • come clean with sb. about sth

Meaning

Coming clean; telling everything.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • come clean
  • lay one's cards on the table
  • spill everything

Example

經過幾小時詢問，他終於對失蹤的款項全盤托出。

After hours of questioning, he finally came clean about the missing funds.

Usage

For confessions or revelations; suggests being pressed or finally giving in.

Similar

  • 🃏坦承相見tǎn chéng xiāng jiàn

    come clean; lay one's cards on the table — Being completely honest with each other.

    It's time to come clean about what really happened.

  • 📑一五一十yī wǔ yī shí

    tell everything in detail — Telling it all in detail.

    He told the police everything that had happened, in full detail.

Opposite

  • 🤐守口如瓶shǒu kǒu rú píng

    tight-lipped; keep mum — Keeping one's lips sealed.

    He stayed tight-lipped about the merger.

  • 🙊絕口不提jué kǒu bù tí

    be reticent about sth — Never mentioning it.

    He remained reticent about his family history.
