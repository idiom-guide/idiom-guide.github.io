拋磚引玉

pāo zhuān yǐn yù

In English

  • just tossing an idea out there

Meaning

Casting a brick to attract jade: offering a modest opinion to draw out better ones.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • throw out a brick to attract jade
  • float an idea to draw out better ones

Example

我只是先拋出一個想法，拋磚引玉，讓討論開始。

I'm just tossing an idea out there to get the discussion started.

Usage

A humble way of calling one's remarks a starting point; also used for getting others involved.

Similar

  • 🌞野人獻曝yě rén xiàn pù

    my humble contribution — Humbly offering something modest; about giving.

    Please take this as my humble contribution to the discussion.

  • 💭一點淺見yī diǎn qiǎn jiàn

    my two cents; just my two cents — Just my two cents; a spoken disclaimer.

    Here's my two cents: we should wait a week.

Opposite

  • 🏍獨斷獨行dú duàn dú xíng

    act arbitrarily; go it alone — Acting alone without consulting anyone.

    He acts arbitrarily and never listens to his team.

  • 🤓自以為是zì yǐ wéi shì

    be self-righteous; think one knows best — Thinking oneself always right.

    He thinks he knows best and never listens to anyone.
