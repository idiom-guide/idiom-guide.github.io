才智出眾

cái zhì chū zhòng

In English

  • luminary
  • leading luminaries

Meaning

Exceptionally gifted.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • exceptionally gifted

Example

這場會議聚集了該領域才智出眾的頂尖人物。

The conference gathered the leading luminaries of the field.

Usage

For scholars, experts and leaders.

Similar

  • 🏆出類拔萃chū lèi bá cuì

    outstanding — Outstanding.

    Her grades are outstanding, even in a class of top students.

  • 🥇頂尖人才dǐng jiān rén cái

    tip of the spear, edge of the knife — Top talent.

    We recruit the tip of the spear: the sharpest engineers in the country.

Opposite

  • 🥔非常普通fēi cháng pǔ tōng

    garden-variety — Garden-variety.

    It's just a garden-variety cold, nothing serious.

  • 🙉不學無術bù xué wú shù

    ignoramus; uninformed — Ignorant and unskilled.

    The ignoramus has never opened a book.
