碩果僅存

shuò guǒ jǐn cún

In English

  • the sole survivor
  • the last of its kind

Meaning

The sole survivor; the last of its kind.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • the last one standing

Example

她是原班船員中碩果僅存的一位。

She is the sole survivor of the original crew.

Usage

From the Bo hexagram of the Book of Changes; for people, old shops and traditional crafts.

Similar

  • 🦄鳳毛麟角fèng máo lín jiǎo

    few and far between; extremely rare — Rare and precious, not necessarily the only one left.

    Interpreters with that language pair are few and far between.

  • 🖐️屈指可數qū zhǐ kě shǔ

    can be counted on one hand — Few enough to count on one hand.

    Experts in this field can be counted on one hand.

Opposite

  • 👀比比皆是bǐ bǐ jiē shì

    everywhere; all too common — Everywhere.

    Such errors are everywhere in the report.

  • 🐂多如牛毛duō rú niú máo

    a dime a dozen — A dime a dozen.

    Apps like this are a dime a dozen.
