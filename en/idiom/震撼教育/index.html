震撼教育

zhèn hàn jiào yù

In English

  • baptism of fire

Meaning

A baptism of fire.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • a baptism of fire
  • a rude awakening
  • a crash course

Example

她在急診室的第一週，簡直是一場震撼教育。

Her first week in the emergency room was a baptism of fire.

Usage

For newcomers facing a tough situation for the first time.

Similar

  • 🩹現實考驗xiàn shí kǎo yàn

    reality bites — A reality check.

    His first month in the big city taught him that reality bites.

  • 📞當頭棒喝dāng tóu bàng hè

    a wake-up call — A wake-up call.

    That failure was a wake-up call for him.

Opposite

  • 📋按部就班àn bù jiù bān

    step by step; by the book — Step by step.

    New hires are trained step by step.

  • 🔼循序漸進xún xù jiàn jìn

    proceed step by step; make gradual progress — Gradually, from easy to hard.

    Learn to swim step by step; don't jump into the deep end at once.
