天下奇觀

tiān xià qí guān

In English

  • wondrous spectacle

Meaning

A wondrous spectacle.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • a wonder of the world
  • a sight to behold

Example

極光是天下奇觀。

The aurora was a wondrous spectacle.

Usage

For natural wonders and grand buildings.

Similar

  • 🤩歎為觀止tàn wéi guān zhǐ

    breathtaking; awe-inspiring — Breathtaking.

    The view from the summit was breathtaking.

  • 🌄卓越美景zhuó yuè měi jǐng

    scenery of exceptional charm — Scenery of exceptional charm.

    The valley is a scenery of exceptional charm.

Opposite

  • 🥱平淡無奇píng dàn wú qí

    run-of-the-mill — Run-of-the-mill.

    It was just a run-of-the-mill day at the office.
