產業空洞

chǎn yè kōng dòng

In English

  • deindustrialization

Meaning

Deindustrialization.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • hollowing out of industry
  • rust belt

Example

產業空洞讓這個小鎮沒了工作機會。

Deindustrialization left the town without jobs.

Usage

An economics term; also 產業空洞化.

Similar

  • 🏚️百業蕭條bǎi yè xiāo tiáo

    an across-the-board slump — An across-the-board slump.

    The lockdown caused an across-the-board slump.

Opposite

  • 🌳欣欣向榮xīn xīn xiàng róng

    thriving; booming — Thriving.

    The local startup scene is thriving.

  • 📈風生水起fēng shēng shuǐ qǐ

    prosperous — Flourishing.

    The small business has been prosperous since the move.
