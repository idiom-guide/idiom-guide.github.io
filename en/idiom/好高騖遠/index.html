好高騖遠

hào gāo wù yuǎn

In English

  • aim too high vs. down-to-earth

Meaning

Aiming too high.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • aim too high
  • bite off more than one can chew

Example

他好高騖遠，忽略基礎；試著腳踏實地一點。

He aims too high and ignores the basics; try to be more down-to-earth.

Usage

Often a warning to master the basics first.

Similar

  • 🙄眼高手低yǎn gāo shǒu dī

    aim high but fall short; all talk and no skill — All talk and no skill.

    He aims high but falls short.

  • 😴癡人說夢chī rén shuō mèng

    wildly unrealistic — Wildly unrealistic.

    His plan to cross the ocean on a raft is wildly unrealistic.

Opposite

  • 👣腳踏實地jiǎo tà shí dì

    staying grounded and pushing ahead — Down-to-earth.

    He is a down-to-earth man who stays grounded and keeps pushing ahead.

  • 📋按部就班àn bù jiù bān

    step by step; by the book — Step by step.

    New hires are trained step by step.
