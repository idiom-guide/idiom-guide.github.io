毫不客氣

háo bù kè qì

In English

  • unceremoniously

Meaning

Without ceremony; blunt.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • without any ceremony
  • bluntly

Example

她毫不客氣地請吵鬧的客人離開。

She unceremoniously told the noisy guests to leave.

Usage

Neutral when it means direct, negative when it means rude.

Similar

  • 😈毫不留情háo bù liú qíng

    ruthless; without mercy — Merciless.

    The critic was ruthless in his review.

  • 😤咄咄逼人duō duō bī rén

    confrontational; pugnacious, aggressive — Overbearing and aggressive.

    The lawyer's confrontational style made the witness nervous.

Opposite

  • 🎩彬彬有禮bīn bīn yǒu lǐ

    courteous; well-mannered — Polite and courteous.

    The waiter was courteous throughout.

  • 😚和顏悅色hé yán yuè sè

    be kind and friendly; with a pleasant look — Warm and friendly.

    He always treats customers with a kind and friendly look.
