感同身受

gǎn tóng shēn shòu

In English

  • relatable

Meaning

Feeling someone's pain as one's own.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • feel sb's pain as one's own
  • empathize with
  • put oneself in sb's shoes

Example

她的故事讓我感同身受，字字都像在說我。

Her story was so relatable that I felt every word as if it were my own.

Usage

For empathy with others' situations; the listed relatable is an adjective, while the Chinese describes a feeling.

Similar

  • 👟設身處地shè shēn chǔ dì

    see things from someone else's position — Putting oneself in someone's shoes.

    Try to see things from her position.

  • 💞心有戚戚焉xīn yǒu qī qī yān

    have the same feeling — Striking a chord.

    When she described her childhood, I had the same feeling, as if she were telling my story.

Opposite

  • 📴事不關己shì bù guān jǐ

    none of one's business; not one's concern — Not one's business.

    It was none of his business, so he stood by and did nothing.

  • 🧍冷眼旁觀lěng yǎn páng guān

    on the sidelines — Watching coolly from the sidelines.

    While the others argued, she watched from the sidelines.
