魯莽倉促行動

lǔ mǎng cāng cù xíng dòng

In English

  • jump the gun to v

Meaning

Jumping the gun.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • act prematurely
  • get ahead of oneself

Example

結果還沒確認前，別魯莽倉促行動，急著宣布。

Don't jump the gun and announce the results before they are confirmed.

Usage

For announcements, decisions and acting before the time is right.

Similar

  • 🚀貿然行動mào rán xíng dòng

    acting with precipitation — Acting rashly.

    He acted with precipitation and signed before reading the contract.

  • ⚠️魯莽行事lǔ mǎng xíng shì

    throw caution/risk to the winds — Throwing caution to the winds.

    He threw caution to the winds and invested everything.

Opposite

  • 🚶謹慎前進jǐn shèn qián jìn

    proceed with caution — Proceeding with caution.

    On the icy road, we proceeded with caution.

  • 🗿深思熟慮shēn sī shú lǜ

    think it through carefully; give careful thought — Thinking it through.

    Major decisions require careful thought, not haste.
