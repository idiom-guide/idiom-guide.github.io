情理之中

qíng lǐ zhī zhōng

In English

  • sth is expected

Meaning

Reasonable and understandable; within what one would expect.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • be only to be expected
  • it stands to reason
  • come as no surprise

Example

她訓練得那麼刻苦，獲勝是情理之中的事。

Given how hard she trained, her victory was only to be expected.

Usage

Often paired: 意料之外，情理之中 (unexpected, yet it makes sense).

Similar

  • 🉑意料之中yì liào zhī zhōng

    come as no surprise; be as expected — As expected; not necessarily reasonable.

    The team's loss came as no surprise.

  • 🙆‍♂️理所當然lǐ suǒ dāng rán

    go without saying; be only natural — Only natural; more everyday.

    Being paid for hard work is only natural.

Opposite

  • 🎇出人意料chū rén yì liào

    beyond all expectation; be unexpected — Contrary to what everyone expected.

    The result was beyond all expectation: the weakest team won.

  • 😯始料未及shǐ liào wèi jí

    never have anticipated; be caught off guard — Not foreseen at all, often of something bad or big.

    The scale of the epidemic was something experts never anticipated.
