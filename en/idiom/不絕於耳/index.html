不絕於耳

bù jué yú ěr

In English

  • is the refrain

Meaning

The constant refrain.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • nonstop
  • ring in one's ears

Example

每次住戶大會上，對噪音的抱怨都不絕於耳。

Complaints about the noise are the refrain of every residents' meeting.

Usage

For sounds, or for remarks and complaints that keep coming.

Similar

  • 🔁沒完沒了méi wán méi liǎo

    incessant; never-ending — Never-ending.

    The incessant chatter on the train gave me a headache.

  • 🦜喋喋不休dié dié bù xiū

    ad nauseam — Going on and on.

    He went on about his holiday ad nauseam.

Opposite

  • 🦗鴉雀無聲yā què wú shēng

    be dead silent; you could hear a pin drop — So quiet you could hear a pin drop.

    After the result was announced, the hall fell dead silent.

  • ⏹️戛然而止jiá rán ér zhǐ

    come to an abrupt halt; stop short — Stopping abruptly.

    The music came to an abrupt halt.
