不打草驚蛇

bù dǎ cǎo jīng shé

In English

  • don't tip one's hand
  • don't alert the target

Meaning

Don't tip one's hand.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • keep one's cards close to one's chest

Example

在證據足夠之前，務必不打草驚蛇。

Don't tip your hand until we have enough evidence.

Usage

Common in investigations and negotiations; also 以免打草驚蛇 (so as not to alert them).

Similar

  • 😐不動聲色bù dòng shēng sè

    keep a poker face; show no emotion — Keeping a poker face.

    He kept a poker face and quietly got the plan ready.

  • 🪑靜觀其變jìng guān qí biàn

    stay tuned; sit tight and assess — Sitting tight and watching how things develop.

    Let's sit tight and assess the situation before we act.

Opposite

  • 🐍打草驚蛇dǎ cǎo jīng shé

    wake a sleeping dog — Tipping off the target.

    Don't wake a sleeping dog by questioning him too early.

  • 🚩大張旗鼓dà zhāng qí gǔ

    with great fanfare — With great fanfare.

    The mayor launched the campaign with great fanfare.
