如履薄冰

rú lǚ bó bīng

In English

  • walk on thin ice
  • tread carefully

Meaning

Walking on thin ice; treading very carefully.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • on tenterhooks

Example

醜聞爆發後，經理行事如履薄冰。

After the scandal, the manager had to walk on thin ice.

Usage

Often paired with 如臨深淵; from the Book of Songs (Xiao Min).

Similar

  • 🥚小心翼翼xiǎo xīn yì yì

    tread carefully; be extremely careful — Treading carefully.

    He chose his words very carefully to avoid trouble.

  • 🙈心驚膽戰xīn jīng dǎn zhàn

    frightened — Terrified.

    We were frightened by the loud thunder.

Opposite

  • 🌋肆無忌憚sì wú jì dàn

    with no restraint; unbridled — With no restraint.

    The bully acted with no restraint.

  • 😎泰然自若tài rán zì ruò

    unfazed — Composed and unruffled.

    She remained unfazed by the critics' attacks.
