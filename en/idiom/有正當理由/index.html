有正當理由

yǒu zhèng dàng lǐ yóu

In English

  • justify

Meaning

With good reason; legitimately justified.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • have a legitimate reason
  • be justified
  • have good cause

Example

經理要他說明，這些額外支出有沒有正當理由。

The manager asked him to justify the extra expenses.

Usage

Common in rules, laws and documents, as in 無正當理由 (without good reason).

Similar

  • 🎌師出有名shī chū yǒu míng

    have a justified cause; act with just cause — A just cause for war; figuratively, justified action.

    His action was justified, so nobody could object.

  • 🛂名正言順míng zhèng yán shùn

    be fully legitimate; be justified in every sense — Legitimate and proper.

    He took over as the founder, which was perfectly legitimate.

Opposite

  • 🚯師出無名shī chū wú míng

    act without justification; have no just cause — Acting without a just cause.

    The dispute had no just cause, so nobody supported it.

  • 🧒無理取鬧wú lǐ qǔ nào

    make trouble for no reason; throw a tantrum — Making a scene for no reason.

    He is making trouble for no reason again; ignore him.
