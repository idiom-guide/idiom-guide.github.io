不可踰越

bù kě yú yuè

In English

  • unbridgeable

Meaning

Impossible or forbidden to cross; said of boundaries, gaps or bottom lines.Neutral · Formal

Example

雙方立場之間的差距，可說是不可踰越的。

The gap between the two parties' positions proved unbridgeable.

Usage

"An unbridgeable gulf" is the classic use; for red lines, English says inviolable or a line that must not be crossed.

Similar

  • 🌍天壤之別tiān rǎng zhī bié

    worlds apart — Stresses how big the gap is; 不可踰越 stresses that it cannot be crossed.

    The two drafts are worlds apart.

  • 🏦固若金湯gù ruò jīn tāng

    impregnable; rock-solid — Impregnable defenses or systems; about defense, not distance.

    The castle was impregnable and had never been taken.

Opposite

  • 🖐️易如反掌yì rú fǎn zhǎng

    has never been so easy — As easy as turning over one's hand.

    With the new software, filing taxes has never been so easy.

  • 🥅一步之遙yī bù zhī yáo

    be just one step away — Just one step away from the goal.

    The team was just one step away from the championship.
