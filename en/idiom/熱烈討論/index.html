熱烈討論

rè liè tǎo lùn

In English

  • a heated discussion
  • lively debate

Meaning

A lively discussion.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • a lively discussion
  • a heated debate
  • spark a lively debate

Example

這項提案在會議上引發熱烈討論。

The proposal sparked a heated discussion.

Usage

Stresses a lively atmosphere; the listed heated suggests tension, which Chinese would call 激烈爭論.

Similar

  • ⚔️唇槍舌戰chún qiāng shé zhàn

    a war of words; verbal sparring — A war of words.

    The debate turned into a war of words.

  • 💬眾說紛紜zhòng shuō fēn yún

    opinions vary widely — Opinions vary widely.

    Opinions vary widely on what caused the blackout.

Opposite

  • 🦗鴉雀無聲yā què wú shēng

    be dead silent; you could hear a pin drop — Dead silence.

    After the result was announced, the hall fell dead silent.

  • 😶沉默寡言chén mò guǎ yán

    has a reticence about — Taciturn.

    My grandfather is a man of few words, with a certain reticence about his past.
