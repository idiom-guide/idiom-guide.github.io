整理門面

zhěng lǐ mén miàn

In English

  • spruce up
  • smarten up one's image

Meaning

Sprucing things up.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • put on a good face

Example

檢查之前，他們先整理門面，把店裡打點得乾乾淨淨。

They spruced up the shop before the inspection.

Usage

Literal sprucing up or figuratively improving an image.

Similar

  • 🍬粉飾太平fěn shì tài píng

    sugarcoating — Papering over problems; negative.

    The report was sugarcoating the real situation.

  • 🦋脫胎換骨tuō tāi huàn gǔ

    totally changed; transformed significantly — Transformed completely.

    Three years abroad transformed her completely.

Opposite

  • 🧔不修邊幅bù xiū biān fú

    scruffy, slovenly, disheveled, unkempt, slob — Scruffy.

    He turned up scruffy and unshaven for the interview.

  • 🗿一成不變yī chéng bù biàn

    unchanging; set in stone — Unchanging.

    The curriculum is not set in stone.
