前景看好

qián jǐng kàn hǎo

In English

  • marketable

Meaning

Promising prospects.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • promising prospects
  • looking up

Example

這款新裝置前景看好。

The new device is marketable, with promising prospects.

Usage

For products, industries, markets and careers.

Similar

  • 🚀前途無量qián tú wú liàng

    got a future — A bright future.

    The young scientist has a bright future ahead of her.

  • 🌳欣欣向榮xīn xīn xiàng róng

    thriving; booming — Thriving.

    The local startup scene is thriving.

Opposite

  • 🎱前途未卜qián tú wèi bǔ

    it's anyone's guess how — Anyone's guess.

    It's anyone's guess how the economy will look next year.

  • 🏞️江河日下jiāng hé rì xià

    in steady decline — In steady decline.

    The industry has been in steady decline.
