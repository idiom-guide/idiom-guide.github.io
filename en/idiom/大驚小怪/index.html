大驚小怪

dà jīng xiǎo guài

In English

  • tiny problem gigantic reaction
  • drama queen

Meaning

Making a fuss over nothing.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • make a mountain out of a molehill

Example

別大驚小怪，只是擦傷而已。

Don't be a drama queen; it's only a scratch.

Usage

Often telling someone not to overreact.

Similar

  • 💔玻璃心bō lí xīn

    fragile ego — A fragile ego.

    He has a fragile ego and takes every joke personally.

  • 🐍杯弓蛇影bēi gōng shé yǐng

    Afraid of one's own shadow; extremely suspicious — Afraid of one's own shadow.

    After the burglary, she was afraid of her own shadow.

Opposite

  • 😄一笑置之yī xiào zhì zhī

    laugh it off — Laughing it off.

    She laughed off the insult.

  • 😎泰然自若tài rán zì ruò

    unfazed — Unfazed.

    She remained unfazed by the critics' attacks.
