分身乏術

fēn shēn fá shù

In English

  • spread too thin
  • can't be in two places at once

Meaning

Spread too thin.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • stretched thin

Example

兼兩份工作，我分身乏術。

With two jobs, I'm spread too thin.

Usage

Often for politely declining or explaining one is too busy.

Similar

  • 🎩身兼數職shēn jiān shù zhí

    wear many hats — Wearing many hats.

    As the only doctor in a small town, she wears many hats.

  • 👕捉襟見肘zhuō jīn jiàn zhǒu

    cash-strapped; stretched thin — Stretched thin.

    The cash-strapped agency froze hiring.

Opposite

  • 🎈無事一身輕wú shì yī shēn qīng

    unencumbered — Unencumbered.

    With the deadline behind him, he felt unencumbered.

  • 👌綽綽有餘chuò chuò yǒu yú

    more than enough — More than enough.

    Ten thousand dollars is more than enough for the trip.
