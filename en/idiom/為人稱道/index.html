為人稱道

wéi rén chēng dào

In English

  • widely praised/complimented
  • commendable

Meaning

Widely praised.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • widely praised
  • widely complimented

Example

她對陌生人的善意為人稱道。

Her kindness to strangers was widely praised.

Usage

For character, deeds and acts of kindness.

Similar

  • 👄有口皆碑yǒu kǒu jiē bēi

    good reputation — Praised by all.

    The clinic has a good reputation among all the locals.

  • 👏值得讚頌zhí dé zàn sòng

    laudable; commendable — Laudable.

    Their courage is laudable.

Opposite

  • 👉千夫所指qiān fū suǒ zhǐ

    universally condemned — Universally condemned.

    The attack was universally condemned.

  • 🤮令人反感lìng rén fǎn gǎn

    distasteful; off-putting — Distasteful.

    His constant bragging was distasteful.
