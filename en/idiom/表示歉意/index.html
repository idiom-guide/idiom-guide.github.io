3 · Goodwill & Courtesy

表示歉意

biǎo shì qiàn yì

In English

  • as a penance
  • do penance for

Meaning

Making amends; apologizing.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • make amends

Example

他買花給她表示歉意。

He bought her flowers as a penance.

Usage

For formal apologies or apologizing through actions.

Similar

  • 🙏誠心一點chéng xīn yī diǎn

    say like you mean it — Saying it like you mean it.

    If you want to apologize, say it like you mean it.

Opposite

  • 💁毫無歉意háo wú qiàn yì

    unapologetically — Unapologetic.

    She unapologetically cut in line.
