歪打正著

wāi dǎ zhèng zháo

In English

  • stumble into success
  • by a lucky accident

Meaning

Succeeding by a lucky accident.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • a happy accident

Example

她歪打正著，意外成功了。

She stumbled into success by a lucky accident.

Usage

Colloquial; for success that was never planned.

Similar

  • 🔀陰錯陽差yīn cuò yáng chā

    in a strange circumstance; for certain reasons — By a strange coincidence; the outcome can be good or bad.

    By a strange coincidence, we booked the same room for the same night.

  • 🎳誤打誤撞wù dǎ wù zhuàng

    by sheer luck; happen upon sth — Happening upon it by chance; more casual.

    He happened upon the root of the problem by sheer luck.

Opposite

  • ↩️適得其反shì dé qí fǎn

    sth risks backfiring; backfire — Backfiring.

    His efforts to calm the crowd only backfired.

  • 🤦‍♂️弄巧成拙nòng qiǎo chéng zhuō

    outsmart oneself; be too clever by half — Being too clever by half.

    He tried to hide it but outsmarted himself.
