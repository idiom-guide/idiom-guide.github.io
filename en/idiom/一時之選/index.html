一時之選

yī shí zhī xuǎn

In English

  • the best and the brightest

Meaning

The best and the brightest of the day.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • top picks
  • the cream of the crop

Example

評審團成員都是一時之選。

The panel was made up of the best and the brightest.

Usage

For panels, teams, casts and candidates.

Similar

  • 🥇頂尖人才dǐng jiān rén cái

    tip of the spear, edge of the knife — Top talent.

    We recruit the tip of the spear: the sharpest engineers in the country.

  • 🏅箇中翹楚gè zhōng qiáo chǔ

    cream of the crop; in a league of its own — Cream of the crop.

    This winery is the cream of the crop.

Opposite

  • 🥔非常普通fēi cháng pǔ tōng

    garden-variety — Garden-variety.

    It's just a garden-variety cold, nothing serious.
