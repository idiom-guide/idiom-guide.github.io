吃了秤砣鐵了心

chī le chèng tuó tiě le xīn

In English

  • dead set
  • dig in one's heels

Meaning

Dead set on something.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • dead set on
  • have one's mind made up

Example

他吃了秤砣鐵了心，就是要辭職。

He's dead set on quitting his job.

Usage

A colloquial saying; can mean determination or stubbornness.

Similar

  • 🎯下定決心xià dìng jué xīn

    set sb's sights on sth; be hell bent on n — Making up one's mind.

    She set her sights on becoming a judge and never looked back.

  • 🐺一意孤行yī yì gū xíng

    go it alone; act arbitrarily — Going it alone, ignoring advice.

    He went it alone, ignoring the advice of every adviser.

Opposite

  • 🤷躊躇不定chóu chú bù dìng

    vacillate between sth — Vacillating.

    She vacillated between the two job offers for weeks.

  • 🥶臨時反悔lín shí fǎn huǐ

    get cold feet — Getting cold feet.

    She got cold feet the night before the wedding.
