奇貨可居

qí huò kě jū

In English

  • hold on to a rare commodity
  • a rare find worth hoarding

Meaning

A rare find worth hoarding.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • sit on a gold mine

Example

他認為這張稀有郵票奇貨可居，所以留著不賣。

He thought the rare stamp was a find worth hoarding, so he held on to it.

Usage

Formal; from the Records of the Grand Historian.

Similar

  • 🦄鳳毛麟角fèng máo lín jiǎo

    few and far between; extremely rare — Few and far between.

    Interpreters with that language pair are few and far between.

  • 🧾供不應求gōng bù yìng qiú

    demand outstrips supply — Demand outstripping supply.

    Demand for chips still outstrips supply.

Opposite

  • 🌐隨處可見suí chù kě jiàn

    a dime a dozen — A dime a dozen.

    Coffee shops are a dime a dozen in this city.

  • 📦供過於求gōng guò yú qiú

    oversupply; a glut — A glut.

    A glut of office space has pushed rents down.
