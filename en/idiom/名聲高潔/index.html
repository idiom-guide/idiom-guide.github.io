名聲高潔

míng shēng gāo jié

In English

  • reputationally pristine

Meaning

An unblemished reputation.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • an unblemished reputation
  • squeaky clean

Example

連他的對手都說他名聲高潔。

Even his rivals say he is reputationally pristine.

Usage

For officials, scholars and public figures.

Similar

  • ☀️光明磊落guāng míng lěi luò

    scrupulous; upright — Open and aboveboard.

    He is a scrupulous businessman who always follows the rules.

  • 🧓德高望重dé gāo wàng zhòng

    venerable — Venerable.

    The venerable teacher was respected by three generations of students.

Opposite

  • 🍷道德敗壞dào dé bài huài

    Debauchery; promiscuous — Morally corrupt.

    The film depicts a decade of debauchery at the royal court.

  • 🪑德不配位dé bù pèi wèi

    unworthy of one's position; unfit for the role — Unworthy of one's position.

    A leader who is unworthy of his position will lose people's trust.
