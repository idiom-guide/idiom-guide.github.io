不出鋒頭

bù chū fēng tóu

In English

  • unassuming

Meaning

Unassuming; keeping a low profile.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • low-key
  • keep a low profile

Example

他為人不出鋒頭，從不追求鎂光燈。

He is an unassuming man who never seeks the spotlight.

Usage

Describes personality; positive.

Similar

  • 🐴深藏不露shēn cáng bù lù

    keep one's talents hidden; be a dark horse — Hidden depths.

    He kept his talents hidden, and nobody knew he was the champion.

  • 🏙️大隱隱於市dà yǐn yǐn yú shì

    hide in plain sight — Hiding in plain sight.

    The famous author lives quietly downtown, hiding in plain sight.

Opposite

  • 📢愛出風頭ài chū fēng tou

    sb is loud — Loving the limelight.

    My cousin is loud and loves being the center of attention.

  • ✨光彩奪目guāng cǎi duó mù

    stealing the spotlight; hog the limelight — Stealing the spotlight.

    The young actress was stealing the spotlight at the premiere.
