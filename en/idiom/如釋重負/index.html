3 · Joy & Satisfaction

如釋重負

rú shì zhòng fù

In English

  • let shoulders drop
  • relieved
  • take heavy weight off

Meaning

Relieved.Positive · General

Example

考試結束後，她如釋重負。

She felt relieved when the exams were over, as if a heavy weight had been lifted.

Usage

For the moment a task or pressure is over.

Similar

  • 🎈無事一身輕wú shì yī shēn qīng

    unencumbered — Unencumbered.

    With the deadline behind him, he felt unencumbered.

  • 🤗令人安心lìng rén ān xīn

    reassuring — Reassuring.

    The doctor's calm voice was reassuring.

Opposite

  • 😔心事重重xīn shì chóng chóng

    preoccupied; weighed down by worries — Weighed down by worries.

    He seemed preoccupied all day.

  • 🦋惴惴不安zhuì zhuì bù ān

    feel unsettled; have butterflies in one's stomach — Feeling unsettled.

    She felt butterflies in her stomach before the interview.
