3 · Goodwill & Courtesy

承蒙厚愛

chéng méng hòu ài

In English

  • thanks for liking my sth

Meaning

Thank you for your kind support.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • thank you for your kind support
  • much obliged

Example

承蒙厚愛，您的支持對我意義重大。

Thanks for liking my book; your support means a lot.

Usage

For authors and performers thanking their supporters.

Similar

  • 😳受寵若驚shòu chǒng ruò jīng

    I'm flattered — I'm flattered.

    You remembered my birthday? I'm flattered.

  • 😳愧不敢當kuì bù gǎn dāng

    I'm flattered — I don't deserve this.

    You are too kind. I'm flattered.

Opposite

  • 👎不受青睞bù shòu qīng lài

    out of favor; unpopular — Out of favor.

    The new model was out of favor with buyers.

  • 🥶冷眼看待lěng yǎn kàn dài

    faces a frosty reception — A frosty reception.

    The proposal faced a frosty reception from the board.
