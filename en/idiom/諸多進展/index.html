諸多進展

zhū duō jìn zhǎn

In English

  • has come a long way

Meaning

Considerable progress.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • considerable progress
  • make great strides

Example

過去十年，這個國家在潔淨能源上有諸多進展。

The country has come a long way in clean energy over the past decade.

Usage

For countries, industries and research fields.

Similar

  • 🐇突飛猛進tū fēi měng jìn

    advance by leaps and bounds — Advancing by leaps and bounds.

    Her English has advanced by leaps and bounds.

  • 📆今非昔比jīn fēi xī bǐ

    things are different now — Things are different now.

    Things are different now; this neighborhood used to be a slum.

Opposite

  • ↘️每況愈下měi kuàng yù xià

    go from bad to worse — Going from bad to worse.

    The company's finances went from bad to worse.
