2 · Competition & Power

發號施令

fā hào shī lìng

In English

  • call the shots

Meaning

Calling the shots.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • give the orders
  • be in charge

Example

在這個家，是我姊姊在發號施令。

In this house, my sister calls the shots.

Usage

Neutral; when critical, it means bossing people around.

Similar

  • 👑位高權重wèi gāo quán zhòng

    highly placed; powerful — Highly placed and powerful.

    A highly placed official can make or break a business.

  • 🎩有力人士yǒu lì rén shì

    the influencers; head honcho — The head honcho.

    The head honcho of the studio will attend the party.

Opposite

  • 🎮聽命於人tīng mìng yú rén

    do sth at sb's behest — Acting at someone's behest.

    The agents act at the behest of a foreign government.

  • 🙆唯唯諾諾wéi wéi nuò nuò

    be a yes-man; agree servilely — A yes-man.

    He is a yes-man who never dares to disagree.
