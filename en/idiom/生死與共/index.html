生死與共

shēng sǐ yǔ gòng

In English

  • live with it, die with it

Meaning

Bound together in life and death.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • live or die together
  • stand by each other to the end

Example

兩組船員互相扶持，準備生死與共。

The two crews stood by each other, ready to live or die together.

Usage

Formal and solemn; for comrades, partners and friends in hard times.

Similar

  • ⛵同舟共濟tóng zhōu gòng jì

    solidarity; help each other — Pulling together through a crisis, not necessarily life-or-death.

    In times of crisis, neighbors help each other.

  • ⛈️患難與共huàn nàn yǔ gòng

    stick together through thick and thin — Sticking together through hard times; less extreme.

    They stuck together through thick and thin.

Opposite

  • 🔀分道揚鑣fēn dào yáng biāo

    part ways — Going separate ways.

    After the project, the partners decided to part ways.

  • ✂️恩斷義絕ēn duàn yì jué

    cut all ties — Cutting all ties.

    After the betrayal, she cut all ties with him.
