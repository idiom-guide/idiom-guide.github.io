東施效顰

dōng shī xiào pín

In English

  • a clumsy imitation
  • ape

Meaning

A homely girl frowns like a famous beauty and only looks worse: blind imitation that backfires.Negative · Formal

Example

不了解他人的風格，一味模仿只會東施效顰。

Copying someone's style without understanding it only makes you a clumsy imitation.

Usage

Criticizes blindly copying others without regard to one's own situation, with embarrassing results. The word for imitating is also used modestly of oneself, as in daring not to follow suit.

Similar

  • 🥾邯鄲學步hán dān xué bù

    lose one's own way by imitating others — Learning another's walk and forgetting one's own; about losing one's own way.

    By copying others blindly, you forget your own stride.

  • 🦜人云亦云rén yún yì yún

    echo other's words — Repeating what others say; about lacking one's own view.

    Don't just echo other people's words; form your own opinion.

Opposite

  • 🌳獨樹一幟dú shù yī zhì

    unique in — Having a style of one's own.

    The restaurant is unique in its blend of Taiwanese and French cooking.

  • ♻️推陳出新tuī chén chū xīn

    breathe new life into tradition — Creating something new from the old.

    Young designers are breathing new life into tradition.
