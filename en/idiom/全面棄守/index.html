全面棄守

quán miàn qì shǒu

In English

  • total capitulation

Meaning

Total capitulation.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • complete surrender
  • cave in

Example

聯盟全面棄守，令支持者意外。

The coalition's total capitulation surprised its supporters.

Usage

For politics, negotiations and military matters.

Similar

  • 🏳️棄械投降qì xiè tóu xiáng

    throw in the towel — Throwing in the towel.

    After the third round, the boxer threw in the towel.

  • 🏳️認輸求饒rèn shū qiú ráo

    cry uncle — Crying uncle.

    The wrestler had to cry uncle.

Opposite

  • 🤚寸步不讓cùn bù bù ràng

    not yield an inch — Not giving an inch.

    Neither side would yield an inch on price.

  • 🌊背水一戰bèi shuǐ yī zhàn

    a last stand; do-or-die — A do-or-die battle.

    With their backs to the wall, the team played a do-or-die match.
