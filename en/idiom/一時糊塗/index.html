一時糊塗

yī shí hú tú

In English

  • a lapse of judgment

Meaning

A lapse of judgment.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • a moment of madness
  • brain fade

Example

他一時糊塗，簽了那份表格。

In a lapse of judgment, he signed the form.

Usage

Often to explain or excuse a one-off mistake.

Similar

  • 🤦不智行為bù zhì xíng wéi

    ill-advised — Ill-advised.

    It was ill-advised to sign the contract without reading it.

  • ⚠️魯莽行事lǔ mǎng xíng shì

    throw caution/risk to the winds — Throwing caution to the winds.

    He threw caution to the winds and invested everything.

Opposite

  • 🗿深思熟慮shēn sī shú lǜ

    think it through carefully; give careful thought — Thinking it through.

    Major decisions require careful thought, not haste.

  • 💎思考清晰sī kǎo qīng xī

    clear insight — Clear thinking.

    Her answer showed clear insight into the problem.
