喘一口氣

chuǎn yī kǒu qì

In English

  • breathe easy

Meaning

Breathing easy.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • catch one's breath
  • breathe a sigh of relief

Example

考試結束了，我終於可以喘一口氣。

Now that the exams are over, I can finally breathe easy.

Usage

Colloquial; for after a busy or stressful stretch.

Similar

  • 😌如釋重負rú shì zhòng fù

    let shoulders drop; relieved — Relieved.

    She felt relieved when the exams were over, as if a heavy weight had been lifted.

  • ⏸️喘息空間chuǎn xī kōng jiān

    have some breathing room — Breathing room.

    The extension gave us some breathing room.

Opposite

  • 🤹分身乏術fēn shēn fá shù

    spread too thin; can't be in two places at once — Spread too thin.

    With two jobs, I'm spread too thin.

  • ⏱️爭分奪秒zhēng fēn duó miǎo

    race against time — A race against time.

    Rescuers are racing against time.
