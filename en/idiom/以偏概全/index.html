以偏概全

yǐ piān gài quán

In English

  • a broad generalization

Meaning

Generalizing from a part to the whole.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • make a sweeping generalization
  • generalize from a single case

Example

憑一個觀光客就評斷整個國家，是以偏概全。

Judging a whole country by one tourist is a broad generalization.

Usage

Points out flawed reasoning; often 不要以偏概全.

Similar

  • 🗜一概而論yī gài ér lùn

    lump everything together; generalize — Lumping everything together; often 不能一概而論.

    Everyone's situation is different, so we can't lump them together.

  • 📷管中窺豹guǎn zhōng kuī bào

    see only a small part of the whole; judge the whole from a narrow view — A limited view; 以偏概全 goes further and draws a conclusion.

    Judging by a single report is like seeing the whole through a keyhole.

Opposite

  • 🌎綜觀全局zōng guān quán jú

    look at the big picture — Looking at the whole picture.

    Looking at the big picture, the policy does more good than harm.

  • 🔄全面檢視quán miàn jiǎn shì

    take a comprehensive look; review from every angle — Reviewing every aspect.

    Before making a decision, review all the data from every angle.
