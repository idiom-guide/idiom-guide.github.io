說句公道話

shuō jù gōng dào huà

In English

  • in fairness
  • to be fair

Meaning

To be fair.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • in all fairness

Example

說句公道話，他們只有兩週準備，已經很不錯了。

In fairness, the team had only two weeks to prepare.

Usage

Usually defends someone after others have criticized them.

Similar

  • ♎持平而論chí píng ér lùn

    To be fair — In all fairness; more formal.

    To be fair, the referee had only a split second to decide.

  • 💼公事公辦gōng shì gōng bàn

    business is business; transactional — Going by the book, without favoritism.

    Sorry, it's nothing personal; business is business.

Opposite

  • 🍕有失公允yǒu shī gōng yǔn

    not fair — Unfair.

    The report was not fair to the smaller companies.

  • 🥉厚此薄彼hòu cǐ bó bǐ

    play favorites; treat one side better than the other — Playing favorites.

    A teacher shouldn't play favorites; everyone should be treated alike.
