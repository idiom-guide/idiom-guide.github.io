加緊馬力

jiā jǐn mǎ lì

In English

  • gear up

Meaning

Stepping it up.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • step it up
  • ramp up

Example

旺季快到了，我們得加緊馬力。

We need to gear up for the busy season.

Usage

For work, production and preparation.

Similar

  • 🏁全力衝刺quán lì chōng cì

    put the pedal to the metal — Putting the pedal to the metal.

    For the final week, the team put the pedal to the metal.

  • 🔥火力全開huǒ lì quán kāi

    be firing on all cylinders; do sth at full throttle — Firing on all cylinders.

    The team was firing on all cylinders in the second half.

Opposite

  • 🔋養精蓄銳yǎng jīng xù ruì

    recharge one's batteries; conserve one's strength — Recharging one's batteries.

    We'll rest today to recharge our batteries for tomorrow's match.

  • 🚩偃旗息鼓yǎn qí xī gǔ

    call a halt; lie low — Calling it off.

    The band announced they would call a halt for now: a pause, they said, not a farewell.
