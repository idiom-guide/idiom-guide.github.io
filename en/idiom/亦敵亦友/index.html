亦敵亦友

yì dí yì yǒu

In English

  • frenemies

Meaning

Both rival and friend.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • friendly rivals
  • both friend and foe

Example

他們亦敵亦友，緊要時會互相幫忙。

They are frenemies who help each other when it matters.

Usage

For competitors, politicians and countries.

Similar

  • 💞愛恨交織ài hèn jiāo zhī

    love-hate relationship — A love-hate relationship; about feelings.

    She has a love-hate relationship with her hometown.

  • 🕊️化干戈為玉帛huà gān gē wéi yù bó

    bury the hatchet — Turning from enemies into friends.

    After years of silence, the two families finally buried the hatchet.

Opposite

  • 🆚勢不兩立shì bù liǎng lì

    irreconcilable — Irreconcilable enemies.

    The two factions are irreconcilable.

  • 💗肝膽相照gān dǎn xiāng zhào

    utterly loyal friends — Utterly loyal friends.

    The two are utterly loyal friends.
