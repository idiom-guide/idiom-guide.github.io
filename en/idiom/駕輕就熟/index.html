駕輕就熟

jià qīng jiù shú

In English

  • mastering
  • getting into one's stride

Meaning

On familiar ground.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • on familiar ground

Example

一個月後，她對新工作已駕輕就熟。

After a month, she was getting into her stride at the new job.

Usage

For jobs, skills and routines one knows well.

Similar

  • ✋得心應手dé xīn yìng shǒu

    be good at it — Doing it with ease.

    After years of practice, he is good at it and handles the tools with ease.

  • 🎹熟能生巧shú néng shēng qiǎo

    practice makes perfect — Practice makes perfect.

    Practice makes perfect: sight-translate a page every day.

Opposite

  • 🐣職場菜鳥zhí chǎng cài niǎo

    be new to the workforce — New to the workforce.

    I'm new to the workforce, so please be patient with me.

  • 🈳一竅不通yī qiào bù tōng

    all Greek to me — All Greek to me.

    Quantum physics is all Greek to me.
