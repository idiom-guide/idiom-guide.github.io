家家有本難念的經

jiā jiā yǒu běn nán niàn de jīng

In English

  • every family has its own troubles

Meaning

Every family has its own troubles.Neutral · Colloquial

Example

看起來大家都很幸福，其實家家有本難念的經。

Everyone looks happy, but every family has its own troubles.

Usage

Often said to remind people not to envy others' seemingly perfect lives.

Similar

  • 👩‍⚖️清官難斷家務事qīng guān nán duàn jiā wù shì

    even an upright magistrate can't settle family quarrels — Outsiders can't judge family quarrels.

    Even an upright magistrate can't settle family quarrels; outsiders shouldn't interfere.

  • 🔏難言之隱nán yán zhī yǐn

    have something one can't speak of — A difficulty one can't talk about.

    He won't say more; he probably has something he can't speak of.

Opposite

  • 👨‍👩‍👧家庭幸福jiā tíng xìng fú

    family happiness; domestic bliss — A happy, harmonious family.

    To him, domestic bliss is worth more than any promotion.

  • 🍲家和萬事興jiā hé wàn shì xīng

    a harmonious family prospers — A harmonious family prospers.

    As the saying goes, a harmonious family prospers.
