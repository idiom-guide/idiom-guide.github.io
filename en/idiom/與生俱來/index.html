與生俱來

yǔ shēng jù lái

In English

  • innate
  • sb see sth as one's birthright
  • natural, inherent

Meaning

Innate.Neutral · General

Example

她的節奏感是與生俱來的。

She has an innate sense of rhythm.

Usage

For talents, traits and rights one is born with.

Similar

  • 🎻天賦異稟tiān fù yì bǐng

    has a knack for — Naturally gifted.

    She has a knack for languages.

  • 🧠很有慧根hěn yǒu huì gēn

    talented — A quick study.

    The teacher says the boy is talented and picks up ideas fast.

Opposite

  • 🎹熟能生巧shú néng shēng qiǎo

    practice makes perfect — Practice makes perfect.

    Practice makes perfect: sight-translate a page every day.

  • 🐢勤能補拙qín néng bǔ zhuō

    hard work makes up for lack of talent — Hard work makes up for lack of talent.

    Hard work makes up for lack of talent.
