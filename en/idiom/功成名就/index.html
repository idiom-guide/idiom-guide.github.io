功成名就

gōng chéng míng jiù

In English

  • achieve fame and success
  • have it made

Meaning

Fame and success.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • make it big

Example

多年努力後，她終於功成名就。

After years of work, she finally achieved fame and success.

Usage

For careers that have reached their peak.

Similar

  • 🧍出人頭地chū rén tóu dì

    make a name for oneself; stand out from the crowd — Making a name for oneself.

    He wanted to make a name for himself.

  • 🏆光宗耀祖guāng zōng yào zǔ

    bring honor to the family — Bringing honor to the family.

    He hoped his success would bring honor to the family.

Opposite

  • 🤕一敗塗地yī bài tú dì

    fall flat on your face — Falling flat on one's face.

    The campaign fell flat on its face.

  • ⌛生不逢時shēng bù féng shí

    born at the wrong time — Born at the wrong time.

    The inventor was born at the wrong time; the world wasn't ready for his ideas.
