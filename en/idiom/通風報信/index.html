通風報信

tōng fēng bào xìn

In English

  • tip...off

Meaning

Tipping someone off.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • tip sb off
  • leak information
  • give sb a heads-up

Example

有人在突襲前向嫌犯通風報信。

Someone tipped the suspect off before the raid.

Usage

Usually leaking information to the other side; can be a neutral heads-up.

Similar

  • 🐍打草驚蛇dǎ cǎo jīng shé

    wake a sleeping dog — Tipping off the target.

    Don't wake a sleeping dog by questioning him too early.

  • 🕵️暗渡陳倉àn dù chén cāng

    take a covert route; feint to the east and strike to the west — Doing things on the sly; 通風報信 is passing on information.

    The general feinted to the east and struck to the west.

Opposite

  • 🤐守口如瓶shǒu kǒu rú píng

    tight-lipped; keep mum — Keeping one's lips sealed.

    He stayed tight-lipped about the merger.

  • ㊙秘而不宣mì ér bù xuān

    keep it secret; not make it public — Keeping something under wraps.

    The plan is being kept secret for now.
