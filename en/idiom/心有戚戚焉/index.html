心有戚戚焉

xīn yǒu qī qī yān

In English

  • have the same feeling

Meaning

It strikes a chord with me.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • strike a chord with sb
  • resonate with sb
  • feel the same way

Example

聽她說起童年，我心有戚戚焉。

When she described her childhood, I had the same feeling, as if she were telling my story.

Usage

From Mencius (King Hui of Liang I), King Xuan's response to Mencius; now means "that's exactly how I feel."

Similar

  • 🤗感同身受gǎn tóng shēn shòu

    relatable — Feeling someone's pain as one's own.

    Her story was so relatable that I felt every word as if it were my own.

  • 💞將心比心jiāng xīn bǐ xīn

    be empathetic; put oneself in sb's shoes — Seeing things from the other person's side.

    Try to put yourself in her shoes before you criticize.

Opposite

  • 📴事不關己shì bù guān jǐ

    none of one's business; not one's concern — Not one's business.

    It was none of his business, so he stood by and did nothing.

  • 🐟格格不入gé gé bù rù

    be out of place; be like a fish out of water — Out of place.

    He felt like a fish out of water among his new colleagues.
