3 · Joy & Satisfaction

心曠神怡

xīn kuàng shén yí

In English

  • completely relaxed and happy

Meaning

Refreshed and happy.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • refreshed in body and mind
  • blissful

Example

在湖邊散步讓我心曠神怡。

A walk by the lake leaves me completely relaxed and happy.

Usage

For scenery, walks and holidays.

Similar

  • 🌸如沐春風rú mù chūn fēng

    feel so good; pleasant — Like a spring breeze.

    Talking with her made me feel so good, like a spring breeze.

  • 😊滿心愉悅mǎn xīn yú yuè

    delight — Full of delight.

    She smiled with delight at the surprise.

Opposite

  • 🔋身心俱疲shēn xīn jù pí

    physically and mentally exhausted — Worn out in body and mind.

    After the long shift, she was physically and mentally exhausted.

  • ☁️愁雲慘霧chóu yún cǎn wù

    gloom and doom — Gloom and doom.

    A mood of gloom and doom hung over the office after the layoffs.
