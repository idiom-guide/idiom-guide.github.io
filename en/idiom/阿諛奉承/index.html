4 · Character & Conduct

阿諛奉承

ē yú fèng chéng

In English

  • blandishment
  • Sycophant

Meaning

Sycophancy.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • suck up to

Example

那人阿諛奉承，董事長說什麼他都稱讚。

The sycophant praised every word the chairman said.

Usage

For criticizing flatterers.

Similar

  • 📈趨炎附勢qū yán fù shì

    fawn on the powerful; curry favor with those in power — Social climbing.

    He always fawns on the powerful and follows whoever holds authority.

  • 🌹溢美之詞yì měi zhī cí

    effusive praise; overpraise — Effusive praise.

    He was embarrassed by the effusive praise.

Opposite

  • 📢直言不諱zhí yán bù huì

    speak frankly; not mince words — Calling a spade a spade.

    She spoke frankly and pointed out the flaws in the plan.

  • 🙋恕我直言shù wǒ zhí yán

    with all due respect; frankly speaking — With all due respect.

    With all due respect, I think the plan has a few problems.
