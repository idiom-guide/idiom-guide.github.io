付出代價

fù chū dài jià

In English

  • pay the piper

Meaning

Paying the price.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • pay the price
  • face the music

Example

遲早你得付出代價。

Sooner or later, you'll have to pay the piper.

Usage

For warnings and facing consequences.

Similar

  • ⚖️罪有應得zuì yǒu yīng dé

    comeuppance — Getting one's comeuppance.

    The thief got his comeuppance.

  • ⚡現世報xiàn shì bào

    instant karma — Instant karma.

    He cheated everyone and then lost all his money: instant karma.

Opposite

  • 🔓逍遙法外xiāo yáo fǎ wài

    at large — Still at large.

    The fraudster is still at large.

  • 🥪不勞而獲bù láo ér huò

    get something for nothing; get a free lunch — Getting something for nothing.

    There is no such thing as a free lunch.
