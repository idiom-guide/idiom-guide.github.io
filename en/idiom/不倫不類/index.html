不倫不類

bù lún bù lèi

In English

  • neither fish nor fowl
  • a hodgepodge

Meaning

Neither fish nor fowl.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • a mishmash

Example

這棟建築不倫不類，四不像。

The building is a hodgepodge: neither fish nor fowl.

Usage

For styles, designs and failed mash-ups.

Similar

  • 🧶雜亂無章zá luàn wú zhāng

    haphazard — Haphazard.

    The files were piled up in a haphazard manner.

Opposite

  • 🌿渾然天成hún rán tiān chéng

    seamless; naturally perfect — Naturally perfect.

    The garden looks seamless, as if nature made it.

  • 👌恰到好處qià dào hǎo chù

    just right — Just right.

    The seasoning is just right.
