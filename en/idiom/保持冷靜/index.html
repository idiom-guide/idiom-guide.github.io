保持冷靜

bǎo chí lěng jìng

In English

  • be dispassionate

Meaning

Staying calm.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • stay calm
  • keep a cool head

Example

遇到緊急狀況時，要保持冷靜、清楚思考。

In an emergency, try to stay dispassionate and think clearly.

Usage

Often as advice or a reminder; for emergencies and conflicts.

Similar

  • 🧊沉得住氣chén de zhù qì

    keep one's cool; stay patient — Keeping one's cool.

    Keep your cool and wait for the right moment.

  • 🧖心平氣和xīn píng qì hé

    be calm and composed; keep one's temper — Calm and even-tempered.

    Let's talk calmly and keep our tempers in check.

Opposite

  • 💔感情用事gǎn qíng yòng shì

    be susceptible to n; sentimental — Letting feelings rule.

    Don't be susceptible to sentiment when you make business decisions.

  • 😨驚慌失措jīng huāng shī cuò

    panic; consternation, To sb's consternation, fill sb with consternation — Panicking.

    To the teacher's consternation, the whole class started laughing.
