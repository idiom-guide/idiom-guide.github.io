道貌岸然

dào mào àn rán

In English

  • sanctimonious, sanctimony

Meaning

Sanctimonious.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • sanctimonious
  • holier-than-thou
  • pious hypocrite

Example

那位道貌岸然的傳道人對大家大談誠實。

The sanctimonious preacher lectured everyone on honesty.

Usage

For mocking people who preach what they don't practice.

Similar

  • 🧐衛道人士wèi dào rén shì

    moralist — Moralists.

    Moralists demanded that the film be banned.

  • 🎭心口不一xīn kǒu bù yī

    disingenuous (slightly dishonest); disingenuous — Disingenuous.

    He claims to support the plan but quietly works against it, which is disingenuous.

Opposite

  • ☀️光明磊落guāng míng lěi luò

    scrupulous; upright — Open and aboveboard.

    He is a scrupulous businessman who always follows the rules.

  • 🤝言行一致yán xíng yī zhì

    practice what one preaches; walk the talk — Walking the talk.

    A good leader practices what he preaches.
