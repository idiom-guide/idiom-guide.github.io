美酒佳餚

měi jiǔ jiā yáo

In English

  • fine food and wine
  • wine and dine

Meaning

Good wine and good dishes: a refined, plentiful feast.Positive · General

Example

席間美酒佳餚不斷，賓客盡興而歸。

With fine food and wine all evening, the guests left well pleased.

Usage

For banquets and celebrations. Covers both drink and food; a lavish spread of delicacies covers food only.

Similar

  • 🍻賓主盡歡bīn zhǔ jìn huān

    a good time was had by all — Hosts and guests alike enjoy themselves; about the mood.

    The dinner ended on a high note, and a good time was had by all.

  • 🦞山珍海味shān zhēn hǎi wèi

    delicacies; a lavish spread — Prized ingredients and a rich spread; food only, no wine.

    The wedding table groaned with delicacies from land and sea.

Opposite

  • 🥣粗茶淡飯cū chá dàn fàn

    plain fare; simple meals — Plain, simple meals; leans toward a frugal life.

    He lived on plain fare all his life and was perfectly content.
