大悲無言

dà bēi wú yán

In English

  • speechless because of the deepest grief

Meaning

Speechless with grief.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • beyond words
  • grief-stricken

Example

葬禮上，她大悲無言。

At the funeral, she was speechless with grief.

Usage

For bereavement and great misfortune.

Similar

  • 💔痛徹心扉tòng chè xīn fēi

    devastated — Devastated.

    I was devastated by the news.

  • 😢傷心欲絕shāng xīn yù jué

    heartbroken — Heartbroken.

    She was heartbroken when her dog died.

Opposite

  • 😊滿心愉悅mǎn xīn yú yuè

    delight — Full of delight.

    She smiled with delight at the surprise.

  • 🐦歡欣雀躍huān xīn què yuè

    be jubilant at/about/over sth — Jubilant.

    The fans were jubilant over the victory.
