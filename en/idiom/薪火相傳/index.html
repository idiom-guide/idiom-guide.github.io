薪火相傳

xīn huǒ xiāng chuán

In English

  • pass the torch

Meaning

Passing the torch.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • hand down
  • carry on the tradition

Example

老師傅把技藝傳給徒弟，薪火相傳。

The old master passed the torch to his apprentice.

Usage

Formal; from Zhuangzi's image of fire passing on as each stick of firewood burns out; for skills, scholarship and traditions.

Similar

  • 🏠繼承家業jì chéng jiā yè

    honor family legacy — Taking over the family business; family only.

    He returned home to honor the family legacy by running the bakery.

  • 💙青出於藍qīng chū yú lán

    the student surpasses the master — The student surpassing the master.

    The student has surpassed the master.

Opposite

  • 🍎碩果僅存shuò guǒ jǐn cún

    the sole survivor; the last of its kind — The last of its kind.

    She is the sole survivor of the original crew.
