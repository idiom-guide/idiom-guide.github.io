迫於情勢

pò yú qíng shì

In English

  • do sth. out of desperation

Meaning

Forced by circumstances; doing something out of necessity.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • be forced by circumstances
  • have no choice but to
  • out of desperation

Example

迫於情勢，這家人只好賣掉房子。

Out of desperation, the family sold their home.

Usage

Stresses it wasn't one's wish; often followed by 只好 or 不得不.

Similar

  • 😰情急之下qíng jí zhī xià

    in desperation — In a moment of desperation; about a panicked reaction.

    In desperation, he grabbed a stick and chased the dog away.

  • 🪀身不由己shēn bù yóu jǐ

    have no say; not be one's own master — Not one's own master; about ongoing constraints.

    He has no say at the company and does whatever he is told.

Opposite

  • 🦢從容不迫cóng róng bù pò

    unruffled — Calm and unhurried.

    The host remained unruffled when the lights went out.

  • 👨‍✈️自作主張zì zuò zhǔ zhāng

    take matters into one's own hands; act on one's own authority — Acting on one's own initiative.

    He took it upon himself to change the contract, and it caused trouble.
