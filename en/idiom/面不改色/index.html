面不改色

miàn bù gǎi sè

In English

  • not (even) bat an eyelid

Meaning

Not batting an eyelid.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • keep a straight face
  • unfazed

Example

聽到價錢，他面不改色。

He didn't bat an eyelid when he heard the price.

Usage

For staying unfazed by danger, shock or a hefty price.

Similar

  • 😐不動聲色bù dòng shēng sè

    keep a poker face; show no emotion — Showing nothing; can also mean acting quietly.

    He kept a poker face and quietly got the plan ready.

  • ⛰️泰山崩於前而面不改色tài shān bēng yú qián ér miàn bù gǎi sè

    remain unruffled even if the sky falls; unflappable — Unflappable even if the sky falls; stronger.

    The pilot remained unruffled even when the engine failed.

Opposite

  • 😱大驚小怪dà jīng xiǎo guài

    tiny problem gigantic reaction; drama queen — Making a fuss over nothing.

    Don't be a drama queen; it's only a scratch.

  • 😨驚慌失措jīng huāng shī cuò

    panic; consternation, To sb's consternation, fill sb with consternation — Panicking.

    To the teacher's consternation, the whole class started laughing.
