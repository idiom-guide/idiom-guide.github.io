漠不關心

mò bù guān xīn

In English

  • uncaring

Meaning

Uncaring.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • indifferent
  • couldn't care less

Example

房東對房客的抱怨漠不關心。

The landlord was uncaring about his tenants' complaints.

Usage

For indifference to others' troubles or social issues.

Similar

  • 😑漠不在乎mò bù zài hū

    nonchalant — Nonchalant.

    He shrugged with a nonchalant air.

  • 🥶不理不睬bù lǐ bù cǎi

    give sb the cold shoulder; ignore — The cold shoulder.

    She gave him the cold shoulder.

Opposite

  • 🔎無微不至wú wēi bù zhì

    meticulous arrangements — Attentive to every need.

    The nurse made meticulous arrangements for every patient's comfort.

  • 📡熱切關注rè qiè guān zhù

    follow closely; with keen interest — Following closely.

    The public is following the trial closely.
