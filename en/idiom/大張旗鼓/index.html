大張旗鼓

dà zhāng qí gǔ

In English

  • with great fanfare

Meaning

With great fanfare.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • make a big splash
  • in a big way

Example

市長大張旗鼓地展開這項宣傳活動。

The mayor launched the campaign with great fanfare.

Usage

Neutral, but can imply being too high-profile.

Similar

  • 📢大力鼓吹dà lì gǔ chuī

    champion; advocate — Vigorously promoting.

    My mother is all for learning a second language.

  • 🎺大肆張揚dà sì zhāng yáng

    fanfare — Trumpeting it everywhere.

    The mayor announced the opening with great fanfare.

Opposite

  • 🤫不打草驚蛇bù dǎ cǎo jīng shé

    don't tip one's hand; don't alert the target — Not tipping one's hand.

    Don't tip your hand until we have enough evidence.

  • 😐不動聲色bù dòng shēng sè

    keep a poker face; show no emotion — Keeping a poker face.

    He kept a poker face and quietly got the plan ready.
