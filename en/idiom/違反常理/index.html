違反常理

wéi fǎn cháng lǐ

In English

  • go against the grain to do sth

Meaning

Against common sense.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • defy common sense
  • go against all logic
  • be counterintuitive

Example

用低於土地價值的價格賣房子，違反常理。

Selling a house for less than the land is worth goes against all common sense.

Usage

Describes something strange or illogical; more about logic than 不合情理.

Similar

  • 🤨不合情理bù hé qíng lǐ

    unreasonable; illogical — Against reason and human feeling; stresses feeling.

    Her demand for an immediate refund was unreasonable.

  • 🤯匪夷所思fěi yí suǒ sī

    mind-boggling — Bizarre beyond imagination; stresses surprise.

    The magician's last trick was mind-boggling.

Opposite

  • 🆗合情合理hé qíng hé lǐ

    be fair and reasonable; make perfect sense — Reasonable and humane.

    The company's compensation plan was fair and reasonable, and everyone could accept it.

  • 🚆順理成章shùn lǐ chéng zhāng

    follow naturally; be a natural result — A natural consequence.

    Given his excellent performance, becoming manager was a natural step.
