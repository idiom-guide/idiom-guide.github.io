顧人怨

gù rén yuàn

In English

  • despised

Meaning

Despised; a pain in the neck.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • a pain in the neck
  • universally disliked

Example

那個吵鬧的鄰居很顧人怨，整層樓的人都討厭他。

The loud neighbor is despised by everyone on the floor.

Usage

From Taiwanese Hokkien; colloquial.

Similar

  • 🤮令人反感lìng rén fǎn gǎn

    distasteful; off-putting — Distasteful.

    His constant bragging was distasteful.

  • 👉千夫所指qiān fū suǒ zhǐ

    universally condemned — Universally condemned.

    The attack was universally condemned.

Opposite

  • ❤️惹人憐愛rě rén lián ài

    adorable — Adorable.

    The puppy looked adorable in its tiny coat.

  • 🗳️眾望所歸zhòng wàng suǒ guī

    the people's choice; enjoy popular support — The people's choice.

    She was the people's choice for the post.
