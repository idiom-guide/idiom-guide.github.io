觀察風向

guān chá fēng xiàng

In English

  • see where the wind is blowing

Meaning

Seeing which way the wind is blowing.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • see which way the wind is blowing
  • test the political winds
  • wait and see

Example

發言之前，這位政治人物先觀察風向。

Before speaking out, the politician saw where the wind was blowing.

Usage

Often implies opportunism, though it can simply mean caution.

Similar

  • 🪑靜觀其變jìng guān qí biàn

    stay tuned; sit tight and assess — Sitting tight and watching.

    Let's sit tight and assess the situation before we act.

  • 🏊隨波逐流suí bō zhú liú

    swim with the stream — Going with the flow.

    Don't just swim with the stream; choose your own path.

Opposite

  • 🐺一意孤行yī yì gū xíng

    go it alone; act arbitrarily — Going it alone, ignoring advice.

    He went it alone, ignoring the advice of every adviser.

  • 🌳獨樹一幟dú shù yī zhì

    unique in — Standing out on one's own.

    The restaurant is unique in its blend of Taiwanese and French cooking.
