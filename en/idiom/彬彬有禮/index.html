彬彬有禮

bīn bīn yǒu lǐ

In English

  • courteous
  • well-mannered

Meaning

Courteous and well-mannered.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • polite

Example

服務生從頭到尾都彬彬有禮。

The waiter was courteous throughout.

Usage

Describes manners and speech.

Similar

  • 🎩文質彬彬wén zhì bīn bīn

    educated; sensible — Refined and cultured; about bearing.

    He is an educated and gentle young man.

  • 🎀家教良好jiā jiào liáng hǎo

    be raised well — Raised well.

    You can tell she was raised well.

Opposite

  • 😏傲慢無禮ào màn wú lǐ

    arrogant — Arrogant and rude.

    The clerk was arrogant and rude.

  • 🦍粗魯無禮cū lǔ wú lǐ

    indecorous; obnoxious — Crude and impolite.

    The obnoxious customer shouted at the waiter.
