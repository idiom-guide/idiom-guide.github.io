全身而退

quán shēn ér tuì

In English

  • just walk away

Meaning

Walking away unscathed.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • walk away unscathed
  • get out while the going is good

Example

他設法從這筆交易中全身而退。

He managed to just walk away from the deal.

Usage

For business, politics and crises.

Similar

  • 🏞️急流勇退jí liú yǒng tuì

    quit while one is ahead; go out on top — Quitting while one is ahead.

    He chose to go out on top, retiring after his best season.

  • 🛡️毫髮無傷háo fà wú shāng

    unscathed; scathing — Unscathed.

    Both drivers walked away unscathed.

Opposite

  • 🕳️血本無歸xuè běn wú guī

    lose one's entire investment — Losing one's entire investment.

    Many retail investors lost their entire investment.

  • 🤕一敗塗地yī bài tú dì

    fall flat on your face — Falling flat on one's face.

    The campaign fell flat on its face.
