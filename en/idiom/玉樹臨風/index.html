玉樹臨風

yù shù lín fēng

In English

  • dashing
  • strikingly handsome

Meaning

Like a jade tree standing in the wind: a handsome, elegant man.Positive · General

Example

他穿著合身的西裝，玉樹臨風。

He cut a dashing figure in his tailored suit.

Usage

Used for men, usually as praise. In casual speech it can be said with a joking tone.

Similar

  • 🕴️風流倜儻fēng liú tì tǎng

    dashing; debonair — Carefree and debonair; leans toward temperament.

    The prince is dashing and debonair.

  • 🤵一表人才yī biǎo rén cái

    a fine figure of a man; good-looking and capable — Good-looking and capable; looks plus talent.

    He is a fine figure of a man with a bright future.

Opposite

  • 🤷其貌不揚qí mào bù yáng

    unprepossessing; plain-looking — Plain-looking; a gentler way of putting it.

    He was plain-looking, but everyone admired his talent.

  • 🥱平淡無奇píng dàn wú qí

    run-of-the-mill — Ordinary, with nothing special about it.

    It was just a run-of-the-mill day at the office.
