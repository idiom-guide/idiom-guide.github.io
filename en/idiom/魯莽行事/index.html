魯莽行事

lǔ mǎng xíng shì

In English

  • throw caution/risk to the winds

Meaning

Throwing caution to the winds.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • act recklessly
  • go off half-cocked

Example

他魯莽行事，把全部家當都投進去。

He threw caution to the winds and invested everything.

Usage

For investments, decisions and risks.

Similar

  • 💣不計後果bù jì hòu guǒ

    recklessness — Reckless.

    His recklessness cost him his job.

  • 🎲孤注一擲gū zhù yī zhì

    a desperate play — A desperate play.

    Selling his house to fund the film was a desperate play.

Opposite

  • 🐾謹慎行動jǐn shèn xíng dòng

    tread carefully — Treading carefully.

    In unfamiliar markets, it pays to tread carefully.

  • 🗿深思熟慮shēn sī shú lǜ

    think it through carefully; give careful thought — Thinking it through.

    Major decisions require careful thought, not haste.
