循規蹈矩

xún guī dǎo jǔ

In English

  • by the book
  • play by the rules

Meaning

By the book.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • toe the line

Example

他循規蹈矩，從不冒險。

He plays by the rules and never takes risks.

Usage

Can be praise or criticism; negatively, it means overly cautious and conventional.

Similar

  • 📋按部就班àn bù jiù bān

    step by step; by the book — Step by step.

    New hires are trained step by step.

  • 🍪蕭規曹隨xiāo guī cáo suí

    cookie cutter — Following precedent.

    Instead of making changes, the new mayor kept the old policies in cookie-cutter fashion.

Opposite

  • 🚫不守規矩bù shǒu guī jǔ

    Wild — Unruly.

    The kids were wild at the party.

  • 🌋肆無忌憚sì wú jì dàn

    with no restraint; unbridled — Unbridled.

    The bully acted with no restraint.
