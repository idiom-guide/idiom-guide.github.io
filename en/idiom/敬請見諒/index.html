9 · Thanks & Humble Phrases

敬請見諒

jìng qǐng jiàn liàng

In English

  • we ask for your understanding

Meaning

We ask for your understanding.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • we apologize for any inconvenience
  • please excuse us

Example

行程有所變動，敬請見諒。

The schedule has changed, and we ask for your understanding.

Usage

Formal notices; often 造成不便，敬請見諒 'we apologize for any inconvenience'.

Similar

  • 😅多多包涵duō duō bāo hán

    please bear with us — Please bear with us.

    This is our first time hosting, so please bear with us.

  • 🙏請別見怪qǐng bié jiàn guài

    no hard feelings — No hard feelings.

    I was only joking. No hard feelings?

Opposite

  • 💁毫無歉意háo wú qiàn yì

    unapologetically — Unapologetic.

    She unapologetically cut in line.
