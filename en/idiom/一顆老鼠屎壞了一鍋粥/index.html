一顆老鼠屎，壞了一鍋粥

yī kē lǎo shǔ shǐ, huài le yī guō zhōu

In English

  • a bad apple spoils the bunch

Meaning

One rotten apple spoils the barrel.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • one bad apple spoils the barrel
  • a single flaw ruins the whole
  • one rotten apple spoils the whole bunch

Example

一個不誠實的員工毀了我們的名聲，真是一顆老鼠屎，壞了一鍋粥。

One dishonest employee ruined our reputation: a bad apple spoils the bunch.

Usage

A proverb blaming one bad member for ruining the group; also 一粒老鼠屎壞了一鍋粥.

Similar

  • 🐏害群之馬hài qún zhī mǎ

    a black sheep; one who harms the group — The black sheep; specifically a person.

    He is the company's black sheep and keeps leading newcomers astray.

  • ⏬毀於一旦huǐ yú yī dàn

    be ruined in an instant — Years of work destroyed in a moment.

    Years of effort were ruined in an instant by a single oversight.

Opposite

  • 🔸瑕不掩瑜xiá bù yǎn yú

    a flaw cannot hide the jade's beauty; the merits outweigh the flaws — A small flaw doesn't hide the jewel's luster.

    The book has a few errors, but its merits still outweigh them.

  • ☺無傷大雅wú shāng dà yǎ

    be harmless; do no real harm — Harmless; no big deal.

    This small error does no real harm; don't worry about it.
