令人反感

lìng rén fǎn gǎn

In English

  • distasteful = unpleasant

Meaning

Distasteful.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • distasteful
  • off-putting
  • objectionable

Example

他不停吹噓，令人反感。

His constant bragging was distasteful.

Usage

For behavior, attitudes and advertising.

Similar

  • 🤢令人不快lìng rén bù kuài

    unpalatable — Unpleasant; milder.

    The smell was unpalatable to most guests.

  • 😒顧人怨gù rén yuàn

    despised — Despised.

    The loud neighbor is despised by everyone on the floor.

Opposite

  • 🌸如沐春風rú mù chūn fēng

    feel so good; pleasant — Like a spring breeze.

    Talking with her made me feel so good, like a spring breeze.

  • ❤️惹人憐愛rě rén lián ài

    adorable — Adorable.

    The puppy looked adorable in its tiny coat.
