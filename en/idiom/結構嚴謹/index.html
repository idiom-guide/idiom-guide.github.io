結構嚴謹

jié gòu yán jǐn

In English

  • solid structure

Meaning

Tightly structured.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • tightly structured
  • well constructed

Example

這部小說結構嚴謹，每一章都朝結局推進。

The novel has a solid structure, with every chapter building toward the ending.

Usage

For novels, essays, arguments and buildings.

Similar

  • 🗃️有條有理yǒu tiáo yǒu lǐ

    orderly; methodical — Orderly and methodical.

    Her desk is always orderly.

  • 🧷環環相扣huán huán xiāng kòu

    be tightly interlinked — Tightly interlocking.

    The plot of this thriller is so tightly interlinked that you can't look away.

Opposite

  • 🧶雜亂無章zá luàn wú zhāng

    haphazard — Haphazard.

    The files were piled up in a haphazard manner.

  • 🧀漏洞百出lòu dòng bǎi chū

    be full of holes; be riddled with flaws — Full of holes.

    His story was full of holes, and nobody believed it.
