燃眉之急

rán méi zhī jí

In English

  • in extremis

Meaning

A pressing emergency.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • a pressing emergency
  • urgent need

Example

醫院的物資已是燃眉之急，補給在最後一刻送達。

The hospital needed supplies in extremis, and the delivery arrived just in time.

Usage

Often 解燃眉之急 (meet an urgent need).

Similar

  • 1️⃣當務之急dāng wù zhī jí

    top priority; priority one — The top priority.

    Fixing the leak is our top priority.

  • ⏰迫在眉睫pò zài méi jié

    time is a luxury we don't have; sth. is looming — Looming right in front of us.

    The deadline is looming, and time is a luxury we don't have.

Opposite

  • 🌅來日方長lái rì fāng cháng

    there will be many more opportunities ahead — There's plenty of time ahead.

    There will be many more opportunities ahead to work together.

  • 🤏無關緊要wú guān jǐn yào

    immaterial — Of no importance.

    Whether we meet at six or seven is immaterial.
