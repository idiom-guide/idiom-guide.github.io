扛鼎之作

gāng dǐng zhī zuò

In English

  • tour de force

Meaning

A tour de force.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • masterpiece
  • magnum opus

Example

這部電影是敘事的扛鼎之作。

The film is a tour de force of storytelling.

Usage

For a creator's most important or representative work.

Similar

  • 📚集大成jí dà chéng

    the culmination; the epitome of sth — The culmination.

    The album is the culmination of her career.

  • 🌊博大精深bó dà jīng shēn

    extensive and profound — Broad and profound.

    Chinese medicine is extensive and profound.

Opposite

  • 🥱平淡無奇píng dàn wú qí

    run-of-the-mill — Run-of-the-mill.

    It was just a run-of-the-mill day at the office.
