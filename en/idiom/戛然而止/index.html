戛然而止

jiá rán ér zhǐ

In English

  • come to an abrupt halt
  • stop short

Meaning

Stopping abruptly.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • cut off

Example

音樂戛然而止。

The music came to an abrupt halt.

Usage

戛 is read jiá; for sounds, performances, conversations and plans.

Similar

  • 💨無疾而終wú jí ér zhōng

    fizzle out; come to nothing — Fizzling out.

    The peace talks fizzled out.

  • 🥀胎死腹中tāi sǐ fù zhōng

    stillborn; die on the vine — Dying on the vine.

    The project died on the vine.

Opposite

  • 🚰源源不絕yuán yuán bù jué

    endless; a steady stream — An endless supply.

    The river provides an endless, renewable source of power.

  • 🌊餘波蕩漾yú bō dàng yàng

    aftermath — A lingering aftermath.

    The aftermath of the scandal rippled through the company for months.
