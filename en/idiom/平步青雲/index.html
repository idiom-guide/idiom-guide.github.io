平步青雲

píng bù qīng yún

In English

  • meteoric ascension

Meaning

A meteoric rise.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • a meteoric rise
  • rise rapidly

Example

他平步青雲，登上公司最高層。

He enjoyed a meteoric ascension to the top of the firm.

Usage

For careers in business, politics and entertainment.

Similar

  • 📈扶搖直上fú yáo zhí shàng

    leapfrogging-skyrocketing — Skyrocketing; also of figures.

    Her career has been skyrocketing since her last film.

  • 📶步步高升bù bù gāo shēng

    rise steadily through the ranks — Rising steadily through the ranks.

    He rose steadily through the ranks to become a partner.

Opposite

  • 📉一落千丈yī luò qiān zhàng

    plummet; nosedive — Plummeting.

    Sales plummeted after the recall.

  • 🏞️江河日下jiāng hé rì xià

    in steady decline — In steady decline.

    The industry has been in steady decline.
