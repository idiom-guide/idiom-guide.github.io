機智反諷

jī zhì fǎn fěng

In English

  • quipster

Meaning

Witty irony.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • witty irony
  • dry wit

Example

主持人擅長機智反諷，犀利的反話讓觀眾笑聲不斷。

The host is a quipster whose sharp irony keeps the audience laughing.

Usage

反諷 means saying the opposite of what one means; 機智 stresses quick wit.

Similar

  • 🕶️指桑罵槐zhǐ sāng mà huái

    make veiled criticism; throw shade (informal) — Pointing at the mulberry to curse the locust: veiled criticism.

    She threw shade at her rival without naming her.

  • 🤭話中有話huà zhōng yǒu huà

    have a hidden meaning; imply something — There's more to it than the words.

    She was hinting at something, as if blaming me.

Opposite

  • 📢直言不諱zhí yán bù huì

    speak frankly; not mince words — Speaking bluntly; no irony involved.

    She spoke frankly and pointed out the flaws in the plan.

  • 🚪開門見山kāi mén jiàn shān

    get straight to the point — Getting straight to the point.

    He got straight to the point and asked about the price.
