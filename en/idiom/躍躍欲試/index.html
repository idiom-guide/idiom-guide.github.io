躍躍欲試

yuè yuè yù shì

In English

  • raring to go
  • itching to try

Meaning

Raring to go.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • eager to have a go

Example

孩子們躍躍欲試，想玩新遊戲。

The kids were itching to try the new game.

Usage

For newcomers, children or athletes eager to start.

Similar

  • 🏹蓄勢待發xù shì dài fā

    born ready — Poised and ready.

    Backstage, the band was poised to start; they were born ready.

  • 🐜心癢難耐xīn yǎng nán nài

    be itching to do sth — Itching to do something.

    The kids are itching to open the presents.

Opposite

  • 🥱無精打采wú jīng dǎ cǎi

    listless; lethargic — Listless.

    He looked listless after the long trip.

  • 🥀萎靡不振wěi mǐ bù zhèn

    listless; in low spirits — Listless and dispirited.

    The team was in low spirits after the defeat.
