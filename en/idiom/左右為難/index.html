左右為難

zuǒ yòu wéi nán

In English

  • caught between a rock and a hard place

Meaning

Caught between a rock and a hard place.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • torn
  • in a dilemma

Example

我左右為難。

I'm caught between a rock and a hard place.

Usage

For being stuck between two sides.

Similar

  • ↔️進退維谷jìn tuì wéi gǔ

    be in a quandary; caught in a bind — In a quandary.

    She is in a quandary over the job offer.

  • 🚷動輒得咎dòng zhé dé jiù

    no-win situation — A no-win situation.

    Whatever I do, someone complains. It's a no-win situation.

Opposite

  • ✌️兩全其美liǎng quán qí měi

    get the best of both — The best of both worlds.

    The new plan lets us get the best of both.

  • 🏄左右逢源zuǒ yòu féng yuán

    reap rewards; benefit from both sides — Benefiting from both sides.

    A savvy broker can reap rewards by benefiting from both sides.
