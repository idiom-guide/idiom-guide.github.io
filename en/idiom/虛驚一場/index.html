虛驚一場

xū jīng yī cháng

In English

  • false alarm

Meaning

A false alarm.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • a false alarm
  • much ado about nothing
  • a scare that came to nothing

Example

原來是虛驚一場，煙是從烤麵包機冒出來的。

It was a false alarm; the smoke came from the toaster.

Usage

At the start or end of a sentence, with a sense of relief.

Similar

  • 😦杞人憂天qǐ rén yōu tiān

    worry about nothing; fear that the sky will fall — Worrying about what won't happen.

    Don't worry about nothing; it isn't that bad.

  • 🐍杯弓蛇影bēi gōng shé yǐng

    Afraid of one's own shadow; extremely suspicious — Mistaking a bow's reflection for a snake: frightened by one's own imagination.

    After the burglary, she was afraid of her own shadow.

Opposite

  • 🔮一語成讖yī yǔ chéng chèn

    one's words prove prophetic — An offhand remark that came true.

    His casual remark about a storm proved prophetic.

  • 🥠不幸言中bù xìng yán zhōng

    unfortunately guess right — Unfortunately proven right.

    Unfortunately he was right: we really were laid off.
