毫不吝惜

háo bù lìn xī

In English

  • profusely

Meaning

Giving freely, without holding back.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • without holding back
  • lavishly

Example

她毫不吝惜地稱讚學生。

She praised her students profusely.

Usage

Often with praise, effort or donations.

Similar

  • 👏大加讚賞dà jiā zàn shǎng

    speak highly of — Heaping praise on.

    Critics spoke highly of the young director's first film.

  • 💪不遺餘力bù yí yú lì

    has gone to great pains; spare no efforts — Sparing no effort.

    The volunteers spared no effort in rescuing the stranded hikers.

Opposite

  • 🤑斤斤計較jīn jīn jì jiào

    petty; calculating — Penny-pinching.

    Don't be so petty about the bill.

  • 🐿惜字如金xī zì rú jīn

    be sparing with words; a man of few words — Sparing with words.

    He is sparing with words and never answers in more than three.
