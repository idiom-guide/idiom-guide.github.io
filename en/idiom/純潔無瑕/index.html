純潔無瑕

chún jié wú xiá

In English

  • pristine

Meaning

Pristine.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • spotless
  • unblemished

Example

雪地純潔無瑕。

The snow was pristine.

Usage

For scenery, and also for character and reputation.

Similar

  • 🧼一塵不染yī chén bù rǎn

    spotless — Spotless.

    The hospital floors were spotless.

  • 🕊️名聲高潔míng shēng gāo jié

    reputationally pristine — Reputationally pristine.

    Even his rivals say he is reputationally pristine.

Opposite

  • 💼欺詐腐敗qī zhà fǔ bài

    chicanery and corruption — Chicanery and corruption.

    The company's collapse revealed years of chicanery and corruption.
