深諳此道

shēn ān cǐ dào

In English

  • know how to play the game

Meaning

Knowing how to play the game.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • well versed in
  • know the ropes

Example

她深諳此道，每場談判都贏。

She knows how to play the game and wins every negotiation.

Usage

For negotiation, politics, business and other skills.

Similar

  • 🥋箇中高手gè zhōng gāo shǒu

    best of the best — An expert.

    He is the best of the best in the world of sushi.

  • 🚗駕輕就熟jià qīng jiù shú

    mastering; getting into one's stride — Getting into one's stride.

    After a month, she was getting into her stride at the new job.

Opposite

  • 🈳一竅不通yī qiào bù tōng

    all Greek to me — All Greek to me.

    Quantum physics is all Greek to me.

  • 🌓一知半解yī zhī bàn jiě

    have only a smattering of knowledge; know just a little — A little knowledge.

    He has only a smattering of knowledge about the issue, yet loves to give his opinion.
