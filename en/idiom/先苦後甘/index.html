2 · Change & Fate

先苦後甘

xiān kǔ hòu gān

In English

  • take short term pain for long term gain

Meaning

Short-term pain for long-term gain.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • no pain, no gain
  • hard work pays off

Example

訓練很辛苦，但先苦後甘。

Training is hard, but it is short-term pain for long-term gain.

Usage

For study, training, saving and starting a business.

Similar

  • 🌅否極泰來pǐ jí tài lái

    the tide will turn; every cloud has a silver lining — The tide will turn; about luck.

    After a hard year, the tide will turn.

  • 🎓十年寒窗無人問，一舉成名天下知shí nián hán chuāng wú rén wèn, yī jǔ chéng míng tiān xià zhī

    a decade of obscure toil, then overnight fame — Years of obscure toil, then overnight fame.

    From a decade of obscure toil to overnight fame, the novelist's story reads like a fairy tale.

Opposite

  • 🥂及時行樂jí shí xíng lè

    carpe diem; eat, drink, and be merry — Carpe diem.

    Carpe diem! Let's go out tonight.

  • 🎭樂極生悲lè jí shēng bēi

    extreme joy turns sorrow — Joy turning to sorrow.

    At the party he danced so hard that he broke his leg; extreme joy turned to sorrow.
