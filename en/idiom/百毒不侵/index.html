百毒不侵

bǎi dú bù qīn

In English

  • Teflon-esque
  • be impervious to

Meaning

Impervious to everything.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • bulletproof

Example

這位政治人物彷彿百毒不侵，任何醜聞都沾不上他。

The politician seems Teflon-esque: no scandal sticks to him.

Usage

For people unaffected by criticism, scandal or illness; sometimes ironic.

Similar

  • 🛡️刀槍不入dāo qiāng bù rù

    impenetrable — Impenetrable.

    The old castle's walls are impenetrable.

  • 💯無懈可擊wú xiè kě jī

    be flawless; be unassailable — Unassailable.

    Her argument was flawless.

Opposite

  • 🍃弱不禁風ruò bù jīn fēng

    frail; delicate — Frail.

    She looked frail in the cold.

  • 🥚不堪一擊bù kān yī jī

    collapse at the first blow; be easily crushed — Collapsing at the first blow.

    The team looks strong but crumbles at the first blow.
