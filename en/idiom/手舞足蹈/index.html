手舞足蹈

shǒu wǔ zú dǎo

In English

  • jump for joy
  • dance with delight

Meaning

Hands waving and feet jumping: so delighted that one can hardly keep still.Neutral · General

Example

孩子們聽到消息，高興得手舞足蹈。

The kids jumped for joy when they heard the news.

Usage

Leans toward body movement, often for children or very excited people. The near-synonym about flying brows is about the face.

Similar

  • 🗣️眉飛色舞méi fēi sè wǔ

    animated; beaming with excitement — Excited and animated; about the face.

    He described the trip, animated and beaming with excitement.

  • 😆興高采烈xìng gāo cǎi liè

    gleeful; a gleeful smile/shout — In high spirits; about mood.

    The children ran out of school in gleeful anticipation of summer.

Opposite

  • 😞垂頭喪氣chuí tóu sàng qì

    dejected; hang one's head — Head hanging, spirits low.

    After losing the match, everyone went home dejected.

  • 🥱無精打采wú jīng dǎ cǎi

    listless; lethargic — Listless and lacking energy.

    He looked listless after the long trip.
