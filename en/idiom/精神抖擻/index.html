精神抖擻

jīng shén dǒu sǒu

In English

  • do sth ...with bells on

Meaning

Bright-eyed and raring to go.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • do sth with bells on
  • raring to go
  • bright-eyed and bushy-tailed

Example

他精神抖擻地來上班。

He turned up to work with bells on.

Usage

Often of arriving at work, school or an event.

Similar

  • 😁精神飽滿jīng shén bǎo mǎn

    (high) spirited — In high spirits.

    The children arrived in high spirits.

  • 🌬️意氣風發yì qì fēng fā

    be in high spirits; full of vigor — Full of vigor and confidence.

    The graduates were in high spirits.

Opposite

  • 🥱無精打采wú jīng dǎ cǎi

    listless; lethargic — Listless.

    He looked listless after the long trip.

  • 🥀萎靡不振wěi mǐ bù zhèn

    listless; in low spirits — In low spirits.

    The team was in low spirits after the defeat.
