探索自我

tàn suǒ zì wǒ

In English

  • self-discovery

Meaning

Self-discovery.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • find oneself
  • soul-searching

Example

這趟旅行是一段探索自我的旅程。

The trip was a journey of self-discovery.

Usage

For travel, personal growth and turning points in life.

Similar

  • 🧘自我反省zì wǒ fǎn xǐng

    soul-searching — Soul-searching.

    After the defeat, the coach did some serious soul-searching.

  • 🧘修身養性xiū shēn yǎng xìng

    spiritual journey — A spiritual journey.

    He sees his daily walks as part of a spiritual journey.

Opposite

  • 🏊隨波逐流suí bō zhú liú

    swim with the stream — Going with the flow.

    Don't just swim with the stream; choose your own path.
