自我反省

zì wǒ fǎn xǐng

In English

  • soul-searching

Meaning

Self-reflection.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • self-reflection
  • introspect
  • reflect on oneself

Example

比賽輸了之後，教練做了深刻的自我反省。

After the defeat, the coach did some serious soul-searching.

Usage

Often 深刻的自我反省 (deep self-reflection); positive, implying growth.

Similar

  • 😖悔不當初huǐ bù dāng chū

    rue the day — Regretting what one did; about regret.

    He rues the day he signed that contract.

  • 🛀洗心革面xǐ xīn gé miàn

    turn over a new leaf; reform oneself — Turning over a new leaf.

    After his release he turned over a new leaf and started afresh.

Opposite

  • 🤓自以為是zì yǐ wéi shì

    be self-righteous; think one knows best — Thinking oneself always right.

    He thinks he knows best and never listens to anyone.

  • 🙅自欺欺人zì qī qī rén

    lying to oneself; bury one's head in the sand — Fooling oneself.

    Pretending nothing is wrong is just burying your head in the sand.
