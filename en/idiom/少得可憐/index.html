少得可憐

shǎo de kě lián

In English

  • measly (income)

Meaning

Pitifully small.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • measly
  • pitifully small
  • a pittance

Example

他少得可憐的收入，幾乎付不起房租。

His measly income barely covers the rent.

Usage

For money, time or resources; sympathetic or complaining.

Similar

  • 🤏寥寥無幾liáo liáo wú jǐ

    very few; a mere handful — Very few.

    Very few applicants met the requirements.

  • 🥤杯水車薪bēi shuǐ chē xīn

    far too little to help — A drop in the bucket.

    The subsidy is far too little to help.

Opposite

  • 🔢不計其數bù jì qí shù

    countless — Countless.

    Countless families were affected.

  • 👌綽綽有餘chuò chuò yǒu yú

    more than enough — More than enough.

    Ten thousand dollars is more than enough for the trip.
