獨立自主

dú lì zì zhǔ

In English

  • cut the (umbilical) cord; become independent
  • cut loose from

Meaning

Independent; standing on one's own feet.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • cut the (umbilical) cord
  • become independent

Example

二十歲那年，他獨立自主，搬出去自己住。

At twenty, he cut the cord and moved out on his own.

Usage

For people, organizations and countries.

Similar

  • 🏗️白手起家bái shǒu qǐ jiā

    a self-made man/woman; start from scratch — Self-made.

    She is a self-made woman who started from scratch.

  • 🌏闖蕩世界chuǎng dàng shì jiè

    navigate this world — Going out into the world.

    At eighteen, he left home to navigate this world on his own.

Opposite

  • 🎮聽命於人tīng mìng yú rén

    do sth at sb's behest — Doing someone else's bidding.

    The agents act at the behest of a foreign government.

  • 🍼嬌生慣養jiāo shēng guàn yǎng

    pampered; spoiled — Pampered and spoiled.

    The pampered prince had never washed a dish.
