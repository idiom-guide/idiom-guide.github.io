驚濤駭浪

jīng tāo hài làng

In English

  • great challenges and volatility

Meaning

Stormy seas; turbulent times.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • stormy seas
  • turbulent times

Example

這家公司在危機中熬過了驚濤駭浪。

The company survived stormy seas during the crisis.

Usage

Usually figurative, for turmoil or upheaval in life.

Similar

  • ⚠️危機四伏wēi jī sì fú

    be fraught with risks — Fraught with risks.

    The jungle path is fraught with risks.

  • 🚧困難重重kùn nán chóng chóng

    be fraught with problems/difficulties — Fraught with difficulties.

    The plan is fraught with problems.

Opposite

  • ⛵一帆風順yī fān fēng shùn

    smooth sailing — Smooth sailing.

    I wish you smooth sailing in your new post.

  • 🎤歌舞昇平gē wǔ shēng píng

    peaceful and prosperous — Peace and prosperity.

    The capital looked peaceful and prosperous, with no sign of the war to come.
