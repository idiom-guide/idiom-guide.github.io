逢場作戲

féng chǎng zuò xì

In English

  • dalliance with sb

Meaning

A fling; just playing along.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • a fling
  • just playing along

Example

他和那位女演員只是逢場作戲。

It was only a brief dalliance with the actress.

Usage

For romances and social obligations not taken seriously.

Similar

  • 🎭虛情假意xū qíng jiǎ yì

    insincere; hollow sentiments — Insincere.

    Her feigned friendliness fooled no one.

Opposite

  • 🎬假戲真做jiǎ xì zhēn zuò

    what began as an act became real — An act that turned real.

    What began as an act became real for the two actors.

  • 🥄弱水三千，只取一瓢ruò shuǐ sān qiān, zhǐ qǔ yī piáo

    out of all the beauties, I only have eyes for you — Only having eyes for one.

    Out of all the beauties in the world, I only have eyes for you.
