水漲船高

shuǐ zhǎng chuán gāo

In English

  • a rising tide lifts all boats

Meaning

A rising tide lifts all boats.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • go up in tandem
  • ride the wave

Example

指數一反彈，個股水漲船高。

When the index rallies, a rising tide lifts all boats.

Usage

For stock markets, prices and someone's market value.

Similar

  • 🌦️雨露均霑yǔ lù jūn zhān

    spread wealth evenly; distribute benefits evenly — Spreading the benefits evenly.

    The program aims to spread wealth evenly across all regions.

  • 🛶順勢而為shùn shì ér wèi

    go with the trend; go with the flow — Going with the flow.

    When starting a business, go with the trend instead of fighting it.

Opposite

  • 📉一落千丈yī luò qiān zhàng

    plummet; nosedive — Plummeting.

    Sales plummeted after the recall.
