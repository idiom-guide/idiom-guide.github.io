10 · Learning & Scholarship

不恥下問

bù chǐ xià wèn

In English

  • not too proud to ask

Meaning

Not too proud to ask.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • there's no shame in asking
  • ask and learn

Example

真正的學者不恥下問。

A real scholar is not too proud to ask.

Usage

Formal; from the Analects.

Similar

  • 🙏不吝賜教bù lìn cì jiào

    I welcome your feedback — I welcome your feedback.

    This is only a first draft, and I welcome your feedback.

  • 🙇‍♂️虛懷若谷xū huái ruò gǔ

    be humble and open-minded — Humble.

    The more accomplished a person is, the more humble and open-minded they tend to be.

Opposite

  • 🤴自命不凡zì mìng bù fán

    overestimate oneself; hotshot — A hotshot.

    The young hotshot thought he was above the rules.

  • 🛑剛愎自用gāng bì zì yòng

    opinionated; stubborn — Opinionated.

    The stubborn chairman ignored every warning.
