欲擒故縱

yù qín gù zòng

In English

  • give sb enough rope
  • let sb go in order to catch them later

Meaning

Letting go in order to catch later.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • play hard to get
  • let sb go to catch them later

Example

他故意放走嫌犯，欲擒故縱。

He let the suspect go on purpose, giving him enough rope.

Usage

A military tactic now also used in negotiation and romance.

Similar

  • 🐷扮豬吃老虎bàn zhū chī lǎo hǔ

    sandbagging — Playing dumb to win.

    He played dumb at the table, sandbagging his rivals before winning the pot.

  • 🐆伺機而動sì jī ér dòng

    bide one's time; wait for the right moment — Biding one's time.

    The investors are waiting for the right moment to strike.

Opposite

  • 🐍打草驚蛇dǎ cǎo jīng shé

    wake a sleeping dog — Tipping off the target.

    Don't wake a sleeping dog by questioning him too early.

  • 🚅操之過急cāo zhī guò jí

    be overly hasty; act in haste — Overhasty.

    This matter must be handled slowly; we can't be overly hasty.
