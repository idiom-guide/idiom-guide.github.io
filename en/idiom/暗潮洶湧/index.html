暗潮洶湧

àn cháo xiōng yǒng

In English

  • undercurrents
  • simmering tensions

Meaning

A calm surface over hidden conflict, rivalry or unease that is building underneath.Neutral · General

Example

董事會表面上一團和氣，私底下卻暗潮洶湧。

The board looks all smiles on the surface, but there are strong undercurrents.

Usage

Used for politics, the workplace, the inner life of groups or personal relationships, stressing a calm surface over hidden tension. Once the conflict is out in the open, an idiom for open hostility fits better.

Similar

  • 🌀暗流湧動àn liú yǒng dòng

    undercurrents at work; hidden forces stirring — Hidden forces stirring; nearly synonymous.

    Beneath the calm surface, undercurrents were at work as every side waited.

  • ⛈山雨欲來shān yǔ yù lái

    a storm is brewing; trouble looms — Signs that a storm is about to break; about imminence.

    As word of layoffs spread, the whole company sensed a storm brewing.

Opposite

  • ⛵風平浪靜fēng píng làng jìng

    calm; all quiet — Calm, with nothing stirring.

    After the row, the office was calm again.

  • 🕊相安無事xiāng ān wú shì

    coexist peacefully; keep the peace — Getting along without conflict.

    The two firms have coexisted peacefully for years.
