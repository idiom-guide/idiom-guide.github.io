感謝忠告

gǎn xiè zhōng gào

In English

  • words to live by

Meaning

Thank you for the advice.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • thank you for the advice
  • point taken

Example

感謝忠告，這些話值得一生奉行。

Thank you for the advice; those are words to live by.

Usage

A polite response when accepting advice.

Similar

  • 📌謹記在心jǐn jì zài xīn

    be taken to heart — Taken to heart.

    Her advice will be taken to heart.

  • 👂洗耳恭聽xǐ ěr gōng tīng

    be all ears — All ears.

    Go ahead, I'm all ears.

Opposite

  • 🐐固執己見gù zhí jǐ jiàn

    opinionated; assertive — Stubbornly holding one's own view.

    He is opinionated and rarely listens to advice.

  • 🐺一意孤行yī yì gū xíng

    go it alone; act arbitrarily — Going it alone.

    He went it alone, ignoring the advice of every adviser.
