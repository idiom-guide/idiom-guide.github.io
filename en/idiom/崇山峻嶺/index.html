崇山峻嶺

chóng shān jùn lǐng

In English

  • towering and steep mountains

Meaning

Towering mountains.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • rugged mountains
  • soaring peaks

Example

這條鐵路穿越崇山峻嶺。

The railway crosses towering and steep mountains.

Usage

Formal; found in Wang Xizhi's Preface to the Orchid Pavilion Poems.

Similar

  • ⛰️山峰連綿shān fēng lián mián

    range upon range of hills — Range upon range of hills.

    Range upon range of hills disappeared into the mist.

  • 🗺️千山萬水qiān shān wàn shuǐ

    a long and tiring journey — A long and tiring journey.

    He made a long and tiring journey across mountains and rivers to see her.
