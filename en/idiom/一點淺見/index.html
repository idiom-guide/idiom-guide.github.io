一點淺見

yī diǎn qiǎn jiàn

In English

  • my two cents
  • just my two cents

Meaning

Just my two cents.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • my humble opinion
  • for what it's worth

Example

一點淺見：我們應該再等一個星期。

Here's my two cents: we should wait a week.

Usage

A modest phrase that doesn't mean the view is shallow; used before or after giving an opinion.

Similar

  • 🧱拋磚引玉pāo zhuān yǐn yù

    just tossing an idea out there — Casting a brick to attract jade.

    I'm just tossing an idea out there to get the discussion started.

  • 🐞微不足道wēi bù zú dào

    insignificant; trivial — Insignificant; used modestly of one's contribution.

    The difference is trivial.

Opposite

  • 🔍真知灼見zhēn zhī zhuó jiàn

    penetrating insight(s); clear-sighted comments — Penetrating insight.

    His clear-sighted comments helped the team see the real problem.

  • 🎙️高談闊論gāo tán kuò lùn

    pontificate on/about sth — Pontificating.

    He loves to pontificate about politics at dinner.
