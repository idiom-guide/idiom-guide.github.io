大開眼界

dà kāi yǎn jiè

In English

  • broaden one's horizons

Meaning

An eye-opener; broadening one's horizons.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • be an eye-opener
  • see the world

Example

出國留學真的讓我大開眼界。

Studying abroad really broadened my horizons.

Usage

Often 真是大開眼界; can be ironic about seeing something strange.

Similar

  • 🗾見多識廣jiàn duō shí guǎng

    be worldly; have seen a lot — Worldly and well-traveled.

    He is worldly and has seen a lot, so no situation frightens him.

  • 🛳增廣見聞zēng guǎng jiàn wén

    broaden one's knowledge; see and learn new things — Broadening one's knowledge.

    The best thing about travel is that it broadens your knowledge.

Opposite

  • 🐸孤陋寡聞gū lòu guǎ wén

    ill-informed; poorly informed — Ill-informed.

    He is ill-informed about world events.

  • 📷管中窺豹guǎn zhōng kuī bào

    see only a small part of the whole; judge the whole from a narrow view — Seeing only a small part.

    Judging by a single report is like seeing the whole through a keyhole.
