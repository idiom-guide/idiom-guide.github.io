不屈不撓

bù qū bù náo

In English

  • invincible, indomitable, dauntless

Meaning

Indomitable.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • indomitable
  • dauntless
  • invincible

Example

這位不屈不撓的登山者冒著暴風雪繼續前進。

The dauntless climber kept going despite the storm.

Usage

For people facing hardship, pressure or opposition.

Similar

  • 🥊不受打擊bù shòu dǎ jī

    undeterred — Undeterred by setbacks.

    Undeterred by the first rejection, she applied to ten more schools.

  • 💧滴水穿石dī shuǐ chuān shí

    constant dripping wears away a stone; perseverance pays off — Perseverance pays off.

    Constant dripping wears away a stone, so keep practicing.

Opposite

  • 🤕一蹶不振yī jué bù zhèn

    never recover; be unable to get back on one's feet — Never recovering.

    He never recovered after the scandal.

  • 🏳️棄械投降qì xiè tóu xiáng

    throw in the towel — Throwing in the towel.

    After the third round, the boxer threw in the towel.
