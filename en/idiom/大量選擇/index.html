大量選擇

dà liàng xuǎn zé

In English

  • a smorgasbord of choices

Meaning

Plenty to choose from.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • a wide range of choices
  • plenty to choose from

Example

飯店的早餐有大量選擇。

The hotel breakfast offers a smorgasbord of choices.

Usage

A plain phrase; in writing, 琳瑯滿目 or 選擇繁多 are common.

Similar

  • 🛍️應有盡有yīng yǒu jìn yǒu

    have everything you could want — Having everything one could want.

    The market has everything you could want.

  • 🌐包羅萬象bāo luó wàn xiàng

    all-encompassing — All-encompassing.

    The encyclopedia is all-encompassing.

Opposite

  • 🔚別無選擇bié wú xuǎn zé

    have no other choice — Having no other choice.

    Weighed down by debt, he had no choice but to sell the house.

  • 🤏寥寥無幾liáo liáo wú jǐ

    very few; a mere handful — Very few.

    Very few applicants met the requirements.
