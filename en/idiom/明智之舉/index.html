明智之舉

míng zhì zhī jǔ

In English

  • smart play

Meaning

A wise move.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • a wise move
  • a smart move

Example

存一些錢以備不時之需，是明智之舉。

Saving some money for emergencies was a smart play.

Usage

Often as 是明智之舉, 'is a smart move'.

Similar

  • ☂️未雨綢繆wèi yǔ chóu móu

    be fully prepared; provident — Being prepared in advance.

    Keep an emergency kit at home; it pays to be fully prepared.

  • 🧮精打細算jīng dǎ xì suàn

    astute with money; budget carefully — Careful budgeting.

    She is astute with money and plans every purchase.

Opposite

  • ⚖️得不償失dé bù cháng shī

    too expensive to be worth it — Not worth the cost.

    Staying up all night to finish was too expensive to be worth it.

  • ⚰️自掘墳墓zì jué fén mù

    dig your own grave — Digging one's own grave.

    By lying to the investors, he dug his own grave.
