粉飾太平

fěn shì tài píng

In English

  • sugarcoating

Meaning

Sugarcoating.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • paper over the cracks
  • whitewash

Example

這份報告在粉飾太平，掩蓋真實情況。

The report was sugarcoating the real situation.

Usage

For reports, official statements and propaganda.

Similar

  • 🎭表面工夫biǎo miàn gōng fu

    tokenism — Lip service.

    The company's diversity program was just tokenism.

  • 🎻大難臨頭仍歌舞升平dà nàn lín tóu réng gē wǔ shēng píng

    Fiddle while Rome burns — Fiddling while Rome burns.

    The board held a party while the firm collapsed. That's fiddling while Rome burns.

Opposite

  • 📢直言不諱zhí yán bù huì

    speak frankly; not mince words — Calling a spade a spade.

    She spoke frankly and pointed out the flaws in the plan.

  • 🔓開誠布公kāi chéng bù gōng

    in full transparency — In full transparency.

    The two leaders spoke in full transparency about their differences.
