脫離群體

tuō lí qún tǐ

In English

  • cut loose from

Meaning

Breaking away from the group.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • break away from
  • go it alone

Example

他脫離群體，獨自旅行。

He cut loose from the group and traveled alone.

Usage

Neutral; can mean independence or isolation.

Similar

  • 🏝️自我放逐zì wǒ fàng zhú

    self-exile — Self-exile.

    After the scandal, he went into self-exile.

Opposite

  • ⛵同舟共濟tóng zhōu gòng jì

    solidarity; help each other — Pulling together.

    In times of crisis, neighbors help each other.

  • 🤝志同道合zhì tóng dào hé

    like minded — Like-minded.

    She found like-minded friends in the chess club.
