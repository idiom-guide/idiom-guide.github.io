別意氣用事

bié yì qì yòng shì

In English

  • keep a cool head
  • don't let emotions drive you

Meaning

Don't act on emotion; keep a cool head.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • don't act on impulse

Example

回那封信之前，先冷靜下來，別意氣用事。

Keep a cool head before replying to that email.

Usage

Advice, often before replying in an argument, negotiating or making a big decision.

Similar

  • 🗺️謀定而後動móu dìng ér hòu dòng

    think before you act — Plan first, then act.

    Think before you act, and make a plan first.

  • 🧖心平氣和xīn píng qì hé

    be calm and composed; keep one's temper — Calm and composed.

    Let's talk calmly and keep our tempers in check.

Opposite

  • 💥意氣用事yì qì yòng shì

    emotional (player) — Acting on emotion.

    He's an emotional player who makes rash choices when angry.

  • ⚡不假思索bù jiǎ sī suǒ

    on the spur of the moment; on a whim — Without a second thought.

    On the spur of the moment, he jumped into the river to help.
