11 · Friendship & Loyalty

兩肋插刀

liǎng lèi chā dāo

In English

  • go to the wall for a friend

Meaning

Going to the wall for a friend.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • take a bullet for sb
  • go to bat for sb

Example

他願意為朋友兩肋插刀。

He would go to the wall for a friend.

Usage

Colloquial; usually 為朋友兩肋插刀.

Similar

  • 💗肝膽相照gān dǎn xiāng zhào

    utterly loyal friends — Utterly loyal friends.

    The two are utterly loyal friends.

  • ❄️雪中送炭xuě zhōng sòng tàn

    help when it is needed most — Help when it's needed most.

    A true friend helps when it is needed most.

Opposite

  • 🦶落井下石luò jǐng xià shí

    kick sb when he's down; pile on — Kicking someone when they're down.

    Don't kick him when he's down.

  • 🍖酒肉朋友jiǔ ròu péng yǒu

    fair-weather friends — Fair-weather friends.

    When the money ran out, his fair-weather friends disappeared.
