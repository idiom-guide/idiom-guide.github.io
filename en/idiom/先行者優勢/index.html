先行者優勢

xiān xíng zhě yōu shì

In English

  • first-mover advantage

Meaning

First-mover advantage.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • early-bird advantage
  • being first to market

Example

這款應用程式在市場上享有先行者優勢。

The app enjoys a first-mover advantage in this market.

Usage

A business term, common in tech and start-ups.

Similar

  • 🏁先發優勢xiān fā yōu shì

    got a head start over/on sb — A head start.

    The team got a head start on its rivals by training in July.

  • 🌙近水樓臺先得月jìn shuǐ lóu tái xiān dé yuè

    the closer you stay, the more chances you'll get — Proximity breeds opportunity.

    Being close to the boss gives you more chances: the closer you stay, the more chances you get.

Opposite

  • 🏃後來居上hòu lái jū shàng

    surpass — Overtaking from behind.

    The newcomer surpassed the veterans within a year.

  • 💙青出於藍qīng chū yú lán

    the student surpasses the master — The student surpasses the master.

    The student has surpassed the master.
