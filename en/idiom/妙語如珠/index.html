妙語如珠

miào yǔ rú zhū

In English

  • witty
  • scintillating repartee

Meaning

Full of witty remarks, one gem after another.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • be witty
  • sparkle with witty remarks
  • be full of clever lines

Example

講者妙語如珠，整場演講機鋒不斷。

The speaker was witty, and her talk was full of scintillating repartee.

Usage

Praise for speeches, conversations or writing; stresses a string of bright remarks.

Similar

  • 🌸舌燦蓮花shé càn lián huā

    articulate — Silver-tongued; sometimes ironic.

    The salesman was so articulate that he could talk anyone into buying.

  • 🍸談笑風生tán xiào fēng shēng

    chat and laugh with ease — Chatting and laughing in a relaxed atmosphere.

    Everyone chatted and laughed easily around the table.

Opposite

  • 😶沉默寡言chén mò guǎ yán

    has a reticence about — A man of few words.

    My grandfather is a man of few words, with a certain reticence about his past.

  • 🥱平淡無奇píng dàn wú qí

    run-of-the-mill — Plain and unremarkable.

    It was just a run-of-the-mill day at the office.
