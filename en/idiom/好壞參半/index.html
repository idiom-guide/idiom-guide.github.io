好壞參半

hǎo huài cān bàn

In English

  • A chequered past

Meaning

A mix of good and bad.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • be a mixed bag
  • have mixed results
  • a chequered past

Example

這家公司的過去好壞參半，有輝煌的成功，也有代價慘痛的失敗。

這家公司過去好壞參半，既有大成功，也有昂貴的失敗。

The company has a chequered past, with both great successes and costly failures.

Usage

Used of results, experiences and performance.

Similar

  • 🌔毀譽參半huǐ yù cān bàn

    have both admirers and critics; be praised and criticized in equal measure — Praise and blame in equal measure; usually about people.

    The director has both admirers and critics, yet always keeps a loyal following.

  • 🔷瑕瑜互見xiá yú hù jiàn

    have both flaws and merits — Strengths and flaws side by side; formal, often in reviews.

    The novel has both strengths and weaknesses: some brilliant passages and some tedious ones.

Opposite

  • 🌕盡善盡美jìn shàn jìn měi

    be perfect; leave nothing to be desired — Perfect in every way.

    The performance was flawless.

  • 🚮一無是處yī wú shì chù

    be good for nothing; have no redeeming quality — Good for nothing.

    He talks as if he has not one redeeming quality.
