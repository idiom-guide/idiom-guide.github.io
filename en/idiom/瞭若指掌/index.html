瞭若指掌

liǎo ruò zhǐ zhǎng

In English

  • know sth. inside out
  • you know that

Meaning

Knowing something like the back of one's hand.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • know sth like the back of one's hand
  • know sth inside out
  • have sth at one's fingertips
  • be thoroughly familiar with

Example

她對這座城市的公車路線瞭若指掌。

She knows the city's bus routes inside out.

Usage

Also 了如指掌; for places, people or matters one knows well.

Similar

  • 🚗駕輕就熟jià qīng jiù shú

    mastering; getting into one's stride — Skilled and practiced at something.

    After a month, she was getting into her stride at the new job.

  • 🌐融會貫通róng huì guàn tōng

    consilient; fully conversant with — Having a thorough, integrated understanding.

    After years of study, she is fully conversant with the whole field.

Opposite

  • 🌓一知半解yī zhī bàn jiě

    have only a smattering of knowledge; know just a little — Knowing only a little.

    He has only a smattering of knowledge about the issue, yet loves to give his opinion.

  • 🈳一竅不通yī qiào bù tōng

    all Greek to me — Knowing nothing at all.

    Quantum physics is all Greek to me.
