2 · Competition & Power

兩敗俱傷

liǎng bài jù shāng

In English

  • lose-lose situation

Meaning

A lose-lose situation.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • a lose-lose situation
  • no winners
  • mutually destructive

Example

價格戰對兩家店來說都是兩敗俱傷。

A price war would be a lose-lose situation for both stores.

Usage

A warning about conflicts, price wars or lawsuits.

Similar

  • 🦈同類相噬tóng lèi xiāng shì

    cannibalization — Cannibalizing each other.

    The new phone is cannibalizing sales of the older model.

  • 🔥焦土策略jiāo tǔ cè lüè

    scorched earth policy; obliterated — A scorched-earth policy.

    The retreating army used a scorched-earth policy.

Opposite

  • ✌️兩全其美liǎng quán qí měi

    get the best of both — The best of both worlds.

    The new plan lets us get the best of both.

  • 🔀互惠互利hù huì hù lì

    mutually beneficial; win-win — Mutually beneficial.

    We are looking for a mutually beneficial arrangement.
