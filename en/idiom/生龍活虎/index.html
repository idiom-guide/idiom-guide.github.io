生龍活虎

shēng lóng huó hǔ

In English

  • be alive and well

Meaning

Full of life; alive and kicking.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • alive and kicking
  • full of beans

Example

爺爺雖然年紀大了，仍生龍活虎，每天打網球。

Despite his age, grandpa is alive and well and plays tennis every day.

Usage

For energetic children, athletes and active older people.

Similar

  • ⚡精力充沛jīng lì chōng pèi

    ebullient — Full of energy.

    The ebullient host kept the crowd cheering all night.

  • 🔔精神抖擻jīng shén dǒu sǒu

    full of energy; bright-eyed and bushy-tailed — Raring to go.

    He turned up to work with bells on.

Opposite

  • 🍃弱不禁風ruò bù jīn fēng

    frail; delicate — Frail.

    She looked frail in the cold.

  • 🥱無精打采wú jīng dǎ cǎi

    listless; lethargic — Listless.

    He looked listless after the long trip.
