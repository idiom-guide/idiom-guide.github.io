心直口快

xīn zhí kǒu kuài

In English

  • straight shooter

Meaning

Outspoken; says whatever comes to mind.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • be a straight shooter
  • speak one's mind
  • have a quick tongue

Example

她心直口快，常因此惹上麻煩，但至少為人坦率。

Her quick tongue sometimes gets her into trouble, but at least she is a straight shooter.

Usage

Describes personality; frank without malice, but not very diplomatic.

Similar

  • 📢直言不諱zhí yán bù huì

    speak frankly; not mince words — Speaking bluntly; about attitude.

    She spoke frankly and pointed out the flaws in the plan.

  • 🛵快人快語kuài rén kuài yǔ

    be outspoken and quick; speak frankly and promptly — Frank and quick to speak.

    She is outspoken and says what she thinks, without delay.

Opposite

  • 🐴深藏不露shēn cáng bù lù

    keep one's talents hidden; be a dark horse — Keeping one's abilities or thoughts hidden.

    He kept his talents hidden, and nobody knew he was the champion.

  • 🐙城府極深chéng fǔ jí shēn

    be deep and scheming; be a calculating person — Deep and calculating.

    He is deeply calculating, and nobody can read him.
