天生麗質

tiān shēng lì zhì

In English

  • a natural beauty
  • naturally beautiful

Meaning

Born with a lovely appearance: beauty that needs no help.Positive · General

Example

她天生麗質，不太需要化妝。

She is a natural beauty and hardly needs makeup.

Usage

Mostly praise for natural beauty without makeup. Also common in advertising copy.

Similar

  • 🐟沉魚落雁chén yú luò yàn

    drop-dead gorgeous; stunningly beautiful — Beauty that dazzles; about wonder.

    She was so stunningly beautiful that heads turned wherever she went.

  • 🥺楚楚動人chǔ chǔ dòng rén

    winsome; endearingly delicate — Delicate and endearing; about presence.

    In her pale blue dress, she looked delicate and winsome.

Opposite

  • 💄濃妝豔抹nóng zhuāng yàn mǒ

    heavily made-up; overdone — Heavy makeup; about deliberate adornment.

    She showed up heavily made-up, as if for a stage show.

  • 🤷其貌不揚qí mào bù yáng

    unprepossessing; plain-looking — Plain-looking; a gentler way of putting it.

    He was plain-looking, but everyone admired his talent.
