遭遇失敗

zāo yù shī bài

In English

  • bite the dust

Meaning

Biting the dust.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • suffer a defeat
  • crash out

Example

衛冕冠軍在第一輪就遭遇失敗。

The champion bit the dust in the first round.

Usage

For sport, elections and business.

Similar

  • 🤕一敗塗地yī bài tú dì

    fall flat on your face — Falling flat on one's face.

    The campaign fell flat on its face.

  • 🏳️鎩羽而歸shā yǔ ér guī

    return in defeat; come back empty-handed — Returning in defeat.

    The explorers returned in defeat.

Opposite

  • 🎉凱旋歸來kǎi xuán guī lái

    return victorious — Returning victorious.

    The team returned victorious from Paris.

  • 🎋勢如破竹shì rú pò zhú

    sweep all before one; unstoppable — Sweeping all before it.

    The army swept all before it.
