危言聳聽

wēi yán sǒng tīng

In English

  • alarmist, fearmongers, spread fears
  • scaremonger

Meaning

Alarmist.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • alarmist
  • fearmonger

Example

那位專欄作家危言聳聽，每篇標題都在嚇讀者。

The columnist is an alarmist who scares readers with every headline.

Usage

For media, commentary and predictions.

Similar

  • 🧨煽動誇大shān dòng kuā dà

    inflammatory — Inflammatory.

    The headline was inflammatory and misleading.

  • 😦杞人憂天qǐ rén yōu tiān

    worry about nothing; fear that the sky will fall — Worrying needlessly.

    Don't worry about nothing; it isn't that bad.

Opposite

  • 🤗令人安心lìng rén ān xīn

    reassuring — Reassuring.

    The doctor's calm voice was reassuring.

  • ⚛實事求是shí shì qiú shì

    seek truth from facts; be down-to-earth — Seeking truth from facts.

    Research should seek truth from facts, not start from a conclusion and look for evidence.
