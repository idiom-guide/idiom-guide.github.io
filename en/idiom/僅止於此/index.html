僅止於此

jǐn zhǐ yú cǐ

In English

  • end there

Meaning

That's as far as it goes; nothing more.Neutral · Formal

Example

討論僅止於此，我們進行下一項。

Let's let the discussion end there and move on to the next item.

Usage

Used to close a topic or limit the scope.

Similar

  • ⏏到此為止dào cǐ wéi zhǐ

    stop here; that's all — That's the end of it; colloquial, can be a warning.

    That's all for today's meeting.

  • 🔕點到為止diǎn dào wéi zhǐ

    stop at a hint; say just enough — Stopping at the right point.

    The teacher only hinted at the point and left the rest for the students to work out.

Opposite

  • ➕不只如此bù zhǐ rú cǐ

    that's not all; there is more to it — There's more; it doesn't stop there.

    That's not all; the price also includes free delivery.

  • 📈變本加厲biàn běn jiā lì

    go from bad to worse; escalate — Getting worse than before.

    Instead of stopping, the bullies went from bad to worse.
