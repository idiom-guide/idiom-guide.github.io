4 · Pain & Unease

心懷不滿

xīn huái bù mǎn

In English

  • be disgruntled with

Meaning

Disgruntled.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • resentful
  • have an axe to grind

Example

許多員工對新班表心懷不滿。

Many employees are disgruntled with the new schedule.

Usage

For employees, the public and supporters.

Similar

  • 😠怨聲載道yuàn shēng zài dào

    widespread discontent — Widespread discontent.

    The fare hike caused widespread discontent.

  • 😠忿忿不平fèn fèn bù píng

    indignant; aggrieved — Indignant.

    The fans were indignant about the referee's decision.

Opposite

  • 😊知足常樂zhī zú cháng lè

    be happy with enough — Contentment is happiness.

    Be happy with enough: contentment is a treasure.
