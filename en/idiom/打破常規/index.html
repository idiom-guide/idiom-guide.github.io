打破常規

dǎ pò cháng guī

In English

  • think outside the box

Meaning

Thinking outside the box.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • break the mold
  • go against convention

Example

要解決這個問題，我們得打破常規。

To solve this problem, we need to think outside the box.

Usage

Usually positive, about innovation; can also mean breaking the rules.

Similar

  • 🛤️另闢蹊徑lìng pì xī jìng

    alternative methods — Finding another path.

    When the usual methods failed, she tried an alternative approach.

  • ♻️推陳出新tuī chén chū xīn

    breathe new life into tradition — Breathing new life into tradition.

    Young designers are breathing new life into tradition.

Opposite

  • 🚋墨守成規mò shǒu chéng guī

    stick to old rules; stay in a rut — Sticking rigidly to old ways.

    The company sticks to old rules and finds it hard to innovate.

  • 📏循規蹈矩xún guī dǎo jǔ

    by the book; play by the rules — Playing by the rules.

    He plays by the rules and never takes risks.
