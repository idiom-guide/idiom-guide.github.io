正在勢頭

zhèng zài shì tou

In English

  • in one's prime

Meaning

On a roll; in one's prime.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • on a roll
  • riding high

Example

這位歌手正在勢頭上，每季都有新歌爆紅。

The singer is in her prime, with a new hit every season.

Usage

Colloquial; often as 正在勢頭上.

Similar

  • 🏔️巔峰狀態diān fēng zhuàng tài

    at peak level — At one's peak.

    The athlete is at peak level just before the Olympics.

  • 🌬️意氣風發yì qì fēng fā

    be in high spirits; full of vigor — In high spirits.

    The graduates were in high spirits.

Opposite

  • 🏞️江河日下jiāng hé rì xià

    in steady decline — In steady decline.

    The industry has been in steady decline.

  • 📉一落千丈yī luò qiān zhàng

    plummet; nosedive — Plummeting.

    Sales plummeted after the recall.
