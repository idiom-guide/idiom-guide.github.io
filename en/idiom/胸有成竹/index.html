胸有成竹

xiōng yǒu chéng zhú

In English

  • quietly confident

Meaning

Quietly confident, with a plan already in mind.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • have it all figured out
  • have a plan in mind

Example

她胸有成竹地走進面試會場。

She walked into the interview quietly confident.

Usage

From the painter Wen Yuke, who had the bamboo in mind before painting it; also 成竹在胸.

Similar

  • 🏀十拿九穩shí ná jiǔ wěn

    be all but certain; be a safe bet — A sure thing.

    With this contract in hand, winning the bid is all but certain.

  • ✅萬事俱備wàn shì jù bèi

    good to go — Good to go.

    The tents are up and the food is ready. We're good to go.

Opposite

  • 🌑毫無頭緒háo wú tóu xù

    grope in the dark; have no clue — Having no clue.

    I have no clue where to begin.

  • 🤹手忙腳亂shǒu máng jiǎo luàn

    get flustered — Flustered.

    I get flustered when the phone rings during a meeting.
