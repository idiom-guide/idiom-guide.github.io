食古不化

shí gǔ bù huà

In English

  • dinosaur

Meaning

A dinosaur.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • stuck in the past
  • behind the times

Example

經理食古不化，拒絕使用電子郵件。

The manager is a dinosaur who refuses to use email.

Usage

For criticizing old-fashioned people or ideas.

Similar

  • 🏺貴古賤今guì gǔ jiàn jīn

    golden age thinking — Golden-age thinking.

    Golden age thinking makes him believe nothing today is as good as the past.

  • 🚋墨守成規mò shǒu chéng guī

    stick to old rules; stay in a rut — Sticking to the old rules.

    The company sticks to old rules and finds it hard to innovate.

Opposite

  • ⏩與時俱進yǔ shí jù jìn

    keep evolving — Keeping up with the times.

    A good company keeps evolving with the times.

  • 🏺古為今用gǔ wéi jīn yòng

    adapt the past for the present — Adapting the past for the present.

    The architect adapts the past for the present.
