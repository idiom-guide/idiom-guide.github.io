傾國傾城

qīng guó qīng chéng

In English

  • ravishing
  • a legendary beauty

Meaning

A beauty fit to topple a city and a nation: an utterly stunning woman.Positive · Formal

Example

傳說中那位公主傾國傾城。

The princess was said to be a legendary beauty.

Usage

A great exaggeration, found in legends, novels and praise of beauties. It carries the old idea of beauty bringing ruin, so it can sound over the top in daily talk.

Similar

  • 👸風華絕代fēng huá jué dài

    timeless beauty; unparalleled/unsurpassable beauty — Peerless in charm and talent; broader in range.

    The actress was a woman of unparalleled beauty.

  • 🐟沉魚落雁chén yú luò yàn

    drop-dead gorgeous; stunningly beautiful — So beautiful that fish and geese are dazzled; about wonder.

    She was so stunningly beautiful that heads turned wherever she went.

Opposite

  • 🤷其貌不揚qí mào bù yáng

    unprepossessing; plain-looking — Plain-looking; a gentler way of putting it.

    He was plain-looking, but everyone admired his talent.

  • 🥱平淡無奇píng dàn wú qí

    run-of-the-mill — Ordinary, with nothing special about it.

    It was just a run-of-the-mill day at the office.
