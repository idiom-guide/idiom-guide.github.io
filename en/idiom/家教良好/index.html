家教良好

jiā jiào liáng hǎo

In English

  • be raised well

Meaning

Raised well.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • well-bred
  • well brought up

Example

看得出來她家教良好。

You can tell she was raised well.

Usage

For praising someone's manners and upbringing.

Similar

  • 🎩彬彬有禮bīn bīn yǒu lǐ

    courteous; well-mannered — Well-mannered.

    The waiter was courteous throughout.

Opposite

  • 😛沒大沒小méi dà méi xiǎo

    being rude, impolite — Rude to one's elders.

    Don't talk to your grandmother like that; it's rude.

  • 😤成何體統chéng hé tǐ tǒng

    absurd; improper — Having no manners.

    Shouting in a library? How absurd and improper!
