令人稱羨

lìng rén chēng xiàn

In English

  • has an enviable record of

Meaning

Enviable.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • enviable
  • the envy of

Example

這支球隊連勝二十場，成績令人稱羨。

The team has an enviable record of twenty straight wins.

Usage

For records, careers, marriages and lifestyles.

Similar

  • 👍為人稱道wéi rén chēng dào

    widely praised/complimented; commendable — Widely praised.

    Her kindness to strangers was widely praised.

  • 🏆功成名就gōng chéng míng jiù

    achieve fame and success; have it made — Fame and success.

    After years of work, she finally achieved fame and success.

Opposite

  • 🤮令人反感lìng rén fǎn gǎn

    distasteful; off-putting — Distasteful.

    His constant bragging was distasteful.

  • 😞大失所望dà shī suǒ wàng

    sorely disappointed; let down — Sorely disappointed.

    We were sorely disappointed by the ending.
