歷久彌新

lì jiǔ mí xīn

In English

  • timeless

Meaning

Timeless.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • stand the test of time
  • evergreen

Example

她的設計歷久彌新。

Her designs are timeless.

Usage

Formal; for works, ideas and friendships.

Similar

  • 🔬經得起檢驗jīng de qǐ jiǎn yàn

    (not) hold up to scrutiny — Standing up to scrutiny.

    A good theory has to hold up to scrutiny.

  • 🎶膾炙人口kuài zhì rén kǒu

    widely loved and often quoted — Widely loved and often quoted.

    The poem is widely loved and often quoted.

Opposite

  • 🌸曇花一現tán huā yī xiàn

    temporary; short-lived — A flash in the pan.

    Their hit song was a flash in the pan.

  • 📼陳舊過時chén jiù guò shí

    obsolescence — Obsolete.

    The software suffers from obsolescence.
