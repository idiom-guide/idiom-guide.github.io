11 · Love & Marriage

有情人終成眷屬

yǒu qíng rén zhōng chéng juàn shǔ

In English

  • love finds a way

Meaning

Love finds a way.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • true love wins
  • all's well that ends well

Example

分隔多年後，有情人終成眷屬。

After years apart, love found a way.

Usage

For blessings or happy endings; from The Romance of the Western Chamber.

Similar

  • 🔗破鏡重圓pò jìng chóng yuán

    get back together — Getting back together.

    After two years apart, the couple got back together.

  • 💒天作之合tiān zuò zhī hé

    a match made in heaven — A match made in heaven.

    Everyone says they are a match made in heaven.

Opposite

  • 🐦勞燕分飛láo yàn fēn fēi

    go their separate ways — Going their separate ways.

    After graduation the couple went their separate ways.
