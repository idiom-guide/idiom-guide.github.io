同舟共濟

tóng zhōu gòng jì

In English

  • solidarity
  • help each other

Meaning

In the same boat, helping each other through.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • in the same boat
  • pull together

Example

危機時期，鄰居們同舟共濟、互相幫忙。

In times of crisis, neighbors help each other.

Usage

From Sunzi; for crises and teamwork.

Similar

  • 🤝共體時艱gòng tǐ shí jiān

    share the burden together — Sharing the burden together.

    In this crisis, let's share the burden together.

  • 🤜並肩作戰bìng jiān zuò zhàn

    fight side by side — Fighting side by side.

    The two nations fought side by side.

Opposite

  • 🦈同類相噬tóng lèi xiāng shì

    cannibalization — Cannibalizing each other.

    The new phone is cannibalizing sales of the older model.

  • 🏴派系衝突pài xì chōng tū

    factionalism — Factional infighting.

    Factionalism has paralyzed the party.
