1 · Knowing & Thinking

不自覺地

bù zì jué de

In English

  • inadvertently

Meaning

Without realizing it; unconsciously.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • without realizing it
  • unconsciously
  • involuntarily

Example

他工作時不自覺地哼起了那首歌。

He inadvertently hummed the tune while he worked.

Usage

An adverb before the verb; often for habits and emotional reactions. The listed inadvertently suggests carelessness, which the Chinese doesn't necessarily imply.

Similar

  • 🤖不知不覺bù zhī bù jué

    unwittingly — Without noticing; about time or change slipping by.

    Unwittingly, he had walked into the wrong room.

  • 🪀身不由己shēn bù yóu jǐ

    have no say; not be one's own master — Not being one's own master; pushed by circumstances.

    He has no say at the company and does whatever he is told.

Opposite

  • 🥌刻意為之kè yì wèi zhī

    do sth deliberately; do sth on purpose — Doing something deliberately.

    He made the typo on purpose to test whether people were reading carefully.

  • 🦂處心積慮chǔ xīn jī lǜ

    scheme meticulously; go to great lengths — Scheming meticulously; usually negative.

    He schemed meticulously to get close to the chairman.
