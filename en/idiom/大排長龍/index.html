大排長龍

dà pái cháng lóng

In English

  • queue around the block
  • line out the door

Meaning

People lined up like a long dragon: a very long queue.Neutral · Colloquial

Example

這家麵包店的鳳梨酥總是大排長龍。

There is always a queue around the block for the bakery's pineapple cakes.

Usage

For queues at famous shops, limited-edition goods and popular sights. The English idiom is line out the door.

Similar

  • 👥人山人海rén shān rén hǎi

    huge crowds of faces; sea of people — Crowds like mountains and seas; about numbers, not necessarily a queue.

    A vast crowd filled the square for the fireworks.

  • 🎟️座無虛席zuò wú xū xí

    a packed house — Every seat taken; about indoor places.

    It is wonderful to see a packed house this morning.

Opposite

  • 🕸️門可羅雀mén kě luó què

    deserted; hardly a customer in sight — Deserted.

    Once packed, the mall is now deserted.
