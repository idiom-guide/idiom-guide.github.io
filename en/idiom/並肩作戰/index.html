並肩作戰

bìng jiān zuò zhàn

In English

  • fight side by side

Meaning

Fighting side by side.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • stand shoulder to shoulder
  • join forces

Example

兩國並肩作戰。

The two nations fought side by side.

Usage

Literal combat, or figuratively colleagues and teammates working together.

Similar

  • ⛵同舟共濟tóng zhōu gòng jì

    solidarity; help each other — In the same boat, helping each other through.

    In times of crisis, neighbors help each other.

  • ⚔️生死與共shēng sǐ yǔ gòng

    live with it, die with it — Living or dying together.

    The two crews stood by each other, ready to live or die together.

Opposite

  • 🦸‍♀️孤軍奮戰gū jūn fèn zhàn

    fight a lone battle; go it alone — Fighting a lone battle.

    She fought a lone battle on the board with no one behind her.

  • ☝️一己之力yī jǐ zhī lì

    single-handedly — Single-handedly.

    She built the whole website single-handedly.
