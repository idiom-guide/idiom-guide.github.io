守望相助

shǒu wàng xiāng zhù

In English

  • look out for one another

Meaning

Looking out for one another.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • neighborhood watch
  • mutual aid

Example

這個社區的居民守望相助。

In this neighborhood, residents look out for one another.

Usage

For neighborhoods and communities; found in Mencius.

Similar

  • 🏘️敦親睦鄰dūn qīn mù lín

    good-neighborliness — Good-neighborliness.

    The foreign minister stressed good-neighborliness in his first speech.

  • ⛵同舟共濟tóng zhōu gòng jì

    solidarity; help each other — In the same boat.

    In times of crisis, neighbors help each other.

Opposite

  • 🚧以鄰為壑yǐ lín wéi hè

    beggar-thy-neighbor — Beggar-thy-neighbor.

    Competitive devaluation is a beggar-thy-neighbor policy.

  • 📴事不關己shì bù guān jǐ

    none of one's business; not one's concern — None of one's business.

    It was none of his business, so he stood by and did nothing.
