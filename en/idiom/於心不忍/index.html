4 · Pain & Unease

於心不忍

yú xīn bù rěn

In English

  • can't bear to
  • have not the heart to

Meaning

Not having the heart.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • don't have the heart

Example

我於心不忍，不敢告訴她真相。

I can't bear to tell her the truth.

Usage

For not wanting to hurt or refuse someone.

Similar

  • 🤗感同身受gǎn tóng shēn shòu

    relatable — Feeling it oneself.

    Her story was so relatable that I felt every word as if it were my own.

Opposite

  • 🔪心狠手辣xīn hěn shǒu là

    ruthless; cold-blooded — Ruthless.

    The villain is ruthless and cold-blooded.

  • 😈幸災樂禍xìng zāi lè huò

    schadenfreude; take delight in sb's misfortunes — Schadenfreude.

    Taking delight in a rival's misfortunes is schadenfreude.
