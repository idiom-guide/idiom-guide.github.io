旁敲側擊

páng qiāo cè jī

In English

  • probe

Meaning

Dropping hints; probing indirectly.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • hint indirectly
  • beat around the bush

Example

他旁敲側擊，想知道她是不是打算離職。

He probed gently to find out whether she planned to quit.

Usage

Indirect probing; unlike 拐彎抹角 (talking in circles), it usually aims to find something out or hint at something.

Similar

  • ↪️拐彎抹角guǎi wān mò jiǎo

    beat around the bush; obliquely — Talking in circles.

    Stop beating around the bush and tell me what happened.

  • 🎸弦外之音xián wài zhī yīn

    an undertone; a hidden meaning — The unspoken message; the content of the hint.

    There is an undertone in his words; did you catch it?

Opposite

  • 🚪開門見山kāi mén jiàn shān

    get straight to the point — Getting straight to the point.

    He got straight to the point and asked about the price.

  • 📢直言不諱zhí yán bù huì

    speak frankly; not mince words — Speaking bluntly.

    She spoke frankly and pointed out the flaws in the plan.
