開始運作

kāi shǐ yùn zuò

In English

  • once the wheels are in motion, there is no going back

Meaning

Up and running; the wheels are in motion.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • the wheels are in motion
  • up and running
  • get going

Example

一旦開始運作，就沒有回頭路了。

Once the wheels are in motion, there is no going back.

Usage

For new systems, agencies or equipment.

Similar

  • ⛵木已成舟mù yǐ chéng zhōu

    ship has sailed — The ship has sailed.

    It's too late to complain; the ship has sailed.

  • 🚄勢在必行shì zài bì xíng

    it's a necessity; we have to do this — Something that has to be done.

    The reform is a necessity; we have to do this.

Opposite

  • 🎈懸而未決xuán ér wèi jué

    sth be still up in the air; sth be up in the air — Still undecided.

    The decision is still up in the air.

  • ✏️尚未定案shàng wèi dìng àn

    be yet to be finalized; be still under discussion — Not yet final.

    The itinerary has yet to be finalized, so please wait a moment.
