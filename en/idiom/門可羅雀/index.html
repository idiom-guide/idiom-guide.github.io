門可羅雀

mén kě luó què

In English

  • deserted
  • hardly a customer in sight

Meaning

Deserted.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • a ghost town

Example

曾經擠滿人的購物中心，如今門可羅雀。

Once packed, the mall is now deserted.

Usage

For shops and attractions; from the Records of the Grand Historian.

Similar

  • 🕸️乏人問津fá rén wèn jīn

    attract no buyers; go unnoticed — Going unnoticed.

    The old house has attracted no buyers.

  • 🏚️百業蕭條bǎi yè xiāo tiáo

    an across-the-board slump — An across-the-board slump.

    The lockdown caused an across-the-board slump.

Opposite

  • 🏪門庭若市mén tíng ruò shì

    bustling with customers — Bustling with customers.

    The new bakery is bustling with customers.

  • 🏮生意興隆shēng yì xīng lóng

    business is booming — Business is booming.

    Business is booming at the night market.
