沈痾自滿

chén kē zì mǎn

In English

  • stagnant and complacent
  • be carried away

Meaning

Stagnant and complacent.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • resting on one's laurels

Example

這家沈痾自滿的公司被對手超越。

The stagnant, complacent firm was overtaken by its rivals.

Usage

For organizations, companies and institutions.

Similar

  • 🎈自我膨脹zì wǒ péng zhàng

    have an inflated ego — An inflated ego.

    Success gave him an inflated ego.

  • 🦚驕兵必敗jiāo bīng bì bài

    pride comes before a fall — Pride comes before a fall.

    Pride comes before a fall, and the champions lost to a weaker team.

Opposite

  • ⏩與時俱進yǔ shí jù jìn

    keep evolving — Keeping up with the times.

    A good company keeps evolving with the times.

  • 🚵力爭上游lì zhēng shàng yóu

    strive to move upward; aim high — Striving to get ahead.

    He strove upward and finally got into the school he wanted.
