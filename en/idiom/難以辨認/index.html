難以辨認

nán yǐ biàn rèn

In English

  • indecipherable

Meaning

Hard to make out.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • hard to make out
  • illegible
  • unrecognizable

Example

他的字跡太潦草，這張便條難以辨認。

His handwriting was so messy that the note was indecipherable.

Usage

For handwriting, voices, faces and so on; broader than 潦草難懂.

Similar

  • ✍️潦草難懂liáo cǎo nán dǒng

    hieroglyphic — Illegible handwriting.

    The doctor's notes looked like hieroglyphics to me.

  • 👻難以察覺nán yǐ chá jué

    imperceptible — Barely noticeable.

    The change in pitch was almost imperceptible.

Opposite

  • 🚥一目了然yī mù liǎo rán

    be clear at a glance — Clear at a glance.

    The chart is clear at a glance.

  • 👀顯而易見xiǎn ér yì jiàn

    beyond obvious — Obvious.

    It was beyond obvious that he wasn't listening.
