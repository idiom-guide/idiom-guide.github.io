尚未定案

shàng wèi dìng àn

In English

  • under development
  • preliminary findings
  • drafts

Meaning

Not yet final.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • not yet final
  • still up in the air

Example

計畫還在研擬，尚未定案。

The plan is still under development, so nothing is final.

Usage

For policies, plans or appointments; common in official statements.

Similar

  • 🎈懸而未決xuán ér wèi jué

    sth be still up in the air; sth be up in the air — Still undecided.

    The decision is still up in the air.

  • ↔️轉圜空間zhuǎn huán kōng jiān

    room to maneuver; wiggle room — Room to maneuver.

    There is little room to maneuver in the negotiations.

Opposite

  • 🔨板上釘釘bǎn shàng dìng dīng

    a done deal; locked in — Settled once and for all.

    The contract has been signed, so the deal is locked in.

  • 🌫️塵埃落定chén āi luò dìng

    waiting for the dust to settle — The dust has settled.

    Let's wait for the dust to settle before we make any decision.
