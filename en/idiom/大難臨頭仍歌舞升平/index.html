大難臨頭仍歌舞升平

dà nàn lín tóu réng gē wǔ shēng píng

In English

  • Fiddle while Rome burns

Meaning

Fiddling while Rome burns.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • party on the Titanic
  • bury one's head in the sand

Example

公司快倒了，董事會還在辦派對，真是大難臨頭仍歌舞升平。

The board held a party while the firm collapsed. That's fiddling while Rome burns.

Usage

For criticizing those who carry on as normal in a crisis.

Similar

  • 🍬粉飾太平fěn shì tài píng

    sugarcoating — Sugarcoating.

    The report was sugarcoating the real situation.

  • 🍾縱情享樂zòng qíng xiǎng lè

    indulge oneself; live it up — Living it up.

    They indulged themselves at the festival.

Opposite

  • ☂️未雨綢繆wèi yǔ chóu móu

    be fully prepared; provident — Preparing in advance.

    Keep an emergency kit at home; it pays to be fully prepared.

  • 🛡️嚴陣以待yán zhèn yǐ dài

    stand ready; be braced for — Standing ready.

    The police stood ready for the protest.
