豁然開朗

huò rán kāi lǎng

In English

  • eureka
  • refreshing moment

Meaning

Suddenly everything falls into place.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • everything falls into place
  • have a sudden clear understanding
  • the fog lifts
  • eureka moment

Example

一切突然豁然開朗，真是靈光乍現的一刻。

Everything suddenly became clear. It was a real eureka moment.

Usage

From Tao Yuanming's Peach Blossom Spring, where a narrow cave opens onto a bright landscape; now for sudden understanding or relief.

Similar

  • 💥醍醐灌頂tí hú guàn dǐng

    have a sudden enlightenment; be struck by sudden insight — A sudden, complete understanding.

    His advice struck me like a sudden enlightenment.

  • 🧘大徹大悟dà chè dà wù

    wide awake — Complete enlightenment.

    After his near-death experience, he was wide awake to what truly mattered in life.

Opposite

  • 🤷百思不解bǎi sī bù jiě

    flummoxed — Unable to figure it out.

    I'm flummoxed by why the system keeps crashing.

  • 🌫️一頭霧水yī tóu wù shuǐ

    baffled; at a loss — Completely confused.

    I'm baffled by this form.
