事不宜遲

shì bù yí chí

In English

  • there's no time to lose

Meaning

There's no time to lose.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • the sooner the better
  • act now

Example

事不宜遲，快打電話給醫生。

There's no time to lose, so call the doctor.

Usage

Often at the start of a sentence, followed by what to do.

Similar

  • 🚨刻不容緩kè bù róng huǎn

    urgent; brook no delay — Allowing no delay.

    Climate action is urgent.

  • ⏳分秒必爭fēn miǎo bì zhēng

    not a moment to be lost; time-sensitive — Every second counts.

    In the emergency room, there is not a moment to be lost.

Opposite

  • 🌅來日方長lái rì fāng cháng

    there will be many more opportunities ahead — There's plenty of time ahead.

    There will be many more opportunities ahead to work together.

  • 🦢從容不迫cóng róng bù pò

    unruffled — Calm and unhurried.

    The host remained unruffled when the lights went out.
