投機取巧

tóu jī qǔ qiǎo

In English

  • opportunistic
  • find loopholes in

Meaning

Opportunistic.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • cut corners

Example

投機取巧的商人鑽規則的漏洞。

Opportunistic traders find loopholes in the rules.

Usage

For business, exams and work.

Similar

  • 🦈投機主義tóu jī zhǔ yì

    opportunism — Opportunism.

    The party was accused of pure opportunism.

  • 📈趨炎附勢qū yán fù shì

    fawn on the powerful; curry favor with those in power — A social climber.

    He always fawns on the powerful and follows whoever holds authority.

Opposite

  • 👣腳踏實地jiǎo tà shí dì

    staying grounded and pushing ahead — Down-to-earth.

    He is a down-to-earth man who stays grounded and keeps pushing ahead.

  • 📋按部就班àn bù jiù bān

    step by step; by the book — Step by step.

    New hires are trained step by step.
