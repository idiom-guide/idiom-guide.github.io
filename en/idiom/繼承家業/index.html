繼承家業

jì chéng jiā yè

In English

  • honor family legacy

Meaning

Taking over the family business.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • take over the family business
  • carry on the family trade

Example

他回到家鄉繼承家業，經營麵包店。

He returned home to honor the family legacy by running the bakery.

Usage

For family firms, old shops and traditional crafts.

Similar

  • 🔥薪火相傳xīn huǒ xiāng chuán

    pass the torch — Passing the torch; not limited to family.

    The old master passed the torch to his apprentice.

  • 💼成家立業chéng jiā lì yè

    settle down and build a career — Settling down and building a career, not necessarily the family's.

    By thirty he had settled down and built a career.

Opposite

  • 🏗️白手起家bái shǒu qǐ jiā

    a self-made man/woman; start from scratch — Self-made; starting from scratch.

    She is a self-made woman who started from scratch.

  • 🌏闖蕩世界chuǎng dàng shì jiè

    navigate this world — Going out into the world.

    At eighteen, he left home to navigate this world on his own.
