11 · Family & Kinship

含辛茹苦

hán xīn rú kǔ

In English

  • endure hardship to raise a family

Meaning

Enduring hardship to raise a family.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • toil and sacrifice
  • scrimp and save

Example

她含辛茹苦，獨自撫養三個孩子。

She endured hardship to raise three children alone.

Usage

For the sacrifices parents make for their children.

Similar

  • 🤲相依為命xiāng yī wéi mìng

    have only each other to rely on — Having only each other.

    The mother and daughter had only each other to rely on.

  • 🐄舐犢情深shì dú qíng shēn

    deep parental love — Deep parental love.

    The letter shows a father's deep parental love.

Opposite

  • 🥪不勞而獲bù láo ér huò

    get something for nothing; get a free lunch — Something for nothing.

    There is no such thing as a free lunch.
