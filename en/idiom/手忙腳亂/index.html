手忙腳亂

shǒu máng jiǎo luàn

In English

  • get flustered

Meaning

Getting flustered.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • all fingers and thumbs
  • scramble

Example

開會時電話一響，我就手忙腳亂。

I get flustered when the phone rings during a meeting.

Usage

For surprises or juggling too much at once.

Similar

  • 🤷不知所措bù zhī suǒ cuò

    overwhelmed; catch sb. off base — At a loss.

    Overwhelmed by the crowd, the child didn't know what to do.

  • 🦶亂了陣腳luàn le zhèn jiǎo

    be caught wrong-footed — Caught wrong-footed.

    The sudden question left him caught wrong-footed.

Opposite

  • 🦢從容不迫cóng róng bù pò

    unruffled — Unruffled.

    The host remained unruffled when the lights went out.

  • 🗃️有條有理yǒu tiáo yǒu lǐ

    orderly; methodical — Orderly and methodical.

    Her desk is always orderly.
