膽戰心驚

dǎn zhàn xīn jīng

In English

  • tremble with fear

Meaning

Trembling with fear.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • scared stiff
  • quake in one's boots

Example

他在黑暗中膽戰心驚。

He trembled with fear in the dark.

Usage

For frightening or dangerous situations.

Similar

  • 😱非常驚駭fēi cháng jīng hài

    shit a brick — Jumping out of one's skin.

    He nearly jumped out of his skin when the alarm went off.

  • 🐍杯弓蛇影bēi gōng shé yǐng

    Afraid of one's own shadow; extremely suspicious — Afraid of one's own shadow.

    After the burglary, she was afraid of her own shadow.

Opposite

  • 🦁天不怕地不怕tiān bù pà dì bù pà

    I eat fear for breakfast — Afraid of nothing.

    The toddler is fearless. She eats fear for breakfast.

  • 🐅勇猛無畏yǒng měng wú wèi

    intrepid — Intrepid.

    The intrepid explorers crossed the frozen lake.
