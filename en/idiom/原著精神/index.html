原著精神

yuán zhù jīng shén

In English

  • true to the spirit of

Meaning

The spirit of the original work.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • true to the spirit of the original
  • the spirit of the original work
  • faithful to the original

Example

這部電影忠於小說的原著精神。

The film stays true to the spirit of the original novel.

Usage

Used in reviews of adaptations: 忠於原著精神 or 違背原著精神.

Similar

  • 🖊忠於原著zhōng yú yuán zhù

    be faithful to the original — Faithful to the original; about content rather than spirit.

    The play is faithful to the original, and the plot is unchanged.

  • 🥘原汁原味yuán zhī yuán wèi

    authentic; true to the original flavor — Authentic and unaltered; also for food and culture.

    The restaurant's dishes keep their original flavor.

Opposite

  • 🧟面目全非miàn mù quán fēi

    be unrecognizable — Changed beyond recognition.

    After the adaptation, the plot is unrecognizable.

  • ✂️斷章取義duàn zhāng qǔ yì

    take sb's comments "out of context."; take sth. out of context — Quoting out of context.

    The newspaper took her comments out of context.
