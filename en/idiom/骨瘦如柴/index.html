骨瘦如柴

gǔ shòu rú chái

In English

  • skin and bones
  • rake-thin

Meaning

Thin as firewood, nothing but bones: extremely emaciated.Negative · General

Example

大病一場之後，他變得骨瘦如柴。

After weeks of illness, he was skin and bones.

Usage

For extreme thinness from illness or hunger. Take care when describing someone's body, as it can offend.

Similar

  • 🍂面黃肌瘦miàn huáng jī shòu

    gaunt; emaciated — Pale and thin; about malnourishment.

    Chronic malnutrition left the children gaunt and pale.

  • 🍃弱不禁風ruò bù jīn fēng

    frail; delicate — Frail; about weakness.

    She looked frail in the cold.

Opposite

  • 🐻虎背熊腰hǔ bèi xióng yāo

    burly; broad-shouldered — Burly and broad.

    The bouncer was a burly man with broad shoulders.

  • 🍺大腹便便dà fù pián pián

    paunchy; pot-bellied — Paunchy, with a big belly.

    A paunchy middle-aged man sat at the counter.
