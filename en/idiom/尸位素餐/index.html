尸位素餐

shī wèi sù cān

In English

  • draw a salary without doing the job
  • hold a sinecure

Meaning

Holding a sinecure.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • deadwood

Example

尸位素餐的官員應該辭職。

Officials who draw a salary without doing the job should resign.

Usage

From the Book of Han; for criticizing officials and managers.

Similar

  • 🛋️怠忽職守dài hū zhí shǒu

    be negligent in one's duties — Negligent in one's duties.

    The guard was fired for being negligent in his duties.

  • 🛌逃避責任táo bì zé rèn

    skive v — Dodging responsibility.

    He tried to skive off his share of the housework.

Opposite

  • 🛠️責無旁貸zé wú páng dài

    it's my duty — One's bounden duty.

    Looking after my parents is my duty.

  • 📏一絲不苟yī sī bù gǒu

    meticulous; scrupulous — Meticulous.

    He is meticulous about every detail.
