破釜沉舟

pò fǔ chén zhōu

In English

  • (we've) burned the boats
  • reaching a point of no return; to stake everything on success

Meaning

Burning one's boats; no turning back.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • burn one's boats
  • the point of no return
  • stake everything on success

Example

我們已經破釜沉舟，沒有回頭路了。

We've burned the boats; there is no going back now.

Usage

From Xiang Yu at the Battle of Julu; used to show resolve.

Similar

  • 🌊背水一戰bèi shuǐ yī zhàn

    a last stand; do-or-die — A do-or-die battle.

    With their backs to the wall, the team played a do-or-die match.

  • 🎲孤注一擲gū zhù yī zhì

    a desperate play — A desperate all-or-nothing bet.

    Selling his house to fund the film was a desperate play.

Opposite

  • 🏃臨陣脫逃lín zhèn tuō táo

    shy from battle — Running away when the time comes.

    The goalkeeper shied from battle at the last moment.

  • 💰見好就收jiàn hǎo jiù shōu

    quit while one is ahead — Quitting while one is ahead.

    Quit while you're ahead and don't gamble the winnings.
