打消主意

dǎ xiāo zhǔ yì

In English

  • give up the idea
  • think better of it

Meaning

Thinking better of it; dropping the idea.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • drop the idea

Example

他打消主意，不出國定居了。

He gave up the idea of moving abroad.

Usage

Also 打消念頭; usually after second thoughts.

Similar

  • 🥶臨時反悔lín shí fǎn huǐ

    get cold feet — Getting cold feet.

    She got cold feet the night before the wedding.

  • 🚫不在考量bù zài kǎo liáng

    off the table; not under consideration — Off the table.

    Layoffs are off the table for now.

Opposite

  • 🐺一意孤行yī yì gū xíng

    go it alone; act arbitrarily — Going it alone, ignoring all advice.

    He went it alone, ignoring the advice of every adviser.

  • ⛵破釜沉舟pò fǔ chén zhōu

    (we've) burned the boats; reaching a point of no return — Burning one's boats.

    We've burned the boats; there is no going back now.
