得不償失

dé bù cháng shī

In English

  • too expensive to be worth it

Meaning

Not worth the cost.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • not worth it
  • a Pyrrhic victory

Example

熬夜趕完工作，實在得不償失。

Staying up all night to finish was too expensive to be worth it.

Usage

For weighing costs against gains.

Similar

  • 🥚殺雞取卵shā jī qǔ luǎn

    kill the goose that lays the golden eggs — Killing the goose that lays the golden eggs.

    Raising fees again would kill the goose that lays the golden eggs.

  • 🤦不智行為bù zhì xíng wéi

    ill-advised — Ill-advised.

    It was ill-advised to sign the contract without reading it.

Opposite

  • ✨事半功倍shì bàn gōng bèi

    great result with less effort — Great results with less effort.

    A good tool can get great results with less effort.

  • 🎰一本萬利yī běn wàn lì

    a small outlay for a huge return — A small outlay for a huge return.

    There is no such thing as a small outlay for a guaranteed huge return.
