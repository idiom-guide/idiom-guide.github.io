先見之明

xiān jiàn zhī míng

In English

  • be prescient of
  • proved prescient

Meaning

Foresight.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • foresight

Example

她提早賣出，事後證明很有先見之明。

Her decision to sell early proved prescient.

Usage

Often as 很有先見之明, 'very prescient'.

Similar

  • 🔭洞燭機先dòng zhú jī xiān

    see it coming; have uncanny foresight — Seeing the opportunity ahead of time; more formal.

    She could see it coming long before the market crashed.

  • 🔮一語成讖yī yǔ chéng chèn

    one's words prove prophetic — Words that prove prophetic, usually about bad things.

    His casual remark about a storm proved prophetic.

Opposite

  • ⏪事後諸葛shì hòu zhū gé

    hindsight; Monday morning quarterback — Hindsight; Monday-morning quarterbacking.

    He's a Monday morning quarterback who has all the answers after the game.

  • 🚇目光短淺mù guāng duǎn qiǎn

    short-sighted — Short-sighted.

    The decision was short-sighted.
