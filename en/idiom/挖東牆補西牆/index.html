挖東牆補西牆

wā dōng qiáng bǔ xī qiáng

In English

  • robbing Peter to pay Paul

Meaning

Robbing Peter to pay Paul.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • juggling debts
  • stopgap

Example

他用信用卡挖東牆補西牆。

He is robbing Peter to pay Paul with his credit cards.

Usage

Colloquial; for finances and staffing.

Similar

  • 🗓️寅吃卯糧yín chī mǎo liáng

    borrow against the future — Borrowing against the future.

    Funding today's pensions with new debt is borrowing against the future.

  • ☠️飲鴆止渴yǐn zhèn zhǐ kě

    a short-term fix that causes long-term harm — A cure worse than the disease.

    Borrowing more money is a short-term fix that causes long-term harm.

Opposite

  • 🧮量入為出liàng rù wéi chū

    live within one's means — Living within one's means.

    A startup has to live within its means.

  • 🧮精打細算jīng dǎ xì suàn

    astute with money; budget carefully — Careful budgeting.

    She is astute with money and plans every purchase.
