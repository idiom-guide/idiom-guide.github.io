大舉進攻

dà jǔ jìn gōng

In English

  • unleash the hounds

Meaning

An all-out attack.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • launch an all-out attack
  • go on the offensive

Example

辯論時，反對黨對部長大舉進攻。

The opposition unleashed the hounds on the minister during the debate.

Usage

Military, or figuratively in debates or markets.

Similar

  • 🐝群起圍攻qún qǐ wéi gōng

    drew flak from sb for reason — Ganging up.

    The company drew flak from customers over the price rise.

  • 🔥火力全開huǒ lì quán kāi

    be firing on all cylinders; do sth at full throttle — Firing on all cylinders.

    The team was firing on all cylinders in the second half.

Opposite

  • 🚩偃旗息鼓yǎn qí xī gǔ

    call a halt; lie low — Calling it off.

    The band announced they would call a halt for now: a pause, they said, not a farewell.

  • 🪑靜觀其變jìng guān qí biàn

    stay tuned; sit tight and assess — Sitting tight and watching.

    Let's sit tight and assess the situation before we act.
