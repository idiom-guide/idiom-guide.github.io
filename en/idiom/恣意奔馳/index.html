恣意奔馳

zì yì bēn chí

In English

  • drive it like you stole it

Meaning

Driving like you stole it.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • floor it
  • tear down the road

Example

他開著車恣意奔馳。

He drove it like he stole it.

Usage

For the thrill of driving, riding or running.

Similar

  • 🌊放浪不羈fàng làng bù jī

    unrestrained; free-spirited — Free-spirited.

    He lives a free-spirited life on the road.

  • 🎲豁出去huō chū qù

    go for broke; throw caution to the wind — Going for broke.

    I'll go for broke.

Opposite

  • 🐾謹慎行動jǐn shèn xíng dòng

    tread carefully — Treading carefully.

    In unfamiliar markets, it pays to tread carefully.

  • 🎚️小力一點xiǎo lì yī diǎn

    dial back — Dialing it back.

    Dial back your speed a little.
