成何體統

chéng hé tǐ tǒng

In English

  • absurd
  • improper
  • behave badly
  • having no manners

Meaning

Improper; what a disgrace.Negative · Colloquial

Example

在圖書館大吼大叫？成何體統！

Shouting in a library? How absurd and improper!

Usage

For scolding unseemly behavior, often as a rhetorical question.

Similar

  • 🤦太離譜了tài lí pǔ le

    egregious; way out of line — Way out of line.

    It's an egregious mistake to ignore the safety warnings.

  • 🙊不太文雅bù tài wén yǎ

    unprintable — Not very polite.

    He said something unprintable about the referee.

Opposite

  • 📏循規蹈矩xún guī dǎo jǔ

    by the book; play by the rules — By the book.

    He plays by the rules and never takes risks.

  • 🎩彬彬有禮bīn bīn yǒu lǐ

    courteous; well-mannered — Courteous.

    The waiter was courteous throughout.
