振奮人心

zhèn fèn rén xīn

In English

  • uplifting
  • inspiring

Meaning

Uplifting.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • heartening

Example

教練的演說振奮人心。

The coach's speech was uplifting.

Usage

For speeches, news, victories and music.

Similar

  • 🔊擲地有聲zhì dì yǒu shēng

    carry real weight; be forceful and weighty — Words that ring out.

    His speech carried real weight and silenced the room.

  • 💪極為有力jí wéi yǒu lì

    incredibly powerful — Incredibly powerful.

    It was an incredibly powerful speech.

Opposite

  • 😨人心惶惶rén xīn huáng huáng

    widespread jitters; public panic — Public panic.

    Rumors of a bank run caused widespread jitters.

  • 🥱枯燥無味kū zào wú wèi

    lackluster; uninspiring — Uninspiring.

    The lecture was lackluster and uninspiring.
