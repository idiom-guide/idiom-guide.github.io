凜冬將至

lǐn dōng jiāng zhì

In English

  • Winter is coming

Meaning

Winter is coming.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • winter is coming
  • hard times ahead
  • brace yourself

Example

多備些柴火，凜冬將至。

Stock up on firewood. Winter is coming.

Usage

Popularized by Game of Thrones; often for economic or industry downturns.

Similar

  • 🌩️山雨欲來風滿樓shān yǔ yù lái fēng mǎn lóu

    the calm before the storm; storm clouds are gathering — Storm clouds gathering.

    Storm clouds are gathering over the market.

  • 🔥大難臨頭dà nàn lín tóu

    disaster looms; doom is at hand — Disaster looming.

    The staff laughed at the alarm, but this was no fire drill: disaster was about to hit.

Opposite

  • 🌅否極泰來pǐ jí tài lái

    the tide will turn; every cloud has a silver lining — The tide will turn.

    After a hard year, the tide will turn.

  • 🌈未來會更好wèi lái huì gèng hǎo

    Better days ahead — Better days ahead.

    Hang in there: better days ahead.
