長久之計

cháng jiǔ zhī jì

In English

  • a long term solution

Meaning

A long-term solution.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • a long-term solution
  • a lasting solution
  • a sustainable plan

Example

漲價不是解決短缺的長久之計。

Raising prices is not a long-term solution to the shortage.

Usage

Often in the negative: 不是長久之計 (not a long-term solution).

Similar

  • 🔭超前部署chāo qián bù shǔ

    visionary — Planning ahead.

    The visionary mayor planned for the flood years before it came.

  • ☂️未雨綢繆wèi yǔ chóu móu

    be fully prepared; provident — Preparing for a rainy day.

    Keep an emergency kit at home; it pays to be fully prepared.

Opposite

  • 🩹權宜之計quán yí zhī jì

    expedient; temporary — A stopgap measure.

    Renting a car was only an expedient while ours was being repaired.

  • 🔧權變措施quán biàn cuò shī

    workaround (n, v.) — A workaround.

    We need a workaround until the server is fixed.
