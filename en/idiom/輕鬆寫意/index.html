輕鬆寫意

qīng sōng xiě yì

In English

  • sb doesn't show much stress

Meaning

Easy and relaxed.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • easy and relaxed
  • breezy

Example

他輕鬆寫意地回答每個問題。

He answered every question and didn't show much stress.

Usage

For handling tasks or questions without visible strain.

Similar

  • 🔪遊刃有餘yóu rèn yǒu yú

    looks effortless; be more than equal to — Looking effortless.

    She handles the busiest shifts with ease, making it look effortless.

  • 🦢從容不迫cóng róng bù pò

    unruffled — Unruffled.

    The host remained unruffled when the lights went out.

Opposite

  • 🤹手忙腳亂shǒu máng jiǎo luàn

    get flustered — Getting flustered.

    I get flustered when the phone rings during a meeting.

  • 😓勞心勞力láo xīn láo lì

    exhaust oneself; brain and brawn — Exhausting oneself.

    Running the shop alone exhausted him, in both brain and brawn.
