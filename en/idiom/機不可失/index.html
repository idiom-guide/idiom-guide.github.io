機不可失

jī bù kě shī

In English

  • sb will never get this chance again

Meaning

Now or never.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • now or never
  • don't miss this chance
  • you'll never get this chance again

Example

優惠今晚截止，機不可失。

This offer ends tonight, and you will never get this chance again.

Usage

Full form: 機不可失，時不再來; common in sales pitches and when urging someone to seize a chance.

Similar

  • 🌠千載難逢qiān zǎi nán féng

    once in a lifetime; sth of a lifetime — Once in a lifetime; about rarity.

    Meeting my idol was a once-in-a-lifetime chance.

  • ⏰時間不等人shí jiān bù děng rén

    you snooze, you lose; Clock's ticking — Time waits for no one.

    Sign up today. You snooze, you lose.

Opposite

  • 🌅來日方長lái rì fāng cháng

    there will be many more opportunities ahead — There's plenty of time ahead.

    There will be many more opportunities ahead to work together.

  • 📋按部就班àn bù jiù bān

    step by step; by the book — Step by step, no rush.

    New hires are trained step by step.
