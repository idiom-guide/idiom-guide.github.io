4 · Character & Conduct

不可一世

bù kě yī shì

In English

  • act high and mighty
  • cocky

Meaning

High and mighty.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • full of oneself

Example

新科冠軍顯得不可一世。

The new champion acted high and mighty.

Usage

For the arrogance that comes with power or success.

Similar

  • 🙄目中無人mù zhōng wú rén

    arrogant; look down on everyone — Looking down on everyone.

    The star was arrogant and looked down on everyone.

  • 🚜盛氣凌人shèng qì líng rén

    steamroller — Overbearing.

    The new manager steamrolled over every objection.

Opposite

  • 🙇‍♂️虛懷若谷xū huái ruò gǔ

    be humble and open-minded — Humble and open-minded.

    The more accomplished a person is, the more humble and open-minded they tend to be.

  • 🤗平易近人píng yì jìn rén

    approachable — Approachable.

    The professor is so approachable that students visit him with every question.
