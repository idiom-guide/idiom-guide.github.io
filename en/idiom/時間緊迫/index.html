時間緊迫

shí jiān jǐn pò

In English

  • on the clock

Meaning

Pressed for time.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • pressed for time
  • time is tight

Example

晚餐尖峰時段，廚房裡每個人都在跟時間賽跑，時間緊迫。

Everyone in the kitchen is on the clock during the dinner rush.

Usage

Often 時間緊迫，長話短說 (time is short, so I'll be brief); also for work deadlines.

Similar

  • 🚨刻不容緩kè bù róng huǎn

    urgent; brook no delay — Allowing no delay.

    Climate action is urgent.

  • ⏰迫在眉睫pò zài méi jié

    time is a luxury we don't have; sth. is looming — Looming right in front of us.

    The deadline is looming, and time is a luxury we don't have.

Opposite

  • 👌綽綽有餘chuò chuò yǒu yú

    more than enough — More than enough.

    Ten thousand dollars is more than enough for the trip.

  • 🌅來日方長lái rì fāng cháng

    there will be many more opportunities ahead — There's plenty of time ahead.

    There will be many more opportunities ahead to work together.
