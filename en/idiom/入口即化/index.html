入口即化

rù kǒu jí huà

In English

  • melt-in-your-mouth
  • melting tender

Meaning

Dissolving the moment it enters the mouth: extremely soft, smooth texture.Positive · General

Example

這塊五花肉燉得入口即化。

The braised pork belly was melt-in-your-mouth tender.

Usage

For stewed meat, desserts, ice treats and tofu. Mostly a compliment on texture.

Similar

  • 🥩鮮嫩多汁xiān nèn duō zhī

    juicy; succulent — Fresh, tender and full of juice; leans toward juiciness.

    The steak was cooked to perfection: juicy and tender.

  • 🥓肥而不膩féi ér bù nì

    unctuous; rich but not greasy — Rich in fat yet not greasy; leans toward richness.

    The Dongpo pork is rich but not greasy and melts in the mouth.

Opposite

  • 🤮難以下嚥nán yǐ xià yàn

    barely edible; hard to swallow — Barely edible.

    The dish was so salty that it was barely edible.
