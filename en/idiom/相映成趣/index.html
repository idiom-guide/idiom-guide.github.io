相映成趣

xiāng yìng chéng qù

In English

  • form a delightful contrast

Meaning

A delightful contrast.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • complement each other
  • make a charming pair

Example

古廟和玻璃大樓相映成趣。

The old temple and the glass tower form a delightful contrast.

Usage

Formal; for contrasts of scenery, old and new, or color.

Similar

  • 🍛相得益彰xiāng dé yì zhāng

    complement and enhance each other — Bringing out the best in each other.

    The visuals and the music complement and enhance each other.

Opposite

  • 🐟格格不入gé gé bù rù

    be out of place; be like a fish out of water — A fish out of water.

    He felt like a fish out of water among his new colleagues.
