左右命運

zuǒ yòu mìng yùn

In English

  • seal sb's fate

Meaning

Deciding someone's fate.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • decide sb's fate
  • make or break

Example

一個錯誤的簽名，就可能左右命運。

One mistaken signature could seal his fate.

Usage

For decisions, votes, rulings and turning points.

Similar

  • 🏋️舉足輕重jǔ zú qīng zhòng

    pivotal; carry great weight — Carrying great weight.

    The swing states are pivotal.

  • 💀攸關生死yōu guān shēng sǐ

    a matter of life and death — A matter of life and death.

    For patients on the waiting list, this decision is a matter of life and death.

Opposite

  • 🤏無關緊要wú guān jǐn yào

    immaterial — Immaterial.

    Whether we meet at six or seven is immaterial.

  • 🐞微不足道wēi bù zú dào

    insignificant; trivial — Insignificant.

    The difference is trivial.
