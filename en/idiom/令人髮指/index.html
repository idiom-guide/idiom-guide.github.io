4 · Pain & Unease

令人髮指

lìng rén fà zhǐ

In English

  • heinous

Meaning

Heinous.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • outrageous
  • monstrous

Example

這起令人髮指的罪行震驚全國。

The heinous crime shocked the nation.

Usage

For crimes and atrocities.

Similar

  • 👹窮兇惡極qióng xiōng è jí

    atrocious; diabolic — Atrocious.

    The gang committed atrocious crimes.

  • 🎋罄竹難書qìng zhú nán shū

    too numerous to count; too many to record (of misdeeds) — Too many misdeeds to record.

    The dictator's crimes are too numerous to count.

Opposite

  • 👏值得讚頌zhí dé zàn sòng

    laudable; commendable — Laudable.

    Their courage is laudable.

  • 👍為人稱道wéi rén chēng dào

    widely praised/complimented; commendable — Widely praised.

    Her kindness to strangers was widely praised.
