喋喋不休

dié dié bù xiū

In English

  • ad nauseam

Meaning

Going on and on.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • go on and on
  • talk sb's ear off

Example

他對自己的假期喋喋不休。

He went on about his holiday ad nauseam.

Usage

For people who won't stop talking.

Similar

  • 🔁沒完沒了méi wán méi liǎo

    incessant; never-ending — Never-ending.

    The incessant chatter on the train gave me a headache.

  • 🚰滔滔不絕tāo tāo bù jué

    talk nonstop; go on without a pause — Talking nonstop.

    The moment football comes up, he talks nonstop.

Opposite

  • 📝言簡意賅yán jiǎn yì gāi

    concise and to the point — Concise and to the point.

    Her report was concise and to the point.

  • 🤐守口如瓶shǒu kǒu rú píng

    tight-lipped; keep mum — Tight-lipped.

    He stayed tight-lipped about the merger.
