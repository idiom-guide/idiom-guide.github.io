如火如荼

rú huǒ rú tú

In English

  • in full swing

Meaning

In full swing.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • in full force
  • at its height

Example

選戰正如火如荼地展開。

The election campaign is in full swing.

Usage

For events, campaigns and movements at their height.

Similar

  • 🌱方興未艾fāng xīng wèi ài

    unabated — Still on the rise.

    The boom in electric cars is still unabated.

  • 🎉非常熱絡fēi cháng rè luò

    frenetic — Very lively.

    The trading floor was frenetic that morning.

Opposite

  • 🚩偃旗息鼓yǎn qí xī gǔ

    call a halt; lie low — Calling it off.

    The band announced they would call a halt for now: a pause, they said, not a farewell.

  • 🕸️門可羅雀mén kě luó què

    deserted; hardly a customer in sight — Deserted.

    Once packed, the mall is now deserted.
