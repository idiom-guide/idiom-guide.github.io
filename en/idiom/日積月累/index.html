2 · Change & Fate

日積月累

rì jī yuè lěi

In English

  • no quick fixes

Meaning

Built up bit by bit over time.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • steady, accumulated effort
  • build up over time

Example

學語言沒有捷徑，進步要靠日積月累。

There are no quick fixes in language learning; progress comes from steady, accumulated effort.

Usage

For learning, skills, savings and habits.

Similar

  • 💧滴水穿石dī shuǐ chuān shí

    constant dripping wears away a stone; perseverance pays off — Constant dripping wears away a stone.

    Constant dripping wears away a stone, so keep practicing.

  • 🔼循序漸進xún xù jiàn jìn

    proceed step by step; make gradual progress — Step by step.

    Learn to swim step by step; don't jump into the deep end at once.

Opposite

  • 🌠一蹴可幾yī cù kě jǐ

    achieved overnight; an instant success — Overnight success.

    Fluency is not achieved overnight; nobody succeeds at the first try.

  • 🚀一飛沖天yī fēi chōng tiān

    gravity-defying — Soaring to the top in one go.

    The startup's gravity-defying rise stunned investors.
