8 · Markets & the Economy

供不應求

gōng bù yìng qiú

In English

  • demand outstrips supply

Meaning

Demand outstripping supply.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • in short supply
  • sell like hot cakes

Example

晶片仍然供不應求。

Demand for chips still outstrips supply.

Usage

For markets, goods and talent.

Similar

  • 💎奇貨可居qí huò kě jū

    hold on to a rare commodity; a rare find worth hoarding — A rare find worth hoarding.

    He thought the rare stamp was a find worth hoarding, so he held on to it.

  • 🏪門庭若市mén tíng ruò shì

    bustling with customers — Bustling with customers.

    The new bakery is bustling with customers.

Opposite

  • 📦供過於求gōng guò yú qiú

    oversupply; a glut — A glut.

    A glut of office space has pushed rents down.

  • 🕸️乏人問津fá rén wèn jīn

    attract no buyers; go unnoticed — Going unnoticed.

    The old house has attracted no buyers.
