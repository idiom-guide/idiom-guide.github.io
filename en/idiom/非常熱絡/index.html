非常熱絡

fēi cháng rè luò

In English

  • frenetic

Meaning

Very lively; buzzing with activity.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • bustling
  • brisk

Example

那天早上，交易大廳非常熱絡。

The trading floor was frenetic that morning.

Usage

For markets, trading, interaction and discussion; the listed frenetic implies frenzy, while the Chinese is positive.

Similar

  • 🏪門庭若市mén tíng ruò shì

    bustling with customers — Packed with customers.

    The new bakery is bustling with customers.

  • 🚦熙來攘往xī lái rǎng wǎng

    hustle and bustle — Bustling with people.

    The hustle and bustle of the night market never stops.

Opposite

  • 🕸️門可羅雀mén kě luó què

    deserted; hardly a customer in sight — Deserted.

    Once packed, the mall is now deserted.

  • 🦗鴉雀無聲yā què wú shēng

    be dead silent; you could hear a pin drop — Dead silence.

    After the result was announced, the hall fell dead silent.
