認清現實

rèn qīng xiàn shí

In English

  • reality check

Meaning

Facing reality.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • face reality
  • get a reality check
  • come to terms with reality
  • wake up to the facts

Example

該認清現實了：我們根本買不起這間房子。

It's time for a reality check: we simply can't afford this house.

Usage

Urges someone to let go of illusions; can be stern or sympathetic.

Similar

  • 👣腳踏實地jiǎo tà shí dì

    staying grounded and pushing ahead — Down to earth.

    He is a down-to-earth man who stays grounded and keeps pushing ahead.

  • ⚛實事求是shí shì qiú shì

    seek truth from facts; be down-to-earth — Seeking truth from facts.

    Research should seek truth from facts, not start from a conclusion and look for evidence.

Opposite

  • 🪁異想天開yì xiǎng tiān kāi

    quixotic, magical thinking; vagaries — Fanciful and unrealistic.

    His plan to sell ice in the Arctic was pure magical thinking.

  • 🙅自欺欺人zì qī qī rén

    lying to oneself; bury one's head in the sand — Fooling oneself.

    Pretending nothing is wrong is just burying your head in the sand.
