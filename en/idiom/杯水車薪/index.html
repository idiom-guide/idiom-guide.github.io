12 · Quantity

杯水車薪

bēi shuǐ chē xīn

In English

  • far too little to help

Meaning

Far too little to help.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • a drop in the bucket
  • too little, too late

Example

這筆補助只是杯水車薪。

The subsidy is far too little to help.

Usage

From Mencius; for aid, relief or resources that fall short.

Similar

  • 🥜九牛一毛jiǔ niú yī máo

    peanuts; just a drop in the bucket — Just a drop in the bucket.

    The donation was just a drop in the bucket compared with what the hospital needed.

  • 🩹於事無補yú shì wú bǔ

    be cold comfort to v — Cold comfort.

    An apology now is cold comfort to the victims.

Opposite

  • 👌綽綽有餘chuò chuò yǒu yú

    more than enough — More than enough.

    Ten thousand dollars is more than enough for the trip.
