欲速則不達

yù sù zé bù dá

In English

  • more haste less speed
  • haste makes waste

Meaning

More haste, less speed.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • more haste, less speed
  • slow and steady wins the race

Example

烤蛋糕別急，欲速則不達。

Don't rush the cake. More haste, less speed.

Usage

From the Analects; used to warn against rushing.

Similar

  • 🌽揠苗助長yà miáo zhù zhǎng

    spoil things by being over-eager; pull up the seedlings to help them grow — Pulling up seedlings to help them grow; forcing things ruins them.

    Forcing children to learn too much too early is like pulling seedlings up to help them grow.

  • 🚅操之過急cāo zhī guò jí

    be overly hasty; act in haste — Overhasty; 欲速則不達 is the result.

    This matter must be handled slowly; we can't be overly hasty.

Opposite

  • 🔼循序漸進xún xù jiàn jìn

    proceed step by step; make gradual progress — Step by step.

    Learn to swim step by step; don't jump into the deep end at once.

  • ⛑穩紮穩打wěn zhā wěn dǎ

    build steadily; play it safe and steady — Steady and sure.

    A start-up should build steadily and not rush to expand.
