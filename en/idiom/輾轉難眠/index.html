4 · Pain & Unease

輾轉難眠

zhǎn zhuǎn nán mián

In English

  • toss and turn
  • lose sleep over sth

Meaning

Tossing and turning.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • can't sleep a wink

Example

我整晚輾轉難眠。

I tossed and turned all night.

Usage

Adapted from 'tossing and turning' in the first poem of the Book of Songs.

Similar

  • 😔心事重重xīn shì chóng chóng

    preoccupied; weighed down by worries — Weighed down by worries.

    He seemed preoccupied all day.

  • 😟忐忑不安tǎn tè bù ān

    anxious; on edge — Perturbed.

    She was perturbed by the strange noise downstairs.

Opposite

  • 🎈無事一身輕wú shì yī shēn qīng

    unencumbered — Unencumbered.

    With the deadline behind him, he felt unencumbered.

  • 😌如釋重負rú shì zhòng fù

    let shoulders drop; relieved — Relieved.

    She felt relieved when the exams were over, as if a heavy weight had been lifted.
