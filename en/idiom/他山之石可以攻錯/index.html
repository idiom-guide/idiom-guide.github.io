他山之石，可以攻錯

tā shān zhī shí, kě yǐ gōng cuò

In English

  • benchmark learning

Meaning

Stones from other hills can polish jade: learn from others to improve oneself.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • learn from others' experience
  • take a leaf out of sb's book
  • borrow ideas from elsewhere
  • use others' successes as a benchmark

Example

他山之石，可以攻錯，我們不妨研究新加坡的交通管理。

Studying how Singapore manages traffic gave us a useful benchmark; stones from other hills can polish our own jade.

Usage

From the Book of Songs (Xiao Ya, He Ming); also 他山之石，可以攻玉; used to suggest drawing on others' experience.

Similar

  • 📚教學相長jiào xué xiāng zhǎng

    learn from teaching — Teaching and learning go hand in hand.

    Teaching has taught me as much as it has taught my students. I truly learn from teaching.

  • 🧩相輔相成xiāng fǔ xiāng chéng

    complement each other; go hand in hand — Complementing each other.

    Good teaching and curiosity complement each other.

Opposite

  • 🚘閉門造車bì mén zào chē

    work in isolation from reality — Working behind closed doors.

    You can't build products in isolation; listen to your users.

  • 🚋墨守成規mò shǒu chéng guī

    stick to old rules; stay in a rut — Sticking rigidly to old ways.

    The company sticks to old rules and finds it hard to innovate.
