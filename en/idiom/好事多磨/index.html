好事多磨

hǎo shì duō mó

In English

  • the road to success is full of setbacks
  • good things come to those who wait

Meaning

Good things come to those who wait.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • nothing worth having comes easy

Example

好事多磨，耐心等就對了。

Good things come to those who wait.

Usage

For comforting or encouraging people facing setbacks.

Similar

  • 🌅否極泰來pǐ jí tài lái

    the tide will turn; every cloud has a silver lining — The tide will turn.

    After a hard year, the tide will turn.

Opposite

  • ⛵一帆風順yī fān fēng shùn

    smooth sailing — Smooth sailing.

    I wish you smooth sailing in your new post.

  • 🧁輕而易舉qīng ér yì jǔ

    be a piece of cake — A piece of cake.

    For him, this is a piece of cake.
