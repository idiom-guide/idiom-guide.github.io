肺腑之言

fèi fǔ zhī yán

In English

  • from the bottom of my heart

Meaning

Heartfelt words; sincere advice from the heart.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • words from the heart
  • heartfelt words
  • sincere words

Example

這些都是我的肺腑之言。

These words come from the bottom of my heart.

Usage

Used in advice, apologies and thanks.

Similar

  • 🤲推心置腹tuī xīn zhì fù

    speak from the heart; confide in sb — Opening one's heart to someone.

    After years of friendship, she could confide in him about anything.

  • ❤️發自肺腑fā zì fèi fǔ

    genuine — From the bottom of one's heart; used as a modifier.

    Her apology was genuine and clearly came from the heart.

Opposite

  • 🎭虛情假意xū qíng jiǎ yì

    insincere; hollow sentiments — Feigned feelings.

    Her feigned friendliness fooled no one.

  • 🎀冠冕堂皇guān miǎn táng huáng

    sound lofty but be hollow; be high-sounding — High-sounding but hollow; often negative.

    His words sound lofty, but he is really only thinking of himself.
