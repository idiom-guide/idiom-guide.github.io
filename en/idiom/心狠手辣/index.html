4 · Character & Conduct

心狠手辣

xīn hěn shǒu là

In English

  • ruthless
  • cold-blooded

Meaning

Ruthless.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • merciless

Example

那個反派心狠手辣、冷血無情。

The villain is ruthless and cold-blooded.

Usage

For villains, criminals and cold-hearted rivals.

Similar

  • 👹窮兇惡極qióng xiōng è jí

    atrocious; diabolic — Atrocious.

    The gang committed atrocious crimes.

Opposite

  • 😊慈眉善目cí méi shàn mù

    likable — A kind face.

    The old monk was a kind, likable man.

  • 🧡樂善好施lè shàn hào shī

    good Samaritan — Charitable.

    A good Samaritan paid for the stranger's medical bills.
