深藏不露

shēn cáng bù lù

In English

  • hidden depth
  • hidden talent
  • dark horse
  • still water runs deep

Meaning

Hidden depths.Positive · General

Example

安靜的新實習生原來深藏不露。

The quiet new intern turned out to be a dark horse.

Usage

For quiet people who turn out to be highly capable.

Similar

  • 🐷扮豬吃老虎bàn zhū chī lǎo hǔ

    sandbagging — Sandbagging.

    He played dumb at the table, sandbagging his rivals before winning the pot.

  • 🐯臥虎藏龍wò hǔ cáng lóng

    a place or situation that is full of people with unusual talents — A place full of hidden talent.

    The village looks sleepy, but it is full of people with unusual talents.

Opposite

  • 🐶虛張聲勢xū zhāng shēng shì

    bluff; more bark than bite — More bark than bite.

    His threats were just a bluff; he is more bark than bite.

  • 📢愛出風頭ài chū fēng tou

    sb is loud — Loving the limelight.

    My cousin is loud and loves being the center of attention.
