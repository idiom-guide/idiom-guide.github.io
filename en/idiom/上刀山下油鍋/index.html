上刀山下油鍋

shàng dāo shān xià yóu guō

In English

  • go through hell and high water

Meaning

Going through hell and high water.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • go through fire and water
  • do whatever it takes

Example

為了帶你回家，上刀山下油鍋我也願意。

I'd go through hell and high water to get you home.

Usage

Used to show loyalty or resolve; hyperbolic.

Similar

  • 🚀義無反顧yì wú fǎn gù

    be resolute and not look back; go ahead without hesitation — Resolute, never looking back.

    For her children, she resolutely quit her job without looking back.

  • 💪不遺餘力bù yí yú lì

    has gone to great pains; spare no efforts — Sparing no effort.

    The volunteers spared no effort in rescuing the stranded hikers.

Opposite

  • 🏃臨陣脫逃lín zhèn tuō táo

    shy from battle — Running away when the time comes.

    The goalkeeper shied from battle at the last moment.

  • 🦔明哲保身míng zhé bǎo shēn

    play it safe; look after one's own interests — Keeping oneself out of trouble.

    Faced with the injustice, he played it safe and said nothing.
