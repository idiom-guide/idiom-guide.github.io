不知不覺

bù zhī bù jué

In English

  • unwittingly

Meaning

Without realizing.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • without realizing
  • before one knows it

Example

他不知不覺走進了錯的房間。

Unwittingly, he had walked into the wrong room.

Usage

For doing something unawares, or for time slipping by.

Similar

  • 🤖不自覺地bù zì jué de

    inadvertently — Inadvertently.

    He inadvertently hummed the tune while he worked.

  • 🌱潛移默化qián yí mò huà

    imperceptibly; learning by osmosis — Shaped imperceptibly.

    Children learn manners by osmosis, almost imperceptibly.

Opposite

  • 🦂處心積慮chǔ xīn jī lǜ

    scheme meticulously; go to great lengths — Scheming long and hard.

    He schemed meticulously to get close to the chairman.
