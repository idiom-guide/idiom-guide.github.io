四面八方

sì miàn bā fāng

In English

  • four corners of the earth

Meaning

From all directions.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • from all directions
  • the four corners of the earth
  • far and wide

Example

球迷從四面八方趕來看比賽。

Fans came from the four corners of the earth to watch the match.

Usage

Often followed by 趕來, 湧來 or 傳來 (rush, pour or come from).

Similar

  • 🌍遍佈全球biàn bù quán qiú

    global prevalence — All over the world.

    The global prevalence of smartphones has changed how we live.

  • 📡無遠弗屆wú yuǎn fú jiè

    far-reaching; without borders — Reaching even the farthest places.

    The internet's influence is far-reaching.

Opposite

  • 🖐️屈指可數qū zhǐ kě shǔ

    can be counted on one hand — Few enough to count on one hand.

    Experts in this field can be counted on one hand.

  • 🤏寥寥無幾liáo liáo wú jǐ

    very few; a mere handful — Very few.

    Very few applicants met the requirements.
