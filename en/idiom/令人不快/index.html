令人不快

lìng rén bù kuài

In English

  • unpalatable

Meaning

Unpleasant.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • unpleasant
  • off-putting

Example

這股氣味令人不快，多數客人都受不了。

The smell was unpalatable to most guests.

Usage

For smells, behavior, facts and news.

Similar

  • 🤮令人反感lìng rén fǎn gǎn

    distasteful; off-putting — Distasteful; stronger.

    His constant bragging was distasteful.

Opposite

  • 🌸如沐春風rú mù chūn fēng

    feel so good; pleasant — Like a spring breeze.

    Talking with her made me feel so good, like a spring breeze.

  • 😊滿心愉悅mǎn xīn yú yuè

    delight — Full of delight.

    She smiled with delight at the surprise.
