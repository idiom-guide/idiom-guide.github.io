言行一致

yán xíng yī zhì

In English

  • be aligned
  • consistent

Meaning

Walking the talk.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • walk the talk

Example

她言行一致。

Her actions are consistent with her words.

Usage

For leaders, teachers and anyone held to their word.

Similar

  • 🤞講話負責jiǎng huà fù zé

    be true to one's word; stand by one's words — Standing by one's words.

    A leader should stand by his words.

  • ➖始終如一shǐ zhōng rú yī

    stay consistent; remain the same throughout — Consistent from start to finish.

    She treated her friends the same throughout and never changed when they fell on hard times.

Opposite

  • 🎭心口不一xīn kǒu bù yī

    disingenuous (slightly dishonest); disingenuous — Disingenuous.

    He claims to support the plan but quietly works against it, which is disingenuous.

  • 🤫陽奉陰違yáng fèng yīn wéi

    pay lip service while secretly defying — Paying lip service.

    He paid lip service to the policy while secretly defying it.
