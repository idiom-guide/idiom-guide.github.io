輕描淡寫

qīng miáo dàn xiě

In English

  • understatement
  • downplay

Meaning

Playing something down; mentioning it only lightly.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • play down
  • understate
  • gloss over

Example

說這場風暴是「一點小雨」，實在是輕描淡寫。

Calling the storm "a little rain" was a huge understatement.

Usage

Can describe a writing style or criticize glossing over; often 只是輕描淡寫地說.

Similar

  • 🔕點到為止diǎn dào wéi zhǐ

    stop at a hint; say just enough — Stopping at the right point; about tact.

    The teacher only hinted at the point and left the rest for the students to work out.

  • 〰一筆帶過yī bǐ dài guò

    gloss over; mention only in passing — Mentioning only in passing.

    The report only touched on the cost issue in passing.

Opposite

  • 🔠大書特書dà shū tè shū

    make a great fuss in print; write at length about — Writing at great length about something.

    The media made a great fuss about the matter in print.

  • 🔉言過其實yán guò qí shí

    exaggerate; overstate — Overstating things.

    The restaurant's advertising overstates the reality.
