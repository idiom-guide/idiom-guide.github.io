光怪陸離

guāng guài lù lí

In English

  • sth very strange

Meaning

Very strange.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • very strange
  • bizarre
  • kaleidoscopic

Example

這個節慶處處是光怪陸離的景象。

The festival was full of very strange sights.

Usage

Formal; for sights, phenomena and the ways of society.

Similar

  • 👾荒誕不經huāng dàn bù jīng

    be absurd; be preposterous — Preposterous.

    The story is absurd, and nobody will believe it.

  • 🤯匪夷所思fěi yí suǒ sī

    mind-boggling — Mind-boggling.

    The magician's last trick was mind-boggling.

Opposite

  • 🥱平淡無奇píng dàn wú qí

    run-of-the-mill — Run-of-the-mill.

    It was just a run-of-the-mill day at the office.

  • 🥱司空見慣sī kōng jiàn guàn

    a common sight; commonplace — Commonplace.

    Delays are commonplace on this line.
