無庸置疑

wú yōng zhì yí

In English

  • indisputable

Meaning

Beyond doubt.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • beyond doubt
  • there is no doubt that

Example

規律運動能促進健康，這點無庸置疑。

It is indisputable that regular exercise improves health.

Usage

Also written 毋庸置疑; stresses that the facts are clear and need no debate.

Similar

  • 💯毫無疑問háo wú yí wèn

    indisputably — Without a doubt; more colloquial.

    She is indisputably the best player on the team.

  • 👆無可否認wú kě fǒu rèn

    be undeniable — Undeniable; often concedes before a "but."

    It is undeniable that he has put in a lot of effort.

Opposite

  • 🔅疑點重重yí diǎn chóng chóng

    be full of doubts; be riddled with questions — Full of suspicious points.

    The case is full of doubts and must be re-investigated.

  • ❓啟人疑竇qǐ rén yí dòu

    raise suspicion; sth. be doubtful — Raising suspicion; an action, not a state.

    The sudden change in his story raised suspicion.
