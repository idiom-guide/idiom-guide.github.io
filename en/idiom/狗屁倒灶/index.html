狗屁倒灶

gǒu pì dǎo zào

In English

  • a total mess
  • a snafu

Meaning

A total mess.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • a shambles

Example

這次搬家狗屁倒灶，箱子弄丟、家具摔壞。

The move was a total mess, with boxes lost and furniture broken.

Usage

Crude slang; avoid in formal settings.

Similar
