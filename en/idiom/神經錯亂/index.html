神經錯亂

shén jīng cuò luàn

In English

  • unhinged

Meaning

Unhinged.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • off one's rocker
  • crazy

Example

他突然大吼，讓一些人以為他神經錯亂了。

His sudden shouting made some people think he had become unhinged.

Usage

Colloquially an exaggeration for erratic behavior.

Similar

  • 🤪精神錯亂jīng shén cuò luàn

    deranged; unhinged — Deranged.

    The hermit seemed unhinged after years alone.

  • 😵神智不清shén zhì bù qīng

    not in sb's right mind — Not in one's right mind.

    He was not in his right mind after the accident.

Opposite

  • 💎思考清晰sī kǎo qīng xī

    clear insight — Clear thinking.

    Her answer showed clear insight into the problem.

  • 🧊保持冷靜bǎo chí lěng jìng

    be dispassionate — Staying calm.

    In an emergency, try to stay dispassionate and think clearly.
