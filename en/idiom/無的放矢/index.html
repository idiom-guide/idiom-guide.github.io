無的放矢

wú dì fàng shǐ

In English

  • take potshots at sb

Meaning

Taking potshots.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • shoot in the dark
  • baseless criticism

Example

那位評論家連電影都沒看就無的放矢。

The critic was taking potshots at the film without having seen it.

Usage

For criticizing groundless remarks.

Similar

  • 👂道聽塗說dào tīng tú shuō

    hearsay; go by hearsay — Hearsay.

    That story is nothing but hearsay.

  • 🎐捕風捉影bǔ fēng zhuō yǐng

    chase shadows; act on groundless rumors — Groundless speculation.

    These reports are groundless speculation with no evidence behind them.

Opposite

  • 🎯一針見血yī zhēn jiàn xiě

    hit the nail on the head — Hitting the nail on the head.

    You hit the nail on the head with that comment.

  • 💊對症下藥duì zhèng xià yào

    a targeted remedy; address the root cause — A targeted remedy.

    Without reliable data, the government cannot offer a targeted remedy.
