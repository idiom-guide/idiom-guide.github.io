一筆勾銷

yī bǐ gōu xiāo

In English

  • let it go

Meaning

Writing it off; wiping the slate clean.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • write off
  • wipe the slate clean

Example

算了吧，這筆債我就一筆勾銷了。

Let it go. I'm writing off the debt.

Usage

For debts, grudges or mistakes.

Similar

  • 🧽既往不咎jì wǎng bù jiù

    wipe the slate clean — Letting bygones be bygones.

    I'll wipe the slate clean, and we can start over.

  • 🧊冰釋前嫌bīng shì qián xián

    bury the hatchet — Burying the hatchet.

    The two rivals buried the hatchet.

Opposite

  • 🧾秋後算帳qiū hòu suàn zhàng

    settle old scores; call sb to account later — Settling old scores later.

    He fears the boss will settle old scores after the project.

  • 😒耿耿於懷gěng gěng yú huái

    hold a grudge; can't let it go — Unable to let go.

    He still holds a grudge about that remark.
