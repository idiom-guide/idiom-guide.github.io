孤注一擲

gū zhù yī zhì

In English

  • a desperate play

Meaning

Staking everything on one throw.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • go for broke
  • stake everything on one throw

Example

賣房子拍電影，是一場孤注一擲的賭博。

Selling his house to fund the film was a desperate play.

Usage

Usually implies risk and desperation.

Similar

  • ⛵破釜沉舟pò fǔ chén zhōu

    (we've) burned the boats; reaching a point of no return — Burning one's boats.

    We've burned the boats; there is no going back now.

  • 🎲賭上一切dǔ shàng yī qiè

    risk everything; bet it all — Risking everything.

    He risked all he had on one last throw of the dice.

Opposite

  • 💰見好就收jiàn hǎo jiù shōu

    quit while one is ahead — Quitting while one is ahead.

    Quit while you're ahead and don't gamble the winnings.

  • ⛑穩紮穩打wěn zhā wěn dǎ

    build steadily; play it safe and steady — Steady and sure.

    A start-up should build steadily and not rush to expand.
