焦土策略

jiāo tǔ cè lüè

In English

  • scorched earth policy
  • obliterated

Meaning

A scorched-earth policy.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • a scorched-earth policy
  • burn it all down
  • total destruction

Example

撤退的軍隊採取了焦土策略。

The retreating army used a scorched-earth policy.

Usage

Originally military; now also for business, politics and lawsuits.

Similar

  • 🤕兩敗俱傷liǎng bài jù shāng

    lose-lose situation — A lose-lose situation.

    A price war would be a lose-lose situation for both stores.

  • ⛵破釜沉舟pò fǔ chén zhōu

    (we've) burned the boats; reaching a point of no return — Burning one's boats.

    We've burned the boats; there is no going back now.

Opposite

  • 🕊️化干戈為玉帛huà gān gē wéi yù bó

    bury the hatchet — Burying the hatchet.

    After years of silence, the two families finally buried the hatchet.

  • 🌱休養生息xiū yǎng shēng xī

    recuperate and rebuild — Recuperating and rebuilding.

    After the war, the country needed a decade to recuperate and rebuild.
