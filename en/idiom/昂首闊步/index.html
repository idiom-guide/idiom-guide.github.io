昂首闊步

áng shǒu kuò bù

In English

  • stride
  • walk tall

Meaning

Head raised and taking long steps: a proud, confident walk.Positive · General

Example

冠軍昂首闊步走進球場。

The champion strode into the arena with his head held high.

Usage

Can describe an actual walk or confidently moving toward the future. Mostly positive, sometimes a bit proud.

Similar

  • 🧍抬頭挺胸tái tóu tǐng xiōng

    stand tall; chin up — Head up, chest out; about posture.

    Stand tall and look the audience in the eye.

  • 🐧大搖大擺dà yáo dà bǎi

    swagger; strut — Swaggering with no regard for others; about attitude.

    He swaggered into the meeting as if he owned the place.

Opposite

  • 😞垂頭喪氣chuí tóu sàng qì

    dejected; hang one's head — Head hanging, spirits low.

    After losing the match, everyone went home dejected.

  • 🦯步履蹣跚bù lǚ pán shān

    totter; walk unsteadily — Walking unsteadily and slowly.

    The old man walked unsteadily.
