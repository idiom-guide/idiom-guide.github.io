風華絕代

fēng huá jué dài

In English

  • timeless beauty
  • unparalleled/unsurpassable beauty

Meaning

Of unparalleled beauty and grace.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • unparalleled beauty
  • unsurpassable beauty

Example

這位女演員風華絕代。

The actress was a woman of unparalleled beauty.

Usage

Formal; mostly of women, often of stars of the past.

Similar

  • ✨光彩照人guāng cǎi zhào rén

    radiant; dazzling — Radiantly beautiful.

    At the gala she looked radiant; her beauty had an incandescent quality.

  • ✨光彩奪目guāng cǎi duó mù

    stealing the spotlight; hog the limelight — Dazzling; stealing the spotlight.

    The young actress was stealing the spotlight at the premiere.

Opposite

  • 🥔非常普通fēi cháng pǔ tōng

    garden-variety — Garden-variety.

    It's just a garden-variety cold, nothing serious.

  • 🌾樸實無華pǔ shí wú huá

    unsophisticated — Plain and simple.

    The village church is unsophisticated, yet beautiful.
