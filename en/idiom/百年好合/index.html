9 · Congratulations & Good Wishes

百年好合

bǎi nián hǎo hé

In English

  • a lifetime of happiness together

Meaning

A lifetime of happiness together.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • happily ever after
  • a long and happy marriage

Example

敬新郎新娘：祝你們百年好合。

To the bride and groom: a lifetime of happiness together.

Usage

A wedding blessing, often paired with 永浴愛河 or 早生貴子.

Similar

  • 👵白頭偕老bái tóu xié lǎo

    grow old together — Growing old together.

    We promised to grow old together.

  • 💞永浴愛河yǒng yù ài hé

    everlasting love — Everlasting love.

    We wish the newlyweds everlasting love.

Opposite

  • 🐦勞燕分飛láo yàn fēn fēi

    go their separate ways — Going their separate ways.

    After graduation the couple went their separate ways.
