風光不再

fēng guāng bù zài

In English

  • sb has lost one's shine
  • have seen better days

Meaning

Having seen better days.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • past one's prime

Example

那家老牌飯店已風光不再。

The old star hotel has seen better days.

Usage

For people, companies, cities and brands.

Similar

  • 📆今非昔比jīn fēi xī bǐ

    things are different now — Things are different now.

    Things are different now; this neighborhood used to be a slum.

  • 🏞️江河日下jiāng hé rì xià

    in steady decline — In steady decline.

    The industry has been in steady decline.

Opposite

  • 💪正在勢頭zhèng zài shì tou

    in one's prime — On a roll.

    The singer is in her prime, with a new hit every season.

  • 🌳欣欣向榮xīn xīn xiàng róng

    thriving; booming — Thriving.

    The local startup scene is thriving.
