7 · Public Opinion

眾矢之的

zhòng shǐ zhī dì

In English

  • a lightning rod for criticism

Meaning

A lightning rod for criticism.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • the target of attacks
  • in the firing line

Example

這位部長成了眾矢之的。

The minister became a lightning rod for criticism.

Usage

For politics, the workplace and public figures.

Similar

  • 👉千夫所指qiān fū suǒ zhǐ

    universally condemned — Universally condemned.

    The attack was universally condemned.

  • 🐦槍打出頭鳥qiāng dǎ chū tóu niǎo

    the tall poppy gets cut down; the first to stick one's neck out pays the price — The tall poppy gets cut down.

    Nobody volunteered to criticize the boss, because the first to stick one's neck out usually pays the price.

Opposite

  • 🗳️眾望所歸zhòng wàng suǒ guī

    the people's choice; enjoy popular support — The people's choice.

    She was the people's choice for the post.
