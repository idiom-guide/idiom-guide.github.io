三振出局

sān zhèn chū jú

In English

  • strike three

Meaning

Strike three; out of chances.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • three strikes and you're out
  • out of chances

Example

他揮棒落空，三振出局，比賽結束。

He swung and missed for strike three, and the game was over.

Usage

From baseball; also for 'three strikes' rules where a third offense means dismissal.

Similar

  • 🤕一敗塗地yī bài tú dì

    fall flat on your face — Falling flat on one's face, not only in games.

    The campaign fell flat on its face.

Opposite

  • 💓起死回生qǐ sǐ huí shēng

    bring back from the dead; a miraculous recovery — Brought back from the dead.

    The new owner's plan brought the company back from the dead.

  • 🤙網開一面wǎng kāi yī miàn

    show leniency; give sb a break — Getting another chance.

    The judge showed leniency to the first-time offender.
