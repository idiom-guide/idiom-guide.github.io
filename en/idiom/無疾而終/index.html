無疾而終

wú jí ér zhōng

In English

  • fizzle out
  • come to nothing

Meaning

Fizzling out.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • peter out

Example

和平談判無疾而終。

The peace talks fizzled out.

Usage

For plans, negotiations and relationships.

Similar

  • 🥀胎死腹中tāi sǐ fù zhōng

    stillborn; die on the vine — Dying on the vine.

    The project died on the vine.

  • 🐯虎頭蛇尾hǔ tóu shé wěi

    start with a bang and end with a whimper; peter out — Starting with a bang and ending with a whimper.

    The campaign started with a bang and ended with a whimper.

Opposite

  • 💧水到渠成shuǐ dào qú chéng

    when time is ripe; fall into place — Falling into place.

    When the time is ripe, things will fall into place.
