支吾其詞

zhī wú qí cí

In English

  • hem and haw
  • stammer evasively

Meaning

Hemming and hawing.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • beat around the bush

Example

被問到錢的事，他支吾其詞。

He hemmed and hawed when asked about the money.

Usage

For dodging tough questions or a guilty conscience.

Similar

  • ↪️拐彎抹角guǎi wān mò jiǎo

    beat around the bush; obliquely — Beating around the bush.

    Stop beating around the bush and tell me what happened.

  • ❓答非所問dá fēi suǒ wèn

    give an irrelevant answer — Dodging the question.

    The politician gave an irrelevant answer.

Opposite

  • 🚪開門見山kāi mén jiàn shān

    get straight to the point — Straight to the point.

    He got straight to the point and asked about the price.

  • 📢直言不諱zhí yán bù huì

    speak frankly; not mince words — Calling a spade a spade.

    She spoke frankly and pointed out the flaws in the plan.
