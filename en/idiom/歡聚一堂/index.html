歡聚一堂

huān jù yī táng

In English

  • gather together

Meaning

Gathering together.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • come together
  • a happy gathering

Example

很高興看到這麼多朋友歡聚一堂。

It is a pleasure to see so many friends gathered together.

Usage

For parties, ceremonies and festivals.

Similar

  • 🌟濟濟一堂jǐ jǐ yī táng

    a gathering of talent — A gathering of talent.

    Tonight is truly a gathering of talent.

  • 🍽️高朋滿座gāo péng mǎn zuò

    a full house of distinguished guests — A full house of distinguished guests.

    We are delighted to have a full house of distinguished guests.

Opposite

  • 🍂天下無不散的筵席tiān xià wú bù sàn de yán xí

    all good things must come to an end — All good things must come to an end.

    All good things must come to an end, and so must this conference.
