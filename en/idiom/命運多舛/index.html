命運多舛

mìng yùn duō chuǎn

In English

  • ill-fated

Meaning

Ill-fated.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • star-crossed
  • dogged by misfortune

Example

那支命運多舛的探險隊始終沒能登頂。

The ill-fated expedition never reached the summit.

Usage

For lives, projects and journeys.

Similar

  • 👟禍不單行huò bù dān xíng

    wait for the other shoe to drop; one-two punch — Misfortunes never come singly.

    First the roof leaked, then the pipes burst. When it rains, it pours.

  • 🎭造化弄人zào huà nòng rén

    a twist of fate; ironically — A twist of fate.

    In a twist of fate, they met again thirty years later at the same station.

Opposite

  • ⛵一帆風順yī fān fēng shùn

    smooth sailing — Smooth sailing.

    I wish you smooth sailing in your new post.

  • 🎡時來運轉shí lái yùn zhuǎn

    one's luck turns; one's time has come — One's luck turning.

    After years of bad luck, his time has come.
