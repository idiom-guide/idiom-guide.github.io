人才濟濟

rén cái jǐ jǐ

In English

  • talent

Meaning

Full of talent.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • full of talent
  • a deep bench

Example

這間實驗室人才濟濟。

The lab is full of talent.

Usage

For teams, schools, companies and events.

Similar

  • 🐯臥虎藏龍wò hǔ cáng lóng

    a place or situation that is full of people with unusual talents — Full of hidden talent.

    The village looks sleepy, but it is full of people with unusual talents.

  • 🌟濟濟一堂jǐ jǐ yī táng

    a gathering of talent — A gathering of talent.

    Tonight is truly a gathering of talent.

Opposite

  • 🤏寥寥無幾liáo liáo wú jǐ

    very few; a mere handful — A mere handful.

    Very few applicants met the requirements.
