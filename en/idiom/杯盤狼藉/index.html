杯盤狼藉

bēi pán láng jí

In English

  • the aftermath of a feast
  • a table in disarray

Meaning

Cups and plates scattered about: a table in a mess after a banquet.Neutral · General

Example

宴會結束後，桌上杯盤狼藉。

After the banquet, the table was in disarray, strewn with cups and plates.

Usage

Describes the scene after a good meal. It can be teasing and is not always critical.

Similar

  • 🗑一片狼藉yī piàn láng jí

    a complete mess; in shambles — A scene of utter mess; broader, for any place.

    After the typhoon, the street was a complete mess.

  • 😌酒足飯飽jiǔ zú fàn bǎo

    sated; well fed — Full and content; about the diners, not the table.

    Once everyone was well fed, we sat in the yard and talked.

Opposite

  • ✨窗明几淨chuāng míng jī jìng

    spotless; immaculate — Bright windows and clean tables; spotless.

    The room was spotless, without a speck of dust.
