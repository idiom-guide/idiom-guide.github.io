10 · Learning & Scholarship

博覽群書

bó lǎn qún shū

In English

  • well-read

Meaning

Well-read.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • widely read
  • a bookworm

Example

他博覽群書，熟悉歷史和哲學。

He is well-read in history and philosophy.

Usage

Formal; for praising someone's learning.

Similar

  • 🗾見多識廣jiàn duō shí guǎng

    be worldly; have seen a lot — Well-traveled and well-informed.

    He is worldly and has seen a lot, so no situation frightens him.

  • 📗開卷有益kāi juàn yǒu yì

    reading is always rewarding — Reading is always rewarding.

    Reading is always rewarding, whatever the book.

Opposite

  • 🐸孤陋寡聞gū lòu guǎ wén

    ill-informed; poorly informed — Ill-informed.

    He is ill-informed about world events.

  • 🙉不學無術bù xué wú shù

    ignoramus; uninformed — Uneducated.

    The ignoramus has never opened a book.
