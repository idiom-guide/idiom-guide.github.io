鎩羽而歸

shā yǔ ér guī

In English

  • return in defeat
  • come back empty-handed

Meaning

Returning in defeat.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • go home with nothing

Example

探險隊鎩羽而歸。

The explorers returned in defeat.

Usage

For competitions, bids and expeditions.

Similar

  • 🤕一敗塗地yī bài tú dì

    fall flat on your face — Falling flat on one's face.

    The campaign fell flat on its face.

  • 🔄白忙一場bái máng yī cháng

    resultless; in vain — All for nothing.

    We worked all weekend in vain; the client canceled.

Opposite

  • 🎉凱旋歸來kǎi xuán guī lái

    return victorious — Returning victorious.

    The team returned victorious from Paris.

  • 🎒滿載而歸mǎn zài ér guī

    come away with a great deal — Coming away with a great deal.

    I hope everyone comes away with a great deal from this forum.
