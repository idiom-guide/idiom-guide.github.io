全力衝刺

quán lì chōng cì

In English

  • put the pedal to the metal

Meaning

Going flat out to the finish.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • go flat out
  • sprint to the finish

Example

最後一週，團隊全力衝刺。

For the final week, the team put the pedal to the metal.

Usage

For races, exams and wrapping up projects.

Similar

  • 🏎️加緊馬力jiā jǐn mǎ lì

    gear up — Stepping it up.

    We need to gear up for the busy season.

  • ⏳分秒必爭fēn miǎo bì zhēng

    not a moment to be lost; time-sensitive — Every second counts.

    In the emergency room, there is not a moment to be lost.

Opposite

  • 🔋養精蓄銳yǎng jīng xù ruì

    recharge one's batteries; conserve one's strength — Recharging one's batteries.

    We'll rest today to recharge our batteries for tomorrow's match.

  • 🚩偃旗息鼓yǎn qí xī gǔ

    call a halt; lie low — Calling it off.

    The band announced they would call a halt for now: a pause, they said, not a farewell.
