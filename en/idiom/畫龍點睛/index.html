畫龍點睛

huà lóng diǎn jīng

In English

  • put the finishing touches to

Meaning

The finishing touch.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • the finishing touch
  • the crowning touch

Example

一條亮色圍巾為她的穿搭畫龍點睛。

A bright scarf put the finishing touches to her outfit.

Usage

From the tale of the painter Zhang Sengyou, whose dragons flew off the wall once he painted in their eyes.
