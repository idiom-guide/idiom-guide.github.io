備受推崇

bèi shòu tuī chóng

In English

  • widely honored/acclaimed

Meaning

Widely acclaimed.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • widely honored
  • widely acclaimed
  • highly regarded

Example

教授的著作備受推崇。

The professor's book is widely acclaimed.

Usage

For books, scholars, works and products.

Similar

  • 👄有口皆碑yǒu kǒu jiē bēi

    good reputation — Praised by all.

    The clinic has a good reputation among all the locals.

  • ⭐佳評如潮jiā píng rú cháo

    rave reviews; glowing reviews — Rave reviews.

    The novel won rave reviews.

Opposite

  • 👉千夫所指qiān fū suǒ zhǐ

    universally condemned — Universally condemned.

    The attack was universally condemned.

  • 👃嗤之以鼻chī zhī yǐ bí

    pour scorn on sb; scorn — Pouring scorn on it.

    Experts poured scorn on the miracle diet.
