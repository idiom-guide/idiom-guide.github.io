虛假之言

xū jiǎ zhī yán

In English

  • adulterated

Meaning

Falsehoods.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • falsehoods
  • misinformation

Example

這份報告摻雜了誤導人的虛假之言。

The report was adulterated with misleading claims.

Usage

For reports, statements and propaganda.

Similar

  • 🗯️胡說八道hú shuō bā dào

    hogwash; nonsense — Hogwash.

    That's hogwash.

  • 🍯花言巧語huā yán qiǎo yǔ

    sweet talk; smooth talk — Sweet talk.

    Don't be taken in by his sweet talk.

Opposite

  • 💌肺腑之言fèi fǔ zhī yán

    from the bottom of my heart — From the bottom of one's heart.

    These words come from the bottom of my heart.

  • ❕千真萬確qiān zhēn wàn què

    be absolutely true — Absolutely true.

    What he says is absolutely true; I saw it with my own eyes.
