風險自負

fēng xiǎn zì fù

In English

  • at one's (own) peril

Meaning

At one's own risk.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • at one's own risk
  • you've been warned

Example

在浮球外游泳，風險自負。

Swimming beyond the buoys is at your own peril.

Usage

Common on signs and in disclaimers; calm but warning.

Similar

  • 🎢膽小勿試dǎn xiǎo wù shì

    not for the faint of heart — Not for the faint of heart.

    This roller coaster is not for the faint of heart.

  • 🌾自食其果zì shí qí guǒ

    To reap what you sow — Reaping what one has sown.

    He cheated others, and now he reaps what he sowed.

Opposite

  • 🔐萬無一失wàn wú yī shī

    foolproof; failsafe — Foolproof.

    The plan is foolproof.

  • 🏀十拿九穩shí ná jiǔ wěn

    be all but certain; be a safe bet — A sure thing.

    With this contract in hand, winning the bid is all but certain.
