腳踏實地

jiǎo tà shí dì

In English

  • staying grounded and pushing ahead

Meaning

Down-to-earth and hardworking.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • down-to-earth
  • steady and practical

Example

他是個腳踏實地的人，穩穩地持續前進。

He is a down-to-earth man who stays grounded and keeps pushing ahead.

Usage

Describes work attitude and character; usually praise.

Similar

  • 📋按部就班àn bù jiù bān

    step by step; by the book — Step by step; by the book.

    New hires are trained step by step.

  • ⛑穩紮穩打wěn zhā wěn dǎ

    build steadily; play it safe and steady — Slow and steady.

    A start-up should build steadily and not rush to expand.

Opposite

  • 🌠好高騖遠hào gāo wù yuǎn

    aim too high; overreach — Aiming too high.

    He aims too high and ignores the basics; try to be more down-to-earth.

  • 📄紙上談兵zhǐ shàng tán bīng

    armchair general — An armchair general.

    He is an armchair general who has never visited a real battlefield.
