幫忙打點

bāng máng dǎ diǎn

In English

  • oil the wheels

Meaning

Smoothing the way; oiling the wheels.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • smooth the way
  • pull some strings

Example

給櫃檯人員一點小禮物，可以幫忙打點。

A small gift for the receptionist can oil the wheels.

Usage

Can mean arranging things, and often implies gifts to grease the wheels.

Similar

  • 💸花錢消災huā qián xiāo zāi

    buy indulgences — Paying to make trouble go away.

    After the scandal, the firm tried to buy indulgences with a large donation.

  • 🧳全力準備quán lì zhǔn bèi

    square away — Getting every detail squared away.

    She is squaring away every detail for the launch.

Opposite

  • 🦟從中作梗cóng zhōng zuò gěng

    stand in the way; put obstacles in the way — Throwing a spanner in the works.

    Someone stood in the way, and that is why the deal fell through.

  • 💼公事公辦gōng shì gōng bàn

    business is business; transactional — Going by the book.

    Sorry, it's nothing personal; business is business.
