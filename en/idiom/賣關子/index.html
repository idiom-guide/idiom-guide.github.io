賣關子

mài guān zi

In English

  • leave it for suspense

Meaning

Keeping someone in suspense.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • keep sb in suspense
  • tease

Example

別賣關子了，告訴我們誰贏了。

Stop leaving it for suspense and tell us who won.

Usage

From storytellers pausing at the best part; common in speech.

Similar

  • 🔯天機不可洩漏tiān jī bù kě xiè lòu

    keep my own counsel — Keeping one's own counsel.

    Don't ask about the surprise; I'll keep my own counsel.

  • ↪️拐彎抹角guǎi wān mò jiǎo

    beat around the bush; obliquely — Beating around the bush.

    Stop beating around the bush and tell me what happened.

Opposite

  • 🚪開門見山kāi mén jiàn shān

    get straight to the point — Straight to the point.

    He got straight to the point and asked about the price.

  • ▶直截了當zhí jié liǎo dàng

    be straightforward; be direct — Straightforward.

    She said exactly what she thought without beating around the bush.
