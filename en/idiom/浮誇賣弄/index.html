浮誇賣弄

fú kuā mài nòng

In English

  • puffery

Meaning

Puffery; showing off.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • showing off
  • hype

Example

這本手冊充滿浮誇賣弄，吹噓其「世界級」服務。

The brochure was full of puffery about its "world-class" service.

Usage

For advertising, self-promotion and talk.

Similar

  • 🔉言過其實yán guò qí shí

    exaggerate; overstate — Overstating things.

    The restaurant's advertising overstates the reality.

  • 🦩誇張造作kuā zhāng zào zuò

    flamboyant (gesture, lifestyle, clothes) — Flamboyant.

    Her flamboyant gestures drew every eye in the room.

Opposite

  • 🌾樸實無華pǔ shí wú huá

    unsophisticated — Plain and unadorned.

    The village church is unsophisticated, yet beautiful.

  • 🐭不出鋒頭bù chū fēng tóu

    unassuming — Unassuming.

    He is an unassuming man who never seeks the spotlight.
