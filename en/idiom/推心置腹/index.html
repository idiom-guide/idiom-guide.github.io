11 · Friendship & Loyalty

推心置腹

tuī xīn zhì fù

In English

  • speak from the heart
  • confide in sb

Meaning

Confiding in someone.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • open up to sb

Example

多年的友誼讓她可以和他推心置腹，無話不談。

After years of friendship, she could confide in him about anything.

Usage

From the Book of the Later Han; for heart-to-heart talks between friends or partners.

Similar

  • 💗肝膽相照gān dǎn xiāng zhào

    utterly loyal friends — Utterly loyal friends.

    The two are utterly loyal friends.

  • 🔓開誠布公kāi chéng bù gōng

    in full transparency — In full transparency.

    The two leaders spoke in full transparency about their differences.

Opposite

  • 🃏爾虞我詐ěr yú wǒ zhà

    deceive and outwit one another; cutthroat scheming — Cutthroat scheming.

    The negotiation became a game of deceiving and outwitting each other.

  • 🃏勾心鬥角gōu xīn dòu jiǎo

    intrigue; scheming against each other — Office intrigue.

    All the office intrigue is wearing everyone out.
