情緒勒索

qíng xù lè suǒ

In English

  • emotional blackmail

Meaning

Emotional blackmail.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • guilt-tripping
  • manipulation

Example

威脅說你不答應就要哭，就是情緒勒索。

Threatening to cry unless you agree is emotional blackmail.

Usage

A psychology term, common for parent–child, couple and workplace relationships.

Similar

  • 🥕威脅利誘wēi xié lì yòu

    cajole and entice; be cajoled into Ving — Carrot and stick.

    He was cajoled into signing with a mix of threats and promises.

  • 😨威嚇逼迫wēi hè bī pò

    terrorize sb. into doing sth — Strong-arming.

    The gang tried to terrorize shopkeepers into paying protection money.

Opposite

  • 🤝通情達理tōng qíng dá lǐ

    reasonable; as a matter of course — Reasonable and understanding.

    He is a reasonable man who listens to both sides.

  • 🔓開誠布公kāi chéng bù gōng

    in full transparency — In full transparency.

    The two leaders spoke in full transparency about their differences.
