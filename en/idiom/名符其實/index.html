名符其實

míng fú qí shí

In English

  • genuine

Meaning

Living up to its name; the real thing.Positive · General

Example

她是名符其實的專家，不只是頭銜漂亮。

She is a genuine expert, not just someone with an impressive title.

Usage

Also written 名副其實; both are correct.

Similar

  • 🎗實至名歸shí zhì míng guī

    be well deserved — Recognition that is fully deserved.

    Winning the annual award was well deserved.

  • 💎貨真價實huò zhēn jià shí

    the genuine article — Genuine goods at a fair price; for things or ability.

    Customers want to know the product is the genuine article.

Opposite

  • 🏷️徒具虛名tú jù xū míng

    A in name only — A name without substance.

    He was king in name only; the real power rested with the council.

  • ⚰️名存實亡míng cún shí wáng

    cease to exist except in name — Exists in name only; the substance is gone.

    The treaty still exists on paper, but it has ceased to exist except in name.
