勇猛無畏

yǒng měng wú wèi

In English

  • intrepid

Meaning

Intrepid.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • fearless
  • bold

Example

勇猛無畏的探險家們越過了結冰的湖面。

The intrepid explorers crossed the frozen lake.

Usage

For explorers, soldiers and athletes.

Similar

  • 🦁天不怕地不怕tiān bù pà dì bù pà

    I eat fear for breakfast — Afraid of nothing; more colloquial.

    The toddler is fearless. She eats fear for breakfast.

  • 🚀義無反顧yì wú fǎn gù

    be resolute and not look back; go ahead without hesitation — Without hesitation.

    For her children, she resolutely quit her job without looking back.

Opposite

  • 🥶臨陣退縮lín zhèn tuì suō

    get cold feet — Getting cold feet.

    She got cold feet at the altar.

  • 🐍杯弓蛇影bēi gōng shé yǐng

    Afraid of one's own shadow; extremely suspicious — Afraid of one's own shadow.

    After the burglary, she was afraid of her own shadow.
