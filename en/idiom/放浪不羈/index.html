放浪不羈

fàng làng bù jī

In English

  • unrestrained
  • free-spirited

Meaning

Free-spirited.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • bohemian

Example

他過著放浪不羈、四處漂泊的生活。

He lives a free-spirited life on the road.

Usage

Can be praise or criticism: carefree, or dissolute.

Similar

  • 🕊️毫無羈絆háo wú jī bàn

    weightlessness; unencumbered — Free of all ties.

    She felt unencumbered after quitting her job.

  • 🤠豪邁瀟灑háo mài xiāo sǎ

    generous and dashing, carefree — Dashing and carefree.

    He was generous, dashing and carefree.

Opposite

  • 📏循規蹈矩xún guī dǎo jǔ

    by the book; play by the rules — By the book.

    He plays by the rules and never takes risks.

  • 🧐衛道人士wèi dào rén shì

    moralist — Moralists.

    Moralists demanded that the film be banned.
