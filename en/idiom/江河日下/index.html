江河日下

jiāng hé rì xià

In English

  • in steady decline

Meaning

In steady decline.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • going downhill
  • on the wane

Example

這個產業江河日下。

The industry has been in steady decline.

Usage

For industries, national fortunes and social mores.

Similar

  • ↘️每況愈下měi kuàng yù xià

    go from bad to worse — Going from bad to worse.

    The company's finances went from bad to worse.

  • 🥀世風日下shì fēng rì xià

    moral decline — Moral decline.

    Every generation complains of moral decline.

Opposite

  • ☀️蒸蒸日上zhēng zhēng rì shàng

    go from strength to strength — Going from strength to strength.

    Under her care, the family bakery went from strength to strength.

  • 🌳欣欣向榮xīn xīn xiàng róng

    thriving; booming — Thriving.

    The local startup scene is thriving.
