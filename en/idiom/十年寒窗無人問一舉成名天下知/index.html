十年寒窗無人問，一舉成名天下知

shí nián hán chuāng wú rén wèn, yī jǔ chéng míng tiān xià zhī

In English

  • a decade of obscure toil, then overnight fame

Meaning

Years of obscure toil, then overnight fame.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • an overnight success years in the making
  • hard work finally pays off

Example

十年寒窗無人問，一舉成名天下知，這位小說家的故事讀來像童話。

From a decade of obscure toil to overnight fame, the novelist's story reads like a fairy tale.

Usage

Originally of the imperial examinations; now of long hard work that suddenly pays off.

Similar

  • 🍵先苦後甘xiān kǔ hòu gān

    take short term pain for long term gain — Short-term pain for long-term gain.

    Training is hard, but it is short-term pain for long-term gain.

  • 🚀一飛沖天yī fēi chōng tiān

    gravity-defying — Soaring to the top.

    The startup's gravity-defying rise stunned investors.

Opposite

  • 🌠一蹴可幾yī cù kě jǐ

    achieved overnight; an instant success — Success in one step, with no long struggle.

    Fluency is not achieved overnight; nobody succeeds at the first try.

  • ⌛生不逢時shēng bù féng shí

    born at the wrong time — Born at the wrong time.

    The inventor was born at the wrong time; the world wasn't ready for his ideas.
