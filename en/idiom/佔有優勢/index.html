佔有優勢

zhàn yǒu yōu shì

In English

  • has the upper hand

Meaning

Having the upper hand.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • have the upper hand
  • have the edge
  • be in a strong position

Example

陣容較強，讓我們的球隊佔有優勢。

With a stronger lineup, our team has the upper hand.

Usage

Neutral; for sport, negotiation and markets.

Similar

  • 🔝位居上風wèi jū shàng fēng

    have the upper hand — Having the upper hand.

    Our team has the upper hand in the second half.

  • ⏱️占得先機zhàn dé xiān jī

    stay ahead of the curve — Getting ahead of the curve.

    Investing in AI early helped them stay ahead of the curve.

Opposite

  • 🥈屈居之下qū jū zhī xià

    play second fiddle to sb — Playing second fiddle.

    For years, she played second fiddle to her more famous brother.

  • 🐘實力懸殊shí lì xuán shū

    A overmatch B in sth; be overmatched by — A mismatch.

    The local team was overmatched by the national champions.
