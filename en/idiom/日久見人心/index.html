日久見人心

rì jiǔ jiàn rén xīn

In English

  • time will tell

Meaning

Time reveals a person's true heart.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • it takes time to see people's true colors

Example

耐心點，日久見人心，誰是真朋友自然會知道。

Be patient; time will tell who your real friends are.

Usage

Often paired with 路遙知馬力; used to comfort or advise patience in judging someone.

Similar

  • 🐪路遙知馬力lù yáo zhī mǎ lì

    a long road tests a horse's strength; time proves a person's worth — A long road tests a horse's strength; often paired with this saying.

    A long road tests a horse's strength; he has stood by us for ten years.

  • 🐚水落石出shuǐ luò shí chū

    the truth comes to light — The truth comes out; stresses revelation.

    The truth of the case finally came to light.

Opposite

  • 👚以貌取人yǐ mào qǔ rén

    judge by appearances — Judging people by appearance.

    Don't judge by appearances; he is actually very professional.

  • 🕶️先入為主xiān rù wéi zhǔ

    preconceived notions; a bias — Prejudging without giving it time.

    Don't let preconceived notions color your judgment of the candidate.
