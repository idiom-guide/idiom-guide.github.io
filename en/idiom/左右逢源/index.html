左右逢源

zuǒ yòu féng yuán

In English

  • reap rewards
  • benefit from both sides

Meaning

Doing well with everyone; benefiting from both sides.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • play both sides

Example

精明的仲介能從買賣雙方同時獲利，左右逢源。

A savvy broker can reap rewards by benefiting from both sides.

Usage

Originally of deep learning; now of smooth operators who gain from both sides, sometimes slightly negative.

Similar

  • 💎八面玲瓏bā miàn líng lóng

    be all things to all people — Being all things to all people.

    A good diplomat has to be all things to all people.

  • 🌿牆頭草qiáng tóu cǎo

    fence-sitter; turncoat — A fence-sitter; clearly negative.

    He's a fence-sitter who changes sides with the wind.

Opposite

  • ↔️左右為難zuǒ yòu wéi nán

    caught between a rock and a hard place — Torn between two sides.

    I'm caught between a rock and a hard place.

  • ↔️進退維谷jìn tuì wéi gǔ

    be in a quandary; caught in a bind — Stuck with no good way forward or back.

    She is in a quandary over the job offer.
