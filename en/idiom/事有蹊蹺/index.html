事有蹊蹺

shì yǒu qī qiāo

In English

  • something's wrong

Meaning

Something's fishy.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • something's fishy
  • something's off
  • there's more to this than meets the eye
  • smell a rat

Example

門沒鎖、燈全暗，事有蹊蹺。

The door was unlocked and the lights were off. Something's wrong here.

Usage

蹊蹺 (qī qiāo) means odd and suspicious; usually leads to an investigation or doubt.

Similar

  • ❓啟人疑竇qǐ rén yí dòu

    raise suspicion; sth. be doubtful — Raising suspicion.

    The sudden change in his story raised suspicion.

  • 🔅疑點重重yí diǎn chóng chóng

    be full of doubts; be riddled with questions — Full of suspicious points.

    The case is full of doubts and must be re-investigated.

Opposite

  • 🧵天衣無縫tiān yī wú fèng

    flawless; seamless — Seamless, with no flaw to find.

    The magician's trick was flawless.

  • 🆗合情合理hé qíng hé lǐ

    be fair and reasonable; make perfect sense — Reasonable and sensible.

    The company's compensation plan was fair and reasonable, and everyone could accept it.
