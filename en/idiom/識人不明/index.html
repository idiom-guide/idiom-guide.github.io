識人不明

shí rén bù míng

In English

  • trust the wrong guy

Meaning

A poor judge of character.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • a poor judge of character
  • misplace one's trust

Example

她識人不明，把積蓄託付給錯的人。

She trusted the wrong guy with her savings.

Usage

For hiring, friendships and investments.

Opposite

  • 🔑知人善任zhī rén shàn rèn

    put the right people in the right jobs — Putting the right people in the right jobs.

    A good manager puts the right people in the right jobs.

  • 👁️獨具慧眼dú jù huì yǎn

    have unique insight into sth — A unique eye.

    The editor had unique insight into what readers wanted.
