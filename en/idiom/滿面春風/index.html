滿面春風

mǎn miàn chūn fēng

In English

  • beaming
  • glowing with happiness

Meaning

A face full of joy, as soft and warm as a spring breeze.Positive · General

Example

他聽到好消息，滿面春風地走了進來。

He walked in beaming after hearing the good news.

Usage

For a joyful look after good news or success. Leans toward the glow of good fortune.

Similar

  • 😁笑容可掬xiào róng kě jū

    beaming; all smiles — A warm, friendly smile; about how one treats people.

    The shop owner greeted every customer, all smiles.

  • ✨容光煥發róng guāng huàn fā

    glowing; radiant — Glowing health; about the radiance of the face.

    After the holiday, she came back looking radiant.

Opposite

  • 😟愁眉苦臉chóu méi kǔ liǎn

    look so woebegone; with a woebegone expression — A gloomy, troubled face.

    He looked so woebegone that the waiter asked if he was ill.

  • 🥱無精打采wú jīng dǎ cǎi

    listless; lethargic — Listless and lacking energy.

    He looked listless after the long trip.
