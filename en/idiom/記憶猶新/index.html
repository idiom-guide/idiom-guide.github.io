記憶猶新

jì yì yóu xīn

In English

  • fresh in one's memory

Meaning

Still fresh in one's memory.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • be fresh in one's memory
  • remain vivid in one's mind
  • still remember clearly

Example

那場水災，至今仍讓我們記憶猶新。

The flood is still fresh in our memory.

Usage

For recent or striking events; often ……至今仍記憶猶新.

Similar

  • 📽歷歷在目lì lì zài mù

    be vivid before one's eyes; be clearly visible in one's mind — Vivid before one's eyes.

    The scene from that day is still vivid before my eyes.

  • 📌謹記在心jǐn jì zài xīn

    be taken to heart — Bearing in mind; deliberately remembered.

    Her advice will be taken to heart.

Opposite

  • 🌥過眼雲煙guò yǎn yún yān

    a passing cloud; be fleeting and insubstantial — Fleeting, not worth dwelling on.

    Wealth and glory are no more than passing clouds.

  • 🥏拋諸腦後pāo zhū nǎo hòu

    put sth out of one's mind; forget about sth completely — Putting something out of one's mind.

    He put the teacher's advice completely out of his mind.
