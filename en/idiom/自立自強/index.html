自立自強

zì lì zì qiáng

In English

  • self-reliance
  • self-reliant

Meaning

Standing on one's own feet and striving to improve, without relying on others.Positive · General

Example

他從小家境清寒，靠打工與獎學金自立自強，順利完成學業。

Coming from a modest family, he showed great self-reliance, paying his own way through university with part-time jobs and scholarships.

Usage

Mostly used of a person's attitude to life, but also of families and groups. It joins two ideas, independence and self-improvement; for financial independence alone, use the idiom for earning one's own living, and for freedom from others' control, the one for independence and autonomy.

Similar

  • 💪自食其力zì shí qí lì

    stand on one's own legs — Earning one's own living; about financial independence.

    At sixteen, she decided to stand on her own legs.

  • ✂️獨立自主dú lì zì zhǔ

    cut the (umbilical) cord; become independent — Neither dependent on nor controlled by others; about making one's own decisions.

    At twenty, he cut the cord and moved out on his own.

  • 🚲自力更生zì lì gēng shēng

    rely on one's own efforts; stand on one's own feet — Rebuilding or sustaining life through one's own efforts, often after hardship.

    After the disaster, the villagers relied on their own efforts to rebuild their livelihoods step by step.

Opposite

  • 🍹坐享其成zuò xiǎng qí chéng

    reap the fruits of others' work; free-ride — Doing none of the work yet enjoying what others achieved.

    He never pulls his weight on group projects, yet expects to reap the fruits of others' work.

  • 🧎仰人鼻息yǎng rén bí xī

    be at others' mercy; depend on others' goodwill — Relying on others and watching their moods; about being at their mercy.

    The small firm has long been at the mercy of its biggest client and is in jeopardy once that client pulls its orders.
