強心針

qiáng xīn zhēn

In English

  • a shot in the arm

Meaning

A shot in the arm.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • a boost
  • a lifeline

Example

這筆補助對小劇場來說是一劑強心針。

The grant was a shot in the arm for the small theater.

Usage

Often 打一劑強心針 (give a shot in the arm).

Similar

  • 💉給予增長jǐ yǔ zēng zhǎng

    give a shot in the arm — Giving something a boost.

    The tax cut gave the economy a shot in the arm.

  • 🌬️注入活力zhù rù huó lì

    breathe life into — Breathing new life into.

    The young designers breathed new life into the old brand.

Opposite

  • ❄️雪上加霜xuě shàng jiā shuāng

    to make matters worse; add to one's woes — One misfortune on top of another.

    To make matters worse, the power went out.

  • 🚿潑冷水pō lěng shuǐ

    throw a wet blanket on; pour cold water on — Pouring cold water on.

    Don't pour cold water on his dream.
