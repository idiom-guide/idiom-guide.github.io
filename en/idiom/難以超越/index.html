難以超越

nán yǐ chāo yuè

In English

  • unsurpassable

Meaning

Unsurpassable.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • hard to beat
  • the gold standard

Example

她的紀錄難以超越。

Her record is unsurpassable.

Usage

For records, achievements and works.

Similar

  • 🥇無人能及wú rén néng jí

    second to none; unrivaled — Second to none.

    Her skill at chess is second to none.

  • 🏔️登峰造極dēng fēng zào jí

    reach the pinnacle — Reaching the pinnacle.

    Her career reached its pinnacle with that role.

Opposite

  • 🏃後來居上hòu lái jū shàng

    surpass — Latecomers overtaking the leaders.

    The newcomer surpassed the veterans within a year.

  • 💙青出於藍qīng chū yú lán

    the student surpasses the master — The student surpassing the master.

    The student has surpassed the master.
