當務之急

dāng wù zhī jí

In English

  • top priority
  • priority one

Meaning

The top priority.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • the most pressing task

Example

修好漏水是我們的當務之急。

Fixing the leak is our top priority.

Usage

Often 當務之急是……; about priority, not necessarily crisis.

Similar

  • 🧯燃眉之急rán méi zhī jí

    in extremis — As urgent as a fire singeing one's eyebrows; about crisis.

    The hospital needed supplies in extremis, and the delivery arrived just in time.

  • 🚨迫切需要pò qiè xū yào

    in pressing needs of; a pressing imperative for — Urgently needing something.

    The flood victims are in pressing need of clean water.

Opposite

  • 🤏無關緊要wú guān jǐn yào

    immaterial — Of no importance.

    Whether we meet at six or seven is immaterial.

  • 🔹枝微末節zhī wēi mò jié

    minor details; trivial matters — Trivial details.

    Don't spend too much time on trivial details.
