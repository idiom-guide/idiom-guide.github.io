觸目驚心

chù mù jīng xīn

In English

  • shocking

Meaning

Shocking to see.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • harrowing
  • a shocking sight

Example

淹水小鎮的照片觸目驚心。

The photos of the flooded town were shocking.

Usage

For disasters, casualties and figures; also written 怵目驚心.

Similar

  • 🙈慘不忍睹cǎn bù rěn dǔ

    a sorry sight; too horrible to look at — Too horrible to look at.

    The accident scene was a sorry sight.

  • 😡令人髮指lìng rén fà zhǐ

    heinous — Outrageous.

    The heinous crime shocked the nation.

Opposite

  • 🤩歎為觀止tàn wéi guān zhǐ

    breathtaking; awe-inspiring — Breathtaking.

    The view from the summit was breathtaking.

  • 🥱司空見慣sī kōng jiàn guàn

    a common sight; commonplace — So common no one is surprised.

    Delays are commonplace on this line.
