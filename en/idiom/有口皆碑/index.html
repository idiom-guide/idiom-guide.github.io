有口皆碑

yǒu kǒu jiē bēi

In English

  • good reputation

Meaning

Praised by all.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • praised by all
  • universally praised

Example

這家診所在當地人之間有口皆碑。

The clinic has a good reputation among all the locals.

Usage

For shops, doctors, teachers and services.

Similar

  • ⭐佳評如潮jiā píng rú cháo

    rave reviews; glowing reviews — Rave reviews.

    The novel won rave reviews.

  • 👍為人稱道wéi rén chēng dào

    widely praised/complimented; commendable — Widely praised.

    Her kindness to strangers was widely praised.

Opposite

  • 👉千夫所指qiān fū suǒ zhǐ

    universally condemned — Universally condemned.

    The attack was universally condemned.
