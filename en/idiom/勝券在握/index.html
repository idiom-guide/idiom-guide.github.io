勝券在握

shèng quàn zài wò

In English

  • book a win
  • eminently winnable

Meaning

A win in the bag.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • in the bag

Example

對手的王牌球員受傷，決賽看來勝券在握。

With the opponent's star player injured, the final looks eminently winnable.

Usage

For games, elections and deals that look certain.

Similar

  • 🏆穩操勝券wěn cāo shèng quàn

    X is B's to lose — Victory is theirs to lose.

    The title is Spain's to lose.

  • 🔝位居上風wèi jū shàng fēng

    have the upper hand — Having the upper hand.

    Our team has the upper hand in the second half.

Opposite

  • 🎱前途未卜qián tú wèi bǔ

    it's anyone's guess how — Anyone's guess.

    It's anyone's guess how the economy will look next year.

  • 📉失去優勢shī qù yōu shì

    lose some ground; lose ground to — Losing ground.

    Our company is losing ground to cheaper rivals.
