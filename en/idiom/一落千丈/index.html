一落千丈

yī luò qiān zhàng

In English

  • plummet
  • nosedive

Meaning

Plummeting.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • fall off a cliff

Example

產品召回後，銷售一落千丈。

Sales plummeted after the recall.

Usage

For sales, grades and reputations.

Similar

  • 🏞️江河日下jiāng hé rì xià

    in steady decline — In steady decline.

    The industry has been in steady decline.

  • 🤕一蹶不振yī jué bù zhèn

    never recover; be unable to get back on one's feet — Never recovering.

    He never recovered after the scandal.

Opposite

  • ☀️蒸蒸日上zhēng zhēng rì shàng

    go from strength to strength — Going from strength to strength.

    Under her care, the family bakery went from strength to strength.

  • ⛰️東山再起dōng shān zài qǐ

    make a comeback — Making a comeback.

    After a long absence, the singer made a comeback.
