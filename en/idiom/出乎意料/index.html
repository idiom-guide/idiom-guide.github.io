出乎意料

chū hū yì liào

In English

  • take sb by surprise

Meaning

Unexpectedly; contrary to expectations.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • unexpectedly
  • come as a surprise
  • beyond expectation

Example

突然的宣布出乎意料，大家都愣住了。

The sudden announcement took everyone by surprise.

Usage

Positive or negative depending on the outcome; at the start of a sentence or as a predicate.

Similar

  • 🎁出人意表chū rén yì biǎo

    you will never know — Surprising and ingenious.

    With his surprising ideas, you never know what he will do next.

  • 😯始料未及shǐ liào wèi jí

    never have anticipated; be caught off guard — Not foreseen at all.

    The scale of the epidemic was something experts never anticipated.

Opposite

  • 🉑意料之中yì liào zhī zhōng

    come as no surprise; be as expected — As expected.

    The team's loss came as no surprise.

  • 📓意料中事yì liào zhōng shì

    be in the playbook — Just as expected.

    Blaming the previous manager is straight out of the playbook, so nobody was surprised.
