下不為例

xià bù wéi lì

In English

  • make sure this is the last

Meaning

Just this once; never again.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • let this be the last time
  • never again

Example

這次就算了，下不為例。

I'll let it go this time, but make sure this is the last.

Usage

Lenient but warning; usually from a superior.

Similar

  • ⚖️從輕發落cóng qīng fā luò

    be treated leniently — Being let off lightly; about punishment.

    The first-time offender was treated leniently.

  • 🤙網開一面wǎng kāi yī miàn

    show leniency; give sb a break — Showing leniency; giving someone a way out.

    The judge showed leniency to the first-time offender.

Opposite

  • ➕有一就有二yǒu yī jiù yǒu èr

    one leads to another — Once it happens, it will happen again; what 下不為例 tries to prevent.

    If we allow one exception, one leads to another.

  • 🐐屢教不改lǚ jiào bù gǎi

    be incorrigible; never learn — Never learning from correction.

    He is incorrigible, and the teacher is at a loss.
