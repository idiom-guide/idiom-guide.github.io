精心打造

jīng xīn dǎ zào

In English

  • well orchestrated

Meaning

Carefully crafted.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • carefully crafted
  • meticulously designed

Example

整場活動都經過精心打造。

The whole event was well orchestrated.

Usage

For events, products, spaces and campaigns.

Similar

  • 🔨匠心獨具jiàng xīn dú jù

    show true craftsmanship — True craftsmanship.

    Every detail of the garden shows true craftsmanship.

  • 📏一絲不苟yī sī bù gǒu

    meticulous; scrupulous — Meticulous.

    He is meticulous about every detail.

Opposite

  • 😪漫不經心màn bù jīng xīn

    be offhand; do sth carelessly — Offhand.

    He answered offhandedly, as if he couldn't care less.
