各司其職

gè sī qí zhí

In English

  • each does their own job

Meaning

Each doing their own job.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • everyone plays their part
  • stay in one's lane

Example

專案進行順利，因為大家各司其職。

The project ran smoothly because each of us did our own job.

Usage

For how organizations and teams run.

Similar

  • 🐜分工合作fēn gōng hé zuò

    divide the work and cooperate — Dividing the work.

    We divided the work and finished ahead of schedule.

  • 🙅不在其位，不謀其政bù zài qí wèi, bù móu qí zhèng

    mind your own business — Minding one's own business.

    That's not your department. If you're not in the position, mind your own business.

Opposite

  • 🪑人浮於事rén fú yú shì

    overstaffed — Overstaffed.

    The agency is overstaffed and slow.
