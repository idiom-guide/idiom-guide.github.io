想入非非

xiǎng rù fēi fēi

In English

  • indulge in fantasy

Meaning

Indulging in fantasy.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • daydream
  • let one's imagination run wild

Example

別想入非非了，回去工作吧。

Don't indulge in fantasy; get back to work.

Usage

Colloquial; sometimes hints at romantic daydreams.

Similar

  • 🪁異想天開yì xiǎng tiān kāi

    quixotic, magical thinking; vagaries — Magical thinking.

    His plan to sell ice in the Arctic was pure magical thinking.

  • 😴癡人說夢chī rén shuō mèng

    wildly unrealistic — Wildly unrealistic.

    His plan to cross the ocean on a raft is wildly unrealistic.

Opposite

  • 👣腳踏實地jiǎo tà shí dì

    staying grounded and pushing ahead — Down-to-earth.

    He is a down-to-earth man who stays grounded and keeps pushing ahead.
