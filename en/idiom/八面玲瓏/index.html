八面玲瓏

bā miàn líng lóng

In English

  • be all things to all people

Meaning

All things to all people.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • smooth operator
  • socially adept

Example

好的外交官必須八面玲瓏。

A good diplomat has to be all things to all people.

Usage

Can be praise or criticism; negatively, it means slick and worldly.

Similar

  • 🏄左右逢源zuǒ yòu féng yuán

    reap rewards; benefit from both sides — Benefiting from both sides.

    A savvy broker can reap rewards by benefiting from both sides.

  • 🥂交友廣闊jiāo yǒu guǎng kuò

    has connections; a wide range of contacts — Well connected.

    As a journalist, she has a wide range of contacts.

Opposite

  • 🎏不識時務bù shí shí wù

    (not) know/see which way the wind blows — Out of touch with the times.

    He didn't know which way the wind blew and argued with the boss.

  • ⚡心直口快xīn zhí kǒu kuài

    straight shooter — A straight shooter.

    Her quick tongue sometimes gets her into trouble, but at least she is a straight shooter.
