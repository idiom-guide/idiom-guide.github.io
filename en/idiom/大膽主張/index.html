大膽主張

dà dǎn zhǔ zhāng

In English

  • bold claim
  • long shot
  • audacious

Meaning

A bold proposal few would dare to make.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • a bold claim
  • a bold proposal
  • an audacious idea

Example

這位科學家提出了一個前人不敢提出的大膽主張。

The scientist made a bold claim that no one had dared to make before.

Usage

Neutral to positive; if seen as unrealistic, it implies risk.

Similar

  • 🪁異想天開yì xiǎng tiān kāi

    quixotic, magical thinking; vagaries — Fanciful and unrealistic.

    His plan to sell ice in the Arctic was pure magical thinking.

  • 🌳獨樹一幟dú shù yī zhì

    unique in — Striking out on one's own; stresses being different.

    The restaurant is unique in its blend of Taiwanese and French cooking.

Opposite

  • 🦜人云亦云rén yún yì yún

    echo other's words — Echoing what others say.

    Don't just echo other people's words; form your own opinion.

  • 🚋墨守成規mò shǒu chéng guī

    stick to old rules; stay in a rut — Sticking rigidly to old ways.

    The company sticks to old rules and finds it hard to innovate.
