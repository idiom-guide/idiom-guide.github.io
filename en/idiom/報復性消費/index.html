報復性消費

bào fù xìng xiāo fèi

In English

  • revenge spending
  • pent-up spending

Meaning

Revenge spending: a burst of buying after a long period of restraint, as if getting back at the restrictions.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • splurge

Example

封城結束後，報復性消費大幅湧現。

Revenge spending surged after the lockdown ended.

Usage

A post-pandemic coinage, often about travel or shopping.

Similar

  • 🏪門庭若市mén tíng ruò shì

    bustling with customers — Shops packed with customers.

    The new bakery is bustling with customers.

  • 🛍️應有盡有yīng yǒu jìn yǒu

    have everything you could want — Having everything one could want; about the range on offer.

    The market has everything you could want.

Opposite

  • 🧮量入為出liàng rù wéi chū

    live within one's means — Living within one's means.

    A startup has to live within its means.

  • 🧮精打細算jīng dǎ xì suàn

    astute with money; budget carefully — Counting every penny.

    She is astute with money and plans every purchase.
