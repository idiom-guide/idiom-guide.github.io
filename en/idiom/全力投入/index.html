全力投入

quán lì tóu rù

In English

  • fully on board

Meaning

All in; fully committed.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • be fully on board
  • be all in
  • throw oneself into

Example

整個團隊都全力投入這項計畫。

The whole team is fully on board with the plan.

Usage

Often 全力投入工作 or 比賽 (throw oneself into work or a match).

Similar

  • 🏃全力以赴quán lì yǐ fù

    go all out — Going all out.

    The whole team went all out to finish before the deadline.

  • 💻廢寢忘食fèi qǐn wàng shí

    work day and night; forget to eat and sleep — Forgetting to eat and sleep.

    He worked day and night on the proposal, barely stopping to eat or sleep.

Opposite

  • 💭心不在焉xīn bù zài yān

    distracted absent minded — Absent-minded.

    He was distracted and absent-minded during the meeting.

  • 🛑半途而廢bàn tú ér fèi

    give up halfway — Giving up halfway.

    Don't give up halfway.
