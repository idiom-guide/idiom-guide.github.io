4 · Pain & Unease

無地自容

wú dì zì róng

In English

  • squirmed

Meaning

Mortified.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • want the ground to swallow one up
  • mortified

Example

她的錯誤被當眾宣布，讓她無地自容。

She squirmed in embarrassment when her mistake was announced.

Usage

For public embarrassment or being caught out.

Similar

  • 🥚丟盡顏面diū jìn yán miàn

    have egg on sb's face — Egg on one's face.

    After the failed demo, the CEO had egg on his face.

  • 😳丟人現眼diū rén xiàn yǎn

    embarrassing; embarrass oneself — Making a fool of oneself.

    Don't embarrass yourself by singing off-key at the party.

Opposite

  • 🦒理直氣壯lǐ zhí qì zhuàng

    be fully justified and assertive; with righteous confidence — Standing one's ground.

    He demanded an apology with righteous confidence.

  • 😌問心無愧wèn xīn wú kuì

    true to oneself; look oneself in the mirror — With a clear conscience.

    I did my best, so I can look myself in the mirror with a clear conscience.
