志同道合

zhì tóng dào hé

In English

  • like minded

Meaning

Like-minded.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • like-minded
  • share the same goals
  • on the same page

Example

她在棋社找到了志同道合的朋友。

She found like-minded friends in the chess club.

Usage

Often modifies friends or partners.

Similar

  • 🤝同道中人tóng dào zhōng rén

    sisters/brothers in arms; welcome to the club — Kindred spirits.

    You like old maps too? Welcome to the club!

  • 🧠英雄所見略同yīng xióng suǒ jiàn lüè tóng

    great minds think alike — Great minds think alike.

    We both picked the same title for the book. Great minds think alike!

Opposite

  • 🔀道不同不相為謀dào bù tóng bù xiāng wéi móu

    have nothing in common; go separate ways — Parting ways over different values.

    We want different things, so we should go our separate ways.

  • 🐟格格不入gé gé bù rù

    be out of place; be like a fish out of water — Out of place.

    He felt like a fish out of water among his new colleagues.
