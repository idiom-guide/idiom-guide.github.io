毫不尊重

háo bù zūn zhòng

In English

  • show no respect for

Meaning

Showing no respect.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • disrespect
  • flout

Example

他對規則毫不尊重。

He showed no respect for the rules.

Usage

For rules, elders and others' rights.

Similar

  • 🙄目中無人mù zhōng wú rén

    arrogant; look down on everyone — Looking down on everyone.

    The star was arrogant and looked down on everyone.

  • 😛沒大沒小méi dà méi xiǎo

    being rude, impolite — Disrespectful to elders.

    Don't talk to your grandmother like that; it's rude.

Opposite

  • 🍎尊師重道zūn shī zhòng dào

    respect teachers and their teaching — Respecting teachers and learning.

    The festival celebrates respect for teachers and their teaching.

  • 🎩以禮相待yǐ lǐ xiāng dài

    in the name of decorum — Treating others with courtesy.

    In the name of decorum, the hosts treated every guest with courtesy.
