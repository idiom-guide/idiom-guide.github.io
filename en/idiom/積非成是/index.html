積非成是

jī fēi chéng shì

In English

  • two wrongs make a right (can't)

Meaning

A wrong repeated long enough comes to be accepted as right.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • a lie repeated often enough becomes truth
  • wrong practice becomes the norm

Example

若一直放著這個錯誤不管，終將積非成是。

If we keep ignoring the error, a mistake repeated often enough may come to be accepted as truth.

Usage

A warning not to tolerate small errors.

Similar

  • 🗣️以訛傳訛yǐ é chuán é

    spread misinformation — A rumor that gets more distorted as it spreads; stresses spreading.

    People shared the post without checking and spread misinformation.

  • 🦓指鹿為馬zhǐ lù wéi mǎ

    call a stag a horse; deliberately twist the truth — Deliberately calling black white.

    He called a deer a horse in the meeting, passing off the wrong as right.

Opposite

  • 🧭撥亂反正bō luàn fǎn zhèng

    set things right; restore order — Setting things right again.

    The interim government's first task was to restore order and set things right.

  • 🌰正本清源zhèng běn qīng yuán

    get to the root and set things right — Fixing a problem at its root.

    To solve the problem, we must tackle it at the root.
