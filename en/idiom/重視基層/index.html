重視基層

zhòng shì jī céng

In English

  • listen to the ground

Meaning

Listening to the front line.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • listen to the front line
  • bottom-up

Example

好的主管在制定政策前，會先重視基層的聲音。

A good manager listens to the ground before making policy.

Usage

For management and governance.

Similar

  • 🧠集思廣益jí sī guǎng yì

    draw on collective wisdom — Pooling everyone's ideas.

    The company draws on collective wisdom by asking every employee for ideas.

  • 🦻兼聽則明jiān tīng zé míng

    hear both sides to see clearly — Hearing all sides gives a clearer view.

    Before deciding, hear both sides; don't rely on a single account.

Opposite

  • 🚘閉門造車bì mén zào chē

    work in isolation from reality — Working behind closed doors.

    You can't build products in isolation; listen to your users.

  • 🐺一意孤行yī yì gū xíng

    go it alone; act arbitrarily — Going it alone, ignoring advice.

    He went it alone, ignoring the advice of every adviser.
