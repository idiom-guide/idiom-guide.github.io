話不投機半句多

huà bù tóu jī bàn jù duō

In English

  • We don't speak the same language

Meaning

When you don't get along, even half a sentence is too much.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • we don't speak the same language
  • have nothing to say to each other

Example

爭了十分鐘後，我明白我們話不投機半句多，便不再開口。

After ten minutes of arguing, I realized we don't speak the same language, so I stopped talking.

Usage

Full couplet: 酒逢知己千杯少，話不投機半句多.

Similar

  • 🔀道不同不相為謀dào bù tóng bù xiāng wéi móu

    have nothing in common; go separate ways — People with different goals can't work together.

    We want different things, so we should go our separate ways.

  • 🐟格格不入gé gé bù rù

    be out of place; be like a fish out of water — Out of place; not fitting in.

    He felt like a fish out of water among his new colleagues.

Opposite

  • 💬相談甚歡xiāng tán shèn huān

    had a nice talk — Having a great conversation.

    We had a nice talk over coffee.

  • 🥃酒逢知己千杯少jiǔ féng zhī jǐ qiān bēi shǎo

    good company makes the drinks flow — With a kindred spirit, a thousand cups aren't enough.

    With a kindred spirit, no amount of drinks is too much; we talked until dawn.
