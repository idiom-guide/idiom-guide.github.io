關鍵畫面

guān jiàn huà miàn

In English

  • the defining shot

Meaning

The key shot or image in a film or event.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • the key image
  • the money shot

Example

這部電影的關鍵畫面是最後的夕陽。

The defining shot of the film is the final sunset.

Usage

For film, news and sports; also for the crucial frame of security footage.

Similar

  • 🐉畫龍點睛huà lóng diǎn jīng

    put the finishing touches to — The finishing touch that brings it to life.

    A bright scarf put the finishing touches to her outfit.

  • 🤩歎為觀止tàn wéi guān zhǐ

    breathtaking; awe-inspiring — Breathtaking.

    The view from the summit was breathtaking.

Opposite

  • 🥱平淡無奇píng dàn wú qí

    run-of-the-mill — Plain and unremarkable.

    It was just a run-of-the-mill day at the office.

  • 🔹枝微末節zhī wēi mò jié

    minor details; trivial matters — Trivial details.

    Don't spend too much time on trivial details.
