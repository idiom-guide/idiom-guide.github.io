共體時艱

gòng tǐ shí jiān

In English

  • share the burden together

Meaning

Sharing the burden in hard times.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • tighten our belts together
  • weather the storm together

Example

在這次危機中，讓我們共體時艱。

In this crisis, let's share the burden together.

Usage

Common when governments or companies call for pay cuts or austerity.

Similar

  • ⛵同舟共濟tóng zhōu gòng jì

    solidarity; help each other — In the same boat, helping each other through.

    In times of crisis, neighbors help each other.

  • ⛈️患難與共huàn nàn yǔ gòng

    stick together through thick and thin — Sticking together through thick and thin.

    They stuck together through thick and thin.

Opposite

  • 🍿作壁上觀zuò bì shàng guān

    watch from the sidelines; stand idly by — Watching from the sidelines.

    While the two firms fought a price war, the rest of the industry could only watch from the sidelines.

  • 📴事不關己shì bù guān jǐ

    none of one's business; not one's concern — Not one's business.

    It was none of his business, so he stood by and did nothing.
