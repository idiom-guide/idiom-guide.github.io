口無遮攔

kǒu wú zhē lán

In English

  • sb have no filter
  • shoot from the lip
  • blurt sth out (without thinking)

Meaning

Having no filter.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • blurt sth out

Example

我叔叔口無遮攔，想到什麼就說什麼。

My uncle has no filter; he says whatever pops into his head.

Usage

For people who speak without thinking.

Similar

  • ⚡心直口快xīn zhí kǒu kuài

    straight shooter — A straight shooter; more positive.

    Her quick tongue sometimes gets her into trouble, but at least she is a straight shooter.

  • 📢大放厥詞dà fàng jué cí

    spout nonsense; fling wild accusations — Spouting nonsense.

    He spouted nonsense on live TV.

Opposite

  • 🤐守口如瓶shǒu kǒu rú píng

    tight-lipped; keep mum — Tight-lipped.

    He stayed tight-lipped about the merger.

  • 🔎字字斟酌zì zì zhēn zhuó

    carefully examine — Weighing every word.

    She carefully examined every word of the contract before signing.
