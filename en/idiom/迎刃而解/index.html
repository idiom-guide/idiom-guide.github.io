迎刃而解

yíng rèn ér jiě

In English

  • sth be quickly resolved

Meaning

Everything falls into place.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • be quickly resolved
  • fall into place
  • solve itself

Example

主要問題一解決，其他問題就迎刃而解了。

Once the main issue was settled, the other problems were quickly resolved.

Usage

Often 問題迎刃而解 (the problems sort themselves out).

Similar

  • 💧水到渠成shuǐ dào qú chéng

    when time is ripe; fall into place — When the time is ripe, things happen naturally.

    When the time is ripe, things will fall into place.

  • 🧁輕而易舉qīng ér yì jǔ

    be a piece of cake — As easy as pie.

    For him, this is a piece of cake.

Opposite

  • 🚧困難重重kùn nán chóng chóng

    be fraught with problems/difficulties — Fraught with difficulties.

    The plan is fraught with problems.

  • 🌑毫無頭緒háo wú tóu xù

    grope in the dark; have no clue — Having no clue.

    I have no clue where to begin.
