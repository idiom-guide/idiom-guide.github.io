強勢打入

qiáng shì dǎ rù

In English

  • muscle sb's way into sth/place

Meaning

Muscling one's way in.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • muscle one's way into
  • break into
  • make inroads into

Example

這個韓國品牌強勢打入美國市場。

The Korean brand muscled its way into the American market.

Usage

For business, entertainment and sport.

Similar

  • ➡️長驅直入cháng qū zhí rù

    penetrate — Driving deep without resistance.

    The army was able to penetrate deep into enemy territory without resistance.

  • ⚔️所向披靡suǒ xiàng pī mǐ

    unbeatable — Unbeatable.

    The team was unbeatable that season.

Opposite

  • 🚧遭遇阻力zāo yù zǔ lì

    hit a (major) speedbump — Meeting resistance.

    The project hit a major speedbump when the funding fell through.

  • 🙅打消主意dǎ xiāo zhǔ yì

    give up the idea; think better of it — Thinking better of it.

    He gave up the idea of moving abroad.
