滴水穿石

dī shuǐ chuān shí

In English

  • constant dripping wears away a stone
  • perseverance pays off

Meaning

Constant dripping wears away a stone.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • slow and steady wins the race

Example

滴水穿石，所以要持續練習。

Constant dripping wears away a stone, so keep practicing.

Usage

Often as encouragement to keep practicing or studying.

Similar

  • 🧱日積月累rì jī yuè lěi

    no quick fixes — Built up bit by bit.

    There are no quick fixes in language learning; progress comes from steady, accumulated effort.

  • 🦾不屈不撓bù qū bù náo

    invincible, indomitable, dauntless — Indomitable.

    The dauntless climber kept going despite the storm.

Opposite

  • 🌤️一曝十寒yī pù shí hán

    sporadic effort — Sporadic effort.

    His sporadic effort won't get him anywhere.

  • 🐯虎頭蛇尾hǔ tóu shé wěi

    start with a bang and end with a whimper; peter out — Starting with a bang and ending with a whimper.

    The campaign started with a bang and ended with a whimper.
