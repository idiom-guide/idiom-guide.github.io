蕭規曹隨

xiāo guī cáo suí

In English

  • cookie cutter

Meaning

Following in a predecessor's footsteps.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • follow in one's predecessor's footsteps
  • stay the course

Example

新市長沒有改革，而是蕭規曹隨，沿用舊政策。

Instead of making changes, the new mayor kept the old policies in cookie-cutter fashion.

Usage

Formal; from the Records of the Grand Historian; neutral for continuity, or a dig at a lack of ideas.

Similar

  • 🚋墨守成規mò shǒu chéng guī

    stick to old rules; stay in a rut — Sticking to the old rules.

    The company sticks to old rules and finds it hard to innovate.

  • 🗿一成不變yī chéng bù biàn

    unchanging; set in stone — Set in stone.

    The curriculum is not set in stone.

Opposite

  • 🎻改弦更張gǎi xián gēng zhāng

    change course; a policy overhaul — Changing course.

    Faced with falling support, the cabinet changed course on energy policy.

  • ♻️推陳出新tuī chén chū xīn

    breathe new life into tradition — Breathing new life into tradition.

    Young designers are breathing new life into tradition.
