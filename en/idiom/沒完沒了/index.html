沒完沒了

méi wán méi liǎo

In English

  • incessant + chatter, complaints, rain

Meaning

Never-ending.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • incessant
  • never-ending
  • on and on

Example

火車上沒完沒了的聊天聲讓我頭痛。

The incessant chatter on the train gave me a headache.

Usage

Colloquial; for chatter, complaints, rain and disputes.

Similar

  • 🦜喋喋不休dié dié bù xiū

    ad nauseam — Going on ad nauseam.

    He went on about his holiday ad nauseam.

  • 🚰滔滔不絕tāo tāo bù jué

    talk nonstop; go on without a pause — Talking nonstop.

    The moment football comes up, he talks nonstop.

Opposite

  • ✋適可而止shì kě ér zhǐ

    know when to stop — Knowing when to stop.

    Criticism is fine, but know when to stop.

  • ⏹️戛然而止jiá rán ér zhǐ

    come to an abrupt halt; stop short — Stopping abruptly.

    The music came to an abrupt halt.
