群起圍攻

qún qǐ wéi gōng

In English

  • drew flak from sb for reason

Meaning

Attacked from all sides.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • draw flak from
  • be attacked from all sides
  • gang up on

Example

公司漲價，引來顧客群起圍攻。

The company drew flak from customers over the price rise.

Usage

Usually public criticism; can also be a literal attack.

Similar

  • 🐕大舉進攻dà jǔ jìn gōng

    unleash the hounds — An all-out attack.

    The opposition unleashed the hounds on the minister during the debate.

  • 👉千夫所指qiān fū suǒ zhǐ

    universally condemned — Universally condemned.

    The attack was universally condemned.

Opposite

  • 👄有口皆碑yǒu kǒu jiē bēi

    good reputation — Praised by everyone.

    The clinic has a good reputation among all the locals.

  • 🗳️眾望所歸zhòng wàng suǒ guī

    the people's choice; enjoy popular support — The people's choice.

    She was the people's choice for the post.
