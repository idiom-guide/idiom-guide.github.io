1 · Knowing & Thinking

預見未來

yù jiàn wèi lái

In English

  • clairvoyant

Meaning

Foreseeing the future.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • foresee the future
  • be clairvoyant
  • be prophetic
  • see what lies ahead

Example

這位小說家彷彿能預見未來，在網路出現前幾十年就描寫了它。

The novelist seemed clairvoyant, describing the internet decades before it existed.

Usage

Can mean predicting or prophecy, or simply foresight; often praises long-term vision.

Similar

  • 🔭洞燭機先dòng zhú jī xiān

    see it coming; have uncanny foresight — Seeing it coming.

    She could see it coming long before the market crashed.

  • 🦉先見之明xiān jiàn zhī míng

    be prescient of; proved prescient — Foresight.

    Her decision to sell early proved prescient.

Opposite

  • ⏪事後諸葛shì hòu zhū gé

    hindsight; Monday morning quarterback — Being wise after the event.

    He's a Monday morning quarterback who has all the answers after the game.

  • 🚇目光短淺mù guāng duǎn qiǎn

    short-sighted — Short-sighted.

    The decision was short-sighted.
