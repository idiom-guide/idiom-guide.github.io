欲蓋彌彰

yù gài mí zhāng

In English

  • draw attention by trying to cover up
  • backfire

Meaning

The more one tries to hide something, the more obvious it becomes.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • the cover-up makes it worse
  • a cover-up only makes things more obvious

Example

他冗長的否認反而引人注意，想掩蓋錯誤卻欲蓋彌彰。

His long denial only drew attention; trying to cover up the mistake backfired.

Usage

Describes explanations that only raise suspicion; often 只會欲蓋彌彰.

Similar

  • 😝不打自招bù dǎ zì zhāo

    give oneself away; confess without being questioned — Giving oneself away unprompted.

    He let it slip and gave himself away.

  • 🤦‍♂️弄巧成拙nòng qiǎo chéng zhuō

    outsmart oneself; be too clever by half — Too clever by half; backfires.

    He tried to hide it but outsmarted himself.

Opposite

  • 💧滴水不漏dī shuǐ bù lòu

    airtight; watertight — Watertight.

    The contract is airtight; there is not a single loophole in it.

  • 🐴深藏不露shēn cáng bù lù

    keep one's talents hidden; be a dark horse — Keeping one's abilities or intentions hidden.

    He kept his talents hidden, and nobody knew he was the champion.
