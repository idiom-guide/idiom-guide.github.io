盛裝打扮

shèng zhuāng dǎ bàn

In English

  • be dressed to the nines

Meaning

Dressed to the nines.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • dressed up
  • in one's Sunday best

Example

大家都為晚宴盛裝打扮。

Everyone was dressed to the nines for the gala.

Usage

For parties, ceremonies and festivals.

Similar

  • 👗穿搭出色chuān dā chū sè

    sth looks exquisite on sb — Looking exquisite.

    That dark-blue suit looks exquisite on him.

  • ✨光彩照人guāng cǎi zhào rén

    radiant; dazzling — Radiant.

    At the gala she looked radiant; her beauty had an incandescent quality.

Opposite

  • 🧔不修邊幅bù xiū biān fú

    scruffy, slovenly, disheveled, unkempt, slob — Scruffy.

    He turned up scruffy and unshaven for the interview.
