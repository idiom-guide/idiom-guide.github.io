言簡意賅

yán jiǎn yì gāi

In English

  • concise and to the point

Meaning

Concise yet complete.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • pithy
  • succinct

Example

她的報告言簡意賅。

Her report was concise and to the point.

Usage

Praise for tight writing or speech; common for letters, reports and slogans.

Similar

  • ✂️簡明扼要jiǎn míng è yào

    be succinct; succinctly — Concise and to the point.

    Please keep your report succinct; we only have ten minutes.

  • 🔍微言大義wēi yán dà yì

    subtle words with profound meaning — Few words with profound meaning; stresses depth.

    In a few subtle words, the author conveyed a profound message.

Opposite

  • 🗞長篇大論cháng piān dà lùn

    go on at length; a long-winded speech — A long-winded speech.

    He went on at length again, and nobody could take it in.

  • 🥫言之無物yán zhī wú wù

    have no substance; be empty talk — Empty talk with no substance.

    The entire speech had no substance, and I remembered nothing.
