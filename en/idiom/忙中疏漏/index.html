忙中疏漏

máng zhōng shū lòu

In English

  • lost in the shuffle

Meaning

Lost in the shuffle.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • slip through the cracks
  • an oversight

Example

一陣忙亂中，她的請求因忙中疏漏而被遺忘。

In the rush, her request got lost in the shuffle.

Usage

For things overlooked in a busy period.

Similar

  • ↘️出了差錯chū le chā cuò

    things go sideways — Things going sideways.

    When things go sideways, stay calm.

Opposite

  • 📏一絲不苟yī sī bù gǒu

    meticulous; scrupulous — Meticulous.

    He is meticulous about every detail.

  • 🔎無微不至wú wēi bù zhì

    meticulous arrangements — Attentive to every need.

    The nurse made meticulous arrangements for every patient's comfort.
