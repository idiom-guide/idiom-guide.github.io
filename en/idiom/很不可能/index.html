很不可能

hěn bù kě néng

In English

  • be next to impossible

Meaning

Highly unlikely.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • be highly unlikely
  • there is no way

Example

要在今晚前寫完報告，很不可能。

Finishing the report by tonight is next to impossible.

Usage

A conversational phrase; formal writing tends to use 幾乎不可能.

Similar

  • 🧗難如登天nán rú dēng tiān

    insanely difficult — As hard as climbing to the sky; hyperbolic.

    Finding a taxi at rush hour in the rain is insanely difficult.

  • 🔬微乎其微wēi hū qí wēi

    negligible — So slim it's almost zero; often about odds.

    The risk is negligible.

Opposite

  • 🧁輕而易舉qīng ér yì jǔ

    be a piece of cake — Done with no effort at all.

    For him, this is a piece of cake.

  • 🏀十拿九穩shí ná jiǔ wěn

    be all but certain; be a safe bet — Almost certain to succeed.

    With this contract in hand, winning the bid is all but certain.
