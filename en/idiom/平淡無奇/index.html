平淡無奇

píng dàn wú qí

In English

  • run-of-the-mill

Meaning

Run-of-the-mill.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • nothing to write home about
  • humdrum

Example

那只是辦公室裡平淡無奇的一天。

It was just a run-of-the-mill day at the office.

Usage

For days, works and experiences.

Similar

  • 🥔非常普通fēi cháng pǔ tōng

    garden-variety — Garden-variety.

    It's just a garden-variety cold, nothing serious.

  • 😐印象平平yìn xiàng píng píng

    unimpressed with sth — Unimpressed.

    The critics were unimpressed with the sequel.

Opposite

  • 🎆不同凡響bù tóng fán xiǎng

    be larger than life — Extraordinary.

    His performance was larger than life.

  • 🤩歎為觀止tàn wéi guān zhǐ

    breathtaking; awe-inspiring — Breathtaking.

    The view from the summit was breathtaking.
