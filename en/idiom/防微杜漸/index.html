防微杜漸

fáng wēi dù jiàn

In English

  • nip something in the bud

Meaning

Nipping it in the bud.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • nip sth in the bud
  • stop it before it spreads
  • prevention is better than cure

Example

現在快點處理，就能防微杜漸。

A quick fix now will nip the problem in the bud.

Usage

For systems, education and discipline.

Similar

  • ☂️未雨綢繆wèi yǔ chóu móu

    be fully prepared; provident — Preparing for a rainy day.

    Keep an emergency kit at home; it pays to be fully prepared.

  • 👀提高警覺tí gāo jǐng jué

    eyes up — Staying on the alert.

    Keep your eyes up when you cross the road.

Opposite

  • ❌積非成是jī fēi chéng shì

    a wrong accepted through repetition; repeated errors become the norm — A wrong repeated until it seems right.

    If we keep ignoring the error, a mistake repeated often enough may come to be accepted as truth.

  • 📈變本加厲biàn běn jiā lì

    go from bad to worse; escalate — Getting worse than before.

    Instead of stopping, the bullies went from bad to worse.
