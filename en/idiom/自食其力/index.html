自食其力

zì shí qí lì

In English

  • stand on one's own legs

Meaning

Supporting oneself.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • stand on one's own two feet
  • support oneself

Example

十六歲時，她決定自食其力。

At sixteen, she decided to stand on her own legs.

Usage

For young people, workers and anyone earning their own living.

Similar

  • ✂️獨立自主dú lì zì zhǔ

    cut the (umbilical) cord; become independent — Independent.

    At twenty, he cut the cord and moved out on his own.

  • 🏗️白手起家bái shǒu qǐ jiā

    a self-made man/woman; start from scratch — Self-made.

    She is a self-made woman who started from scratch.

Opposite

  • 🥪不勞而獲bù láo ér huò

    get something for nothing; get a free lunch — Getting something for nothing.

    There is no such thing as a free lunch.

  • 🍼嬌生慣養jiāo shēng guàn yǎng

    pampered; spoiled — Pampered and spoiled.

    The pampered prince had never washed a dish.
