10 · Art & Performance

維妙維肖

wéi miào wéi xiào

In English

  • remarkably true to life

Meaning

Remarkably true to life.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • spot-on
  • an uncanny likeness

Example

他模仿總統維妙維肖。

His impression of the president is remarkably true to life.

Usage

For impressions, paintings and sculpture; also written 惟妙惟肖.

Similar

  • 🖼️栩栩如生xǔ xǔ rú shēng

    lifelike — Lifelike.

    The wax figures are remarkably lifelike.

Opposite

  • 🧟面目全非miàn mù quán fēi

    be unrecognizable — Unrecognizable.

    After the adaptation, the plot is unrecognizable.
