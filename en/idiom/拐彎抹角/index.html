拐彎抹角

guǎi wān mò jiǎo

In English

  • beat around the bush
  • obliquely

Meaning

Beating around the bush.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • talk in circles

Example

別拐彎抹角了，直接告訴我發生了什麼事。

Stop beating around the bush and tell me what happened.

Usage

Usually used to urge someone to get to the point; also describes rambling writing.

Similar

  • 🥁旁敲側擊páng qiāo cè jī

    probe — Dropping hints; usually with a purpose.

    He probed gently to find out whether she planned to quit.

  • 🌁含糊其詞hán hú qí cí

    talk in generalities; be coy with sb — Being deliberately vague.

    Asked about the budget, the spokesman talked in generalities.

Opposite

  • 🚪開門見山kāi mén jiàn shān

    get straight to the point — Getting straight to the point.

    He got straight to the point and asked about the price.

  • 📢直言不諱zhí yán bù huì

    speak frankly; not mince words — Speaking bluntly.

    She spoke frankly and pointed out the flaws in the plan.
