權衡利弊

quán héng lì bì

In English

  • make the calculation

Meaning

Weighing the pros and cons.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • weigh the pros and cons
  • do a cost-benefit analysis

Example

他仔細權衡利弊，比較每一份工作。

He made the calculation carefully, weighing the pros and cons of each offer.

Usage

For decisions, negotiations and investments.

Similar

  • ⚖️兩害權其輕liǎng hài quán qí qīng

    lesser of two evils — Choosing the lesser of two evils.

    Neither choice is good, so pick the lesser of two evils.

  • 🗿深思熟慮shēn sī shú lǜ

    think it through carefully; give careful thought — Well thought out.

    Major decisions require careful thought, not haste.

Opposite

  • ⚡不假思索bù jiǎ sī suǒ

    on the spur of the moment; on a whim — Without a second thought.

    On the spur of the moment, he jumped into the river to help.

  • 💥意氣用事yì qì yòng shì

    emotional (player) — Acting on emotion.

    He's an emotional player who makes rash choices when angry.
