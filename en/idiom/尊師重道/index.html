10 · Teaching & the Teacher's Way

尊師重道

zūn shī zhòng dào

In English

  • respect teachers and their teaching

Meaning

Respect for teachers and their teaching.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • honor one's teachers
  • reverence for learning

Example

這個節日彰顯尊師重道的精神。

The festival celebrates respect for teachers and their teaching.

Usage

Formal; for Teachers' Day and educational ideals.

Similar

  • 🚰飲水思源yǐn shuǐ sī yuán

    appreciate the heritage — Remembering one's roots.

    We should appreciate our heritage and remember where we came from.

Opposite

  • 😛沒大沒小méi dà méi xiǎo

    being rude, impolite — Rude to one's elders.

    Don't talk to your grandmother like that; it's rude.
