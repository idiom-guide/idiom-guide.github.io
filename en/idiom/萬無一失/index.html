萬無一失

wàn wú yī shī

In English

  • foolproof
  • failsafe

Meaning

Foolproof.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • can't go wrong

Example

這個計畫萬無一失。

The plan is foolproof.

Usage

Used to vouch for a plan; also in negatives as a warning about risk.

Similar

  • 🏀十拿九穩shí ná jiǔ wěn

    be all but certain; be a safe bet — A sure thing.

    With this contract in hand, winning the bid is all but certain.

  • ⛑穩紮穩打wěn zhā wěn dǎ

    build steadily; play it safe and steady — Steady and sure.

    A start-up should build steadily and not rush to expand.

Opposite

  • 🧀漏洞百出lòu dòng bǎi chū

    be full of holes; be riddled with flaws — Full of holes.

    His story was full of holes, and nobody believed it.

  • ☠️冒險玩命mào xiǎn wán mìng

    tempt fate — Tempting fate.

    Driving through this storm is tempting fate.
