沽名釣譽

gū míng diào yù

In English

  • fame seeker

Meaning

Fame-seeking.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • publicity-seeking
  • grandstanding

Example

批評者說他是沽名釣譽之徒，捐款只為上新聞。

Critics called him a fame seeker who donated only for the headlines.

Usage

For criticizing grandstanding politicians and celebrities.

Similar

  • 🎪嘩眾取寵huá zhòng qǔ chǒng

    play to the gallery; sensationalize — Playing to the gallery.

    The mayor's stunts were just playing to the gallery.

  • 🎭表面工夫biǎo miàn gōng fu

    tokenism — Lip service.

    The company's diversity program was just tokenism.

Opposite

  • ⛩淡泊名利dàn bó míng lì

    be indifferent to fame and fortune — Indifferent to fame and fortune.

    He was indifferent to fame and fortune all his life and only wanted to teach well.

  • 🐭不出鋒頭bù chū fēng tóu

    unassuming — Unassuming.

    He is an unassuming man who never seeks the spotlight.
