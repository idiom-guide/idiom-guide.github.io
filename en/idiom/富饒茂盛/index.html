富饒茂盛

fù ráo mào shèng

In English

  • luxuriant

Meaning

Luxuriant.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • lush
  • fertile

Example

這座山谷草木蒼翠，富饒茂盛。

The valley was lush and luxuriant.

Usage

Formal; for land, plants and forests.

Similar

  • 🌸奇花異草qí huā yì cǎo

    exotic flowers and herbs — Exotic flowers and herbs.

    The garden is home to exotic flowers and herbs.

Opposite

  • 🏚️滿目瘡痍mǎn mù chuāng yí

    devastated, battered, ravaged (landscape) — Ravaged.

    After the war, the landscape was devastated and ravaged.
