及時行樂

jí shí xíng lè

In English

  • carpe diem
  • eat, drink, and be merry

Meaning

Carpe diem.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • live for the moment

Example

及時行樂！今晚出去玩吧。

Carpe diem! Let's go out tonight.

Usage

Can mean seizing the day, or living without thought for the future.

Similar

  • 🍾縱情享樂zòng qíng xiǎng lè

    indulge oneself; live it up — Living it up.

    They indulged themselves at the festival.

Opposite

  • 🍵先苦後甘xiān kǔ hòu gān

    take short term pain for long term gain — Short-term pain for long-term gain.

    Training is hard, but it is short-term pain for long-term gain.

  • ☂️未雨綢繆wèi yǔ chóu móu

    be fully prepared; provident — Preparing in advance.

    Keep an emergency kit at home; it pays to be fully prepared.
