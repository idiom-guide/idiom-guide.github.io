11 · Love & Marriage

情投意合

qíng tóu yì hé

In English

  • well-suited to each other

Meaning

Well-suited to each other.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • compatible
  • get on like a house on fire

Example

這對夫妻情投意合。

The couple are well-suited to each other.

Usage

For couples and friends.

Similar

  • 💑兩情相悅liǎng qíng xiāng yuè

    mutual affection — Mutual affection.

    The marriage was built on mutual affection.

  • 🎣志趣相投zhì qù xiāng tóu

    kindred spirits — Kindred spirits.

    The two writers are kindred spirits.

Opposite

  • 🙉話不投機半句多huà bù tóu jī bàn jù duō

    have nothing to say to each other; not see eye to eye — Not speaking the same language.

    We don't see eye to eye, so let's call it a day.

  • 🐟格格不入gé gé bù rù

    be out of place; be like a fish out of water — A fish out of water.

    He felt like a fish out of water among his new colleagues.
