10 · Culture & Heritage

入境隨俗

rù jìng suí sú

In English

  • when in Rome, do as the Romans do

Meaning

When in Rome.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • go native
  • follow local customs

Example

入境隨俗，進門前要脫鞋。

When in Rome, do as the Romans do: take off your shoes at the door.

Usage

For travel and cross-cultural exchange.

Similar

  • 🧩因地制宜yīn dì zhì yí

    adapt to local conditions — Adapting to local conditions.

    Policies should be adapted to local conditions.

  • 📜約定俗成yuē dìng sú chéng

    established by convention; by common usage — Established by convention.

    The rule has become established by convention.

Opposite

  • 🐟格格不入gé gé bù rù

    be out of place; be like a fish out of water — A fish out of water.

    He felt like a fish out of water among his new colleagues.

  • 🤒水土不服shuǐ tǔ bù fú

    fail to acclimatize; not suit the local conditions — Not adjusting to a new place.

    The management system failed to adapt once it reached the branch office.
