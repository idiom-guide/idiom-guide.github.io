一廂情願

yī xiāng qíng yuàn

In English

  • wishful thinking

Meaning

Wishful thinking.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • one-sided
  • kid oneself

Example

以為他會道歉，只是一廂情願。

Thinking he will apologize is wishful thinking.

Usage

For one-sided hopes in love, expectations or partnerships.

Similar

  • 💭癡心妄想chī xīn wàng xiǎng

    truly delusional — A pipe dream.

    Expecting to get rich overnight is truly delusional.

  • 😴癡人說夢chī rén shuō mèng

    wildly unrealistic — Wildly unrealistic.

    His plan to cross the ocean on a raft is wildly unrealistic.

Opposite

  • 💕情投意合qíng tóu yì hé

    well-suited to each other — Well-suited to each other.

    The couple are well-suited to each other.
