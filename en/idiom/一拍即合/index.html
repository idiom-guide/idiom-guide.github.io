一拍即合

yī pāi jí hé

In English

  • hit it off
  • click at once

Meaning

Hitting it off.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • be on the same wavelength

Example

兩位創辦人第一次見面就一拍即合。

The two founders hit it off at their first meeting.

Usage

For partnerships and friendships, sometimes disapproving.

Similar

  • 😄一見如故yī jiàn rú gù

    hit it off like old friends — Hitting it off like old friends.

    We hit it off like old friends.

  • 🤝志同道合zhì tóng dào hé

    like minded — Like-minded.

    She found like-minded friends in the chess club.

Opposite

  • 🙉話不投機半句多huà bù tóu jī bàn jù duō

    have nothing to say to each other; not see eye to eye — Not speaking the same language.

    We don't see eye to eye, so let's call it a day.

  • 🐟格格不入gé gé bù rù

    be out of place; be like a fish out of water — A fish out of water.

    He felt like a fish out of water among his new colleagues.
