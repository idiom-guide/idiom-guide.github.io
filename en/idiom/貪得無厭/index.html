貪得無厭

tān dé wú yàn

In English

  • too greedy

Meaning

Insatiably greedy.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • insatiably greedy
  • never satisfied

Example

房東貪得無厭，每年都漲房租。

The landlord is too greedy and raises the rent every year.

Usage

For money, power and desire.

Similar

  • 🐽得寸進尺dé cùn jìn chǐ

    give an inch and take a mile — Give an inch and they take a mile.

    Don't push your luck; I have already given way a lot.

Opposite

  • 😊知足常樂zhī zú cháng lè

    be happy with enough — Contentment is happiness.

    Be happy with enough: contentment is a treasure.

  • ✋適可而止shì kě ér zhǐ

    know when to stop — Knowing when to stop.

    Criticism is fine, but know when to stop.
