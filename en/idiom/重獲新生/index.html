重獲新生

chóng huò xīn shēng

In English

  • secure/get a new lease of life

Meaning

A new lease of life.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • a fresh start
  • be born again

Example

手術後，他覺得自己重獲新生。

After the operation, he felt he had a new lease of life.

Usage

For recovering from illness, starting life afresh, or reviving old buildings and brands.

Similar

  • 🔥浴火重生yù huǒ chóng shēng

    rise from the ashes — Rising from the ashes.

    After the fire, the old theater rose from the ashes as a modern concert hall.

  • 💓起死回生qǐ sǐ huí shēng

    bring back from the dead; a miraculous recovery — Brought back from the dead.

    The new owner's plan brought the company back from the dead.

Opposite

  • 🍂槁木死灰gǎo mù sǐ huī

    utterly lifeless; apathetic — Utterly lifeless.

    After the loss, she looked utterly lifeless.

  • ⚰️生不如死shēng bù rú sǐ

    a fate worse than death; living in misery — A fate worse than death.

    He lived in misery after the war.
