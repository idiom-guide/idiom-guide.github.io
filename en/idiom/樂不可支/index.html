3 · Joy & Satisfaction

樂不可支

lè bù kě zhī

In English

  • be on cloud nine
  • in seventh heaven

Meaning

On cloud nine.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • over the moon

Example

她得獎後樂不可支。

She was on cloud nine after winning the prize.

Usage

For moments of great happiness.

Similar

  • 😆興高采烈xìng gāo cǎi liè

    gleeful; a gleeful smile/shout — Gleeful.

    The children ran out of school in gleeful anticipation of summer.

  • 🐦歡欣雀躍huān xīn què yuè

    be jubilant at/about/over sth — Jubilant.

    The fans were jubilant over the victory.

Opposite

  • 😟愁眉苦臉chóu méi kǔ liǎn

    look so woebegone; with a woebegone expression — Woebegone.

    He looked so woebegone that the waiter asked if he was ill.

  • 😢令人沮喪lìng rén jǔ sàng

    disheartening — Disheartening.

    The long waiting list is disheartening.
