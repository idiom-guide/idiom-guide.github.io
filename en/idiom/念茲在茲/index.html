念茲在茲

niàn zī zài zī

In English

  • have sth constantly on one's mind

Meaning

Always having something on one's mind.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • be preoccupied with
  • keep sth at the forefront of one's mind

Example

即將到來的考試，是她念茲在茲的事。

The coming exam is something she constantly has on her mind.

Usage

From the Book of Documents (Counsels of Great Yu); now for a goal or wish one never forgets, often admiring.

Similar

  • 📌謹記在心jǐn jì zài xīn

    be taken to heart — Bearing in mind; about remembering content.

    Her advice will be taken to heart.

  • 😒耿耿於懷gěng gěng yú huái

    hold a grudge; can't let it go — Unable to let go, usually of something unpleasant.

    He still holds a grudge about that remark.

Opposite

  • 🌥過眼雲煙guò yǎn yún yān

    a passing cloud; be fleeting and insubstantial — Fleeting, not worth dwelling on.

    Wealth and glory are no more than passing clouds.

  • 😑漠不關心mò bù guān xīn

    uncaring — Indifferent.

    The landlord was uncaring about his tenants' complaints.
