雄心壯志

xióng xīn zhuàng zhì

In English

  • got a large ambition

Meaning

Great ambitions.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • high ambitions
  • big dreams

Example

他懷著雄心壯志：四十歲前要執掌公司。

He had a large ambition: to run the company by forty.

Usage

For young people, entrepreneurs and leaders; positive.

Similar

  • 🚀大展鴻圖dà zhǎn hóng tú

    realize your ambitions — Fulfilling grand ambitions.

    May you realize your ambitions in the year ahead.

  • 🦅鵬程萬里péng chéng wàn lǐ

    a bright future ahead — A bright future ahead.

    To the graduates: you have a bright future ahead.

Opposite

  • 😊知足常樂zhī zú cháng lè

    be happy with enough — Contentment is happiness.

    Be happy with enough: contentment is a treasure.

  • 🔻自暴自棄zì bào zì qì

    give up on oneself — Giving up on oneself.

    Don't give up on yourself because of a single failure.
