戰到最後一刻

zhàn dào zuì hòu yī kè

In English

  • fight to the last
  • go down fighting

Meaning

Fighting to the last.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • never give up

Example

球隊戰到最後一刻。

The team fought to the last.

Usage

For matches, campaigns or holding on through hardship.

Similar

  • 🌊背水一戰bèi shuǐ yī zhàn

    a last stand; do-or-die — A do-or-die battle.

    With their backs to the wall, the team played a do-or-die match.

  • 🎲不成功，便成仁bù chéng gōng, biàn chéng rén

    make or break — Make or break.

    This quarter is make or break for the young company.

Opposite

  • 🛑半途而廢bàn tú ér fèi

    give up halfway — Giving up halfway.

    Don't give up halfway.

  • 🏃臨陣脫逃lín zhèn tuō táo

    shy from battle — Running away when the time comes.

    The goalkeeper shied from battle at the last moment.
