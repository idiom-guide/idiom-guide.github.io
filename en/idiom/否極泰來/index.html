2 · Change & Fate

否極泰來

pǐ jí tài lái

In English

  • the tide will turn
  • every cloud has a silver lining

Meaning

After the worst comes the best.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • things can only get better

Example

熬過辛苦的一年，總會否極泰來。

After a hard year, the tide will turn.

Usage

Formal; 否 and 泰 are two hexagrams in the Book of Changes; often used to comfort or wish someone well.

Similar

  • 🍵先苦後甘xiān kǔ hòu gān

    take short term pain for long term gain — Short-term pain for long-term gain; stresses effort.

    Training is hard, but it is short-term pain for long-term gain.

  • 🎡時來運轉shí lái yùn zhuǎn

    one's luck turns; one's time has come — One's luck turning.

    After years of bad luck, his time has come.

Opposite

  • 🎭樂極生悲lè jí shēng bēi

    extreme joy turns sorrow — Joy turning to sorrow.

    At the party he danced so hard that he broke his leg; extreme joy turned to sorrow.

  • 👟禍不單行huò bù dān xíng

    wait for the other shoe to drop; one-two punch — Misfortunes never come singly.

    First the roof leaked, then the pipes burst. When it rains, it pours.
