飲鴆止渴

yǐn zhèn zhǐ kě

In English

  • a short-term fix that causes long-term harm

Meaning

A cure worse than the disease.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • a band-aid that makes it worse
  • cure worse than the disease

Example

再借更多錢，無異飲鴆止渴。

Borrowing more money is a short-term fix that causes long-term harm.

Usage

From the Book of the Later Han; for quick fixes in finance and policy.

Similar

  • 🧱挖東牆補西牆wā dōng qiáng bǔ xī qiáng

    robbing Peter to pay Paul — Robbing Peter to pay Paul.

    He is robbing Peter to pay Paul with his credit cards.

  • 🗓️寅吃卯糧yín chī mǎo liáng

    borrow against the future — Borrowing against the future.

    Funding today's pensions with new debt is borrowing against the future.

Opposite

  • 💊對症下藥duì zhèng xià yào

    a targeted remedy; address the root cause — A targeted remedy.

    Without reliable data, the government cannot offer a targeted remedy.

  • 🕊️長治久安cháng zhì jiǔ ān

    lasting stability — Lasting stability.

    Lasting stability depends on institutions, not on any single leader.
