福禍相依

fú huò xiāng yī

In English

  • fortune and misfortune are intertwined

Meaning

Good and bad fortune go hand in hand.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • every cloud has a silver lining
  • a blessing in disguise

Example

世事福禍相依，好壞往往交織在一起。

Fortune and misfortune are intertwined.

Usage

Formal; from Laozi: 'misfortune is what fortune leans on; fortune is where misfortune hides.'

Similar

  • 🌅否極泰來pǐ jí tài lái

    the tide will turn; every cloud has a silver lining — The tide will turn; one direction only.

    After a hard year, the tide will turn.

  • 🎭樂極生悲lè jí shēng bēi

    extreme joy turns sorrow — Joy turning to sorrow; also one direction.

    At the party he danced so hard that he broke his leg; extreme joy turned to sorrow.

Opposite

  • 👟禍不單行huò bù dān xíng

    wait for the other shoe to drop; one-two punch — Misfortunes never come singly.

    First the roof leaked, then the pipes burst. When it rains, it pours.

  • 🥀命運多舛mìng yùn duō chuǎn

    ill-fated — Ill-fated.

    The ill-fated expedition never reached the summit.
