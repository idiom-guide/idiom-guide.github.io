渾水摸魚

hún shuǐ mō yú

In English

  • catch fish in muddy water

Meaning

Fishing in troubled waters.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • fish in troubled waters
  • profit from the chaos

Example

市場恐慌時，有些交易員趁機渾水摸魚。

Some traders catch fish in muddy water during a market panic.

Usage

For people who profit from chaos or crisis.

Similar

  • 🔥趁火打劫chèn huǒ dǎ jié

    fish in troubled waters — Looting while the house burns; worse.

    Some landlords fished in troubled waters by raising rents after the storm.

  • 🦊投機取巧tóu jī qǔ qiǎo

    opportunistic; find loopholes in — Opportunistic.

    Opportunistic traders find loopholes in the rules.

Opposite

  • ⬜光明正大guāng míng zhèng dà

    fair and square — Fair and square.

    We won the contest fair and square.

  • 📏循規蹈矩xún guī dǎo jǔ

    by the book; play by the rules — By the book.

    He plays by the rules and never takes risks.
