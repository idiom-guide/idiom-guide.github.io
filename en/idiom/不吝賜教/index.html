9 · Thanks & Humble Phrases

不吝賜教

bù lìn cì jiào

In English

  • I welcome your feedback

Meaning

I welcome your feedback.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • please don't hesitate to advise
  • your comments are most welcome

Example

這只是初稿，請各位不吝賜教。

This is only a first draft, and I welcome your feedback.

Usage

Formal courtesy; for closing a report, article or talk.

Similar

  • 🙋不恥下問bù chǐ xià wèn

    not too proud to ask — Not too proud to ask.

    A real scholar is not too proud to ask.

  • 🧱拋磚引玉pāo zhuān yǐn yù

    just tossing an idea out there — Tossing an idea out there.

    I'm just tossing an idea out there to get the discussion started.

Opposite

  • 🤴自命不凡zì mìng bù fán

    overestimate oneself; hotshot — A hotshot.

    The young hotshot thought he was above the rules.

  • 🛑剛愎自用gāng bì zì yòng

    opinionated; stubborn — Opinionated.

    The stubborn chairman ignored every warning.
