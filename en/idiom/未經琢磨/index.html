未經琢磨

wèi jīng zhuó mó

In English

  • diamond in the rough

Meaning

A diamond in the rough.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • raw talent
  • unpolished

Example

這位年輕歌手才華出眾，只是還未經琢磨。

The young singer is a diamond in the rough.

Usage

For promising newcomers.

Similar

  • 🐴深藏不露shēn cáng bù lù

    keep one's talents hidden; be a dark horse — Hidden depths.

    He kept his talents hidden, and nobody knew he was the champion.

  • 🧠很有慧根hěn yǒu huì gēn

    talented — A quick study.

    The teacher says the boy is talented and picks up ideas fast.

Opposite

  • 🔥爐火純青lú huǒ chún qīng

    consummate skill — Consummate skill.

    The carver works with consummate skill.

  • 🖍精雕細琢jīng diāo xì zuó

    polish meticulously; craft with great care — Carefully polished.

    The speech was polished with painstaking care.
