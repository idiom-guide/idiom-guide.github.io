談判籌碼

tán pàn chóu mǎ

In English

  • bargaining chips
  • give and take
  • leverage

Meaning

A bargaining chip.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • a bargaining chip
  • a card to play

Example

那塊土地是他們最主要的談判籌碼。

The land was their main bargaining chip in the talks.

Usage

Often 手上有談判籌碼 (to have cards to play).

Similar

  • 💪佔有優勢zhàn yǒu yōu shì

    has the upper hand — Having the upper hand.

    With a stronger lineup, our team has the upper hand.

  • 🧰看家本領kān jiā běn lǐng

    one's stock in trade; signature skill — One's best skill.

    Fixing old watches is his craft, and he has done it for forty years.

Opposite

  • 🥺看人臉色kàn rén liǎn sè

    be at the mercy of sb — At someone's mercy.

    I don't want to be at the mercy of my boss's mood every day.

  • 🐘實力懸殊shí lì xuán shū

    A overmatch B in sth; be overmatched by — A mismatch.

    The local team was overmatched by the national champions.
