道不同不相為謀

dào bù tóng bù xiāng wéi móu

In English

  • don't consort with my kind

Meaning

Parting ways over different values.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • go separate ways
  • don't consort with sb's kind
  • we have nothing in common

Example

他選擇不與價值觀和自己相反的人往來，道不同不相為謀。

He chose not to consort with people whose values were the opposite of his own.

Usage

From the Analects; often used to explain a parting of ways.

Similar

  • 🙉話不投機半句多huà bù tóu jī bàn jù duō

    have nothing to say to each other; not see eye to eye — When you don't get along, even half a sentence is too much.

    We don't see eye to eye, so let's call it a day.

  • 🐟格格不入gé gé bù rù

    be out of place; be like a fish out of water — Out of place.

    He felt like a fish out of water among his new colleagues.

Opposite

  • 🤝志同道合zhì tóng dào hé

    like minded — Like-minded.

    She found like-minded friends in the chess club.

  • 🤝同道中人tóng dào zhōng rén

    sisters/brothers in arms; welcome to the club — Kindred spirits.

    You like old maps too? Welcome to the club!
