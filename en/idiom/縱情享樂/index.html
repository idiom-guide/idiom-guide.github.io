3 · Joy & Satisfaction

縱情享樂

zòng qíng xiǎng lè

In English

  • indulge oneself
  • live it up

Meaning

Living it up.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • let one's hair down

Example

他們在節慶中縱情享樂。

They indulged themselves at the festival.

Usage

Neutral to negative; implies a lack of restraint.

Similar

  • 🥂及時行樂jí shí xíng lè

    carpe diem; eat, drink, and be merry — Carpe diem.

    Carpe diem! Let's go out tonight.

  • 🏮燈紅酒綠dēng hóng jiǔ lǜ

    feasting and pleasure seeking — A life of feasting and pleasure.

    The district is a place of feasting and pleasure seeking.

Opposite

  • 🐂吃苦耐勞chī kǔ nài láo

    workhorse — Hardworking and uncomplaining.

    He is a workhorse who never complains.

  • 🧘修身養性xiū shēn yǎng xìng

    spiritual journey — Cultivating one's character.

    He sees his daily walks as part of a spiritual journey.
