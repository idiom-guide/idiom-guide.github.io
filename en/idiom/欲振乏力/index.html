欲振乏力

yù zhèn fá lì

In English

  • lackluster

Meaning

Lackluster.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • struggling to recover
  • run out of steam

Example

經濟欲振乏力，無法恢復元氣。

The economy is lackluster and can't recover its strength.

Usage

For economies, companies, teams and personal form.

Similar

  • 🤕一蹶不振yī jué bù zhèn

    never recover; be unable to get back on one's feet — Never recovering.

    He never recovered after the scandal.

  • 🏋️力有未逮lì yǒu wèi dài

    beyond one's ability; fall short of — Beyond one's ability.

    The task was beyond his ability.

Opposite

  • 🏹蓄勢待發xù shì dài fā

    born ready — Born ready.

    Backstage, the band was poised to start; they were born ready.

  • 🚩重振旗鼓chóng zhèn qí gǔ

    find a floor after rout; regain sb's footing — Regrouping after a setback.

    After the defeat, the team regained its footing and won the next game.
