3 · Good Times & Good Luck

求之不得

qiú zhī bù dé

In English

  • be my guest
  • more than welcome

Meaning

Couldn't ask for more.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • couldn't ask for more

Example

免費的演唱會門票？求之不得！

A free ticket to the concert? More than welcome!

Usage

Colloquial; for gladly accepting something good.

Similar

  • 😊樂意之至lè yì zhī zhì

    gladly; be more than happy to — More than happy to.

    Would you help me move? I'd be more than happy to.

  • 🌠千載難逢qiān zǎi nán féng

    once in a lifetime; sth of a lifetime — Once in a lifetime.

    Meeting my idol was a once-in-a-lifetime chance.

Opposite

  • 😒不情願bù qíng yuàn

    half-hearted (negotiation) — Half-hearted.

    After a half-hearted negotiation, the two sides signed nothing.
