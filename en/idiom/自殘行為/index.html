自殘行為

zì cán xíng wéi

In English

  • self-inflicted wound

Meaning

A self-inflicted wound.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • self-sabotage
  • an own goal

Example

刪減研究經費是一種自殘行為。

Cutting the research budget was a self-inflicted wound.

Usage

Figuratively, for decisions that hurt one's own interests.

Similar

  • 🔫砸自己的腳zá zì jǐ de jiǎo

    shoot yourself in the foot — Shooting oneself in the foot.

    Insulting the host was like shooting yourself in the foot.

  • ⚰️自掘墳墓zì jué fén mù

    dig your own grave — Digging one's own grave.

    By lying to the investors, he dug his own grave.

Opposite

  • 💡明智之舉míng zhì zhī jǔ

    smart play — A wise move.

    Saving some money for emergencies was a smart play.
