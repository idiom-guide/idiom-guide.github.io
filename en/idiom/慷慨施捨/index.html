慷慨施捨

kāng kǎi shī shě

In English

  • sb's largesse

Meaning

Largesse.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • largesse
  • generosity
  • charity

Example

僧侶們靠村民的慷慨施捨過活。

The monks lived on the largesse of the villagers.

Usage

Formal; for donations and relief.

Similar

  • 🧡樂善好施lè shàn hào shī

    good Samaritan — A good Samaritan.

    A good Samaritan paid for the stranger's medical bills.

  • 🦾鼎力相助dǐng lì xiāng zhù

    generous support — Generous support.

    This event would not be possible without your generous support.

Opposite

  • 🤑斤斤計較jīn jīn jì jiào

    petty; calculating — Penny-pinching.

    Don't be so petty about the bill.
