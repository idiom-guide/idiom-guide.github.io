2 · Change & Fate

異軍突起

yì jūn tū qǐ

In English

  • an upstart
  • disruptor
  • emerge as a new force

Meaning

A new force emerging.Neutral · General

Example

一款小型應用程式在市場上異軍突起。

A small app emerged as a new force in the market.

Usage

For startups, newcomers and dark horses in markets, elections and sport.

Similar

  • 🏃後來居上hòu lái jū shàng

    surpass — Latecomers overtaking the leaders.

    The newcomer surpassed the veterans within a year.

  • 🚀一飛沖天yī fēi chōng tiān

    gravity-defying — Soaring to the top.

    The startup's gravity-defying rise stunned investors.

Opposite

  • 🏞️江河日下jiāng hé rì xià

    in steady decline — In steady decline.

    The industry has been in steady decline.

  • 📉一落千丈yī luò qiān zhàng

    plummet; nosedive — A sudden steep fall.

    Sales plummeted after the recall.
