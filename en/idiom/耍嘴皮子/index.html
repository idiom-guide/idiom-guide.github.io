耍嘴皮子

shuǎ zuǐ pí zi

In English

  • talk is cheap

Meaning

Talk is cheap.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • all talk
  • pay lip service

Example

光耍嘴皮子沒用，拿出成績來。

Talk is cheap. Show me the results.

Usage

Colloquial; for criticizing people who talk but don't deliver.

Similar

  • 🎀漂亮話piào liàng huà

    empty words; lip service — Empty words.

    Those are just empty words.

  • 📄紙上談兵zhǐ shàng tán bīng

    armchair general — An armchair general.

    He is an armchair general who has never visited a real battlefield.

Opposite

  • 🤝言行一致yán xíng yī zhì

    practice what one preaches; walk the talk — Walking the talk.

    A good leader practices what he preaches.

  • 👣腳踏實地jiǎo tà shí dì

    staying grounded and pushing ahead — Down-to-earth.

    He is a down-to-earth man who stays grounded and keeps pushing ahead.
