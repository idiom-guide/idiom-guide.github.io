偷工減料

tōu gōng jiǎn liào

In English

  • cut corners
  • skimp on materials

Meaning

Cutting steps and materials to make an improper profit: skimping on quality.Negative · General

Example

這家建商偷工減料，房子不到一年就出現壁癌。

The developer cut corners, and mold appeared on the walls within a year.

Usage

Originally about construction and manufacturing, also used figuratively for doing a job dishonestly. Usually accusatory and aimed at a specific party, such as a developer or supplier.

Similar

  • 🧰粗製濫造cū zhì làn zào

    shoddy; churn out shoddy goods — Mass-produced and shoddy; about poor-quality output.

    Made in a rush to turn a quick profit, the knock-offs were shoddy and broke within days.

  • 🔖以次充好yǐ cì chōng hǎo

    pass off inferior goods; sell shoddy goods as good — Passing inferior goods off as good; about deception.

    Unscrupulous dealers passed off inferior goods as imported ones.

Opposite

  • 💎貨真價實huò zhēn jià shí

    the genuine article — The genuine article, true to its name.

    Customers want to know the product is the genuine article.

  • 🔬精益求精jīng yì qiú jīng

    strive for perfection; keep refining — Perfecting what is already good.

    He keeps refining his work until he is satisfied.
