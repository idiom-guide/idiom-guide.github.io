4 · Incompetence & Failure

重蹈覆轍

chóng dǎo fù zhé

In English

  • repeat the mistakes
  • repeat the same mistakes

Meaning

Repeating the same mistakes.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • history repeats itself

Example

他保證不會重蹈覆轍。

He promised not to repeat the same mistakes.

Usage

Often a warning to learn from past errors.

Similar

  • 🔁積習難改jī xí nán gǎi

    old habits die hard; inveterate — Old habits die hard.

    He is an inveterate gambler and can't quit.

Opposite

  • 📒前車之鑑qián chē zhī jiàn

    a cautionary example; lessons from past mistakes — A lesson from others' mistakes.

    History offers many cautionary examples.

  • 🛀洗心革面xǐ xīn gé miàn

    turn over a new leaf; reform oneself — Turning over a new leaf.

    After his release he turned over a new leaf and started afresh.
