守口如瓶

shǒu kǒu rú píng

In English

  • tight-lipped
  • keep mum

Meaning

Tight-lipped; keeping a secret sealed like a bottle.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • keep one's lips sealed

Example

他對合併案守口如瓶，連家人都不知道。

He stayed tight-lipped about the merger.

Usage

Usually praise for keeping secrets; also for refusing to say anything when questioned.

Similar

  • 🙊絕口不提jué kǒu bù tí

    be reticent about sth — Never mentioning it; deliberate avoidance.

    He remained reticent about his family history.

  • ㊙秘而不宣mì ér bù xuān

    keep it secret; not make it public — Keeping something under wraps.

    The plan is being kept secret for now.

Opposite

  • 📤全盤托出quán pán tuō chū

    lay/put sb's cards on the table; come clean with sb. about sth — Telling everything.

    After hours of questioning, he finally came clean about the missing funds.

  • 😮口無遮攔kǒu wú zhē lán

    sb have no filter; shoot from the lip — Blurting things out without a filter.

    My uncle has no filter; he says whatever pops into his head.
