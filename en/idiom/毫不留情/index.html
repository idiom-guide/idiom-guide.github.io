2 · Competition & Power

毫不留情

háo bù liú qíng

In English

  • ruthless
  • without mercy

Meaning

Without mercy; pulling no punches.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • pull no punches

Example

那位評論家的評論毫不留情。

The critic was ruthless in his review.

Usage

For criticism, competition or law enforcement.

Similar

  • 🦏毫不客氣háo bù kè qì

    unceremoniously — Without ceremony.

    She unceremoniously told the noisy guests to leave.

  • 😤咄咄逼人duō duō bī rén

    confrontational; pugnacious, aggressive — Aggressive.

    The lawyer's confrontational style made the witness nervous.

Opposite

  • 🤙網開一面wǎng kāi yī miàn

    show leniency; give sb a break — Showing leniency.

    The judge showed leniency to the first-time offender.

  • ⚖️從輕發落cóng qīng fā luò

    be treated leniently — Being let off lightly.

    The first-time offender was treated leniently.
