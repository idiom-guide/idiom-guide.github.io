蠅頭小利

yíng tóu xiǎo lì

In English

  • can only buy cabbage and potatoes

Meaning

Peanuts; a pittance.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • a pittance
  • peanuts

Example

他為了蠅頭小利出賣自己的名聲。

He sold his reputation for a sum that could only buy cabbage and potatoes.

Usage

Often where a small gain costs something greater.

Similar

  • 🐞微不足道wēi bù zú dào

    insignificant; trivial — Insignificant.

    The difference is trivial.

Opposite

  • 🎰一本萬利yī běn wàn lì

    a small outlay for a huge return — A small outlay for a huge return.

    There is no such thing as a small outlay for a guaranteed huge return.

  • 💰有利可圖yǒu lì kě tú

    lucrative; promising — Lucrative.

    Selling coffee near the station proved lucrative.
