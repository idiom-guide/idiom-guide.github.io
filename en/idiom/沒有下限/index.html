沒有下限

méi yǒu xià xiàn

In English

  • incredible

Meaning

Having no limits; shameless.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • no bottom line
  • know no shame

Example

這個詐騙犯沒有下限，毫無羞恥心。

The fraudster's lack of shame was incredible; he had no bottom line.

Usage

Colloquial and common online.

Similar

  • 🌋肆無忌憚sì wú jì dàn

    with no restraint; unbridled — Unbridled.

    The bully acted with no restraint.

  • 😈卑鄙下流bēi bǐ xià liú

    despicable; contemptible — Despicable.

    That was a despicable trick.

Opposite

  • ⭐核心原則hé xīn yuán zé

    cardinal principle — A cardinal principle.

    Honesty is the cardinal principle of good journalism.

  • ☀️光明磊落guāng míng lěi luò

    scrupulous; upright — Open and aboveboard.

    He is a scrupulous businessman who always follows the rules.
