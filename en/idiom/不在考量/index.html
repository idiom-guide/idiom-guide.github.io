不在考量

bù zài kǎo liáng

In English

  • off the table
  • not under consideration

Meaning

Off the table.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • not an option

Example

裁員目前不在考量之內。

Layoffs are off the table for now.

Usage

Common in negotiations and policy statements; softer than saying "we won't consider it."

Similar

  • 🙅‍♂️沒得商量méi de shāng liang

    non-negotiable; not up for discussion — Non-negotiable.

    The deadline is non-negotiable.

  • 🙅打消主意dǎ xiāo zhǔ yì

    give up the idea; think better of it — Giving up the idea.

    He gave up the idea of moving abroad.

Opposite

  • 1️⃣當務之急dāng wù zhī jí

    top priority; priority one — The top priority.

    Fixing the leak is our top priority.

  • 💬開放討論kāi fàng tǎo lùn

    open to interpretation; be open-minded — Open for discussion.

    The proposal is open for discussion, and we are open-minded about changes.
