衣冠楚楚

yī guān chǔ chǔ

In English

  • dapper
  • impeccably dressed

Meaning

Neat hats and clothes, smartly turned out.Positive · General

Example

他穿著西裝，衣冠楚楚地出席面試。

He turned up at the interview looking dapper in a suit and tie.

Usage

Mostly for a man in neat, respectable clothes. Sometimes ironic, hinting that only the outside is respectable.

Similar

  • 💅光鮮亮麗guāng xiān liàng lì

    glamorous; dressed to the nines — Dressed brightly and attractively; about glamor.

    She is always glamorously dressed.

  • 🕴️儀表堂堂yí biǎo táng táng

    distinguished-looking; of imposing bearing — Dignified in looks; about presence.

    The new ambassador is a distinguished-looking man in his fifties.

Opposite

  • 🧵衣衫襤褸yī shān lán lǚ

    in rags; ragged — Clothes in tatters.

    A ragged boy stood at the door, begging for food.

  • 🧔不修邊幅bù xiū biān fú

    scruffy, slovenly, disheveled, unkempt, slob — Careless about one's appearance.

    He turned up scruffy and unshaven for the interview.
