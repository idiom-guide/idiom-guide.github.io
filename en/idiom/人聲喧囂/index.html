人聲喧囂

rén shēng xuān xiāo

In English

  • bustling

Meaning

Bustling.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • noisy and crowded
  • a hive of activity

Example

這個市場從清晨就人聲喧囂。

The bustling market was loud from dawn.

Usage

For markets, streets and stations.

Similar

  • 🚦熙來攘往xī lái rǎng wǎng

    hustle and bustle — Hustle and bustle.

    The hustle and bustle of the night market never stops.

  • 👥人山人海rén shān rén hǎi

    huge crowds of faces; sea of people — A sea of people.

    A vast crowd filled the square for the fireworks.

Opposite

  • 🦗鴉雀無聲yā què wú shēng

    be dead silent; you could hear a pin drop — Dead silence.

    After the result was announced, the hall fell dead silent.
