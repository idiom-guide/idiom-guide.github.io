陰錯陽差

yīn cuò yáng chā

In English

  • in a strange circumstance
  • for certain reasons

Meaning

By a strange twist of circumstance.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • by a strange coincidence
  • as luck would have it

Example

陰錯陽差，我們竟訂了同一晚的同一間房。

By a strange coincidence, we booked the same room for the same night.

Usage

For mix-ups, coincidences and unexpected outcomes, good or bad.

Similar

  • 🎭造化弄人zào huà nòng rén

    a twist of fate; ironically — A twist of fate; often rueful.

    In a twist of fate, they met again thirty years later at the same station.

  • 🎳歪打正著wāi dǎ zhèng zháo

    stumble into success; by a lucky accident — Stumbling into success.

    She stumbled into success by a lucky accident.

Opposite

  • 🔮不出所料bù chū suǒ liào

    predictably — Predictably.

    Predictably, the train was late again on the first day of school.

  • 📓意料中事yì liào zhōng shì

    be in the playbook — Part of the playbook.

    Blaming the previous manager is straight out of the playbook, so nobody was surprised.
