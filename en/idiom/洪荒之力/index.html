洪荒之力

hóng huāng zhī lì

In English

  • at full strength

Meaning

Every ounce of strength.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • every ounce of strength
  • go all out

Example

這一季，球隊拿出了洪荒之力。

The team is at full strength this season.

Usage

Internet slang; for giving something everything one has.

Similar

  • 🏃全力以赴quán lì yǐ fù

    go all out — Going all out.

    The whole team went all out to finish before the deadline.

  • 🔋精力旺盛jīng lì wàng shèng

    powerhouse — Bursting with energy.

    The new striker is a powerhouse on the field.

Opposite

  • 🔋欲振乏力yù zhèn fá lì

    lackluster — Lackluster.

    The economy is lackluster and can't recover its strength.

  • 🥱無精打采wú jīng dǎ cǎi

    listless; lethargic — Listless.

    He looked listless after the long trip.
