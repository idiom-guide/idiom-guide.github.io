聰明才智

cōng míng cái zhì

In English

  • intelligence and wit
  • sharp mind

Meaning

Intelligence and talent.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • brains and talent

Example

她的聰明才智贏得了大家的心。

Her intelligence and wit won everyone over.

Usage

Usually a noun phrase, as in 發揮聰明才智, 'make full use of one's talents'.

Similar

  • 💡聰明絕頂cōng míng jué dǐng

    very clever; over smart — Extremely clever.

    He is very clever, even a little over-smart.

  • 🧠很有慧根hěn yǒu huì gēn

    talented — A quick study.

    The teacher says the boy is talented and picks up ideas fast.

Opposite

  • 🙉不學無術bù xué wú shù

    ignoramus; uninformed — Ignorant and unskilled.

    The ignoramus has never opened a book.

  • 🈳一竅不通yī qiào bù tōng

    all Greek to me — All Greek to me.

    Quantum physics is all Greek to me.
