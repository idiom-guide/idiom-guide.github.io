口說無憑

kǒu shuō wú píng

In English

  • a verbal promise is no guarantee

Meaning

A verbal promise is no guarantee.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • put it in writing
  • talk is cheap

Example

口說無憑，請寫成書面。

A verbal promise is no guarantee: please put it in writing.

Usage

For asking for a written contract or proof.

Similar

  • 👅空口無憑kōng kǒu wú píng

    words alone prove nothing — Nothing but words.

    Words alone prove nothing; please give me written proof.

  • 👄耍嘴皮子shuǎ zuǐ pí zi

    talk is cheap — Talk is cheap.

    Talk is cheap. Show me the results.

Opposite

  • 📄白紙黑字bái zhǐ hēi zì

    in black and white — In black and white.

    I'd like the terms in black and white.

  • 📇有憑有據yǒu píng yǒu jù

    be well grounded; be backed by evidence — Backed by evidence.

    His accusation is backed by evidence; it's not empty talk.
