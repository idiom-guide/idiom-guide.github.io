12 · Duration & Speed

刻不容緩

kè bù róng huǎn

In English

  • urgent
  • brook no delay

Meaning

Urgent.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • there's no time to lose

Example

氣候行動刻不容緩。

Climate action is urgent.

Usage

For policy, rescue and crisis response.

Similar

  • ⏰迫在眉睫pò zài méi jié

    time is a luxury we don't have; sth. is looming — Looming.

    The deadline is looming, and time is a luxury we don't have.

  • ⏰事不宜遲shì bù yí chí

    there's no time to lose — No time to lose.

    There's no time to lose, so call the doctor.

Opposite

  • 🌅來日方長lái rì fāng cháng

    there will be many more opportunities ahead — Many more opportunities ahead.

    There will be many more opportunities ahead to work together.

  • 🐌曠日持久kuàng rì chí jiǔ

    protracted — Protracted.

    The lawsuit turned into a protracted battle.
