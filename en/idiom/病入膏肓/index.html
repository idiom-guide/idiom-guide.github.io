病入膏肓

bìng rù gāo huāng

In English

  • beyond cure
  • terminally ill

Meaning

Beyond cure.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • past saving

Example

經濟似乎已病入膏肓。

The economy seems beyond cure.

Usage

From the Zuo Zhuan.

Similar

  • 🩺無力回天wú lì huí tiān

    can't be fixed — Beyond saving.

    The old bridge can't be fixed.

  • 💔難以挽回nán yǐ wǎn huí

    irreparable — Irreparable.

    The damage to the forest is irreparable.

Opposite

  • 💓起死回生qǐ sǐ huí shēng

    bring back from the dead; a miraculous recovery — Brought back from the dead.

    The new owner's plan brought the company back from the dead.

  • 💊對症下藥duì zhèng xià yào

    a targeted remedy; address the root cause — A targeted remedy.

    Without reliable data, the government cannot offer a targeted remedy.
