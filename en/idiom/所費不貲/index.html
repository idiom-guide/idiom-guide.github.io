所費不貲

suǒ fèi bù zī

In English

  • expensive

Meaning

Costly.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • costly
  • cost a fortune

Example

出國留學所費不貲，花費很快就累積起來。

Studying abroad is expensive, and the costs add up quickly.

Usage

Formal; 貲 means to count, so it literally means "too costly to count."

Similar

  • 💸代價不菲dài jià bù fēi

    sth can be costly — Coming at a high price.

    The mistake could prove costly.

  • 💲漫天要價màn tiān yào jià

    ask a sky-high price — Asking a sky-high price.

    The seller asked a sky-high price, expecting to be talked down.

Opposite

  • 🏷️物美價廉wù měi jià lián

    good value for money — Good value for money.

    The brand built its name on good value for money.

  • 🛒薄利多銷bó lì duō xiāo

    low margins, high volume — Small margins, big volume.

    Discount retailers live on low margins and high volume.
