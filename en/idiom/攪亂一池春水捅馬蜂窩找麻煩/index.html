攪亂一池春水 / 捅馬蜂窩 / 找麻煩

jiǎo luàn yī chí chūn shuǐ / tǒng mǎ fēng wō / zhǎo má fán

In English

  • stir up a hornets' nest

Meaning

Stirring up a hornets' nest.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • rock the boat
  • open a can of worms

Example

他的言論在辦公室裡攪亂一池春水。

His comments stirred up a hornets' nest at the office.

Usage

The three forms differ in tone: 攪亂一池春水 is more literary, the other two colloquial.

Similar

  • 📢大放厥詞dà fàng jué cí

    spout nonsense; fling wild accusations — Spouting nonsense.

    He spouted nonsense on live TV.

Opposite

  • 🤫息事寧人xī shì níng rén

    smooth things over — Smoothing things over.

    The manager apologized simply to smooth things over.

  • 🕊️調解爭端tiáo jiě zhēng duān

    pour oil on troubled waters — Pouring oil on troubled waters.

    The mayor poured oil on troubled waters by meeting both sides.
