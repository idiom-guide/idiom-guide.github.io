美人計

měi rén jì

In English

  • a honey trap

Meaning

A honey trap.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • seduction ploy
  • sexpionage

Example

那名間諜中了美人計。

The spy fell into a honey trap.

Usage

One of the Thirty-Six Stratagems; for spy, political or business stories.

Similar

  • 🎣欲擒故縱yù qín gù zòng

    give sb enough rope; let sb go in order to catch them later — Letting go in order to catch later.

    He let the suspect go on purpose, giving him enough rope.

  • 🕵️暗渡陳倉àn dù chén cāng

    take a covert route; feint to the east and strike to the west — Taking a covert route.

    The general feinted to the east and struck to the west.

Opposite

  • ⬜光明正大guāng míng zhèng dà

    fair and square — Fair and square.

    We won the contest fair and square.

  • 🔓開誠布公kāi chéng bù gōng

    in full transparency — Open and frank.

    The two leaders spoke in full transparency about their differences.
