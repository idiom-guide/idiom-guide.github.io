卷土重來

juǎn tǔ chóng lái

In English

  • resurgent + n

Meaning

Coming back with a vengeance.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • stage a comeback
  • come back with a vengeance

Example

這個政黨想在下次選舉中卷土重來。

The resurgent party is trying to stage a comeback in the next election.

Usage

From Du Mu's poem on Wujiang Pavilion; also written 捲土重來; can be ominous, as with a returning epidemic.

Similar

  • ⛰️東山再起dōng shān zài qǐ

    make a comeback — Making a comeback; usually of people.

    After a long absence, the singer made a comeback.

  • 🚩重振旗鼓chóng zhèn qí gǔ

    find a floor after rout; regain sb's footing — Regrouping after defeat.

    After the defeat, the team regained its footing and won the next game.

Opposite

  • 🤕一蹶不振yī jué bù zhèn

    never recover; be unable to get back on one's feet — Never recovering.

    He never recovered after the scandal.

  • 🚩偃旗息鼓yǎn qí xī gǔ

    call a halt; lie low — Calling a halt; lying low.

    The band announced they would call a halt for now: a pause, they said, not a farewell.
