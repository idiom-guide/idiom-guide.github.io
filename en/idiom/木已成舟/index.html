木已成舟

mù yǐ chéng zhōu

In English

  • ship has sailed

Meaning

What's done is done.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • what's done is done
  • too late to change

Example

現在抱怨已經太遲了，木已成舟。

It's too late to complain; the ship has sailed.

Usage

Often used to urge someone to accept the facts.

Similar

  • 🥛覆水難收fù shuǐ nán shōu

    what's done cannot be undone — No use crying over spilt milk; often of feelings or decisions.

    What's done can't be undone, and spoken words can't be taken back.

  • 💔難以挽回nán yǐ wǎn huí

    irreparable — Irreparable; stresses the damage.

    The damage to the forest is irreparable.

Opposite

  • 🎈懸而未決xuán ér wèi jué

    sth be still up in the air; sth be up in the air — Still up in the air.

    The decision is still up in the air.

  • 🎱前途未卜qián tú wèi bǔ

    it's anyone's guess how — Anyone's guess.

    It's anyone's guess how the economy will look next year.
