無所遁形

wú suǒ dùn xíng

In English

  • nowhere to escape

Meaning

Nowhere to hide.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • nowhere to hide
  • exposed

Example

在攝影機下，小偷無所遁形。

Under the cameras, the thieves had nowhere to escape.

Usage

For surveillance, exposure and the truth coming out.

Similar

  • ⭕層層包圍céng céng bāo wéi

    close in on sth — Surrounded.

    Police closed in on the building from every side.

  • 🚥一目了然yī mù liǎo rán

    be clear at a glance — Clear at a glance.

    The chart is clear at a glance.

Opposite

  • 👻神不知鬼不覺shén bù zhī guǐ bù jué

    perfectly undetectable; covert — Without anyone noticing.

    He slipped out of the party without being noticed.

  • 🔓逍遙法外xiāo yáo fǎ wài

    at large — Still at large.

    The fraudster is still at large.
