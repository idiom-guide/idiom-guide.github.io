純屬臆測

chún shǔ yì cè

In English

  • guesswork
  • speculate

Meaning

Pure speculation.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • be pure guesswork
  • be mere speculation
  • be purely conjecture

Example

關於他辭職的原因，目前純屬臆測。

Any talk about why he resigned is pure guesswork.

Usage

Used to dismiss rumors or unsupported claims; common in official statements and news.

Similar

  • 🎐捕風捉影bǔ fēng zhuō yǐng

    chase shadows; act on groundless rumors — Chasing shadows; about rumors.

    These reports are groundless speculation with no evidence behind them.

  • 🈚無憑無據wú píng wú jù

    without any basis; with no evidence — Without any evidence.

    It isn't fair to accuse people without any evidence.

Opposite

  • 🗄鐵證如山tiě zhèng rú shān

    the evidence is irrefutable; the evidence is overwhelming — Evidence as solid as a mountain.

    The evidence is irrefutable; he can no longer argue his way out.

  • 📇有憑有據yǒu píng yǒu jù

    be well grounded; be backed by evidence — Backed by evidence.

    His accusation is backed by evidence; it's not empty talk.
