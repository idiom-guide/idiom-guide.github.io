下定決心

xià dìng jué xīn

In English

  • set sb's sights on sth
  • be hell bent on n

Meaning

Making up one's mind.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • make up one's mind
  • set one's sights on
  • be hell-bent on

Example

她下定決心要當法官，從此不再回頭。

She set her sights on becoming a judge and never looked back.

Usage

Often followed by 要…… (to do something); the decision is firm.

Similar

  • ⚓吃了秤砣鐵了心chī le chèng tuó tiě le xīn

    dead set; dig in one's heels — Dead set on it.

    He's dead set on quitting his job.

  • ⛵破釜沉舟pò fǔ chén zhōu

    (we've) burned the boats; reaching a point of no return — Burning one's boats.

    We've burned the boats; there is no going back now.

Opposite

  • 🤷躊躇不定chóu chú bù dìng

    vacillate between sth — Vacillating.

    She vacillated between the two job offers for weeks.

  • 🤔猶豫不決yóu yù bù jué

    dilly-dally — Unable to make up one's mind.

    Stop dilly-dallying and make a decision.
