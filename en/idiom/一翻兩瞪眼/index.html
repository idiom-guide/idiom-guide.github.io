一翻兩瞪眼

yī fān liǎng dèng yǎn

In English

  • coin flip
  • it's here or nowhere
  • it's a coin toss

Meaning

All or nothing; decided in one go.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • a coin toss
  • all or nothing
  • win or lose on one throw

Example

現在是一翻兩瞪眼：不是拿下合約，就是全盤皆輸。

It's a coin toss now: either we win the contract or we lose everything.

Usage

Colloquial; for gambling, bidding or competition.

Similar

  • 🎲孤注一擲gū zhù yī zhì

    a desperate play — A desperate all-or-nothing bet.

    Selling his house to fund the film was a desperate play.

  • 🔘非此即彼fēi cǐ jí bǐ

    either-or; one or the other — Either this or that.

    Don't see it as either-or; there is a third possibility.

Opposite

  • ↔️轉圜空間zhuǎn huán kōng jiān

    room to maneuver; wiggle room — Room to maneuver.

    There is little room to maneuver in the negotiations.

  • 🎈懸而未決xuán ér wèi jué

    sth be still up in the air; sth be up in the air — Still undecided.

    The decision is still up in the air.
