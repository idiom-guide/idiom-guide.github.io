迴光返照

huí guāng fǎn zhào

In English

  • a false dawn

Meaning

A last flicker before the end; a false dawn.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • a last hurrah
  • a final rally

Example

公司業績突然跳升，只是迴光返照。

The company's sudden jump in sales was only a false dawn.

Usage

Originally of a dying person's brief rally; now of a short-lived revival before collapse.

Similar

  • 🌸曇花一現tán huā yī xiàn

    temporary; short-lived — A flash in the pan, not necessarily before an end.

    Their hit song was a flash in the pan.

  • 🔋欲振乏力yù zhèn fá lì

    lackluster — Lackluster; unable to rally.

    The economy is lackluster and can't recover its strength.

Opposite

  • 💓起死回生qǐ sǐ huí shēng

    bring back from the dead; a miraculous recovery — A genuine recovery from the brink.

    The new owner's plan brought the company back from the dead.

  • 🌳欣欣向榮xīn xīn xiàng róng

    thriving; booming — Thriving.

    The local startup scene is thriving.
