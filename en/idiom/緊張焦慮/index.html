緊張焦慮

jǐn zhāng jiāo lǜ

In English

  • fraught
  • grows more fraught

Meaning

Fraught; tense and anxious.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • tense and anxious
  • on edge

Example

房間裡的氣氛越來越緊張焦慮。

The atmosphere in the room grew more fraught by the hour.

Usage

For moods and atmospheres.

Similar

  • 😟忐忑不安tǎn tè bù ān

    anxious; on edge — Perturbed.

    She was perturbed by the strange noise downstairs.

  • 😔心事重重xīn shì chóng chóng

    preoccupied; weighed down by worries — Weighed down by worries.

    He seemed preoccupied all day.

Opposite

  • 🧖心平氣和xīn píng qì hé

    be calm and composed; keep one's temper — Calm and even-tempered.

    Let's talk calmly and keep our tempers in check.

  • 🌄心曠神怡xīn kuàng shén yí

    completely relaxed and happy — Refreshed and happy.

    A walk by the lake leaves me completely relaxed and happy.
