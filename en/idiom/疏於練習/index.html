疏於練習

shū yú liàn xí

In English

  • out of practice

Meaning

Out of practice.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • rusty
  • lose one's touch

Example

我疏於練習，鋼琴彈得生疏，請見諒。

I'm out of practice, so forgive my rusty piano playing.

Usage

Often modest, as an apology for rusty skills.

Similar

  • 🌤️一曝十寒yī pù shí hán

    sporadic effort — Sporadic effort.

    His sporadic effort won't get him anywhere.

Opposite

  • 🎹熟能生巧shú néng shēng qiǎo

    practice makes perfect — Practice makes perfect.

    Practice makes perfect: sight-translate a page every day.

  • 🚗駕輕就熟jià qīng jiù shú

    mastering; getting into one's stride — On familiar ground.

    After a month, she was getting into her stride at the new job.
