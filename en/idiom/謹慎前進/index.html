謹慎前進

jǐn shèn qián jìn

In English

  • proceed with caution

Meaning

Proceeding with caution.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • tread carefully
  • go slow

Example

路面結冰，我們謹慎前進。

On the icy road, we proceeded with caution.

Usage

Literally on the road, or figuratively for plans and reforms.

Similar

  • 🐾謹慎行動jǐn shèn xíng dòng

    tread carefully — Treading carefully.

    In unfamiliar markets, it pays to tread carefully.

  • 🦶投石問路tóu shí wèn lù

    put a toe in the water; a tentative step toward — Testing the waters.

    The company put a toe in the water by opening a small shop first.

Opposite

  • ☠️冒險玩命mào xiǎn wán mìng

    tempt fate — Tempting fate.

    Driving through this storm is tempting fate.

  • ➡️長驅直入cháng qū zhí rù

    penetrate — Driving deep without resistance.

    The army was able to penetrate deep into enemy territory without resistance.
