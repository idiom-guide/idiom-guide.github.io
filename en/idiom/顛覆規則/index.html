2 · Change & Fate

顛覆規則

diān fù guī zé

In English

  • game changer

Meaning

A game changer.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • rewrite the rules
  • disrupt

Example

對音樂產業而言，智慧型手機是一次顛覆規則的變革。

The smartphone was a game changer for the music industry.

Usage

For technology, business and industry shake-ups.

Similar

  • 🎖️異軍突起yì jūn tū qǐ

    an upstart; disruptor — A disruptor emerging.

    A small app emerged as a new force in the market.

  • 🌋翻天覆地fān tiān fù dì

    a sea change in sth; tectonic shift(s) — A sea change.

    The internet brought a sea change in how we shop.

Opposite

  • 🚋墨守成規mò shǒu chéng guī

    stick to old rules; stay in a rut — Sticking to the old rules.

    The company sticks to old rules and finds it hard to innovate.

  • 🍪蕭規曹隨xiāo guī cáo suí

    cookie cutter — Following precedent.

    Instead of making changes, the new mayor kept the old policies in cookie-cutter fashion.
