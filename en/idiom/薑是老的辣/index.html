薑是老的辣

jiāng shì lǎo de là

In English

  • experience counts
  • there's no substitute for experience

Meaning

Experience counts.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • old hands know best

Example

薑是老的辣，那位老手幾分鐘就解決了。

Experience counts: the veteran solved it in minutes.

Usage

Colloquial; for praising seasoned people.

Similar

  • 🐎老馬識途lǎo mǎ shí tú

    elder wisdom — Elder wisdom.

    The old guide knew the mountain paths, and his elder wisdom saved the day.

  • 🖌️有兩把刷子yǒu liǎng bǎ shuā zi

    know one's stuff; have real skill — Knowing how to do it.

    That mechanic has some secret sauce: he fixes any engine in minutes.

Opposite

  • 🐣職場菜鳥zhí chǎng cài niǎo

    be new to the workforce — A rookie.

    I'm new to the workforce, so please be patient with me.
