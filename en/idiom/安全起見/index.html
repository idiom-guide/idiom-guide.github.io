安全起見

ān quán qǐ jiàn

In English

  • to be on the safe side

Meaning

To be on the safe side.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • just in case
  • for safety's sake

Example

安全起見，帶把傘吧。

To be on the safe side, take an umbrella.

Usage

Often at the start of a sentence, followed by a precaution.

Similar

  • ☂️未雨綢繆wèi yǔ chóu móu

    be fully prepared; provident — Preparing for a rainy day.

    Keep an emergency kit at home; it pays to be fully prepared.

  • 🥚小心翼翼xiǎo xīn yì yì

    tread carefully; be extremely careful — Extremely careful.

    He chose his words very carefully to avoid trouble.

Opposite

  • ☠️冒險玩命mào xiǎn wán mìng

    tempt fate — Tempting fate.

    Driving through this storm is tempting fate.

  • 🧗鋌而走險tǐng ér zǒu xiǎn

    put one's neck on the line; take a desperate gamble — Taking a desperate gamble.

    Desperate for cash, he took a desperate gamble.
