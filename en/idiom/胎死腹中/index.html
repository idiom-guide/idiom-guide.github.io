胎死腹中

tāi sǐ fù zhōng

In English

  • stillborn
  • die on the vine

Meaning

Dying on the vine.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • never get off the ground

Example

這項計畫胎死腹中。

The project died on the vine.

Usage

For plans, bills and joint ventures.

Similar

  • ⏹️戛然而止jiá rán ér zhǐ

    come to an abrupt halt; stop short — Stopping abruptly.

    The music came to an abrupt halt.

  • 💨無疾而終wú jí ér zhōng

    fizzle out; come to nothing — Fizzling out.

    The peace talks fizzled out.

Opposite

  • 💧水到渠成shuǐ dào qú chéng

    when time is ripe; fall into place — Falling into place.

    When the time is ripe, things will fall into place.
