是可忍，孰不可忍

shì kě rěn, shú bù kě rěn

In English

  • I'm fed up with this

Meaning

Fed up.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • enough is enough
  • the last straw

Example

我忍了好幾年，是可忍，孰不可忍。

I've put up with this for years, and I'm fed up with it.

Usage

From the Analects; for when one can take no more.

Opposite

  • 🙇‍♀️忍氣吞聲rěn qì tūn shēng

    swallow one's anger; grin and bear it — Swallowing one's anger.

    Wronged as he was, he could only swallow his anger.
