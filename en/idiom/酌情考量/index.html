酌情考量

zhuó qíng kǎo liáng

In English

  • at your discretion

Meaning

At one's discretion.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • at one's discretion
  • case by case
  • take circumstances into account

Example

逾期費用可由經理酌情考量是否減免。

Late fees may be waived at the manager's discretion.

Usage

Common in rules and notices; leaves room for flexibility.

Similar

  • 🎚️因人而異yīn rén ér yì

    vary from person to person; depend on the individual — It depends on the person.

    How long it takes to learn a language varies from person to person.

  • 🧩因地制宜yīn dì zhì yí

    adapt to local conditions — Adapting to local conditions.

    Policies should be adapted to local conditions.

Opposite

  • 🗜一概而論yī gài ér lùn

    lump everything together; generalize — Lumping everything together.

    Everyone's situation is different, so we can't lump them together.

  • 💼公事公辦gōng shì gōng bàn

    business is business; transactional — Going by the book.

    Sorry, it's nothing personal; business is business.
