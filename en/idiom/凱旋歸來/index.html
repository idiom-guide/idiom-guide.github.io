凱旋歸來

kǎi xuán guī lái

In English

  • return victorious

Meaning

Returning victorious.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • come home in triumph
  • a hero's welcome

Example

球隊從巴黎凱旋歸來。

The team returned victorious from Paris.

Usage

For teams, troops and delegations.

Similar

  • 🏆光宗耀祖guāng zōng yào zǔ

    bring honor to the family — Bringing honor to the family.

    He hoped his success would bring honor to the family.

  • 🏆功成名就gōng chéng míng jiù

    achieve fame and success; have it made — Fame and success.

    After years of work, she finally achieved fame and success.

Opposite

  • 🏳️鎩羽而歸shā yǔ ér guī

    return in defeat; come back empty-handed — Returning in defeat.

    The explorers returned in defeat.

  • 🤕一敗塗地yī bài tú dì

    fall flat on your face — Falling flat on one's face.

    The campaign fell flat on its face.
