荒謬可笑

huāng miù kě xiào

In English

  • ludicrous, ridiculous

Meaning

Ludicrous.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • ludicrous
  • ridiculous
  • laughable

Example

他的藉口荒謬可笑。

His excuse was ludicrous.

Usage

For excuses, claims and proposals.

Similar

  • 👾荒誕不經huāng dàn bù jīng

    be absurd; be preposterous — Outlandish.

    The story is absurd, and nobody will believe it.

  • 🤦太離譜了tài lí pǔ le

    egregious; way out of line — Way out of line.

    It's an egregious mistake to ignore the safety warnings.

Opposite

  • 🆗合情合理hé qíng hé lǐ

    be fair and reasonable; make perfect sense — Fair and sensible.

    The company's compensation plan was fair and reasonable, and everyone could accept it.

  • 🙂情理之中qíng lǐ zhī zhōng

    sth is expected — Expected; reasonable.

    Given how hard she trained, her victory was only to be expected.
