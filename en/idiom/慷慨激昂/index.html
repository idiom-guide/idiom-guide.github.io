慷慨激昂

kāng kǎi jī áng

In English

  • impassioned (speech)
  • passionate

Meaning

Impassioned; fired with righteous fervor.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • impassioned
  • stirring

Example

他慷慨激昂地發表演說，為圖書館請命。

He gave an impassioned speech in defense of the library.

Usage

For speeches, debates and songs; implies passion with a sense of justice.

Similar

  • 🥵熱血沸騰rè xuè fèi téng

    be fired up; be stirred with passion — Fired up inside; 慷慨激昂 is about the way of speaking.

    When the national anthem played, everyone was stirred with passion.

  • 😠忿忿不平fèn fèn bù píng

    indignant; aggrieved — Indignant at an injustice.

    The fans were indignant about the referee's decision.

Opposite

  • 🧖心平氣和xīn píng qì hé

    be calm and composed; keep one's temper — Calm and composed.

    Let's talk calmly and keep our tempers in check.

  • 😐不動聲色bù dòng shēng sè

    keep a poker face; show no emotion — Keeping a poker face.

    He kept a poker face and quietly got the plan ready.
