言行不一

yán xíng bù yī

In English

  • words and deeds are inconsistent
  • never practises what he preaches
  • hypocrite
  • contradicting oneself

Meaning

Not practicing what one preaches.Negative · General

Example

他言行不一，從不實踐自己說的話。

He never practices what he preaches.

Usage

For criticizing people who say one thing and do another.

Similar

  • 🎭心口不一xīn kǒu bù yī

    disingenuous (slightly dishonest); disingenuous — Disingenuous.

    He claims to support the plan but quietly works against it, which is disingenuous.

  • 🤫陽奉陰違yáng fèng yīn wéi

    pay lip service while secretly defying — Paying lip service.

    He paid lip service to the policy while secretly defying it.

Opposite

  • 🤝言行一致yán xíng yī zhì

    practice what one preaches; walk the talk — Walking the talk.

    A good leader practices what he preaches.

  • 🤞講話負責jiǎng huà fù zé

    be true to one's word; stand by one's words — Standing by one's words.

    A leader should stand by his words.

Also

to assert one policy and practice another
