人盡其才

rén jìn qí cái

In English

  • make the most of everyone's talents

Meaning

Making the most of everyone's talents.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • put talent to good use
  • the right person for the job

Example

新的組織架構讓人盡其才。

The new structure makes the most of everyone's talents.

Usage

Formal; for staffing and management, often paired with 物盡其用.

Similar

  • 🔑知人善任zhī rén shàn rèn

    put the right people in the right jobs — Putting the right people in the right jobs.

    A good manager puts the right people in the right jobs.

  • ♻️物盡其用wù jìn qí yòng

    make the best/good use of — Making good use of everything.

    She makes good use of every scrap of fabric.

Opposite

  • 🪑人浮於事rén fú yú shì

    overstaffed — Overstaffed.

    The agency is overstaffed and slow.
