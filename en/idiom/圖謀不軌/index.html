4 · Character & Conduct

圖謀不軌

tú móu bù guǐ

In English

  • conspire
  • nefarious intent(s)

Meaning

Up to no good.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • up to no good

Example

有人看見那兩個男人在附近徘徊，圖謀不軌。

The two men were seen lurking with nefarious intent.

Usage

For crimes, plots and suspicious behavior.

Similar

  • 🕳️居心叵測jū xīn pǒ cè

    have an ulterior motive — An ulterior motive.

    I suspect he has an ulterior motive.

  • 🕵️別有用心bié yǒu yòng xīn

    ulterior motives — Hidden agendas.

    I suspect he has ulterior motives for being so nice.

Opposite

  • ⬜光明正大guāng míng zhèng dà

    fair and square — Fair and square.

    We won the contest fair and square.

  • 💝好心好意hǎo xīn hǎo yì

    well-meaning; well-intentioned — Well-meaning.

    She was well-meaning but clumsy.
