戰功彪炳

zhàn gōng biāo bǐng

In English

  • a distinguished war record

Meaning

A distinguished war record.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • decorated
  • a stellar track record

Example

這位將軍戰功彪炳。

The general had a distinguished war record.

Usage

For generals and soldiers; figuratively for a winning track record.

Similar

  • ⚔️身經百戰shēn jīng bǎi zhàn

    battle-hardened — Battle-hardened.

    The battle-hardened sergeant stayed calm under fire.

  • 🏆功成名就gōng chéng míng jiù

    achieve fame and success; have it made — Fame and success.

    After years of work, she finally achieved fame and success.

Opposite

  • 🏳️潰不成軍kuì bù chéng jūn

    in full rout — In full rout.

    The invaders were in full rout by dawn.

  • 🤕一敗塗地yī bài tú dì

    fall flat on your face — Falling flat on one's face.

    The campaign fell flat on its face.
