放馬過來

fàng mǎ guò lái

In English

  • bring it on

Meaning

Bring it on.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • come and get me
  • I'm ready for you

Example

放馬過來，我準備好了。

Bring it on. I'm ready.

Usage

A confident boast when issuing or accepting a challenge.

Similar

  • 🥊不服來戰bù fú lái zhàn

    fight me — Fight me.

    You think you can beat me? Fight me!

  • 🥊正面交鋒zhèng miàn jiāo fēng

    go toe-to-toe — Going toe-to-toe.

    The two candidates went toe-to-toe in the debate.

Opposite

  • 🏃臨陣脫逃lín zhèn tuō táo

    shy from battle — Running away when the time comes.

    The goalkeeper shied from battle at the last moment.

  • 🙇伏首稱臣fú shǒu chēng chén

    submit to; bow before — Bowing in submission.

    The rebels finally bowed before the king.
