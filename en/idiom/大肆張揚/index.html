大肆張揚

dà sì zhāng yáng

In English

  • fanfare

Meaning

With great fanfare.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • make a big song and dance
  • trumpet

Example

市長大肆張揚地宣布開幕。

The mayor announced the opening with great fanfare.

Usage

Slightly negative, implying excessive publicity.

Similar

  • 🚩大張旗鼓dà zhāng qí gǔ

    with great fanfare — With great fanfare.

    The mayor launched the campaign with great fanfare.

  • 🎬盛大登場shèng dà dēng chǎng

    make an entrance; grand entrance — A grand entrance.

    The band made a grand entrance, riding in on motorcycles.

Opposite

  • 🐭不出鋒頭bù chū fēng tóu

    unassuming — Unassuming.

    He is an unassuming man who never seeks the spotlight.

  • 👻神不知鬼不覺shén bù zhī guǐ bù jué

    perfectly undetectable; covert — Without anyone noticing.

    He slipped out of the party without being noticed.
