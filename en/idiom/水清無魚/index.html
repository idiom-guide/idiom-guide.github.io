水清無魚

shuǐ qīng wú yú

In English

  • In clear water you catch no fish

Meaning

Water that is too clear has no fish: expect perfection and no one will stay with you.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • no fish in water that is too clear
  • if you are too strict, you will end up with no followers
  • perfectionism backfires

Example

水清無魚，你對團隊要求太嚴，到頭來沒人願意跟你合作。

A boss who tolerates no flaw will end up with no team; in clear water you catch no fish.

Usage

Often paired with 人察無徒; used to tell people not to be too demanding.

Similar

  • 🐛吹毛求疵chuī máo qiú cī

    nitpick; find fault with trivial things — Nitpicking; an action, while 水清無魚 states the consequence.

    He is always nitpicking, so his colleagues avoid him.

  • 🈯求全責備qiú quán zé bèi

    demand perfection — Demanding perfection of others; stresses blaming.

    Don't demand perfection from newcomers; give them room to grow.

Opposite

  • 🐋寬宏大量kuān hóng dà liàng

    be magnanimous; be generous in spirit — Generous and forgiving.

    He is magnanimous and doesn't fuss over small matters.

  • 🦥難得糊塗nán dé hú tú

    play dumb on purpose; it takes wisdom to turn a blind eye — Wisely turning a blind eye to small things.

    Sometimes it pays to play dumb in order to keep relationships harmonious.
