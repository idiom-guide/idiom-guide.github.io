披星戴月

pī xīng dài yuè

In English

  • from dawn to dusk
  • burn the candle at both ends

Meaning

Leaving home early and returning late, working or travelling under stars and moon; a picture of hard work.Neutral · General

Example

為了趕在年底前完工，工人們披星戴月地趕工。

To finish before the end of the year, the crew worked from dawn to dusk.

Usage

Mostly an adverbial, as in working or travelling "under the stars and moon". It stresses leaving before daybreak and returning after dark; for sheer absorption that forgets meals and sleep, use the idiom 廢寢忘食.

Similar

  • 💻廢寢忘食fèi qǐn wàng shí

    work day and night; forget to eat and sleep — So absorbed that meals and sleep are forgotten; about focus, not necessarily travel.

    He worked day and night on the proposal, barely stopping to eat or sleep.

  • 🌛夜以繼日yè yǐ jì rì

    night and day; around the clock — Working through night and day; about continuous time, not just early starts and late finishes.

    Rescue crews worked around the clock to restore power.

Opposite

  • 🛹遊手好閒yóu shǒu hào xián

    idle; loaf around — Idling about with no real work; about not working at all.

    He loafs around all day and refuses to look for a job.

  • ⛱悠閒自在yōu xián zì zài

    leisurely; carefree — Relaxed and free of pressure; the opposite of toil and rush.

    Retired, he lives at a leisurely pace, fishing and drinking tea every day.
