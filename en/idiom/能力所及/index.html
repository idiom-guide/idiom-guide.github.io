能力所及

néng lì suǒ jí

In English

  • within sb's capability

Meaning

Within one's ability.Neutral · General

Example

只要是我能力所及，我一定幫你。

I will do everything within my capability to help you.

Usage

Often 在能力所及的範圍內; when something is beyond one's ability, say 力有未逮.

Similar

  • 🏃‍♂️盡力而為jìn lì ér wéi

    do one's best — Doing one's best without promising results.

    I'll do my best, but I can't guarantee success.

  • 🧮量力而為liàng lì ér wéi

    act within one's means — Doing only what one can manage, without overreaching.

    When starting a business, act within your means and don't borrow too much.

Opposite

  • 🏋️力有未逮lì yǒu wèi dài

    beyond one's ability; fall short of — Wanting to but unable.

    The task was beyond his ability.

  • 🤷‍♀️無能為力wú néng wéi lì

    be powerless; can do nothing about it — Utterly powerless; more hopeless.

    Faced with this disaster, we were powerless.
