少碰為妙

shǎo pèng wéi miào

In English

  • be worth avoiding

Meaning

Best avoided.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • best avoided
  • steer clear of

Example

那個路邊攤少碰為妙。

That street-food stall is worth avoiding.

Usage

Colloquial advice, for food, people, investments or topics.

Similar

  • 🏃避之惟恐不及bì zhī wéi kǒng bù jí

    avoid like the plague — Avoiding something like the plague; stronger.

    After the argument, he avoided her like the plague.

  • 👀提高警覺tí gāo jǐng jué

    eyes up — Staying on the alert.

    Keep your eyes up when you cross the road.

Opposite

  • 🦆趨之若鶩qū zhī ruò wù

    flock to; rush after — Flocking to something; often negative.

    People flocked to the new shop when it opened.

  • 🍽️來者不拒lái zhě bù jù

    nothing's off menu — Turning nothing away.

    At our open-mic night, everyone is welcome and nothing's off the menu.
