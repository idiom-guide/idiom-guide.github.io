3 · Joy & Satisfaction

沈醉其中

chén zuì qí zhōng

In English

  • enthralled

Meaning

Enthralled.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • lost in
  • spellbound

Example

觀眾沈醉其中，聽得入迷。

The audience was enthralled by the music.

Usage

For music, performances, scenery and reading.

Similar

  • 😋津津有味jīn jīn yǒu wèi

    with great interest; with relish — With relish.

    The students listened to the story with great interest.

  • 🧲全神貫注quán shén guàn zhù

    be fully absorbed; with rapt attention — With rapt attention.

    The children were fully absorbed in the story.

Opposite

  • 🥱枯燥無味kū zào wú wèi

    lackluster; uninspiring — Uninspiring.

    The lecture was lackluster and uninspiring.
