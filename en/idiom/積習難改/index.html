積習難改

jī xí nán gǎi

In English

  • inveterate + n.-er

Meaning

Old habits die hard.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • old habits die hard
  • set in one's ways

Example

他是積習難改的賭徒，戒不掉。

He is an inveterate gambler and can't quit.

Usage

For bad habits and addictions.

Similar

  • 🐂牛牽到北京還是牛niú qiān dào běi jīng hái shì niú

    A leopard cannot change its spots — A leopard can't change its spots.

    He promised to change, but a leopard cannot change its spots.

  • 🐂冥頑不靈míng wán bù líng

    recalcitrant — Pig-headed.

    The recalcitrant student ignored every warning.

Opposite

  • 🛀洗心革面xǐ xīn gé miàn

    turn over a new leaf; reform oneself — Turning over a new leaf.

    After his release he turned over a new leaf and started afresh.

  • 🦋脫胎換骨tuō tāi huàn gǔ

    totally changed; transformed significantly — Transformed inside and out.

    Three years abroad transformed her completely.
