冒昧擅自

mào mèi shàn zì

In English

  • take the liberty of V-ing

Meaning

Presumptuously, without asking permission first.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • presume to
  • do it without asking

Example

我冒昧擅自幫大家點了餐。

I took the liberty of ordering for everyone.

Usage

Often in apologies (冒昧……，請見諒); strongly self-deprecating.

Similar

  • 👨‍✈️自作主張zì zuò zhǔ zhāng

    take matters into one's own hands; act on one's own authority — Taking matters into one's own hands; often reproachful.

    He took it upon himself to change the contract, and it caused trouble.

  • 📬先斬後奏xiān zhǎn hòu zòu

    act first and report later — Acting first and reporting later.

    He acted first and reported later, signing the contract before telling his boss.

Opposite

  • 📏循規蹈矩xún guī dǎo jǔ

    by the book; play by the rules — Following the rules.

    He plays by the rules and never takes risks.

  • 📪事先請示shì xiān qǐng shì

    ask for approval beforehand — Asking for approval beforehand.

    Important decisions must be approved beforehand.
