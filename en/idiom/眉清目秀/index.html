眉清目秀

méi qīng mù xiù

In English

  • fine-featured
  • pleasant-looking

Meaning

Clear brows and neat eyes: a clean, well-formed face.Positive · General

Example

這男孩眉清目秀，十分討人喜歡。

He is a fine-featured boy with bright eyes.

Usage

Mostly for young people, especially children and teens. About neat, clean features rather than striking beauty.

Similar

  • 🌸天生麗質tiān shēng lì zhì

    a natural beauty; naturally beautiful — Born beautiful; about natural looks.

    She is a natural beauty and hardly needs makeup.

  • 🤵一表人才yī biǎo rén cái

    a fine figure of a man; good-looking and capable — Good-looking and capable; mostly for grown men.

    He is a fine figure of a man with a bright future.

Opposite

  • 🐀賊眉鼠眼zéi méi shǔ yǎn

    shifty-eyed; sneaky-looking — Shifty-looking; suspicious in appearance.

    A shifty-eyed man was loitering outside the shop.

  • 👹面目可憎miàn mù kě zēng

    repulsive; hideous — Repulsive to look at.

    His sneering face was truly repulsive.
