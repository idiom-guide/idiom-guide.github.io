聽命於人

tīng mìng yú rén

In English

  • do sth at sb's behest

Meaning

Taking orders from someone.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • at sb's behest
  • take orders from
  • do sb's bidding

Example

這些特務聽命於人，背後是外國政府。

The agents act at the behest of a foreign government.

Usage

Usually implies a lack of autonomy.

Similar

  • 🥺看人臉色kàn rén liǎn sè

    be at the mercy of sb — At someone's mercy.

    I don't want to be at the mercy of my boss's mood every day.

  • 🙆唯唯諾諾wéi wéi nuò nuò

    be a yes-man; agree servilely — A yes-man.

    He is a yes-man who never dares to disagree.

Opposite

  • 📣發號施令fā hào shī lìng

    call the shots — Calling the shots.

    In this house, my sister calls the shots.

  • 👨‍✈️自作主張zì zuò zhǔ zhāng

    take matters into one's own hands; act on one's own authority — Taking matters into one's own hands.

    He took it upon himself to change the contract, and it caused trouble.
