不偏不倚

bù piān bù yǐ

In English

  • impartial

Meaning

Taking no side; impartial. Can also mean exactly in the middle or right on target.Positive · General

Example

好的裁判不偏不倚，不會袒護任何一方。

A good referee stays impartial and favors neither side.

Usage

Often describes referees, judges, reporting or positions; a ball landing right on the line is also 不偏不倚 (English: right on target).

Similar

  • ⚜公正不阿gōng zhèng bù ē

    rectitude; unbiased — Upright and incorruptible; stresses character more.

    The judge was known for his rectitude and never favored the rich.

  • ♎持平而論chí píng ér lùn

    To be fair — Used for comments and judgments, not actions.

    To be fair, the referee had only a split second to decide.

Opposite

  • 🍕有失公允yǒu shī gōng yǔn

    not fair — Unfair in handling or judging.

    The report was not fair to the smaller companies.

  • 🥉厚此薄彼hòu cǐ bó bǐ

    play favorites; treat one side better than the other — Treating two sides differently; often about pay, staffing or resources.

    A teacher shouldn't play favorites; everyone should be treated alike.
