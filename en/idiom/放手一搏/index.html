放手一搏

fàng shǒu yī bó

In English

  • go big or go home
  • risk all

Meaning

Going for broke.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • go for broke
  • risk it all

Example

他決定放手一搏，自己開餐廳。

He is going to risk it all and open his own restaurant: go big or go home.

Usage

Positive in tone; for starting a business, competing or changing careers.

Similar

  • 🎲孤注一擲gū zhù yī zhì

    a desperate play — A desperate all-or-nothing bet; stresses the risk.

    Selling his house to fund the film was a desperate play.

  • ⚾全力出擊quán lì chū jī

    swing for the fences, full throttle — Swinging for the fences.

    In the last round, he swung for the fences.

Opposite

  • 💰見好就收jiàn hǎo jiù shōu

    quit while one is ahead — Quitting while one is ahead.

    Quit while you're ahead and don't gamble the winnings.

  • 🤔猶豫不決yóu yù bù jué

    dilly-dally — Unable to make up one's mind.

    Stop dilly-dallying and make a decision.
