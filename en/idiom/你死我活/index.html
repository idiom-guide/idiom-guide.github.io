2 · Competition & Power

你死我活

nǐ sǐ wǒ huó

In English

  • cutthroat competition
  • a fight to the death

Meaning

A fight to the death.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • cutthroat
  • life-or-death struggle

Example

兩家公司陷入你死我活的割喉戰。

The two companies are locked in cutthroat competition.

Usage

For competition and struggles; strongly worded.

Similar

  • 🆚勢不兩立shì bù liǎng lì

    irreconcilable — Irreconcilable.

    The two factions are irreconcilable.

  • 🚒水火不容shuǐ huǒ bù róng

    be as incompatible as fire and water — As incompatible as fire and water.

    The two camps are as incompatible as fire and water and cannot reach any agreement.

Opposite

  • ☮️和平共處hé píng gòng chǔ

    peaceful coexistence — Peaceful coexistence.

    Both sides reaffirmed their commitment to peaceful coexistence.

  • 🔀互惠互利hù huì hù lì

    mutually beneficial; win-win — Mutually beneficial.

    We are looking for a mutually beneficial arrangement.
