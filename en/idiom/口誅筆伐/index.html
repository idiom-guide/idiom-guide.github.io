7 · Public Opinion

口誅筆伐

kǒu zhū bǐ fá

In English

  • pilloried in the press

Meaning

Pilloried in the press.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • come under fire
  • a media firestorm

Example

這家公司因產品召回遭到媒體口誅筆伐。

The company was pilloried in the press for the recall.

Usage

Formal; for criticism in the media and public opinion.

Similar

  • 💥猛烈抨擊měng liè pēng jī

    lambaste, harsh criticism — Lambasting.

    Critics lambasted the film as shallow.

  • 👉千夫所指qiān fū suǒ zhǐ

    universally condemned — Universally condemned.

    The attack was universally condemned.

Opposite

  • 👏大加讚賞dà jiā zàn shǎng

    speak highly of — Speaking highly of.

    Critics spoke highly of the young director's first film.

  • 👍為人稱道wéi rén chēng dào

    widely praised/complimented; commendable — Widely praised.

    Her kindness to strangers was widely praised.
