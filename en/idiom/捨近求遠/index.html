捨近求遠

shě jìn qiú yuǎn

In English

  • detour

Meaning

Taking the long way round.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • take the long way round
  • overcomplicate

Example

走高速公路是捨近求遠，地方道路其實比較近。

Taking the highway was a detour; the local road was shorter.

Usage

Literally a detour; figuratively ignoring the simple solution at hand.

Similar

  • 🛒本末倒置běn mò dào zhì

    put the cart before the horse — Putting the cart before the horse.

    Buying a new camera before learning to take photos is putting the cart before the horse.

Opposite

  • ▶直截了當zhí jié liǎo dàng

    be straightforward; be direct — Straight to the point.

    She said exactly what she thought without beating around the bush.

  • ✨事半功倍shì bàn gōng bèi

    great result with less effort — Great results with less effort.

    A good tool can get great results with less effort.
