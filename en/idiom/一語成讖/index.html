一語成讖

yī yǔ chéng chèn

In English

  • one's words prove prophetic

Meaning

An offhand remark that later came true, usually something bad.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • one's words come true
  • jinx

Example

他隨口說了一句會有暴風雨，沒想到一語成讖。

His casual remark about a storm proved prophetic.

Usage

Often 不幸一語成讖; mostly for misfortune.

Similar

  • 🥠不幸言中bù xìng yán zhōng

    unfortunately guess right — Unfortunately came true; about bad news.

    Unfortunately he was right: we really were laid off.

  • 🪐預言成真yù yán chéng zhēn

    a prediction comes true — A prediction comes true; neutral.

    The professor's prediction from ten years ago has come true.

Opposite

  • 😦杞人憂天qǐ rén yōu tiān

    worry about nothing; fear that the sky will fall — Worrying needlessly about what won't happen.

    Don't worry about nothing; it isn't that bad.

  • 😅虛驚一場xū jīng yī cháng

    false alarm — A false alarm.

    It was a false alarm; the smoke came from the toaster.
