面面相覷

miàn miàn xiāng qù

In English

  • look at each other in dismay

Meaning

Looking at each other in dismay.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • exchange glances
  • dumbfounded

Example

老闆宣布辭職時，團隊成員面面相覷。

The team looked at each other in dismay when the boss resigned.

Usage

For moments of surprise, awkwardness or speechlessness.

Similar

  • 🤷不知所措bù zhī suǒ cuò

    overwhelmed; catch sb. off base — At a loss.

    Overwhelmed by the crowd, the child didn't know what to do.

Opposite

  • 🎋胸有成竹xiōng yǒu chéng zhú

    quietly confident — Quietly confident.

    She walked into the interview quietly confident.
