請別見怪

qǐng bié jiàn guài

In English

  • no hard feelings

Meaning

No hard feelings.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • don't take it personally
  • no offense

Example

我只是開玩笑，請別見怪。

I was only joking. No hard feelings?

Usage

Colloquial; after an apology or a remark that might offend.

Similar

  • 😅多多包涵duō duō bāo hán

    please bear with us — Please bear with us.

    This is our first time hosting, so please bear with us.

  • 🙋恕我直言shù wǒ zhí yán

    with all due respect; frankly speaking — With all due respect.

    With all due respect, I think the plan has a few problems.

Opposite

  • 😒耿耿於懷gěng gěng yú huái

    hold a grudge; can't let it go — Holding a grudge.

    He still holds a grudge about that remark.

  • 💁毫無歉意háo wú qiàn yì

    unapologetically — Unapologetic.

    She unapologetically cut in line.
