2 · Competition & Power

位居上風

wèi jū shàng fēng

In English

  • have the upper hand

Meaning

Having the upper hand.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • gain the edge
  • be in the driver's seat

Example

下半場我們隊位居上風。

Our team has the upper hand in the second half.

Usage

For games, negotiations and battles.

Similar

  • 💪佔有優勢zhàn yǒu yōu shì

    has the upper hand — Having an advantage; more neutral.

    With a stronger lineup, our team has the upper hand.

  • 🎟️勝券在握shèng quàn zài wò

    book a win; eminently winnable — A win in the bag.

    With the opponent's star player injured, the final looks eminently winnable.

Opposite

  • 📉失去優勢shī qù yōu shì

    lose some ground; lose ground to — Losing ground.

    Our company is losing ground to cheaper rivals.

  • 🥚不堪一擊bù kān yī jī

    collapse at the first blow; be easily crushed — Collapsing at the first blow.

    The team looks strong but crumbles at the first blow.
