人格缺陷

rén gé quē xiàn

In English

  • core sins

Meaning

A character flaw.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • character flaw
  • fatal flaw

Example

貪婪與嫉妒是這位悲劇英雄的人格缺陷。

Greed and envy are the core sins of the tragic hero.

Usage

For literary analysis and character assessments.

Similar

  • 😈品行不正pǐn xíng bù zhèng

    misconduct; bad character — Of bad character.

    He was dismissed for misconduct.

Opposite

  • 👨‍🏫為人師表wéi rén shī biǎo

    teacher of exemplary virtue — A role model in conduct.

    A teacher should be a model of virtue for students.

  • 🧓德高望重dé gāo wàng zhòng

    venerable — Venerable.

    The venerable teacher was respected by three generations of students.
