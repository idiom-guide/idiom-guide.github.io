無關緊要

wú guān jǐn yào

In English

  • immaterial

Meaning

Immaterial.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • irrelevant
  • it doesn't matter

Example

我們六點還是七點見面都無關緊要。

Whether we meet at six or seven is immaterial.

Usage

For details and conditions under discussion.

Similar

  • 🐞微不足道wēi bù zú dào

    insignificant; trivial — Insignificant.

    The difference is trivial.

  • 🔇無關痛癢wú guān tòng yǎng

    be of no consequence; be beside the point — Of no consequence.

    These inconsequential comments needn't be taken to heart.

Opposite

  • 🏋️舉足輕重jǔ zú qīng zhòng

    pivotal; carry great weight — Pivotal.

    The swing states are pivotal.

  • 💀攸關生死yōu guān shēng sǐ

    a matter of life and death — A matter of life and death.

    For patients on the waiting list, this decision is a matter of life and death.
