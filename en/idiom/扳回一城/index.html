扳回一城

bān huí yī chéng

In English

  • win one back
  • get one back

Meaning

Winning one back; recovering some lost ground.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • pull one back

Example

我們隊在最後一分鐘進球，扳回一城。

Our team won one back with a goal in the last minute.

Usage

For sport, elections and business rivalry; recovering part of a deficit, not necessarily winning.

Similar

  • 🐟鹹魚翻身xián yú fān shēn

    redemption time — Redemption time; rising from rock bottom.

    After years of failure, it's redemption time for the former champion.

  • 🌊力挽狂瀾lì wǎn kuáng lán

    turn the tide; save the day — Turning the tide; a bigger reversal.

    A late goal turned the tide for the home team.

Opposite

  • 🤕一敗塗地yī bài tú dì

    fall flat on your face — Falling flat on one's face.

    The campaign fell flat on its face.

  • 🏳️潰不成軍kuì bù chéng jūn

    in full rout — In full rout.

    The invaders were in full rout by dawn.
