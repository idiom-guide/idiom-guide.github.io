3 · Joy & Satisfaction

欽佩不已

qīn pèi bù yǐ

In English

  • deep admiration

Meaning

Deep admiration.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • filled with admiration
  • take one's hat off to

Example

我對她的勇氣欽佩不已。

I have deep admiration for her courage.

Usage

For courage, perseverance and achievements.

Similar

  • 🙇肅然起敬sù rán qǐ jìng

    be filled with awe; awe-struck — Awe-struck.

    We were awe-struck by the old soldier's story.

  • 🤩歎為觀止tàn wéi guān zhǐ

    breathtaking; awe-inspiring — Breathtaking.

    The view from the summit was breathtaking.

Opposite

  • 👃嗤之以鼻chī zhī yǐ bí

    pour scorn on sb; scorn — Pouring scorn on it.

    Experts poured scorn on the miracle diet.

  • 🙄不屑一顧bù xiè yī gù

    be dismissive of sth — Dismissive.

    The critic was dismissive of the new play.
