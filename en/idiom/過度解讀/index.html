過度解讀

guò dù jiě dú

In English

  • reading too much into

Meaning

Reading too much into it.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • overthink
  • overinterpret

Example

你對她的簡訊過度解讀了。

You're reading too much into her text message.

Usage

For messages, remarks and behavior.

Similar

  • 📎牽強附會qiān qiǎng fù huì

    be farfetched; stretch the meaning — Far-fetched.

    Blaming the weather for this failure is a stretch.

  • ✂️斷章取義duàn zhāng qǔ yì

    take sb's comments "out of context."; take sth. out of context — Taking something out of context.

    The newspaper took her comments out of context.

Opposite

  • 📂就事論事jiù shì lùn shì

    judge the matter on its own merits; stick to the issue — Sticking to the point.

    Let's stick to the issue and leave personal grudges out of it.
