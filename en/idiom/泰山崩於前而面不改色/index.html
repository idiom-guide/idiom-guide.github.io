泰山崩於前而面不改色

tài shān bēng yú qián ér miàn bù gǎi sè

In English

  • remain unruffled even if the sky falls
  • unflappable

Meaning

Unflappable even if the sky falls.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • nerves of steel

Example

引擎故障時，機長依然泰山崩於前而面不改色。

The pilot remained unruffled even when the engine failed.

Usage

Formal; adapted from Su Xun's essay on the mind of a general.

Similar

  • 😐面不改色miàn bù gǎi sè

    not (even) bat an eyelid — Not batting an eyelid; also for smaller moments.

    He didn't bat an eyelid when he heard the price.

  • 😎泰然自若tài rán zì ruò

    unfazed — Unfazed.

    She remained unfazed by the critics' attacks.

Opposite

  • 😨驚慌失措jīng huāng shī cuò

    panic; consternation, To sb's consternation, fill sb with consternation — Panicking.

    To the teacher's consternation, the whole class started laughing.

  • 🐍杯弓蛇影bēi gōng shé yǐng

    Afraid of one's own shadow; extremely suspicious — Afraid of one's own shadow.

    After the burglary, she was afraid of her own shadow.
