4 · Pain & Unease

鬱鬱寡歡

yù yù guǎ huān

In English

  • moper n

Meaning

Moping.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • mope
  • down in the dumps

Example

他失業後一直鬱鬱寡歡。

He has been a moper ever since he lost his job.

Usage

For long-lasting low moods.

Similar

  • 😟愁眉苦臉chóu méi kǔ liǎn

    look so woebegone; with a woebegone expression — A long face.

    He looked so woebegone that the waiter asked if he was ill.

  • 😿自怨自艾zì yuàn zì yì

    feel sorry for oneself; self-pity — Self-pity.

    Stop feeling sorry for yourself and do something.

Opposite

  • 😆興高采烈xìng gāo cǎi liè

    gleeful; a gleeful smile/shout — Gleeful.

    The children ran out of school in gleeful anticipation of summer.

  • 🔥生命熱情shēng mìng rè qíng

    zest for life — A zest for life.

    Her zest for life inspires everyone around her.
