時來運轉

shí lái yùn zhuǎn

In English

  • sb's time has come

Meaning

One's luck turning.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • one's luck turns
  • things are looking up

Example

倒楣了多年，他終於時來運轉。

After years of bad luck, his time has come.

Usage

For a change of fortune after a long bad spell.

Similar

  • 🌅否極泰來pǐ jí tài lái

    the tide will turn; every cloud has a silver lining — The tide will turn.

    After a hard year, the tide will turn.

  • 🐟鹹魚翻身xián yú fān shēn

    redemption time — Turning one's fortunes around.

    After years of failure, it's redemption time for the former champion.

Opposite

  • ☔時運不濟shí yùn bù jì

    be down on one's luck; have bad timing — Down on one's luck.

    He started his business in a recession; it was simply bad timing.

  • 👟禍不單行huò bù dān xíng

    wait for the other shoe to drop; one-two punch — Misfortunes never come singly.

    First the roof leaked, then the pipes burst. When it rains, it pours.
