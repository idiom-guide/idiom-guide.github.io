萬王之王

wàn wáng zhī wáng

In English

  • king of kings

Meaning

King of kings.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • the greatest of all
  • top dog

Example

電影裡，獅子是萬王之王。

In the film, the lion is the king of kings.

Usage

From ancient royal titles and religious usage; now for the very best in a field.

Similar

  • 🥇無人能及wú rén néng jí

    second to none; unrivaled — Second to none.

    Her skill at chess is second to none.

  • 🦁無可匹敵wú kě pǐ dí

    unmatched — Unmatched.

    Her skill on the violin is unmatched.

Opposite

  • 🐞微不足道wēi bù zú dào

    insignificant; trivial — Insignificant.

    The difference is trivial.
