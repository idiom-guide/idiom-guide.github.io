站不住腳

zhàn bù zhù jiǎo

In English

  • untenable
  • doesn't hold water

Meaning

Doesn't hold water.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • not hold water
  • not stand up to scrutiny
  • be untenable

Example

他的論點根本站不住腳。

His argument simply doesn't hold water.

Usage

As in 站不住腳的理由 or 論點; not normally used of people.

Similar

  • ⤵不攻自破bù gōng zì pò

    collapse on its own; fall apart without being refuted — Collapses on its own.

    The rumor fell apart on its own after a few days.

  • 🧀漏洞百出lòu dòng bǎi chū

    be full of holes; be riddled with flaws — Full of holes.

    His story was full of holes, and nobody believed it.

Opposite

  • 💯無懈可擊wú xiè kě jī

    be flawless; be unassailable — Unassailable; often of arguments or defenses.

    Her argument was flawless.

  • 📇有憑有據yǒu píng yǒu jù

    be well grounded; be backed by evidence — Backed by evidence; a lower bar than unassailable.

    His accusation is backed by evidence; it's not empty talk.
