省小錢、花大錢

shěng xiǎo qián, huā dà qián

In English

  • being penny wise, pound foolish

Meaning

Penny-wise and pound-foolish.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • false economy
  • buy cheap, buy twice

Example

買最便宜的輪胎是省小錢、花大錢。

Buying the cheapest tires was penny-wise and pound-foolish.

Usage

Colloquial; for purchases, procurement and repairs.

Similar

  • ⚖️得不償失dé bù cháng shī

    too expensive to be worth it — Not worth the cost.

    Staying up all night to finish was too expensive to be worth it.

  • 🥬蠅頭小利yíng tóu xiǎo lì

    can only buy cabbage and potatoes — Peanuts.

    He sold his reputation for a sum that could only buy cabbage and potatoes.

Opposite

  • 🧮精打細算jīng dǎ xì suàn

    astute with money; budget carefully — Careful budgeting.

    She is astute with money and plans every purchase.

  • 💡明智之舉míng zhì zhī jǔ

    smart play — A wise move.

    Saving some money for emergencies was a smart play.
