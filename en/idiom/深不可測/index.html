深不可測

shēn bù kě cè

In English

  • unfathomable
  • inscrutable

Meaning

Unfathomable.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • beyond measure
  • bottomless

Example

老師傅的功夫深不可測。

The old master's skill seemed unfathomable.

Usage

For a person's depth, strength or intentions; with a touch of awe or wariness.

Similar

  • 🌫️難以捉摸nán yǐ zhuō mō

    elusive; unreadable — Hard to read.

    The elusive thief slipped away from the police once again.

  • 🥴撲朔迷離pū shuò mí lí

    be baffling; be shrouded in mystery — Bewildering; hard to tell true from false.

    The case is baffling, and the police cannot yet make sense of it.

Opposite

  • 🌆一覽無遺yī lǎn wú yí

    be laid bare; be fully in view — Everything laid bare at a glance.

    From the summit, the whole city lies spread out before you.

  • 🚥一目了然yī mù liǎo rán

    be clear at a glance — Clear at a glance.

    The chart is clear at a glance.
