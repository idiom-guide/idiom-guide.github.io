3 · Achievement & Performance

佳評如潮

jiā píng rú cháo

In English

  • rave reviews
  • glowing reviews

Meaning

Rave reviews.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • critically acclaimed

Example

這本小說佳評如潮。

The novel won rave reviews.

Usage

For books, films, performances and products.

Similar

  • 🙇備受推崇bèi shòu tuī chóng

    widely honored/acclaimed — Widely acclaimed.

    The professor's book is widely acclaimed.

  • 👏贏得讚賞yíng dé zàn shǎng

    earn plaudits from sb — Earning plaudits.

    The young chef earned plaudits from critics.

Opposite

  • 👉千夫所指qiān fū suǒ zhǐ

    universally condemned — Universally condemned.

    The attack was universally condemned.

  • 🌔毀譽參半huǐ yù cān bàn

    have both admirers and critics; be praised and criticized in equal measure — Mixed reviews.

    The director has both admirers and critics, yet always keeps a loyal following.
