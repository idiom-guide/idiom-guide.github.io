9 · Closing & Farewell

不虛此行

bù xū cǐ xíng

In English

  • productive trip
  • got what you came for

Meaning

A productive trip.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • a productive trip
  • got what we came for
  • worth the trip

Example

這趟不虛此行，我們拿到了想要的。

The trip was productive: we got what we came for.

Usage

For after a trip, visit or meeting.

Similar

  • 🎒滿載而歸mǎn zài ér guī

    come away with a great deal — Coming away with a great deal.

    I hope everyone comes away with a great deal from this forum.

  • 📈獲益良多huò yì liáng duō

    benefit enormously — Benefiting enormously.

    I benefited enormously from the workshop.

Opposite

  • 💨徒勞無功tú láo wú gōng

    a fool's errand; fruitless — A fool's errand.

    Searching for her keys felt like a fool's errand.

  • 🏳️鎩羽而歸shā yǔ ér guī

    return in defeat; come back empty-handed — Coming back empty-handed.

    The explorers returned in defeat.
