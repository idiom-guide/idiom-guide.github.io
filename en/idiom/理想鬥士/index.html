理想鬥士

lǐ xiǎng dòu shì

In English

  • crusader

Meaning

A crusader for a cause.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • idealist
  • champion of a cause

Example

這位年輕律師是人權的理想鬥士。

The young lawyer is a crusader for human rights.

Usage

For activists, reformers and advocates.

Similar

  • 🦾不屈不撓bù qū bù náo

    invincible, indomitable, dauntless — Indomitable.

    The dauntless climber kept going despite the storm.

  • 🚀義無反顧yì wú fǎn gù

    be resolute and not look back; go ahead without hesitation — Pressing on without hesitation.

    For her children, she resolutely quit her job without looking back.

Opposite

  • 🏊隨波逐流suí bō zhú liú

    swim with the stream — Swimming with the stream.

    Don't just swim with the stream; choose your own path.

  • 🌿牆頭草qiáng tóu cǎo

    fence-sitter; turncoat — A fence-sitter.

    He's a fence-sitter who changes sides with the wind.
