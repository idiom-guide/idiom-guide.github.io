保留各自表述意見

bǎo liú gè zì biǎo shù yì jiàn

In English

  • agree to disagree

Meaning

Agreeing to disagree; each side keeps its own position.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • each side reserves its own view
  • let's differ on this

Example

我們對此看法不同，不如保留各自表述意見。

We can't agree, so let's agree to disagree.

Usage

Common in statements, minutes and the end of negotiations; neutral, keeps the peace.

Similar

  • 🤝求同存異qiú tóng cún yì

    seek common ground; agree to disagree — Seeking common ground while setting aside differences; actively conciliatory.

    The two delegations agreed to seek common ground and set their differences aside.

  • 🎵各抒己見gè shū jǐ jiàn

    each air one's own views — Everyone voicing their own views.

    At the meeting, everyone aired their own views.

Opposite

  • 🤚寸步不讓cùn bù bù ràng

    not yield an inch — Refusing to give an inch.

    Neither side would yield an inch on price.

  • 🤺針鋒相對zhēn fēng xiāng duì

    be diametrically opposed; go head to head — Head-on confrontation.

    The two candidates were diametrically opposed on taxes.
