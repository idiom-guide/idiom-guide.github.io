公正不阿

gōng zhèng bù ē

In English

  • rectitude
  • unbiased

Meaning

Upright and impartial.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • upright and impartial

Example

這位法官以公正不阿著稱，從不偏袒有錢人。

The judge was known for his rectitude and never favored the rich.

Usage

For judges, officials and referees.

Similar

  • 🏅賞罰分明shǎng fá fēn míng

    reward and punish fairly — Fair with rewards and discipline.

    He rewards and punishes fairly when leading his team.

  • 👨‍⚖️毋枉毋縱wú wǎng wú zòng

    (the rule of) protecting the innocent and punishing the guilty; sth. be handled fairly and justly — Protecting the innocent and punishing the guilty.

    The court's duty is to protect the innocent and punish the guilty.

Opposite

  • 🍕有失公允yǒu shī gōng yǔn

    not fair — Not fair.

    The report was not fair to the smaller companies.

  • 🥉厚此薄彼hòu cǐ bó bǐ

    play favorites; treat one side better than the other — Favoring one over another.

    A teacher shouldn't play favorites; everyone should be treated alike.
