目光短淺

mù guāng duǎn qiǎn

In English

  • short-sighted

Meaning

Short-sighted.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • myopic
  • can't see past one's nose

Example

這個決定目光短淺。

The decision was short-sighted.

Usage

For decisions, planning and investments.

Similar

  • 🔭短視近利duǎn shì jìn lì

    short-sighted; improvident — Chasing quick wins.

    The company's short-sighted focus on quick profits hurt it later.

  • 🥚殺雞取卵shā jī qǔ luǎn

    kill the goose that lays the golden eggs — Killing the goose that lays the golden eggs.

    Raising fees again would kill the goose that lays the golden eggs.

Opposite

  • 🦉先見之明xiān jiàn zhī míng

    be prescient of; proved prescient — Foresight.

    Her decision to sell early proved prescient.

  • 💡見識卓越jiàn shí zhuó yuè

    savvy read; visionary — Visionary insight.

    The young investor has a visionary grasp of the market.
