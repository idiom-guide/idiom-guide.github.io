冰山一角

bīng shān yī jiǎo

In English

  • tip of the iceberg

Meaning

Only a small, visible part of a much bigger problem.Neutral · General

Example

目前曝光的醜聞，只是冰山一角。

The scandal we have seen so far is just the tip of the iceberg.

Usage

Often follows "only" or "just"; common when exposing scandals or social problems.

Similar

  • 🥜九牛一毛jiǔ niú yī máo

    peanuts; just a drop in the bucket — Stresses how small the amount is; 冰山一角 implies much more lies hidden.

    The donation was just a drop in the bucket compared with what the hospital needed.

  • 📷管中窺豹guǎn zhōng kuī bào

    see only a small part of the whole; judge the whole from a narrow view — About a limited viewpoint (the viewer), not the thing itself.

    Judging by a single report is like seeing the whole through a keyhole.

Opposite

  • 📤全盤托出quán pán tuō chū

    lay/put sb's cards on the table; come clean with sb. about sth — Telling everything without holding back: the opposite of showing only a tip.

    After hours of questioning, he finally came clean about the missing funds.

  • 🌆一覽無遺yī lǎn wú yí

    be laid bare; be fully in view — Seeing the whole at a glance; about seeing, not telling.

    From the summit, the whole city lies spread out before you.
