人云亦云

rén yún yì yún

In English

  • echo other's words

Meaning

Echoing others.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • parrot
  • follow the herd

Example

別人云亦云，要有自己的看法。

Don't just echo other people's words; form your own opinion.

Usage

Often advice to think for oneself.

Similar

  • 🐑盲從追尋máng cóng zhuī xún

    drink the (sb's own) Kool-Aid — Following blindly.

    Don't drink the Kool-Aid just because everyone else does.

  • 🏊隨波逐流suí bō zhú liú

    swim with the stream — Swimming with the stream.

    Don't just swim with the stream; choose your own path.

Opposite

  • 🌳獨樹一幟dú shù yī zhì

    unique in — One of a kind.

    The restaurant is unique in its blend of Taiwanese and French cooking.

  • 🎨不落俗套bù luò sú tào

    unconventional; no place for clichés — Unconventional.

    The play is unconventional, with no place for clichés.
