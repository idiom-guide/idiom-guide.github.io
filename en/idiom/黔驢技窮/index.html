黔驢技窮

qián lǘ jì qióng

In English

  • come to the end of one's tricks
  • at one's wits' end

Meaning

At one's wits' end.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • out of ideas

Example

沒有論點可用，他已黔驢技窮。

Out of arguments, he was at his wits' end.

Usage

From Liu Zongyuan's fable 'The Donkey of Guizhou'.

Similar

  • 🏋️力有未逮lì yǒu wèi dài

    beyond one's ability; fall short of — Beyond one's ability.

    The task was beyond his ability.

Opposite

  • 🖌️有兩把刷子yǒu liǎng bǎ shuā zi

    know one's stuff; have real skill — Knowing one's stuff.

    That mechanic has some secret sauce: he fixes any engine in minutes.

  • 🔪遊刃有餘yóu rèn yǒu yú

    looks effortless; be more than equal to — Looking effortless.

    She handles the busiest shifts with ease, making it look effortless.
