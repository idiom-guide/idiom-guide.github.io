陳年往事

chén nián wǎng shì

In English

  • ancient history

Meaning

Ancient history.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • water under the bridge
  • old news

Example

別再提了，那都是陳年往事。

Please don't bring that up; it's ancient history.

Usage

Often implies it's not worth bringing up again.

Similar

  • 🌥過眼雲煙guò yǎn yún yān

    a passing cloud; be fleeting and insubstantial — Fleeting, not worth dwelling on.

    Wealth and glory are no more than passing clouds.

  • 🌌物換星移wù huàn xīng yí

    things change — Things change with time.

    Things change as the years go by; the old market is now a shopping mall.

Opposite

  • 📸記憶猶新jì yì yóu xīn

    fresh in one's memory — Still fresh in one's memory.

    The flood is still fresh in our memory.

  • 1️⃣當務之急dāng wù zhī jí

    top priority; priority one — The top priority.

    Fixing the leak is our top priority.
