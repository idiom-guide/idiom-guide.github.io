急流勇退

jí liú yǒng tuì

In English

  • quit while one is ahead
  • go out on top
  • retire at the height of one's career

Meaning

Going out on top.Positive · Formal

Example

他選擇在最好的球季後急流勇退。

He chose to go out on top, retiring after his best season.

Usage

For politicians, professionals and athletes; praises their wisdom.

Similar

  • 💰見好就收jiàn hǎo jiù shōu

    quit while one is ahead — Quitting while one is ahead.

    Quit while you're ahead and don't gamble the winnings.

  • ✋適可而止shì kě ér zhǐ

    know when to stop — Knowing when to stop.

    Criticism is fine, but know when to stop.

Opposite

  • 🤑貪得無厭tān dé wú yàn

    too greedy — Insatiably greedy.

    The landlord is too greedy and raises the rent every year.

  • 🐽得寸進尺dé cùn jìn chǐ

    give an inch and take a mile — Give an inch and they take a mile.

    Don't push your luck; I have already given way a lot.
