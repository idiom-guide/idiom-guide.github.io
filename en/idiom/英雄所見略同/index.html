英雄所見略同

yīng xióng suǒ jiàn lüè tóng

In English

  • great minds think alike

Meaning

Great minds think alike.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • we're on the same page

Example

我們都替這本書取了同一個書名，英雄所見略同！

We both picked the same title for the book. Great minds think alike!

Usage

Said when two people find they agree; lightly self-congratulatory.

Similar

  • 🤝不謀而合bù móu ér hé

    happen to agree; see eye to eye without consulting — Agreeing without having discussed it; no compliment involved.

    We never compared notes, yet we came up with the same idea.

  • ❣心有靈犀xīn yǒu líng xī

    be on the same wavelength — Two hearts beating as one.

    After years as partners, they are on the same wavelength.

Opposite

  • 🙉話不投機半句多huà bù tóu jī bàn jù duō

    have nothing to say to each other; not see eye to eye — When you don't get along, even half a sentence is too much.

    We don't see eye to eye, so let's call it a day.

  • 🗨各執一詞gè zhí yī cí

    each tell a different story; stick to one's own version — Each side sticks to its own story.

    The two men each gave a different account of what happened, leaving the police unable to decide.
