2 · Change & Fate

引咎辭職

yǐn jiù cí zhí

In English

  • resign in disgrace

Meaning

Resigning to take responsibility.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • take responsibility and resign
  • step down

Example

部長因資料外洩引咎辭職。

The minister resigned in disgrace over the data leak.

Usage

For officials and executives accepting blame for a serious failure.

Similar

  • 🛠️責無旁貸zé wú páng dài

    it's my duty — Duty-bound, but not necessarily resigning.

    Looking after my parents is my duty.

  • 🏞️急流勇退jí liú yǒng tuì

    quit while one is ahead; go out on top — Retiring at the top, with no wrongdoing involved.

    He chose to go out on top, retiring after his best season.

Opposite

  • 🪑尸位素餐shī wèi sù cān

    draw a salary without doing the job; hold a sinecure — Holding a sinecure.

    Officials who draw a salary without doing the job should resign.

  • 🛌逃避責任táo bì zé rèn

    skive v — Dodging responsibility.

    He tried to skive off his share of the housework.
