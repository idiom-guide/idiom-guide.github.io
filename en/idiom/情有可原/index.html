情有可原

qíng yǒu kě yuán

In English

  • it makes sense that
  • be forgiven for sth

Meaning

Understandable, even forgivable, given the circumstances.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • be forgivable
  • there are extenuating circumstances
  • it is understandable

Example

他是因為車禍耽擱才錯過開場，情有可原。

He was held up by an accident, so he can be forgiven for missing the start.

Usage

Common pattern: 情有可原，但法不容情 (understandable, but the law makes no exceptions).

Similar

  • 👐無可厚非wú kě hòu fēi

    there is nothing wrong with; be understandable — Not deserving much blame.

    There is nothing wrong with planning for your own future.

  • ℹ事出有因shì chū yǒu yīn

    there is a reason behind it — There is a reason for it; asks for understanding, not forgiveness.

    There is a reason behind his temper today.

Opposite

  • 🈲十惡不赦shí è bù shè

    be utterly unpardonable; be beyond redemption — Unpardonably evil; very strong.

    The criminal did so much evil that he is seen as beyond redemption.

  • 👈難辭其咎nán cí qí jiù

    bear the blame; cannot escape responsibility — Can't escape the blame; about responsibility.

    The supervisor cannot escape responsibility for this accident.
