2 · Competition & Power

使其挫敗

shǐ qí cuò bài

In English

  • thwart
  • foil

Meaning

Thwarting someone.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • frustrate

Example

他們的計畫眼看就要成功，對手卻在最後一刻出手，使其挫敗。

Their plan was thwarted at the last minute.

Usage

Formal; for military matters, plots and competition.

Similar

  • 🦟從中作梗cóng zhōng zuò gěng

    stand in the way; put obstacles in the way — Throwing a spanner in the works.

    Someone stood in the way, and that is why the deal fell through.

  • 🪓動搖根基dòng yáo gēn jī

    thesis breakers — Shaking the foundations.

    That one experiment was a thesis breaker; it shook the foundations of the entire theory.

Opposite

  • ❄️雪中送炭xuě zhōng sòng tàn

    help when it is needed most — Help when it's needed most.

    A true friend helps when it is needed most.

  • 😊樂見其成lè jiàn qí chéng

    be glad to see it happen; welcome — Happy to see it succeed.

    We would be glad to see the two companies merge.
