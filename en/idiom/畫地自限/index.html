畫地自限

huà dì zì xiàn

In English

  • limitations are self-imposed

Meaning

Self-imposed limits.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • box oneself in
  • hold oneself back

Example

你的限制是畫地自限，試試新事物吧。

Your limitations are self-imposed, so try something new.

Usage

Often encouragement to break out of one's comfort zone.

Similar

  • 🚋墨守成規mò shǒu chéng guī

    stick to old rules; stay in a rut — Sticking to the old rules.

    The company sticks to old rules and finds it hard to innovate.

  • ➡️單向思考dān xiàng sī kǎo

    one-track mind — A one-track mind.

    He has a one-track mind and thinks only about work.

Opposite

  • ✉️超越極限chāo yuè jí xiàn

    push the envelope — Pushing the envelope.

    The new engine pushes the envelope of what electric cars can do.

  • 🔥開創新局kāi chuàng xīn jú

    blaze a trail — Blazing a trail.

    The startup blazed a trail in electric bikes.
