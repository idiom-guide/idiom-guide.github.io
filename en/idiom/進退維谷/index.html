4 · Hardship & Decline

進退維谷

jìn tuì wéi gǔ

In English

  • be in a quandary
  • caught in a bind

Meaning

In a quandary.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • between a rock and a hard place

Example

她對這份工作邀約進退維谷。

She is in a quandary over the job offer.

Usage

From the Book of Songs.

Similar

  • 🐯騎虎難下qí hǔ nán xià

    stuck in an awkward situation; holding a wolf by the ears — Stuck with no easy way out.

    Having promised too much, he is stuck in an awkward situation, damned if he does and damned if he doesn't.

  • ↔️左右為難zuǒ yòu wéi nán

    caught between a rock and a hard place — Caught between a rock and a hard place.

    I'm caught between a rock and a hard place.

Opposite

  • 🏄左右逢源zuǒ yòu féng yuán

    reap rewards; benefit from both sides — Benefiting from both sides.

    A savvy broker can reap rewards by benefiting from both sides.

  • 🔪迎刃而解yíng rèn ér jiě

    sth be quickly resolved — Quickly resolved.

    Once the main issue was settled, the other problems were quickly resolved.
