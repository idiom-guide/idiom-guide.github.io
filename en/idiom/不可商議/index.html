不可商議

bù kě shāng yì

In English

  • nonnegotiable

Meaning

Nonnegotiable.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • not up for debate
  • set in stone

Example

安全規定不可商議。

The safety rules are nonnegotiable.

Usage

Formal; in speech, 沒得商量 is more common.

Similar

  • 🙅‍♂️沒得商量méi de shāng liang

    non-negotiable; not up for discussion — Not up for discussion.

    The deadline is non-negotiable.

  • 🔒不容爭辯bù róng zhēng biàn

    inarguable — Beyond dispute.

    The evidence is so clear that the conclusion is inarguable.

Opposite

  • ↔️轉圜空間zhuǎn huán kōng jiān

    room to maneuver; wiggle room — Room to maneuver.

    There is little room to maneuver in the negotiations.

  • 💬開放討論kāi fàng tǎo lùn

    open to interpretation; be open-minded — Open for discussion.

    The proposal is open for discussion, and we are open-minded about changes.
