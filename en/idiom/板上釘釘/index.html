板上釘釘

bǎn shàng dìng dīng

In English

  • a done deal
  • locked in

Meaning

Settled; a done deal that won't change.Neutral · Colloquial

Example

合約已經簽了，這筆交易板上釘釘了。

The contract has been signed, so the deal is locked in.

Usage

Often 已經是板上釘釘的事; used of results and decisions.

Similar

  • ⛵木已成舟mù yǐ chéng zhōu

    ship has sailed — What's done can't be undone; somewhat resigned.

    It's too late to complain; the ship has sailed.

  • 🏀十拿九穩shí ná jiǔ wěn

    be all but certain; be a safe bet — Almost certain; a forecast, not a settled fact.

    With this contract in hand, winning the bid is all but certain.

Opposite

  • 🎈懸而未決xuán ér wèi jué

    sth be still up in the air; sth be up in the air — Still undecided.

    The decision is still up in the air.

  • ✏️尚未定案shàng wèi dìng àn

    be yet to be finalized; be still under discussion — Not yet finalized; for plans and documents.

    The itinerary has yet to be finalized, so please wait a moment.
