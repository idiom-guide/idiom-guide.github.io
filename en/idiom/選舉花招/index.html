選舉花招

xuǎn jǔ huā zhāo

In English

  • electioneering

Meaning

Campaign gimmicks.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • campaign gimmicks
  • political stunts

Example

選民已經厭倦選舉花招和空洞的承諾。

Voters are tired of electioneering and empty promises.

Usage

Usually critical.

Similar

  • 💨煙霧彈yān wù dàn

    a smokescreen; a red herring — A smokescreen.

    The announcement was just a smokescreen for the layoffs.

  • 🎪嘩眾取寵huá zhòng qǔ chǒng

    play to the gallery; sensationalize — Playing to the gallery.

    The mayor's stunts were just playing to the gallery.

Opposite

  • 👣腳踏實地jiǎo tà shí dì

    staying grounded and pushing ahead — Down to earth.

    He is a down-to-earth man who stays grounded and keeps pushing ahead.

  • 🤝言行一致yán xíng yī zhì

    practice what one preaches; walk the talk — Practicing what one preaches.

    A good leader practices what he preaches.
