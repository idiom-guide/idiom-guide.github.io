不動聲色

bù dòng shēng sè

In English

  • composed
  • equanimity

Meaning

Keeping a straight face; showing nothing.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • poker-faced

Example

宣判時，他不動聲色，沒有流露任何情緒。

He stayed composed and showed no emotion when the verdict was read.

Usage

Can mean composure, or acting quietly without tipping one's hand.

Similar

  • 😐面不改色miàn bù gǎi sè

    not (even) bat an eyelid — Not batting an eyelid; stresses nerve.

    He didn't bat an eyelid when he heard the price.

  • 🧊沉得住氣chén de zhù qì

    keep one's cool; stay patient — Keeping one's cool.

    Keep your cool and wait for the right moment.

Opposite

  • 😱大驚小怪dà jīng xiǎo guài

    tiny problem gigantic reaction; drama queen — Making a fuss over nothing.

    Don't be a drama queen; it's only a scratch.

  • 😨驚慌失措jīng huāng shī cuò

    panic; consternation, To sb's consternation, fill sb with consternation — Panicking.

    To the teacher's consternation, the whole class started laughing.
