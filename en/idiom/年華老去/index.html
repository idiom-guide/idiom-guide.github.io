年華老去

nián huá lǎo qù

In English

  • long in the tooth

Meaning

Long in the tooth.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • past one's prime
  • getting on

Example

這位演員年華老去，不太適合演動作片了。

The actor is getting long in the tooth for action roles.

Usage

For actors and athletes, or reflecting on the passing years.

Similar

  • 🌇風光不再fēng guāng bù zài

    sb has lost one's shine; have seen better days — Having seen better days.

    The old star hotel has seen better days.

Opposite

  • 🌠青年才俊qīng nián cái jùn

    young talents — Young talent.

    The company is full of young talents.

  • 🐉生龍活虎shēng lóng huó hǔ

    be alive and well — Full of life.

    Despite his age, grandpa is alive and well and plays tennis every day.
