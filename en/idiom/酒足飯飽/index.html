酒足飯飽

jiǔ zú fàn bǎo

In English

  • sated
  • well fed

Meaning

Enough wine drunk and enough rice eaten: pleasantly full after a meal.Neutral · General

Example

酒足飯飽之後，大家坐在院子裡聊天。

Once everyone was well fed, we sat in the yard and talked.

Usage

Describes the satisfied state after a feast, sometimes with a lazy or teasing tone.

Similar

  • 🍻賓主盡歡bīn zhǔ jìn huān

    a good time was had by all — Hosts and guests alike enjoy themselves; about the mood.

    The dinner ended on a high note, and a good time was had by all.

  • 🍚豐衣足食fēng yī zú shí

    well-fed and well-clothed — Well off, with food and clothes in plenty; about a way of life, not one meal.

    A generation ago, being well-fed and well-clothed was already a blessing.

Opposite

  • 😫飢腸轆轆jī cháng lù lù

    famished; ravenous — Stomach rumbling with hunger.

    After working late, I walked into the convenience store famished.

  • 🍞食不果腹shí bù guǒ fù

    go hungry; not have enough to eat — Not enough to eat; describes poverty.

    In the bitter winter, many people went hungry.
