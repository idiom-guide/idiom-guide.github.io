白手起家

bái shǒu qǐ jiā

In English

  • a self-made man/woman
  • start from scratch

Meaning

Self-made.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • self-made
  • pull oneself up by one's bootstraps

Example

她白手起家，從零開始打拚。

She is a self-made woman who started from scratch.

Usage

For stories of starting a business or getting rich.

Similar

  • 🚵力爭上游lì zhēng shàng yóu

    strive to move upward; aim high — Striving to get ahead.

    He strove upward and finally got into the school he wanted.

  • 👣腳踏實地jiǎo tà shí dì

    staying grounded and pushing ahead — Down-to-earth.

    He is a down-to-earth man who stays grounded and keeps pushing ahead.

Opposite

  • 🛍️紈絝子弟wán kù zǐ dì

    spoiled rich kid; playboy — A spoiled rich kid.

    The spoiled rich kid crashed his father's car.

  • 🥪不勞而獲bù láo ér huò

    get something for nothing; get a free lunch — Something for nothing.

    There is no such thing as a free lunch.
