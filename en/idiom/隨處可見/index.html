隨處可見

suí chù kě jiàn

In English

  • a dime a dozen

Meaning

A dime a dozen.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • everywhere
  • two a penny

Example

這座城市咖啡店隨處可見。

Coffee shops are a dime a dozen in this city.

Usage

For shops, phenomena and things.

Similar

  • 👀比比皆是bǐ bǐ jiē shì

    everywhere; all too common — Everywhere.

    Such errors are everywhere in the report.

  • 🐂多如牛毛duō rú niú máo

    a dime a dozen — Countless.

    Apps like this are a dime a dozen.

Opposite

  • 🦄鳳毛麟角fèng máo lín jiǎo

    few and far between; extremely rare — Few and far between.

    Interpreters with that language pair are few and far between.

  • 🤏寥寥無幾liáo liáo wú jǐ

    very few; a mere handful — A mere handful.

    Very few applicants met the requirements.
