一掃而空

yī sǎo ér kōng

In English

  • make a clean sweep of sth
  • sweep sth away

Meaning

Making a clean sweep.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • make a clean sweep of
  • sweep away
  • clear out

Example

不到一小時，粉絲就把架上的商品一掃而空。

Fans made a clean sweep of the shelves within an hour.

Usage

For selling out, or for clearing away gloom or doubt.

Similar

  • 💨蕩然無存dàng rán wú cún

    vaporized, all gone — All gone.

    After the flood, nothing was left; every trace of the village had vanished.

  • 💨煙消雲散yān xiāo yún sàn

    vaporized, vanished, be gone — Vanishing into thin air.

    His worries vanished the moment he heard the news.

Opposite

  • 🛍️應有盡有yīng yǒu jìn yǒu

    have everything you could want — Having everything one could want.

    The market has everything you could want.

  • 🔢不計其數bù jì qí shù

    countless — Countless.

    Countless families were affected.
