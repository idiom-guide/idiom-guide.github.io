正義終將伸張

zhèng yì zhōng jiāng shēn zhāng

In English

  • justice will be done

Meaning

Justice will prevail in the end.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • justice will prevail
  • truth and justice will win out

Example

耐心點，正義終將伸張。

Be patient; justice will be done in the end.

Usage

Said to comfort or encourage victims and those waiting for an outcome.

Similar

  • 🕸️法網恢恢，疏而不漏fǎ wǎng huī huī, shū ér bù lòu

    the long arm of the law catches up in the end — The net of the law catches every wrongdoer.

    However long he ran, the long arm of the law caught up with him in the end.

  • ♻惡有惡報è yǒu è bào

    the wicked get what they deserve; what goes around comes around — Evil gets its due, not necessarily through the law.

    Those who do evil will in the end get what they deserve.

Opposite

  • 🔓逍遙法外xiāo yáo fǎ wài

    at large — Getting away with a crime.

    The fraudster is still at large.

  • ⚱冤沉海底yuān chén hǎi dǐ

    an injustice goes unredressed; be wronged and never vindicated — A wrong that is never righted.

    Without a witness to come forward, this case may remain an unredressed injustice.
