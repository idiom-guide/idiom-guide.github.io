勢如破竹

shì rú pò zhú

In English

  • sweep all before one
  • unstoppable

Meaning

Sweeping all before it.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • carry all before one

Example

大軍勢如破竹。

The army swept all before it.

Usage

From the History of Jin's account of Du Yu's campaign; for armies, teams and campaigns.

Similar

  • 🚂勢不可擋shì bù kě dǎng

    unstoppable; irresistible — Unstoppable.

    The team looked unstoppable.

  • ⚔️所向披靡suǒ xiàng pī mǐ

    unbeatable — Unbeatable.

    The team was unbeatable that season.

Opposite

  • 🏳️潰不成軍kuì bù chéng jūn

    in full rout — In full rout.

    The invaders were in full rout by dawn.

  • 🤕一敗塗地yī bài tú dì

    fall flat on your face — Falling flat on one's face.

    The campaign fell flat on its face.
