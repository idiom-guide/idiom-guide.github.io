粗製濫造

cū zhì làn zào

In English

  • shoddy
  • churn out shoddy goods

Meaning

Made crudely and carelessly, with quantity put before quality.Negative · General

Example

為了搶快賺錢，這批仿冒品粗製濫造，用不了幾天就壞了。

Made in a rush to turn a quick profit, the knock-offs were shoddy and broke within days.

Usage

Mostly for products, works or content; also used to criticize films or publications that are turned out in bulk but poor in quality.

Similar

  • 🧱偷工減料tōu gōng jiǎn liào

    cut corners; skimp on materials — Skimping on steps and materials; about the method.

    The developer cut corners, and mold appeared on the walls within a year.

  • 🐟魚目混珠yú mù hùn zhū

    pass off fakes as genuine — Passing off fakes as genuine; about deception.

    Some sellers pass off fakes as genuine.

Opposite

  • 💎貨真價實huò zhēn jià shí

    the genuine article — The genuine article, true to its name.

    Customers want to know the product is the genuine article.

  • 🔬精益求精jīng yì qiú jīng

    strive for perfection; keep refining — Perfecting what is already good.

    He keeps refining his work until he is satisfied.
