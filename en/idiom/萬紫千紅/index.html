萬紫千紅

wàn zǐ qiān hóng

In English

  • a riot of colors

Meaning

A riot of colors.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • a riot of color
  • a blaze of color
  • in full bloom

Example

春天讓山坡一片萬紫千紅。

Spring brought a riot of colors to the hillside.

Usage

For spring blossoms; found in Zhu Xi's poem Spring Day.

Similar

  • 🌺奼紫嫣紅chà zǐ yān hóng

    a blaze of bright colors — A blaze of bright colors.

    In April the garden turns into a blaze of bright colors.

  • 🌈絢麗多姿xuàn lì duō zī

    gorgeous and colorful — Gorgeous and colorful.

    The festival costumes were gorgeous and colorful.
