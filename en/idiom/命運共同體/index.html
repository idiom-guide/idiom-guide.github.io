命運共同體

mìng yùn gòng tóng tǐ

In English

  • stake in...survival

Meaning

A community with a shared destiny.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • a shared destiny
  • have a stake in its survival
  • in the same boat

Example

村裡每一戶人家都是這座村子的命運共同體。

Every family in the village has a stake in its survival.

Usage

For nations, communities and supply chains.

Similar

  • 🦷唇亡齒寒chún wáng chǐ hán

    share a common fate — Lips and teeth: if one goes, the other suffers.

    The neighbors share a common fate: if one falls, the other is exposed.

  • ⚔️生死與共shēng sǐ yǔ gòng

    live with it, die with it — Living or dying together.

    The two crews stood by each other, ready to live or die together.

Opposite

  • 📴事不關己shì bù guān jǐ

    none of one's business; not one's concern — Not one's business.

    It was none of his business, so he stood by and did nothing.

  • 🍿作壁上觀zuò bì shàng guān

    watch from the sidelines; stand idly by — Watching from the sidelines.

    While the two firms fought a price war, the rest of the industry could only watch from the sidelines.
