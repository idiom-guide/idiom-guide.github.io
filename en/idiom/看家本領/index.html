看家本領

kān jiā běn lǐng

In English

  • sth is sb's craft (=skill, experienced in sth.)

Meaning

One's signature skill.Positive · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • sth is sb's craft
  • one's signature skill
  • one's bread and butter

Example

修理老錶是他的看家本領，已經做了四十年。

Fixing old watches is his craft, and he has done it for forty years.

Usage

Colloquial; the skill someone is best known for.

Similar

  • 🎾獨家招式dú jiā zhāo shì

    signature shot — A signature move.

    The tennis star's drop volley is her signature shot.

  • 🔥爐火純青lú huǒ chún qīng

    consummate skill — Consummate skill.

    The carver works with consummate skill.

Opposite

  • 🙄眼高手低yǎn gāo shǒu dī

    aim high but fall short; all talk and no skill — All talk and no skill.

    He aims high but falls short.

  • 🐴黔驢技窮qián lǘ jì qióng

    come to the end of one's tricks; at one's wits' end — At one's wits' end.

    Out of arguments, he was at his wits' end.
