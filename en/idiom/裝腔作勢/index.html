裝腔作勢

zhuāng qiāng zuò shì

In English

  • charade
  • posturing + pompous

Meaning

Posturing.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • posturing
  • pompous

Example

他整場演說都在裝腔作勢。

His whole speech was a pompous charade.

Usage

For speeches, attitudes and airs.

Similar

  • 🤵裝模作樣zhuāng mú zuò yàng

    put on airs; act affected — Putting on an act.

    He put on airs, leafing through the report without reading a word.

  • 🐶虛張聲勢xū zhāng shēng shì

    bluff; more bark than bite — A bluff.

    His threats were just a bluff; he is more bark than bite.

Opposite

  • 🌾樸實無華pǔ shí wú huá

    unsophisticated — Plain and unadorned.

    The village church is unsophisticated, yet beautiful.

  • 💗真情流露zhēn qíng liú lù

    the revelation of true inner feelings — Heartfelt.

    His speech was a revelation of his true inner feelings.
