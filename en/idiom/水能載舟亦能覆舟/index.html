水能載舟，亦能覆舟

shuǐ néng zài zhōu, yì néng fù zhōu

In English

  • double edged sword

Meaning

Something that can both help and harm.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • double-edged sword
  • cut both ways
  • a mixed blessing

Example

對任何品牌來說，社群媒體都是水能載舟，亦能覆舟。

Social media is a double-edged sword for any brand.

Usage

Formal; originally a metaphor of the people as water and the ruler as a boat; now for anything with both benefits and risks.

Similar

  • ☯️福禍相依fú huò xiāng yī

    fortune and misfortune are intertwined — Fortune and misfortune turning into each other.

    Fortune and misfortune are intertwined.

  • 🗡雙面刃shuāng miàn rèn

    double-edged sword; cut both ways — The everyday way to say it; same meaning.

    AI is a double-edged sword: used well it saves time, used badly it causes errors.

Opposite

  • ✌️兩全其美liǎng quán qí měi

    get the best of both — Satisfying both sides at once.

    The new plan lets us get the best of both.

  • 🚮一無是處yī wú shì chù

    be good for nothing; have no redeeming quality — Having no merit at all.

    He talks as if he has not one redeeming quality.
