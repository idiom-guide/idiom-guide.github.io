開誠布公

kāi chéng bù gōng

In English

  • in full transparency

Meaning

Frank and open; laying one's cards on the table.Positive · Formal

Other English renderings

  • be frank and open
  • lay one's cards on the table

Example

兩位領導人開誠布公地談了彼此的分歧。

The two leaders spoke in full transparency about their differences.

Usage

Common in negotiations and communication; stresses sincerity and openness.

Similar

  • 🖖坦誠相對tǎn chéng xiāng duì

    level with sb — Being completely honest with each other.

    I'll level with you: we are running out of money.

  • 🤲推心置腹tuī xīn zhì fù

    speak from the heart; confide in sb — Opening one's heart to someone.

    After years of friendship, she could confide in him about anything.

Opposite

  • 😷遮遮掩掩zhē zhē yǎn yǎn

    be evasive; hide and cover up — Hiding and covering up.

    He was evasive and wouldn't tell the truth.

  • 🧣欲蓋彌彰yù gài mí zhāng

    draw attention by trying to cover up; backfire — The more one hides, the more it shows.

    His long denial only drew attention; trying to cover up the mistake backfired.
