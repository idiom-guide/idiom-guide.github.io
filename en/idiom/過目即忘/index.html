4 · Incompetence & Failure

過目即忘

guò mù jí wàng

In English

  • underwhelming

Meaning

Instantly forgettable.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • instantly forgettable
  • forgettable

Example

這部電影令人失望，過目即忘。

The film was underwhelming and instantly forgettable.

Usage

For works, ads and performances.

Similar

  • 😐印象平平yìn xiàng píng píng

    unimpressed with sth — Unimpressed.

    The critics were unimpressed with the sequel.

  • 🥱平淡無奇píng dàn wú qí

    run-of-the-mill — Run-of-the-mill.

    It was just a run-of-the-mill day at the office.

Opposite

  • 📽歷歷在目lì lì zài mù

    be vivid before one's eyes; be clearly visible in one's mind — Vivid in one's memory.

    The scene from that day is still vivid before my eyes.

  • 🤩歎為觀止tàn wéi guān zhǐ

    breathtaking; awe-inspiring — Breathtaking.

    The view from the summit was breathtaking.
