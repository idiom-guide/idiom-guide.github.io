應接不暇

yìng jiē bù xiá

In English

  • be inundated with sth
  • be flooded with

Meaning

Inundated.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • snowed under

Example

新開的咖啡館訂單應接不暇。

The new café is inundated with orders.

Usage

For too many customers, orders or messages.

Similar

  • 🤹分身乏術fēn shēn fá shù

    spread too thin; can't be in two places at once — Spread too thin.

    With two jobs, I'm spread too thin.

  • 🏪門庭若市mén tíng ruò shì

    bustling with customers — Bustling with customers.

    The new bakery is bustling with customers.

Opposite

  • 🕸️門可羅雀mén kě luó què

    deserted; hardly a customer in sight — Hardly a customer in sight.

    Once packed, the mall is now deserted.

  • 🎈無事一身輕wú shì yī shēn qīng

    unencumbered — Unencumbered.

    With the deadline behind him, he felt unencumbered.
