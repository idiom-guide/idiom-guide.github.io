輸到脫褲

shū dào tuō kù

In English

  • lose sb's shirt

Meaning

Losing one's shirt.Negative · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • lose one's shirt
  • lose everything
  • go bust

Example

他在賭場輸到脫褲。

He lost his shirt at the casino.

Usage

Colloquial; for gambling or investment losses.

Similar

  • 🕳️血本無歸xuè běn wú guī

    lose one's entire investment — Losing one's entire investment.

    Many retail investors lost their entire investment.

  • 🤕一敗塗地yī bài tú dì

    fall flat on your face — Falling flat on one's face.

    The campaign fell flat on its face.

Opposite

  • 💰大賺一筆dà zhuàn yī bǐ

    made a killing on sth — Making a killing.

    He made a killing on the stock market.
