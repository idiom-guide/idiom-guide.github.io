2 · Change & Fate

分道揚鑣

fēn dào yáng biāo

In English

  • part ways

Meaning

Parting ways.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • go separate ways
  • split up

Example

計畫結束後，合夥人決定分道揚鑣。

After the project, the partners decided to part ways.

Usage

For partners, groups and couples; not necessarily hostile.

Similar

  • ↔️漸行漸遠jiàn xíng jiàn yuǎn

    grow apart — Growing apart gradually.

    After they moved to different cities, the friends slowly grew apart.

  • 🍂天下無不散的筵席tiān xià wú bù sàn de yán xí

    all good things must come to an end — All good things must come to an end; more sentimental.

    All good things must come to an end, and so must this conference.

Opposite

  • ⛵同舟共濟tóng zhōu gòng jì

    solidarity; help each other — Pulling together in a crisis.

    In times of crisis, neighbors help each other.

  • 🤝志同道合zhì tóng dào hé

    like minded — Like-minded.

    She found like-minded friends in the chess club.
