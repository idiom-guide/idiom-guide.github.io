鋪天蓋地

pū tiān gài dì

In English

  • blanket
  • overwhelming

Meaning

Blanket.Neutral · General

Other English renderings

  • wall-to-wall coverage

Example

產品發表會得到鋪天蓋地的媒體報導。

The launch received blanket media coverage.

Usage

For publicity, media coverage and disasters.

Similar

  • 📱無所不在wú suǒ bù zài

    ubiquitous — Ubiquitous.

    Smartphones are ubiquitous.

  • 🌨️滿山遍谷mǎn shān biàn gǔ

    in the blizzard of — A blizzard of.

    Cherry petals fell like a blizzard of pink snow across the valley.

Opposite

  • 🤏寥寥無幾liáo liáo wú jǐ

    very few; a mere handful — A mere handful.

    Very few applicants met the requirements.
