情急之下

qíng jí zhī xià

In English

  • in desperation

Meaning

In desperation; in the heat of the moment.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • in the heat of the moment
  • in a panic

Example

情急之下，他抓起一根棍子把狗趕走。

In desperation, he grabbed a stick and chased the dog away.

Usage

Often at the start of a sentence, followed by a spur-of-the-moment action.

Similar

  • 🆘迫於情勢pò yú qíng shì

    do sth. out of desperation — Forced by circumstances.

    Out of desperation, the family sold their home.

  • ⚡不假思索bù jiǎ sī suǒ

    on the spur of the moment; on a whim — Without a second thought.

    On the spur of the moment, he jumped into the river to help.

Opposite

  • 🦢從容不迫cóng róng bù pò

    unruffled — Calm and unhurried.

    The host remained unruffled when the lights went out.

  • 😎泰然自若tài rán zì ruò

    unfazed — Composed and unruffled.

    She remained unfazed by the critics' attacks.
