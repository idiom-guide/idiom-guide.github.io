半途而廢

bàn tú ér fèi

In English

  • give up halfway

Meaning

Giving up halfway.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • quit midway
  • throw in the towel

Example

不要半途而廢。

Don't give up halfway.

Usage

Often as encouragement not to quit.

Similar

  • 🐯虎頭蛇尾hǔ tóu shé wěi

    start with a bang and end with a whimper; peter out — Starting with a bang and ending with a whimper.

    The campaign started with a bang and ended with a whimper.

  • 🌤️一曝十寒yī pù shí hán

    sporadic effort — Sporadic effort.

    His sporadic effort won't get him anywhere.

Opposite

  • 🏋️堅持不懈jiān chí bù xiè

    committed — Never letting up.

    Her committed practice paid off in the end.

  • 💧滴水穿石dī shuǐ chuān shí

    constant dripping wears away a stone; perseverance pays off — Perseverance pays off.

    Constant dripping wears away a stone, so keep practicing.
