變本加厲

biàn běn jiā lì

In English

  • go from bad to worse
  • escalate

Meaning

Getting worse than before; escalating.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • get even worse

Example

霸凌者不但沒有收手，反而變本加厲。

Instead of stopping, the bullies went from bad to worse.

Usage

Used of bad behavior or bad situations, never good things.

Similar

  • ↘️每況愈下měi kuàng yù xià

    go from bad to worse — Going downhill; about situations, while 變本加厲 is about behavior intensifying.

    The company's finances went from bad to worse.

  • ❄️雪上加霜xuě shàng jiā shuāng

    to make matters worse; add to one's woes — One misfortune on top of another.

    To make matters worse, the power went out.

Opposite

  • ✋適可而止shì kě ér zhǐ

    know when to stop — Knowing when to stop.

    Criticism is fine, but know when to stop.

  • 🚩偃旗息鼓yǎn qí xī gǔ

    call a halt; lie low — Calling off the fight; quietly backing down.

    The band announced they would call a halt for now: a pause, they said, not a farewell.
