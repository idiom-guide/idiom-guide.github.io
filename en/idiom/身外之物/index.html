身外之物

shēn wài zhī wù

In English

  • expendables

Meaning

Worldly possessions; things not worth taking too seriously.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • worldly possessions
  • material things
  • mere trappings

Example

他把財富和名聲都看作身外之物。

He treats wealth and fame as expendables.

Usage

Often paired with 生不帶來，死不帶去 (you can't take it with you) to urge someone to let go.

Similar

  • 🌥過眼雲煙guò yǎn yún yān

    a passing cloud; be fleeting and insubstantial — Fleeting as passing clouds; often of fame and the past.

    Wealth and glory are no more than passing clouds.

  • ⛩淡泊名利dàn bó míng lì

    be indifferent to fame and fortune — Indifferent to fame and gain; a positive outlook.

    He was indifferent to fame and fortune all his life and only wanted to teach well.

Opposite

  • 🔶視如珍寶shì rú zhēn bǎo

    treasure as a jewel; hold dear — Treasured like a jewel.

    She treasures the ring her grandmother left her.

  • 💟無價之寶wú jià zhī bǎo

    a priceless treasure — Priceless; often of emotional or unique value.

    To him, this letter is a priceless treasure.
