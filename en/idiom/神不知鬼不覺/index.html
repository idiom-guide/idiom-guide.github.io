神不知鬼不覺

shén bù zhī guǐ bù jué

In English

  • perfectly undetectable
  • covert
  • invisible
  • without being noticed

Meaning

Without anyone noticing.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • without anyone noticing
  • covertly
  • on the quiet

Example

他神不知鬼不覺地溜出了派對。

He slipped out of the party without being noticed.

Usage

For slipping away or doing things on the sly; neutral to negative.

Similar

  • 🕵️暗渡陳倉àn dù chén cāng

    take a covert route; feint to the east and strike to the west — Taking a covert route.

    The general feinted to the east and struck to the west.

  • 👻難以察覺nán yǐ chá jué

    imperceptible — Barely noticeable.

    The change in pitch was almost imperceptible.

Opposite

  • 🚩大張旗鼓dà zhāng qí gǔ

    with great fanfare — With great fanfare.

    The mayor launched the campaign with great fanfare.

  • 🎥眾目睽睽zhòng mù kuí kuí

    in plain view of everyone; in front of everybody — In plain view of everyone.

    He signed the contract in front of everybody.
