意料之外

yì liào zhī wài

In English

  • unexpected

Meaning

Unexpected.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • beyond expectations
  • out of the blue
  • come as a surprise

Example

比賽結果令人意料之外。

The result was unexpected.

Usage

Usually a noun phrase: 在意料之外 or 意料之外的結果; plainer and less surprised than 出乎意料.

Similar

  • 🎉出乎意料chū hū yì liào

    take sb by surprise — Contrary to expectations.

    The sudden announcement took everyone by surprise.

  • 😯始料未及shǐ liào wèi jí

    never have anticipated; be caught off guard — Not foreseen at all.

    The scale of the epidemic was something experts never anticipated.

Opposite

  • 🉑意料之中yì liào zhī zhōng

    come as no surprise; be as expected — As expected.

    The team's loss came as no surprise.

  • 🔮不出所料bù chū suǒ liào

    predictably — As predicted.

    Predictably, the train was late again on the first day of school.
