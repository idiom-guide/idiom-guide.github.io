不速之客

bù sù zhī kè

In English

  • an uninvited guest
  • gatecrasher

Meaning

An uninvited guest.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • a gatecrasher
  • an unexpected visitor

Example

晚餐吃到一半，來了一位不速之客。

An uninvited guest arrived in the middle of dinner.

Usage

From the Book of Changes; usually unwelcome.

Similar

  • 📩不請自來bù qǐng zì lái

    turn up uninvited; show up unasked — Turning up uninvited.

    He turned up uninvited and brought three friends.

  • 🎭喧賓奪主xuān bīn duó zhǔ

    be upstaged by; a guest upstages the host — A guest upstaging the host.

    The flashy frame upstaged the painting, like a guest upstaging the host.

Opposite

  • 🛋️賓至如歸bīn zhì rú guī

    feel at home — Making guests feel at home.

    We hope you will feel at home during your stay.

  • 🛎️備受禮遇bèi shòu lǐ yù

    feel very privileged — Treated with great courtesy.

    The visiting scholars felt very privileged.
