趾高氣昂

zhǐ gāo qì áng

In English

  • on one's high horse

Meaning

On one's high horse.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • strut
  • swagger

Example

他只因為升了職，就趾高氣昂起來。

Just because he got promoted, he's on his high horse.

Usage

For arrogance after a promotion or rise; also written 趾高氣揚.

Similar

  • 😏得意洋洋dé yì yáng yáng

    triumphant; smug — Smug.

    He grinned, smug about winning.

  • 😏不可一世bù kě yī shì

    act high and mighty; cocky — High and mighty.

    The new champion acted high and mighty.

Opposite

  • 🙇‍♂️虛懷若谷xū huái ruò gǔ

    be humble and open-minded — Humble and open-minded.

    The more accomplished a person is, the more humble and open-minded they tend to be.

  • 🤗平易近人píng yì jìn rén

    approachable — Approachable.

    The professor is so approachable that students visit him with every question.
