7 · Public Opinion

以訛傳訛

yǐ é chuán é

In English

  • spread misinformation

Meaning

Spreading misinformation.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • Chinese whispers
  • garble a story

Example

大家沒查證就轉貼，以訛傳訛。

People shared the post without checking and spread misinformation.

Usage

For rumors and online resharing.

Similar

  • 🐯三人成虎sān rén chéng hǔ

    a lie repeated often enough becomes the truth — A lie repeated often enough.

    Online, a lie repeated often enough becomes the truth.

  • 🎐捕風捉影bǔ fēng zhuō yǐng

    chase shadows; act on groundless rumors — Chasing shadows.

    These reports are groundless speculation with no evidence behind them.

Opposite

  • ⚛實事求是shí shì qiú shì

    seek truth from facts; be down-to-earth — Seeking truth from facts.

    Research should seek truth from facts, not start from a conclusion and look for evidence.

  • 📇有憑有據yǒu píng yǒu jù

    be well grounded; be backed by evidence — Backed by evidence.

    His accusation is backed by evidence; it's not empty talk.
