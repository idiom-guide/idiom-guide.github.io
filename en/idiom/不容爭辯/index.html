不容爭辯

bù róng zhēng biàn

In English

  • inarguable

Meaning

Beyond dispute; leaves no room for argument.Neutral · Formal

Example

證據如此明確，結論不容爭辯。

The evidence is so clear that the conclusion is inarguable.

Usage

Can also describe a domineering tone that permits no objection.

Similar

  • ✔️無庸置疑wú yōng zhì yí

    indisputable — Beyond doubt; about certainty, not forbidding argument.

    It is indisputable that regular exercise improves health.

  • 🗄鐵證如山tiě zhèng rú shān

    the evidence is irrefutable; the evidence is overwhelming — Evidence as solid as a mountain.

    The evidence is irrefutable; he can no longer argue his way out.

Opposite

  • 💬眾說紛紜zhòng shuō fēn yún

    opinions vary widely — Opinions vary widely; no consensus.

    Opinions vary widely on what caused the blackout.

  • 🤷‍♂️莫衷一是mò zhōng yī shì

    be unable to agree on any one view — So many views that no one can decide.

    Experts disagree, and the public cannot tell which view to believe.
