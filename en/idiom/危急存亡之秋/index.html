危急存亡之秋

wēi jí cún wáng zhī qiū

In English

  • moment of existential threat
  • sth is existential

Meaning

A moment of existential threat.Negative · Formal

Other English renderings

  • a make-or-break moment

Example

公司面臨危急存亡之秋。

The company faces a moment of existential threat.

Usage

From Zhuge Liang's 'Memorial on Dispatching the Troops'.

Similar

  • 🏰深陷危機shēn xiàn wēi jī

    under siege — Under siege.

    The island nation is under siege from rising seas.

  • 🔥大難臨頭dà nàn lín tóu

    disaster looms; doom is at hand — Disaster is about to strike.

    The staff laughed at the alarm, but this was no fire drill: disaster was about to hit.

Opposite

  • 🕊️長治久安cháng zhì jiǔ ān

    lasting stability — Lasting stability.

    Lasting stability depends on institutions, not on any single leader.

  • 🎤歌舞昇平gē wǔ shēng píng

    peaceful and prosperous — Peaceful and prosperous.

    The capital looked peaceful and prosperous, with no sign of the war to come.
