密切關注

mì qiè guān zhù

In English

  • keep tabs on sth/sb

Meaning

Keeping a close eye on.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • keep a close eye on
  • keep tabs on
  • monitor closely

Example

投資人正密切關注央行的下一步動作。

Investors are keeping tabs on the central bank's next move.

Usage

Common in news: 密切關注…情勢 or 動向; neutral, sometimes implying monitoring.

Similar

  • 📡熱切關注rè qiè guān zhù

    follow closely; with keen interest — Following eagerly, with interest or hope.

    The public is following the trial closely.

  • 🧲全神貫注quán shén guàn zhù

    be fully absorbed; with rapt attention — Fully focused; about concentration.

    The children were fully absorbed in the story.

Opposite

  • 😑漠不關心mò bù guān xīn

    uncaring — Indifferent.

    The landlord was uncaring about his tenants' complaints.

  • 📴事不關己shì bù guān jǐ

    none of one's business; not one's concern — Not one's business; staying out of it.

    It was none of his business, so he stood by and did nothing.
