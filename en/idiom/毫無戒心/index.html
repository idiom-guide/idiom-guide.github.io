毫無戒心

háo wú jiè xīn

In English

  • unsuspecting

Meaning

Unsuspecting; completely off guard.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • off one's guard
  • trusting

Example

毫無戒心的遊客直接走進了陷阱。

The unsuspecting tourists walked straight into the trap.

Usage

Describes people just before being tricked or attacked; sympathetic or cautionary.

Similar

  • ☑️信以為真xìn yǐ wéi zhēn

    take sth at face value; believe it to be true — Falling for a lie.

    She took the rumor at face value.

  • 🛡️深信不疑shēn xìn bù yí

    an unshakable faith — Trusting completely.

    She has an unshakable faith in her students.

Opposite

  • 👀提高警覺tí gāo jǐng jué

    eyes up — Staying on the alert.

    Keep your eyes up when you cross the road.

  • 🥚小心翼翼xiǎo xīn yì yì

    tread carefully; be extremely careful — Treading carefully.

    He chose his words very carefully to avoid trouble.
