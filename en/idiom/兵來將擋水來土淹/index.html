兵來將擋，水來土淹

bīng lái jiàng dǎng, shuǐ lái tǔ yān

In English

  • roll with the punches

Meaning

Whatever comes, we'll handle it.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • cross that bridge when we come to it
  • handle whatever comes

Example

不管出什麼問題，兵來將擋，水來土淹。

Whatever problems come up, we'll just roll with the punches.

Usage

Calm and confident; often used to reassure someone.

Similar

  • 🤺見招拆招jiàn zhāo chāi zhāo

    react to each move as it comes — Reacting to each move as it comes.

    We have no fixed plan; we'll just react to each move as it comes.

  • 🦎隨機應變suí jī yìng biàn

    think on one's feet; improvise — Thinking on one's feet.

    A good interpreter must think on her feet.

Opposite

  • 😵措手不及cuò shǒu bù jí

    caught/got sb off guard; XD — Caught off guard.

    The sudden storm caught us off guard.

  • 🤔猶豫不決yóu yù bù jué

    dilly-dally — Unable to make up one's mind.

    Stop dilly-dallying and make a decision.
