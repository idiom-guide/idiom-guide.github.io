奉為聖經

fèng wéi shèng jīng

In English

  • it has become scripture

Meaning

Treating something as gospel.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • become scripture
  • treat sth as gospel
  • swear by

Example

她的書被年輕創業家奉為聖經。

Her book has become scripture for young entrepreneurs.

Usage

Neutral, but can imply blind worship.

Similar

  • 📜奉為聖旨fèng wéi shèng zhǐ

    enshrine — Treating someone's word as law.

    His followers enshrined every word he said as law.

  • 💰金科玉律jīn kē yù lǜ

    golden rules — A golden rule.

    "The customer comes first" is the golden rule in our shop.

Opposite

  • 👃嗤之以鼻chī zhī yǐ bí

    pour scorn on sb; scorn — Sniffing at it.

    Experts poured scorn on the miracle diet.

  • 🗑️棄如敝屣qì rú bì xǐ

    discard like an old shoe; cast aside — Discarding like worn-out shoes.

    He discarded his old friends like old shoes.
