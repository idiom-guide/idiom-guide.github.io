心存僥倖

xīn cún jiǎo xìng

In English

  • by chance/ hope for a windfall

Meaning

Counting on luck.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • by chance
  • hope for a windfall
  • count on luck

Example

別心存僥倖，還是存錢吧。

Don't hope for a windfall; save your money instead.

Usage

Often a warning not to rely on chance.

Similar

  • 🎲孤注一擲gū zhù yī zhì

    a desperate play — A desperate play.

    Selling his house to fund the film was a desperate play.

  • 🪁異想天開yì xiǎng tiān kāi

    quixotic, magical thinking; vagaries — Magical thinking.

    His plan to sell ice in the Arctic was pure magical thinking.

Opposite

  • 👣腳踏實地jiǎo tà shí dì

    staying grounded and pushing ahead — Down-to-earth.

    He is a down-to-earth man who stays grounded and keeps pushing ahead.

  • ☂️未雨綢繆wèi yǔ chóu móu

    be fully prepared; provident — Preparing in advance.

    Keep an emergency kit at home; it pays to be fully prepared.
