11 · Getting Along & Making Up

言歸於好

yán guī yú hǎo

In English

  • make up

Meaning

Making up.Positive · General

Other English renderings

  • patch things up
  • be friends again

Example

沉默多年後，姊妹倆言歸於好。

The sisters made up after years of silence.

Usage

For friends, family or partners reconciling.

Similar

  • 🤝握手言和wò shǒu yán hé

    shake hands and make peace — Shaking hands and making peace.

    The two captains shook hands and made peace.

  • 🧊冰釋前嫌bīng shì qián xián

    bury the hatchet — Burying the hatchet.

    The two rivals buried the hatchet.

Opposite

  • 😡反目成仇fǎn mù chéng chóu

    fall out and become enemies — Falling out and becoming enemies.

    The business partners fell out and became enemies.

  • 🚶形同陌路xíng tóng mò lù

    become like strangers — Like strangers.

    The former friends have become like strangers.
