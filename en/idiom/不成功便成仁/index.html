不成功，便成仁

bù chéng gōng, biàn chéng rén

In English

  • make or break

Meaning

Make or break; succeed or die trying.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • do or die
  • succeed or perish

Example

這一季對這家年輕公司來說，是不成功，便成仁的關鍵。

This quarter is make or break for the young company.

Usage

Solemn in tone; now often used with exaggeration for matches, start-ups or exams.

Similar

  • ⛵破釜沉舟pò fǔ chén zhōu

    (we've) burned the boats; reaching a point of no return — Burning one's boats.

    We've burned the boats; there is no going back now.

  • 🎲孤注一擲gū zhù yī zhì

    a desperate play — A desperate all-or-nothing bet.

    Selling his house to fund the film was a desperate play.

Opposite

  • 💰見好就收jiàn hǎo jiù shōu

    quit while one is ahead — Quitting while one is ahead.

    Quit while you're ahead and don't gamble the winnings.

  • 🏞️急流勇退jí liú yǒng tuì

    quit while one is ahead; go out on top — Retiring at the height of one's career.

    He chose to go out on top, retiring after his best season.
