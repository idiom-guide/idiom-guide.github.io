走火入魔

zǒu huǒ rù mó

In English

  • become obsessed to the point of madness

Meaning

Obsessed to the point of madness.Negative · General

Other English renderings

  • go off the deep end
  • obsessed

Example

他下棋下到走火入魔。

He became obsessed with chess to the point of madness.

Usage

Originally a Buddhist and Daoist term for meditation gone wrong; now for unhealthy obsession.

Similar

  • 😍沈醉其中chén zuì qí zhōng

    enthralled — Enthralled; more positive.

    The audience was enthralled by the music.

Opposite

  • ✋適可而止shì kě ér zhǐ

    know when to stop — Knowing when to stop.

    Criticism is fine, but know when to stop.
