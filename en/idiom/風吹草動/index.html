風吹草動

fēng chuī cǎo dòng

In English

  • straw in the wind

Meaning

The slightest stir: any small sign of change or trouble.Neutral · Colloquial

Other English renderings

  • any sign of trouble
  • the slightest stir

Example

市場一有風吹草動，交易員就開始拋售。

At the slightest straw in the wind, the traders started selling.

Usage

Often 一有風吹草動, followed by panic or action; usually about unsettling signs.

Similar

  • 🌩️山雨欲來風滿樓shān yǔ yù lái fēng mǎn lóu

    the calm before the storm; storm clouds are gathering — The tense calm before a storm.

    Storm clouds are gathering over the market.

  • 👀提高警覺tí gāo jǐng jué

    eyes up — Staying on the alert.

    Keep your eyes up when you cross the road.

Opposite

  • 🦢從容不迫cóng róng bù pò

    unruffled — Calm and unhurried.

    The host remained unruffled when the lights went out.

  • 😎泰然自若tài rán zì ruò

    unfazed — Composed and unruffled.

    She remained unfazed by the critics' attacks.
