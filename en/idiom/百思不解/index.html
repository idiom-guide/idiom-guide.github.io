百思不解

bǎi sī bù jiě

In English

  • flummoxed

Meaning

Baffled; unable to figure it out however hard one thinks.Neutral · Formal

Other English renderings

  • be baffled
  • be at a loss to understand
  • can't make head or tail of sth

Example

系統為什麼一直當機，我百思不解。

I'm flummoxed by why the system keeps crashing.

Usage

Often with 為什麼…; also 百思不得其解.

Similar

  • 🤨莫名其妙mò míng qí miào

    inexplicable; baffling — Baffling; about the thing itself.

    His sudden anger was inexplicable to everyone in the room.

  • 😧不明所以bù míng suǒ yǐ

    be baffled; not understand what is going on — Not knowing the reason or the inside story.

    Everyone was baffled and had to wait for his explanation.

Opposite

  • 🌤️豁然開朗huò rán kāi lǎng

    eureka; refreshing moment — Suddenly seeing the light.

    Everything suddenly became clear. It was a real eureka moment.

  • 💥醍醐灌頂tí hú guàn dǐng

    have a sudden enlightenment; be struck by sudden insight — A sudden, complete understanding.

    His advice struck me like a sudden enlightenment.
