Duration & Speed

Fleeting, lasting, urgent, far-off

16 idiomsChapter 12 · Quantity, Time & Degree
Browse, search and self-test interactively →Back to Chapter 12

B

🐴
白駒過隙bái jū guò xì
time flies
Time flies: it has been ten years already.

J

📆
經年累月jīng nián lěi yuè
year after year
The stone steps have been worn down year after year.

K

🚨
刻不容緩kè bù róng huǎn
urgent
brook no delay
Climate action is urgent.
🐌
曠日持久kuàng rì chí jiǔ
protracted
The lawsuit turned into a protracted battle.

P

⏰
迫在眉睫pò zài méi jié
time is a luxury we don't have
sth. is looming
The deadline is looming, and time is a luxury we don't have.

Q

🌠
千載難逢qiān zǎi nán féng
once in a lifetime
sth of a lifetime
generational opportunity
Meeting my idol was a once-in-a-lifetime chance.

S

⏰
姍姍來遲shān shān lái chí
long overdue
arrive late
The long-overdue reform finally passed.
🌠
稍縱即逝shāo zòng jí shì
blink and you will miss it
fleeting = short, quick
a fleeting moment
(miss the opportunity) in the blink of an eye
The sunset colors are fleeting; blink and you will miss them.

T

🌸
曇花一現tán huā yī xiàn
temporary
short-lived
flash in the pan
Their hit song was a flash in the pan.
⚡
彈指之間tán zhǐ zhī jiān
in the blink of an eye
Twenty years passed in the blink of an eye.

Y

🏜️
遙遙無期yáo yáo wú qī
nowhere in sight
A settlement is nowhere in sight.
🚄
一日千里yī rì qiān lǐ
by leaps and bounds
The technology is advancing by leaps and bounds.
🌗
一朝一夕yī zhāo yī xī
overnight
Fluency cannot be achieved overnight.

Z

⏱️
爭分奪秒zhēng fēn duó miǎo
race against time
Rescuers are racing against time.
🔜
指日可待zhǐ rì kě dài
just around the corner
With trials complete, approval is just around the corner.
💨
轉瞬即逝zhuǎn shùn jí shì
fleeting
The opportunity was fleeting.
