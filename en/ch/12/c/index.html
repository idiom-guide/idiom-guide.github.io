Frequency & Trends

Common, in succession, rising and falling, unchanging

16 idiomsChapter 12 · Quantity, Time & Degree
Browse, search and self-test interactively →Back to Chapter 12

C

🍄
層出不窮céng chū bù qióng
keep cropping up
New scams keep cropping up.

J

🏞️
江河日下jiāng hé rì xià
in steady decline
The industry has been in steady decline.
🎳
接二連三jiē èr lián sān
one after another
Orders came in one after another.

L

🔂
屢見不鮮lǚ jiàn bù xiān
common
nothing new
Data leaks are nothing new.
🚶
絡繹不絕luò yì bù jué
an endless stream
An endless stream of visitors came to the temple.

M

↘️
每況愈下měi kuàng yù xià
go from bad to worse
The company's finances went from bad to worse.

O

🎲
偶一為之ǒu yī wéi zhī
once in a while
Fast food is fine once in a while.

Q

🍪
千篇一律qiān piān yī lǜ
all the same
The new housing estates all look the same.

S

3️⃣
三番兩次sān fān liǎng cì
time and again
He has been warned time and again.
🥱
司空見慣sī kōng jiàn guàn
a common sight
commonplace
Delays are commonplace on this line.

T

🐇
突飛猛進tū fēi měng jìn
advance by leaps and bounds
Her English has advanced by leaps and bounds.

Y

🗿
一成不變yī chéng bù biàn
unchanging
set in stone
The curriculum is not set in stone.
⬆️
有增無減yǒu zēng wú jiǎn
show no sign of abating
Demand shows no sign of abating.
📈
與日俱增yǔ rì jù zēng
grow by the day
Pressure on the minister is growing by the day.

Z

☀️
蒸蒸日上zhēng zhēng rì shàng
go from strength to strength
Under her care, the family bakery went from strength to strength.
🔄
周而復始zhōu ér fù shǐ
cyclical
over and over again
The boom-and-bust pattern repeats over and over again.
