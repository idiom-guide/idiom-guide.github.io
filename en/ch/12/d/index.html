Degree & Scope

Extreme, moderate, weighty or slight, alike or different

18 idiomsChapter 12 · Quantity, Time & Degree

B

⚖️
半斤八兩bàn jīn bā liǎng
six of one and half a dozen of the other
Choosing between the two vendors is six of one and half a dozen of the other.
⚖️
不相上下bù xiāng shàng xià
as adj. as
The two rivals are as strong as each other.

D

👯
大同小異dà tóng xiǎo yì
much the same
The two proposals are much the same.
🏔️
登峰造極dēng fēng zào jí
reach the pinnacle
Her career reached its pinnacle with that role.

G

🌡️
過猶不及guò yóu bù jí
too much is as bad as too little
With revision, too much is as bad as too little.

J

🏋️
舉足輕重jǔ zú qīng zhòng
pivotal
carry great weight
The swing states are pivotal.

P

🌧️
鋪天蓋地pū tiān gài dì
blanket
overwhelming
The launch received blanket media coverage.

Q

👌
恰到好處qià dào hǎo chù
just right
The seasoning is just right.
👌
恰如其分qià rú qí fèn
properly
The punishment was properly matched to the offense.

S

✋
適可而止shì kě ér zhǐ
know when to stop
Criticism is fine, but know when to stop.
🥇
首屈一指shǒu qū yī zhǐ
second to none
The hospital's cardiac unit is second to none.

T

🌍
天壤之別tiān rǎng zhī bié
worlds apart
The two drafts are worlds apart.

W

🐞
微不足道wēi bù zú dào
insignificant
trivial
The difference is trivial.
📱
無所不在wú suǒ bù zài
ubiquitous
Smartphones are ubiquitous.
🔝
無以復加wú yǐ fù jiā
can't go any further
His frustration had reached a level that could hardly go any further.
📡
無遠弗屆wú yuǎn fú jiè
far-reaching
without borders
The internet's influence is far-reaching.
🏆
無與倫比wú yǔ lún bǐ
unparalleled
titanic
The view from the tower is unparalleled.

X

📏
相去無幾xiāng qù wú jǐ
not far apart
The two estimates are not far apart.
