Quantity

Countless, scarce, complete, lacking

16 idiomsChapter 12 · Quantity, Time & Degree
Browse, search and self-test interactively →Back to Chapter 12

B

🌐
包羅萬象bāo luó wàn xiàng
all-encompassing
The encyclopedia is all-encompassing.
🥤
杯水車薪bēi shuǐ chē xīn
far too little to help
The subsidy is far too little to help.
👀
比比皆是bǐ bǐ jiē shì
everywhere
all too common
Such errors are everywhere in the report.
🔢
不計其數bù jì qí shù
countless
Countless families were affected.
📝
不勝枚舉bù shèng méi jǔ
too many to list
Examples are too many to list.

C

🌾
滄海一粟cāng hǎi yī sù
a drop in the ocean
One person's effort may seem like a drop in the ocean.
🏟️
成千上萬chéng qiān shàng wàn
thousands upon thousands
Thousands upon thousands of fans filled the square.

D

➕
多多益善duō duō yì shàn
the more, the better
When it comes to practice, the more, the better.
🐂
多如牛毛duō rú niú máo
a dime a dozen
Apps like this are a dime a dozen.

F

🦄
鳳毛麟角fèng máo lín jiǎo
few and far between
extremely rare
Interpreters with that language pair are few and far between.

J

🥜
九牛一毛jiǔ niú yī máo
peanuts
just a drop in the bucket
The donation was just a drop in the bucket compared with what the hospital needed.

L

🤏
寥寥無幾liáo liáo wú jǐ
very few
a mere handful
Very few applicants met the requirements.

Q

🖐️
屈指可數qū zhǐ kě shǔ
can be counted on one hand
Experts in this field can be counted on one hand.

S

🥣
僧多粥少sēng duō zhōu shǎo
not enough to go around
With so many applicants, there are not enough grants to go around.

W

🔬
微乎其微wēi hū qí wēi
negligible
The risk is negligible.

Y

🛍️
應有盡有yīng yǒu jìn yǒu
have everything you could want
The market has everything you could want.
