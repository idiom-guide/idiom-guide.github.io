Joy & Satisfaction

Happiness, enjoyment, admiration, relief

30 idiomsChapter 3 · Praise & Positive Judgment
Browse, search and self-test interactively →Back to Chapter 3

C

😍
沈醉其中chén zuì qí zhōng
enthralled
The audience was enthralled by the music.

D

🙌
大快人心dà kuài rén xīn
give a lift to the heart
The news that the corrupt official had been jailed gave everyone a lift.

G

💪
感到振奮gǎn dào zhèn fèn
be heartening to = encouraging
It's heartening to see so many volunteers.

H

🎉
歡樂融洽huān lè róng qià
fun
The dinner was fun, and everyone got along.
🐦
歡欣雀躍huān xīn què yuè
be jubilant at/about/over sth
The fans were jubilant over the victory.

J

😋
津津有味jīn jīn yǒu wèi
with great interest
with relish
The students listened to the story with great interest.
🐔
雞皮疙瘩jī pí gē da
goosebumps
The song gave me goosebumps.
🥂
及時行樂jí shí xíng lè
carpe diem
eat, drink, and be merry
Carpe diem! Let's go out tonight.

L

☁️
樂不可支lè bù kě zhī
be on cloud nine
in seventh heaven
She was on cloud nine after winning the prize.
🔁
樂此不疲lè cǐ bù pí
never get tired of it
never get used to it
The kids never get tired of the playground.
😄
樂在其中lè zài qí zhōng
find great pleasure in
He finds great pleasure in gardening.
🤗
令人安心lìng rén ān xīn
reassuring
The doctor's calm voice was reassuring.

M

😊
滿心愉悅mǎn xīn yú yuè
delight
She smiled with delight at the surprise.

Q

🎠
其樂無窮qí lè wú qióng
a hell of a lot of fun
Playing in the snow was a hell of a lot of fun.
💘
情有獨鍾qíng yǒu dú zhōng
have a soft spot for sb/sth
She has a soft spot for old movies.
🙇
欽佩不已qīn pèi bù yǐ
deep admiration
I have deep admiration for her courage.

R

🌸
如沐春風rú mù chūn fēng
feel so good
pleasant
Talking with her made me feel so good, like a spring breeze.
😌
如釋重負rú shì zhòng fù
let shoulders drop
relieved
take heavy weight off
She felt relieved when the exams were over, as if a heavy weight had been lifted.

S

❤️
神魂顛倒shén hún diān dǎo
infatuated
head over heels
He was head over heels in love.
😳
受寵若驚shòu chǒng ruò jīng
I'm flattered
You remembered my birthday? I'm flattered.
🙇
肅然起敬sù rán qǐ jìng
be awe-struck
awe-struck + n
We were awe-struck by the old soldier's story.

W

🎈
無事一身輕wú shì yī shēn qīng
unencumbered
With the deadline behind him, he felt unencumbered.

X

😆
興高采烈xìng gāo cǎi liè
gleeful
a gleeful smile/shout
in gleeful anticipation
elated
The children ran out of school in gleeful anticipation of summer.
🌄
心曠神怡xīn kuàng shén yí
completely relaxed and happy
A walk by the lake leaves me completely relaxed and happy.
🤗
欣然接受xīn rán jiē shòu
with alacrity
She accepted the invitation with alacrity.
🐜
心癢難耐xīn yǎng nán nài
be itching to do sth
The kids are itching to open the presents.

Y

😍
一見傾心yī jiàn qīng xīn
be enamored with/of
He was enamored of the city at first sight.
🏅
與有榮焉yù yǒu róng yān
share sb's honor
We all share in the honor of our school's victory.

Z

💗
真情流露zhēn qíng liú lù
the revelation of true inner feelings
His speech was a revelation of his true inner feelings.
🍾
縱情享樂zòng qíng xiǎng lè
indulge oneself
live it up
They indulged themselves at the festival.
