Teaching & the Teacher's Way

Teaching philosophy, teacher–student bonds, leading by example

14 idiomsChapter 10 · Education, Culture & the Arts
Browse, search and self-test interactively →Back to Chapter 10

B

🌲
百年樹人bǎi nián shù rén
educating people takes generations
Educating people takes generations; there are no shortcuts.

C

🌬️
春風化雨chūn fēng huà yǔ
nurture with love and care/patience
The kindergarten teacher nurtures her pupils with love and patience.

H

📚
誨人不倦huì rén bù juàn
tireless in teaching
He was tireless in teaching, even after retirement.

Q

💙
青出於藍qīng chū yú lán
the student surpasses the master
The student has surpassed the master.

T

🍑
桃李滿天下táo lǐ mǎn tiān xià
have students all over the world
After forty years in the classroom, she has students all over the world.

W

👨‍🏫
為人師表wéi rén shī biǎo
teacher of exemplary virtue
A teacher should be a model of virtue for students.
🚫
誤人子弟wù rén zǐ dì
fail one's students
A careless teacher fails his students.

X

🐾
循循善誘xún xún shàn yòu
guide patiently, step by step
She guides her students patiently, step by step.

Y

👣
言傳身教yán chuán shēn jiào
teach by word and by example
Parents teach by word and by example.
👩‍🏫
因材施教yīn cái shī jiào
teach to each student's ability
differentiated instruction
Good teachers teach to each student's ability.
🏫
有教無類yǒu jiào wú lèi
education for all
The school was founded on the principle of education for all.

Z

🗒️
諄諄教誨zhūn zhūn jiào huì
earnest teaching
I am grateful for my advisor's earnest teaching.
🍎
尊師重道zūn shī zhòng dào
respect teachers and their teaching
The festival celebrates respect for teachers and their teaching.
🎓
作育英才zuò yù yīng cái
nurture talent
The university has nurtured talent for a century.
