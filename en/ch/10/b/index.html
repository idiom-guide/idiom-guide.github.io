Learning & Scholarship

Attitudes to learning, study methods, reading

14 idiomsChapter 10 · Education, Culture & the Arts
Browse, search and self-test interactively →Back to Chapter 10

B

📖
博覽群書bó lǎn qún shū
well-read
He is well-read in history and philosophy.
🙋
不恥下問bù chǐ xià wèn
not too proud to ask
A real scholar is not too proud to ask.

C

🕸️
觸類旁通chù lèi páng tōng
lateral thinking
Once you master one programming language, lateral thinking helps you pick up the others quickly.

H

🍒
囫圇吞棗hú lún tūn zǎo
swallow information whole
Don't swallow information whole; question what you read.

J

💡
舉一反三jǔ yī fǎn sān
learn one thing and apply it to others
A good student learns one thing and applies it to others.

K

📗
開卷有益kāi juàn yǒu yì
reading is always rewarding
Reading is always rewarding, whatever the book.

Q

🐢
勤能補拙qín néng bǔ zhuō
hard work makes up for lack of talent
Hard work makes up for lack of talent.

S

🎹
熟能生巧shú néng shēng qiǎo
practice makes perfect
Practice makes perfect: sight-translate a page every day.
🦜
死記硬背sǐ jì yìng bèi
rote memorization
Rote memorization will not get you through this course.

W

🔁
溫故知新wēn gù zhī xīn
review the old to learn the new
Reviewing the old is the best way to learn the new.

X

🕯️
懸梁刺股xuán liáng cì gǔ
burn the midnight oil
She burned the midnight oil before the bar exam.
♾️
學無止境xué wú zhǐ jìng
there is no end to learning
There is no end to learning.
🛠️
學以致用xué yǐ zhì yòng
put learning into practice
Internships let students put their learning into practice.

Z

🐝
孜孜不倦zī zī bù juàn
diligent
tireless
She has been a tireless student of the language for decades.
