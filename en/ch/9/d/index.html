Closing & Farewell

Wrapping up, looking ahead, seeing people off

14 idiomsChapter 9 · Speeches, Ceremonies & Courtesies
Browse, search and self-test interactively →Back to Chapter 9

B

🍻
賓主盡歡bīn zhǔ jìn huān
a good time was had by all
The dinner ended on a high note, and a good time was had by all.
🛣️
不虛此行bù xū cǐ xíng
productive trip
got what you came for
The trip was productive: we got what we came for.

G

🔭
共創未來gòng chuàng wèi lái
build the future together
We look forward to building the future together.
🏁
功德圓滿gōng dé yuán mǎn
come to a successful close
With this ceremony, the three-year project comes to a successful close.

H

📮
後會有期hòu huì yǒu qī
until we meet again
Thank you, and until we meet again.

L

🌅
來日方長lái rì fāng cháng
there will be many more opportunities ahead
There will be many more opportunities ahead to work together.

M

🎒
滿載而歸mǎn zài ér guī
come away with a great deal
I hope everyone comes away with a great deal from this forum.

R

🛤️
任重道遠rèn zhòng dào yuǎn
the road ahead is long and the responsibility heavy
The road ahead is long and the responsibility heavy.

T

🍂
天下無不散的筵席tiān xià wú bù sàn de yán xí
all good things must come to an end
All good things must come to an end, and so must this conference.

X

🧑‍🤝‍🧑
攜手並進xié shǒu bìng jìn
move forward hand in hand
Let us move forward hand in hand.

Y

🧳
一路順風yī lù shùn fēng
have a safe trip
Have a safe trip home.
😢
依依不捨yī yī bù shě
reluctant to say goodbye
We are all reluctant to say goodbye.

Z

💪
再接再厲zài jiē zài lì
keep up the good work
Congratulations to the team, and keep up the good work.
👋
珍重再見zhēn zhòng zài jiàn
take care and goodbye
Take care, and goodbye for now.
