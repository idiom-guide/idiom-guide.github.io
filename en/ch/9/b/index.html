Thanks & Humble Phrases

Expressing thanks, modesty, asking for forbearance

14 idiomsChapter 9 · Speeches, Ceremonies & Courtesies
Browse, search and self-test interactively →Back to Chapter 9

B

🪓
班門弄斧bān mén nòng fǔ
presume to speak before the experts
I hesitate to speak before so many experts.
🙏
不吝賜教bù lìn cì jiào
I welcome your feedback
This is only a first draft, and I welcome your feedback.

C

🔰
才疏學淺cái shū xué qiǎn
my knowledge is limited
My knowledge is limited, so please correct me where I am wrong.

D

🦾
鼎力相助dǐng lì xiāng zhù
generous support
This event would not be possible without your generous support.
😅
多多包涵duō duō bāo hán
please bear with us
This is our first time hosting, so please bear with us.

G

💐
感激不盡gǎn jī bù jìn
can't thank you enough
I can't thank you enough for your help.

H

📈
獲益良多huò yì liáng duō
benefit enormously
I benefited enormously from the workshop.

J

🙇
敬請見諒jìng qǐng jiàn liàng
we ask for your understanding
The schedule has changed, and we ask for your understanding.

K

😳
愧不敢當kuì bù gǎn dāng
I'm flattered
You are too kind. I'm flattered.

M

🙏
銘感五內míng gǎn wǔ nèi
deeply grateful
I am deeply grateful for your help.
💝
沒齒難忘mò chǐ nán wàng
will never forget
I will never forget your kindness.

S

🍱
盛情款待shèng qíng kuǎn dài
warm hospitality
Thank you for your warm hospitality.

Y

🌞
野人獻曝yě rén xiàn pù
my humble contribution
Please take this as my humble contribution to the discussion.

Z

🥡
招待不周zhāo dài bù zhōu
forgive any shortcomings in our hospitality
Please forgive any shortcomings in our hospitality.
