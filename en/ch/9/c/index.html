Congratulations & Good Wishes

Wishes for success, weddings, the New Year and the road ahead

14 idiomsChapter 9 · Speeches, Ceremonies & Courtesies
Browse, search and self-test interactively →Back to Chapter 9

B

💍
百年好合bǎi nián hǎo hé
a lifetime of happiness together
To the bride and groom: a lifetime of happiness together.

D

🚀
大展鴻圖dà zhǎn hóng tú
realize your ambitions
May you realize your ambitions in the year ahead.

F

🎂
福如東海fú rú dōng hǎi
long life and good fortune
Happy 80th birthday! We wish you long life and good fortune.

G

🧨
恭賀新禧gōng hè xīn xǐ
Happy New Year
Happy New Year to you and your family.

J

📜
金榜題名jīn bǎng tí míng
pass with flying colors
We hope every student passes with flying colors.

M

🐎
馬到成功mǎ dào chéng gōng
wish you every success
We wish you every success with the launch.

P

🦅
鵬程萬里péng chéng wàn lǐ
a bright future ahead
To the graduates: you have a bright future ahead.

Q

🌈
前程似錦qián chéng sì jǐn
a bright future
We wish you a bright future.
🏠
喬遷之喜qiáo qiān zhī xǐ
congratulations on your new home
Congratulations on your new home!

W

🍀
萬事如意wàn shì rú yì
all the best
Wishing you all the best in the new year.

X

🌠
心想事成xīn xiǎng shì chéng
may all your wishes come true
Happy birthday, and may all your wishes come true.

Y

⛵
一帆風順yī fān fēng shùn
smooth sailing
I wish you smooth sailing in your new post.
💞
永浴愛河yǒng yù ài hé
everlasting love
We wish the newlyweds everlasting love.
🎉
圓滿成功yuán mǎn chéng gōng
a complete success
We wish the conference a complete success.
