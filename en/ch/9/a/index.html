9 · A

Welcome & Opening

Greeting guests, introducing them, opening an event

14 idiomsChapter 9 · Speeches, Ceremonies & Courtesies
Browse, search and self-test interactively →Back to Chapter 9

B

🎖️
備感榮幸bèi gǎn róng xìng
deeply honored
I am deeply honored to speak here today.
🛋️
賓至如歸bīn zhì rú guī
feel at home
We hope you will feel at home during your stay.
📅
撥冗出席bō rǒng chū xí
take time out of a busy schedule to attend
Thank you all for taking time out of your busy schedules to attend.
✈️
不遠千里bù yuǎn qiān lǐ
come all this way
Thank you for coming all this way to join us.

D

🚗
大駕光臨dà jià guāng lín
grace us with your presence
Thank you for gracing us with your presence.

G

🍽️
高朋滿座gāo péng mǎn zuò
a full house of distinguished guests
We are delighted to have a full house of distinguished guests.
⏳
恭候多時gōng hòu duō shí
have been looking forward to your arrival
We have been looking forward to your arrival.
🎊
共襄盛舉gòng xiāng shèng jǔ
join us for this special occasion
We invite you to join us for this special occasion.

H

🥂
歡聚一堂huān jù yī táng
gather together
It is a pleasure to see so many friends gathered together.

J

🌟
濟濟一堂jǐ jǐ yī táng
a gathering of talent
Tonight is truly a gathering of talent.
🤝
久仰大名jiǔ yǎng dà míng
Your reputation precedes you
Your reputation precedes you.

P

✨
蓬蓽生輝péng bì shēng huī
your presence honors us
Your presence honors us this evening.

S

🎆
盛況空前shèng kuàng kōng qián
an unprecedented turnout
This year's forum has drawn an unprecedented turnout.

Z

🎟️
座無虛席zuò wú xū xí
a packed house
It is wonderful to see a packed house this morning.
