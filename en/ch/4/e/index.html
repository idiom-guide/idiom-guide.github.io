Words & Quarrels

Smooth talk, criticism and slander, wars of words

36 idiomsChapter 4 · Criticism & Negative Judgment
Browse, search and self-test interactively →Back to Chapter 4

B

🚫
不由分說bù yóu fēn shuō
indiscriminate + n
The police made indiscriminate arrests, giving nobody a chance to explain.

C

🃏
拆穿花招chāi chuān huā zhāo
Call one's bluff
I called his bluff and asked him to show his cards.
🤬
臭罵一頓chòu mà yī dùn
get chewed out
He got chewed out for being late again.
⚔️
唇槍舌戰chún qiāng shé zhàn
a war of words
verbal sparring
The debate turned into a war of words.

D

📢
大放厥詞dà fàng jué cí
spout nonsense
fling wild accusations
He spouted nonsense on live TV.
⚡
大肆撻伐dà sì tà fá
opprobrium has been heaped on sth
Opprobrium has been heaped on the CEO.
🦚
大言不慚dà yán bù cán
rodomontade
His claim that he invented the internet was pure rodomontade.
🦜
喋喋不休dié dié bù xiū
ad nauseam
He went on about his holiday ad nauseam.
🗡️
詆毀言論dǐ huǐ yán lùn
make derogatory comment
He was suspended for making derogatory comments about his colleagues.
✂️
斷章取義duàn zhāng qǔ yì
take sb's comments "out of context."
take sth. out of context
The newspaper took her comments out of context.

F

😤
發牢騷fā láo sāo
grumble
whine
Stop grumbling and help me.

G

🎙️
高談闊論gāo tán kuò lùn
pontificate on/about sth
He loves to pontificate about politics at dinner.
👉
公然譴責gōng rán qiǎn zé
public rebuke
The president issued a public rebuke of the ambassador.

H

🍯
花言巧語huā yán qiǎo yǔ
palaver
Don't be fooled by his palaver.
🗯️
胡說八道hú shuō bā dào
hogwash
nonsense
That's hogwash.
🤺
互相挖苦hù xiāng wā kǔ
trade barbs with sb. over sth
The two comedians traded barbs over their ratings.

J

⌨️
鍵盤專家jiàn pán zhuān jiā
keyboard commando(es)
Online, every keyboard commando knows how to run the country.
⛽
加油添醋jiā yóu tiān cù
add(pour) fuel to(on) the fire
Don't add fuel to the fire by repeating the rumor.

K

😮
口無遮攔kǒu wú zhē lán
sb have no filter
shoot from the lip
blurt sth out (without thinking)
My uncle has no filter; he says whatever pops into his head.
🐍
誇大不實kuā dà bù shí
exaggerate
snake oil
The ads for the miracle pill were exaggerated snake oil.

M

💥
猛烈抨擊měng liè pēng jī
lambaste, harsh criticism
Critics lambasted the film as shallow.
🔨
猛力抨擊měng lì pēng jī
blistering remarks
The senator launched blistering remarks against the plan.

N

🤡
扭曲嘲弄niǔ qū cháo nòng
travesty
The trial was a travesty of justice.

P

🎀
漂亮話piào liàng huà
empty words
lip service
Those are just empty words.
🚿
潑冷水pō lěng shuǐ
throw a wet blanket on
pour cold water on
Don't pour cold water on his dream.

S

🧨
煽動誇大shān dòng kuā dà
inflammatory
The headline was inflammatory and misleading.
📣
煽動言論shān dòng yán lùn
incendiary (remarks)
explosive, provocative, toxic
demagogue
The demagogue's incendiary remarks sparked a riot.
🔥
搧風點火shān fēng diǎn huǒ
fan the flames of
The tabloid fanned the flames of the dispute.
👄
耍嘴皮子shuǎ zuǐ pí zi
talk is cheap
Talk is cheap. Show me the results.

W

🚨
危言聳聽wēi yán sǒng tīng
alarmist, fearmongers, spread fears
scaremonger
The columnist is an alarmist who scares readers with every headline.
🖋️
舞文弄墨wǔ wén nòng mò
wordsmith
fiddle at writing
have a way with words
She is a wordsmith with a way with words.

X

🥊
相互攻訐xiāng hù gōng jié
be at each other's throats
The two partners are at each other's throats.
🤥
虛假之言xū jiǎ zhī yán
adulterated
The report was adulterated with misleading claims.

Y

🐍
油嘴滑舌yóu zuǐ huá shé
flippant
He gave me a flippant answer.

Z

🕶️
指桑罵槐zhǐ sāng mà huái
make veiled criticism
throw shade (informal)
She threw shade at her rival without naming her.
😬
支吾其詞zhī wú qí cí
hem and haw
stammer evasively
He hemmed and hawed when asked about the money.
