Workplace & Management

Hiring, division of labor, leadership, promotion

13 idiomsChapter 8 · Business: Economy & Workplace
Browse, search and self-test interactively →Back to Chapter 8

A

📋
按部就班àn bù jiù bān
step by step
by the book
New hires are trained step by step.

B

📶
步步高升bù bù gāo shēng
rise steadily through the ranks
He rose steadily through the ranks to become a partner.

D

🦸
獨當一面dú dāng yī miàn
able to take charge on one's own
After two years she was able to take charge on her own.

F

🐜
分工合作fēn gōng hé zuò
divide the work and cooperate
We divided the work and finished ahead of schedule.

G

⚙️
各司其職gè sī qí zhí
each does their own job
The project ran smoothly because each of us did our own job.

Q

🧠
群策群力qún cè qún lì
pool ideas and efforts
We pooled our ideas and efforts to meet the deadline.

R

🪑
人浮於事rén fú yú shì
overstaffed
The agency is overstaffed and slow.
🧩
人盡其才rén jìn qí cái
make the most of everyone's talents
The new structure makes the most of everyone's talents.

S

🏅
賞罰分明shǎng fá fēn míng
fair with rewards and discipline
Staff respect her because she is fair with rewards and discipline.
🚩
身先士卒shēn xiān shì zú
lead from the front
The chief engineer led from the front, working beside the crew.
🔧
事必躬親shì bì gōng qīn
hands-on to a fault
micromanage
The founder is hands-on to a fault and signs every invoice himself.

Y

👣
以身作則yǐ shēn zuò zé
lead by example
A good teacher leads by example.

Z

🔑
知人善任zhī rén shàn rèn
put the right people in the right jobs
A good manager puts the right people in the right jobs.
