Markets & the Economy

Supply and demand, prices, boom and bust

15 idiomsChapter 8 · Business: Economy & Workplace
Browse, search and self-test interactively →Back to Chapter 8

B

🏚️
百業蕭條bǎi yè xiāo tiáo
an across-the-board slump
The lockdown caused an across-the-board slump.
🛒
薄利多銷bó lì duō xiāo
low margins, high volume
Discount retailers live on low margins and high volume.

G

🧾
供不應求gōng bù yìng qiú
demand outstrips supply
Demand for chips still outstrips supply.
📦
供過於求gōng guò yú qiú
oversupply
a glut
A glut of office space has pushed rents down.

H

💎
貨真價實huò zhēn jià shí
the genuine article
Customers want to know the product is the genuine article.

K

🍔
綑綁銷售kǔn bǎng xiāo shòu
bundling
bundled sales
Telecom carriers often use bundling, pairing a phone with a monthly plan to win long-term contracts.

M

🕸️
門可羅雀mén kě luó què
deserted
hardly a customer in sight
Once packed, the mall is now deserted.
🏪
門庭若市mén tíng ruò shì
bustling with customers
The new bakery is bustling with customers.

Q

💎
奇貨可居qí huò kě jū
hold on to a rare commodity
a rare find worth hoarding
He thought the rare stamp was a find worth hoarding, so he held on to it.

S

🏮
生意興隆shēng yì xīng lóng
business is booming
Business is booming at the night market.
🚤
水漲船高shuǐ zhǎng chuán gāo
a rising tide lifts all boats
When the index rallies, a rising tide lifts all boats.

W

🏷️
物美價廉wù měi jià lián
good value for money
The brand built its name on good value for money.

X

🌳
欣欣向榮xīn xīn xiàng róng
thriving
booming
The local startup scene is thriving.

Y

📉
一落千丈yī luò qiān zhàng
plummet
nosedive
Sales plummeted after the recall.
💤
有行無市yǒu háng wú shì
priced but not trading
The luxury flats are priced but not trading.
