Getting Along & Making Up

Understanding, conflict, falling out, making peace

15 idiomsChapter 11 · Relationships & Feelings
Browse, search and self-test interactively →Back to Chapter 11

B

🧊
冰釋前嫌bīng shì qián xián
bury the hatchet
The two rivals buried the hatchet.
🥊
不打不相識bù dǎ bù xiāng shí
friendship that grows out of a fight
Theirs is a friendship that grew out of a fight.

E

✂️
恩斷義絕ēn duàn yì jué
cut all ties
After the betrayal, she cut all ties with him.

F

😡
反目成仇fǎn mù chéng chóu
fall out and become enemies
The business partners fell out and became enemies.

G

😒
耿耿於懷gěng gěng yú huái
hold a grudge
can't let it go
He still holds a grudge about that remark.

J

💞
將心比心jiāng xīn bǐ xīn
be empathetic
put oneself in sb's shoes
Try to put yourself in her shoes before you criticize.
🧽
既往不咎jì wǎng bù jiù
wipe the slate clean
I'll wipe the slate clean, and we can start over.

S

👟
設身處地shè shēn chǔ dì
see things from someone else's position
Try to see things from her position.
🆚
勢不兩立shì bù liǎng lì
irreconcilable
The two factions are irreconcilable.

W

🤝
握手言和wò shǒu yán hé
shake hands and make peace
The two captains shook hands and made peace.

X

🚶
形同陌路xíng tóng mò lù
become like strangers
The former friends have become like strangers.
🤫
息事寧人xī shì níng rén
smooth things over
The manager apologized simply to smooth things over.

Y

😊
言歸於好yán guī yú hǎo
make up
The sisters made up after years of silence.
🔪
一刀兩斷yī dāo liǎng duàn
make a clean break
He decided to make a clean break with his old life.
🕊️
以和為貴yǐ hé wéi guì
value harmony above all
In family disputes, we should value harmony above all.
