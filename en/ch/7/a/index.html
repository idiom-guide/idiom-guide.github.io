B

🩺
標本兼治biāo běn jiān zhì
tackle both symptoms and root causes
The plan tackles both the symptoms and the root causes of youth unemployment.
🧭
撥亂反正bō luàn fǎn zhèng
set things right
restore order
The interim government's first task was to restore order and set things right.

C

🕊️
長治久安cháng zhì jiǔ ān
lasting stability
Lasting stability depends on institutions, not on any single leader.

D

💊
對症下藥duì zhèng xià yào
a targeted remedy
address the root cause
Without reliable data, the government cannot offer a targeted remedy.

G

🎻
改弦更張gǎi xián gēng zhāng
change course
a policy overhaul
Faced with falling support, the cabinet changed course on energy policy.

L

🏛️
勵精圖治lì jīng tú zhì
govern with resolve
The young mayor governed with resolve and turned the city's finances around.
📯
令出必行lìng chū bì xíng
orders are strictly enforced
In a crisis, orders must be strictly enforced.

S

👔
上行下效shàng xíng xià xiào
the tone is set at the top
The tone is set at the top: when leaders cut corners, staff follow.

X

🍪
蕭規曹隨xiāo guī cáo suí
cookie cutter
Instead of making changes, the new mayor kept the old policies in cookie-cutter fashion.
🧹
興利除弊xīng lì chú bì
root-and-branch reform
clean up the system
The new minister promised root-and-branch reform of the procurement system.
🌱
休養生息xiū yǎng shēng xī
recuperate and rebuild
After the war, the country needed a decade to recuperate and rebuild.

Y

🧩

customization; localization
ad hoc approach
flexible
contingency plan
The recipe was localized to suit the tastes of each region.

Z

🔄

The boss is a flip-flopper who changes the plan every day.
