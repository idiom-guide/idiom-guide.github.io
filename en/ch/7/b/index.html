Diplomacy & International Relations

B

⚔️
兵戎相見bīng róng xiāng jiàn
resort to arms
Neither capital wants to resort to arms over the dispute.

C

🦷
唇亡齒寒chún wáng chǐ hán
share a common fate
The neighbors share a common fate: if one falls, the other is exposed.

D

🏘️
敦親睦鄰dūn qīn mù lín
good-neighborliness
The foreign minister stressed good-neighborliness in his first speech.

H

☮️
和平共處hé píng gòng chǔ
peaceful coexistence
Both sides reaffirmed their commitment to peaceful coexistence.
♟️
合縱連橫hé zòng lián héng
alliance-building
shifting alliances
Smaller parties are busy alliance-building ahead of the vote.
🕊️
化干戈為玉帛huà gān gē wéi yù bó
bury the hatchet
After years of silence, the two families finally buried the hatchet.
🚢
互通有無hù tōng yǒu wú
trade on each other's strengths
The two economies trade on each other's strengths.

J

💢
劍拔弩張jiàn bá nǔ zhāng
tensions run high
at daggers drawn
Tensions ran high at the border after the incident.

L

🎁
禮尚往來lǐ shàng wǎng lái
reciprocity
Diplomacy runs on reciprocity: a state visit is usually returned.

Q

🤝
求同存異qiú tóng cún yì
seek common ground
agree to disagree
The two delegations agreed to seek common ground and set their differences aside.

Y

🚧
以鄰為壑yǐ lín wéi hè
beggar-thy-neighbor
Competitive devaluation is a beggar-thy-neighbor policy.
🌊
一衣帶水yī yī dài shuǐ
close neighbors separated only by a strip of water
As close neighbors separated only by a strip of water, the two countries have traded for centuries.
🏹
遠交近攻yuǎn jiāo jìn gōng
befriend distant states to pressure nearer ones
The kingdom befriended distant states to put pressure on its nearer rivals.

Z

🗺️
縱橫捭闔zòng héng bǎi hé
deft diplomatic maneuvering
Through deft diplomatic maneuvering, the small state secured guarantees from both powers.
