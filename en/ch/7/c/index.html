A

🏡
安居樂業ān jū lè yè
live and work in peace
The goal of public policy is to let people live and work in peace.

F

🍚
豐衣足食fēng yī zú shí
well-fed and well-clothed
A generation ago, being well-fed and well-clothed was already a blessing.

J

🥶
飢寒交迫jī hán jiāo pò
cold and hungry
destitute
Refugees arrived cold and hungry.

L

⛺
流離失所liú lí shī suǒ
displaced
The floods left thousands displaced.

M

🍞
民不聊生mín bù liáo shēng
destitute
barely able to survive
Years of hyperinflation left ordinary people barely able to survive.
🌋
民怨沸騰mín yuàn fèi téng
public anger boils over
Public anger boiled over after the scandal broke.

P

💰
貧富懸殊pín fù xuán shū
a yawning wealth gap
A yawning wealth gap is fueling political anger.

R

😰
人人自危rén rén zì wēi
everyone feels at risk
After the layoffs began, everyone felt at risk.
😨
人心惶惶rén xīn huáng huáng
widespread jitters
public panic
Rumors of a bank run caused widespread jitters.

S

🥀
世風日下shì fēng rì xià
moral decline
Every generation complains of moral decline.
🔦
守望相助shǒu wàng xiāng zhù
look out for one another
In this neighborhood, residents look out for one another.

Y

🔃

It takes more than a law to change social customs.
😠
怨聲載道yuàn shēng zài dào

The fare hike caused widespread discontent.

Z

🧱

The town pulled together after the quake: unity is strength.
