🧂

⚡

🤖

He inadvertently hummed the tune while he worked.

With his surprising ideas, you never know what he will do next.

🧘

After his near-death experience, he was wide awake to what truly mattered in life.
🌏

Studying abroad really broadened my horizons.
📞

Failing the exam was a wake-up call for him.
👓

I was incredulous when the underdog won the championship.
🔭
洞燭機先dòng zhú jī xiān

have uncanny foresight
She could see it coming long before the market crashed.

F

🔍

Every word of the senator's speech was put under the microscope.
🤯

The magician's last trick was mind-boggling.
💫
福至心靈fú zhì xīn líng
serendipity
Serendipity struck when I found the book I needed on the way home.

G

🎢

Visiting my old school left me with mixed feelings.
🤗

relatable
Her story was so relatable that I felt every word as if it were my own.

🤔

Just let that sink in for a moment before you answer.
🌑
毫無頭緒háo wú tóu xù

have no clue
I have no clue where to begin.
🏺
葫蘆裡賣什麼藥hú lu lǐ mài shén me yào

The new CEO is being very secretive, and nobody knows what's under the hood.
🌤️
豁然開朗huò rán kāi lǎng

refreshing moment
Everything suddenly became clear. It was a real eureka moment.

📚

Teaching has taught me as much as it has taught my students. I truly learn from teaching.
📌

Her advice will be taken to heart.
🧂
謹慎看待jǐn shèn kàn dài
Take...with a grain of salt
Take the rumor with a grain of salt until the company confirms it.
📸
記憶猶新jì yì yóu xīn
fresh in one's memory
The flood is still fresh in our memory.
🎯
聚精會神jù jīng huì shén

stay focused
be laser focused on sth
The students stayed laser-focused throughout the exam.

L

✍️

The doctor's notes looked like hieroglyphics to me.
🎓

you know that
She knows the city's bus routes inside out.
💡
靈光一閃líng guāng yī shǎn
have an epiphany
While washing the dishes, he had an epiphany and solved the design problem.
🧐
令人玩味lìng rén wán wèi
intriguing
thought-provoking
His sudden silence is intriguing.
🔗
理清頭緒lǐ qīng tóu xù
connect the dots
Let me draw a timeline to help us connect the dots.

👀

Investors are keeping tabs on the central bank's next move.
🤨

His sudden anger was inexplicable to everyone in the room.
😲
目瞪口呆mù dèng kǒu dāi
dumbstruck
be flabbergasted
We stood dumbstruck as the magician vanished.

N

✍️

👻

The change in pitch was almost imperceptible.
🗂️

description
Her music is beyond categorization; it is part jazz, part folk.
🌌
難以理解nán yǐ lǐ jiě

Why she quit such a good job is unfathomable to me.
🦄
難以想像nán yǐ xiǎng xiàng

you have no idea
It is inconceivable that such a rich country cannot feed its children.
🃏
難以預料nán yǐ yù liào
wild card
The weather is a wild card for tomorrow's outdoor concert.
🌫️
難以捉摸nán yǐ zhuō mō

unreadable
The elusive thief slipped away from the police once again.
💭
念茲在茲niàn zī zài zī

The coming exam is something she constantly has on her mind.

P

🎦

Sometimes it takes an outsider to see the problem clearly.

Q

🆕

never heard of
A deal like this is unheard of in our industry.
❓

sth. be doubtful
The sudden change in his story raised suspicion.
🔄

ooo from all angles/aspects
We need to review the whole plan from all angles before signing.
🧲

with rapt attention
The children were fully absorbed in the story.

📰

It's time for a reality check: we simply can't afford this house.
👨‍💼

You should take safety rules seriously.
📡
熱切關注rè qiè guān zhù

with keen interest
The public is following the trial closely.
🌐
融會貫通róng huì guàn tōng

fully conversant with
After years of study, she is fully conversant with the whole field.

🧩

puzzling
The riddle was mind-bending, and it took me a week to solve it.
🕳️

unfathomable
inscrutable
The old master's skill seemed unfathomable.
💪
深具信心shēn jù xìn xīn

The fund manager made a high-conviction bet on solar energy.
🤿
深入探討shēn rù tàn tǎo

deep dive into
The article takes a deep dive into the causes of inflation.
🗿
深思熟慮shēn sī shú lǜ
sit and think
It was a well-considered decision, made after much thought.
🛡️
深信不疑shēn xìn bù yí
an unshakable faith
She has an unshakable faith in her students.
😕
事有蹊蹺shì yǒu qī qiāo

The door was unlocked and the lights were off. Something's wrong here.

💎

benchmark learning
Studying how Singapore manages traffic gave us a useful benchmark; stones from other hills can polish our own jade.
💥
醍醐灌頂tí hú guàn dǐng
mind-blowing
Her lecture was mind-blowing; I finally understood the theory.
🔦
突破盲點tū pò máng diǎn
overcome a blind spot
The workshop helped the team overcome a blind spot in their planning.

📝

I took a mental note of the license plate.
😉
心領神會xīn lǐng shén huì

a tacit understanding
We exchanged a look and grasped each other's meaning intuitively.
☑️
信以為真xìn yǐ wéi zhēn
take sth at face value
believe it to be true
She took the rumor at face value.
💞
心有戚戚焉xīn yǒu qī qī yān

When she described her childhood, I had the same feeling, as if she were telling my story.
🤟
心照不宣xīn zhào bù xuān
a tacit understanding
an unspoken agreement
There was a tacit understanding between them.

🔄

❗

📓

👀

The telltale stains on his sleeve showed he had been painting.
🪁

🔮
