B

🔨
板上釘釘bǎn shàng dìng dīng
a done deal
locked in
The contract has been signed, so the deal is locked in.
👫
伴隨而來bàn suí ér lái
Concomitant
Rapid economic growth brings a concomitant rise in pollution.
👏
褒貶不一bāo biǎn bù yī
cheers and jeers
The new policy drew a mix of cheers and jeers from the public.
🧊
冰山一角bīng shān yī jiǎo
tip of the iceberg
The scandal we have seen so far is just the tip of the iceberg.
🔮
不出所料bù chū suǒ liào
predictably
Predictably, the train was late again on the first day of school.
🤨
不合情理bù hé qíng lǐ
unreasonable
illogical
Her demand for an immediate refund was unreasonable.
🚧
不可踰越bù kě yú yuè
unbridgeable
The gap between the two parties' positions proved unbridgeable.
🤝
不謀而合bù móu ér hé
fit/echo...in coincidence
We never compared notes, yet our ideas echoed each other by pure coincidence.
⚖️
不偏不倚bù piān bù yǐ
impartial
A good referee stays impartial and favors neither side.
🏔️
不容低估bù róng dī gū
cannot be underestimated
The impact of social media on elections cannot be underestimated.
🔒
不容爭辯bù róng zhēng biàn
inarguable
The evidence is so clear that the conclusion is inarguable.
🤫
不言而喻bù yán ér yù
self-evident
It is self-evident that a healthy diet leads to a healthier life.
💯
不證自明bù zhèng zì míng
self-evident
To most of us, the value of education is self-evident.
➕
不只如此bù zhǐ rú cǐ
only part of the story
Rising rents are only part of the story; wages have barely moved either.
🔀
不只一種方式bù zhǐ yī zhǒng fāng shì
There is more than one way to skin a cat
Don't worry about the format; there is more than one way to skin a cat.

C

🔄
徹頭徹尾chè tóu chè wěi
through and through
He is a gentleman through and through.
♎
持平而論chí píng ér lùn
To be fair
To be fair, the referee had only a split second to decide.
🧶
穿鑿附會chuān záo fù huì
farfetched
His claim that the poem predicted the election is farfetched.
🎱
純屬臆測chún shǔ yì cè
guesswork
speculate
Any talk about why he resigned is pure guesswork.

D

😇
道德優位dào dé yōu wèi
moral superiority
He claimed moral superiority over his rivals, yet he behaved no better.
❓
大哉問dà zāi wèn
a big question
a-million-dollar question
"What makes a good life?" That's a big question.
🧪
得到驗證dé dào yàn zhèng
be borne out
be validated
His prediction was borne out by the final results.
🔁
等價交換děng jià jiāo huàn
quid pro quo (this for that)
The deal was a quid pro quo: the company got a tax break and the city got a new park.
👌
的確如此dí què rú cǐ
to all intents and purposes
To all intents and purposes, the project is finished; we are just polishing the details.

E

☯️
二元對立èr yuán duì lì
Manichean
The debate has become a Manichean contest of good against evil.

F

👮
法網恢恢fǎ wǎng huī huī
the long arm of the law
He thought he could cheat the tax office, but the long arm of the law caught up with him.
🕸️
法網恢恢，疏而不漏fǎ wǎng huī huī, shū ér bù lòu
even the biggest got landed
Years later even the biggest crime boss got landed; nothing slips through the net of the law.
🥚
覆巢之下無完卵fù cháo zhī xià wú wán luǎn
in a great disaster no one can escape unscathed
As the banks fell one after another, it became clear that in a great disaster no one escapes unscathed.

G

🧩
箇中道理gè zhōng dào lǐ
the underlying reasoning
She explained the reasoning behind the new policy.
📛
顧名思義gù míng sī yì
as (the title) implies
As the name implies, a pop-up store appears only for a short time.

H

🌗
好壞參半hǎo huài cān bàn
A chequered past
The company has a chequered past, with both great successes and costly failures.
😇
好人有好報hǎo rén yǒu hǎo bào
good/bad karma
She helped everyone in town, and now they are all helping her. That's good karma at work.
💯
毫無疑問háo wú yí wèn
indisputably
She is indisputably the best player on the team.
🎰
很不可能hěn bù kě néng
be next to impossible
Finishing the report by tonight is next to impossible.
⭐
核心原則hé xīn yuán zé
cardinal principle
Honesty is the cardinal principle of good journalism.
🙉
話不投機半句多huà bù tóu jī bàn jù duō
We don't speak the same language
After ten minutes of arguing, I realized we don't speak the same language, so I stopped talking.

J

🏠
家家有本難念的經jiā jiā yǒu běn nán niàn de jīng
every family has its own troubles
Everyone looks happy, but every family has its own troubles.
🌳
見樹不見林jiàn shù bù jiàn lín
can't see the forest for the trees
miss the big picture
Focusing on tiny typos, he couldn't see the forest for the trees.
✅
加以證明jiā yǐ zhèng míng
make a (convincing
strong) case for sth
endorse
The data make a strong case for starting classes later.
📌
既成事實jì chéng shì shí
fait accompli
The board was presented with a fait accompli: the building had already been sold.
↔️
截然不同jié rán bù tóng
radically different
The two brothers have radically different personalities.
❌
積非成是jī fēi chéng shì
two wrongs make a right (can't)
If we keep ignoring the error, a mistake repeated often enough may come to be accepted as truth.
🔬
經得起檢驗jīng de qǐ jiǎn yàn
(not) hold up to scrutiny
A good theory has to hold up to scrutiny.
👍
經驗法則jīng yàn fǎ zé
the rule of thumb
As a rule of thumb, arrive at the airport two hours before an international flight.
💰
金科玉律jīn kē yù lǜ
golden rules
"The customer comes first" is the golden rule in our shop.
🔗
緊密關連jǐn mì guān lián
no daylight between us
On this issue, there is no daylight between the two ministers.
🛑
僅止於此jǐn zhǐ yú cǐ
end there
Let's let the discussion end there and move on to the next item.
🦄
絕無僅有jué wú jǐn yǒu
like nowhere else
The view from this hilltop is like nowhere else on earth.
🍊
橘逾淮為枳jú yú huái wéi zhǐ
doesn't travel well
what works in one place may fail in another
Policies copied wholesale from abroad often don't travel well.

K

🥀
可恨之人，必有可憐之處kě hèn zhī rén, bì yǒu kě lián zhī chù
cruelty springs from weakness
Cruelty springs from weakness: people who lash out are often hurting inside.

L

🐉
來龍去脈lái lóng qù mài
context
the whole story
For context
Before you judge, let me explain the whole story.
🧱
牢不可破láo bù kě pò
ironclad
unbreakable
They signed an ironclad agreement.
💰
劣幣驅逐良幣liè bì qū zhú liáng bì
Bad money drives out good
In a market flooded with cheap fakes, bad money drives out good.
👤
另有其人lìng yǒu qí rén
are found elsewhere
The police soon realized that the real culprit was someone else entirely.
🧮
邏輯嚴密luó jí yán mì
logical validity
Her argument is tightly reasoned and has real logical validity.

M

🏷️
名符其實míng fú qí shí
genuine
She is a genuine expert, not just someone with an impressive title.

N

💪
能力所及néng lì suǒ jí
within sb's capability
I will do everything within my capability to help you.
🐂
牛牽到北京還是牛niú qiān dào běi jīng hái shì niú
A leopard cannot change its spots
He promised to change, but a leopard cannot change its spots.

Q

🐦
槍打出頭鳥qiāng dǎ chū tóu niǎo
the tall poppy gets cut down
the first to stick one's neck out pays the price
Nobody volunteered to criticize the boss, because the first to stick one's neck out usually pays the price.
🔃
前後矛盾qián hòu máo dùn
inconsistencies
The witness's statements were full of inconsistencies.
🎲
切身利害qiè shēn lì hài
skin in the game
Residents have real skin in the game when it comes to the new tax.
🙂
情理之中qíng lǐ zhī zhōng
sth is expected
Given how hard she trained, her victory was only to be expected.
💧
蜻蜓點水qīng tíng diǎn shuǐ
skim the surface
The documentary only skims the surface of the issue.
🙏
情有可原qíng yǒu kě yuán
it makes sense that
be forgiven for sth
He was held up by an accident, so he can be forgiven for missing the start.
👉
取決於你qǔ jué yú nǐ
it's up to you
Do you want to stay or leave? It's up to you.

R

📰
人盡皆知rén jìn jiē zhī
be common knowledge
It is common knowledge that the shop closes at nine.
🌡️
人情冷暖rén qíng lěng nuǎn
the fickleness of human relationships
fair-weather friends
After he lost his job, he learned about the fickleness of human relationships.
🩹
人無完人rén wú wán rén
perfect people don't exist
Nobody is perfect, so forgive his small mistakes.
⏳
日久見人心rì jiǔ jiàn rén xīn
time will tell
Be patient; time will tell who your real friends are.

S

🎒
身外之物shēn wài zhī wù
expendables
He treats wealth and fame as expendables.
🚨
事關重大shì guān zhòng dà
sth cannot be overstated
A looms large on B
The importance of this vote cannot be overstated.
📊
事實勝於雄辯shì shí shèng yú xióng biàn
facts speak louder than words
the facts speak for themselves
The sales figures say it all: facts speak louder than words.
📏
失之毫釐，差之千里shī zhī háo lí, chā zhī qiān lǐ
a slight error leads to a huge difference
In surgery, a slight error leads to a huge difference.
🥇
首要規則shǒu yào guī zé
cardinal rule
The cardinal rule of interpreting is to stay faithful to the speaker's meaning.
💧
水清無魚shuǐ qīng wú yú
In clear water you catch no fish
A boss who tolerates no flaw will end up with no team; in clear water you catch no fish.
🔀
殊途同歸shū tú tóng guī
equifinality
common endpoints by disparate beginnings
Although they started from different places, the two scientists arrived at the same conclusion.

T

🌅
天經地義tiān jīng dì yì
a matter of course
categorically
For many parents, paying for their children's education is a matter of course.

W

🙃
違反常理wéi fǎn cháng lǐ
go against the grain to do sth
Selling a house for less than the land is worth goes against all common sense.
🔨
未審先判wèi shěn xiān pàn
prejudgment
snap (quick) judgment
Calling him guilty before the trial is prejudgment.
🌬️
無風不起浪wú fēng bù qǐ làng
no smoke without fire
There must be something behind that rumor. There's no smoke without fire.
🐟
無稽之談wú jī zhī tán
not in the cards
a fish story
His claim that he caught a shark with his bare hands sounds like a fish story.
🛡️
無可非議wú kě fēi yì
beyond reproach
be above/beyond reproach
Her conduct as a judge has always been beyond reproach.
🤷
無論怎樣wú lùn zěn yàng
At any rate = anyway
At any rate, we need to leave before six.
👨‍⚖️
毋枉毋縱wú wǎng wú zòng
(the rule of) protecting the innocent and punishing the guilty
sth. be handled fairly and justly
The court's duty is to protect the innocent and punish the guilty.
✔️
無庸置疑wú yōng zhì yí
indisputable
It is indisputable that regular exercise improves health.
🧸
無知是福wú zhī shì fú
ignorance is bliss
I never read the reviews. Ignorance is bliss.

X

👀
顯而易見xiǎn ér yì jiàn
beyond obvious
It was beyond obvious that he wasn't listening.
🧩
相輔相成xiāng fǔ xiāng chéng
complement each other
go hand in hand
Good teaching and curiosity complement each other.
🕶️
先入為主xiān rù wéi zhǔ
preconceived notions
a bias
Don't let preconceived notions color your judgment of the candidate.
🧬
息息相關xī xī xiāng guān
be closely related to
closely bound up
Our health is closely bound up with the environment around us.

Y

🎭
眼見不一定為憑yǎn jiàn bù yī dìng wéi píng
Appearances can be deceiving
Appearances can be deceiving, so check the facts before you judge.
👁️
眼見為憑yǎn jiàn wéi píng
seeing is believing
optics matter
Show me the photos; seeing is believing.
📖
言下之意yán xià zhī yì
read between the lines
She called the room "cozy," but if you read between the lines, she meant it was tiny.
⏳
言之過早yán zhì guò zǎo
premature (too early to say that)
It is premature to call the election; it's too early to say who will win.
🐭
一顆老鼠屎，壞了一鍋粥yī kē lǎo shǔ shǐ, huài le yī guō zhōu
a bad apple spoils the bunch
One dishonest employee ruined our reputation: a bad apple spoils the bunch.
🧠
英雄所見略同yīng xióng suǒ jiàn lüè tóng
great minds think alike
We both picked the same title for the book. Great minds think alike!
🎚️
因人而異yīn rén ér yì
YMMV your mileage may vary = it depends
How long it takes to learn a language varies from person to person; your mileage may vary.
🍰
以偏概全yǐ piān gài quán
a broad generalization
Judging a whole country by one tourist is a broad generalization.
🔮
一語成讖yī yǔ chéng chèn
one's words prove prophetic
His casual remark about a storm proved prophetic.
💯
一字不假yī zì bù jiǎ
unadulterated
Every word he said was unadulterated truth.
👣
有跡可循yǒu jì kě xún
discernible, predictable
undiscerning
Stock prices look random, but the pattern is discernible if you look closely.
💰
有錢能使鬼推磨yǒu qián néng shǐ guǐ tuī mò
money talks
money makes the world go round
Money talks in this business, and everyone knows it.
🍕
有失公允yǒu shī gōng yǔn
not fair
The report was not fair to the smaller companies.
➕
有一就有二yǒu yī jiù yǒu èr
one leads to another
If we allow one exception, one leads to another.
📝
有正當理由yǒu zhèng dàng lǐ yóu
justify
The manager asked him to justify the extra expenses.
♻️
冤冤相報何時了yuān yuān xiāng bào hé shí liǎo
an eye for an eye makes the whole world blind
An eye for an eye makes the whole world blind, so let's end this feud.
📜
原著精神yuán zhù jīng shén
true to the spirit of
The film stays true to the spirit of the original novel.
⚖️
於法有據yú fǎ yǒu jù
with a legal basis
The police acted with a legal basis for every step.
🧣
欲蓋彌彰yù gài mí zhāng
draw attention by trying to cover up
backfire
His long denial only drew attention; trying to cover up the mistake backfired.
☁️
雲泥之別yún ní zhī bié
a huge difference between
There is a huge difference between a guess and a verified fact.
🐢
欲速則不達yù sù zé bù dá
more haste less speed
haste makes waste
Don't rush the cake. More haste, less speed.

Z

🦶
站不住腳zhàn bù zhù jiǎo
untenable
doesn't hold water
His argument simply doesn't hold water.
🔦
昭然若揭zhāo rán ruò jiē
can't be more obvious
His plan to take over the company couldn't be more obvious.
🏷️
正如其名zhèng rú qí míng
true to its name
True to its name, the Quiet Café is peaceful and calm.
⚖️
正義終將伸張zhèng yì zhōng jiāng shēn zhāng
justice will be done
Be patient; justice will be done in the end.
📣
眾所周知zhòng suǒ zhōu zhī
as is well known
As is well known, Taiwan produces many of the world's most advanced chips.
🎓
專家背書zhuān jiā bèi shū

endorsed by
The new product is endorsed by leading doctors.
🐖

with the wind behind you, even pigs fly

🌬️

🌀
