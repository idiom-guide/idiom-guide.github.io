Stance & Communication

B

👍
保留各自表述意見bǎo liú gè zì biǎo shù yì jiàn
agree to disagree
We can't agree, so let's agree to disagree.
💎
不含糊的bù hán hu de
unambiguous
She gave an unambiguous answer: no.
🧴
不加修飾bù jiā xiū shì
unfiltered
The documentary offers an unfiltered look at life in prison.
😒
不以為然bù yǐ wéi rán
give sb the side-eye
When I suggested a pay cut, my colleague gave me the side-eye.

C

⏩
長話短說cháng huà duǎn shuō
long story short
Long story short, we missed the flight and took the train.
🙏
誠心一點chéng xīn yī diǎn
say like you mean it
If you want to apologize, say it like you mean it.
😶
沉默寡言chén mò guǎ yán
has a reticence about
My grandfather is a man of few words, with a certain reticence about his past.

D

🦁
大膽主張dà dǎn zhǔ zhāng
bold claim
long shot
audacious
The scientist made a bold claim that no one had dared to make before.
👏
大加讚賞dà jiā zàn shǎng
speak highly of
Critics spoke highly of the young director's first film.
📢
大力鼓吹dà lì gǔ chuī
champion
advocate
My mother is all for learning a second language.

E

💊
扼要表述è yào biǎo shù
encapsulate A in B
She managed to encapsulate the whole idea in a single sentence.

F

❤️
發自肺腑fā zì fèi fǔ
genuine
Her apology was genuine and clearly came from the heart.
💗
發自內心fā zì nèi xīn
visceral + n
He felt a visceral fear when he saw the flames.
💌
肺腑之言fèi fǔ zhī yán
from the bottom of my heart
These words come from the bottom of my heart.

G

🎤
歌功頌德gē gōng sòng dé
sing the praises of sb/sth
The speeches did nothing but sing the praises of the mayor.
↪️
拐彎抹角guǎi wān mò jiǎo
beat around the bush
obliquely
Stop beating around the bush and tell me what happened.
😄
故作戲謔gù zuò xì xuè
tongue in cheek
His remark that he "loved paperwork" was clearly tongue in cheek.

H

🌁
含糊其詞hán hú qí cí
talk in generalities
be coy with sb
Asked about the budget, the spokesman talked in generalities.
🦏
毫不客氣háo bù kè qì
unceremoniously
She unceremoniously told the noisy guests to leave.
😎
毫不掩飾háo bù yǎn shì
unabashed
He showed unabashed delight when his team won.

J

🤞
講話負責jiǎng huà fù zé
be true to one's word
stand by one's words
A leader should stand by his words.
🏹
講話直率jiǎng huà zhí shuài
straight shooter
Our new manager is a straight shooter who says exactly what she thinks.
✂️
簡明扼要jiǎn míng è yào
be succinct
succinctly
concise
Please keep your report succinct; we only have ten minutes.
😏
機智反諷jī zhì fǎn fěng
quipster
The host is a quipster whose sharp irony keeps the audience laughing.
🙊
絕口不提jué kǒu bù tí
be reticent about sth
He remained reticent about his family history.
✊
據理力爭jù lǐ lì zhēng
fight tooth and nail
She fought tooth and nail for the funding.

K

🔓
開誠布公kāi chéng bù gōng
in full transparency
The two leaders spoke in full transparency about their differences.
💬
開放討論kāi fàng tǎo lùn
open to interpretation
be open-minded
The proposal is open for discussion, and we are open-minded about changes.
🚪
開門見山kāi mén jiàn shān
straight to the point
cut to the punchline
bottom-line it
She went straight to the point: the company was being sold.
🔥
慷慨激昂kāng kǎi jī áng
impassioned (speech)
passionate
He gave an impassioned speech in defense of the library.

L

🍽️
來者不拒lái zhě bù jù
nothing's off menu
At our open-mic night, everyone is welcome and nothing's off the menu.
😊
樂見其成lè jiàn qí chéng
be glad to see it happen
welcome
We would be glad to see the two companies merge.
👍
令人激賞lìng rén jī shǎng
commendable
admirable
Her patience with the students is truly admirable.

M

🙇
冒昧擅自mào mèi shàn zì
take the liberty of V-ing
I took the liberty of ordering for everyone.
💎
妙語如珠miào yǔ rú zhū
witty
scintillating repartee
The speaker was witty, and her talk was full of scintillating repartee.
🌫️
模稜兩可mó léng liǎng kě
equivocal
The minister gave an equivocal answer that satisfied no one.

N

😬
難以啟齒nán yǐ qǐ chǐ
hate to say it but
I hate to say it, but your presentation needs more work.

P

🥁
旁敲側擊páng qiāo cè jī
probe
He probed gently to find out whether she planned to quit.
🧱
拋磚引玉pāo zhuān yǐn yù
just tossing an idea out there
I'm just tossing an idea out there to get the discussion started.

Q

🖌️
輕描淡寫qīng miáo dàn xiě
understatement
downplay
Calling the storm "a little rain" was a huge understatement.
📤
全盤托出quán pán tuō chū
lay/put sb's cards on the table
come clean with sb. about sth
After hours of questioning, he finally came clean about the missing funds.

R

🗯️
熱烈討論rè liè tǎo lùn
a heated discussion
lively debate
The proposal sparked a heated discussion.

S

🎭
煞有其事shà yǒu qí shì
with an air of solemnity
as if it were true
He spoke with an air of solemnity, as if announcing a royal decree.
🌸
舌燦蓮花shé càn lián huā
articulate
The salesman was so articulate that he could talk anyone into buying.
🤐
守口如瓶shǒu kǒu rú píng
tight-lipped
keep mum
He stayed tight-lipped about the merger.
➗
說句公道話shuō jù gōng dào huà
in fairness
to be fair
In fairness, the team had only two weeks to prepare.
🙋
恕我直言shù wǒ zhí yán
with all due respect
frankly speaking
With all due respect, I think the plan has a few problems.
🚪
私底下sī dǐ xià
in private
behind closed doors
She told me in private that she plans to resign.

T

🖖
坦誠相對tǎn chéng xiāng duì
level with sb
I'll level with you: we are running out of money.
🃏
坦承相見tǎn chéng xiāng jiàn
come clean
lay one's cards on the table
It's time to come clean about what really happened.
👨‍🎤
坦率直言tǎn shuài zhí yán
give sb straight talk about sth
My coach gave me straight talk about my chances of making the team.
🔯
天機不可洩漏tiān jī bù kě xiè lòu
keep my own counsel
Don't ask about the surprise; I'll keep my own counsel.

W

👁️
未加掩飾wèi jiā yǎn shì
unvarnished (truth, statement)
The report presents the unvarnished truth about the crisis.
🔍
微言大義wēi yán dà yì
subtle words with profound meaning
In a few subtle words, the author conveyed a profound message.

X

☝️
下不為例xià bù wéi lì
make sure this is the last
I'll let it go this time, but make sure this is the last.
⚡
心直口快xīn zhí kǒu kuài
straight shooter
Her quick tongue sometimes gets her into trouble, but at least she is a straight shooter.

Y

📝
言簡意賅yán jiǎn yì gāi
concise and to the point
Her report was concise and to the point.
🔤
咬文嚼字yǎo wén jiáo zì
split hairs over wording
be pedantic
Stop being pedantic. Everyone understood what I meant.
💭
一點淺見yī diǎn qiǎn jiàn
my two cents
just my two cents
Here's my two cents: we should wait a week.
🎶
異口同聲yì kǒu tóng shēng
with one voice
in unison
The students answered in unison.
🙍
義正辭嚴yì zhèng cí yán
in a stern, righteous tone
He turned down the bribe in a stern, righteous tone.
🎯
一針見血yī zhēn jiàn xiě
hit the nail on the head
You hit the nail on the head with that comment.

Z

🤔
暫不表態zàn bù biǎo tài
on the fence
The senator remains on the fence about the bill.
💪
爭氣一點zhēng qì yī diǎn
step up the game = improve
Come on, step up your game. The final is next week.
📜
鄭重聲明zhèng zhòng shēng míng
For the record
For the record, I never approved this expense.
🧭
指點迷津zhǐ diǎn mí jīn
point sb in the right direction
My mentor pointed me in the right direction when I was lost.
📢
直言不諱zhí yán bù huì
be open about that
frankly
let's call a spade a spade
outspoken
Let's call a spade a spade: the plan has failed.
🔎
字字斟酌zì zì zhēn zhuó
carefully examine
She carefully examined every word of the contract before signing.
