6

Scenery & Settings

Describing landscapes, atmosphere and scenes: polish for writing and narration.

32 idioms
Browse, search and self-test interactively →Guide

C

🌺
奼紫嫣紅chà zǐ yān hóng
a blaze of bright colors
In April the garden turns into a blaze of bright colors.
🏔️
崇山峻嶺chóng shān jùn lǐng
towering and steep mountains
The railway crosses towering and steep mountains.

D

🏮
燈紅酒綠dēng hóng jiǔ lǜ
feasting and pleasure seeking
The district is a place of feasting and pleasure seeking.

F

🌿
富饒茂盛fù ráo mào shèng
luxuriant
The valley was lush and luxuriant.

G

🏯
古色古香gǔ sè gǔ xiāng
of antique beauty
The tea house has an antique beauty.

H

🏞️
湖光山色hú guāng shān sè
beautiful scenery of lakes and mountains
The hotel offers beautiful scenery of lakes and mountains.

J

🏛️
金碧輝煌jīn bì huī huáng
resplendent and magnificent
The palace was resplendent and magnificent.
🔭
盡收眼底jìn shōu yǎn dǐ
command a panoramic view of
From the tower, you command a panoramic view of the city.

L

❄️
凜冬將至lǐn dōng jiāng zhì
Winter is coming
Stock up on firewood. Winter is coming.
🔮
玲瓏剔透líng lóng tī tòu
beautifully wrought
The jade carving was beautifully wrought.
🛍️
琳琅滿目lín láng mǎn mù
a superb collection of beautiful things
a dazzling line-up
The market offered a dazzling lineup of handmade crafts.
🌊
綠波盪漾lǜ bō dàng yàng
rippling
The lake was rippling in the breeze.

M

🌨️
滿山遍谷mǎn shān biàn gǔ
in the blizzard of
Cherry petals fell like a blizzard of pink snow across the valley.

Q

🗺️
千山萬水qiān shān wàn shuǐ
a long and tiring journey
He made a long and tiring journey across mountains and rivers to see her.
🛠️
巧奪天工qiǎo duó tiān gōng
exquisite workmanship
The ivory carving shows exquisite workmanship.
🌸
奇花異草qí huā yì cǎo
exotic flowers and herbs
The garden is home to exotic flowers and herbs.

R

👥
人山人海rén shān rén hǎi
huge crowds of faces; sea of people; a multitude; a vast crowd
A vast crowd filled the square for the fireworks.
🖼️
如詩如畫rú shī rú huà
picturesque
idyllic
The village is picturesque.

S

⛰️
山峰連綿shān fēng lián mián
range upon range of hills
Range upon range of hills disappeared into the mist.
👗
盛裝打扮shèng zhuāng dǎ bàn
be dressed to the nines
Everyone was dressed to the nines for the gala.
🍁
四季分明sì jì fēn míng
xxx has seasons
The region has four distinct seasons.

T

🌋
天下奇觀tiān xià qí guān
wondrous spectacle
The aurora was a wondrous spectacle.

W

🌷
萬紫千紅wàn zǐ qiān hóng
a riot of colors
Spring brought a riot of colors to the hillside.

X

🎭
相映成趣xiāng yìng chéng qù
form a delightful contrast
The old temple and the glass tower form a delightful contrast.
🚦
熙來攘往xī lái rǎng wǎng
hustle and bustle
The hustle and bustle of the night market never stops.
🌈
絢麗多姿xuàn lì duō zī
gorgeous and colorful
The festival costumes were gorgeous and colorful.

Y

🌴
異國情調yì guó qíng diào
exoticism
The restaurant's decor has a certain exoticism.
✨
熠熠生輝yì yì shēng huī
dazzling
The crown jewels were dazzling under the lights.

Z

🦪
珍珠光澤zhēn zhū guāng zé
pearlescent
The silk had a pearlescent shine.
🏘️
櫛比鱗次zhì bǐ lín cì
placed closely side by side
Shops stood closely side by side along the street.
🕊️
莊嚴肅穆zhuāng yán sù mù
solemn
solemn and serene
The ceremony was solemn and serene.
🌄
卓越美景zhuó yuè měi jǐng
scenery of exceptional charm
The valley is a scenery of exceptional charm.
