pass

pass · passed · passedMotion & positionNo. 37 in the original

Core imageGoing past or through: passing a place, passing things along, passing exams and bills; extended to time passing, things ending and passing on a turn.

Meanings and synonyms 18 meanings

Interpreting tip

"The bill passed its third reading." "I'll pass" declines a question or turn. Pass the buck is the everyday way to say shirk responsibility.
