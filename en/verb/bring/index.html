bring

bring · brought · broughtMotion & positionNo. 54 in the original

Core imageCarrying something to where the speaker is, extended to bringing about results or change and bringing up a topic.

Meanings and synonyms 2 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with bring; when you would rather not repeat bring, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 帶來・拿來take
    carryfetchdeliverconveyaccompanyescortusherlead

    Please bring your ID to the registration desk.

    報到時請攜帶身分證件。

  2. 帶來・造成cause
    causeproduceresult inlead to

    The new year brought fresh challenges.

    新的一年帶來新的挑戰。

    科技為我們這一行帶來巨大變化。

Interpreting tip

Bring is toward the speaker; take is away. Bring up a question is more conversational than raise.
