become

become · became · becomeDoing & changingNo. 84 in the original

Core imageTurning into something: a change of state or identity; in an older sense, suiting or befitting someone.

Meanings and synonyms 3 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with become; when you would rather not repeat become, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 變得・成為grow
    growdevelopturn intocome to beget

    It's becoming harder to find qualified staff.

    找到合格人才越來越難。

  2. 適合・相稱suit
    suitflatterbefitbe fitting

    Such remarks hardly become a diplomat.

    這種言論實在不符外交官身分。

  3. （某人）後來怎麼了happen
    happen to

    Whatever became of your old business partner?

    你以前的生意夥伴後來怎麼樣了？

Chinese idioms on this site

忿忿不平become indignant形同陌路become like strangers走火入魔become obsessed to the point of madness

Interpreting tip

In speech, get often replaces become: It's getting harder. "What became of…?" asks what happened to someone.
