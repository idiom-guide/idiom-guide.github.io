see

see · saw · seenMind & sensesNo. 14 in the original

Core imageSeeing with your eyes, extended to understanding, experiencing, meeting someone and seeing to it that something gets done.

Meanings and synonyms 5 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with see; when you would rather not repeat see, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 看見・察覺・明白perceive
    catch sight ofglimpsediscernmake outrecogniseidentifynoticeobserveunderstand

    I see what you mean.

    我懂你的意思。

  2. 觀賞（電影、演出）attend
    watchattend

    We saw a great play last weekend.

    我們上週末看了一齣很棒的戲。

  3. 見證・經歷witness
    witnessexperienceundergo

    The region has seen rapid growth over the past decade.

    這個地區過去十年快速成長。

  4. 見面・看（醫生）visit
    visitconsultmeet

    You should see a doctor about that cough.

    你咳成這樣，應該去看醫生。

  5. 確保・負責辦妥ensure
    ensuremake suresee to it

    I'll see that everyone gets a copy.

    我會確保每個人都拿到一份。

Interpreting tip

"I see" is the light, natural way to say you understand. When describing trends, English often says "The country has seen…" where Chinese might say 經歷 or 見證.
