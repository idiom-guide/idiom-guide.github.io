like

like · liked · likedMind & sensesNo. 70 in the original

Core imageLiking and enjoying; would like is the polite way to say you want something.

Meanings and synonyms 2 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with like; when you would rather not repeat like, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 喜歡・欣賞appreciatevt.
    approve ofadmirebe fond ofenjoybe keen onlovedelight in

    I really like the new layout.

    我很喜歡新的版面。

  2. 想要（would like）wantvt.
    preferwantwishwould rather

    I'd like to make a short statement.

    我想發表一段簡短的聲明。

Idioms 4

  • how do you like…?你覺得…怎麼樣；你喜歡…怎麼做
  • like it or lump it不管喜不喜歡都得接受
  • what's not to like?有什麼好挑剔的
  • likes and dislikes好惡
