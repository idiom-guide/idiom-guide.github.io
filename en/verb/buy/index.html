buy

buy · bought · boughtGetting & givingNo. 6 in the original

Core imageGetting something in exchange for money; extended to buying someone's loyalty, accepting a story and gaining time.

Meanings and synonyms 3 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with buy; when you would rather not repeat buy, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 買・購買purchase
    purchasepay forprocuresnap up

    We bought the tickets online.

    我們在網路上買票。

  2. 收買・賄賂bribe
    bribecorruptsuborn
  3. 相信・接受（說法）believe
    acceptbelieveswallow

    I don't buy his excuse.

    我才不信他的藉口。

Interpreting tip

Buy time is more idiomatic than gain time. Buy also means to accept an explanation: "They didn't buy it."
