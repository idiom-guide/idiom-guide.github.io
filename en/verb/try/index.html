try

try · tried · triedDoing & changingNo. 17 in the original

Core imageHaving a go: trying something out, tasting, testing; extended to a court trying a case and to trying someone's patience.

Meanings and synonyms 4 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with try; when you would rather not repeat try, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 試用・試吃・試試看testvt.
    check outexperimenttry outinvestigatetestput to the test

    Have you tried the new noodle place downstairs?

    你吃過樓下那家新開的麵店嗎？

  2. 審判・審理judgevt.
    hear a caseconduct the trial of

    The case will be tried in the High Court.

    本案將由高等法院審理。

  3. 考驗・使煩躁strainvt.
    exertwear outtest

    His constant questions were starting to try her patience.

    他一直問個不停，讓她快沒耐性了。

  4. 嘗試・努力attemptvi.
    attemptseekhave a gogive it a gostrive

    I'll try to get there by seven.

    我盡量七點前到。

Phrasal verbs 4

  • try on試穿

    Can I try this jacket on?

    這件外套可以試穿嗎？

    try on
  • try it on試探・耍花招
    attempt to deceive
  • try out試用；參加選拔
    testaudition
  • try sth out on sb先拿給某人試試
    test

Idioms 4

  • try one's hand at sth嘗試（新事物）
  • try one's luck碰碰運氣
  • try sb's patience考驗某人的耐性
  • a good try不錯的嘗試

Interpreting tip

"I'll try" or "I'll do my best". Try to do means make an effort; try doing means experiment: Try restarting the computer.
