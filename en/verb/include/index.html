include

include · included · includedGetting & givingNo. 93 in the original

Core imageCounting something as part of a whole, and letting people take part.

Meanings and synonyms 2 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with include; when you would rather not repeat include, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 包含・包括incorporate
    compriseencompasscovercontaininvolvetake into account

    The price includes breakfast.

    價格含早餐。

  2. 把（某人）算在內count in
    involvecount inadd

    Thanks for including me.

    謝謝你把我算進來。

Interpreting tip

Including and excluding for lists; "Is tax included?" Formal documents use including but not limited to.
