require

require · required · requiredGetting & givingNo. 95 in the original

Core imageNeeding and demanding; more formal than need, common in rules and conditions.

Meanings and synonyms 4 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with require; when you would rather not repeat require, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 需要need
    needdepend uponwant

    The job requires good English.

    這份工作需要良好的英文能力。

  2. 必須付出・必然涉及demand
    demandcall forentailnecessitateinvolve

    Simultaneous interpreting requires intense concentration.

    同步口譯需要高度專注。

  3. （規定）要求order
    orderdirectcommandcompelobligeinstruct

    The new rules require companies to disclose emissions data.

    新規定要求企業揭露排放數據。

  4. 要求（某人做）ask
    askinstructoblige

    Visitors are required to sign in.

    訪客須登記。

Interpreting tip

Notices use be required to: All passengers are required to… In speech, need to or have to is enough.
