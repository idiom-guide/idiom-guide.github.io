talk

talk · talked · talkedSpeakingNo. 46 in the original

Core imageConversation: talking, chatting, negotiating, also gossiping and confessing; in speech it can emphasize "now we're talking about…".

Meanings and synonyms 5 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with talk; when you would rather not repeat talk, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 說話・談・討論speakvi.
    speakchatconversediscussaddresscover

    Can we talk about this later?

    這件事我們晚點再談好嗎？

  2. 談判・協商negotiatevi.
    negotiatediscussbargain

    The two sides are still talking.

    雙方仍在協商。

  3. 說閒話gossipvi.
    gossipspread rumours
  4. 招供・吐實confessvi.
    confessadmitrevealspill the beans
  5. 強調（we're talking…）emphasisevt.
    meanrefer to

    We're talking millions of dollars here.

    這可是好幾百萬美元的事。

Interpreting tip

Diplomatic talks are held: hold talks, and "The two sides are still talking." Talk somebody into something is more conversational than persuade.
