pull

pull · pulled · pulledMotion & positionNo. 39 in the original

Core imageDrawing toward yourself: dragging, extracting, extended to drawing crowds, pulling a muscle, pulling over, pulling out and pulling through.

Meanings and synonyms 5 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with pull; when you would rather not repeat pull, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 拉・拖dragvt.
    drawhaultrailtowtugjerkyankwrench

    Pull the door, don't push it.

    門要用拉的，不是推的。

  2. 拔出・抽出extractvt.
    pickremovetake outpluckuprootdraw out
  3. 吸引（人潮）attractvt.
    attractdrawbring inlurepull in

    The festival pulls huge crowds every year.

    這個節慶每年都吸引大批人潮。

  4. 拉傷strainvt.
    straintearsprainwrench

    He pulled a muscle during the warm-up.

    他熱身時拉傷了肌肉。

  5. 用力拉dragvi.
    tugheave

Interpreting tip

Pull out of a deal or market is common in news; pull strings means using connections; pull together means joining forces.
