learn

learn · learned/learnt · learned/learntMind & sensesNo. 78 in the original

Core imageAcquiring skills and memorising, and also hearing or finding out about something.

Meanings and synonyms 3 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with learn; when you would rather not repeat learn, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 學・學會mastervt.
    acquirepick upbe taughtgain knowledge ofmaster

    It takes years to learn simultaneous interpreting.

    學會同步口譯要好幾年。

  2. 背・記住memorizevt.
    commit to memorylearn by heart

    I learned the speech by heart.

    我把講稿背下來了。

  3. 得知・獲悉discovervt.
    find outcome to knowbecome awarehearbe informed of

    We learned that the flight had been cancelled.

    我們得知班機取消了。

Interpreting tip

News reports use "It has been learned that…" or "We have learned that…". Learn from mistakes.
