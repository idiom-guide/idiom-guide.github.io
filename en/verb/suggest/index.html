suggest

suggest · suggested · suggestedSpeakingNo. 96 in the original

Core imagePutting forward an idea: suggesting, extended to implying and to evidence indicating something.

Meanings and synonyms 3 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with suggest; when you would rather not repeat suggest, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 建議・提議propose
    put forwardproposeadvocaterecommendadvise

    I suggest we take a short break.

    我建議我們休息一下。

  2. 暗示・意思是imply
    implyhintinsinuateallude

    Are you suggesting I'm wrong?

    你是在暗示我錯了嗎？

  3. 顯示・表明indicate
    indicatepoint toshowmean

    The data suggest a slowdown in demand.

    數據顯示需求放緩。

Idioms 2

  • suggest itself（想法）浮現
  • at sb's suggestion在某人建議下

Interpreting tip

Suggest takes a that-clause or -ing: suggest that we leave, suggest leaving, never suggest somebody to do. In research, suggest is more cautious than prove.
