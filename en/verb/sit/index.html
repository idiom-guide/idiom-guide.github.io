sit

sit · sat · satMotion & positionNo. 16 in the original

Core imageSitting down and staying put: taking a seat, sitting on a committee; extended to a parliament or court sitting, and a building sitting somewhere.

Meanings and synonyms 6 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with sit; when you would rather not repeat sit, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

    take a seatperchsettle downbe seated

    Please sit wherever you like.

    請隨意入座。

  2. 擔任模特兒（讓人畫）posevi.

  3. 擔任（委員）be a member ofvi.

  4. （議會、法院）開會convenevi.

Interpreting tip

To attend a meeting as an observer is sit in on; board or committee members sit on it. Sit on the fence (refuse to take sides) is mildly critical.
