raise

raise · raised · raisedDoing & changingNo. 80 in the original

Core imageLifting up: raising and increasing, extended to bringing up children, raising funds, raising questions and raising concerns.

Meanings and synonyms 9 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with raise; when you would rather not repeat raise, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 舉起・抬起liftvt.
    elevatelifthoist

    Please raise your hand if you have a question.

    有問題請舉手。

  2. 提高・調升increasevt.
    put uppush upincreaseboostamplify

    The central bank raised interest rates.

    央行升息。

  3. 晉升promotevt.
    promoteupgradeelevate
  4. 養育；飼養bring upvt.
    rearbring upnurturebreed

    She was born and raised in Kaohsiung.

    她在高雄出生長大。

  5. 募集・籌措collectvt.
    collectgatherobtainamassmuster

    The startup raised NT$100 million.

    這家新創募得一億元新臺幣。

  6. 引起（疑慮、警覺）cause to occurvt.
    give rise tocausepromptelicitprovoke

    The report raises serious concerns.

    這份報告引起嚴重疑慮。

  7. 解除（封鎖、禁令）abandonvt.
    liftendcancel
  8. 提出（問題、議題）mentionvt.
    bring upput forwardsubmitmention

    I'd like to raise a point of order.

    我想提出程序問題。

  9. 使復活resurrectvt.
    bring back from the dead

Interpreting tip

Raise funds, raise capital, raise rates, raise a question, raise an issue. Raise is everywhere in finance and meeting interpreting.
