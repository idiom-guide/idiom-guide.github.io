need

need · needed · neededGetting & givingNo. 73 in the original

Core imageNot being able to do without something; extended to must and to being necessary.

Meanings and synonyms 3 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with need; when you would rather not repeat need, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 需要（依賴）requirevt.
    wantdemandcall forrely on

    We need more time.

    我們需要更多時間。

  2. 需要（補足）wantvt.
    lackrequirehave need of

    The building needs some repairs.

    這棟建築需要整修。

  3. 必須・有必要obligationvt.
    have tobe obliged tomust

    You don't need to bring anything.

    你什麼都不用帶。

Idioms 7

  • need I say more?還需要我多說嗎？
  • needs must情勢所逼
  • as the need arises視需要
  • there's no need to不必
  • if need be必要時
  • in need貧困・有需要的
  • in need of需要

Interpreting tip

You don't need to… or There's no need to… is softer than You don't have to. People in need are those who lack basic support.
