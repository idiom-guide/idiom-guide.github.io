die

die · died · diedDoing & changingNo. 8 in the original

Core imageComing to a stop: a life ending, something fading away, a machine dying; and, in exaggeration, wanting something badly.

Meanings and synonyms 4 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with die; when you would rather not repeat die, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 死亡・去世stop livingvi.
    pass awayperishdepartexpirestarvedrownwither

    Her grandfather died at the age of 98.

    她祖父九十八歲過世。

  2. 消失・衰退disappearvi.
    dwindlefadeebbwanepeter outvanishfade awaydecaylapseendmelt away

    The tradition is slowly dying.

    這項傳統正逐漸消失。

  3. （機器）停擺・沒電stop workingvi.
    break downfailconk out

    My phone died halfway through the call.

    電話講到一半，手機沒電了。

  4. 渴望・想得要命cravevi.
    long foryearndesirebe desperate for

    I'm dying to know what happened.

    我超想知道發生了什麼事。

Phrasal verbs 3

  • die down平息・減弱

    The controversy took months to die down.

    這場爭議過了幾個月才平息。

    go outsubsidefadeabatewear offpeter out
  • die off相繼死去・逐漸凋零
  • die out滅絕・消失
    become extinct

Idioms 6

  • die a death徹底失敗・無疾而終
  • die laughing笑死
  • old habits die hard積習難改
  • to die for好得不得了
  • in the dying minutes在最後幾分鐘
  • never say die永不放棄

Chinese idioms on this site

英年早逝die young胎死腹中die on the vine

Interpreting tip

In formal settings pass away is gentler; die is neutral and direct, common in news and writing. "I nearly died laughing" is everyday hyperbole.
