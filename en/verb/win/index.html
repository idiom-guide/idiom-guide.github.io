win

win · won · wonGetting & givingNo. 19 in the original

Core imageComing out on top in a contest, and also gaining prizes, support and goodwill.

Meanings and synonyms 4 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with win; when you would rather not repeat win, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 贏（比賽、選舉）be victoriousvt.
    succeedcome firsttriumphprevailconquer

    Who won the election?

    誰贏得選舉？

  2. 贏得・爭取到getvt.
    achievebe awardedearntakeobtainacquiregainsecure

    The film won three awards at Golden Horse.

    這部電影在金馬獎拿下三個獎項。

  3. 獲勝be victoriousvi.
    succeedcome throughbe victoriousbe right

    Our team won by two points.

    我們隊以兩分之差獲勝。

  4. 獲選・獲准getvi.
    be approvedbe electedbe selected

Interpreting tip

Win support and win trust sound more dynamic than obtain. Win applause, win the contract.
