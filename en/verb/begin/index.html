begin

begin · began · begunDoing & changingNo. 77 in the original

Core imageStarting; close to start but slightly more formal, common in writing and ceremonies.

Meanings and synonyms 2 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with begin; when you would rather not repeat begin, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 開始・起源startvi.
    commencestartoriginateariseemergestem from

    The ceremony will begin at ten.

    典禮十點開始。

  2. 著手・開創initiatevt.
    inauguratelaunchembark oninitiatefoundset in motion

    The two sides will begin talks next week.

    雙方下週將展開會談。

Idioms 3

  • can't begin to根本無法
  • charity begins at home先照顧好自己人
  • to begin with首先；起初

Interpreting tip

Begin and start are mostly interchangeable, but engines only start. To begin with… is a little more formal than First of all.
