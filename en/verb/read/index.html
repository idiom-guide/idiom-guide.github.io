read

read · read · readSpeakingNo. 40 in the original

Core imageReading text, extended to reading minds and situations, reading aloud, an instrument giving a reading and, in British usage, studying a subject at university.

Meanings and synonyms 8 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with read; when you would rather not repeat read, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 閱讀・看（文件）readvt.
    look atpore overstudyperusescanexamineskim

    Have you read the briefing notes?

    簡報資料你讀過了嗎？

  2. 解讀・理解understandvt.
    interpretcomprehenddecipherdecode

    It's hard to read his mood.

    很難摸清他的心情。

  3. 唸出・朗讀recitevt.
    deliverrecitenarrate

    She read the statement aloud.

    她把聲明唸了出來。

  4. （儀器）顯示measurevt.
    showdisplayregister

    The thermometer read 38 degrees.

    溫度計顯示三十八度。

  5. （在大學）主修studyvt.
    studymajor in

    He read law at Oxford.

    他在牛津攻讀法律。

  6. （電腦）讀取computingvt.
    processaccess
  7. 識字・會讀literacyvi.
    be literate
  8. 從書報得知discovervi.
    learnhearfind out

    I read about it in the news.

    我在新聞上看到這件事。

Phrasal verbs 4

  • read out唸出來・宣讀
    reciteannounce
  • read up (on)研讀・惡補

    I need to read up on the semiconductor industry before the job.

    接案前我得先惡補半導體產業。

    researchstudybrush up on
  • read over從頭看一遍・校對
    proofreadcheckexamine
  • read into過度解讀

    Don't read too much into it.

    別想太多。

    inferover-interpret

Idioms 5

  • read between the lines讀出言外之意
  • read sb like a book看透某人
  • take sth as read視為理所當然
  • read sb the riot act嚴厲訓斥
  • read my lips聽清楚

Interpreting tip

Read up on a topic is how interpreters prepare for an assignment. Read also means interpret: "How do you read the market?"
