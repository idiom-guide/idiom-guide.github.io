stand

stand · stood · stoodMotion & positionNo. 59 in the original

Core imageBeing upright: standing and being located, extended to taking a position, figures standing at a level, and being able to stand something.

Meanings and synonyms 7 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with stand; when you would rather not repeat stand, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 站・站起來rise / be on one's feetvi.
    riseget upstand up

    Please stand for the national anthem.

    請起立，唱國歌。

  2. 矗立；（數字）為bevi.
    be locatedexistbeadd up to

    Unemployment stands at 3.4%.

    失業率為百分之三點四。

  3. 立場・持什麼看法have an opinionvi.
    thinktake a position

    Where do you stand on nuclear power?

    你對核能持什麼立場？

  4. 可能（獲得或損失）be likely tovi.
    be likely tobe in a position to

    Small exporters stand to lose the most.

    小型出口商可能損失最大。

  5. 仍然有效remainvi.
    be validapplyhold

    My offer still stands.

    我的提議仍然有效。

  6. 豎放・擺放putvt.
    placeset upright
  7. 忍受・受得了toleratevt.
    endurebeartoleratecope withabide

    I can't stand the heat.

    我受不了這種熱。

Interpreting tip

For data, stand at is the standard: GDP growth stands at 2.1%. "Where do you stand on…?" is more conversational than "What is your position on…?"
