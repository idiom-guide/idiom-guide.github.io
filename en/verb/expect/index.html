expect

expect · expected · expectedMind & sensesNo. 88 in the original

Core imageAnticipating: thinking something will happen, waiting for someone, extended to demanding, and to "I suppose".

Meanings and synonyms 3 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with expect; when you would rather not repeat expect, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 預期・預計・等候anticipate
    predictforecastawaitanticipatelook forward to

    The talks are expected to last two days.

    會談預計為期兩天。

  2. 要求・指望require
    ask forcall forinsist ondemandrequire

    We expect all members to attend.

    我們要求所有成員出席。

  3. 猜想・認為suppose
    presumethinkbelieveimagineassume

    I expect you're tired after the flight.

    搭了這麼久的飛機，我想你一定累了。

Idioms 3

  • be expecting (a baby)懷孕
  • only to be expected意料之中
  • what do you expect?不然你以為呢

Interpreting tip

News uses be expected to: The bill is expected to pass. As expected. "I expect you to…" carries a demand, so mind the tone.
