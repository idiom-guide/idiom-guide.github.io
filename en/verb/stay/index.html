stay

stay · stayed · stayedMotion & positionNo. 44 in the original

Core imageRemaining in place or unchanged: staying, lodging, keeping a state; in law, a stay is a suspension.

Meanings and synonyms 3 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with stay; when you would rather not repeat stay, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 保持・留下remainvi.
    lastcontinuepersistkeependurelingerremain

    Please stay seated until the plane stops.

    飛機停妥前請留在座位上。

  2. 住宿・暫住spend the nightvi.
    stopboardlodgeput upbe accommodatedsojourn

    Where are you staying in Taipei?

    你在臺北住哪裡？

  3. 暫緩・延緩（執行）suspendvt.
    restraincheckhold backhaltpostponedelaysuspend

Interpreting tip

Stay in touch; stay stable or remain stable. Stay is conversational and remain more formal, so choose by register.
