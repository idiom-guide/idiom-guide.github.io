put

put · put · putMotion & position★ Start hereNo. 12 in the original

Core imagePutting something in a place, then extended to putting thoughts into words, putting a figure on something and putting someone in a situation.

Meanings and synonyms 6 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with put; when you would rather not repeat put, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 放・擺・寫上・歸類place
    placepositionset downlay downdepositleavestickattribute toascribewriteassign toallocate to

    Where did you put the signed contract?

    簽好的合約你放在哪裡？

  2. 表達・措辭express
    framerenderconveystatesaywordphrase

    Let me put it another way.

    我換個方式說。

  3. 估計estimate
    reckonassessevaluatejudgecalculate

    Analysts put the cost at around NT$2 billion.

    分析師估計成本約新臺幣二十億元。

  4. 提出（問題、提案）submit
    presentsuggestproposeput forwardpose

    May I put a question to the minister?

    我可以向部長提個問題嗎？

  5. 投入devote
    investdevotework hard

    She put years of work into this report.

    她為這份報告投入多年心血。

  6. 使處於（某種處境）cause
    easecomfortcalmendangerjeopardizerisk

    The scandal put the whole team under pressure.

    這起醜聞讓整個團隊承受壓力。

Interpreting tip

Put is the verb for rephrasing: to put it simply, let me put it another way, how shall I put it. Interpreters use these lines to restate or to buy a moment.
