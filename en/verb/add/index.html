add

add · added · addedDoing & changingNo. 4 in the original

Core imagePutting more on top: adding numbers, adding content, and adding a remark when you speak.

Meanings and synonyms 3 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with add; when you would rather not repeat add, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 加總・相加count up
    totalreckonsum upcomputeadd up

    If you add the shipping fee, it comes to NT$1,200.

    加上運費，總共新臺幣一千二百元。

  2. 增加・附加include
    includeattachsupplementincreaseannexamplifyaugmentextend

    We've added two new routes this year.

    我們今年新增了兩條航線。

  3. 補充說continue to speak
    go oncarry oninterject

    "And it has to be done by Friday," she added.

    她補了一句：「而且週五前要完成。」

Idioms 3

  • added to this/that除此之外・再者

Interpreting tip

To add a point, say "If I may add…" or "I'd just like to add that…". Supplement sounds stiff in speech.
