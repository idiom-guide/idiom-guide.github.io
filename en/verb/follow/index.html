follow

follow · followed · followedMotion & positionNo. 65 in the original

Core imageComing after: following and happening next, extended to following a route, following rules, following an argument and following news.

Meanings and synonyms 9 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with follow; when you would rather not repeat follow, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 跟著・接在…之後go / come aftervt.
    pursuetrailtrackchaseshadowsucceedcome next

    A reception will follow the ceremony.

    典禮之後有酒會。

  2. 推論出resultvt.
    follow logically
  3. 沿著（路）走tracevt.
    go alongproceed alongtake

    Follow the signs to the main hall.

    沿著指標走到大廳。

  4. 遵照・效法adhere tovt.
    adoptheedobeybe guided bystick tocomply with

    Please follow the instructions carefully.

    請仔細遵照指示。

  5. 聽懂・跟得上understandvt.
    understandcomprehendgraspgetcatch

    Sorry, I don't quite follow.

    抱歉，我不太懂。

  6. 關注・追蹤keep up withvt.
    keep up to date withwatchmonitorkeep abreast of

    Are you following the trade talks?

    你有在關注貿易談判嗎？

  7. 隨後發生go / come aftervi.
    ensue

    Details will follow.

    細節稍後公布。

  8. （邏輯上）成立resultvi.
    ensue
  9. 聽懂understandvi.
    comprehendgraspget

Interpreting tip

A follow-up question; follow up on something. "I'm not sure I follow" is a gentler way to say you don't understand.
