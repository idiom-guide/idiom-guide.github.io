walk

walk · walked · walkedMotion & positionNo. 50 in the original

Core imageGoing on foot and walking with someone, extended to a suspect walking free and walking someone through a process.

Meanings and synonyms 4 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with walk; when you would rather not repeat walk, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

Interpreting tip

Walk out means a protest exit; walk somebody through something is livelier than explain in detail. In negotiation, the walk-away point is your limit.
