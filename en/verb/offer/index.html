offer

offer · offered · offeredGetting & givingNo. 79 in the original

Core imageHolding something out to someone: putting things or opportunities in front of them and waiting for acceptance.

Meanings and synonyms 2 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with offer; when you would rather not repeat offer, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 提供・提議・開價presentvt.
    provideproposeput forwardpresentpromisegive

    They offered me a job in Tokyo.

    他們提供我一份東京的工作。

  2. 提供（機會、設施）providevt.
    make availableaffordprovide

    The hotel offers free shuttle buses.

    飯店提供免費接駁車。

Idioms 6

  • offer itself（機會）出現
  • offer an olive branch遞出橄欖枝
  • offer one's condolences致哀
  • open to offers價錢好商量
  • on offer特價中；可供選擇
  • under offer已有買家出價

Interpreting tip

Offer to help; offer condolences; offer an olive branch, a phrase diplomatic interpreters hear often.
