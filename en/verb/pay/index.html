pay

pay · paid · paidGetting & givingNo. 11 in the original

Core imageGiving something up: money, a price, attention or respect. Turned around, something that "pays" is worthwhile.

Meanings and synonyms 4 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with pay; when you would rather not repeat pay, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 付錢・支付give moneyvt.
    spendgive money torecompenseremuneraterewardsettlecleardischargeearnbring inyield

    Can I pay by card?

    可以刷卡嗎？

  2. 付出（代價、注意）bearvt.
    bestowdevotesufferpay the priceatonesuffer the consequences

    Please pay attention to the safety instructions.

    請注意安全說明。

  3. 進行（拜訪、致意）makevt.
    makegive (a visita calla compliment)

    The minister paid a visit to the flood area.

    部長前往水災災區視察。

  4. 划得來・值得worthwhilevi.
    be profitablemake moneymake a returnbe worthwhilebe advantageous

    It pays to read the contract carefully.

    合約仔細讀，絕對值得。

Interpreting tip

Pay attention to is more common than focus on. Pay a visit and pay tribute are set phrases, and pay the price is the most direct way to say 付出代價.
