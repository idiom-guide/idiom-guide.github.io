serve

serve · served · servedDoing & changingNo. 81 in the original

Core imageWorking for others: serving, holding office, serving food, extended to serving a sentence and serving as something.

Meanings and synonyms 4 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with serve; when you would rather not repeat serve, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 服務・任職・效力work for
    perform duties forbe employed bybe of service towork formake a contribution to

    She served two terms as mayor.

    她擔任了兩屆市長。

  2. 服（刑、役）・完成（任期）perform
    completedocarry outfulfil

    He is serving a five-year sentence.

    他正在服五年徒刑。

  3. 上（菜）・供應present
    provideofferdish up

    Lunch will be served in the foyer.

    午餐將在大廳供應。

  4. 接待・招呼（顧客）attend to
    wait ondeal withattend to

    Are you being served?

    有人為您服務了嗎？

Idioms 6

  • serve as充當・作為
  • if my memory serves (me)如果我沒記錯
  • serve sb right活該
  • serve its purpose夠用・達到目的
  • serve two masters腳踏兩條船・兩面討好
  • revenge is a dish best served cold君子報仇，十年不晚

Interpreting tip

Serve as for holding a post: She served as chair of the committee. "Let this serve as a lesson." At a banquet: "Dinner is served."
