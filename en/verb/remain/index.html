remain

remain · remained · remainedMotion & positionNo. 90 in the original

Core imageStaying behind or staying the same: continuing in a state, staying in place, and being left over.

Meanings and synonyms 1 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with remain; when you would rather not repeat remain, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 保持・仍然・留下・剩下stayvi.
    staybecontinuekeeppersistlingersurvivebe left

    Please remain seated.

    請留在座位上。

Chinese idioms on this site

泰山崩於前而面不改色remain unruffled even if the sky falls

Interpreting tip

Remain is more formal than stay and common in notices and news: Prices remain high. "It remains to be seen whether…" is invaluable in current-affairs interpreting.
