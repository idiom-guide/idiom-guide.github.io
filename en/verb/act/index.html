act

act · acted · actedDoing & changingNo. 3 in the original

Core imageTwo strands: taking action and playing a part. To act is to do something, to behave like something, to perform on stage, or to function in a given role.

Meanings and synonyms 7 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with act; when you would rather not repeat act, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 裝出（某種樣子）pretendvt.
    masqueradepose as

    Don't act the innocent with me.

    別跟我裝無辜。

  2. 扮演（角色）playvt.
    impersonateinterpretperformplayportray
  3. 採取行動dovi.
    take actionintervene

    The government must act before prices rise further.

    政府必須在物價繼續上漲前採取行動。

  4. 表現得・舉止behavevi.
    behaveappearseemlooksoundcome across as

    She acted as if nothing had happened.

    她表現得像什麼事都沒發生。

  5. 假裝pretendvi.
    assumedissemblefakefeignpass forpretendput on
  6. 演戲playvi.
    perform
  7. 起作用・擔任have effectvi.
    functionoperateserveworktake effect

    The deputy acted as chair while she was away.

    她不在的時候，由副手代理主席。

Phrasal verbs 2

Interpreting tip

For 代理 or 暫代 (an interim post), think acting first: acting director, acting minister. There is no need to say temporary.
