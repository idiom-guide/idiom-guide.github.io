sell

sell · sold · soldGetting & givingNo. 41 in the original

Core imageTurning goods into money, extended to selling ideas and persuading; things that "sell" are popular.

Meanings and synonyms 2 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with sell; when you would rather not repeat sell, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 賣・出售；推銷（想法）tradevt.
    exchangebarterauctiondispose ofvendretailstockdeal inpromotemarketexport

    We'll have to sell the plan to the board first.

    我們得先說服董事會接受這個計畫。

  2. 賣得好・暢銷；售價為tradevi.
    be commercially successfulbe populargo down wellfetch

    The new model is selling well in Asia.

    新機型在亞洲賣得很好。

Phrasal verbs 2

  • sell off拋售・出清

    The group is selling off its overseas assets.

    集團正在出售海外資產。

    divestget rid ofdispose ofstrip
  • sell out賣光；出賣；背棄原則

    Tickets sold out within an hour.

    票一小時內就賣光了。

    be out of stockbetrayturn commercial

Idioms 5

  • sell oneself short低估自己
  • sell one's soul (to the devil)出賣靈魂
  • sell oneself推銷自己
  • sell like hot cakes熱賣・搶手
  • sell sb down the river出賣某人

Related glossary terms

Sell-off拋售（賣壓）Finance & Capital Markets

Interpreting tip

In pitches and negotiations, sell the idea, sell the plan. Sell like hot cakes means to be in demand; a sell-off is a wave of selling in markets.
