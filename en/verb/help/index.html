help

help · helped · helpedDoing & changingNo. 68 in the original

Core imageLending a hand: assisting and contributing, extended to "can't help it" and "help yourself".

Meanings and synonyms 4 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with help; when you would rather not repeat help, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 幫忙・協助assistvi.
    assistlend a handcooperatesupportcontribute to

    Volunteers helped with registration.

    志工協助報到作業。

  2. 有幫助・緩解make easier/bettervi.
    relieveeasefacilitatefurther

    Talking about it might help.

    說出來或許會好一點。

  3. 忍不住（can't help）resistvi.
    stop oneselfrestrain oneself

    I couldn't help laughing.

    我忍不住笑了。

  4. 幫助（某人）aidvt.
    aidassistsupport

    Could you help me with these boxes?

    可以幫我搬這些箱子嗎？

Chinese idioms on this site

Interpreting tip

"I can't help thinking…" means you cannot avoid the thought. "Please help yourself" invites guests to take food. Help can take a bare infinitive: help reduce costs.
