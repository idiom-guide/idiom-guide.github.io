play

play · played · playedDoing & changingNo. 38 in the original

Core imagePlaying, acting and performing: sports and games, roles, music, extended to having an effect and coming across a certain way.

Meanings and synonyms 6 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with play; when you would rather not repeat play, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 比賽・對打sportvt.
    faceplay againsttake onrepresentcompete against

    Taiwan plays Japan in the semi-final.

    臺灣在準決賽對上日本。

  2. 扮演（角色）actvt.
    performportrayact as

    Taiwan plays a key role in the chip supply chain.

    臺灣在晶片供應鏈中扮演關鍵角色。

  3. 演奏；播放musicvt.
    performbroadcastput on

    She plays the cello in a local orchestra.

    她在地方樂團拉大提琴。

  4. 玩・開玩笑amuse oneselfvi.
    have funenjoy oneselfjoke
  5. 給人（好壞）觀感be receivedvi.
    go downbe receivedcome across

    The speech played well with young voters.

    這場演說在年輕選民間反應不錯。

  6. （光影、笑容）浮現be seenvi.
    danceflickertwinklegleam

Interpreting tip

Play a role or play a part is the formal standard. Play it by ear means to improvise; play devil's advocate means arguing the other side to test an idea.
