fall

fall · fell · fallenMotion & positionNo. 22 in the original

Core imageGoing down: dropping and falling over, extended to declining, collapsing, being killed in battle, and falling on a date or a person.

Meanings and synonyms 5 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with fall; when you would rather not repeat fall, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 落下・垂下・降臨descendvi.
    dropcome downgo downdescenddroophang downarriveseizegrip

    Snow fell all night.

    雪下了一整晚。

  2. 跌倒・倒塌collapsevi.
    lose one's balancetriptumbleslipcollapse

    She slipped and fell on the ice.

    她在冰上滑倒了。

  3. 下降・下跌decreasevi.
    decreasego downdeclinedropweaken

    Exports fell by 5% last quarter.

    上一季出口下滑百分之五。

  4. 陣亡；（政權）倒台；（城市）失守losevi.
    be killeddielose powerbe defeatedbe capturedsurrendercapitulate

    The government fell after a vote of no confidence.

    不信任案通過後，政府垮台。

  5. 砍倒（樹木，作 fell）cutvt.
    cut downchop down

Interpreting tip

For figures, fall or drop is enough: Sales fell sharply. The fall-out from something means its aftermath, common in economic and political news.
