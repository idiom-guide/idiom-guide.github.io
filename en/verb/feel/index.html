feel

feel · felt · feltMind & sensesNo. 23 in the original

Core imageSensing with body and heart: touching, feeling and sensing, extended to holding an opinion.

Meanings and synonyms 5 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with feel; when you would rather not repeat feel, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 觸摸・摸索touchvt.
    fingertouchhandlerubcaressexploregrope

    Feel how soft this fabric is.

    摸摸看這塊布料有多軟。

  2. 感覺到・感受到experiencevt.
    senseperceiveexperienceundergosufferendurebe affected by

    You could feel the tension in the room.

    會場裡可以感受到緊張氣氛。

  3. 認為・覺得believevt.
    thinkbelieveholdreckon

    I feel we should wait for more data.

    我覺得我們應該等更多數據出來。

  4. 摸起來touchvi.
    seembe

    The water feels warm.

    水摸起來溫溫的。

  5. 感到（某種情緒）emotionvi.
    beseem

    I feel much better today.

    我今天好多了。

Interpreting tip

I feel… is a softer way to share a view than I think. "Please feel free to ask questions" is a staple of speeches and introductions.
