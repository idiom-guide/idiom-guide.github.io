go

go · went · goneMotion & position★ Start hereNo. 2 in the original

Core imageMoving away from where you are; extended to running, disappearing, reaching as far as, becoming (often worse) and being spent.

Meanings and synonyms 5 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with go; when you would rather not repeat go, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 去・前往・運轉movevi.
    travelproceedjourneyadvanceleavedepartwithdrawfunctionworkbe sold to

    We'll go by train and be back by six.

    我們搭火車去，六點前回來。

  2. 消失・衰退leavevi.
    deterioratebecome impaireddisappeardeclinevanish

    My appetite has completely gone.

    我完全沒胃口了。

  3. 延伸・通到extendvi.
    lead tospanunfoldspreadstretch

    The trail goes up to the summit.

    這條步道通往山頂。

  4. 變成（多半變差）becomevi.
    turnbecome

    The milk has gone sour.

    牛奶酸掉了。

  5. （錢、資源）花在spendvi.
    be spent onbe used for

    Half the grant went on equipment.

    補助款有一半花在設備上。

Interpreting tip

"How did it go?" and "It went well" sound far more natural than anything with proceed. Go + adjective usually means a change for the worse: go bad, go bankrupt, go wrong.
