lead

lead · led · ledMotion & positionNo. 29 in the original

Core imageWalking in front and showing the way: guiding, leading and being ahead, extended to leading to a result and leading a certain life.

Meanings and synonyms 7 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with lead; when you would rather not repeat lead, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 帶領・引導guidevt.
    conductescortshowsteerushertakedirect

    The host led us to the conference room.

    主人帶我們到會議室。

  2. 領導・率領・領先be firstvt.
    governrunheadbe in charge ofpreside overdirectspearheadchampion

    She led the delegation to Brussels.

    她率團前往布魯塞爾。

  3. 使（某人）認為・誘導convincevt.
    swayinfluencepersuademake

    The data led us to rethink our approach.

    這些數據讓我們重新思考做法。

  4. 過（某種生活）livevt.
    livehaveexperience

    They lead a simple life in the mountains.

    他們在山上過著簡樸的生活。

  5. 通往govi.
    gorunstretch

    This corridor leads to the main hall.

    這條走廊通往大廳。

  6. 導致causevi.
    result inproducecontribute tobring about

    Poor planning can lead to delays.

    規劃不周可能導致延誤。

  7. 領導・帶頭guidevi.
    commanddirectguide

Interpreting tip

Lead to is the most natural way to say "result in". Lead a delegation. In news, "The evening news led with the earthquake" means it was the top story.
