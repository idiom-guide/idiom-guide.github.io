break

break · broke · brokenDoing & changing★ Start hereNo. 53 in the original

Core imageBreaking apart: snapping, damaging and violating, extended to breaking a deadlock, taking a break, news breaking and weather turning.

Meanings and synonyms 10 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with break; when you would rather not repeat break, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 打破・折斷separatevt.
    smashsplitfracturetearsnap

    Someone broke the window.

    有人打破了窗戶。

  2. 弄斷（骨頭）injurevt.
    fracturehurt

    She broke her wrist skiing.

    她滑雪時摔斷了手腕。

  3. 弄壞not functionvt.
    damagecrash

    Who broke the printer?

    誰把印表機弄壞了？

  4. 違反・違背violatevt.
    infringebreachignoredefydisobey

    They broke the terms of the contract.

    他們違反了合約條款。

  5. 打破（僵局、沉默）endvt.
    interruptend

    The two sides failed to break the deadlock.

    雙方未能打破僵局。

  6. 透露（壞消息）informvt.
    announcerevealdisclosetell

    I'm sorry to be the one to break the news.

    很遺憾由我來告訴你這個消息。

  7. 破掉・碎go into piecesvi.
    fall to piecesshatter
  8. 休息・暫停restvi.
    stoppause

    Let's break for lunch.

    我們休息吃午飯吧。

  9. （天亮、暴風雨）開始；（天氣）轉變beginvi.
    beginappearchangeshift
  10. （新聞）傳開・爆出make knownvi.
    eruptemergebecome public

    The story broke late last night.

    這則新聞昨晚深夜爆出。
