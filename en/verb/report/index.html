report

report · reported · reportedSpeakingNo. 91 in the original

Core imageReporting back: reporting, covering news and notifying, and also reporting for duty and reporting to a superior.

Meanings and synonyms 3 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with report; when you would rather not repeat report, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 報到present oneself
    check inturn up

    Please report to the front desk on arrival.

    抵達時請到櫃檯報到。

  2. 向…負責・隸屬answer to
    be accountable toanswer to

    The task force reports directly to the premier.

    該工作小組直接向行政院長負責。

  3. 報告・報導・通報relatevt.
    declaretellinformdescribediscloserecordnote

    The incident was reported to the police.

    這起事件已向警方報案。

Idioms 2

  • be reported to據報導
  • report back回報結果

Interpreting tip

Reportedly or It is reported that… mark hearsay in news. Your line manager is "the person you report to".
