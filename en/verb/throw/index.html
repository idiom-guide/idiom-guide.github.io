throw

throw · threw · thrownMotion & positionNo. 62 in the original

Core imageSending something through the air: throwing and tossing, extended to casting light or glances, confusing people, throwing parties and throwing tantrums.

Meanings and synonyms 2 meanings

Interpreting tip

"It threw me" means it caught me off guard. Throw a party is more relaxed than hold a party. Throw out an idea means to float it.
