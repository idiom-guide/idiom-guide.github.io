turn

turn · turned · turnedMotion & position★ Start hereNo. 48 in the original

Core imageRotating: turning around, changing direction, flipping over, extended to changing, becoming, and reaching an age.

Meanings and synonyms 5 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with turn; when you would rather not repeat turn, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 轉動・旋轉revolvevi.
    rotatego roundspinwhirltwirlswivel

    The wheels began to turn.

    輪子開始轉動。

  2. 變成・（年紀）滿becomevi.
    changedevelopgrowbecome

    She turns forty next month.

    她下個月滿四十歲。

  3. 轉身・轉向change directionvi.
    turn roundveerswerve

    Turn left at the second light.

    第二個紅綠燈左轉。

  4. （牛奶）變酸sourvi.
    sourspoilcurdlego off
  5. 翻（頁）・轉動（某物）movevt.
    rotaterevolveflipchange the position of

    Please turn to page twelve.

    請翻到第十二頁。

Interpreting tip

"It turned out (that)…" is invaluable in interpreting. Turnout is voter participation; turnover is British for revenue. A turning point is a decisive moment.
