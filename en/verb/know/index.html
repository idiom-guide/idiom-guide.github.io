know

know · knew · knownMind & sensesNo. 69 in the original

Core imageKnowing and being acquainted: one verb covers both knowing facts (savoir) and knowing people (connaître), which Chinese separates.

Meanings and synonyms 2 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with know; when you would rather not repeat know, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 知道・懂・經歷過understand
    comprehendperceivebe awarebe conscious ofexperience

    I know what you mean.

    我懂你的意思。

  2. 認識・認得be acquainted with
    be friends withrecognisebe familiar with

    Do you know anyone at the embassy?

    你在大使館有認識的人嗎？

Idioms 3

  • know thine enemy知己知彼
  • I know what我有個主意
  • be in the know知情

Chinese idioms on this site

適可而止know when to stop有兩把刷子knows how to do it名聞遐邇known far and wide瞭若指掌know sth. inside out深諳此道know how to play the game知所進退Know when to make a move and when to stay put

Interpreting tip

Know a fact (知道), know a person (認識), know a language (會). As far as I know is the standard hedge.
