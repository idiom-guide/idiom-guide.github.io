tell

tell · told · toldSpeakingNo. 47 in the original

Core imagePassing information on: informing, recounting, instructing, extended to telling things apart and to stress starting to show.

Meanings and synonyms 7 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with tell; when you would rather not repeat tell, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 告訴・通知informvt.
    notifylet knowinformreportcommunicate

    Could you tell me where the press room is?

    請問新聞室在哪裡？

  2. 講述narratevt.
    recountrelatedescribenarrate

    Tell us a bit about your background.

    跟我們談談你的背景。

  3. 吩咐・叫（某人做）ordervt.
    commandinstructdirectorder

    The guard told us to wait outside.

    警衛叫我們在外面等。

  4. 分辨・看得出discernvt.
    distinguishrecogniseidentifyperceivedifferentiate

    I could tell she was nervous.

    我看得出她很緊張。

  5. 告誡・提醒warnvt.
    warnadvise against

    I told you it wouldn't work.

    我早就說過行不通。

  6. （壓力、疲勞）顯現affectvi.
    take its tollmake itself feltshow

    The long hours are starting to tell.

    長時間工作開始讓人吃不消。

  7. 告密・打小報告inform onvi.
    reportdenouncesnitchblow the whistle on

Interpreting tip

"I can tell" and "I can't tell the difference" are more natural than distinguish. Tell takes a person (tell me); say takes the content (say that…).
