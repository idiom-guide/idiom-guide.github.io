come

come · came · comeMotion & position★ Start hereNo. 21 in the original

Core imageMoving toward the speaker or a goal: coming, arriving, reaching; extended to coming from, turning out, becoming and adding up to.

Meanings and synonyms 6 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with come; when you would rather not repeat come, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 來・靠近・進展advance
    move towardsapproachmove nearernear

    Come and sit with us.

    過來跟我們一起坐。

  2. 達到・達成reach
    attainarrive atgainsecureachieve

    The two sides finally came to an agreement.

    雙方終於達成協議。

  3. 產生（結果）result
    occurhappenresulttranspire

    Nothing good can come of rushing.

    操之過急不會有好結果。

  4. 來自・出身originate
    hail frombe a native ofbe derived from

    Where do you come from?

    你是哪裡人？

  5. 變成（某種狀態）become
    becomegetturn

    The knot has come undone.

    結鬆開了。

  6. 總計add up
    amount tototal

    The bill came to NT$3,400.

    帳單總共新臺幣三千四百元。

Interpreting tip

"I'm coming", not "I'm going": English takes the other person's position as the reference point. Come to an agreement or come to a consensus sounds less formal than reach.
