appear

appear · appeared · appearedMind & sensesNo. 83 in the original

Core imageComing into view: turning up and appearing, extended to seeming, appearing in the media, being published and appearing in court.

Meanings and synonyms 5 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with appear; when you would rather not repeat appear, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 看起來・似乎seem
    seemlookstrike one asgive the impression of

    The minister appeared relaxed.

    部長看起來很輕鬆。

  2. 出現・現身arrive
    come into viewturn upshow upemerge

    A message appeared on the screen.

    螢幕上出現一則訊息。

  3. 登上（媒體）・出現在feature
    featurearisecome upoccur

    She often appears on TV talk shows.

    她常上電視談話節目。

  4. 出版・刊出be published
    come outbe released

    The article will appear in next month's issue.

    這篇文章將刊在下個月號。

  5. 出庭be present in court
    attend court

    The suspect will appear in court tomorrow.

    嫌犯明天將出庭。

Idioms 3

  • to all appearances就表面看來
  • give every appearance of看來十足
  • keep up appearances撐門面

Interpreting tip

It appears that… is more formal than It seems that…, suited to news and official texts. Appear in court; make an appearance.
