call

call · called · calledSpeakingNo. 20 in the original

Core imageRaising your voice: calling out, naming, phoning; extended to calling a meeting or election, making a ruling and dropping in on someone.

Meanings and synonyms 10 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with call; when you would rather not repeat call, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

    Someone called my name from across the room.

    有人在房間另一頭叫我的名字。

  2. 稱為・認為是describevt.

Interpreting tip

A chair opens a meeting with "I'd like to call the meeting to order." Call on and call for are the everyday verbs for 呼籲. "That's a tough call" means a hard decision.
