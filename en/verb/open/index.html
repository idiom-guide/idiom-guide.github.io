open

open · opened · openedDoing & changingNo. 36 in the original

Core imageOpening doors and packages, unfolding; extended to opening an inquiry or dialogue, opening a business and a film opening.

Meanings and synonyms 5 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with open; when you would rather not repeat open, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 打開・拆開unfastenvt.
    unsealundounlockuntieunwrap

    Please open the attachment.

    請打開附件。

  2. 展開・撐開unfoldvt.
    unrollunfurluncork
  3. 開啟・展開（調查、談判）beginvt.
    startcommenceinitiatelaunch

    The police have opened an investigation.

    警方已展開調查。

  4. 開門營業start businessvi.
    open for business

    The museum opens at nine.

    博物館九點開館。

  5. 上映・開幕be releasedvi.
    premieredebutopen

    The film opens in Taipei on Friday.

    電影週五在臺北上映。
