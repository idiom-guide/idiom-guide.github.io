live

live · lived · livedDoing & changingNo. 30 in the original

Core imageBeing alive and spending your days: surviving, residing, living a certain way, living on something, living life to the full.

Meanings and synonyms 6 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with live; when you would rather not repeat live, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 活著・存活existvi.
    be alivesurvivewalk the earth

    She lived to 102.

    她活到一百零二歲。

  2. 住・居住residevi.
    residedwellinhabithave one's home in

    Where do you live?

    你住哪裡？

  3. 過（某種生活）spend one's lifevi.
    have a lifestyle

    They live quite simply.

    他們過得相當簡樸。

  4. 維生・過活survivevi.
    make a livingearn one's livingsupport oneselfmake ends meet

    It's hard to live on a part-time salary.

    靠兼職薪水很難過活。

  5. 盡情享受人生enjoy oneselfvi.
    enjoy lifehave funlive life to the full
  6. 經歷・過著experiencevt.
    leadhavego throughundergo

    He lived a long and happy life.

    他度過了長壽而快樂的一生。

Interpreting tip

"I can live with that" is a relaxed, flexible way to accept terms. Live up to expectations means to deliver what people hoped for.
