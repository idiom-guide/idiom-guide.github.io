kill

kill · killed · killedDoing & changingNo. 28 in the original

Core imageEnding something's existence: killing and being fatal, extended to killing plans, killing time, and hyperbole about pain, laughter and longing.

Meanings and synonyms 6 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with kill; when you would rather not repeat kill, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 殺死・致死take lifevt.
    cause deathmurderassassinateslay

    Two workers were killed in the explosion.

    爆炸造成兩名工人死亡。

  2. 扼殺・終止・關掉stopvt.
    quashsilenceenddestroyruinscupperswitch off

    The budget cuts killed the project.

    預算刪減讓這項計畫胎死腹中。

  3. 讓人痛苦・要人命cause painvt.
    hurttormentmake suffer

    My back is killing me.

    我的背痛死了。

  4. 讓人笑死amusevt.
    make laughamusecrack up

    Your stories kill me.

    你講的故事笑死我了。

  5. 渴望（could kill for）wantvt.
    desirecravelong foryearn for

    I'd kill for a cold drink right now.

    我現在好想來杯冰飲。

  6. 致命cause deathvi.
    be fatal

    Speed kills.

    超速會要人命。

Interpreting tip

Kill time; kill two birds with one stone. Kill is common in casual hyperbole, so "My feet are killing me" should never be rendered literally.
