send

send · sent · sentMotion & positionNo. 42 in the original

Core imageSending things off: mailing and dispatching, extended to putting people into a state and sending things flying or figures soaring.

Meanings and synonyms 3 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with send; when you would rather not repeat send, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 寄・送・派dispatchvt.
    mailpostforwarddelivertransmittransportdispatch

    I'll send you the slides after the meeting.

    會後我把投影片寄給你。

  2. 使（某人）變得makevt.
    driverendermake

    The noise is sending me crazy.

    噪音快把我逼瘋了。

  3. 使飛出・使（價格）急變cause to movevt.
    knockflinghurlpropeldrivepush

    The news sent oil prices soaring.

    這則消息讓油價飆漲。

Phrasal verbs 5

  • send for派人去請・叫來

    Should we send for a doctor?

    要不要請醫生來？

    summoncall forcontact
  • send in派遣；提交

    Please send in your application by Friday.

    請在週五前提交申請。

    dispatchsubmitput forward
  • send off罰下場；寄出
    ejectdismissmail
  • send out發出；發送；趕出去
    emitgive outdistributeissue
  • send up模仿嘲弄
    make fun ofimitateparodytease

Idioms 5

  • send sb packing把人打發走
  • send sb off with a flea in their ear嚴詞拒絕
  • send word捎個信
  • send sb to Coventry排擠・不理睬
  • send shivers down one's spine讓人不寒而慄

Interpreting tip

Send a signal or send a message sounds more natural than convey: The decision sends a clear message to investors.
