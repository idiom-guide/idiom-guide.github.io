want

want · wanted · wantedGetting & givingNo. 51 in the original

Core imageDesiring and wishing someone would do something. The old sense is "to lack", which is why a fugitive is wanted and something can be found wanting.

Meanings and synonyms 3 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with want; when you would rather not repeat want, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 想要・希望desirevt.
    wishlikecravecovetfancyhope forlong foryearn for

    I want everyone to read this before Friday.

    我要大家在週五前讀完這份資料。

  2. 通緝・找（某人）pursuevt.
    seekhunt forlook for

    You're wanted on the phone.

    有你的電話。

  3. 缺少・欠缺lackvt.
    lackbe deficient ingo shortfall short

    The plan was found wanting in several areas.

    這項計畫在好幾方面都有不足。

Phrasal verbs 3

  • want for缺乏
    lackgo short of
  • want in想加入
    wish to join
  • want out想退出
    wish to leave

Idioms 2

  • for want of a better word姑且這麼說
  • waste not, want not儉以防匱

Interpreting tip

In formal settings, I'd like… is more polite than I want, so interpreters should mind the speaker's status. Be found wanting is a formal way to say "judged inadequate".
