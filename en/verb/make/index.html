make

make · made · madeDoing & changing★ Start hereNo. 33 in the original

Core imageGiving things shape: producing, causing results, making people act. Even arriving in time and earning money are things you "make".

Meanings and synonyms 6 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with make; when you would rather not repeat make, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 使得・造成cause
    effectgenerateengendercreateoccasiongive rise tobring about

    The delay made everyone nervous.

    延誤讓大家都緊張起來。

  2. 做出・製造・制定・構成produce
    manufactureassembleconstructcomposefashionforgecreateissueconstitute

    We've made a shortlist of three candidates.

    我們擬出三人的候選名單。

  3. 準備（飯菜、床鋪）；會成為prepare
    preparecookdevelop intobecome

    You'd make an excellent moderator.

    你會是很出色的主持人。

  4. 趕上・到得了arrive
    arrivecatchreach in time

    If we leave now, we can still make the last train.

    現在出發還趕得上末班車。

  5. 賺・獲利・交（朋友）get
    earngainhave income ofbe profitableturn a profit

    The startup hasn't made a profit yet.

    這家新創公司還沒獲利。

  6. 迫使force
    coerceobligecompelpressureforce

    Nobody can make you sign it.

    沒有人能逼你簽字。

Interpreting tip

Make it covers arriving, attending and succeeding: "Can you make it on Friday?", "She made it to the finals." When the idea is getting there or pulling it off, try make it first.
