continue

continue · continued · continuedDoing & changingNo. 98 in the original

Core imageGoing on, or picking up again after a pause.

Meanings and synonyms 4 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with continue; when you would rather not repeat continue, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 持續・延續carry onvi.
    lastpersistkeep ongo onproceed

    Prices are expected to continue rising.

    物價預計將持續上漲。

  2. （中斷後）繼續resumevi.
    resumerestartproceedcarry on

    After the break, the speaker continued.

    休息過後，講者繼續發言。

  3. 延續（傳統、學業）keepvt.
    keep upmaintainpreservepursue

    She hopes to continue her studies abroad.

    她希望出國繼續深造。

  4. 接著（討論）begin againvt.
    resumepick uprenewcarry on

    Let's continue this discussion tomorrow.

    我們明天再接著討論。

Interpreting tip

In speech, go on and carry on replace continue: "Please go on." Continue to grow; to be continued.
