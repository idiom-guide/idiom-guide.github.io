change

change · changed · changedDoing & changingNo. 85 in the original

Core imageSwapping and altering: changing, replacing and exchanging, and also changing trains, clothes and money.

Meanings and synonyms 7 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with change; when you would rather not repeat change, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 改變・更改replacevt.
    altermodifytransformvaryreformrestructurereorganise

    It's time to change our approach.

    是時候改變做法了。

  2. 交換・更換exchangevt.
    swapswitchtradeinterchange

    Can I change seats with you?

    我可以跟你換位子嗎？

  3. 轉乘・換（車、機）transfervt.
    transferswitch

    We changed planes in Bangkok.

    我們在曼谷轉機。

  4. 換（尿布、床單）clothingvt.
    changeput fresh…on
  5. 變化・改變become differentvi.
    altervaryturnevolvedevelopadaptadjustfluctuate

    The city has changed a lot.

    這座城市變了很多。

  6. 轉車transfervi.
    transferswitch

    Change at Taipei Main Station for the airport line.

    在臺北車站轉搭機場線。

  7. 換衣服dressvi.
    get changed

    I need ten minutes to change.

    我需要十分鐘換衣服。

Interpreting tip

Change one's mind, change hands, change tack. Change is also a noun for coins: "Keep the change."
