grow

grow · grew · grownDoing & changingNo. 67 in the original

Core imageGetting bigger: growing up and growing, extended to gradually becoming, growing crops, and growing a business or economy.

Meanings and synonyms 4 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with grow; when you would rather not repeat grow, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 增長・擴大increasevi.
    expandincreaseintensifygatherdevelopemergestrengthen

    Demand for interpreters continues to grow.

    口譯需求持續成長。

  2. 生長・長大maturevi.
    developmature

    The children have grown so much!

    孩子們長得好快！

  3. 逐漸變得becomevi.
    becomeget

    It's growing dark.

    天色漸暗。

  4. 種植；留（鬍子）；發展（經濟）cause to growvt.
    plantcultivateraisedevelopexpand

    The government wants to grow the digital economy.

    政府希望發展數位經濟。

Interpreting tip

The economy grew by 3%. Grow can be transitive: grow the business. "It grows on you" means you come to like it.
