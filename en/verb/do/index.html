do

do · did · doneDoing & changingNo. 1 in the original

Core imageThe most general verb of action: if you can name the task, you can usually "do" it. It also judges whether something is good enough, or how well someone is faring.

Meanings and synonyms 5 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with do; when you would rather not repeat do, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

    Let me know if there's anything I can do.

  2. 帶來（好處或傷害）bring aboutvt.

  5. 表現（好壞）farevi.
    succeedexcelsurpass oneselffailflunkunderperformdisappoint

Interpreting tip

Chinese 做 does not always become do: 做決定 is make a decision, 做研究 is do research, and 做飯 is usually cook or make dinner. Do tends to go with work, homework and chores.
