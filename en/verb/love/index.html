love

love · loved · lovedMind & sensesNo. 71 in the original

Core imageLoving someone, and more broadly being very fond of something; I'd love to is an enthusiastic yes.

Meanings and synonyms 2 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with love; when you would rather not repeat love, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 愛・深愛adorevt.
    cherishhold deardote onbe devoted tothink the world ofadore

    She loves her job.

    她熱愛她的工作。

  2. 非常喜歡・很想enjoyvt.
    likefancyappreciaterelishtake pleasure indelight in

    I'd love to come, thanks.

    我很樂意去，謝謝。

Interpreting tip

Answering an invitation with "I'd love to" is much warmer than "Yes, I will." "The market has fallen out of love with tech stocks" means investors have lost interest.
