think

think · thought · thoughtMind & sensesNo. 61 in the original

Core imageTurning things over in your mind: thinking, believing, imagining, expecting. The book lists only one broad meaning, but the uses are almost endless.

Interpreting tip

Vary your verbs of opinion: I think, I believe, I'd say, it seems to me. "Let me think it over" is more conversational than consider.
