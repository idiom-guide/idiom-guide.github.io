show

show · showed · shownSpeakingNo. 43 in the original

Core imageLetting people see: displaying, presenting, pointing out, extended to indicating, proving, guiding, being visible and turning up.

Meanings and synonyms 8 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with show; when you would rather not repeat show, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 出示・展示・表現出displayvt.
    producepresentexhibitdisplaymanifestdemonstrate

    Please show your passport at the gate.

    請在登機門出示護照。

  2. 顯露（髒污）revealvt.
    revealexpose
  3. 顯示・指出indicatevt.
    readpoint outindicate

    The map shows where the exits are.

    地圖上標示出口的位置。

  4. 帶（某人）去・領路leadvt.
    conductaccompanyescortguide

    Let me show you to your seat.

    我帶您到座位。

  5. 描繪・呈現depictvt.
    portraypicturedepict

    The documentary shows life in a fishing village.

    這部紀錄片呈現漁村生活。

  6. 證明；示範demonstratevt.
    provebe evidence forsuggestteachinstructillustrate

    The figures show that demand is recovering.

    數字顯示需求正在回升。

  7. 看得出來・顯露be visiblevi.
    be visiblebe noticeableappear

    Try not to let your nerves show.

    盡量別讓人看出你很緊張。

  8. 露面・出現appearvi.
    show upcometurn up

Interpreting tip

"The data show…" is plainer than indicate. Show up means to turn up. Guiding a guest: "Let me show you the way."
