move

move · moved · movedMotion & positionNo. 35 in the original

Core imageChanging position: moving and relocating, extended to rescheduling, making progress, prompting action, touching emotions and moving a motion.

Meanings and synonyms 11 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with move; when you would rather not repeat move, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 移動・動changevi.
    shiftstirbudgeadvanceproceedgo

    Don't move — there's a bee on your arm.

    別動，你手臂上有隻蜜蜂。

  2. 改期change timevi.
    reschedulerearrangebring forwardpostpone

    The meeting has moved to Thursday.

    會議改到星期四。

  3. （價格、數字）變動change degreevi.
    risefallincreasedecrease
  4. （看法、趨勢）轉向change opinionvi.
    shifttend towardturn away from
  5. 進展progressvi.
    proceedadvanceprogress

    Negotiations are moving quickly.

    談判進展快速。

  6. 搬家・遷移relocatevi.
    relocate

    We're moving to Taichung next month.

    我們下個月搬到臺中。

  7. 搬動・移走changevt.
    transportcarrytransfershift

    Could you move your car, please?

    可以請你挪一下車嗎？

  8. 促使causevt.
    promptdriveinduceimpellead
  9. 使改變主意change opinionvt.
    swayshiftchange someone's mind
  10. 感動feelingvt.
    touchaffectimpressstir

    We were all deeply moved by her speech.

    她的演講讓我們都深受感動。

  11. 提出（動議）suggestvt.
    proposeput forward

    I move that the meeting be adjourned.

    我提議散會。

Chinese idioms on this site

攜手並進move forward hand in hand

Interpreting tip

A chair says "Let's move on to…"; in formal procedure, "I move that…" proposes a motion. Be moved is a common way to say "be touched".
