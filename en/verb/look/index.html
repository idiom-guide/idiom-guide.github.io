look

look · looked · lookedMind & senses★ Start hereNo. 31 in the original

Core imageDirecting your eyes: looking and searching, extended to appearing a certain way. With particles it yields looking after, looking into, looking up to and more.

Meanings and synonyms 3 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with look; when you would rather not repeat look, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 看・注視use sight
    observeviewsurveyregardgazestareexamineinspectcheckscrutinizescanglance

    Look at this chart for a moment.

    請先看一下這張圖表。

  2. 找・搜尋search
    seeksearchscourcomb

    We looked everywhere for the keys.

    我們到處找鑰匙。

  3. 看起來・顯得appear
    appearseem

    You look tired — are you OK?

    你看起來很累，還好嗎？

Interpreting tip

Look + adjective (look tired) or look like + noun (look like rain). "We'll look into it" is the classic official reply, gentler than investigate.
