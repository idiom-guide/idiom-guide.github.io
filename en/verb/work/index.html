work

work · worked · workedDoing & changingNo. 52 in the original

Core imagePutting in effort to make things run: working, operating, machines working, methods working, and gradually shaping material.

Interpreting tip

"It works" and "It won't work" are plainer than effective or ineffective. Work it out means to find a solution; work out what happened means to figure it out.
