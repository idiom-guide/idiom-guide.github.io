decide

decide · decided · decidedMind & sensesNo. 87 in the original

Core imageSettling a question: making a choice, extended to determining an outcome and ruling on a dispute.

Meanings and synonyms 3 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with decide; when you would rather not repeat decide, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 決定・下定決心choosevi.
    make up one's minddetermineresolvechoose

    We decided to postpone the launch.

    我們決定延後上市。

  2. 左右・決定（結果）influencevt.
    determinesettleaffectconvince

    A single goal decided the match.

    一球定勝負。

  3. 裁決make an official decisionvt.
    settleresolvearbitratejudgerule

    The court will decide the case next month.

    法院將於下月裁決此案。

Idioms 1

  • decide against決定不（做）；判決不利於

Interpreting tip

Decide to do is leaner than make a decision. The deciding factor is the decisive one.
