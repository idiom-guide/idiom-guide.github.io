understand

understand · understood · understoodMind & sensesNo. 100 in the original

Core imageGrasping meaning: understanding, extended to sympathising with someone and to "I'm told that…".

Meanings and synonyms 3 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with understand; when you would rather not repeat understand, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 懂・理解comprehend
    comprehendgetgraspfollowseerealizeappreciate

    Do you understand the question?

    你懂這個問題嗎？

  2. 體諒・理解sympathize with
    sympathize withempathize withappreciatebe aware of

    I understand your concerns.

    我理解你的顧慮。

  3. 據了解・聽說believe
    believegatherheartake it

    I understand you're moving to Tokyo.

    聽說你要搬到東京。

Idioms 4

  • do you understand?聽清楚了嗎？（強調）
  • it is understood that大家心照不宣・據了解
  • make oneself understood讓別人聽懂自己的意思
  • be given to understand據告知

Interpreting tip

In negotiations, "I understand your position" signals understanding, not agreement, and should be interpreted precisely. "I understand that…" or "We understand that…" introduce information you have heard.
