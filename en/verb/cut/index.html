cut

cut · cut · cutDoing & changingNo. 7 in the original

Core imageSlicing with a blade; extended to reducing, deleting, laying off, interrupting and recording an album.

Meanings and synonyms 6 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with cut; when you would rather not repeat cut, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 切・割・剪sever/slicevt.
    slicechopcarvedicetrimsnipclipcropslitslashhackhewwoundgraze

    Cut the cake into eight pieces.

    把蛋糕切成八塊。

  2. 削減・降低reducevt.
    curtailbring downtrimslashlowershorten

    The company plans to cut costs by ten percent.

    公司打算削減一成成本。

  3. 刪除・裁撤removevt.
    exciseremovedeletepruneloplay offmake redundantsackdismiss

    The editor cut two paragraphs from my article.

    編輯刪掉我文章裡的兩段。

  4. 停止（拍攝、引擎）stopvt.
    stopswitch off
  5. 錄製（唱片）recordvt.
    record
  6. 鋒利・好切be sharpvi.
    be sharp

Interpreting tip

For budget cuts, cut is stronger than reduce: budget cuts, cut spending. To get to the point quickly: "Let me cut to the chase."
