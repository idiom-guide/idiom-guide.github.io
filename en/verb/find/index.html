find

find · found · foundGetting & givingNo. 24 in the original

Core imageLocating something or a solution, extended to finding something to be a certain way and to a court finding a verdict.

Meanings and synonyms 6 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with find; when you would rather not repeat find, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 找到・發現discovervt.
    locatetrack downtracespotunearthuncovercome acrossstumble acrossdetect

    I can't find my badge anywhere.

    我到處都找不到我的識別證。

  2. 找到・設法取得obtainvt.
    attainachievewingainearnacquiresecureget

    We need to find funding for the second phase.

    我們得替第二期找到資金。

  3. 覺得thinkvt.
    considerjudgeratebelieve

    I find his argument convincing.

    我覺得他的論點很有說服力。

  4. 感到・發覺（自己）experiencevt.
    feelperceiverealiseend up

    She found herself alone in a foreign city.

    她發覺自己孤身一人在異鄉。

  5. 存在・可見於bevt.
    existoccur

    This species is found only in Taiwan.

    這個物種只見於臺灣。

  6. 判定（有罪、無罪）judgevt.
    pronounce a verdictruledecree

    The jury found him not guilty.

    陪審團判他無罪。

Phrasal verbs 1

  • find out查出・得知；揭穿

    Let me find out what time the session starts.

    我去查一下場次幾點開始。

Interpreting tip

Find + object + adjective is very idiomatic: I find it hard to believe. Find out is far more conversational than ascertain.
