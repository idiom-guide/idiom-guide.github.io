hold

hold · held · heldGetting & giving★ Start hereNo. 26 in the original

Core imageKeeping in your grip: holding, gripping and detaining, extended to maintaining, containing, owning, holding events, and an argument holding up.

Interpreting tip

Hold a meeting, hold talks, hold elections are more common than conduct or organise. "The argument holds" means it stands up. On the phone: "Please hold."
