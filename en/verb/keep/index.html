keep

keep · kept · keptGetting & giving★ Start hereNo. 27 in the original

Core imageKeeping things where and as they are: retaining, maintaining, observing rules. Even "doing something over and over" is keeping an action going.

Meanings and synonyms 8 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with keep; when you would rather not repeat keep, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 留著・保存・存放retainvt.
    holdpreservehang on tosavestoreconservepossesskeep secret

    You can keep this copy; I have another one.

    這份你留著，我還有一份。

  2. 保持（某種狀態）maintainvt.
    maintain

    Please keep your answers brief.

    請簡短作答。

  3. 養（家、動物）supportvt.
    raiseprovide forsupportlook afterfeedtend

    They keep chickens in the backyard.

    他們在後院養雞。

  4. 耽擱・阻止・隱瞞detainvt.
    stoppreventimpedeobstructdeterdelayconfinewithhold

    Sorry to keep you waiting.

    抱歉讓你久等了。

  5. 遵守・信守observevt.
    comply withrespectobeyadhere tofulfilcarry outhonour

    He always keeps his promises.

    他一向說到做到。

  6. 記錄（日記、帳）writevt.
    compilerecordlog

    We keep a record of every request.

    每一筆申請我們都有記錄。

  7. 一直・持續continuevi.
    continuepersistpersevere

    The phone kept ringing throughout the meeting.

    開會時電話一直響個不停。

  8. （食物）保鮮stay freshvi.
    be preservedlast

    Cooked rice won't keep for long.

    煮好的飯放不久。

  • keep at (it)堅持下去

    Fluency comes slowly, so keep at it.

    avoidstay away
  • keep off避開；戒掉

    I'm trying to keep off sugar this month.

    Please keep to the five-minute time limit.

    請遵守五分鐘的時間限制。

    講者語速快的時候，很難跟上。

Interpreting tip

He kept interrupting is more idiomatic than anything with continuously. Keep + -ing can carry a note of complaint, worth preserving in translation.
