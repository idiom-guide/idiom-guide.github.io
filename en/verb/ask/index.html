ask

ask · asked · askedSpeakingNo. 5 in the original

Core imageOpening your mouth to someone: asking questions, making requests, inviting, and even naming a price.

Meanings and synonyms 4 meanings

Interpreting tip

Chinese 請問 is not "Please ask": say "May I ask…?" or "Could you tell me…?". Demand is forceful; for ordinary requests, ask is enough.
