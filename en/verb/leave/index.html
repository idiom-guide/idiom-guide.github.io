leave

leave · left · leftMotion & positionNo. 56 in the original

Core imageGoing away and leaving things behind: departing, extended to leaving things in place, leaving decisions to others, and bequeathing.

Meanings and synonyms 8 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with leave; when you would rather not repeat leave, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 離開・出發departvt.
    departexitget offdisembarkstep down

    The flight leaves Taoyuan at 8:40.

    班機八點四十分從桃園起飛。

  2. 離開（學校、伴侶）・拋下abandonvt.
    finishquitdesertabandon

    He left school at sixteen.

    他十六歲就離開學校了。

  3. 留到以後・擱著delayvt.
    postponedelayput off

    Let's leave the details until next week.

    細節我們下週再談。

  4. 讓…保持・剩下remainvt.
    keeplet remain

    Please leave the door open.

    請讓門開著。

  5. 給（某人）留下（印象、選擇）givevt.
    providecauseproduce

    The deadline leaves us little choice.

    期限逼得我們幾乎別無選擇。

  6. 遺贈；忘了帶losevt.
    bequeathhand downforgetmislay

    I left my umbrella in the taxi.

    我把傘忘在計程車上。

  7. 交給（某人）處理entrustvt.
    entrustassignconsignlet decide

    Leave it with me — I'll sort it out.

    交給我吧，我來處理。

  8. 離開・辭職go awayvi.
    departset offresignquit

    She left before the end of the meeting.

    她會議還沒結束就走了。

Interpreting tip

"Leave it to me" or "Leave it with me". Leave a lot to be desired is a polite, formal criticism. On leave; parental leave.
