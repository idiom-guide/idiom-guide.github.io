get

get · got · got（美式 gotten）Getting & giving★ Start hereNo. 9 in the original

Core imageGoing from not having to having: receiving things, reaching places, entering a state. Even understanding is "getting" the meaning.

Meanings and synonyms 14 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with get; when you would rather not repeat get, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 得到・收到・領取receivevt.

  2. 擁有・患有（have got）havevt.

  3. 去拿・去叫・去買fetchvt.

  7. 使・讓（某人或某物）causevt.
    causemakehave

    Can you get the projector working?

  8. 惹惱・讓人受不了annoyvt.

  9. 開始（get going）startvt.

  10. 必須（have got to）mustvt.
    be obliged tohave to

    我中午前得把這個做完。

  11. 變得becomevi.
    becomegrow

    The discussion was getting heated.

  12. 有機會・得以（get to do）managevi.
    manage tohave the opportunity to

    I never got to thank her in person.

    我一直沒機會當面謝謝她。

  13. 遭遇（get + 過去分詞）passivevi.
    be + past participle

    He got stuck in traffic for an hour.

    他塞車塞了一小時。

  14. 陷入（某種處境）processvi.
    fall intobecome embroiled in

    They got into debt within a year.

    他們不到一年就負債了。

Interpreting tip

Where a Chinese speaker reaches for obtain or acquire (取得、獲得), get is more natural in speech; save the formal verbs for writing. Get + past participle describes something that happens to you: got stuck, got promoted. It is more conversational than be + past participle.
