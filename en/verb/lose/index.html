lose

lose · lost · lostGetting & givingNo. 32 in the original

Core imageLosing something: misplacing things, losing games, losing rights, wasting time, and leaving a listener confused.

Meanings and synonyms 6 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with lose; when you would rather not repeat lose, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 弄丟・跟丟mislayvt.
    misplacemislay

    I've lost my boarding pass.

    我把登機證弄丟了。

  2. 輸掉failvt.
    be defeatedbe beatensuffer defeatbe unsuccessful

    They lost the final on penalties.

    他們在決賽踢點球輸了。

  3. 失去・喪失be deprived ofvt.

Interpreting tip

When you can't follow a speaker, "I'm afraid you've lost me" is polite. Missing a chance is miss; losing an advantage is lose ground.
