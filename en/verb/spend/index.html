spend

spend · spent · spentGetting & givingNo. 82 in the original

Core imageUsing up: spending money and time, extended to exhausting energy or anger.

Meanings and synonyms 3 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with spend; when you would rather not repeat spend, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 花（錢）pay outvt.
    expendlay outsplash outdisburse

    We spent too much on the venue.

    我們在場地上花太多錢了。

  2. 度過・花（時間、心力）passvt.
    passoccupydevoteinvestput in

    I spent the weekend preparing the glossary.

    我花了整個週末準備詞彙表。

  3. 耗盡exhaustvt.
    use updepleteconsumedrainexhaust

Idioms 4

  • spend a penny上廁所（委婉語）
  • spend a packet花一大筆錢
  • don't spend it all in one place別一次花光（玩笑話）
  • (advertising) spend開支・支出（名詞）

Chinese idioms on this site

入不敷出spend more than one earns

Interpreting tip

Spend time doing, not spend time to do. In financial news, spend is also a noun: marketing spend.
