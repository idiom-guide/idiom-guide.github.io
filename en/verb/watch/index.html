watch

watch · watched · watchedMind & sensesNo. 63 in the original

Core imageKeeping your eyes on something over time: watching, extended to monitoring, guarding and taking care.

Meanings and synonyms 4 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with watch; when you would rather not repeat watch, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

    We watched the debate live.

    我們看了辯論的現場直播。

  2. 監視・追蹤spy onvt.
    followtrackmonitorkeep an eye on

    The journalist suspected she was being watched.

    Watch your language.

    說話注意一點。

Interpreting tip

Watch closely and keep a close eye on are more conversational than monitor. "It happened on his watch" assigns political responsibility.
