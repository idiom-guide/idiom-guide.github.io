give

give · gave · givenGetting & giving★ Start hereNo. 25 in the original

Core imageHanding over: giving and providing, extended to performing an action (a speech, advice, a sigh) and to giving way under pressure.

Meanings and synonyms 6 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with give; when you would rather not repeat give, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 給・交付・判（刑）hand overvt.
    presentawardofferdonatecontributeprovidesupplygrantentrustdeliversentence

    Could you give this to the chair?

    可以把這個交給主席嗎？

  2. 提供・給予（權限、消息）providevt.
    allowprovidepermitfurnishconvey

    The pass gives delegates entry to every session.

    這張通行證讓代表可以進入每一場會議。

  3. 做出（演講、證詞、聲音、信號）makevt.
    delivertestifyemitlet outcausegive rise to

    She gave an excellent speech.

    她發表了一場精彩的演講。

  4. 帶給・害（某人得到）causevt.
    causegive rise to

    The news gave us a lot of hope.

    這個消息帶給我們很大希望。

  5. 捐獻donatevi.
    donatecontribute
  6. 讓步；塌陷yieldvi.
    collapsemake concessionsgive way

    Each side will need to give a little to reach a deal.

    雙方都得各退一步才能達成協議。

Interpreting tip

Many Chinese action nouns pair with give: give a speech, give advice, give a presentation, give a sigh. It saves hunting for a specific verb.
