speak

speak · spoke · spokenSpeakingNo. 58 in the original

Core imageOpening your mouth: speaking, speaking a language, giving a speech. More formal than talk, with more focus on one person speaking.

Meanings and synonyms 2 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with speak; when you would rather not repeat speak, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 說（語言）・說出talkvt.
    statedeclareutterarticulatevoice

    How many languages do you speak?

    你會說幾種語言？

  2. 說話・發言・演講pronounce wordsvi.
    uttervoiceaddresslecturediscuss

    The minister will speak at the opening ceremony.

    部長將在開幕典禮上致詞。
