believe

believe · believed · believedMind & sensesNo. 92 in the original

Core imageAccepting as true and trusting someone; also a polite way to state an opinion.

Meanings and synonyms 3 meanings

Every meaning on the left can be expressed with believe; when you would rather not repeat believe, the synonyms on the right can replace it. Select an icon to see which other verbs share that meaning.

  1. 認為・相信（某事）thinkvt.
    considersupposeassumereckonpresumeimagine

    I believe we've met before.

    我想我們之前見過。

  2. 相信（說法、某人）acceptvt.
    trusthave faith increditaccept

    Don't believe everything you read online.

    別相信網路上看到的一切。

  3. 有信仰religionvi.
    have faithbe religious

Idioms 7

  • believe in信仰・相信…的價值
  • believe me相信我
  • believe it or not信不信由你
  • don't you believe it!別信那套
  • can't believe one's eyes/ears不敢相信自己的眼睛／耳朵
  • can't believe one's luck不敢相信自己運氣這麼好
  • make believe假裝

Chinese idioms on this site

信以為真believe it to be true

Interpreting tip

I believe… states a position more firmly than I think. Believe in means to hold a value: We believe in open markets.
