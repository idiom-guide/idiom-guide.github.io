live

live · lived · lived行動與變化原書第 30 條

核心意象「活著、過日子」：生存、居住、過某種生活，延伸為靠…維生、盡情享受人生。

義項與同義詞 6 個義項

左欄每個意思都能用 live 來說；反過來，不想一再重複 live 時，右欄的同義詞都能替換。點左側圖示，可以看到還有哪些動詞能表達同一個意思。

  1. 活著・存活existvi.
    be alivesurvivewalk the earth

    She lived to 102.

    她活到一百零二歲。

  2. 住・居住residevi.
    residedwellinhabithave one's home in

    Where do you live?

    你住哪裡？

  3. 過（某種生活）spend one's lifevi.
    have a lifestyle

    They live quite simply.

    他們過得相當簡樸。

  4. 維生・過活survivevi.
    make a livingearn one's livingsupport oneselfmake ends meet

    It's hard to live on a part-time salary.

    靠兼職薪水很難過活。

  5. 盡情享受人生enjoy oneselfvi.
    enjoy lifehave funlive life to the full
  6. 經歷・過著experiencevt.
    leadhavego throughundergo

    He lived a long and happy life.

    他度過了長壽而快樂的一生。

片語動詞 7

  • live it down讓人淡忘（糗事）
    get over the shame
  • live off靠…生活
    depend onsubsist on
  • live on靠…為生；以…為食

    Many retirees live on their pensions.

    許多退休人士靠退休金過活。

    subsist onsurvive on
  • live out度過（餘生）；實現
    spend the rest of one's lifefulfil
  • live through經歷（並熬過）

    My grandparents lived through the war.

    我祖父母經歷過戰爭。

    experiencesurviveendure
  • live up to達到（期望）・不負

    The sequel didn't live up to expectations.

    續集沒有達到大家的期待。

    fulfilmeetmatch
  • live with接受・忍受；同住
    accepttolerateput up with

慣用語 11

  • live by one's wits靠小聰明過活
  • man cannot live by bread alone人不能只靠麵包活著
  • live dangerously冒險・鋌而走險
  • live from hand to mouth勉強糊口
  • live in hope抱著希望
  • live in the past活在過去
  • live it up盡情享樂
  • live and let live自己活也讓別人活・互相包容
  • I can live with that這我可以接受
  • live in an ivory tower住在象牙塔裡
  • live on borrowed time來日無多

本站成語

縱情享樂live it up生不如死living in misery安居樂業live and work in peace量入為出live within one's means生死與共live with it, die with it不負眾望live up to sb's name/reputation/expectation/billing

口譯員提醒

「這個條件我可以接受」可以說 I can live with that.，語氣輕鬆，比 accept 有彈性。「不負眾望」是 live up to expectations。
