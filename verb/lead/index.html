lead

lead · led · led移動與位置原書第 29 條

核心意象「走在前面帶路」：引導、領導、領先，延伸為導致某種結果，以及過某種生活。

義項與同義詞 7 個義項

左欄每個意思都能用 lead 來說；反過來，不想一再重複 lead 時，右欄的同義詞都能替換。點左側圖示，可以看到還有哪些動詞能表達同一個意思。

  1. 帶領・引導guidevt.
    conductescortshowsteerushertakedirect

    The host led us to the conference room.

    主人帶我們到會議室。

  2. 領導・率領・領先be firstvt.
    governrunheadbe in charge ofpreside overdirectspearheadchampion

    She led the delegation to Brussels.

    她率團前往布魯塞爾。

  3. 使（某人）認為・誘導convincevt.
    swayinfluencepersuademake

    The data led us to rethink our approach.

    這些數據讓我們重新思考做法。

  4. 過（某種生活）livevt.
    livehaveexperience

    They lead a simple life in the mountains.

    他們在山上過著簡樸的生活。

  5. 通往govi.
    gorunstretch

    This corridor leads to the main hall.

    這條走廊通往大廳。

  6. 導致causevi.
    result inproducecontribute tobring about

    Poor planning can lead to delays.

    規劃不周可能導致延誤。

  7. 領導・帶頭guidevi.
    commanddirectguide

片語動詞 4

  • lead off (from)從…岔出
    branch offstart from
  • lead sb on誤導・吊人胃口
    encourageenticelurestring along
  • lead up to在…之前；導致

    In the days leading up to the summit, security was tightened.

    峰會前幾天，維安層級提高。

    precederesult inbring about
  • lead with以…為頭條・開場
    open withfeature

慣用語 8

  • lead sb by the nose牽著某人的鼻子走
  • lead the field/world居領先地位
  • lead nowhere毫無結果
  • lead sb a merry dance讓人疲於奔命
  • lead from the front以身作則
  • lead sb up the garden path誤導・欺騙
  • take the lead帶頭；取得領先
  • a lead線索

口譯員提醒

「導致」用 lead to 最自然，比 result in、give rise to 口語；「率團」是 lead a delegation。新聞裡「頭條報導」常說 lead with…：The evening news led with the earthquake.
