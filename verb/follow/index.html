follow

follow · followed · followed移動與位置原書第 65 條

核心意象「跟在後面」：跟隨、接著發生，延伸為沿著走、遵照、聽懂，以及持續關注。

義項與同義詞 9 個義項

左欄每個意思都能用 follow 來說；反過來，不想一再重複 follow 時，右欄的同義詞都能替換。點左側圖示，可以看到還有哪些動詞能表達同一個意思。

  1. 跟著・接在…之後go / come aftervt.
    pursuetrailtrackchaseshadowsucceedcome next

    A reception will follow the ceremony.

    典禮之後有酒會。

  2. 推論出resultvt.
    follow logically
  3. 沿著（路）走tracevt.
    go alongproceed alongtake

    Follow the signs to the main hall.

    沿著指標走到大廳。

  4. 遵照・效法adhere tovt.
    adoptheedobeybe guided bystick tocomply with

    Please follow the instructions carefully.

    請仔細遵照指示。

  5. 聽懂・跟得上understandvt.
    understandcomprehendgraspgetcatch

    Sorry, I don't quite follow.

    抱歉，我不太懂。

  6. 關注・追蹤keep up withvt.
    keep up to date withwatchmonitorkeep abreast of

    Are you following the trade talks?

    你有在關注貿易談判嗎？

  7. 隨後發生go / come aftervi.
    ensue

    Details will follow.

    細節稍後公布。

  8. （邏輯上）成立resultvi.
    ensue
  9. 聽懂understandvi.
    comprehendgraspget

片語動詞 2

  • follow through貫徹・落實

    It's not enough to promise; we need to follow through.

    光承諾不夠，還必須落實。

    implementcarry out
  • follow up追蹤・跟進

    I'll follow up with an email.

    我會再寄電子郵件跟進。

    investigatepursue

慣用語 5

  • follow in sb's footsteps步某人後塵・子承父業
  • follow one's nose循著氣味走；憑直覺
  • follow suit跟進
  • follow the crowd隨波逐流
  • a hard act to follow難以超越

口譯員提醒

會議口譯常聽到 follow-up question（追問）和 follow up on…（追蹤處理）。「我沒聽懂」可以禮貌地說 I'm not sure I follow.，語氣比 I don't understand 委婉。
