go

go · went · gone移動與位置★ 先學原書第 2 條

核心意象離開原點往外移動；延伸為「運轉」「消失」「延伸到」「變成（多半變差）」「花掉」。

義項與同義詞 5 個義項

左欄每個意思都能用 go 來說；反過來，不想一再重複 go 時，右欄的同義詞都能替換。點左側圖示，可以看到還有哪些動詞能表達同一個意思。

  1. 去・前往・運轉movevi.
    travelproceedjourneyadvanceleavedepartwithdrawfunctionworkbe sold to

    We'll go by train and be back by six.

    我們搭火車去，六點前回來。

  2. 消失・衰退leavevi.
    deterioratebecome impaireddisappeardeclinevanish

    My appetite has completely gone.

    我完全沒胃口了。

  3. 延伸・通到extendvi.
    lead tospanunfoldspreadstretch

    The trail goes up to the summit.

    這條步道通往山頂。

  4. 變成（多半變差）becomevi.
    turnbecome

    The milk has gone sour.

    牛奶酸掉了。

  5. （錢、資源）花在spendvi.
    be spent onbe used for

    Half the grant went on equipment.

    補助款有一半花在設備上。

片語動詞 8

  • go along with同意・附和

    I'll go along with the majority view.

    我配合多數人的意見。

    concuracceptfollow
  • go by（時間）過去
    elapsepass
  • go in for喜歡・熱衷於
    enjoybe keen on
  • go off變質；（警報）響起；不再喜歡

    The fire alarm went off during the exam.

    考試時火災警報突然響了。

    spoilsoundlose interest in
  • go on發生；繼續；說個沒完

    What's going on here?

    這裡發生什麼事了？

    happencontinueramblenatter
  • go out熄滅；交往；（心）同情
    die downsubsidedatecourt
  • go over檢查・複習；討論；排練

    Let's go over the main points once more.

    我們再把重點過一遍。

    reviewexamineinspectcheckrehearse
  • go through經歷；仔細檢查

    The country went through a deep recession.

    這個國家經歷了一場嚴重衰退。

    undergoexperienceendureexamine

口譯員提醒

「事情進行得如何？」可以說 How did it go?；「一切順利」是 It went well.，比 proceed 口語自然。go + 形容詞多半指變壞：go bad、go bankrupt、go wrong。
