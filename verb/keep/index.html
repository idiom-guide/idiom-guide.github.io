keep

keep · kept · kept取得與給予★ 先學原書第 27 條

核心意象讓東西「留在原處、維持原狀」：保留、保持、遵守；連「一直做」也是讓動作持續下去。

義項與同義詞 8 個義項

左欄每個意思都能用 keep 來說；反過來，不想一再重複 keep 時，右欄的同義詞都能替換。點左側圖示，可以看到還有哪些動詞能表達同一個意思。

  1. 留著・保存・存放retainvt.
    holdpreservehang on tosavestoreconservepossesskeep secret

    You can keep this copy; I have another one.

    這份你留著，我還有一份。

  2. 保持（某種狀態）maintainvt.
    maintain

    Please keep your answers brief.

    請簡短作答。

  3. 養（家、動物）supportvt.
    raiseprovide forsupportlook afterfeedtend

    They keep chickens in the backyard.

    他們在後院養雞。

  4. 耽擱・阻止・隱瞞detainvt.
    stoppreventimpedeobstructdeterdelayconfinewithhold

    Sorry to keep you waiting.

    抱歉讓你久等了。

  5. 遵守・信守observevt.
    comply withrespectobeyadhere tofulfilcarry outhonour

    He always keeps his promises.

    他一向說到做到。

  6. 記錄（日記、帳）writevt.
    compilerecordlog

    We keep a record of every request.

    每一筆申請我們都有記錄。

  7. 一直・持續continuevi.
    continuepersistpersevere

    The phone kept ringing throughout the meeting.

    開會時電話一直響個不停。

  8. （食物）保鮮stay freshvi.
    be preservedlast

    Cooked rice won't keep for long.

    煮好的飯放不久。

片語動詞 5

  • keep at (it)堅持下去

    Fluency comes slowly, so keep at it.

    流利需要時間，堅持下去就對了。

    perseverepersistcontinue
  • keep away (from)遠離
    avoidstay away
  • keep off避開；戒掉

    I'm trying to keep off sugar this month.

    這個月我在戒糖。

    avoidabstain from
  • keep to遵守；不偏離

    Please keep to the five-minute time limit.

    請遵守五分鐘的時間限制。

    stick toadhere toobey
  • keep up (with)跟上；維持

    It's hard to keep up with a fast speaker.

    講者語速快的時候，很難跟上。

    followstay abreast ofcontinuemaintain

口譯員提醒

「一直、不斷」不必用 continuously：He kept interrupting.（他一直插話。）就很道地。keep + V-ing 有時帶點抱怨語氣，譯成中文時可以保留。
