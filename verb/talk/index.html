talk

talk · talked · talked言語與溝通原書第 46 條

核心意象「交談」：說話、聊天、談判，也指說閒話與招供；口語中還能強調「我們說的可是…」。

義項與同義詞 5 個義項

左欄每個意思都能用 talk 來說；反過來，不想一再重複 talk 時，右欄的同義詞都能替換。點左側圖示，可以看到還有哪些動詞能表達同一個意思。

  1. 說話・談・討論speakvi.
    speakchatconversediscussaddresscover

    Can we talk about this later?

    這件事我們晚點再談好嗎？

  2. 談判・協商negotiatevi.
    negotiatediscussbargain

    The two sides are still talking.

    雙方仍在協商。

  3. 說閒話gossipvi.
    gossipspread rumours
  4. 招供・吐實confessvi.
    confessadmitrevealspill the beans
  5. 強調（we're talking…）emphasisevt.
    meanrefer to

    We're talking millions of dollars here.

    這可是好幾百萬美元的事。

片語動詞 6

  • talk back頂嘴
    answer backbe impudent
  • talk down殺價・說服對方降價
    bargain downbeat down
  • talk down to以高高在上的口氣說話
    condescend topatronise
  • talk into說服（某人做）

    She talked me into giving the keynote.

    她說服我擔任主講。

    persuadeconvincewin overinduce
  • talk out of勸阻
    dissuade
  • talk over商量・討論

    Let's talk it over with the team.

    我們跟團隊商量一下。

    discuss

口譯員提醒

外交場合的「會談」可以說 hold talks，「雙方持續溝通」是 The two sides are still talking.。「說服」不一定要 persuade，talk sb into sth 更口語。
