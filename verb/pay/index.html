pay

pay · paid · paid取得與給予原書第 11 條

核心意象「付出」：付錢、付代價，也付出注意力與敬意；反過來說，事情「值得」也是 pay。

義項與同義詞 4 個義項

左欄每個意思都能用 pay 來說；反過來，不想一再重複 pay 時，右欄的同義詞都能替換。點左側圖示，可以看到還有哪些動詞能表達同一個意思。

  1. 付錢・支付give moneyvt.
    spendgive money torecompenseremuneraterewardsettlecleardischargeearnbring inyield

    Can I pay by card?

    可以刷卡嗎？

  2. 付出（代價、注意）bearvt.
    bestowdevotesufferpay the priceatonesuffer the consequences

    Please pay attention to the safety instructions.

    請注意安全說明。

  3. 進行（拜訪、致意）makevt.
    makegive (a visita calla compliment)

    The minister paid a visit to the flood area.

    部長前往水災災區視察。

  4. 划得來・值得worthwhilevi.
    be profitablemake moneymake a returnbe worthwhilebe advantageous

    It pays to read the contract carefully.

    合約仔細讀，絕對值得。

片語動詞 6

  • pay back還錢；報答；報復

    I'll pay you back next week.

    我下週還你。

    repayreturnreciprocateget revenge
  • pay down分期償還（債務）
    pay offreduce
  • pay for為…付出代價
    answer forbe punished formake amends for
  • pay off還清；終於有成果

    All that preparation finally paid off.

    那些準備終於有了回報。

    settlebear fruitbe worth it
  • pay out支付（大筆款項）・理賠
    disbursedistribute
  • pay up付清（欠款）
    settle up

慣用語 7

  • pay lip service to口頭上支持・說說而已
  • pay the piper承擔後果
  • pay tribute to向…致敬
  • pay one's way自己付帳・自食其力
  • pay through the nose付出高昂代價・花冤枉錢
  • pay one's last respects弔唁・送最後一程

口譯員提醒

「關注」「留意」說 pay attention to，比 focus on 更常見；「拜訪」「致意」可以說 pay a visit、pay tribute。口譯時遇到「付出代價」，pay the price 最直接。
