make

make · made · made行動與變化★ 先學原書第 33 條

核心意象讓事情「成形」：做出東西、造成結果、促使別人行動；連「趕上」「賺到」也是把結果做出來。

義項與同義詞 6 個義項

左欄每個意思都能用 make 來說；反過來，不想一再重複 make 時，右欄的同義詞都能替換。點左側圖示，可以看到還有哪些動詞能表達同一個意思。

  1. 使得・造成cause
    effectgenerateengendercreateoccasiongive rise tobring about

    The delay made everyone nervous.

    延誤讓大家都緊張起來。

  2. 做出・製造・制定・構成produce
    manufactureassembleconstructcomposefashionforgecreateissueconstitute

    We've made a shortlist of three candidates.

    我們擬出三人的候選名單。

  3. 準備（飯菜、床鋪）；會成為prepare
    preparecookdevelop intobecome

    You'd make an excellent moderator.

    你會是很出色的主持人。

  4. 趕上・到得了arrive
    arrivecatchreach in time

    If we leave now, we can still make the last train.

    現在出發還趕得上末班車。

  5. 賺・獲利・交（朋友）get
    earngainhave income ofbe profitableturn a profit

    The startup hasn't made a profit yet.

    這家新創公司還沒獲利。

  6. 迫使force
    coerceobligecompelpressureforce

    Nobody can make you sign it.

    沒有人能逼你簽字。

片語動詞 6

  • make for有助於；朝…走去

    A clear agenda makes for a shorter meeting.

    議程清楚，會議就開得短。

    promotefacilitatefurthercontribute tohead for
  • make off (with)逃走；捲走
    fleerun awayboltsteal
  • make out看清・聽清；理解；把…說成

    The audio was so poor that we couldn't make out the speaker's words.

    音質太差，我們聽不清楚講者在說什麼。

    perceivedecipherdiscerncomprehendgraspmaintain
  • make over翻修；過戶
    redecoraterenovatetransfer
  • make up編造；整理（房間）；和好；構成

    Women make up 40 percent of the workforce.

    女性占勞動力的四成。

    inventdevisefabricatedream uppreparereconcileconstitute
  • make up for彌補

    We added an extra session to make up for the cancelled class.

    我們加開一堂課，補回停掉的那堂。

    compensate foroffset

口譯員提醒

make it 一個說法就能涵蓋「趕上、出席、成功」：Can you make it on Friday?（週五能來嗎？）、She made it to the finals.（她打進決賽。）中譯英遇到「來得及、到得了、成功了」，先想到 make it。
