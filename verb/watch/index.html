watch

watch · watched · watched心智與感知原書第 63 條

核心意象「持續地看著」：觀看、盯著，延伸為監視、照看，以及「留意、注意」。

義項與同義詞 4 個義項

左欄每個意思都能用 watch 來說；反過來，不想一再重複 watch 時，右欄的同義詞都能替換。點左側圖示，可以看到還有哪些動詞能表達同一個意思。

  1. 看・觀看look atvt.
    observevieweyegaze atstare atscrutinisesurvey

    We watched the debate live.

    我們看了辯論的現場直播。

  2. 監視・追蹤spy onvt.
    followtrackmonitorkeep an eye on

    The journalist suspected she was being watched.

    那位記者懷疑自己遭到監視。

  3. 看守・照看guardvt.
    protectsafeguardlook aftertake care ofmind

    Could you watch my bag for a minute?

    可以幫我看一下包包嗎？

  4. 注意・小心mindvt.
    be careful aboutpay heed tomind

    Watch your language.

    說話注意一點。

片語動詞 3

  • watch out (for)小心・留意
    be carefulbe on the alertbeware of
  • watch over看守・照顧
    protectguardlook after
  • watch for等著・留意

    Investors are watching for signs of a slowdown.

    投資人正留意景氣放緩的跡象。

    be on the lookout forawait

慣用語 6

  • watch one's back小心提防
  • watch one's step走路小心；謹言慎行
  • watch sb like a hawk緊盯不放
  • watch the world go by看人來人往
  • keep watch守望・看守
  • on one's watch在某人任內

本站成語

注意後防watch your six

口譯員提醒

「密切關注」說 watch closely 或 keep a close eye on，比 monitor 口語。「在他任內發生」是 It happened on his watch.，常用來追究政治責任。
