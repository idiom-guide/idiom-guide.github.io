愛情與婚姻

相戀、婚配、分合

ㄅ

🏰
不愛江山愛美人
the world well lost for love
choose love over a crown
He gave up the throne for love, choosing the woman over the crown.
👵
白頭偕老
grow old together
We promised to grow old together.

ㄆ

🔗
破鏡重圓
get back together
After two years apart, the couple got back together.

ㄇ

🚪
門當戶對
well-matched in family background
Her parents wanted a husband well-matched in family background.

ㄊ

💒
天作之合
a match made in heaven
Everyone says they are a match made in heaven.

ㄌ

💑
兩情相悅
mutual affection
The marriage was built on mutual affection.
🐦
勞燕分飛
go their separate ways
After graduation the couple went their separate ways.

ㄏ

⛰️
海誓山盟
vows of eternal love
They exchanged vows of eternal love.

ㄑ

🎋
青梅竹馬
childhood sweethearts
They were childhood sweethearts.
💕
情投意合
well-suited to each other
The couple are well-suited to each other.

ㄒ

🆕
喜新厭舊
fickle in love
always chasing the new
She accused him of being fickle in love.
👫
相敬如賓
treat each other with respect
After fifty years of marriage, they still treat each other with respect.

ㄧ

❤️
一見鍾情
love at first sight
They fell in love at first sight.
💔
移情別戀
fall for someone else
He fell for someone else while working abroad.
💖
有情人終成眷屬
love finds a way
After years apart, love found a way.
💘
英雄難過美人關
a weakness for beauty
fall for a pretty face
Even a great leader can fall for a pretty face.

ㄡ

🧵
藕斷絲連
still not fully over each other
They have broken up but are still not fully over each other.
