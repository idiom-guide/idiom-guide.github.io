ㄇ

🤗
莫逆之交
bosom friends
They have been bosom friends since college.

ㄉ

🙂
點頭之交
a nodding acquaintance
He is only a nodding acquaintance.

ㄊ

🤲
推心置腹
speak from the heart
confide in sb
After years of friendship, she could confide in him about anything.
🍬
同甘共苦
share the good times and the bad
The partners have shared the good times and the bad.

ㄌ

🗡️
兩肋插刀
go to the wall for a friend
He would go to the wall for a friend.

ㄍ

💗
肝膽相照
utterly loyal friends
The two are utterly loyal friends.

ㄏ

⛈️
患難與共
stick together through thick and thin
They stuck together through thick and thin.

ㄐ

🍖
酒肉朋友
fair-weather friends
When the money ran out, his fair-weather friends disappeared.

ㄑ

👬
情同手足
as close as brothers
The teammates are as close as brothers.

ㄒ

⌛
相見恨晚
wish we had met sooner
We got on so well that I wish we had met sooner.
👥
形影不離
inseparable
The twins are inseparable.
❄️
雪中送炭
help when it is needed most
A true friend helps when it is needed most.

ㄓ

🎣
志趣相投
kindred spirits
The two writers are kindred spirits.

ㄧ

😄
一見如故
hit it off like old friends
We hit it off like old friends.
