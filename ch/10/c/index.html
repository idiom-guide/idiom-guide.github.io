文化與傳承

ㄅ

🌊
博大精深
extensive and profound
Chinese medicine is extensive and profound.

ㄊ

♻️
推陳出新
breathe new life into tradition
Young designers are breathing new life into tradition.

ㄌ

🕰️
歷久彌新
timeless
Her designs are timeless.

ㄍ

🏺
古為今用
adapt the past for the present
The architect adapts the past for the present.

ㄎ

🎶
膾炙人口
widely loved and often quoted
The poem is widely loved and often quoted.

ㄐ

🏠
家喻戶曉
household name
The singer became a household name overnight.
🌈
兼容並蓄
embrace diverse views
all-embracing
The university embraces diverse views.

ㄔ

🌉
承先啟後
bridge the past and the future
The exhibition bridges the past and the future of the craft.

ㄕ

🌱
數典忘祖
forget sb's root
To abandon your own language is to forget your roots.

ㄖ

🥢
入境隨俗
when in Rome, do as the Romans do
When in Rome, do as the Romans do: take off your shoes at the door.

ㄧ

🧬
一脈相承
in an unbroken line
The two schools of painting descend in an unbroken line.
🚰
飲水思源
appreciate the heritage
We should appreciate our heritage and remember where we came from.

ㄩ

📜
約定俗成
established by convention
by common usage
The rule has become established by convention.
🏞️
源遠流長
a long course
Tea culture in Asia has a long course of history.
