友善與客套

ㄅ

🙇
表示歉意
as a penance
do penance for
He bought her flowers as a penance.
🛎️
備受禮遇
feel very privileged
The visiting scholars felt very privileged.

ㄇ

🎤
媒體朋友
friends from the media
Welcome to all our friends from the media.

ㄉ

🎊
獨樂樂不如眾樂樂
activity...is best when shared
Music is best when shared, so let's play together.

ㄌ

😊
樂意之至
gladly
be more than happy to
Would you help me move? I'd be more than happy to.

ㄍ

🏙️
高大上
high caliber
The restaurant is high-caliber and very classy.
📝
感謝忠告
words to live by
Thank you for the advice; those are words to live by.

ㄏ

💝
好心好意
well-meaning
well-intentioned
She was well-meaning but clumsy.

ㄐ

🙏
敬請各位不吝指教
your remarks and comments are kindly welcomed
Your remarks and comments are kindly welcomed.

ㄒ

👂
洗耳恭聽
be all ears
Go ahead, I'm all ears.
💬
相談甚歡
had a nice talk
We had a nice talk over coffee.

ㄓ

🎖️
至高榮幸
it is my great honor
I am deeply honored
It is my great honor to speak here today.
🍀
祝你好運
good luck
best of luck
Good luck on your exam.
📌
鄭重看待
attach importance to
The committee attaches great importance to student feedback.

ㄔ

💐
承蒙厚愛
thanks for liking my sth
Thanks for liking my book; your support means a lot.
😰
誠惶誠恐
with great humility
He accepted the award with great humility.

ㄖ

🥄
弱水三千，只取一瓢
out of all the beauties, I only have eyes for you
Out of all the beauties in the world, I only have eyes for you.
🙏
榮幸之至
feel more than blessed
I feel more than blessed to be here tonight.
🎉
熱烈歡迎
roll out the welcome wagon for
The town rolled out the welcome wagon for the visiting team.

ㄧ

🎩
以禮相待
in the name of decorum
In the name of decorum, the hosts treated every guest with courtesy.
🌹
溢美之詞
effusive praise
overpraise
He was embarrassed by the effusive praise.
🤝
友誼長存
everlasting friendship
Let's drink to our everlasting friendship.

ㄨ

🌈
未來會更好
Better days ahead
Hang in there: better days ahead.
