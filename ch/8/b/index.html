經營與財務

收支、投資、盈虧、資金壓力

ㄅ

🏗️
白手起家
a self-made man/woman
start from scratch
She is a self-made woman who started from scratch.

ㄌ

🧮
量入為出
live within one's means
A startup has to live within its means.

ㄎ

🚰
開源節流
boost revenue and cut costs
The CFO's plan is simple: boost revenue and cut costs.

ㄐ

🧮
精打細算
astute = sharp
She is astute with money and plans every purchase.

ㄒ

🕳️
血本無歸
lose one's entire investment
Many retail investors lost their entire investment.

ㄓ

👕
捉襟見肘
cash-strapped
stretched thin
The cash-strapped agency froze hiring.
🏗️
債台高築
debt-ridden
up to one's neck in debt
The developer is up to its neck in debt.

ㄕ

🥚
殺雞取卵
kill the goose that lays the golden eggs
Raising fees again would kill the goose that lays the golden eggs.

ㄖ

💵
日進斗金
rake in money
The app was raking in money within months.
💸
入不敷出
run a deficit
spend more than one earns
The hospital has been running a deficit for years.

ㄘ

🧧
財源廣進
may business prosper (greeting)
We wish you a prosperous new year: may business prosper!

ㄧ

🎰
一本萬利
a small outlay for a huge return
There is no such thing as a small outlay for a guaranteed huge return.
🗓️
寅吃卯糧
borrow against the future
Funding today's pensions with new debt is borrowing against the future.
