in black and white
I'd like the terms in black and white.

💲
漫天要價
ask a sky-high price
The seller asked a sky-high price, expecting to be talked down.

ㄊ

🤑
討價還價
haggle
bargain
The two sides haggled over the delivery schedule.

ㄍ

↔️
各退一步
meet halfway
Let's meet halfway and close the deal today.
🛒
各取所需
give and take
The partnership is give and take: she gets funding and he gets exposure.

ㄎ

👄
口說無憑
a verbal promise is no guarantee
A verbal promise is no guarantee: please put it in writing.

ㄏ

🔀
互惠互利
mutually beneficial
win-win
We are looking for a mutually beneficial arrangement.
🔍
貨比三家
shop around
Shop around before you sign with a vendor.

ㄑ

🤜
強強聯手
a powerhouse partnership
The merger is a powerhouse partnership between two market leaders.

ㄗ

🔺
坐地起價
jack up the price at the last minute
The contractor jacked up the price at the last minute.

ㄧ

👏
一拍即合
hit it off
click at once
The two founders hit it off at their first meeting.
🤝
一言為定
deal
Ten o'clock tomorrow? Deal.
🤞
言而有信
keep one's word
Suppliers trust her because she keeps her word.
🤥
言而無信
go back on one's word
No one wants a partner who goes back on his word.
